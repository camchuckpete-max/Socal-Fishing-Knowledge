# Halco Laser Pro

The Halco Laser Pro is a bibbed trolling minnow — the deep-diving **XDD** bib
version dives hard for its size. It's the lightest lure in Cameron's trolling
box, which makes it a swimmer that fishes the lighter **40–60 lb class**
(cameron). It rides the **low gunnel holders** as a diver. Covered here is
the **LP190 XDD** with the 6M+ "crazy deep" bib.

## How to run it

Run it around the 6.5 kt optimum on 40–60 lb line from a low gunnel holder so
the deep XDD bib gets down, default ~27 yd back (cameron). Its light weight
(1-5/8 oz) makes it a picky-bite change-up on calm days (cameron). Choosing a
deep diver over a swimmer, a spreader bar, or a speed-trolled
[Mad Mac](../lures/mad-mac.md) is decided in
[bluefin trolling](../species/bluefin-tuna-trolling.md); general towing
mechanics live in [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md).
