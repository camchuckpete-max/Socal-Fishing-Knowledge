# Rapala Crush City Mooch Minnow

A shad/baitfish-imitation soft-plastic swimbait in Rapala's Crush City line,
fished in SoCal harbors on a light jig head. Two independent Tackle Express
citations — one on the bait's construction and fall, one on scent/hardware
and harbor jig-head pairings — are gathered here rather than left as a single
bullet buried inside [soft-plastic swimbaits](soft-plastic-swimbaits.md),
the general class note this bait belongs to. Neither citation names a
species on camera.

## Construction — fork-tail profile for a slower, more natural fall

Tackle Express, `2Ee2DFZ9Wk4`, 2025-02-21; unregistered channel, single
mention — medium confidence.

Built like a paddle-tail swimbait but with a smaller, weaker **fork tail** in
place of a full paddle, so it kicks less — framed as a more natural
presentation for local (SoCal) fish. The Crush City material is described as
extremely **stretchy, buoyant, and durable**. Paired with a **lighter jig
head**, the buoyancy slows the fall, which the presenter likes for fishing
the **more suspended area** of the water column — the same application he
reaches for [flukes](bay-bass-plastics.md) for, calling the two "very very
similar" in that use. No specific species, jig-head weight, or location
named on camera.

## Scent, terminal hardware, and two harbor jig-head pairings (Logan, Tackle Express staff pick)

Logan, Tackle Express staff pick, `zlelqp7Qo9Y`, 2025-01-30; unregistered
channel, single mention — medium confidence. Fished **in the harbor** (no
specific harbor named on camera).

- **Scent:** the bait is **scent-infused**, which Logan states he believes
  "does antagonize bites."
- **Terminal hardware:** he states it ships with something he calls "the
  last tech" (garbled ASR, meaning not confidently resolved —
  `asr-uncertain`), which he credits with getting more fish per bait.
- **Two jig-head pairings, tried in sequence on the same session:**
  - A **ball-head jig**, worked with a **slow pop off the bottom**.
  - When that bite **slowed down**, he switched to a **1/4 oz** jig head
    (captioned "under spin" — read here as an **underspin** jig head, the
    same blade-equipped class documented in [Bay Bass
    Plastics](bay-bass-plastics.md) under "Big Spin," whose stated size
    range (1/4 oz up to 2 oz) matches this weight; not confirmed against
    typed metadata — `asr-uncertain`), fished with a **slow grind along the
    bottom**.
  - Both retrieves are stated to have produced fish for him; no species is
    named on camera.
