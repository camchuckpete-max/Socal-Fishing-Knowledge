# Bucktail Jigs

A bucktail is a leadhead jig dressed with hair instead of plastic, fished on a
cast and worked up off the bottom rather than swum at a steady speed. The corpus
carries exactly one record of one in SoCal or Baja waters: a **white** bucktail
in the [Magdalena Bay](../locations/bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md) estuary took
a [snook](../species/snook.md) on a light rig, and it is the only lure any source
in the corpus lands a snook on (`OVwqUKim9Pc`). Everything below that one record
is a gap, not doctrine.

## Specs

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Colour fished | white — the crew call it an "o.g. old school bucktail" (`OVwqUKim9Pc`) |
| Presentation | "popping it up off the bottom" — the bait is on the bottom and lifted off it, not retrieved steadily (`OVwqUKim9Pc`) |
| Water fished | Magdalena Bay estuary channel, off the mangrove edge (`OVwqUKim9Pc`) |
| Outfit | given only as "a light rig"; no rod, reel, line or leader spec is stated (`OVwqUKim9Pc`) |
| Head weight, hook size, hair length, trailer | ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source |

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: head weight, hook size, profile, cadence
beyond the phrase quoted above, any colour rationale, or a second bucktail
record anywhere in the SoCal/Baja corpus. Gear class also has no source — see
[gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md) for the class lexicon this lure has no
entry in yet.

⚠ Fact-check (single-source): the whole note rests on one trip on an
unregistered, sponsor-heavy channel, and on the corpus's only record of a snook
on a lure of any kind — no weight, colour rationale or cadence beyond "popping
it up off the bottom" (`OVwqUKim9Pc`). Relocated here with the content from
[snook](../species/snook.md); single-source is not wrong, and the row stands
until a second source lands.

## When to choose it

- **Estuary channel off a mangrove edge, fish on or near the bottom** — the one
  documented application, routed from
  [snook](../species/snook.md#situations--techniques). The live-bait programs
  that own the rest of that fishery are
  [flyline](../techniques/flyline.md) inside the roots and
  [sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md) at the boca.
