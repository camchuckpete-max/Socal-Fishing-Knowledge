# Cedar Plug

The cedar plug is a plain weighted-nose wood cylinder — no bib, no rattle — that
has caught tuna in SoCal/Baja for generations. It's a surface/wash lure that runs
either right in the prop wash or a long way back, and it fishes a
species-matched **40–60 lb-and-up class** depending on rig. Covered here are
standard 6" cedar plugs.

## Running specs

- **Troll speed:** 5–9 kt (**bluefin 5–5.5, yellowfin 6–6.5**).
- **Running depth:** surface / wash.
- **Setback:** either **15–40 ft in the prop wash OR 50–100+ yd back — nothing in
  between** (the two positions where a cedar plug swims right; the dead zone
  between them does not produce).
- **Leader / top-shot:** rig-dependent (see owned example below).

## How to run it

Match the speed to the species (5–5.5 kt for bluefin, 6–6.5 kt for yellowfin) and
place it in one of its two working positions — tight in the wash (15–40 ft) or
way back (50–100+ yd), never the middle. It's the calm, mixed-grade, over-bait
change-up. **When** to reach for a cedar plug versus a skirt, a swimmer, or a
speed-trolled [Mad Mac](../lures/mad-mac.md) is decided in
[bluefin trolling](../species/bluefin-trolling.md); general towing mechanics live
in [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md).

## Daisy chain — confidence lure, color vs. material (Justin Botril / Capt. Dave Hansen)

Justin Botril (Long Beach/LA Harbor livestreamer/guide), Your Saltwater Guide
live seminar, 2024 PCS Show, `AH4Eiya1Hn0`, 2024-03-09; unregistered speaker,
single mention — medium confidence, with an on-camera correction from Capt.
Dave Hansen (subscription-pitch context — low confidence per the
[source registry](../sources/source-registry.md)):

- **A confidence lure to always carry:** described as having caught
  "hundreds of fish" trolling daisy-chained cedar plugs — "these will save
  your day."
- **Patterns favored:** the natural/"wood" pattern, plus an anchovy pattern
  (transcribed "amp chobby," asr-uncertain) and a sardine pattern.
- **Material, not color (Hansen's on-camera correction):** "wood" doesn't
  name a color — every cedar plug is made of wood/cedar regardless of what
  color it's painted (yellow, blue, purple, black, orange, green, or brown);
  that's the origin of the name "cedar plug," not a specific paint scheme.
  Consistent with this note's framing of the cedar plug as a wood-bodied lure
  across any color choice.
- **Running parameters:** trolled at **~7 mph**, set back **~30 yd** — both
  land inside this note's existing speed (5–9 kt, ~6–7.5 kt for yellowfin)
  and setback (15–40 ft in the wash, or 50–100+ yd back) framework rather
  than adding a new parameter.
