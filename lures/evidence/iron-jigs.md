# Evidence — Iron Jigs

Trip reports and per-source provenance for [iron jigs](../iron-jigs.md).

## Hooks — assist vs. treble

- `VpW91AKOFVQ` (Scotty, Brothers Sportfishing, via Roman Castro, 2020-01-28):
  on one yellowtail landed on an assist-rigged Daiwa SK jig, only one of the
  two assist hooks caught inside the mouth — the second caught outside, on
  the face/nose, and still held through the fight.

## Named models

- `_C7TJoGT0nw` (Tackle Express, "What You ACTUALLY Need for Cedros Fishing
  Trips | The Gear Vault," 2025-09-26, Cedros Island): on a 19–33 lb Cedros
  yellowtail trip, the angler fished more Colt Sniper (100-size) than actual
  yo-yo jigs for its cast/swim-or-yo-yo versatility. On a slower first day the
  fish were keyed in specifically on the Colt Sniper baits over a
  smaller-profile jig named on camera as a Salas 6X Jr. (kept as a distinct
  name rather than merged into "Colt Sniper," since the transcript is
  ambiguous whether this is a second lure or a nickname for a smaller
  Colt Sniper size) — by the second full day the bite had opened up enough
  that the fish would eat almost anything thrown at them. A triple trout
  hardbait and an SP Minnow 115 stickbait were both fished successfully the
  same trip (this hardbait/stickbait detail does not currently appear on
  [Cedros Island](../../locations/cedros-island.md)).
- `F7jLrt2j2X8` (Cesar, Eric's Tackle, via Dirty Hookers, 2023-10-09): a
  traditional heavy jig in this lineup is read on camera as a Tady 4/0 (the
  caption renders it "doua," not fully confirmed by name).
- `BLbUu_mfMJY` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 17,"
  2018-10-18): a "good assortment of lightweight surface irons" for a
  long-range yellowtail trip is given as the Tady 45 and a jig the captions
  render as "SJR eyes" (read here as the Salas 6X Jr. — the caption string
  does not clearly spell the model, but it fits the existing Tady 45 / Salas
  7X / 6X Jr. pairing rather than naming anything new) plus the Salas 7X.
  This is the earliest dated occurrence of the three-jig pairing in this
  corpus, predating the `1uYIApdQQSM` corroboration (2020-06-11) by nearly
  two years.
- `OL8D1l73RVg` (SearcherSportfishing, 2020-10-08): a jig shown on camera as
  "one of the new JRI DW ones" (the "DW" designation is asr-uncertain)
  appears alongside a rod or jig the captions separately render "Cal Star
  100 J" (also asr-uncertain; unclear from the audio whether that name refers
  to the rod being held or the jig itself).
