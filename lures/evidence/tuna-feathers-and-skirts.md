# Evidence — Tuna Feathers and Skirts

Trip reports and per-source provenance for [Tuna Feathers and
Skirts](../tuna-feathers-and-skirts.md).

## Specs

- `9JnIS8HkZlA` (Steve Carson, Penn; San Diego Rod and Reel Club talk,
  2023-11-14): identified his favorite trolled bluefin lure, fished on his
  50 lb outfit, as a Williamson-brand lure he places in the class other
  anglers call "cold snipers"/"current snipers" — exact model name not
  confidently resolved from the captions. Unregistered channel/speaker,
  single mention, medium confidence.
- `ouBrIdO7d4k` (SD Fish and Sips, "Pacific Coast Sportfishing Show 2025,"
  2025-03-08 upload; booth interview at the 2025 OC Fairgrounds trade show,
  a SoCal charter captain not confidently named): described a small,
  finesse-profile trolling lure built for working dolphin/porpoise pods for
  yellowfin, developed from a King Buster-style small trolling lure fished
  around Dana Point roughly a decade-plus prior (mid-2010s per the source).
  The original run used cheap stock King Busters, which have a plastic head
  and can pop out of the water at trolling speed; the rebuild swaps in a
  tungsten 1/2 oz head to keep the lure running true/submerged at speed
  instead of skipping out, rigged with a small Yamashita stainless steel
  double hook (not a single), trolled straight to 60 or 80 lb leader at
  7–9 mph specifically around dolphin/porpoise schools (see [trolling —
  backside of Catalina](../../techniques/trolling.md#socal-backside-of-catalina--porpoise-associated-yellowfin-finesse-trolling)
  for the approach method — stay out in front of the pod, never troll
  through it — and the 60–70 lb yellowfin result cited on camera). The same
  small-profile trolled-feather approach was also volunteered on camera as a
  standard Hawaii method for skipjack (out-of-region contrast, not SoCal
  doctrine). Unregistered channel/speaker, single mention, medium
  confidence; region: socal-bight.
- `SCQnyVEQfHY` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 100 (Test of
  Time)," 2020-05-21): framed the Zuker Zucchini on camera as one of three
  items in the SoCal offshore fishery that "passed the test of time," still
  fished unchanged as of the 2020 upload. `searcher-sportfishing` is a
  registered channel, but this segment is a product/model pick — low
  confidence per the channel's registry sponsored-claim caveat.
