# Evidence — Bay Bass Plastics

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[Bay Bass Plastics](../bay-bass-plastics.md). One line per observation,
grouped under the parent note's section headings.

## Specs — colour and bite reports

- `k4LCL9ALryA` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-04-17, San Diego Bay): during an early
  stop of SWBA tournament pre-fishing, a bite in roughly 18–20 ft came on a
  basic natural shad-pattern soft plastic, worked either right on the drop or
  on a slow wind-back near bottom; the crew also noted an anchovy smell in the
  water. Multi-speaker, unregistered channel.
- `FurifnQ27mM` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-04-19, San Diego Bay/Chula Vista
  Marina): during the SWBA Border Town Brawl tournament day itself
  (spotted-bay-bass-only event), the crew reported a shrimp-pattern lure
  working on fish through the session; narration cuts off before naming the
  bait type or rigging detail. Multi-speaker, unregistered channel.
- `SImABCBBxAo` (StokedOnFishing, 2020-05-13, location not stated): when
  overcast moved in, two anglers on the same boat both switched to
  darker-colored plastics and each drew a fish shortly after the change — a
  color-to-light-condition adjustment, not a doctrine claim. Multi-speaker,
  unregistered channel.
- `SImABCBBxAo` (StokedOnFishing, 2020-05-13, location not stated): with
  smelt or anchovies visibly present in the water, the presenter swapped to a
  bait described as matching that baitfish's small profile (exact
  color/model garbled in the audio) — a bait-profile-to-forage matching move,
  not a doctrine claim. Multi-speaker, unregistered channel.
- `SImABCBBxAo` (StokedOnFishing, 2020-05-13, location not stated): a small
  Yamamoto grub tail in hot orange was named as the bait in play late in the
  session and produced a (small) fish. Multi-speaker, unregistered channel.

## Freshwater-bass leadheads

- `NXvqLUZ6qp8` (Fisher Newb / Robert Schneider, 2019-03-10, San Diego Bay
  jetties): he throws a football jig in pumpkin there, by his own account
  because an early trip on that color produced and he stuck with it — an
  example of the confirmation-bias colour pattern noted beside it in the main
  note, not a claim that pumpkin outperforms other colors there.
