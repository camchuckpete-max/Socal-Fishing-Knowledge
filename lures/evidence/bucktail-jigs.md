## Specs

- `OVwqUKim9Pc` (StokedOnFishing, 2020-10-14, Magdalena Bay estuary, final day

  worked up off the bottom on a light rig, kept for the table. The corpus's only

  the white bucktail you called it" — and the presentation was described on
  camera as "popping it up off the bottom"; the angler noted he was "on a light
  rig". No weight, hook or cadence detail is given anywhere in the episode.
