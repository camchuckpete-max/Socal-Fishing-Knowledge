# Jerkbaits (Baja Cabrilla Program)

**Region: Baja — Sea of Cortez / Bay of LA.** Slender minnow-profile hard
baits burned fast on a cast-and-wind retrieve for
[cabrilla (leopard grouper)](../species/cabrilla.md) working shoreline and
submerged rock structure. Specs and hook upgrades below are from a single
dedicated source (Cesar, via Dirty Hookers, EmZO8QiOfik, 2025-03-04) —
Cesar is a [registered voice](../sources/source-registry.md), but this is
his first committed jerkbait doctrine, so mechanism/parameters sit at
`medium` pending repetition; the specific lure/hook model picks are
product endorsements and stay `low` per his registry caveat.

## The models

- **Yo-Zuri Hydro Minnow LC** (captions render "yui" — the ASR-corrupted
  form of Yo-Zuri; corrected per this corpus's known corruption map) —
  Cesar's #1 pick, and the most consistent out-of-the-box performer: no
  hook or split-ring changes needed, ready to fish as sold. Runs
  **1–3 ft** deep (occasionally to 4 ft).
- **Yo-Zuri Mag Minnow** — a bigger-profile alternative in the same family
  that also casts well; not detailed further in this source.
- **Current Sniper** — Cesar's pick when fish are sitting a little deeper
  (mid-to-late morning on): runs deeper than the Hydro Minnow, **5–7 ft**.
  Comes with a lighter, thinner stock hook and needs the hook upgraded (see
  Rigging below). Fish the **170 size only** — smaller sizes don't have
  enough action to trigger the cabrilla bite.
- **Micro-profile jerkbaits (fall/winter Oct+ match-the-hatch, ~1–1.5 in
  bait):** small-profile jerkbaits sized to Sea-of-Cortez micro-bait; this
  source names additional small-lure picks that could not be confidently
  reconstructed from the captions (asr-uncertain — not carried by name; see
  extraction log). Small [jig paras](iron-jigs.md) fill the same role on
  the yo-yo-iron side.

## Sizing by time of day

- **Early morning / late afternoon (low light), fish aggressive:** the
  larger **170 size**.
- **After ~9–10 am, fish more finicky:** drop to the **150 size** (Hydro
  Minnow LC) or the Mag Minnow.
- **Color:** low light (before/after full sun) — white, bright, or even
  dark colors all get bit. Once the sun is up and light penetrates —
  brighter, flashier, metallic colors get bit better.

## Retrieve — action is the trigger

- **Burn it, don't finesse it.** These baits are designed to be swum fast;
  fishing them on anything slower than a **6.5:1** retrieve reel makes the
  angler work much harder to get bit. Cesar fishes 6.5:1–7:1 reels.
- **Want:** a fast, **tight wobbling/kicking action** with a little
  "hunting" (slight off-track wander) — that's the trigger.
- **Don't want:** a **rolling** action. Slow-rolling baits generally do not
  get bit; the one stated (unconfirmed) exception is lethargic fish during
  the spawn.
- **Cast rate:** the program runs **~2–3 casts/minute** on average —
  fast, high-volume casting, not a soak.

## Rigging — the Current Sniper hook upgrade

The Current Sniper ships with a lighter, thinner-wire hook that needs
upgrading for cabrilla: go **one hook size bigger** and **one wire gauge
heavier** than stock. Two hook choices, situational (not a doctrine
conflict — a deliberate trade):

- **Owner STX, size 1, Z-wire** — lighter wire, preserves the bait's
  swimming action; the pick for a finicky bite.
- **Owner ST-66, size 1, 1X (heavier wire)** — holds up to anything you
  hook in a hot, wide-open frenzy bite, but the added weight dulls the
  swimming action a little; the pick when the bite is wide open.

The Hydro Minnow LC needs no hook or split-ring changes out of the package.
See [hooks](../tackle/hooks.md#baja-sea-of-cortez-sizing) for the broader
Baja/Sea of Cortez hook-sizing context, including split-ring sizing for
these baits.

## How many to carry

Cabrilla hit hard enough to twist hook hangers and destroy jerkbaits, and a
loaded spot can burn through baits fast: on one Tony Reyes panga trip
(Sea of Cortez / Bay of LA; exact date not given in this source), three
break-offs/spectra pull-outs happened in the same spot inside ~45 seconds —
attributed to the sheer size/power of the fish, not lure or hook failure.
Bring:

- **Hardcore cabrilla-only session (Cesar's own kit):** ~15 baits per
  6-hour morning trip — at least 3 of every color fished regularly, 4–5 of
  favorite colors.
- **Casual angler, not targeting cabrilla exclusively:** ~6 baits per day —
  at least 2–3 of the colors you expect to use most.
- Always carry **multiples of a confidence color** — losing the only bait
  of a color that's working kills confidence for the rest of the session.

## How to fish it

Cast tight to structure (shoreline rock, submerged "dark spot" rock) and
burn back at speed. Which situation calls for the 170 vs. 150 size, which
color, and jerkbait vs. [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) is routed
from [cabrilla](../species/cabrilla.md).
