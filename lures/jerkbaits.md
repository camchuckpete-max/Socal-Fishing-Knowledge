# Jerkbaits (Baja Cabrilla Program)

Slender minnow-profile hardbaits burned fast on a cast-and-wind retrieve for
[cabrilla (leopard grouper)](../species/cabrilla.md) working shoreline rock
and submerged structure in the Sea of Cortez / Bay of LA (`EmZO8QiOfik`).
Two models cover most of a day: a shallow runner fished stock out of the
package, and a deeper runner switched to by midday or later in the morning,
once fish are sitting a little deeper. Speed and action are the trigger —
these baits are built to be burned, not finessed.

The Hydro Minnow LC is the most consistent out-of-the-box performer of the

## Sizing, color, and retrieve

- **Time of day / size:** early morning and late afternoon (low light),
  fish are aggressive — fish the larger **170**. After roughly 9–10 am, as
  fish get more finicky, drop to the **150** (Hydro Minnow LC) or the Mag
  Minnow (`EmZO8QiOfik`).
- **Color:** in low light (before/after full sun), white, bright, and dark
  colors all draw bites. Once the sun is up and light penetrates, brighter,
  flashier, metallic colors out-fish the rest (`EmZO8QiOfik`).
- **Retrieve speed:** these baits are built to be swum fast — a reel slower
  than **6.5:1** makes it harder to hit the retrieve speed that triggers a
  bite; fish a 6.5:1–7:1 gear ratio (`EmZO8QiOfik`).
- **Action:** the trigger is a fast, tight wobbling/kicking action with a
  little "hunting" (slight off-track wander). A rolling action rarely draws
  a bite; the one exception raised — lethargic fish eating a slow-rolled
  bait during the spawn — is stated as unconfirmed (`EmZO8QiOfik`; see
  [cabrilla spawning](../species/cabrilla.md#spawning)).
- **Cast rate:** the program runs **~2–3 casts/minute** on average — fast,
  high-volume casting, not a soak (`EmZO8QiOfik`).
- **Presentation:** cast tight to structure — shoreline rock, submerged
  "dark spot" rock — and burn it straight back at speed (`EmZO8QiOfik`).

## Rigging — Current Sniper hook upgrade

The Current Sniper ships with a lighter, thinner-wire hook that needs
upgrading for cabrilla: go one hook size bigger and one wire gauge heavier
than stock. Two hook choices trade action for durability, not a doctrine
conflict (`EmZO8QiOfik`):

- **Owner STX, size 1, Z-wire** — lighter wire, preserves the bait's
  swimming action; the pick for a finicky bite.
- **Owner ST-66, size 1, 1X (heavier wire)** — holds up to anything hooked
  in a hot, wide-open frenzy bite, at the cost of some swimming action.

The Hydro Minnow LC needs no hook or split-ring changes out of the package
(`EmZO8QiOfik`). See [hooks](../tackle/hooks.md#baja-sea-of-cortez-sizing)
for the broader Baja/Sea of Cortez hook-sizing context, including
split-ring sizing for these baits.

## How many to carry

Cabrilla hit hard enough to twist hook hangers and destroy jerkbaits, and a
loaded spot can burn through baits fast (`EmZO8QiOfik`). Bring:

- **Hardcore cabrilla-only session:** ~15 baits per 6-hour morning trip —
  at least 3 of every color fished regularly, 4–5 of favorite colors
  (`EmZO8QiOfik`).
- **Casual angler, not targeting cabrilla exclusively:** ~6 baits per day —
  at least 2–3 of the colors expected to see the most use (`EmZO8QiOfik`).
- Carry multiples of a confidence color: losing the only bait of a color
  that's working kills confidence for the rest of the session
  (`EmZO8QiOfik`).

## When to choose it

- Casting tight to shoreline rock and submerged structure for cabrilla,
  choosing a jerkbait over [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) or a
  bait soak: routed from [cabrilla](../species/cabrilla.md).
- Size, color, and jerkbait-vs-iron selection by time of day and bite state
  is the [cabrilla](../species/cabrilla.md) router's call, not restated
  here.
