# A-Rig (Alabama Rig)

The **A-Rig** — a freshwater-bass-style Alabama rig carrying several small
swimbait heads off wire arms on one main line, presenting as a small bait
ball rather than a single lure — crossed over into SoCal inshore/kelp bass
and yellowtail fishing (Tackle Express, `SclIN_ceduA`, 2026-07-10;
unregistered channel, single mention — medium confidence). This is a distinct
rig platform from the single Kicker swimbaits (booty spin, PK3/PK5) already
covered in [soft-plastic swimbaits](soft-plastic-swimbaits.md); those baits
can be, but were not in this source, the arms fished on a rig like this one.
This note covers the **Kicker-brand rig and the calico/yellowtail bait
pairing**; the rig-building and fishing mechanics of the same rig class (arm
bending, hook count, knot, line, rod/reel, retrieve) are already documented
for a **Recon Baits-style A-rig fished for bay bass** in [swimbaits —
Umbrella rig setup, bay bass, Roman
Castro](../techniques/swimbaits.md#umbrella-rig-setup-bay-bass-roman-castro);
this source adds a second brand and a different (kelp/open-coast calico and
yellowtail) application rather than new execution detail.

## The rig and the bait pairing

- **Rig:** an Alabama rig made by **Kicker** — the example shown on camera was
  missing a few of its blades from heavy use. Kicker is the same brand
  documented for the booty-spin and PK-series swimbaits in [soft-plastic
  swimbaits](soft-plastic-swimbaits.md).
- **Bait:** small **Z-Man Diesel Minnow** soft plastics, rigged on what the
  presenter estimates as a **1/4 oz lead head**.
- **Stated reason for the small bait:** when small anchovies are the bait
  present in the water, match that size rather than fishing a big profile —
  "you don't want to throw a big old stupid plastic like this when that
  small bait's around... the fish get really keyed in on it." This is a
  size-DOWN call made under a specific stated condition (small anchovy bait
  present); it sits beside, not against, the opposite "bigger bait, fewer
  bites but bigger fish" trophy-grade doctrine in [calico
  bass](../species/calico-bass.md#doctrine--conflicts) — both are
  size-matching rules tied to different stated goals (matching the present
  forage vs. deliberately targeting trophy grade), not a contradiction to
  reconcile.
- **Species produced on this rig/bait pairing, per the presenter:** calico
  bass, yellowtail ("yellows"), and a "sea bass" he says he has "seen... on
  one of these." The sea bass species is not further specified on camera —
  plausibly [white seabass](../species/white-seabass.md) given the SoCal
  inshore/kelp context, but that identification is not confirmed by name, so
  it is not logged as a routed white seabass catch.

## How to fish it

Not otherwise described on camera beyond "a great tool, you catch everything
on these." Treat it as a variant presentation within
[swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md) mechanics — cast and retrieve like a
standard soft-plastic swimbait, with the multi-arm rig doing the bait-ball
presentation work instead of a single trailer. For the actual rig-building
and retrieve mechanics (arm-bending, hook count, knot, line, rod/reel), see
[swimbaits — Umbrella rig setup, bay bass, Roman
Castro](../techniques/swimbaits.md#umbrella-rig-setup-bay-bass-roman-castro) —
built on a different-brand rig (Recon Baits) for a different species (bay
bass) but the same rig class, and the only on-camera "how to fish it" detail
this KB has for an A-rig.

Router: [calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md#doctrine--conflicts).
