# Skip Jigging

Skip jigging is a topwater cast-and-burn presentation: instead of swimming a
lure through the water column, the lure is made to skip and skitter ACROSS
the surface itself. Billy, Fisherman's Landing Tackle Shop, via
SearcherSportfishing ("Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 120 (Surface Skip Lure),"
`OL8D1l73RVg`, 2020-10-08; `searcher-sportfishing` is a registered
channel/series — this is a single-mention topic on it, so **medium**
confidence per the repetition rule) calls it *"a fairly crude technique...
very easy to do"* and names it as a way to draw **tuna, dorado, and
yellowtail** up from underneath to blast a lure they can see splashing on
top.

## Reach for this when

- You want a **loud, splashy topwater presentation** rather than a
  subsurface swim — the visual commotion on top is the draw, not a swimming
  kick.
- [Dorado](../species/dorado.md) or [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md)
  (and tuna — species not specified in this source) are around and you want
  to trigger a reaction strike from something they can see skipping on the
  surface above them.

## Mechanics

- **Gear: just a long rod and a lure** — no other tackle is called out in
  this source.
- Cast out.
- **Point the rod straight up in the air** — the opposite of the tip-down
  position [surface iron](surface-iron.md) calls for on its standard
  swimming retrieve.
- **Turn the handle as fast as you can.** The speed is what keeps the lure
  riding up and skipping across the top of the water instead of swimming
  through it.
- The lure skips/skitters across the surface; tuna, dorado, or yellowtail
  come up from underneath and blast it.
- On the bite, **keep winding as fast as you can** to come tight on the fish.

⚠ **Not reconciled with, and in direct tension with, this KB's standing
surface-iron retrieve doctrine.** [Surface iron](surface-iron.md) documents a
tip-down, steady-to-moderate-grind retrieve as the baseline and flags
reeling too fast as *"the single most common mistake."* This source presents
skip jigging as a **deliberately different, tip-up, flat-out-fast**
presentation — a distinct technique choice, not an error in the surface-iron
retrieve. Kept side by side, attributed, rather than merged into either
note's doctrine.
