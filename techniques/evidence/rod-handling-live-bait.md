## Mechanics

  (How To Hold Your Rod)," 2022-12-08): source for the off-hand grip,
  thumb-on-lever readiness, fingertip line feed, and backpedal-a-stalled-bait
  doctrine. `searcher-sportfishing` is a registered channel/series, but this
  specific mechanism is a single mention there — medium confidence pending

  this") without a narrated hand/arm motion — asr-uncertain, not resolvable
  from audio alone; the note records the stated outcome (bait turns around
  and swims back out), not a precise stroke.

## At the bait tank / Yellowtail-specific hookset sequence

- `SAltQjih0ms` (SearcherSportfishing, Tackle Talk Live, 2020-05-20): source
  for the hook-then-release-immediately bait-tank handling, the 12–18 in
  hang-height carry to the rail, and the full J-hook yellowtail hookset
  sequence (free-spool on the take, count to six or seven, engage to strike,
  wait for the bend, wind-down-and-reset the hookset repeated once or
  twice). `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel — high confidence.
