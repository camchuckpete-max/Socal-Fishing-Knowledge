# Evidence — Bait-and-Switch (Striped Marlin)

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[bait-and-switch](../bait-and-switch.md). One line per observation, grouped
under the parent note's section headings.

## Dropback bait execution

- `3qSY328fFYo` (StokedOnFishing, "Marlin Fishing with Jimmy Decker,"
  2014-11-08, Catalina tanker lanes/277 bank): Capt. Jimmy Decker guided the
  trip and narrated the dropback method live on camera; unregistered channel —
  medium confidence. On camera the play was described as reactive, not
  scripted — "you think you have a plan but then as soon as one of the riggers
  goes off... it all goes out the window." The trip produced a striped marlin
  (~160 lb estimate by the crew) landed and released by angler Jimmy Decker,
  hooked in the corner of the mouth.

## Gear class

- `Mwx5AAXNMvE` (StokedOnFishing, "Stoked On Cabo San Lucas, Part 2,"
  2014-12-03 upload, offshore Cabo San Lucas, Hurricane Fishing charter;
  continuation of the same trip as the Part 1 dropback data point in
  [striped marlin](../../species/evidence/striped-marlin.md); unregistered
  channel — medium confidence): the trip's closing tips segment named the
  drop-back as "definitely the best bet" for hookups, contrasted same trip
  with two blue marlin strikes on trolled lures that didn't stay pinned
  (logged in [trolling — Cabo San Lucas lure
  entry](../trolling.md#cabo-san-lucas--offshore-marlin-lure-size-selection-and-afternoon-chop-rigging)).
  Rig named on camera: 80 lb "Soft Steel Ultra" monofilament (sponsor-named
  line; the same "Soft Steel" branding is heard elsewhere in this corpus for
  the same sponsor, e.g. [yellowfin tuna](../../species/yellowfin-tuna.md))
  tied to a #9 circle hook.

## Common failures

- `3qSY328fFYo`: same Catalina trip — a second angler, Roger, shook a fish off
  ("shook one off at 14:04") on a trolled flying-fish teaser lure, a same-day
  illustration that a hooked marlin can still throw the hook.
