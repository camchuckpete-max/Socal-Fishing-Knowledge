# Evidence — Wahoo Trolling — Speed, Leader, and Fight Mechanics

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[wahoo trolling](../wahoo-trolling.md). One line per observation, grouped
under the parent note's section headings.

## Mechanics

- `4uNPLknRAQg` (Searcher Sportfishing, "Tackle Talk Live Recap (Wahoo
  5.5.20)," 2020-05-06, aboard Searcher): a crew member ("Art from the upper
  deck") yells at anglers who pump the rod while fighting a hooked wahoo —
  the no-pump discipline enforced live on deck, not just stated as advice.

## Lure choice

- `KrIednxCEKI` (SearcherSportfishing, upload 2010-02-12, aboard Searcher,
  location not stated): a Dorado-colored Yo-Zuri Marauder trolling lure was
  "just getting killed" by wahoo that trip per angler Joe interviewed
  dockside — he landed four wahoo (plus tuna and yellowtail on the same trip,
  not tied to this lure) and the lure came back chewed up enough that he
  retired it unrepainted rather than fish it again. No reason was stated for
  the color choice — outcome only, not doctrine.
