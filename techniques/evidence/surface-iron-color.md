# Evidence — Surface Iron — Colour

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing [surface iron — colour](../surface-iron-color.md). Every entry below is a single-angler or single-trip colour report, logged and not generalized into the parent note's doctrine — the parent's own "confidence is a real input" claim predicts exactly this scatter of personal picks. One line per observation, grouped under the parent's section headings. Observations never change the parent's stated doctrine.

## The doctrine

- `WE643Fue1_A` (Ray Sharifi, Dirty Hookers, 2022-09, Cedros Island, Baja): stated confidence colour is **mint** — most of his easy-to-grab, most-trusted irons (including his top Tady 45s) run mint, and his father's easy-grab side runs holographic mint; framed as fishing whatever colour you trust mattering as much as the colour itself. `ray-sharifi` registered, Baja-scoped — single mention on colour, medium.
- `_C8w6zeVPak` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-01-20, Cedros Island, Baja): colours called out as producing best that trip were any mint pattern and blue and white. Unregistered channel — medium.
- `ILBl12Jm7-0` (StokedOnFishing, October 2024 trip, 2025-03-15 upload, Cedros Island, Baja, "Epic Yellowtail Fishing — This Season Is on Another Level"): one angler switched to a "minty brownish," chartreuse-leaning colour on a Tady 45 (captioned "Tedy 45"/"T 45" — known Tady→"Tatty"/"teddy" corruption) as a change-up; no bite result on the new colour shown on camera. Unregistered channel — medium.
- `_C7TJoGT0nw` (Tackle Express, "What You ACTUALLY Need for Cedros Fishing Trips | The Gear Vault," 2025-09-26, Cedros Island, Baja, lighter 19–33 lb trip): stated go-to colours were mints, greens, blues, scrambled egg; a mint swirl surface iron was called out as outproducing a Salas 7X in mint swirl the same trip, no mechanism given. Unregistered channel — medium.
- `pUwVpvjJ23w` (Tackle Express, "Think lure color doesn't matter? Think again for your next Baja trip," 2026-05-15): 35-second short cut from the same interview as `_C7TJoGT0nw`, carries the identical mint-swirl-vs-Salas-7X claim verbatim; too short to clear the pre-filter, not separately extracted — `duplicate-of: _C7TJoGT0nw`.
- `KLoEJInlmZo` (Dave Hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, "How to Catch Yellowtail | Surface Iron SoCal Style," 2021-03-23): identifies his go-to Wounded Warrior pattern as a Tady 45 and calls it his one-lure pick across yellowtail, calico bass, white seabass, and yellowfin tuna. `dave-hansen` registered — high confidence source, but a personal go-to colour pick, not generalized.
- `3dVc-2rsYII` (Dave Hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-03-25): later video, same Wounded Warrior pattern named as his calico bass go-to, no brand/model given beyond a light aluminum-alloy build; corroborates `KLoEJInlmZo` rather than adding a new colour.
- `IwxqgocsQTY` (StokedOnFishing, "Intrepid Style 8 Day, part 1," 2015-10-29 upload, day one of an 8-day charter aboard the Intrepid, SoCal or a transit zone en route south): first thing in the morning at a boiler rock, one angler opened on a surface iron in the "scrambled egg pattern"; no bite result tied to that colour confirmed on camera. Unregistered, sponsor-heavy episode — medium.
- `ilINTeknKB4` (StokedOnFishing, "Intrepid Style 8 Day, part 3," 2015-10-29 upload, San Benito Island, Baja, same charter as `IwxqgocsQTY`): closing tips summary named mint the best colour for yellowtail sitting up in the water column on surface iron, and scrambled egg the best producer for fish sitting down deep — the deep presentation is not confirmed on camera as surface iron rather than a yo-yo iron worked in the same colour (see [yo-yo iron](../yo-yo-iron.md#gear-class-detail)). Unregistered channel — medium.
- `VpW91AKOFVQ` (Scotty, Brothers Sportfishing, via Roman Castro, 2020-01-28, La Jolla): a red crab pattern iron produced 2 of the day's 3 boated yellowtail, but the captain's own read is that the angler mattered more than the jig colour that trip. Registered voice on other claims (see [evidence — surface iron](surface-iron.md)); single mention on colour — medium.
- `V_ONnegk95M` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-05-04, San Clemente Island, SoCal): yellowtail called biting a green-and-yellow ("mac color") surface iron repeatedly late in the session. Unregistered channel — medium.
- `I-QBxuV2p7M` (StokedOnFishing, 2021-10 trip per on-camera reference, 2022-06-15 upload, Cedros Island, Baja, "Stoked to Be Back in Cedros Island Calico Bass Fishing," day two of the same trip as the [yellowtail](../../species/yellowtail.md) `QSmE3mdEL28` entry): during a wide-open calico bass bite over reef structure, two anglers ran different colours side by side — one white, the other blue/greenish — both produced. Unregistered channel — medium.
- `XyKF34C5iB4` (Matt, Time On The Water panel, 2025-04-19): daytime SoCal calico bass picks named on camera — brown, orange, and sometimes mint. Unregistered channel — medium.
- `1uYIApdQQSM` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 103," 2020-06-11): names two "standard-bearer" surface-iron colours said to have been on the scene roughly 25–30 years as of this upload (since roughly 1990–1995) — a Tady 45 in blue/green/white ("mackerel" pattern) and a Salas 7X in sardine green/white; no reasoning given beyond track record. From an independent channel, this duration claim corroborates the early-1990s (~1993) dating of the Tady 45 / Salas 7X pairing as the surface-iron standard given in [surface iron — size / weight selection](../surface-iron.md#size--weight-selection). `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel/series, but this is product/model naming — caps low per the channel's sponsored-claim caveat, regardless of repetition.

Product/model naming — caps low per the channel's sponsored-claim caveat.
- `lf3S28nh-kk` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Talk Live with Team Searcher," 2021-03-26): for yellowtail boiling on a kelp paddy, a Tady 4/0 in mint is named "really really popular and really really effective," fished on 30 or 40 lb test with a slow retrieve; a Tady 45, in the same mint or in sardine colour, named as the rigged backup for the same stop. Full session outfit context in [Searcher spring bluefin/yellowtail quiver](../../tackle/searcher-spring-bluefin-yellowtail-quiver.md#yellowtail-on-a-kelp-paddy-stop--three-methods-one-stop). `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel/series, single mention of this claim; the named colour/model portion caps low per the channel's sponsored-claim caveat.
