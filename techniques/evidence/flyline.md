# Evidence — Flyline

Trip reports and per-source provenance for [flyline](../flyline.md).

## Hook position by situation

- `ROWgdFE9Ehc` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-10-28, "the channel," SoCal — exact
  location not stated): nose-hooked sardine, slow-trolled 150–200 ft behind
  the boat, produced yellowtail on a kids' charity trip; a mix of circle and
  wide-gap hooks was run. Unregistered channel — medium confidence.
- `HMdrP4-i9MM` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-10-11, off North County San Diego near
  Camp Pendleton — kids/family Dana Wharf Sportfishing charter, Dana Point):
  crew rigged kids' sardines nose-hooked or lower toward the tail (called a
  "buzz hook" on camera, asr-uncertain whether a distinct placement or a
  mishearing of belly/butt hook); a straight (unweighted) flylined sardine
  produced a kid's fish that session. Unregistered channel — medium
  confidence.
- `M7BtON4GZgQ` (StokedOnFishing, "Bluefin Fishing on the El Dorado," trip
  dated August 2020, uploaded 2021-02-03, west end of Catalina Island —
  85 ft El Dorado, Long Beach Sportfishing): a butt-hooked flylined sardine
  was the "hot ticket" bait that morning, worked on roughly a 2/0–3/0 hook
  and 25–30 lb leader; the pre-dawn briefing also stressed checking the drag
  before fishing. Unregistered channel — medium confidence.

## Bait, hook, and leader selection

- `LsFMBCa9DOQ` (StokedOnFishing, "Three Days of Epic Bluefin Fishing on the
  Ranger 85," Cortez/Tanner Bank, mid-August 2024): a crew member reinforced
  the bait-freshness rule for a finicky bluefin bite — anglers who change
  baits often catch more fish, wanting a lively bait on at all times.
  Unregistered channel — medium confidence.
- `V_ONnegk95M` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-05-04, San Clemente Island —
  pre-trip captain briefing, day-and-a-half El Dorado charter): stated
  tackle for the sardine flyline bite was 30 lb test to about a 3/0 hook,
  rigged at the bait barge as a circle hook on 30 lb fluorocarbon; the
  captain stressed the drag must be set and called bait selection (scales
  intact, good color, slime) the deciding factor. Unregistered channel —
  medium confidence.
- `nsUdT-zXI8s` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-12-07 upload, front side of San
  Clemente Island — veterans-charity overnight trip aboard the El Dorado):
  the day's sardines were sized for roughly a 2/0–3/0 hook, fished on 30 lb
  test as the crew's standard, with some anglers going heavier or lighter by
  preference. Unregistered channel — medium confidence.
- `zQtExV8Z2eY` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-10-21 upload, San Clemente Island,
  "Thunderbird Yellowtail," aboard the Thunderbird): stated terminal tackle
  was 30 lb mono main line to a 4 ft top shot of 30 lb fluorocarbon; some
  yellowtail came on a flylined sardine worked away from the boat, no hook
  size given on camera. The same leader was also fished that morning with a
  sliding-sinker live-squid rig — see [sliding
  sinker](../sliding-sinker.md#san-clemente-island-yellowtail-14-oz-slider--squid-stokedonfishing).
  Unregistered channel — medium confidence.
- `_PGm-TlFU2A` (StokedOnFishing, Summer 2015 footage / 2015-11-17 upload,
  Newport Beach, "Thunderbird Tuna Time," overnight charter aboard the
  Thunderbird out of Davey's Locker): crew got bit on anchovy (3–4 in),
  fly-lined on No. 4 to No. 2 hooks, with 25–30 lb fluorocarbon leader called
  "a must have these days"; a small split shot with a rubber core was
  mentioned as an optional add. Later the same session, spinning anglers ran
  25 lb fluorocarbon on 40 lb backing, and a separate angler called 30 lb
  fluorocarbon "smart" after a prior trip's 15 lb leader failed. Unregistered
  channel — medium confidence.
- `fcdWAo0VAKw` (SearcherSportfishing, 2015-08-02, San Diego, "Wayne Burnette
  fights a yellowtail," ~1-minute interview clip): angler Wayne Burnette
  landed a yellowtail on a flylined "small sliding shaker" bait on 40 lb
  test Izorline to a hook captioned "2030" (asr-uncertain, possibly 2/0).
  Registered channel, single mention (angler interview, not crew doctrine) —
  medium confidence.
- `48ZFXnCTTQE` (Dirty Hookers, ~2019–2020 per "three years ago" against the
  2023-11-01 upload date, Guadalupe Island): a finicky bite that produced
  fish in the morning on 50–60 lb test went dead by mid-morning; downsizing
  to 30 lb test got a bite within two minutes and landed a 130 lb fish in

## Fishing it

  bites before the bait balls sank out and scattered the fish; bait, hook,
  and leader detail not captured on camera. Unregistered channel — medium
  confidence.
- `Rb5I2ljAqeE` (StokedOnFishing, "Top Gun 80 Epic 5 Day, part 1,"
  2017-05-05 upload, West End tuna zone off San Clemente Island, anchored at

  flylined leftover dead squid still produced repeat bluefin bites; with
  current running against the wind, the crew added an unusually light 1/2 oz
  weight to keep the bait swinging naturally. The same session also worked
  flylined baits suspended under a balloon — grade/catch detail in [bluefin
  tuna](../../species/bluefin-tuna.md#where--when). Unregistered channel —
  medium confidence.
- `Ow3an9lSVh4` (StokedOnFishing, "Stoked on Simrad, part 3," 2014-11-03, off
  Pyramid Head, San Clemente Island; sponsor-heavy Simrad/Avet/Soft Steel
  episode): on a second day of yellowfin fishing where fish were a better
  grade and wanted bait deeper than the day before, the crew added 1/2–3/4 oz
  of rubber-core/sliding sinker to flylined sardines, which were also
  butt-hooked to swim down deep. Unregistered channel — medium confidence.
- `mL4Ph7t0WcQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-12-16, East Cape, Baja California Sur

  the local panga crew the previous night.
- `84XPJAeH0Rw` (StokedOnFishing, "Non-Stop Tuna Action in Baja Mexico, part

- `GptrotE0x5M` (StokedOnFishing, presenter Shay, "Non-Stop Tuna, Dorado, And

## Baja mangrove structure-casting (Mag Bay)

- `FxgLol_IHa0` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-02-22, Lopez Mateos, Magdalena
  Bay, 2 days before `-5kooyIyavs` in the same trip with the same crew): with
  the anchor lay read correctly off the tide, the first bait cast into the
  zone the moment the boat settled was eaten instantly, no soak required — a
  ~5 lb snook, called "perfect eating size" on camera. Bait was Monterey
  sardine, cast/net-caught en route. Unregistered channel — medium
  confidence.
- `frX09YMQxKE` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-01-28, Lopez Mateos, Magdalena
  Bay): the presenter free-dove the spot before fishing, then flylined bait
  into one specific mangrove-edge slot identified from that look; casts
  landing in the slot drew fast, repeated bites — a small grouper was
  landed, and small pargo plus a larger snook were seen holding in the same
  slot. Local guide Reuben ran the trip. Unregistered channel — medium
  confidence.
- `vyX5FGoDH0A` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-01-26, Lopez Mateos, Magdalena
  Bay, predates `frX09YMQxKE` but postdates `dlxA22FVNGc`): a wide-open snook
  bite on sardina flyline, multiple snook landed in quick succession; one
  fish first called "grouper" on camera was corrected to spotted bay bass.
  One fish ran the bait into shoreline brush and cost the crew a hook; by
  partway through the session the crew was down to their "last box" of
  hooks. Unregistered channel — medium confidence.
- `dlxA22FVNGc` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-01-23, Lopez Mateos, Magdalena
  Bay, the earliest-dated clip in this series): anchored and chumming live
  sardines produced a fast multi-species bite along the mangrove root edge
  and a dead-tree cove mouth — snook, a small broomtail grouper, and jack
  (released) all taken on the same pattern. Anchoring over drifting was
  stressed as crucial to keep presenting bait to the zone continuously
  rather than re-approaching after each drift. Guide Reuben ran the anchor
  and chum; the presenter broke off on the roots mid-session and re-tied.
  Tide was falling from a super-high mark through the session. Unregistered
  channel — medium confidence.

## La Paz / La Ventana open-water high spots

- `qM7iOO7fOBw` (StokedOnFishing, "The Ultimate Baja Mexico Fishing Charter:
  Stoked on La Paz," 2023-11-03 upload, La Paz, Baja California Sur, Baja
  Rancheros fleet, day 2 of a two-day trip — day 1, logged separately as
  `ORC1A68cEeM`, was windy with strong current and slow fishing at a
  different spot): the fleet worked one crowded shared high spot,
  flylining live mackerel; the session produced a mixed bag — multiple
  yellowtail, several cabrilla, at least one dorado, and at least one large
  pargo. A yellowtail/jack school was spotted by bait "shattering" on the
  surface. The crew deliberately fished outside/away from the rest of the
  fleet rather than anchoring in the pack; the bite reportedly went from
  nothing to an instant hookup once fish moved through. Two anglers compared
  line setups on camera (asr-uncertain on both): roughly 65 lb braid to
  ~60 lb mono with a 40 lb top shot, versus 40 lb to 40 lb fluorocarbon. One
  angler fished a cast hardbait (a "Colt Sniper" stickbait, brand not stated
  on camera) rather than a flylined bait, alongside the boat's flyline
  program. A separate fish was fought on an extra-heavy rod paired with a
  reel whose name was not confidently resolved from captions (not carried
  per the ASR-hazard rule). Unregistered channel — medium confidence.
- `1nK7vSPl2sg` (StokedOnFishing, presenter Shay McKinty, captain Andrew
  Pereira, "Giant Amberjack, Possible La Ventana Record," 2019-10-25, La
  Ventana, Baja California Sur, six-pack charter aboard a 35 ft Everglades):
  a local captain directed the boat to a named high spot rising from roughly
  200 ft to 80 ft; a bait swam freely about seven seconds before getting bit
  on a 30 lb fluorocarbon top shot to a hook read as a 3/0 circle hook
  (asr-uncertain), 30 lb line on an Okuma reel. The bite produced a large
  fish the crew called an amberjack, framed on camera as a possible La
  Ventana record — a claim from the video only, not independently verified.
  Unregistered channel, batch-2 registry caps Shay McKinty at medium —
  medium confidence.

  same trip as the night-jigging tackle logged in [knife
  jigging](../knife-jigging.md)): the day's bluefin bit strictly on flyline
  — the boat tried a sinker rig and the fish would not take it. Stated
  tackle: 25–40 lb test to a straight No. 2 hook, sardine bait, fluorocarbon

  No. 4) circle hook with a ~90% corner-of-the-mouth hookup rate that trip,

  80–100 ft down and not coming up to the chum line. Unregistered channel —
  medium confidence.
