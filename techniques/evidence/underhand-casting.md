  the underhand lob, and the pull-slack tip for a light bait (anchovy on a
  small rubber-core sinker). Single mention of this specific mechanism —
  medium confidence.
- `7iSGGb9ueAk` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 98 (Underhand
  Cast)," 2020-05-07): an earlier, shorter tip covering the same cast in more
  general terms, not tied to a specific bait weight — corroborates rather
  than adds a distinct claim.

## Underhand vs. overhand casting

- `QHY5kmU7OTU` (Steve Carson, Penn, SearcherSportfishing, Tackle Talk Live,
  "5 Outfits: Don't Leave the Dock Without Them!," 2021-01-28): Carson's own
  stated preference for underhand over overhand when casting live bait, and
  his reasoning (gentle landing over distance). `searcher-sportfishing` is a
  registered channel/series and Carson a recurring named voice on it.

## Soft hook placements need the gentler swing

- `SAltQjih0ms` (SearcherSportfishing, Tackle Talk Live Recap (Yellowtail
  5/19/20), 2020-05-20): source for the shoulder-hook/belly-hook soft-spot
  doctrine and the underhand-only rule for those placements.
  `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel — high confidence.
