## Mechanics

- `Ul5FLB2dFgQ`: primary source for the bow-forward/walk-back boat
  positioning method, the coordinated stroke (rod lift + full handle turn),
  the slack-on-the-drop mechanic, the night "blinking" slow-worked drop with
  a flip-slider jig, and (see Hookset/Fighting/Gear class below) most of
  this note's baseline mechanics, hookset, and terminal-rig doctrine. No
  channel/date is given on camera in this note's source clips; treated as
  the note's primary tackle-shop-seminar voice.
- `UfuiWFVvz2E` (StokedOnFishing, video uploaded 2026-08-11, Cortez Bank,
  SoCal; Constitution charter captain's pre-trip briefing; unregistered
  channel — medium confidence): the cast-down-swell stern positioning method
  (jig settles ~200–250 ft under the boat); angler-participation-as-mechanism
  observation (jig spread reading as a moving bait school, emphasized most
  at night); also sources the 300 g night jig-weight floor (250 g for
  "savvy" anglers only) and an 80 lb braid/130 lb mono-or-fluoro line floor
  logged under Depth control and Gear class below.
- `G1YIlakiRqg` (Gavin, Pacific Queen, SD Fish and Sips seminar, uploaded
  2024-03-13; unregistered channel/speaker — medium confidence): the
  cast-downwind/never-midship positioning rule and its conditions-dependent
  cast-distance guidance; the ~80%-bites-on-the-fall figure; rod-holding
  mechanics (armpit/spool fingers/thumb-on-lever) with the 5–6 second vs.
  ~half-second reaction-time comparison; the free-spool-and-redrop-on-a-miss
  rule (~8/10 rebite rate, 15–60 lb working grade).
- `M8hOYQ_6rSg`: pump-up/wind-down phrasing and the faster-than-slow-pitch
  pace (Cesar, via Dirty Hookers, 2025-05-20; registered voice, single
  mention — medium confidence). The yellowtail bump-without-hookup response
  moved with the species program to [yellowtail — knife / speed jigging
  evidence](../../species/evidence/yellowtail-knife-jigging.md).
- `Et2TUskzCSs`: the daytime-warmth-can-override-species-speed rule ("as
  slow as you want, don't stop") — also tied by the source to Gulf of Mexico
  yellowfin as an out-of-region, general-mechanism-only corroboration, not
  carried as a SoCal claim.
- `gimFbgN5Jjk`: the corroborating full-day San Diego trip (9–11 bluefin
  landed once the crew matched a fast, darting retrieve).

## Fighting

- `Et2TUskzCSs`: the mono-vs-fluoro leader/fighting-style conflict (James
  Kikkawa's mono-for-stretch build vs. George's fluoro-plus-progressive-drag
  build, the latter explicitly framed on camera as personal opinion from
  Spain-based bluefin experience, not a correction of the SoCal mono
  doctrine); the rod-tip-at-the-face and circle-cutting technique
  (attributed to James Kikkawa on the same panel, with Benny Ortiz
  contributing the ring/swivel jig-flutter reasoning logged under Gear
  class).
- `gimFbgN5Jjk` (James Kikkawa & Brian Wen, 2025 PCS Show panel): the
  quantified drag-loss mechanism ("well over" 5 lb off a 25 lb setting from
  rod bend + line stretch), the nose-first broadside-turn mechanism, the
  stated reason to avoid two-speed low gear in this fighting style, and the
  stuck-bottom-rockfish corroborating example — a same-source restatement
  and quantification of the `Et2TUskzCSs` doctrine roughly a year later.
