## Improvising when the bite goes dead

- `RUuPNpK0x-s` (n6yun panel, 2013-03-09): the specific example offered for
  the "almost anything goes" rule — one panelist's client, named on camera as
  "Dr. Chris Medic," described as running unusual, self-tied "weird"
  combinations (including a reverse drop shot) who reportedly out-fished the
  panelist's own boat on slow days; read on camera as a reason to pay
  attention to what an observant angler on the boat is doing differently,
  even a client rather than the guide. The retrieve-speed tell is illustrated
  with two on-camera anecdotes: a fish tearing the rod from a first-timer's
  hand while paused mid-retrieve to take a bite of a hot dog, and the same
  happening on a fast, distracted retrieve while the angler answered a phone
  call.
