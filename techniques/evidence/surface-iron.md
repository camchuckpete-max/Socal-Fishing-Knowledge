# Evidence — Surface Iron

## Retrieve & cadence

The serpentine/kick-out mechanism, the no-species-specific-retrieve doctrine, and the mono/setup material under Gear class are **Dan Wade** (Strictly Irons guest / Fishing Syndicate) — 60+ years on the iron, 68 lb personal best on the surface iron at Guadalupe on 40 lb. `dan-wade` is a registered voice ([source registry](../../sources/source-registry.md), promoted by Cameron 2026-08-17), and the mechanism is stated in both videos — high.

- `Ec2GaNpiOHI` (Strictly Irons, "Q & A with Dan Wade," 2019-03-19): serpentine/"z pattern" kick mechanism; the bait-ball inside-outside explanation and "goes around wrong"; "perky-jerky… you gotta make them bite it"; "no normal retrieve — when the lure swims right, fish bite it"; "calico bass can chase down the lure faster than you can [reel]."
- `yn1suHH6b1Y` (Fishing Syndicate, "Jig Fishing Masterclass with Danny Wade & Oliver Solis," Day at the Docks, 2026-05-13): the same serpentine mechanism seven years on, with the quoted "every third or fourth time it's going to kick out to the side… That kick-out is what triggers a bite" wording, and its own bait-ball version ("the baits that miss are the ones that get eaten").
- `3dVc-2rsYII` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, "How to Cast Surface Iron Jigs," 2022-03-25): `dave-hansen` registered voice — high; wind the instant the jig lands, slow-to-medium crank for calico, tip-down-in-the-water retrieve, match crank speed to gear ratio (4:1–5:1 period conventionals — Newell, Penn Senator — do the slow-down for you), reeling too fast called the single most common mistake, wind-through hookset with the crank-until-line-peels tell.
- `KLoEJInlmZo` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, "How to Catch Yellowtail | Surface Iron SoCal Style," 2021-03-23): registered voice — high; earlier occurrence of the wind-through doctrine with the mechanism spelled out — roughly 9 of 10 strikes (his estimate) are the fish grabbing the iron amidships and shaking it ("dog boning") before turning.
- `VpW91AKOFVQ` (Scotty, Brothers Sportfishing, via Roman Castro, 2020-01-28, La Jolla): `capt-scotty-brothers` registered voice — high; tip-down-so-it-swims doctrine, boat-side/kayak-side eats from trailing fish, and the Coronado-big-school-vs-mainland-small-school (Del Mar to Imperial Beach) finickiness scaling.

Six on-the-water Strictly Irons sessions (2020–2026) below are narrated by **Jared Saaib** — the channel's own voice, distinct from the Dan Wade guest tutorials. `jared-saaib` is a registered voice ([source registry](../../sources/source-registry.md), promoted by Cameron 2026-08-17), under his standing rule that Strictly Irons content is Jared unless a guest is clearly identified on camera, as Wade is. Ratings turn on repetition, not registry status — a point stated across two or more sessions is high, a single session medium; the jig and rod models in several sessions are product launches and sit at low. These are stated reasoning, not observations, so they are recorded as attributed doctrine in the parent.

- `ejUTAnONf7o` (Strictly Irons, 2026-04-21, Coronado Islands): coaching a first-timer through a live cast — "You want that surface lure to be gliding on the surface… keep going a little faster. Get it up there where you can see it. You can even lift up your rod tip… Now keep it right there — now it's nice and slow" — the slowest-wind-that-keeps-it-visible speed rule; also the swipe-and-drop-off finicky signature ("they weren't pushing through and swallowing it") and the crew's wind-turned-the-bite conclusion; single session — medium (the finicky-signature pair rates high across two sessions).
- `u80AI37soFI` (Strictly Irons, 2020-06-27, Coronado Islands): a fish blew up on the jig and missed; "keep winding, keep on" — it came back and ate; single session — medium.
- `0DKmYId1NN4` (Strictly Irons, 2026-04-16 upload, session 2026-03-21 per on-camera date): burn-and-return change-up with fish showing; the "grabbing one and going back down" finicky signature; single session — medium (signature pair high).
- `_MygUHw19q8` (Strictly Irons, big-yellowtail session dated 2024-06-08, 2025-03-14 upload, Cedros Island): "I sped it up to go for another cast" — bit on the pick-up acceleration; single session — medium.

- `NGxyOlPx3ug` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-01-20, Cedros/Gono Islands, Baja, "Shogun Skiff Trip part 1"): anchored on a kelp bed off the mother ship (breakers close by on one side), anglers bombed the surface iron as far as they could and let it fall right after the cast rather than starting the swim, getting bit on the fall; one fish taken this way stayed down and would not come up, the angler crediting the landing to running Spectra (braid); a different fall-bite context (initial cast, at anchor over kelp) from the mid-retrieve sink-out — kept side by side, not merged; unregistered channel — medium.
