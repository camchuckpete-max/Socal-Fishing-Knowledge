# Evidence — Surface Iron — The Cast

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing [surface iron — the
cast](../surface-iron-casting.md). One line per observation, grouped under
the parent note's section headings, in its order. Observations never change
the parent's stated doctrine; a contradicting one is also traced beside that
doctrine in the parent.

## Mechanics

- `eL1Qm33-Mj0` (StokedOnFishing, uploaded 2025-03-31, Cortez Bank, SoCal,
  aboard the El Dorado out of Long Beach): mid-trip, anglers were coached to
  watch the **direction** they cast, not just look behind them — don't cast
  toward or on top of a rail-mate (or a knot of anglers) already bent on a
  fish, since a new lure landing in that fish's water tangles other lines and
  costs the hooked angler his fish; a distinct crowded-rail hazard from the
  behind-you/safety check, about not fouling someone else's hookup rather
  than hitting a person with the cast; unregistered channel — medium.
- `ejUTAnONf7o` (Jared Saaib, Strictly Irons, 2026-04-21, Coronado Islands):
  coaching a rail-mate mid-trip — "No, no, no. Look at your reel.
  Sideways." — second, independent voice on the reel-on-its-side detail
  first logged from `KLoEJInlmZo` (Dave Hansen), six years earlier, on a
  different channel.

## Aim — the follow-through, not your feet

- `6EDQtQHEwFE` (StokedOnFishing, October 2024 trip per on-camera reference,
  2025-03-07 upload, Cedros Island, Baja, "This Year's Yellowtail Bite Has
  Been the Best Ever"): individually spotted yellowtail — not a full
  boil/school — were sight-cast directly ("spotted him, pointed at him,
  casted on him"), with hookups following within a couple of casts on
  several occasions; birds working overhead were called out as a supporting
  visual sign on the same session, worked "using nothing but the iron the
  entire time"; unregistered channel — medium. See
  [yellowtail](../../species/yellowtail.md) for the rest of this trip's
  detail.
