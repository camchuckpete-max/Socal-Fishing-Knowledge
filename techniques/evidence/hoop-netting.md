## Retrieval

- `Qs9oEsh3b_w` (Roman Castro / Capt. Scotty, 2020-10-17): constant-steady-pull
  retrieval doctrine.
- `Q196GVwVqro` (Justin Botril, B-Line Sport Fishing, Long Beach Harbor, live
  hoop-netting stream recapped at Your Saltwater Guide's 2024 PCS Show
  seminar, 2024-03-07; unregistered speaker/channel — medium confidence): a
  lobster "hovering over the side" of the boat mid-retrieval, already too big
  to pass through the ring of a 36 in hoop net — by the time it was visible,
  the whole net appeared "filled with whiskers." A crew member stopped
  pulling and reached down to grab it by hand instead of continuing the
  pull; the lobster slipped free and was lost over the rail, framed on camera
  as a possible U.S.-record loss. The rule drawn from the telling matches the
  retrieval doctrine above verbatim: "don't ever stop pulling the rope...
  only stop when it's in the boat."
- `cEscIy278ew` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, 2020-11-13): post-lift
  handling (dump/shake, measure immediately, legals to the tank, shorts back
  over the side).
