# Evidence — Sliding Sinker

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing [sliding sinker](../sliding-sinker.md).
One line per observation, grouped by species/location. Observations never
change the parent note's stated doctrine.

## Yellowtail — Cedros / San Benito / SoCal islands

- `d0yGBQDeY_4` (StokedOnFishing, 2019-03-05, Cedros Island, Baja, "Big
  Yellowtail Part 1"; Cedros Outdoor Adventures charter, sponsor-heavy
  episode; unregistered channel — medium confidence): a **1/2 oz** egg
  sliding-sinker (Carolina rig) — called out on camera as "something
  different" from the rest of the boat's spread that morning — landed a
  yellowtail; a separate fish earlier in the same session was also taken on
  an unspecified-weight sliding-sinker rig. No leader length or hook size
  given. See [yellowtail](../../species/yellowtail.md) for the same trip's
  other detail.
- `6EDQtQHEwFE` (StokedOnFishing, October 2024 trip per on-camera reference,
  2025-03-07 upload, Cedros Island, Baja, "This Year's Yellowtail Bite Has
  Been the Best Ever"; unregistered channel — medium confidence): a
  yellowtail called out on camera as caught "on the sinker rig ... the one
  with the weight on it," alongside the same day's mackerel-livebait and
  surface-iron bites — no weight or leader detail given, but consistent with
  the sliding-sinker-produces-Cedros-yellowtail pattern above. See
  [yellowtail](../../species/yellowtail.md) for the rest of this trip's
  detail.
- `ilINTeknKB4` (StokedOnFishing, "Intrepid Style 8 Day, part 3," 2015-10-29
  upload, San Benito Island, Baja, part 3 of the 8-day Intrepid charter also
  logged in [yellowtail](../../species/evidence/yellowtail.md); unregistered
  channel, sponsor-heavy episode — medium confidence): the trip's closing
  tackle-tips recap named a **half-oz sliding sinker with a nose-hooked
  sardine** as the rig that worked when yellowtail were sitting **suspended**
  in the water column, contrasted on the same recap with a mint-color surface
  iron for fish up top and a scrambled-egg-pattern jig for fish down deep —
  see [surface iron](../surface-iron.md#color) for the color half. No leader
  length or hook size given.
- `zQtExV8Z2eY` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-10-21 upload, San Clemente Island,
  SoCal, "Thunderbird Yellowtail," aboard the Thunderbird; unregistered
  channel — medium confidence): the trip-tips recap named a **1/4 oz sliding
  sinker to a No. 2 hook of angler's choice**, baited with live squid, fished
  off a 30 lb mono main line with a 4 ft top shot of 30 lb fluorocarbon, as
  one of two producing rigs that morning. A lighter build than the 1/2 oz
  Cedros/Benitos egg-sinker data points above. See
  [yellowtail](../../species/yellowtail.md) for the flylined-sardine side of
  the same trip and the day's tally.
- `MUpvP-Yl2R0` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-11-23 upload, front side of Catalina
  Island, SoCal, "Catalina Island Fishing," sponsor-heavy Navico/Simrad
  electronics demo episode; unregistered channel — medium confidence):
  pre-bait, the crew called out a **size 4 hook** on a small slider sinker,
  weighted at roughly **1/4 oz**, as "perfect for squid" — fished on live
  squid at anchor and chum, producing multiple yellowtail. A lighter hook
  than the No. 2 named at San Clemente Island above, though both share the
  same 1/4 oz slider weight. See [yellowtail](../../species/yellowtail.md)
  for the full session.

## Bluefin — nose-hooked, worked on the retrieve

- `IwxqgocsQTY` (StokedOnFishing, "Intrepid Style 8 Day, part 1," 2015-10-29
  upload, day one of the 8-day Intrepid charter, Captain Kevin Osborne,
  fished at anchor on a kelp paddy close to home, SoCal; unregistered
  channel, sponsor-heavy episode — medium confidence): with bluefin marked
  **80 to 150 ft** down, a sardine nose-hooked and fished on a **2 oz
  sinker**, **40 lb** line with a fluorocarbon top shot, drew a strike **on
  the retrieve** rather than the drop or the soak — the angler described
  winding the bait back in "like you're slow trolling it back in." A heavier
  build than the **1/2 oz** trip-tips build logged for this species in
  [rubber-band deep
  rig](../../rigging/rubber-band-deep-rig.md#a-lighter-sliding-sinker-variant--trip-tips-segment-stokedonfishing).
  See [bluefin tuna](../../species/bluefin-tuna.md#where--when) for the same
  session's butt-hooked-bait alternative and grade.

## Albacore — light torpedo-sinker slide, ~37 mi off San Diego

- `w37pHf0xjrw` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-03-19, ~37 mi off San Diego, SoCal,
  "Voyager Albacore Fishing," aboard the Voyager; unregistered channel —
  medium confidence): while prepping bait for the next day's trip, the crew
  relayed fisherman friends' scoop of a wide-open albacore bite ("50 fish up
  till noon" before slowing on one school) and noted the fleet was expected
  to be crowded the next day given the short run. Two light-line
  presentations shown on camera: (1) dropped back on the jig-rod hookup — a
  **2 oz torpedo sinker** on "a nice leader" to a **No. 3, small hook**, with
  a small ring on the leader/terminal connection said to help "the bait swim
  more like natural"; (2) chum-and-drift on a strong meter mark — the
  smallest sliding sinker they could find to a **size 2 hook**, line let out
  to swim as freely as possible rather than held tight. Bait was straight
  anchovy ("chovy"). No leader test, sinker brand, or hook brand given on
  camera.
