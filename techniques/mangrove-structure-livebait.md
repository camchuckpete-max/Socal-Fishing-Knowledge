# Mangrove Structure Livebait

Mangrove structure livebait is a precision live-bait presentation for fish
holding tight against oyster-crusted mangrove roots in skinny water, down to
about 2 ft over the roots (`EcQK5elTPJE`). It inverts the usual open-water
live-bait problem: instead of building the rig around a natural swim, the
whole build is aimed at giving a hooked fish zero room to move before it
reaches abrasive structure.

## Reach for this when

- Fishing snook (or another structure-holding fish) tight against mangrove
  roots in skinny water (~2 ft, `EcQK5elTPJE`) — see
  [snook](../species/snook.md).
- The structure itself is sharp/abrasive (oyster-crusted roots), so a fish
  is lost the instant it gets even a short run of slack line.

## Presentation

- Cast the live bait to land directly on the mangrove root, not just near
  it. An off-target cast still draws a bite, but on bycatch rather than the
  target species (`EcQK5elTPJE`).
- Fish it in skinny water, down to about 2 ft over the roots (`EcQK5elTPJE`).
- Lock the drag down as tight as the reel allows before the cast. The goal
  isn't landing the fish gently — it's denying it any line at all before it
  can reach the sharp structure and cut the leader (`EcQK5elTPJE`).

## Gear class

Baja Pacific mangrove-snook class (see
[gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md)): ~9 ft rod, star-drag reel with
the drag locked near max, 65 lb braid to an 80 lb fluorocarbon leader, 4/0
thin-wire hook (`EcQK5elTPJE`).

The failure mode in this presentation is abrasion, not fighting power: a
fish that gets even a short run against an oyster-crusted root parts the
leader almost immediately. Heavy fluorocarbon paired with a maxed-out drag
is the fix — there's no finesse version of this presentation in water this
skinny and this sharp (`EcQK5elTPJE`).

## Common failures

- Casting near the structure instead of onto it — still draws bites, but
  from bycatch rather than the target fish (`EcQK5elTPJE`).
- Fishing lighter leader or a looser drag suited to open water — the
  oyster-covered roots cut that off fast (`EcQK5elTPJE`).
