# Mangrove Structure Livebait

**Single-source technique note.** Capt. Dave Hansen (Your Saltwater Guide,
`dave-hansen` registered — `medium` confidence, single mention of this
specific build), Lopez Mateos / Magdalena Bay, Baja California Sur,
`EcQK5elTPJE`, 2024-01-27.

Precision livebait presentation for fish holding tight to oyster-covered
mangrove roots in very skinny water. This inverts the usual open-water
livebait problem: instead of building the rig around a natural swim, the
whole build is aimed at giving a hooked fish **zero room to move** before
it reaches abrasive structure.

## Reach for this when

- Fishing snook (or another structure-holding fish) tight against mangrove
  roots in skinny water (~2 ft) — see [snook](../species/snook.md).
- The structure itself is sharp/abrasive (oyster-crusted roots), so a fish
  is lost the instant it gets even a short run of slack line.

## The build

- **Rod:** ~9 ft.
- **Reel:** star-drag, drag locked down as tight as the reel allows.
- **Line:** 65 lb braid to an 80 lb fluorocarbon leader.
- **Hook:** 4/0 thin-wire.

See [gear classes — Baja Pacific mangrove-snook class](../tackle/gear-classes.md).

## Presentation

- Cast the live bait to land **directly on the mangrove root**, not just
  near it. An off-target cast still draws a bite, but on unwanted or
  incidental fish rather than the target species.
- Fished in skinny water, down to about 2 ft over the roots.
- The locked-down drag isn't about landing the fish gently — it's about
  denying it any line at all before it can reach the sharp structure and
  cut the leader.

## Why heavy fluoro and a locked drag in 2 ft of water

The failure mode here isn't fighting power, it's abrasion: a fish that
gets even a short run against an oyster-crusted root parts the leader
almost immediately. Heavy fluorocarbon paired with a maxed-out drag is the
fix — there's no finesse version of this presentation in water this skinny
and this sharp.

## Common failures

- Casting near the structure instead of onto it — still draws bites, but
  from bycatch rather than the target fish.
- Fishing lighter leader or a looser drag suited to open water — the
  oyster-covered roots cut that off fast.
