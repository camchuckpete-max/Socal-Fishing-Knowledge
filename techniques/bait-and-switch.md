# Bait-and-Switch (Striped Marlin)

**⚠ Flagged stub — method skeleton only; no corpus execution source yet.**
The sighted-fish play for [striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md):
raise the fish on the trolled spread, then feed it a live bait. It is stubbed
per the router rule (a method real in the fishery must not be silent) and
awaits execution-depth sources from the ~400-video batch.

## Reach for this when

- A **tailing / finning marlin is sighted** — the single highest-value marlin
  encounter in SoCal; seconds matter.
- The trolled spread raises ("lights up") a fish that won't eat the lures.

## Method skeleton (unsourced — carried from the initial build)

**Provenance warning:** this skeleton was written into the marlin router at
the initial build without a corpus source (no transcript covers tailing fish
or the pitch, and Cameron's export lists marlin only as an open learning
item). Treat every line below as unverified until a real source lands.

- **Tease:** keep the trolled spread swimming — the raised fish is committed to
  the teaser, not to any hook.
- **Switch:** **pitch a live bait** to the raised fish; the pitched bait is
  what it eats. For a tailer spotted off the spread, **cast the bait ahead of
  the fish's line of travel**.
- **Gear:** a **flyline / live-bait class** rod rigged and ready to pitch the
  instant a fish lights up — the pitch rod sits ready the whole troll; heavy
  leader per [wind-on leader](../rigging/wind-on-leader.md).
