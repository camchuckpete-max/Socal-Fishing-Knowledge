# Holding the Rod for Live Bait — Off-Hand Grip and the Backpedal

(Fred, SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 208 (How To Hold Your
Rod)," `VcJManCizRE`, 2022-12-08. `searcher-sportfishing` is a registered
channel/series per the source registry, but this specific mechanism is a
single mention there, so it stands at `medium` confidence pending
corroboration.)

Baseline rod-handling for soaking a live bait off the rail, aimed at newer
anglers. Stated reason to get it right early: a bad grip habit formed here
"stays with you for a long time," and is hard to break once it sets.

## Reach for this when

- You've got a **live bait out** — flylined, on a
  [dropper loop](dropper-loop.md), or any other rail presentation — and
  you're waiting on the bite.
- You're new to sportboat rail fishing and haven't settled into a grip yet;
  this is the default to build the habit around from the start rather than
  correct later.

## Mechanics

- **Hold the rod in your off hand.** A right-handed angler fishes the rod in
  the **left hand**, not the right — holding it in the casting/dominant hand
  is named as the wrong habit newer anglers commonly fall into.
- **Thumb rests on the lever, ready to engage** — the rod hand's job while
  soaking the bait is staying ready to push into gear the instant you get
  bit, not casting or reeling.
- **Feed line out with your fingertips**, not with a thumb pinned on the
  spool. Thumbing the spool to control bait going out is the habit this
  corrects — see the general free-spool grip (index finger on the spool,
  thumb on the reel's trigger/lever) already logged in
  [rod & reel selection](../tackle/rod-and-reel-selection.md).
- **Backpedal a bait that isn't swimming well.** Work the rod to walk the
  bait back toward the boat a little rather than leaving it dead in the
  water — described as a way to get the bait to "turn around and go back
  out" on its own, reviving a stalled bait. (The video gestures "like this"
  without narrating the hand/arm motion in words, so the exact stroke isn't
  resolvable from audio alone — recorded here as the stated outcome, not a
  precise motion.)
- **On the bite:** with the reel already in gear (thumb having pushed the
  lever at the take), come tight to the fish.
