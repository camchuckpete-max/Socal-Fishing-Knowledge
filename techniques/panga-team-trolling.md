# Panga Team Trolling — Follow-Up Cast on a Troll Hookup

A 3-angler panga program for yellowtail: the moment one of two trolled rods
gets bit, the crew's other anglers work the same water instead of only
fighting the hooked fish. Yellowtail school, so a strike on a trolled line
means more fish are almost certainly still around the boat (`jo8lMdFZ1bk`).
⚠ Fact-check (single-source): the 3-angler/2-trolled-rod configuration is
this note's entire premise and rests on one 39-second clip alone.
No location is named on camera, so this applies to Baja panga fishing
generally (panga being a Baja/Mexico open-boat type) rather than a specific
bank or island.

## Mechanics — role split on the hookup

- **The angler on the hooked rod** fights the fish.
- **A second angler grabs the other trolled rod and winds it in** — that rod
  normally gets bit too as it comes tight, so it is worked as a second
  hookup opportunity rather than left to trail passively behind the boat
  (`jo8lMdFZ1bk`).
- **The third angler, with no rod already committed, immediately casts a
  lure into the same water** — a stickbait (named on camera as a
  ["Colt Sniper"-class bait](../lures/tuna-poppers-and-stickbaits.md#colt-sniper-stickbait--jerkbait-yellowtail--a-different-product-from-the-colt-sniper-casting-jig-above))
  or a [surface iron](surface-iron.md) — rather than waiting for the fight to
  finish. The cast is high-percentage: "your chances of getting bit are
  pretty high" — the school is still there and already keyed up by the
  commotion (`jo8lMdFZ1bk`). ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the
  auto-generated transcript renders this line "and your chances of getting
  better pretty high" — not the "getting bit" wording quoted here.

## Follow-up jig — the "stupid tube"

A second speaker on the same clip names a standing go-to for this same
moment — casting right behind any hookup, not only a troll strike — a
**1.5–2 oz** jig nicknamed "the stupid tube." No model or brand name is
given on camera (nothing else survived the auto-captions clearly enough to
carry), so this is logged as the described weight class, not a named
product. The doctrine is simplicity: "as long as they're in the water, you
have a good shot at catching a fish" (`jo8lMdFZ1bk`). ⚠ Fact-check
(single-source): the 1.5–2 oz weight class rests on this one clip alone.

## Gear class

[Jig-stick class](../tackle/gear-classes.md#rod--reel-classes) covers both
roles: the two trolled rods (the same class doubles as a troll outfit — see
[jig stick as trolling outfit](../tackle/tackle-express-jig-stick-trolling-outfit.md))
and the third angler's follow-up cast, where the same rod throws the
1.5–2 oz "stupid tube" jig, a Colt Sniper-class stickbait, or a
[surface iron](surface-iron.md) (`jo8lMdFZ1bk`).
