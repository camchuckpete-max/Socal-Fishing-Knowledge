# Spinnerbaits (Bay Bass)

A freshwater-bass-style spinnerbait crossed over onto SoCal harbor/bay
structure for [spotted bay bass](../species/spotted-bay-bass.md) and the
mixed bag sharing the grass with them. It fills a different lane than the
finesse [bay bass plastics](../lures/bay-bass-plastics.md) program and
[inshore crankbaits](inshore-crankbaits.md): the blade creates **lift** on
the retrieve rather than a crankbait's dig or a plastic's fall, which is why
it gets reached for specifically over eelgrass, where a bait that rides up
out of the grass on its own is the advantage (`XyKF34C5iB4`).

## Reach for this when

- **Shore-pounding or skiff-fishing shallow eelgrass flats in a harbor**
  (demonstrated for Newport Harbor) — a long cast covers water fast, and the
  blade keeps the bait riding up out of the grass instead of fouling in it
  (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- **Windy conditions** — the bait's weight and profile cast well into wind,
  where a lighter finesse plastic struggles (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- **The water is carrying some color rather than running clear** — the read
  that puts this bait and grass ahead of the docks; see [Newport Harbor plan
  — grass vs. docks](#newport-harbor-plan--grass-vs-docks) below.
- **Want a visual, dramatic fight** — the take and fight are dramatic enough
  that it draws a comparison to a [surface iron](surface-iron.md) take: "it
  reminds me of fishing a certain [surface] iron" (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- **A day-long confidence bait for any SoCal bay** — one presenter runs it as
  one of only two lures fished in the harbor day to day, alongside a
  crankbait, "whether in any bait fishing bay, Newport, San Diego Bay"
  (`XyKF34C5iB4`).

## The bait

- **War Baits HD spinner bait, 1 oz** — the model/weight fished for the
  Newport Harbor grass plan. The 1 oz size reads heavy for a harbor that's
  often only **10-12 ft** deep in the grass zones being fished, but the
  weight is deliberate (see [Execution](#execution) below), not an oversight
  (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- A second on-camera confidence pick, named as a **"1 oz HP Warhead,"** is
  likely the same product line as the War Baits HD above, but the model name
  as captioned differs enough ("HP Warhead" vs. "HD," "Warhead" vs. "War
  Baits") that it is not confidently resolved to the same model here
  (asr-uncertain; see [evidence](evidence/spinnerbaits.md)) — kept as a
  separate data point rather than merged.
- Blades are mounted **on top of** the bait rather than trailing behind it —
  the stated reason the retrieve lifts the bait rather than just spinning
  behind it (`XyKF34C5iB4`).

## Execution

- **Long cast, let it hit bottom.** Don't start the retrieve on splashdown —
  let the bait sink all the way to bottom first (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- **Rip it out of the grass on the pickup.** The first turns of the retrieve
  are a deliberate rip to clear any grass fouled on the blades/hooks and
  confirm the bait is turning and working properly before settling into the
  retrieve proper (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- **Wind it as the blades lift the bait off bottom.** As line comes tight and
  the bait starts moving, the blades create lift that brings the bait up and
  out of the grass on their own, rather than needing to be worked with the
  rod tip the way a crank does (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- **Slow the line down, let it pause and flutter, then repeat.** Once the
  bait is up and swimming, ease off, let it flutter back toward bottom, then
  resume — a stop-and-flutter cadence rather than a steady grind
  (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- **Pop it periodically to trigger a strike.** Popping the rod every now and
  then makes the blades flash and vibrate in place rather than just spin on a
  steady retrieve; bites often come right after a pop — a recurring pattern,
  not a one-off (`XyKF34C5iB4`).

## Newport Harbor plan — grass vs. docks

Planning a Newport Harbor shore-pounding session starts with the tide, then
water clarity, which picks grass or docks (`XyKF34C5iB4`):

- **Grass, when the water carries some color** — a two-bait plan: a
  downsized **PK3 swimbait on a neck-breaker head** (see [soft-plastic
  swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md)) in the **"spotty" color**,
  deliberately matching a spotted bay bass's own coloring ("feeding a spotty
  its own kind"), paired with the War Baits 1 oz HD spinnerbait above.
  Called sufficient on its own for a shore-pounding grass session: "I don't
  have a third" (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- **Docks instead of grass, when the water runs clear.** The fish are still
  living in the grass, but in clear water they are less willing to leave it
  to feed: doing so exposes them to predators — birds, seals — with nothing
  to hide their approach, while docks and pilings give the same fish cover to
  feed from without that exposure. Under the docks, presentation switches to
  a flipped slow-pitch jig or a crankbait worked right on the pilings,
  instead of the spinnerbait/moving-bait program above (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- **Reading which water you'll get:** clearer water is more likely later in
  the cooler months; a rain event on the Wednesday and Thursday immediately
  before a hypothetical next-day trip, in the panel's example, dirties the
  water back up and tips the read back toward the grass plan (`XyKF34C5iB4`).

## Common failures

- **Skipping the rip-clear on the pickup** — a blade still fouled with grass
  from the sink doesn't spin or lift; the deliberate rip on the first turns
  of the retrieve is what clears it and confirms the bait is working before
  the retrieve proper (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
- **Starting the retrieve on splashdown** — winding before the bait reaches
  bottom skips the sink-to-bottom presentation the bait is fished around; let
  it hit bottom first (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
