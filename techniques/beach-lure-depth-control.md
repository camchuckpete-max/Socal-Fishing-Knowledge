# Beach Depth Control — Reading Bottom by Rod-Tip Height

A single lever controls how deep a lure runs while beach/surf casting, without
swapping lures: rod-tip angle and height during the retrieve (`QJd7A6oiV4U`).
The mechanism is rod geometry, not lure design — the same three positions work
across a leadhead-and-plastic, a metal lure, and a minnow-style hardbait
(`QJd7A6oiV4U`). Reading how a beach's bottom shape and texture change through
the retrieve, then adjusting tip height in real time, keeps a lure out of
trouble without a lure change.

## Reach for this when

- Beach/surf casting any lure type and needing to hold it off, or push it
  deeper into, the water column without changing lures.
- The bottom changes shape or texture across the retrieve — a sandbar into a
  trough, or a patch of harder structure — and depth needs adjusting on the
  fly rather than swapping lures mid-retrieve.
- Keeping a lure from digging into or hanging up on a bottom feature that can
  be felt or seen coming, e.g. a gravelly shell bar that snags gear and can
  cost the lure (`QJd7A6oiV4U`).

## Mechanics

- **Rod tip down** (low, toward the water) runs the lure at its **maximum
  depth** (`QJd7A6oiV4U`).
- **Rod tip at mid-height** moderates the depth, holding the lure **a little
  higher** in the column than tip-down (`QJd7A6oiV4U`).
- **Rod tip all the way up** keeps the lure from running into the bottom at
  all — the shallowest of the three positions (`QJd7A6oiV4U`).
- The three positions apply the same way across a plastic lure on a leadhead,
  a metal lure, and a minnow-style hardbait — the mechanism is rod angle and
  rod-tip height, not lure design (`QJd7A6oiV4U`).
- Different beaches carry different bottom shapes and textures; reading that
  by feel and by eye through the retrieve is what lets the tip-height
  adjustment happen before a lure reaches trouble (`QJd7A6oiV4U`).

## Common failures

- **Holding one rod-tip height into a changing bottom.** A beach's shape and
  texture change through the retrieve; keeping the same tip height into a
  harder patch — the named example is a gravelly shell bar — risks a hang-up
  and losing the lure. Read the bottom by feel and by eye and adjust tip
  height before reaching it (`QJd7A6oiV4U`).
- ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: failure modes beyond the hang-up case
  (e.g. tip held too high and losing bottom contact/depth entirely) are not
  covered by this source.
