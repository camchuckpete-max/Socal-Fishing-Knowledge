# Cheater Troll

A stopgap trolling move for a SoCal live-bait sportboat (half-day through
multi-day trips): rig a spare rod with a swimbait/jig built on a
**double-collar keeper** — so the soft-plastic trailer doesn't slide or wrap
(see [bait-keeper collar design](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md#bait-keeper-collar-design--why-it-matters-robert-schneider-phoenix-baits))
— then troll or slow-drag it off the side or back of the boat during the
boat's dead time: the stretch between a hookup/chum stop and the deckhand's
bait call, when most of the rail is standing idle at the bait tank
(`NXvqLUZ6qp8`). It picks up an occasional extra yellowtail in that window —
*"I'm out there when everybody else is waiting for bait"* (`NXvqLUZ6qp8`) —
as an add-on to whatever the boat is actually doing, not a program to run
instead of it.

## Reach for this when

- The rail is idle at the bait tank between stops and a spare rod is already
  rigged with a keeper-collar swimbait/jig (`NXvqLUZ6qp8`).
- You're working **the slide** (a drifting presentation): stick the rod out
  wide off the rail so the cheater-troll line stays clear of the other
  anglers fishing live bait (`NXvqLUZ6qp8`).

## Etiquette

Captains tolerate a cheater-troll line on the condition that it stays out of
the way of the boat's actual trolling pattern and the live-bait fishermen at
the rail (`NXvqLUZ6qp8`). The live-bait rotation always wins: the instant the
deckhand starts passing out bait, the cheater-troll line has to come in and
clear before the rail gets going (`NXvqLUZ6qp8`).

## Common failures

- **Fouling the boat's trolling gear.** Run carelessly — especially while
  working the slide — the cheater-troll line can foul the crew's actual
  trolling gear when it's brought up (`NXvqLUZ6qp8`).
- **Missing the bait call.** The cheater-troll line has to be in and clear the
  moment the deckhand starts passing out bait, or it costs the rotation
  (`NXvqLUZ6qp8`).
