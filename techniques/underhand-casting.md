# Underhand Casting

Execution for the underhand cast — a simple lob toss for live bait off a
sportboat rail or bow, alternative to a full [overhead surface-iron
cast](surface-iron-casting.md) when you don't need the distance an overhead
swing buys.

(Armando, aboard the Searcher, SearcherSportfishing, `0hQJxESLTm4`,
2020-08-06; unregistered channel — medium confidence.)

## Reach for this when

- You're **not experienced with overhead casting** — the underhand cast is
  the simpler motion to learn first.
- You're **standing in a corner** of the rail or bow, where there isn't room
  to bring an overhead cast out cleanly.
- You're fishing a **lighter bait** — the source's example is an anchovy on a
  small rubber-core sinker — rather than a heavier iron or weighted rig.

## Mechanics

- Keep the bait clear of other passengers as you set up.
- Thumb on the spool, put the reel in **free spool**.
- Bring the rod up to the rail.
- Toss the bait out with a simple underhand lob — no wind-up, no overhead
  swing.

## Light-bait tip: pull slack first

With a lighter bait — the source's example is an anchovy fished on a small
rubber-core sinker — the bait alone may not have enough weight to pull line
smoothly off the reel. Pulling a bit of line off the spool by hand before the
toss makes the cast easier.
