# San Augustine

**[Baja only]** — a crew-named stop on Cedros Island, in the
[Cedros / San Benitos](cedros-island.md) zone, and the zone's one named
[yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) ground. Day three of an October 2024
lodge trip opened here — the same yo-yo spot the crew had fished the day before,
with theirs the only boat on it that morning — and an iron dropped to **130 ft**
drew an immediate, sustained bite (`u0scEBby7nA`).

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
The single source names it by name only, so a conditions pull has to be run
against the zone rather than against this stop.

## Getting there

**The documented pattern is a lodge panga day, and this is the first stop of the
morning.** The corpus gives the sequence and nothing else: the crew ran here to
open day three, having already worked it on day two (`u0scEBby7nA`). The zone's
two access modes — the Cedros fly-in chain and the San Diego long-range boat —
are on [the zone page](cedros-island.md#getting-there), and neither is written
around a run to this stop.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The corpus gives one number here, and it is a working depth rather than a
sounding.** The yo-yo iron is dropped to **130 ft** at this stop (`u0scEBby7nA`)
— the deepest working depth recorded anywhere on Cedros itself, against the
zone's 28–30 ft resident rock and its 60–68 ft surface-iron band, both of which
attach to other grounds ([zone page](cedros-island.md#structure--bathymetry)).
Nothing is said on camera about relief, rock, crest depth, bottom composition or
how far off the beach the boats sit.

**Kelp is in the day-three water.** One strike came right next to a small,
isolated piece of kelp rather than a full paddy — small patches hold fish, not
only paddies (`u0scEBby7nA`). ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): "isolated"
is contradicted in the same breath — "right there off that little piece of kelp
dude see that little piece yeah they out there oh that's another piece out
there" — so several small pieces were in the water, not one on its own; the
small-patch doctrine the line carries is unaffected, and the word "paddy" is
never spoken in the source. The footage does not separate that cast from the
San Augustine stop, so read it as day-three water rather than as this ground
specifically.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: soundings, bottom composition, kelp extent, or
what the 130 ft drop is actually being fished over — whether that is bottom, a
ledge, or fish suspended over deeper water.

## How it fishes

**This is a yo-yo stop first.** The iron goes to **130 ft** and the bite is
immediate and sustained, including a hookup on the second drop (`u0scEBby7nA`);
the retrieve mechanics are in [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md). The
colours called out on this bite were **blue and white** (`u0scEBby7nA`) — see
[iron jigs](../lures/iron-jigs.md). One outfit fishing it ran **40 lb** line,
about **100 yards** of it (`u0scEBby7nA`); the reel and rod named on camera did
not decode confidently enough to carry, and the zone's wider line-class argument
— a 40/50 lb floor against an 80–100 lb bracket — is on
[the zone page](cedros-island.md) with parameters in
[line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md).

**The stop repeats.** The same ground produced on two consecutive days of the
same trip, and the crew had it to themselves on the second morning
(`u0scEBby7nA`) — the one piece of repeatability evidence the corpus offers for
any named Cedros stop.

**By the end of that session all three outfits were producing** — surface iron,
[dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) and yo-yo at once, "you name it,
they're biting" (`u0scEBby7nA`) — the zone's three-outfits pattern, though the
session had moved beyond the opening stop by then. The zone-level version is on
[the zone page](cedros-island.md#how-it-fishes); the general method is
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md).

**Release was the constraint, not the bite.** Fish were released once the boat's
kept-fish allotment was reached (`u0scEBby7nA`); handling is in
[fish care](../fish-care/README.md).

never a tide window.
