# 1010 Trench / 378 / 213

**A zone is a run grouping.** 1010 Trench / 378 / 213 is a piece of the numbered
offshore-bank grid in open water well west of Ensenada — five charted positions
(cameron) spanning **17 minutes of latitude** (31°37.000'N to 31°54.000'N) and
**23 minutes of longitude** (117°44.000'W to 118°07.000'W). Three of them —
**378**, **213** and the **1010 Trench** — sit inside 10 nm of each other and
are the cluster the zone is named for; **The Airplane** to the west and the
**1067 Knuckle** to the south are lone banks that the census attached here as
the nearest grouping they could be fished with.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these five marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid — fifty-one charted positions over seventeen bank zones with
no depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
spot names claim, and what is missing.

## Getting there

**This is a Mexican-side offshore zone reached across open water.** It lies in
[Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md), so a boat leaving the United
States crosses the border to fish it. The nearest charted positions the spot
library offers on either side are **Punta San Miguel** at **31°54.110'N
116°44.980'W** in the Ensenada zone, **51–70 nm east** of the five marks here,
and the **Point Loma Pipe** at **32°40.350'N 117°17.000'W** in San Diego, about
**61 nm** from the zone centre (cameron). For scale, the
[Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) — the closest island fishing to a major
US port — run **~13 nm from the San Diego bay entrance** (cameron), so this
zone sits roughly four to five times that distance out.

**Ensenada is the working port on this side, and it is the only one the corpus
attaches to offshore fishing here.** Bait is taken outside the Hotel Coral
marina and the offshore fleet departs from it (`0dIwWiOc1NY`, `0bcDBGzQnGw`).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states that these banks are fished out of

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three-bank core is what makes this one zone.** The 378 at **31°54.000'N
117°50.000'W**, the 213 at **31°48.200'N 117°51.800'W** and the 1010 Trench at
**31°45.833'N 117°44.000'W** (cameron) sit **6.0 nm** (378 to 213), **7.0 nm**
(213 to 1010 Trench) and **9.6 nm** (378 to 1010 Trench) apart — every pair
inside the census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, which is the clustering rule
that grouped them.

**The two outer banks were attached, not clustered.** The Airplane at
**31°50.000'N 118°07.000'W** and the 1067 Knuckle at **31°37.000'N
117°53.000'W** (cameron) were each their own lone bank in the coordinate
clustering and joined this zone as the nearest one they could be fished with:

- **The Airplane** lies **12.1 nm** from the zone centre and **13.0 nm** from
  the 213, but **20.0 nm** from the 1010 Trench — the widest span in the zone
  and past the 12 nm cap (cameron).
- **The 1067 Knuckle** lies **10.0 nm** from the zone centre and **11.2 nm**
  from the 213, at the zone's southern end (cameron).

Both are carried here as queued rather than re-parented, because the zone list
is set by the census and not by this page.

**The names are the only depth claims in the zone, and two of them do not
read.** A bank takes the **fathom depth of its shallowest point** as its name,
at six feet to a fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read
that way the 378 tops out at **378 fathoms — about 2,268 ft** and the 213 at
**213 fathoms — about 1,278 ft**, both deep-water banks rather than anything
fished on the surface of the structure. The same reading puts the 1010 Trench
at **6,060 ft** and the 1067 Knuckle at **6,402 ft**, deeper than any depth the
corpus fishes by a wide margin — SoCal daytime swordfish baits average
**800–1,100 ft** ([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)),
which is the deepest working number in the KB. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source confirms any of the four numbers as a charted depth, or says whether the
fathom convention extends to four-digit names at all.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what the qualifiers *Trench* and *Knuckle*
denote. The lexicon records only that the 475 Knuckle is a distinct spot from
the 475, not what the word describes, and nothing defines *Trench* as a
structure term.

**Three charted positions in the library carry an "Airplane" name.** This one,
[The Airplane rockfish area](airplane-rockfish-area.md) at 32°45.570'N
117°18.460'W in [La Jolla](la-jolla.md), and [Airplane Rock](airplane-rock.md)
at 33°16.100'N 119°27.840'W at
[San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md) (cameron). Under the
landmass-prefix collision rule this one would be "Baja - The Airplane"
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)); the library does not prefix it. ⚠ Flagged
gap — no corpus source resolves the three names against each other.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bottom composition, relief, contour, high-spot
shape or current behaviour anywhere in this zone. The `depth_band` is unknown
for that reason, not because the water is shallow.

**The zone's charted neighbours have no pages yet.** Upper Cross / 421 / 390
sits about **14 nm** north of the zone centre, Baja - 270 / Double 220 / 295
about **17 nm** southeast, and Upper 500 / Hidden Bank about **20 nm** east
(cameron). ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: none of the three carries
corpus material or a page to hand off to.

Three areas were being worked as of **2021-05** — one west of San Diego and two
west of Ensenada — with nothing running further down the coast to Colonet or

[bluefin router](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) carries that material at region

**Both corpus distance figures offshore of Ensenada fall short of this zone.**
Yellowfin were reported showing roughly **30–40 mi out** from Ensenada, with
the crew weighing the run against working structure closer in and choosing the
closer option (`0dIwWiOc1NY`); the units are stated as miles. A second Ensenada
trip planned **20–30 mi straight west** to a temperature break and put the
working fleet **45–50 mi** off (`0bcDBGzQnGw`); the units are stated as miles
there too, and no bank is named in either. Every mark in this zone sits beyond
**50 nm** from Punta San Miguel, so both reports describe water inshore of the
nearest mark here.

## Spots

Five charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. They group as a core plus
two attachments:

- **The core three — the 378, the 213 and the 1010 Trench**, inside 10 nm of
  each other (cameron) and the only part of this zone that clustered on its own.
- **The Airplane** — the western outlier, 12.1 nm off the zone centre and 20 nm
  from the 1010 Trench; also the name that collides with two other charted
  "Airplane" marks in the library.
- **The 1067 Knuckle** — the southern outlier, 10.0 nm off the zone centre and
  the deepest-reading name in the zone.

Charted positions for all five are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
