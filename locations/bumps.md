# The Bumps

**A zone is a run grouping.** This one is a single charted mark — **The Bumps**
at **30°37.000'N 117°13.000'W** (cameron), out on the offshore grid of
[Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md), roughly **39 nm** west-southwest
of the charted Colonet high spots. The position in the infobox is the charted
mark itself, not an average of anything: a zone of one has no centre to compute.

**No corpus source names it.** "The Bumps" appears in no transcript, no evidence
file and no piece of fishing doctrine; the only places it occurs in the repo are
its coordinate line in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md), the
flagged-stub row on [the region page](baja-pacific-north.md), and two neighbour
pages that reach this way and find nothing to hand off to
([179 / 220](179-220.md), [Lower Cross](lower-cross.md)). The zone exists on
coordinates (cameron), so this is a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where
the mark is, what water it sits in, what its name does and does not claim, and
what a source would have to supply. Nothing here lifts the region page's stub.
The flags below aggregate in [the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

**This is a zone of one because nothing else is within reach of it.** The census
clusters banks under a **12 nm** zone-diameter cap and then attaches a leftover
lone bank to the nearest zone inside **25 nm** (cameron). The nearest charted
position in the whole spot library is **Catchers Mitt**, **31.3 nm** away at
**312°** — past both thresholds — so this mark clustered with nothing and
attached to nothing, and minted a zone in its own name. **Nothing charted lies
within 30 nm of it in any direction**, and only **five** of the library's 391
positions lie inside **40 nm**.

**The library's four single-mark zones are exactly its four most isolated
positions, and this is the third of them.** Ranked by distance to the nearest
other charted mark: [12 Mile Reef](12-mile-reef.md) at **35.8 nm**, the **380**
at **31.4 nm**, The Bumps at **31.3 nm** and
[San Juan Seamount](san-juan-seamount.md) at **31.0 nm** (cameron). Two of the
four are in this region and they are neighbours: the **380** is **31.4 nm** away
at **217°**, which makes The Bumps the 380's nearest charted position in the
entire library and the 380 this mark's second-nearest, **0.1 nm** behind
Catchers Mitt. Two marks that are each other's closest company and still **6 nm**
outside the attach radius is what a zone of one looks like from both sides.
⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: the 380 has no page on this ladder.

**Do not confuse it with The Bump off La Jolla.** The library carries both names
— [The Bump](bump.md), singular, is a La Jolla mark **134.6 nm** north of here
in US waters (cameron). They are different places in different jurisdictions,
and the plural is the offshore one.

## Structure & bathymetry

**One position, and the name is all there is.** The mark sits at
**30°37.000'N 117°13.000'W** (cameron), the sixth-southernmost of the **125**
charted offshore-bank positions in the library. Only **12** of the library's
**391** positions lie south of it, spread across three zones — the
[179 / 220](179-220.md) grouping, [San Quintín](san-quintin.md) and the 380
(cameron). This is the southern end of the charted grid, not the middle of it.

**The name is a shape, not a depth.** Across the offshore-bank section the
un-numbered names are shapes and objects — The Pistol, The Worm, Mushroom,
Elephant, Peanut Bank, the Sniffer, Catchers Mitt and The Bumps among them
(cameron, [Lower Cross](lower-cross.md)). A bank is otherwise named for the
**fathom depth of its shallowest point**, at six feet to a fathom (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)) — so a numbered neighbour like the 330 or the
1140 Finger states a depth and this one states nothing. The `depth_band` is
unknown for that reason, not because the mark is shallow. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source says what shape the name describes, or whether these shape names
report bottom contour on a sounder screen, a chart outline, or something else.

**The plural is the one clue on the page, and the corpus does not license
reading it.** The library's own exception to the fathom convention is a bank
whose relief has no single number: Nine Mile Bank is named for distance rather
than depth because it carries **many closely spaced high spots of similar
depth** rather than one dominant peak (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). A plural name over a single charted
coordinate is the same shape of problem — but no source says The Bumps is
multiple crests, and **one position cannot resolve several of them** if it is.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: how many high spots the name covers, what
they crest at, how far apart they sit, or which of them the charted position
marks.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain has no lee to work against, and the
face to bias toward is the up-current one, where current forced against the high
spot drives the upwelling that stacks the food chain
([current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md),
[island structure](island-structure.md)) — though each individual mark still has
its own preferred directions learned over repeat trips. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: bottom composition, relief, contour, surrounding depth, preferred
directions, drift or current behaviour at this mark.

**Everything charted is north and east of it.** Nearest positions in the spot
library, nearest first (cameron): **Catchers Mitt** at **31.3 nm** northwest in
[Lower Cross](lower-cross.md) — which records the same distance from its side —
the **380** at **31.4 nm** southwest, the **330** at **33.9 nm** east in
[Colonet](colonet.md), the **Lower 500** at **34.7 nm** north in
[1140 Finger / 450](1140-finger-450.md), the two **Colonet** high spots at
**39.1 nm** and **40.5 nm**, the **1140 Finger** itself at **41.1 nm** and the
**179** at **42.4 nm** in [179 / 220](179-220.md). In the whole arc from
**150°** clockwise through south and west round to **300°**, the library charts
only **three** positions inside **55 nm** — the 380 at **31.4 nm**, Lower Cross
at **46.5 nm** and the 950 at **50.9 nm** — and the sectors **150°–210°** and
**240°–270°** are empty of any charted position at all, at any range. **328** of
the other **390** positions lie in the northern arc between **330°** and
**030°** (cameron). Read that as a fact about the library rather than about the
ocean, but read it: every fallback this zone has is north or east.

**The one corpus statement that reaches this water is about bluefin, and it is
about the water rather than the mark.** The structure outside Colonet and San
Quintín is described as "a good structure of water down there that, in the
beginning when this all started, that's where that fish was at, and if that
water structure is still there then I'm sure there's bluefin down there too"
(`fgTmUq78ofQ`, 2021-05-14) — said of ground the fleet had left unfished as of
**2021-05** because the closer grounds west of San Diego and Ensenada were
producing. This mark sits **39.1 nm** outside the charted Colonet high spots and
**56.3 nm** outside the nearest San Quintín position (cameron), so it is
plausibly inside the water that statement describes and no source says how far
"outside" reaches. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source puts a
[bluefin](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) on this position or gives it a month.

**Bluefin were sighted on this latitude in winter, 58 nm inshore.** Schools were
seen near San Martin Island in early **January 2021** in **63–65 °F** water
(`shZCjX2-fkI`) — presence outside the SoCal slide rather than a fishable
pattern, and recorded at a coastal island, not out here.

**Colonet is named in the corpus for depth, and this mark is further out than
it.** Colonet and Guadalupe are the two places named in the deepest of three
yo-yo tiers, where the pick is a Salas 7X heavy rather than the 6X or 6X Jr
fished at 60–150 ft (`hteLeDIy9Qs`, [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md)).
That is stated for the charted coastal high spots at **28 and 41 fathoms**
([Colonet](colonet.md)), not for a bank **39 nm** offshore of them, and it is
the closest thing to a depth-and-technique reading anything near this water has.

**The region's year-round pattern is stated for coastal structure, not for
this.** Baja coastal structure from Ensenada southward holds
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) more or less year-round with the grade
improving as you go south (cameron,
[Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md)). These are offshore banks and no
source extends it to them.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states an
approach, a starting depth, a drift, or what tide, light or current do here.

**A single mark reads as a compact spot-check rather than a search box, but no
source says so.** The two zone shapes the corpus draws are the complex — a
**~50–60 square-mile** box you plan hours of looking across — and the compact
spot you idle onto, grade on the meter, and either commit to or leave in
**20–30 minutes** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). One charted position
is the shape of the second — unless the plural name means several crests, in
which case it is neither, and nothing in the corpus settles that. Either way the
consequence of a blank here is harsh: the nearest fallback mark is **31.3 nm**
off and is itself corpus-silent, so a blank costs most of an hour's run before
you are looking at anything else.

**The region's own offshore method is the break, not the bank.** The stated plan
is to run west to a temperature break and then work north or west off it by sea
conditions (`0bcDBGzQnGw`), which makes the mark a reference point in a
temperature search rather than a destination in itself
([water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md),
[water regimes](../conditions/water-regimes.md)). Break position and break age
decide whether this zone is on a given day's plan
([report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)),
and the meter decides once you are there
([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**This mark is not a BightSST eval target, and the nearest one is 100 nm away.**
Conditions here are pulled per charted position rather than read off a scored
spot ([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)); the closest scored spot is the
Rockpile at the [Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md), **100.7 nm** due north
(cameron) — far enough that it stands in for nothing out here. The per-position
pull is the only honest conditions read this zone has.
