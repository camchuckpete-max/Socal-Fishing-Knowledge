# 475 Knuckle / Upper Finger Bank

**A zone is a run grouping.** 475 Knuckle / Upper Finger Bank is a two-mark
cluster on the numbered offshore-bank grid west of the Baja mainland coast
between Descanso and Ensenada: the **Upper Finger Bank** at **32°06.800'N
117°04.400'W** and the **475 Knuckle** at **32°04.000'N 117°07.000'W**
(cameron), **3.6 nm** apart on a **218°** line. Both come out of the spot
library's latitude-sorted offshore-banks list, so this is a coordinate cluster
rather than a boundary the fishery drew.

**It is the smallest kind of zone the census can produce and the tightest one
written in this region.** Two marks **3.6 nm** apart (cameron) span less than a third of the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, and less than half the **8.5 nm** core of
[385 / 238 / 475](385-238-475.md), the tightest bank grouping the review had
written before it. A boat working one of these positions is already on top of
the other.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing either mark.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid — charted positions with no depth, no target species and no
doctrine attached to one of them. This is therefore a **position-and-ladder
page**: it says where the zone is, what the two names claim, and what is
missing.

**What makes this zone worth reading anyway is a name collision it sits at the
centre of.** Five depth-labelled spots in a different zone carry the *Upper
Finger Bank* name while lying north and west of the position charted under that
name, and the 475 Knuckle shares its number with a bank **28 nm** south
(cameron). Both are
laid out under [Structure & bathymetry](#structure--bathymetry) below, because a
planner reading a spot list will otherwise assume the names mean what they say.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run, heading, timing or fuel figure to
either mark, and no source states that this zone is fished out of Ensenada, off
the Baja coast, out of San Diego or anywhere else.

**Ensenada is the working port this water sits outside of, and the zone is
inside the port's documented offshore band but off its documented bearing.**
Ensenada is a drive-to port with bait made outside the marina and the offshore
fleet departing from it (`0dIwWiOc1NY`, `0bcDBGzQnGw`; [Ensenada](ensenada.md)).
Measured from **Punta San Miguel** at **31°54.110'N 116°44.980'W**, the
northern headland of that zone (cameron), the two marks lie **20.8–21.1 nm**
away — **23.9–24.3 statute miles** — on a bearing of about **303°**. The stated
Ensenada offshore plan runs **20–30 mi due west** to a temperature break
(`0bcDBGzQnGw`), so this zone falls inside that distance band but roughly
**30° north of the track**: a boat following the documented program passes to
the south of it. That is a distance coincidence worth knowing when planning, not
evidence that these positions were the ones fished.

**The coast directly inshore is closer than the port is, and has no staging
point in the KB.** Punta Mesquite at **32°09.580'N 116°54.800'W** and La Fonda
at **32°08.460'N 116°54.150'W** lie **8.6–8.8 nm** east of the Upper Finger Bank
([La Fonda / Bajamar / Salsipuedes](la-fonda-bajamar-salsipuedes.md)), and
Sugarloaf Rock and *Descanso rockfish 1* the same distance to the northeast
([Rosarito / Descanso](rosarito-descanso.md)) (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: any port, ramp, hoist, fuel dock or bait receiver on that stretch
of coast, so the nearest water is not the nearest launch.

**From the United States it is a long way past the last documented island
stop.** The Point Loma Pipe at **32°40.350'N 117°17.000'W** is **35.2–37.3 nm**
from the two marks (cameron), and the Coronados' outlying southeastern mark, the
**Rockpile** at **32°17.620'N 117°10.030'W**, is **11.8–13.9 nm** north
([Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md)). The Coronados are the closest island
fishing to a major US port at roughly **13 nm** off the San Diego bay entrance
(cameron), which makes this zone about another island-run beyond them.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether these banks are ever worked as an
extension of a Coronados day, or reached from the US side at all.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
closures, protected areas or restricted zones anywhere in this zone.

## Structure & bathymetry

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bottom composition, relief, contour,
high-spot shape, surrounding depth, preferred directions, drift or current
behaviour at either mark. The `depth_band` is unknown for that reason, not
because the water is shallow. Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes
in **any** direction, but each individual bank still has its own **preferred
directions** learned over repeat trips ([island structure](island-structure.md));
nothing states what those are here.

**The zone's name and the five spots that share it are not in the same place.**
The spot library's `## Finger Bank rockfish` section holds five depth-labelled
marks — *Upper Finger Bank rockfish 180ft*, *240ft*, *300ft*, the *71 Fathom
Spot* and the *27 Fathom Spot* (cameron) — and every one of them carries the
Upper Finger Bank name while lying **7.6–12.7 nm** north-northwest of the
position charted under that name, on bearings of **332–360°**
([Finger Bank rockfish](finger-bank-rockfish.md)). The nearest of them, the
27 Fathom Spot, is **7.6 nm** from the Upper Finger Bank — inside the census's
12 nm cap, so on coordinates alone it would have clustered here. It did not,
because those five sit in a `##` section of their own and a fisherman's carve-up
outranks anything the clustering infers. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
whether those marks sit on a northern extension of the same bank feature — a
*finger* would suggest exactly that — or whether the label is inherited loosely
from the nearest named bank.

**The two halves of that name disagree about depth, which is the part a planner
should not smooth over.** The five northern marks label **162–426 ft** of water,
shallow enough to be worked on the bottom ([Finger Bank rockfish](finger-bank-rockfish.md)).
The name charted *here* carries no number at all — one of the few bank names in
the region's grid that makes no depth claim — while its zone partner claims
**475**. Under the naming convention a bank takes the **fathom depth of its
shallowest (highest) point**, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`), which reads the 475 Knuckle at
**475 fathoms — about 2,850 ft**. That is deeper than anything fished on the
bottom in the KB and well below the **800–1,100 ft** SoCal daytime swordfish
bait ([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)). So one
name says rockfish ground and the other says open-ocean depth, roughly 8 nm
apart. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms the 475 Knuckle's number as a
charted depth, and none reconciles it with the shallow labels to the north.

**The 475 Knuckle and the 475 are different spots and the charts agree.** The
library records the pair as distinct names rather than a collision
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)), and the two charted positions are **28.1 nm**
apart — the 475 at **31°36.000'N 117°05.000'W** lies almost due south, in
[385 / 238 / 475](385-238-475.md) (cameron). If the fathom convention holds for
both, the shared number is two banks topping out at the same depth rather than
one place under two names, and *Knuckle* is what separates them. ⚠ Flagged gap —
no corpus source says what *Knuckle* denotes as a structure term; the
**1067 Knuckle** in [1010 Trench / 378 / 213](1010-trench-378-213.md) leaves the
same question open.

**The one other *Finger* name in the library is far south.** The **1140 Finger**
sits about **49 nm** below the Upper Finger Bank in
[1140 Finger / 450](1140-finger-450.md) (cameron), and *Upper* here implies a
lower counterpart the library does not chart under that name. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source says what *Finger* denotes, or whether the two are the same
feature.

**The zone's charted neighbours are coastal, not offshore.** The nearest marks
in any direction after the Finger Bank rockfish five are the Descanso and
La Fonda coastal positions **8.6–12.7 nm** east and northeast; the nearest bank
zone is [101 / 425](101-425.md), whose 101 is **12.2 nm** north, and
[Upper 500 / Hidden Bank](upper-500-hidden-bank.md) is **17.8 nm** or more to
the southeast (cameron). This zone is therefore the **inshore edge of the Baja
bank grid at these latitudes** — the last structure before the coast — which is
the one thing about it that would shape a plan. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
frames it that way, or any way.

**What the corpus does put in this general water is placed by distance from
Ensenada, not by mark.** Yellowfin were reported showing **30–40 mi** out of that
port (`0dIwWiOc1NY`), and skipjack, yellowfin and a striped marlin were worked

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the pair is worked as one circuit, or what
current, tide, light or swell do here.

**The only program the corpus scopes to this water is the port's, and it is
chart-first rather than spot-first.** The stated Ensenada offshore plan is to run
**20–30 mi due west** to a temperature break, then continue north or west off it
by sea conditions, with a fallback search **20–30 mi north**; trolling cedar
plugs locates fish and the crew deliberately worked away from the fleet
(`0bcDBGzQnGw`; [Ensenada](ensenada.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). Applied here that makes
these two positions **water you would cross on a break search**, not
destinations the corpus sends you to. General reading layers are
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md) and
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md); neither is scoped to this zone.

**If the 475 Knuckle's name is a depth, no bottom method in the KB reaches it.**
[Rockfish deep-dropping](../techniques/rockfish-deep-dropping.md) and the
[dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) are the methods the neighbouring
labels imply, and [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) is what high spots
in that shallower band get elsewhere in the KB — but at **475 fathoms** none of
them applies, and the Upper Finger Bank has no depth at all to judge by.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any method scoped to either mark.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron) — the whole zone, and close enough together to be
one stop:

- **The Upper Finger Bank**, the northern and eastern of the pair, carrying a
  name shared with five depth-labelled marks in another zone and no depth claim
  of its own. It is the closer of the two to every neighbour the library charts —
  the coast, the Coronados, the Finger Bank rockfish ladder and the 101 alike
  (cameron).
- **The 475 Knuckle**, **3.6 nm** southwest of it, the deepest-reading name in
  this water and a distinct spot from the 475 far to the south.

Charted positions for both are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
