# Dana Point

The mainland strip either side of Dana Point Harbor, running from the Orange
County coast down across the county line to Trestles: **9 charted spots** in
the spot library (cameron), from **The Hospital 100 Fathom Curve (33°29.500'N
117°47.100'W)** at the north end south to **Trestles (33°22.790'N
117°36.280'W)**. It is a **kelp-and-boiler coast with a deep edge** — two named
kelp beds, boiler rock, beach-break points, one artificial reef, and a spot
named for the 100-fathom contour itself.

Dana Point is also a **port**, and the zone carries two facts the rest of the
Bight plans around: it is where the sportboat fleet's **6:00 a.m.** departure
sets the bait-barge rush (`ztpj1Xll0-U`), and it is the Bight's **northern
paddy limit** — [kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md) are scarcer and
hold fewer fish north of here, and the good paddy water runs from Dana Point
south toward [Oceanside](oceanside-north-county.md) (`HzE4FHHcvTk`).

## Getting there

**The bait comes first, and the clock is the whole problem.** The Dana Point
sportboat fleet leaves at **6:00 a.m.**, which is also when the private-boat
crowd converges on the barge and a line forms — by a quarter to six it is
already building (`ztpj1Xll0-U`, `bM2vUS1B-yQ`). Leaving the dock at **5:00
a.m.**, or **4:30**, beats the line entirely; **7:00 a.m.** is too late
(`ztpj1Xll0-U`). Loading mid-day also avoids it (`7WapaxdtjQg`).

**At the barge itself** — the Dana Point barge is the corpus's worked example
— the barge marks the side it wants boats tied to, here **a cone on the ocean
side**; hail on **VHF channel 11** with the barge's name twice plus your own if
no attendant is visible; never board the barge to fend your boat off
(`7WapaxdtjQg`). Full etiquette and load sizing are
[making bait](../bait/making-bait.md) and [bait tanks](../bait/bait-tanks.md).
A **21 ft Wellcraft based in Dana Point** carries a **60-gallon** bait tank
(`m-M0iwX8DjA`) — the small-boat end of the "biggest tank the boat will carry"
ladder.

**Deep water starts close.** Across the stretch from Dana Point through
Newport and Marina del Rey, the bottom a **mile to a mile-and-a-half off the
beach** is already **2,000–3,000 ft** (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Range to structure here
is short; range to anything with relief offshore is not.

**Boats also use this zone as a start line rather than a destination** — a
charter left Dana Point and worked **south down the coast** to the Camp
Pendleton end, fishing [Oceanside / North County](oceanside-north-county.md)
water (`HMdrP4-i9MM`).

**The return leg from Catalina lands here.** Dana Point is one of the named
mainland ports for the multi-hour beat home from
[Catalina](catalina-island-front-side.md) during a Santa Ana, when an offshore
NE wind pushes water back against the prevailing swell and stacks the sea
(`NC3-3pJDEgo`) — see [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md).

**Kayak access is real here, and wind picks the stretch.** This is the home
water of a kayak guide whose method is to choose the day's stretch of coast off
wind direction — a windy day pushes the choice **south**, so the paddle home
has the wind at your back rather than into it, with guided runs of roughly
**8 miles** cited (`LqW32NSkObY`). Execution is
[kayak bass fishing](../techniques/kayak-bass-fishing.md).

**One MPA edge is charted in this zone.** At **The Hospital 100 Fathom Curve**,
the library's boundary note is to stay **south of 33°30.050'N** (cameron). No
other spot here carries one, and latitude alone does not establish where a
boundary runs. California / CDFW — verify current MPA boundaries, closures and
take rules with CDFW before fishing (as of 2026-08).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the launch ramps and hoists in Dana Point
Harbor, run time or distance from the harbour mouth to any spot in the zone,
and the sea-state envelope the run demands.

## Structure & bathymetry

The zone is a **north–south coastal ribbon** about 7 minutes of latitude long,
sorting into four families (cameron):

- **Kelp beds — two.** **Dana Point Kelp — Red Buoy** (33°27.320'N
  117°43.380'W) and **San Clemente Kelp** (33°24.450'N 117°38.140'W). The Dana
  Point bed is the one worked on camera: a kelp bed carrying **a little
  current** running through it, fished at anchor (`M6U_FVdosr4`).
- **Rock and boilers — two.** **Monarch Boilers** (33°28.970'N 117°44.020'W)
  and **Barber Poles** (33°26.710'N 117°41.410'W). Boiler rock is close-range,
  breaking structure — the small-craft approach and bail-out plan for working
  it are in [kayak bass fishing](../techniques/kayak-bass-fishing.md).
- **Beach-break points — three.** **Salt Creek** (33°28.565'N 117°44.040'W),
  **San Mateo Point** (33°22.960'N 117°36.800'W) and **Trestles**
  (33°22.790'N 117°36.280'W). Salt Creek sits barely half a minute of latitude
  off Monarch Boilers at effectively the same longitude — a rock-and-beach pair
  rather than two separate runs; San Mateo Point and Trestles are a tighter
  pair still, under two-tenths of a minute apart, at the zone's south end.
- **Built and deep structure — two.** **San Clemente Artificial Reef**
  (33°23.890'N 117°37.200'W) and **The Hospital 100 Fathom Curve**
  (33°29.500'N 117°47.100'W). The Hospital is the zone's only spot out on a
  depth contour rather than against the beach, and it is both the northernmost
  and by far the westernmost position in the library's Dana Point section.

**Naming collision — read this before you read any report.** The zone's
**San Clemente Kelp** and **San Clemente Artificial Reef** are off the
**mainland city of San Clemente**, and the spot library labels both
"(mainland city)" for exactly that reason (cameron). They are **not**
San Clemente Island, the offshore island that carries its own zones
([back side](san-clemente-island-back-side.md)) and its own fishery. Do not
read island reports onto these two coastal spots.

**The only depths the corpus works in this zone** are the kayak-range bands:
**0–8 ft** and **20–100 ft**, occasionally a little past 100 ft
(`LqW32NSkObY`).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: charted depths, relief and bottom

## What's there

| --- | --- | --- |
| [Calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md) | year-round, with a stated personal temperature preference of roughly **65–68°F** and fish going lethargic and dropping to cooler water past about **70°F** (`LqW32NSkObY`) | The zone's kelp and boiler rock are the structure. Calico here are worked mostly in **0–8 ft** and **20–100 ft** (`LqW32NSkObY`), and came off the Dana Point kelp bed in July at anchor, three of them on a squid strip in a morning session (`M6U_FVdosr4`) |
| [Sand bass](../species/sand-bass.md) | ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: season shape scoped to this zone | Five sand bass came off the Dana Point kelp bed in a single July morning session, every one on a strip of squid (`M6U_FVdosr4`) |
| [Barracuda](../species/barracuda.md) | July on the kelp (`M6U_FVdosr4`) | "Short" barracuda showed mid-session in the Dana Point kelp bed and were taken on the **smaller sardines** — a bait-size read that runs opposite the big-mackerel screen used at the islands (`M6U_FVdosr4`) |
| [Bonito](../species/bonito.md) | early July (`poqjnb1r1zk`) | Open-water schools just off the harbour, taken on the troll either side of the harbour mouth. Bonito are around the zone in numbers when they are in — "a lot of Bonita around right now" was the reason for the trip (`poqjnb1r1zk`) |
| [Yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) | ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: season shape scoped to this zone | **Porpoise-associated** yellowfin were trolled on small King Buster-style lures around Dana Point in the **mid-2010s**, a program later rebuilt with a tungsten head (`ouBrIdO7d4k`). This is open water offshore of the zone's coastal spots, not the spots themselves |

**The paddy line runs through here.** Paddies are **scarcer and hold less north
of Dana Point**, and the Bight's best paddy water runs **from Dana Point to
Oceanside and southward** (and south of Catalina / San Clemente Island); the
closer to a dock and the more traffic, the less willing the fish
(`HzE4FHHcvTk`). That is a property of the open water offshore of this strip,
not of the 9 coastal spots — see
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md) and
[August](../seasonal/august.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether yellowtail, white seabass, halibut,
rockfish, lingcod or sheephead are targeted anywhere in this zone, and no
month-by-month season is scoped here for any species.

## How it fishes

**The day starts at the barge, and it starts early.** Beat the 6:00 a.m. fleet
wave rather than joining it (`ztpj1Xll0-U`) — see Getting there. The one dated
skiff start in the corpus was on the water at **5:45 a.m.** (`poqjnb1r1zk`).

**On the kelp: anchor, don't drift.** The worked Dana Point kelp session was
fished **at anchor** on a light current running through the bed, not drifted
through it (`M6U_FVdosr4`) — the same call the general kelp doctrine makes,
where drifting through gives up the edge-holding pattern entirely. Which
corner of a bed to sit on is a current read:
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md), with the structure
read in [bass structure](bass-structure.md).

**Carry both baits — the zone's own bait read is unsettled.** On one July day
at the Dana Point kelp bed, the morning trip produced **5 sand bass and 3
calico, every one on a strip of squid**, which is why the crew called squid
over a sardine strip for the afternoon; by the day's end the same crew reported
bass "actually biting the smaller sardines today," and called it a surprise
(`M6U_FVdosr4`). Both results are from the same bed on the same day, so this is
not a doctrine conflict to resolve — it is a live bait-choice that turns on the
session, and the practical call is to have squid strip and small sardines both
aboard. Execution is [flyline](../techniques/flyline.md) and
[fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md); for anglers who cannot cast,
the same trip pattern uses [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md)
straight down.

**Troll the harbour mouth for bonito, both sides of it.** A skiff worked
**south of Dana Point Harbor** first — "a pretty slow morning" — then moved
**north of the harbour**, pulling **small feathers rigged as a short daisy
chain at roughly 5.5 kt**, and cast a **small sardine-profile hard bait
(Rapala-class diver)** behind the boat on the troll bite, twitching it while
still under way (`poqjnb1r1zk`). Detail in
[trolling](../techniques/trolling.md) and
[tuna feathers and skirts](../lures/tuna-feathers-and-skirts.md).

**Wind picks the stretch before you launch, not after.** From the kayak
program: read wind direction the night before or that morning and choose the
stretch of coast that puts the wind at your back on the way home — from Dana
Point, a windy day pushes the choice south (`LqW32NSkObY`). Spots are
relocated on paired shore landmarks (a lifeguard tower, a buoy) at a fixed
offset rather than on electronics, and a kelp frond serves as an anchor
(`LqW32NSkObY`).

**Rain is the zone's water-quality driver.** Runoff after heavy rain is the
named cause of dirty water in this home water; bass then hunker on the bottom
and largely go off the bite, while bright, clear days warm the surface and pull
fish up into a top-water window. In murk, a blade bait — a **1 oz+
spinnerbait** — cuts through in a way a subtler presentation does not
(`LqW32NSkObY`). Mechanism in
[water color](../conditions/water-color.md).

## Spots

Nine charted spots (cameron), listed north to south, grouped by what the
structure is rather than by how close they sit:

- **The deep edge** — The Hospital 100 Fathom Curve. The zone's northernmost
  and westernmost position, out on the contour rather than against the beach,
  and the one spot carrying an MPA boundary note — stay south of 33°30.050'N
  (cameron).
- **The Monarch / Salt Creek pair** — Monarch Boilers and Salt Creek, boiler
  rock and beach sitting at effectively the same longitude about half a minute
  of latitude apart.
- **The harbour kelp** — Dana Point Kelp (Red Buoy), the bed the corpus's one
  worked session was fished on, and Barber Poles just south of it.
- **The San Clemente city stretch** — San Clemente Kelp and San Clemente
  Artificial Reef, both off the mainland city and neither of them San Clemente
  Island.
- **The south end** — San Mateo Point and Trestles, a tight pair at the
  bottom of the zone, where the coast runs on into
  [Oceanside / North County](oceanside-north-county.md).

Charted positions for all nine are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No individual spot in the zone
carries corpus detail beyond its position and, for the Dana Point kelp bed, the
one July session above.
