# Sniffer / West 400 / 300 (The Rampart)

**A zone is a run grouping.** Sniffer / West 400 / 300 (The Rampart) is the
southwestern corner of the numbered offshore-bank grid — three charted positions
(cameron) in open water, spanning **6.8 minutes of latitude** (31°03.000'N to
31°09.800'N) and **12.1 minutes of longitude** (118°24.917'W to 118°37.000'W).
The **300 (The Rampart)** is the westernmost mark on the Baja grid south of
31°30'N (cameron).

**This zone is a true cluster, not a core plus an attached outlier.** Its
longest pair — the Sniffer to the Rampart at **10.8 nm** — is inside the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, so all three marks grouped on coordinates
together. That makes it the second-tightest three-or-more-spot bank zone in the
region, behind [Baja - 230 / Peanut Bank / 60](baja-230-peanut-bank-60.md) at
8.3 nm, and unlike [372 / 245 / 250](372-245-250.md) or
[483/500 / 437](483-500-437.md) it has no mark that had to be attached from
outside the cap (cameron). By spacing alone, these three are one day's work.

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal, resolved by the census script): the KB
disagrees with itself about what the **12 nm** figure measures, and this page
takes the reading the script supports. `scripts/review/build-geo-worklist.py`
sets `MAX_ZONE_DIAMETER_NM = 12.0` and `complete_linkage()` refuses any merge
whose **maximum pairwise** distance exceeds it, so it is a diameter cap on pair
separation exactly as stated here. Standing flags on
[372 / 245 / 250](372-245-250.md) and on

diagnostic over attached and section-derived spots, not the clustering rule.
Cameron to reverse one side; the conclusion here survives either way, because
this zone's own centre distances (3.9 / 4.4 / 6.8 nm) are all inside 12 nm too
(cameron).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the **second-tightest** ranking holds only
if "bank zone" means a numbered-grid cluster zone, which is how
[Baja - 230 / Peanut Bank / 60](baja-230-peanut-bank-60.md) scopes its
reciprocal ("the region's bank grid"). Read literally, the
[Finger Bank rockfish](finger-bank-rockfish.md) zone is a five-position bank
zone in this same region spanning **3.7 nm** corner to corner — tighter than
both this zone's 10.8 nm and the Baja - 230 cluster's 8.3 nm (cameron).

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these three marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid — fifty-one charted positions over seventeen bank zones with
no depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
spot names claim, and what is missing.

**Two of the library's naming rules meet here.** **West 400** is one half of the
single stated exception to the landmass-prefix collision rule, paired with East
400 in the neighbouring zone, and **300 (The Rampart)** is one of three distinct
"300" names the library keeps apart rather than merging (cameron), per
[the zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md) and
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Both are covered below.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The zone is a short line running southwest.** The West 400 sits at
**31°09.800'N 118°28.300'W**, the Sniffer at **31°05.967'N 118°24.917'W** and
the 300 (The Rampart) at **31°03.000'N 118°37.000'W** (cameron). West 400 to the
Sniffer is **4.8 nm** on **143°**; the Sniffer to the Rampart is **10.8 nm** on
**254°**; West 400 to the Rampart is **10.1 nm** on **228°**. Measured from the centre of the
three, the marks lie **3.9 nm** (West 400), **4.4 nm** (Sniffer) and
**6.8 nm** (the Rampart) out — the most compact
three-spot spread on the Baja grid after the Baja - 230 cluster (cameron).

fishes in **any** direction, but each individual bank still has its own
