# Sniffer / West 400 / 300 (The Rampart)

**A zone is a run grouping.** Sniffer / West 400 / 300 (The Rampart) is the
southwestern corner of the numbered offshore-bank grid — three charted positions
(cameron) in open water, spanning **6.8 minutes of latitude** (31°03.000'N to
31°09.800'N) and **12.1 minutes of longitude** (118°24.917'W to 118°37.000'W).
The **300 (The Rampart)** is the westernmost mark on the Baja grid south of
31°30'N (cameron).

**This zone is a true cluster, not a core plus an attached outlier.** Its
longest pair — the Sniffer to the Rampart at **10.8 nm** — is inside the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, so all three marks grouped on coordinates
together. That makes it the second-tightest three-or-more-spot bank zone in the
region, behind [Baja - 230 / Peanut Bank / 60](baja-230-peanut-bank-60.md) at
8.3 nm, and unlike [372 / 245 / 250](372-245-250.md) or
[483/500 / 437](483-500-437.md) it has no mark that had to be attached from
outside the cap (cameron). By spacing alone, these three are one day's work.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these three marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid — fifty-one charted positions over seventeen bank zones with
no depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
spot names claim, and what is missing.

**Two of the library's naming rules meet here.** **West 400** is one half of the
single stated exception to the landmass-prefix collision rule, paired with East
400 in the neighbouring zone, and **300 (The Rampart)** is one of three distinct
"300" names the library keeps apart rather than merging (cameron), per
[the zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md) and
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Both are covered below.

**Every distance the corpus states offshore of Ensenada falls far inshore of
this zone.** The port's figures are stated in miles and run from a high spot
**7–8 mi** out (`CKq0Z6ExVs4`), through a temperature break **20–30 mi due
west** (`0bcDBGzQnGw`), yellowfin reported showing **30–40 mi** out
(`0dIwWiOc1NY`), a floating object about **35 mi** out (`0bcDBGzQnGw`) and an
August 2024 tournament bank **42–43 mi** out (`Ix0gG0-l3v0`), to a
supplier-conference fleet working **45–50 mi** out (`0bcDBGzQnGw`). The marks
here sit at **112.7–124.6 statute miles** from Punta San Miguel — roughly **2.3
to 2.5 times** the 50 mi outer edge of that furthest documented band (cameron).

**The reach is the planning fact.** One tournament day out of this port totalled
roughly **180 mi** run with about **40 mi** still to get in (`U1AgwmlY5bI`), on
grounds inshore of everything here; the same reference position puts a round
trip to these three marks at about **225–249 statute miles** of running before a
minute is spent searching (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says
whether the port's figures are statute or nautical miles, and none of them names
a bank, so nothing attaches the Ensenada program to these positions.

**No nearer corpus-named access exists.** The closest charted coastal position in
the library is the **48 Fa. Spot** in
[Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md) at **87.1–97.9 nm**;
**Todos Santos Island** is **93.2–103.8 nm** and the Colonet high spots
**99.1–109.3 nm** ([Colonet](colonet.md)) (cameron). Running down the coast does
not shorten the trip. ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: the Banda Bank /

**This is Mexican-side water by a wide margin.** The Point Loma Pipe at
**32°40.350'N 117°17.000'W** in [Point Loma](point-loma.md) is

crosses the border to fish here, and the crossing is the smaller part of the
run.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The zone is a short line running southwest.** The West 400 sits at
**31°09.800'N 118°28.300'W**, the Sniffer at **31°05.967'N 118°24.917'W** and
the 300 (The Rampart) at **31°03.000'N 118°37.000'W** (cameron). West 400 to the
Sniffer is **4.8 nm** on **143°**; the Sniffer to the Rampart is **10.8 nm** on
**254°**; West 400 to the Rampart is **10.1 nm** on **228°**. From the computed
centre at **31°06.256'N 118°30.072'W** the marks lie **3.9 nm** (West 400),
**4.4 nm** (Sniffer) and **6.8 nm** (the Rampart) out — the most compact
three-spot spread on the Baja grid after the Baja - 230 cluster (cameron).

**The zone's northern line runs through a shorter hop than the zone itself.**
The **245** is **7.1 nm** north of West 400 — closer than either of West 400's
own zone-mates — but is parented to [372 / 245 / 250](372-245-250.md), which
notes the same crossing from its side. The **250** is **11.0 nm** and the
**372** **13.4 nm** off West 400 (cameron). Anyone working the north end of this
zone is within a short run of that one.

**Two other zones reach to within 25 nm of the Sniffer.** The **950** is
**21.0 nm** southeast of it and **Lower Cross** **22.2 nm** east, both parented
to the Lower Cross zone — whose third mark, **Catchers Mitt**, is **39.3 nm**
out; **East 400** is **22.1 nm** east-northeast in
[483/500 / 437](483-500-437.md) (cameron). ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source
yet: the Lower Cross zone has no page. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says which of these marks are fished
together, or whether this corner of the grid is run as a circuit at all.

**Two of the names are depth claims and the third is not.** A bank takes the
**fathom depth of its shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`):

| mark | name read as depth | at six feet to a fathom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 300 (The Rampart) | 300 fathoms | about 1,800 ft |
| West 400 | 400 fathoms | about 2,400 ft |
| Sniffer | no number in the name | no claim |

**Read that way the two split across the deepest band the corpus works.** The
swordfish router gives **150–350 fathoms** for the contour lines, thumbs,
notches and humps worth fishing ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)), so the
Rampart's name falls inside that band while West 400's sits **50 fathoms** below
it; SoCal daytime deep-drop baits average **800–1,100 ft**
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)), above the
bottom either name claims. That band is stated for SoCal water, not for this
coast. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms either number as a charted
depth, and nothing says what the shallowest point of any of the three marks
actually reads.

**The 400 pair checks out across a zone line.** The library records East 400 and
West 400 as "both Baja offshore, roughly **44 km** apart east–west at the same
latitude," the stated exception to the landmass-prefix rule (cameron;
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). The charted positions give **23.9 nm —
44.2 km** — so the distance is exact; the latitudes are close rather than
identical, **31°09.800'N** here against **31°13.633'N** for East 400,
**3.8 minutes** apart (cameron). East 400 is parented to
[483/500 / 437](483-500-437.md), so the pair sits either side of a zone
boundary.

**The three 300s are three different places.** The library lists "300" vs "Bell
Bank (300)" vs "300 (The Rampart)" as distinct names rather than a collision
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). The Rampart is the one in this zone; **Bell
Bank (300)** is **37.0 nm** north-northwest of it in
[The Pistol / Bell Bank (300)](pistol-bell-bank-300.md), and the SoCal **300**
at **32°35.750'N 118°00.500'W** is **97.8 nm** away in
[43 / 91 / 300](43-91-300.md) (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says
what "The Rampart" describes — a wall, a ridge line, a steep face — or why the
mark carries a word name on top of its number.

**The Sniffer is the only name in this corner of the grid with no number at
all.** ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source defines the name or says what is on the
bottom there. Searching the corpus on the bare word returns non-geographic
hits — deck talk on a 2010 charter video (`pNNrYXlgkO4`) and a jerkbait line in
a tackle rundown (`WE643Fue1_A`) — and searching on the bare number *300*
returns reel sizes, jig grams and line ratings in gear notes, which is what the
census's depth signal for this zone was made of. Neither the word nor the number
returns a fishing report about this water.

**The general read on a bank is the only structure doctrine available here.**
Against otherwise deep, featureless bottom a bank is an isolated high spot — a
submerged mountain where bait stages for shelter and gamefish follow the bait
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). Standalone structure like this
fishes in **any** direction, but each individual bank still has its own
**preferred directions**, learned spot by spot over repeat trips
([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
bottom composition, relief, contour, high-spot shape, surrounding depth,
preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at any of these three marks.
The `depth_band` is unknown for that reason, not because the water is shallow.

**The nearest documented fish are the better part of seventy miles north.** A
productive stretch in **2020** ran "from the 302 bank down to the hidden bank,"
holding fish for the months before it, with private boats getting bites and
[bluefin](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) to **300 lb** and **30 lb**
[yellowfin](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) mixed in (`49joKHD7Umc`). That
corridor's southern anchor, Hidden Bank, is **66.5–76.6 nm** from the three
marks here (cameron) — this zone lies far past the end of it, and
[Upper 500 / Hidden Bank](upper-500-hidden-bank.md) carries that report.

**Everything else the corpus puts in this water is placed by distance from
Ensenada, and all of it is inshore.** The August 2024 tournament bluefin were
breezing and kite-raised on a bank **42–43 mi** out, about **40 boats** landing
**4 fish on day one and 7 on day two** (`Ix0gG0-l3v0`, `U1AgwmlY5bI`); skipjack
and yellowfin showed around a floating object about **35 mi** out with a
[striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md) jumping at the boat the same trip
(`0bcDBGzQnGw`); yellowfin were reported showing **30–40 mi** out
(`0dIwWiOc1NY`). Those routers carry the material at region level.

**The region's year-round frame is a coastal one and does not reach out here.**
Baja structure from Ensenada southward holds
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) more or less year-round, grade improving
south (cameron) — but that frame is stated for reefs, points and coastal high
spots, and nothing extends it to banks a hundred nautical miles offshore
([Ensenada](ensenada.md)).

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, which way the circuit runs, or what current, tide, light
or swell do here. General reading layers are
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md) and
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md); neither is scoped to this water.

**What the geometry does settle is that this is one stop, not three.** Every
pair is inside **10.8 nm** and the whole zone fits a **6.8 by 12.1 minute** box
(cameron), so a boat that has made the run can grade all three marks without a
second long leg — the one planning fact this page can state without a source.
The 245, **7.1 nm** off the north end, extends that circuit into the next zone.

**The only program the corpus scopes anywhere near this water is the port's, and
it is chart-first rather than spot-first.** The stated Ensenada offshore plan is
to run **20–30 mi due west** to a temperature break, then continue north or west
off it by sea conditions, with a fallback search **20–30 mi north**; trolling
locates fish and the crew deliberately worked away from the fleet
(`0bcDBGzQnGw`; [Ensenada](ensenada.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md)). The marks here sit
**80-plus statute miles** beyond the outer edge of that band on a
west-southwest line (cameron), so they are **positions a boat would arrive at by
following water**, not destinations the corpus sends anyone to.

**No bottom program in the corpus reaches these depths.** The deepest tier of
the [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) jig-size ladder — where the pick
goes to a heavier jig for more time in the strike zone — is named against
Colonet, "the ridge," Alijos and Guadalupe (`LTCFjqtSI8g`), and the Colonet
marks the library charts are **28 and 41 fathoms** ([Colonet](colonet.md)). Read
as fathom names, the shallower of the two numbered marks here is more than seven
times that. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source applies any bottom or vertical
technique to a bank whose name reads in the hundreds of fathoms.

**Nothing here is graded as a compact spot or a search box.** The KB's search
vocabulary splits marks you idle up to and grade in 20–30 minutes from big boxes
that cost hours of looking ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source places any mark in this zone on either side of that split.

## Spots

Three charted spots (cameron). They group as a northern pair plus one mark
offset to the southwest:

- **The northern pair — West 400 and the Sniffer**, **4.8 nm** apart on a
  bearing of **143°** and the tightest pair in the zone (cameron). West 400 is the
  northernmost mark, the deeper-reading name at 400 fathoms, and the one that
  sits **7.1 nm** off the 245 in the next zone; the Sniffer is the southeastern
  mark, the only name in this corner of the grid carrying no number, and the
  closest mark in the zone to the Lower Cross and East 400 grounds.
- **300 (The Rampart)** — the southwest corner, **10.1 nm** from West 400 and
  **10.8 nm** from the Sniffer, the westernmost mark on the Baja grid south of
  31°30'N, and the only one of the three with both a number and a word name.

Charted positions for all three are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
