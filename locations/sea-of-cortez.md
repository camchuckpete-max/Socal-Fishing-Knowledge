# Sea of Cortez

**The Gulf side of Baja — a shallow-rock, structure-ambush fishery that plays
like SoCal calico fishing scaled up.** One angler's summary after an hour on
the rocks: *"this is literally calico fishing on steroids"* (`8IRONtdkceM`).

Three ways in, and they are genuinely different trips:

| Access | What it is | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bahía de los Ángeles** | Drive down, stay in a hotel, day-boat pangas | [BOLA](bahia-de-los-angeles.md) |
| **San Felipe mothership** | Multi-day live-aboard carrying its own pangas | this note |
| **Loreto / Puerto Escondido** | Fly in; island fishery, different species mix | [Loreto](loreto.md) |

Species decision logic lives in the routers —
[cabrilla](../species/cabrilla.md), [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md).
Sea-of-Cortez tackle detail is in
[rod & reel selection](../tackle/rod-and-reel-selection.md#baja-sea-of-cortez-gear-quiver)
and [line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md).

## The San Felipe mothership trip

A **week to ten days** aboard a mothership out of San Felipe that carries
pangas on deck and relocates overnight between zones. Documented here through
a nine-episode Dirty Hookers series filmed **2022** (sign-off states "Tony
Reyes 2022" despite 2023 uploads) plus a companion tackle breakdown.

- **The boat moves at night.** One relocation was an **8–9 hour run** to get
  back to the zone they'd fished on day one, "closer to everything we need to
  be at for the rest of the week" (`aT-fL7vaavY`).
- **Weather governs everything.** That crossing ran **12–15 ft rollers** —
  "some of them coming up to the roof" — and on arrival the pangas stayed on
  deck: *"it's been a little too snotty to take the pangas out"*
  (`aT-fL7vaavY`, `BSNGeduOHYM`).
- **Two fallbacks when the pangas can't launch**, both worth knowing before
  you book:
  1. **Fish the mothership sport-boat style** — "everyone's on the bow slinging
     jigs" (`aT-fL7vaavY`).
  2. **Rotate the pangas that can go.** On a marginal morning only three
     launched, running **three anglers per panga in two-hour sessions** until
     the wind died — "two-hour sessions is pretty quick, but that just means we
     gotta grind really hard" (`BSNGeduOHYM`).
- **Gonzaga Bay** is on the itinerary — the last morning of that trip was
  **deep-dropping for pargo and grouper**, with **sierra** showing beside the
  boat (`0dNfsle54Wo`). Gonzaga tackle parameters from a separate trip are in
  [line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md) (`_Wb4z4ammoM`, `A6s-A1NARuA`).

**A naming caution.** One episode is titled "Wide Open Cabrilla Fishing! Bahía
de Los Ángeles" while its own narration says *"I was gone aboard the Tony Reyes
out of San Felipe"* — and the same narrator also calls the grounds "Bay of LA"
(`4Dn7yChmhk0`). Because the mothership relocates for hours at a time, both
labels may be partly true for a single trip. **Treat a San Felipe mothership
episode as San Felipe unless the grounds are named on camera** — do not file
its content under [BOLA](bahia-de-los-angeles.md), which is a
drive-in day-boat fishery with different logistics entirely.

## How the fishing works

- **Find the structure.** *"We're just looking for nice boiling rocks, good
  structure — find the structure"* (`8IRONtdkceM`). Same read as the
  [cabrilla](../species/cabrilla.md) router's boiler-rock and dark-spot
  doctrine.
- **It is genuinely shallow.** Fish were taken in **six feet of water**, which
  is what drives the line class: *"we're in six feet of water… I see now why we
  fish 80 [lb]. I think we would have lost a lot of them already"*
  (`8IRONtdkceM`). The rocks, not the fish, set the tackle.
- **Volume is the character of it.** "We've been on the panga for less than an
  hour, I think I have six or seven." Grade ran **12–15 lb** with the crew
  hoping to break 15 (`8IRONtdkceM`, `0dNfsle54Wo`).
- **Golden cabrilla** (a distinct colour morph) turn up — "two goldens on board
  within the first hour" (`4Dn7yChmhk0`).
- **Bycatch** is constant: triggerfish, yellowtail, sierra.

### What got bit

- **Jerkbaits**, with a specific trigger worth having:
  > *"When I get really close to a rock with a jerkbait I'll go, and then pause
  > for a half second… and on that pause they just launch out of the water for
  > it."* (`ts9rJgxjmSg`)

  A **pause placed next to the rock**, not a steady retrieve, is what draws the
  strike. This is the *why* behind the
  [cabrilla](../species/cabrilla.md) router's cast-tight-to-the-rock row.
- **Surface iron works on cabrilla** — "surface iron cabrilla baby" — and the
  hookset doctrine holds in Baja: *"don't swing, just wind — it's a surface
  iron"* (`ZYAILGyrkRk`). That is an independent Baja corroboration of the
  wind-through-not-swing rule in
  [surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md).
  **Trade-off recorded:** the same angler switched off it because "surface iron
  recovery is so bad" — on a fast, high-volume shallow bite, the slow retrieve
  costs you casts. Kept beside, not merged into, the surface-iron doctrine.
- **Wax Wing** "has gotten bit every cast" (`ZYAILGyrkRk`). Noted because
  [calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md) and
  [inshore crankbaits](../techniques/inshore-crankbaits.md) both carry a
  **⚠ Wax Wing flagged stub** for having no corpus source — this is a Baja
  data point on the lure, not the SoCal calico execution the stub asks for, so
  the stub stands.
- **Yellowtail follow rather than commit** — "you gotta follow, yellowtail
  follows" (`ZYAILGyrkRk`).

## Gear notes from these trips

- **80 lb** line for shallow rock cabrilla (above).
- **Bass gear is the framing** — the series is titled "grouper on bass gear"
  and "big shallow grouper vs bass rods." Full quiver in
  [rod & reel selection](../tackle/rod-and-reel-selection.md).
- **Carry spare split rings and assist/stinger hooks.** Replacing a split ring
  mid-session comes up repeatedly (`ts9rJgxjmSg`, `81YHbZQFzgk`) — the rocks
  and the volume chew hardware.
- Captain named across the series: **Joel** (rendered "Joelle"/"hoell" in
  captions — asr-uncertain).
