# Puerto Escondido Marina

**[Baja only]** — the marina a little south of Loreto that the
[Loreto & Puerto Escondido](loreto.md) zone runs out of: the documented launch,
the bait stop, and the place a broken boat gets towed back to (`n4nMCLLW3tY`,
`ikiXx9sUsoA`). It is an **access point, not a fishing ground** — nothing in the
corpus is caught in the basin or off its mouth.

**The zone is measured from here.** The north end of Isla Carmen is *"about 20
miles, probably an hour to get there"* from this marina,
[Isla Monserrat](isla-monserrat-north-end.md) is the run beyond it, and
[Honeymoon Bay](honeymoon-bay.md) is named by sight on the way out
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`, `ikiXx9sUsoA`). Every run out of here is stated in **statute
miles**, never nautical — see
[the zone's structure section](loreto.md#structure--bathymetry).

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
That library stops at San Quintín on the Pacific side and holds nothing on this
coast, so a conditions pull has to run against [the zone](loreto.md) rather than
against this harbour.

## Getting there

**You fly in, and the boat is waiting.** One documented trip reached the zone by
air through **CBX**, the Cross Border Xpress bridge into the Tijuana airport,
rather than by road (`ikiXx9sUsoA`) — the practical break from
[Cortez North](cortez-north.md), which is drive-only. The full access picture is
on [the zone page](loreto.md#getting-there).

**Two documented programs use this marina:**

- **Launch and return the same day.** The third fishing day of one trip started
  here, with the run planned out to the north end of Isla Carmen
  (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **Working base for a mothership operation.** A **300–308 ft** vessel lay in
  the zone with an **18 ft** Grady White towed behind it on a custom
  cleat-to-cleat rope; the crew lived aboard, fished off the Grady White, and
  worked out of this marina (`ikiXx9sUsoA`). The first afternoon's run out
  followed fuelling (`ikiXx9sUsoA`).

**The catch comes back to a dock to be cleaned before it is cooked at the
hotel** (`ikiXx9sUsoA`, `n4nMCLLW3tY`) — the corpus does not say the cleaning
facility is this marina's, only that the practice is the zone's
([bleeding & general care](../fish-care/dorado-and-general.md)).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md), [the zone page](loreto.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**A harbour basin is what it is, and the corpus describes none of it.** The only
positional facts on the record are relational — the marina sits a little south
of Loreto, and boats leave it heading out to the island cluster (`ikiXx9sUsoA`,
`n4nMCLLW3tY`). No breakwater, mouth, channel, bottom or depth is given.

**The zone's shallow soundings are not this basin's.** The **5–10 ft** cabrilla
rock, the **30 ft** a boat looked down and found on a bird chase, and the
**10 ft** called on the sounder with structure visible through the water were
all recorded against the islands, not here (`n4nMCLLW3tY`, `ikiXx9sUsoA`); they
sit on [the zone page](loreto.md#structure--bathymetry) and do not transfer to
this ground.

## How it fishes

**Treat it as a bait stop and a start line, not water to work.**

**Order bait before the morning you need it.** The sardine order is placed with
the vendor rather than picked up on arrival, and the one time the corpus records
that going wrong the crew was rescued by the vendor still being out front
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`). The vendor's own rig for the bait — a small-to-medium hook
through the nose and eyes so it keeps swimming, fished with the weight
underneath rather than the bait on top — is on
[the zone page](loreto.md#how-it-fishes) and in
[fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md).

**The bait vendor is also the zone's intel.** His report put dorado close to
shore at Punta Perico, under diving birds and around weed, caught roughly
**10:20–11:00**, and the crew fished that report the same morning
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`). See [dorado](../species/dorado.md) and
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md).

**A breakdown out of here is a radio call, not a self-rescue.** A shift-linkage
pin sheared at the start of the 20-mile run to the north end of Isla Carmen; the
crew paddled and swam the boat back toward the marina while a second boat was
called in over the radio to tow it on the cleat-to-cleat rope, the trollers were
left out during the tow, and the day ended at the mechanic (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).

**Leaving here is not necessarily returning here.** One evening's troll was
called off as too dark and the boat ran north for shelter and an overnight
crossing rather than back to the marina (`ikiXx9sUsoA`).
