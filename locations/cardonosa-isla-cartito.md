# Cardonosa / "Isla Cartito"

**[Baja only]** — the one island in the
[Bahía de los Ángeles](bahia-de-los-angeles.md) zone that the corpus names on
camera while fishing it: a **rising shelf** that comes up to **60 ft** "a ways
off" the island, worked on what the crew took to be its **south side**
(`odiIWmut6gM`). The documented stop was a
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) stop, fished on the jig.

⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): the island is named twice in one breath — first
**"Cardonosa"**, repeated, then **"Isla Cartito"** (`odiIWmut6gM`). One of the
two renderings is auto-caption garble, the KB carries no charted position for
either, and this page publishes both rather than picking one. Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md) alongside the same flag
on [the zone page](bahia-de-los-angeles.md#structure--bathymetry).

**It carries no charted position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).** That library stops at San
Quintín on the Pacific side and carries nothing on this coast, so a conditions
pull has to run against
[the zone](bahia-de-los-angeles.md#structure--bathymetry) rather than against
this island.

The whole record here is **one stop, on day four of one trip** whose month is
never established on camera (`odiIWmut6gM`). Read the page as that stop, not as
the island's fishery.

## Getting there

**You arrive in the zone's panga program, and the crew navigates none of it.**
Every documented day in this zone is fished from a local panga that collects you
at your hotel room before dawn, with the captain choosing the ground — the
drive, the lodging, the operators and the day shape are all on
[the zone page](bahia-de-los-angeles.md#getting-there).

**The documented arrival was a mid-morning move, not a run from the ramp.** Day
four opened casting a shallower high spot; after *"a considerable amount of time
casting"* the crew tied the slow periods to that day's tide, asked the captain
to go looking in deeper water for yellows, and that move is what put them here
(`odiIWmut6gM`). See [tide & slack](../conditions/tide-and-slack.md).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md), [the zone page](bahia-de-los-angeles.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**A rising shelf with 60 ft on top, fished off the island rather than against
it.** The crew's own description of the mark is *"kind of like a rising shelf.
It's at 60 ft a ways off… probably the south south side of it"* (`odiIWmut6gM`).
The hedge is part of the record: they state the side they were on as a guess,
not as knowledge.

**How this shelf relates to the day's earlier ground is unresolved.** The same
morning started on a high spot the captions render as "La Rasa", called at
**40–60 ft** (`odiIWmut6gM`), and the move here was described only as going to
deeper water — yet the mark they then name is called at 60 ft as well. The
corpus never says whether the "La Rasa" high spot lies off this island, whether
the 60 ft figure is the shelf's crown or the water the fish were jigged in, or
how deep it goes on the outside.

shelf, what it drops into, or any sounder picture at this mark. Nothing in the
record describes what the meter showed
([electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

## How it fishes

**The tide read is what sends you looking, and deeper water was the answer.**
The crew's correlation between a particular tide and the trip's earlier slow
periods is what prompted the move off the shallow stop to hunt yellows deeper,
and it produced (`odiIWmut6gM`). That is one crew's read on one trip, not a
stated zone rule — [tide & slack](../conditions/tide-and-slack.md).

**The documented program on the shelf is the vertical jig.** The 28–29 lb fish
ate a **200 g** jig fished on a jig stick built on a Calstar 600-class blank
with a Penn Torque-class reel — the captions render the reel "Pen Tour Classic",
so treat the model name as unverified (`odiIWmut6gM`). Weights and shapes are in
[knife jigs](../lures/knife-jigs.md); the retrieve is
[knife / speed jigging](../techniques/knife-jigging.md), and the zone's wider
**150–300 ft** jigging band sits on
[the zone page](bahia-de-los-angeles.md#how-it-fishes).

**Birds moved the boat off the shelf.** *"The birds are bombing"* was answered
with *"we move"*, and the school under them was firecracker yellows that stayed
on the boat — hooked fish brought followers up with them, and both surface iron
and a plastic got bit through what the crew called a hot bite (`odiIWmut6gM`).
General method is [bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md) and
[search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md). ⚠ Fact-check
(single-source): the source never says whether that bird school was still over
this island's ground — one of the anglers asks *"where are we at? Where's this
place?"* on the school — so the bird-school fish may belong to unnamed water
nearby rather than to this shelf.

**One fight on the school was called clean.** The angler landing a fish there
called *"no rocks"* (`odiIWmut6gM`), which is the opposite of the zone's
shallow-rock program, where fish break anglers off and the answer is heavier
line ([the zone page](bahia-de-los-angeles.md#shallow--cast-and-wind-fast),
[line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md)). One call on one fish, not an
established property of this mark.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: line class, leader, hook, jig colour or drift
setup at this stop; a tide, moon or current *window* for the shelf; how long to
give it before moving; or how it fishes for anyone not on a local panga.
