# San Diego Artificial Reefs

The built-structure zone off central San Diego: **18 charted waypoints** in the
spot library (cameron), in three groups — Torrey Pines AR 1–2, the twelve-point
Pacific Beach AR grid, and four Mission Bay Park structures. It is really **two
fisheries under one zone label**. The coastal series outside the beach are the
anchored-chum artificial-reef program that runs the whole SoCal built-reef
system ([chunking](../techniques/chunking.md)); the Mission Bay Park structures
are inside the bay, and a bay is a tidal system first
([bays and harbors](bays-and-harbors.md)).

## Getting there

**Mission Bay is the port on this stretch** — Dana Landing is a named Mission
Bay launch ramp (cameron). The four Mission Bay Park structures are inside the
bay and need no ocean crossing at all; Torrey Pines and Pacific Beach sit
outside on the coastal strip.

Artificial reefs generally sit in **shallow water close to the harbors**, which
is what makes them a short run and also why they have been fished hard for
years (`f4qYtHACGyk`).

**MPA caution.** The spot library carries two MPA edges on this stretch of
coast, both on adjacent La Jolla-area coastal spots rather than on the reefs:
stay **north of 32°49.573'N** at Windansea, and stay **south of 32°47.945'N**
at Crystal Pier (cameron). Crystal Pier (32°47.580'N 117°16.580'W) sits on
essentially the same longitude as the Pacific Beach AR grid, and the grid's
northernmost waypoint, 4B at **32°47.933'N 117°16.583'W**, lies about
**0.012' of latitude** south of that stated line (cameron). Latitude
proximity alone does not establish a boundary. California / CDFW — verify
current MPA boundaries, closures and take rules with CDFW before fishing
(as of 2026-08).

## Structure & bathymetry

**Built structure, not natural bottom.** The SoCal artificial-reef system was
built specifically for sport fishermen in the **1950s–70s**, funded by
sportfishing money rather than by a commercial-fishing program
(`bM2vUS1B-yQ`). What that buys is fish-holding relief in shallow water within
reach of the harbors, and because the reefs have been fished for years the
fish still on them are the ones most anglers fail to catch rather than the
ones that are not there (`f4qYtHACGyk`).

**The three groups**, north to south (cameron):

- **Torrey Pines AR** — two waypoints, **32°53.583'N 117°15.583'W** (AR 2) and
  **32°53.200'N 117°15.833'W** (AR 1) (cameron); the northern end of the zone, and a
  named **sub-pixel cluster** in the [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md).
- **Pacific Beach AR** — a lettered/numbered grid of **twelve** waypoints,
  rows 1–4 running south to north (**32°47.333'N** at 1A up to
  **32°47.933'N** at 4B) and columns A–C running west to east
  (**117°16.917'W** at 4A in to **117°16.200'W** at 1C and 2C). The whole grid
  fits inside about 0.6' of latitude and 0.7' of longitude — one water cell
  for a conditions lookup, twelve distinct drops for navigation
  ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).
- **Mission Bay Park** — four in-bay structures: the **Artificial Kelp Reef**
  (32°46.200'N 117°16.067'W), the **NEL Tower** (32°46.367'N 117°16.050'W),
  the **Ingraham St Bridge Rubble** (32°45.930'N 117°16.278'W) and the **Pier
  Pilings** (32°45.850'N 117°16.517'W). These are the bay's own inventory —
  bridge, pilings, rubble — read the way [bays and harbors](bays-and-harbors.md)
  reads a bay: find the feature, then fish the **edge** where two features
  meet, and expect the tide rather than the swell to switch it on.

Charted positions for all eighteen are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).

## How it fishes

**Two programs, split by the jetty.**

**Outside — anchor, chum, fish light.** The coastal series are fished on the
anchored-chum program; execution lives in
[chunking](../techniques/chunking.md):

- **Build prey density, do not just feed.** Periodically add another handful
  of cut chunks to raise the prey-density level; past a certain density even
  fish that were not feeding come over and eat because they see the others
  doing it, which is what turns a slow pick into a frenzy (`f4qYtHACGyk`),
  stated as a general SoCal principle rather than a reef-only trick
  (`BdRX4b8Fo5w`). A private boat that cannot carry sportboat-scale bait gets
  the same effect from a chum tube (`JK2-cpaXkI8`).
- **Never chum off the stern.** At anchor the current runs down the *sides* of
  the boat, so hand chum goes as far up the side as you can throw it
  (`yKaHcxX46l4`), and a chum bucket is tied off at the bow by the anchor for
  the same reason (`xI9tPJFXbUM`, `bM2vUS1B-yQ`). Cut squid heads, tentacles
  and guts are the cheap chum source (`yKaHcxX46l4`).
- **Fish far lighter than instinct says.** The stated #1 mistake at artificial
  reefs is fishing way too heavy. A **No. 4–No. 6** hook (`bM2vUS1B-yQ`) tied
  straight to mono, a **1/16 oz** split shot 2–3 in above it by default,
  **1/8 oz** only if current demands it, **never heavier than 1/4 oz**, and a
  spaghetti-thin strip of bait rather than a wad (`f4qYtHACGyk`). Heavier puts
  the bait on the bottom, which draws shovelnose guitarfish and bat rays
  instead of the target species (`f4qYtHACGyk`). Gear class is light spinning
  or a small level-wind ([gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md)).
- For sheephead specifically the terminal detail is a
  [sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md) kept off the bottom — see
  the [sheephead](../species/sheephead.md) router.

**Inside — fish the tide, not the clock.** The Mission Bay Park structures are
bay structure, and harbor spotted bay bass are **fully tide-driven** rather
than current-driven like the calico on the outside
([bays and harbors](bays-and-harbors.md),
[tide and slack](../conditions/tide-and-slack.md)). Tide-swing thresholds,
the slack window and the plastics-vs-crank selection live in the
[spotted bay bass](../species/spotted-bay-bass.md) router. One in-bay method
belongs to this kind of spot: catch whatever small baitfish is around the
bridge or dock on a sabiki, then fish that same bait live on the spot
(`AH4Eiya1Hn0`) — see [fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md).

## Spots

Eighteen charted waypoints in three groups (cameron). A numbered waypoint in
an artificial-reef series carries no fishing identity of its own, so the
series — not the individual waypoint — is the unit a day plan works with, and
both coastal series publish as single complex pages carrying their coordinate
tables. The four Mission Bay Park structures are named, distinct features and
stand on their own.

- **Torrey Pines AR** — 1–2, the northern pair.
- **Pacific Beach AR** — the 1A–4C grid, twelve waypoints in one water cell.
- **Mission Bay Park** — Artificial Kelp Reef, NEL Tower, Ingraham St Bridge
  Rubble, Pier Pilings; in-bay structure, the only group here a corpus source
  names.

No waypoint in the zone carries corpus detail beyond its position, except the
Ingraham St Bridge (`AH4Eiya1Hn0`).
