# San Clemente Island — Back Side

The ocean-facing side of San Clemente Island, running N→S from the 9 Fathom
Spot at the northwest end to Desperation Reef at the southeast end, with 12
charted spots (cameron). It is **the squid fishery**: on an April 2022 bite off
the east end the yellowtail graded high teens to high 30s on a 20–25 lb average
(`YZtX1MiT0y8`). The island is an overnight-range destination rather than a
full-day run (`LTCFjqtSI8g`), it is a U.S. Navy training range with rotating
closures ([island structure](island-structure.md#san-clemente-island--navy-controlled-access)),
and one of its charted spots sits inside a naval security zone (cameron). It is
also the inshore edge of a fall offshore game: the corpus's backside bluefin and
kelp-paddy dorado sessions are worked off this side rather than on its beach
(`r4J5nP5Bkl4`).

## Getting there

**Plan it as an overnight, not a day trip.** San Clemente Island sits at
overnight range with Santa Barbara Island, unlike the full-day-accessible
Coronado Islands, Channel Islands and Catalina — which is why the jig stick runs
a 50 lb top shot here rather than the 40 lb used closer in (`LTCFjqtSI8g`,
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)). The crossing is described as
roughly 60 miles straight off the beach (`XyKF34C5iB4`).

**Check the Navy schedule before you leave.** The island is a training range,
not open coastline, and which zones are open or closed rotates with the Navy's
schedule rather than being fixed; the access check is a standard pre-trip item
alongside safety prep — full statement and as-of date in
[island structure](island-structure.md#san-clemente-island--navy-controlled-access)
(`XyKF34C5iB4`). **Verify current access with the Navy before running the
crossing.**

**Access edge — as recorded in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) as
of 2026-08** (cameron):

| spot | rule |
| --- | --- |
| Castle Rock | naval security zone — not fishable |

**This is the side that holds its water when the north blows out.** In a big
northwest event the northern Channel Islands and the LA/Ventura coastal shelf
roll over 5–8 °F, while the Coronados, the tuna grounds, Catalina and San
Clemente Island held through it (`YZtX1MiT0y8`,
[April](../seasonal/april.md), [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)). An ugly
northern forecast is therefore not automatically a reason to cancel this zone.

## Structure & bathymetry

The shore alternates points, coves and kelp: the fathom-named high spots at each
end (9 Fathom Spot northwest, 29 Fathom Spot southeast), the coves and dunes of
West Cove, The Dunes and Seal Cove, the run of points at Eel Point, Mail Point
and Lost Point, and the southeast end at China Point, The Caves and Desperation
Reef (cameron). **Kelp edges are the working shallow feature** — the corpus's
bass and bonito reads here are both edge-of-kelp reads (`9xNhdu2aBqE`,
`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`).

**Current here does not read off one island-wide direction.** Two named San
Clemente Island spots close enough together to be a short run apart can show
**opposite current directions on the same day** — one running downhill toward
San Diego, the other uphill — with no explained cause, and this is framed as
routine at this island rather than a rare anomaly (`sIoNELGlxmk`,
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). That breaks the usual
shortcut of pre-judging the whole day's spot list off one current read
([island structure](island-structure.md)).

**Desperation Reef is a bank read, not a shoreline read.** It is worked as a
high spot to anchor on, and the reason given for the fish holding there is that
bait squid were staged on the bank rather than anything about the island's
shoreline orientation (`Rb5I2ljAqeE`). ⚠ Flagged gap — the same source calls
that ground the "West End tuna zone" while
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) charts Desperation Reef at the
southeast end of this side's N→S run; the naming is carried as recorded, not
reconciled.

**Sharp offshore edges sit outside this side in late fall.** About 8 mi off the
island on 2022-11-23 a **63 → 61.5 °F break inside roughly 1 mi** carried sign
on the edge with the fish deep and sonar-dependent (`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`,
[November–December](../seasonal/november-december.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: charted depths, bottom composition, kelp
extent and working depth bands for the individual back-side spots; no charted
rockfish pinnacle is assigned to this side.

## How it fishes

**Read the water colour and the current together before you pick an edge.** The
kelp-edge bass read here is a stained-water read, not a clean-water one: a
green-stained, near-opaque colour at roughly **5–10 ft visibility** gives the
fish a place to hide and makes them comfortable feeding up in the open, and it
is paired with moving current judged off whether the kelp lies down under the
surface or floats slack on top (`9xNhdu2aBqE`,
[water color](../conditions/water-color.md)).

**Late in the fall this side inverts the general current rule.** The prior
across the region is that current parallel to the shoreline is best, into the
beach is marginal and off the beach is dead; on 2022-11-20 at this island the
bass bite **required** current running into the island with off-colour water,
the fish using the dirt to ambush against the structure. Encode parallel as the
prior, into-the-island as a viable late-fall/off-colour state, off-the-beach as
dead — a seasonal nuance from the same author, kept side by side rather than
reconciled (`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`,
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md),
[November–December](../seasonal/november-december.md)).

**Do not pre-judge the day's spots off one current read.** Because two spots a
short run apart on this island can run opposite directions on the same day,
the region-wide spatial-coherence shortcut does not hold here — check the
current at the spot (`sIoNELGlxmk`).

**When the squid bite dies, relocate the forage before you leave.** A squid-zone
bite that shuts off is a relocation problem, not an absence problem: the squid
dropped out of the depth you were fishing, so follow it down or move to where it
stacked, reading the squid on the meter before writing the spot off
(`YZtX1MiT0y8`, [April](../seasonal/april.md),
[electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**On the anchor, watch the bow.** Anchored at this island, chum gets pushed
*up-current* toward the bow by the fish themselves — birds locking on up there
mark the school, and the surface iron goes under them (`DnSqw4r7A1s`,
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). The source does not
say which side of the island; carried as an island-level read.

**Gear up for the grade, not the average.** Two independent corpus records point
the same way: the jig stick runs a 50 lb top shot here specifically because an
overnight-range island carries a trophy shot at any moment (`LTCFjqtSI8g`), and
off this side in June 2023 anglers geared for 50–70 lb bluefin found themselves
into 70–90 lb fish on 30 and 40 lb test and paid for it in break-offs and a
leader parted boatside (`r4J5nP5Bkl4`,
[fighting a big bluefin](../techniques/fighting-big-bluefin.md)).

## Spots

Twelve charted spots, grouped as the run reads N→S:

- **Northwest end** — 9 Fathom Spot, Castle Rock, West Cove, The Dunes. Castle
  Rock is a naval security zone and is not fishable (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap —
  no corpus source: how any of these four fish.
- **Mid back side** — Eel Point, Seal Cove, Mail Point, Lost Point. ⚠ Flagged
  gap — no corpus source names any of these four.
- **Southeast end** — China Point, The Caves, 29 Fathom Spot, Desperation Reef.
  This is the "east end" of the corpus's two dated back-side reports — the April
  2022 squid-and-yellowtail bite and the November 2022 off-colour bass and
  bonito day (`YZtX1MiT0y8`, `Z3rZqy2Pi8E`) — and Desperation Reef is the anchor
  spot of the spring bluefin session (`Rb5I2ljAqeE`).

**Name collision.** This China Point is a distinct spot from Catalina's China
Point; the southeast-wind doctrine recorded in
[island structure](island-structure.md) is about Catalina's, not this one. West
Cove is likewise a distinct spot from Catalina's West Cove.

Charted positions for all twelve spots are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md); the per-spot pages carry them.
