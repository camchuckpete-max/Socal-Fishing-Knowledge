# The "lower banks"

**[Baja only]** — **The San Diego long-range fleet's giant-tuna ground, and
the heaviest grade of fish anywhere in this KB.** "The lower banks" is a fleet
name for an area rather than a charted mark: it is narrated on camera as the
water running from **Magdalena Bay** down toward **Cabo San Lucas**, where
giant tuna of **130s to 350 lb** move up every fall to breed and spawn
(`UuyqTE21-kc`). The ground sits in
[Baja Pacific — South](baja-pacific-south.md), which the census gives no zone
of its own, so this page hangs off the region rung the same way
[The Ridge / Uncle Sam Bank](ridge-uncle-sam-bank.md) does — the ground one
boat worked back up toward after its days down here (`UuyqTE21-kc`). It is
offshore of, and separate from,
[Magdalena Bay](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md)'s own banks and the
[Cabo San Lucas](cabo-san-lucas.md) charter water at the far end of the span.

**No bank on this ground is ever named or placed.** The charted sections of
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) stop at San Quintín, so this page
publishes no coordinates and a day plan cannot pull per-spot conditions here;
the run distance is the only geographic figure the corpus gives
(`pk2blktDQ9Y`). ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): whether "the lower banks"
corresponds to charted banks, and which ones — the corpus offers a narrated
span, one run distance and no name, position or sounding for any individual
bank ([fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md)).

**The fish are logged as [yellowfin](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) and are
never named on camera.** ⚠ Fact-check (single-source): the species
identification in the dock-day footage rests on the crew's "cow" language and
on triage evidence, not on an on-camera call (`ASitOLYzFEA`) — the same
standing flag the species router carries
([yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md#where--when)). The grade is
flagged rather than demoted because it is the largest in the KB by a wide
margin.

## Getting there

**The only access the corpus records is a San Diego long-range boat.** The
stated run is roughly **608 nm** south of San Diego on an 11-day trip
(`pk2blktDQ9Y`), which puts this ground well beyond
[Alijos Rocks](alijos-rocks.md) and the Ridge — the two grounds the region's
six-, seven- and eight-day trips fish
([Baja Pacific — South](baja-pacific-south.md#access--range)). A trip that
fishes here can also work back up toward the Ridge on the same run
(`UuyqTE21-kc`).

**The bait load is trip-scale, not day-scale.** The dockside load for the
11-day run was sardine and green mackerel — including stock held over from a
prior trip, described on camera as six-day-old cured bait — at roughly **700
scoops** (`pk2blktDQ9Y`), against the roughly **20 scoops** a 360-gallon
private-boat tank typically carries for an offshore day
([bait tanks](../bait/bait-tanks.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a sea-state or weather envelope for this
ground, a bait stop along the way, a departure time, or any access other than
a San Diego long-range boat.

**Regulatory — Mexico.** Mexican entry and sport-fishing paperwork applies to
every trip that reaches here: see [Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md) and the
region's [paperwork gate](baja-pacific-south.md#access--range). Checked as of
2026-08; **verify current requirements before you fish**. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: the Mexican (CONAPESCA) bag limit, size gauge, gear rule or
closed season for giant yellowfin on this ground
([regulatory claims](../sources/regulatory-claims.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The corpus names the ground and never measures it.** No source gives a
sounding, a bottom composition, a rise-from depth, a bank name or the number
of banks the plural covers; "banks" and the Magdalena-Bay-to-Cabo span are the
whole of it (`UuyqTE21-kc`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any depth,
relief or bottom description for this ground.

**What the meter does show here is fish, not structure.** Ahead of one
morning's bite the marks were read as big fish — reportedly up to **200
lb-plus** — with fleet boats nearby already hanging big fish; the same
evening's brief set the line class for the next day (`pk2blktDQ9Y`; [How it
fishes](#how-it-fishes)). On another day a large **bait ball sitting directly
under the boat**, with fish blowing up all around it on the surface, was the
crew's stated reason for holding position rather than moving grounds; whether
the ball was found on sonar or by surface sign is not stated on camera
(`Fq4aRI3YrKE`). General method:
[electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md) and
[search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).

**In practice the fish here hold to a school, not to a mark.** One boat spent
a couple of days searching, locked onto a single school of giant tuna and then
anchored on that one spot for four days without moving (`ASitOLYzFEA`) — the
behaviour that makes this a ground you find and then stay on, described under
[How it fishes](#how-it-fishes).

`Fq4aRI3YrKE`, `UuyqTE21-kc`) and one dock-day trip (`ASitOLYzFEA`) — so
"season" here is that window and not a month-by-month picture.

## How it fishes

**Surface baits held up by lift, not by the troll.** The presentation
recorded here is a **flying-fish bait floated downwind of the boat under a
helium balloon**, which keeps it riding on the surface looking alive; multiple
hookups came on flyers fished that way (`UuyqTE21-kc`). The kite program on
the same fishery runs **whole squid on a double-hook rig**, called very
popular for this grade, with no hook size, spacing or leader weight given on
camera (`pk2blktDQ9Y`). Execution and the balloon-versus-kite distinction are
in [kite fishing](../techniques/kite-fishing.md).

**Start the morning at 100 lb.** The pre-bite brief for this grade sets
**100 lb test as the floor** — "don't go in the water with anything less than
a hundred pounds" — with a step down to **80 lb** only after the grade has
been assessed, never as the morning's starting point, and **60 lb ruled out
entirely** (`pk2blktDQ9Y`;
[line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md)). That ladder is written for
giant grade and is a different fishery from the 40–50 lb Cedros yellowtail
brackets in the same note.

**Budget about 45 minutes per fish, and know what runs the clock.** On a
**100 lb top shot over 100 lb Spectra backing** a fight runs roughly **45
minutes, maxing out**, and is called "kind of iffy" past that; what drives it
is how many times the fish rolls onto its side and drags the line across its
mouth and teeth, an abrasion mechanism rather than a fixed clock
(`pk2blktDQ9Y`; [fighting a big fish](../techniques/fighting-big-bluefin.md)).
Boat-side, one hooked fish tangled on the **sonar dome** and took a crew
member into the water repeatedly to free it, and a second fish's line was
spliced twice mid-fight (`ASitOLYzFEA`) — the handling hazard the species
router carries at giant grade
([yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md#landing--handling)).

**Find the school and stay on it.** The recorded pattern is search, then
commit: a couple of days looking, then four days anchored on one school,
in the crew's words never moving until the boat "became a big floating life
pad" (`ASitOLYzFEA`). The stated trigger for holding rather than running was a
bait ball under the boat with fish blowing up around it (`Fq4aRI3YrKE`), and
the crew on that trip planned to sit tight at least one more day so the rest
of the boat got a shot (`Fq4aRI3YrKE`).

**Two seasonal markers, and they are not the same claim.** One captain holds
the same **late-November** slot every year specifically because that is when
the bigger fish show up here (`ASitOLYzFEA`); a separate trip framed a fish
taken before Halloween as the "first super cow of the year", a first-sign
marker rather than a peak (`Fq4aRI3YrKE`). They describe different points on
the same fall run and are kept side by side rather than merged: the late-
November claim is a stated annual practice, the pre-Halloween one a single
season's framing, and nothing in the corpus tests either against a third
trip.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: current, tide, moon, wind or light-window
doctrine on this ground, how the boat searches on arrival, or what ends a
stop. Every stated gate on this page is trip length and season.
