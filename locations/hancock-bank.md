# Hancock Bank

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is two charted banks in open water at the
southwest corner of the [Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md) —
**Hancock Bank** at **32°34.100'N 119°41.967'W** and **Northeast Bank** at
**32°20.800'N 119°38.400'W** (cameron). They lie **13.6 nm** apart: Northeast
Bank bears **167°** from Hancock and Hancock bears **347°** back, a
north-northwest/south-southeast line with **13.3 minutes** of the separation in
latitude and **3.6 minutes** in longitude.

**No corpus source names either bank.** Neither "Hancock" nor "Northeast Bank"
appears in a transcript, a knowledge note or an evidence file; the only places
the two names occur in the repo are the coordinate lines in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) and the flagged stubs on
neighbouring pages ([Tanner Bank](tanner-bank.md),
[Sverdrup Bank (126)](sverdrup-bank-126.md)). The zone exists on coordinates
(cameron), so this is a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the two
banks are, what water they sit in, what their names do and do not claim, and
what a source would have to supply. The [region page](socal-bight.md) already
carries this zone as a flagged stub, and nothing here lifts that stub. The
flags below aggregate in [the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

**This is a zone by attachment, not by cluster.** At **13.6 nm** the pair is
past the census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, so the two never clustered:
Hancock was left over as a lone bank and minted a zone in its own name, and
Northeast Bank then attached under the **25 nm** rule that puts a lone bank in
the nearest zone it could realistically be fished with (cameron). Neither could
join [Tanner Bank](tanner-bank.md), the zone that holds the corpus material for
this water: Hancock sits **29.7 nm** from Tanner Bank and **29.8 nm** from
Cortes Bank, Northeast Bank **26.4 nm** from Cortes and **33.3 nm** from Tanner
(cameron), all of them past the attach radius. Read the pairing as an arithmetic
outcome, not as a claim that the two fish alike.

**Northeast Bank is the southwest corner of the charted library, and its name
has no stated referent.** Of the 391 positions in the spot library not one lies
both south and west of it, and nothing charted at all sits in the arc from due
south round to west-northwest inside **59.8 nm**, where Sverdrup Bank is the
first mark to appear (cameron). The name is directional but every neighbour it
could be named against lies the other way — Cortes bears **077°** from it,
Tanner **050°**, Hancock **347°**. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what
Northeast Bank is northeast of, or who Hancock was named for.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a stated distance, heading, run time, fuel
figure or sea-state envelope to either bank from any port, and no source names a
port, ramp or landing this zone is fished out of. Nothing in the corpus records
anyone fishing here at all.

**The nearest water the corpus does describe reaching is Tanner and Cortez, and
every account of that run is an overnight one.** Cortez Bank is given as
**96 mi** southwest of San Pedro and **111 mi** west of Point Loma
(`QSvzVHW9UMk`), and as **~110–115 mi** from Fisherman's Landing in San Diego —
"a full overnight sportboat run" (`UfuiWFVvz2E`), with the fuller table of stated
runs on [Tanner Bank](tanner-bank.md). Cortes lies **26.4 nm** east-northeast of
Northeast Bank and **29.8 nm** east-southeast of Hancock (cameron), so anything
that reaches these two marks is that run plus another leg. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source states that leg.

**Nothing else is close.** The nearest charted land in the spot library is San
Nicolas Island, **40.4 nm** from Hancock at **015°** (Dutch Harbor) and
**52.6 nm** from Northeast Bank; the back side of San Clemente Island is
**61.2 nm** from Hancock and **64.6 nm** from Northeast Bank, and Catalina over
**75 nm** from either (cameron). **Every mainland-side position in the library
is over 110 nm away** — the nearest to either mark is Pelican Point off Crystal
Cove at **110.6 nm** from Hancock (cameron). This is a zone with no fallback
inside a long run in any direction.

**Check the swell upstream, not just the point forecast.** The worked example in
the forecast curriculum clicks the West End of San Nicolas Island and finds a
benign local wind picture hiding a swell that climbs from **4 ft** on a Thursday
afternoon to over **12 ft** by Monday, generated by a system up off Oregon days
earlier (`zKmZ4zql2ws`) — which is why a **48–72 h** upstream fetch scan belongs
in front of any run to the outside
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md#upstream-fetch-check-swell-arrives-after-the-wind-that-made-it)).
That example is drawn at an island **40.4 nm** inshore of Hancock (cameron), and
nothing out here is sheltered by land in any direction.

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries, military schedules
or restricted areas stated for either position. The corpus names the **Cow Cod
Conservation Area** as an offshore closure with its own depth limit and its own
as-of date ([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)) but gives no
boundary for it, so whether either bank falls inside it is unverified — **check
the CCCA boundary against your position with CDFW/NOAA before you drop.**
Boundary compliance is positional, not depth-based ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
This zone also sits nearer the maritime boundary than most of the region, and
the outer-bank programs the corpus records cross into
[Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md) as a matter of course (`UfuiWFVvz2E`) —
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states which side of the line either mark
falls on.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The two positions.** Hancock Bank sits at **32°34.100'N 119°41.967'W** and
Northeast Bank at **32°20.800'N 119°38.400'W** (cameron) — **13.6 nm** apart on
a **167°/347°** line. Working both is one short north–south leg with open water
and no third charted mark between them.

**Neither name claims a depth.** A bank is an isolated high spot rising out of
otherwise deep water, named for the **fathom depth of its shallowest point** at
six feet to a fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`) — but the
convention only speaks through numbers, and these are both proper-named banks
like Tanner and Cortez rather than numbered ones like the 182 or the 43. The
`depth_band` is unknown for that reason. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
states a sounded depth, relief, high-spot shape, bottom composition or
surrounding depth at either mark, and no source names a rock, pinnacle or
sub-feature on either bank.

**This is the western edge of the charted region.** Only ten positions in the
whole spot library lie further west than Hancock's **119°41.967'W**, and twelve
further west than Northeast Bank — all of them well to the north, from Sverdrup
Bank and Patton Ridge up to Arguello Canyon (cameron). Longitude is what these
two share; the water south and west of them is uncharted in this library.

**Neither bank is attached to land, which sets how each would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, and the
face to bias toward is the up-current one, where current forced against the high
spot drives the upwelling that stacks the food chain
([current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md),
[island structure](island-structure.md)) — but each individual bank still has
its own preferred directions learned over repeat trips. At Tanner and Cortez
that shows up as sides: one boat anchored "on the Eastern side of the high spot"
at Tanner (`947solNfiPw`), another read where the fleet sat relative to the high
spot before moving onto the drop-off edge (`nQvJnfb5jQ4`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source:
preferred directions, drift behaviour or current set at Hancock or Northeast
Bank.

**Almost nothing charted is near this zone.** Nearest positions in the spot
library, measured to whichever bank is closer (cameron): **Cherry Banks** at
**23.0 nm** northeast of Hancock, which is parented to
[San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md) rather than out here;
**Cortes Bank** at **26.4 nm** east-northeast of Northeast Bank
([Tanner Bank](tanner-bank.md)); **Albatross Knoll** at **27.0 nm** northwest of
Hancock ([Sverdrup Bank (126)](sverdrup-bank-126.md)); **Tanner Bank** at
**29.7 nm** east-northeast of Hancock; and **Elephant** at **30.1 nm** south of
Northeast Bank. On the nearest-outside-mark measure this is one of the region's
most isolated zones: only the Davis Knoll grouping (**36.5 nm**), 12 Mile Reef
(**35.8 nm**) and San Juan Seamount (**30.9 nm**) sit further from their nearest
neighbour, and this zone ties [311 (Trask Knoll)](311.md) at **23.0 nm**
(cameron). **The nearest zone that carries corpus fishing doctrine at all is
Tanner Bank, 26.4 nm from the southern mark.**

**The fall condensation ends on the banks 26–30 nm east of here, not on these
two.** As the coast cools and the warm band fragments, the bluefin condense
outward toward **Tanner and Cortez** along an arc that runs backside Santa Cruz
/ Rosa → Santa Barbara Island → backside Catalina → the 499 / Clemente
(`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[October](../seasonal/october.md)). Every station on that arc lies north and
east of this zone, and its terminus is the neighbouring zone rather than this
one (cameron). Whether the condensation runs on past Cortes to Hancock and
Northeast Bank is exactly the question no source answers.

**The paddy band the corpus names is well inshore of here.** The best kelp-paddy
water in the Bight is **south of San Clemente Island / Catalina** and along the
coastal strip from Dana Point to Oceanside and southward; north of Dana Point
paddies thin out and hold fewer fish (`HzE4FHHcvTk`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)). The nearest San Clemente position is
**61.2 nm** from Hancock, the nearest Catalina position **75.5 nm**, and the
nearest position in that coastal strip over **110 nm** (cameron). That is a
statement about where the named water is, not about these banks: ⚠ Flagged gap —
no corpus source describes a paddy, bait, squid, temperature or water colour
over either position. Paddy mechanics are in
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which bank to start on, whether the two are run as one leg, or what tide, light,
current or drift do here.

**The geometry reads as a two-stop leg rather than a search box, but no source
says so.** The Bight's two zone types are the complex — a **~50–60 square-mile**
box you plan hours of looking across — and the compact spot you idle onto, grade
on the meter, and either commit to or leave in **20–30 minutes**
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). Two isolated banks
**13.6 nm** apart with open water between them (cameron) is not the shape of a
box, and the outer banks are where the corpus records the opposite extreme:
driving through **20 to 30 miles** of fish at Tanner/Cortez was called "not very
common," with a boat **15 miles away** seeing the same thing rather than the
usual isolated **4–5 sq mi** patch (`LsFMBCa9DOQ`). Both readings are drawn east
of this zone.

**With no corpus program, the general layers are what a plan has.** Break
position and break age decide whether this zone is on a given day's plan
([report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)),
and the meter decides once you are there
([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)). From
October the regime flips to reading **100 % the temperature chart and
chlorophyll**, and across a big area with no temperature differentiation the
fish are most likely on structure like the banks (`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`,
[water regimes](../conditions/water-regimes.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)). That last rule is the only
general doctrine that argues for pointing a boat at an unfished bank, and it is
not scoped to these two.

**The NW-blow map is drawn well inshore of this zone.** A strong NW event pulled
**5–8 °F** off the northern Channel Islands and the LA / Ventura shelf while the
Coronados, San Clemente and Catalina held their water (`YZtX1MiT0y8`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). This zone
sits west and south of everything named in that map, so treat the temperature
question as open here and the exposure question as decided: open ocean this far
out is [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md) first. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source states a wind or swell number that turns this zone off.

**Neither bank is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions here are pulled per
charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)). The nearest eval target is Cortes
Bank at **26.4 nm** from Northeast Bank and **29.8 nm** from Hancock (cameron) —
close enough to be the obvious proxy and far enough that using it as one should
be stated rather than assumed, which makes the per-position pull the honest
conditions read for this zone.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron), north to south — the axis this zone is drawn on:

- **Hancock Bank** — the northern mark and the one the zone is named for, and
  the closer of the two to everything north and west: **23.0 nm** to Cherry
  Banks, **27.0 nm** to Albatross Knoll, **29.7 nm** to Tanner Bank,
  **40.4 nm** to Dutch Harbor at San Nicolas Island (cameron). A proper-named
  bank with no number, so its name claims no depth. Coordinates only; the corpus
  never names it.
- **Northeast Bank** — the southern mark, **13.6 nm** at **167°** from Hancock,
  attached to this zone rather than clustered with it, and the closer of the two
  to everything east and south: **26.4 nm** to Cortes Bank, **30.1 nm** to
  Elephant, **41.0 nm** to The Worm (cameron). It is the southwesternmost
  position in the whole spot library, and its directional name has no referent
  any source gives. Coordinates only.

Charted positions for both are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive. The spot rung here needs a disambiguated filename for Hancock
Bank, whose slug collides with this zone page's own — a gazetteer question, not
one this page resolves.
