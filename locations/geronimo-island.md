# Geronimo Island

**[Baja only]** — the island at the south end of the
[Cedros / San Benitos](cedros-island.md) complex, and one end of a single
continuous stretch of shallow ground running to
[Chester's Rock](chester-s-rock.md) (`3SATCeA3KaU`). It is "endless miles" of
[calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md) water that turns into a home-guard
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) brawl in shallow water, worked from
skiffs off a mothership (`3SATCeA3KaU`).

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
The one source names it by name only, so a conditions pull has to be run
against the zone rather than against this island.

## Getting there

**The documented access is a skiff off a mothership, not a run from a port.**
The island was fished on the closing days of a six-day skiff trip out of Mexico
aboard the 90-ft *Shogun*, with the ground worked from small skiffs
(`3SATCeA3KaU`). The zone's two access modes — the Cedros fly-in program and
the San Diego long-range boat — are on
[the zone page](cedros-island.md#getting-there); neither is written around a
run to this island.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a port, launch, heading, run time, distance
or sea-state envelope for reaching Geronimo Island, or how it is approached by
anyone not travelling on a mothership.

**Regulatory — Mexico.** The zone's operator-level **100% catch-and-release**
agreement for [calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md), grouper (cabrilla) and
black seabass, and the Mexican entry and sport-fishing paperwork, are recorded
on [the zone page](cedros-island.md) and
[Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md). That agreement is an operator agreement,
not a federal or state regulation. Recorded on or before 2023-05-24; **verify
current status with your operator before fishing**
([regulatory claims](../sources/regulatory-claims.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**It is one end of a stretch, not a discrete mark.** The ground from Geronimo
Island to Chester's Rock is worked as a single continuous piece — "endless
miles" of calico water — and the same stretch produces the shallow-water
yellowtail brawl (`3SATCeA3KaU`). Anything read about one end applies down the
stretch unless a source says otherwise, so the bathymetry recorded at the far
end (**12 ft** on the rock dropping to **40–60 ft** a short way out) is the
nearest figure the corpus offers, and it belongs to
[Chester's Rock](chester-s-rock.md#structure--bathymetry), not to this island.

**The fishing is shallow-water fishing.** Both the yellowtail brawl and the
later plug bite happened in shallow water, and doing it there is called "not
easy to do" (`3SATCeA3KaU`). That is what drives the heavy line and hard drag
in [How it fishes](#how-it-fishes).

**A shoreline point is a working feature here.** Several "monster" yellowtail
came on a swimbait/plug worked tight to shore around a point, about a quarter
mile from the mothership (`3SATCeA3KaU`). That is the only piece of named
topography the source gives on this end of the stretch.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: soundings, bottom composition, relief, kelp
extent or the island's shape, and how deep the water is between it and
Chester's Rock. A third island named in the same run — rendered *Sandra's
Island*, asr-uncertain — is uncorroborated and is not carried as a place here
(`3SATCeA3KaU`).

## How it fishes

**Birds over the ground are the entry to the iron.** "Birds flared up, came out
here, threw the jig" is the stated search cue on this stretch (`3SATCeA3KaU`,
[calico bass — surface iron](../species/calico-bass-surface-iron.md)). Bird work
is [bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md) and
[search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).

**Hold the cast until the yellows show.** On the first-light push under working
birds the discipline was to wait for the yellowtail to actually appear under the
birds rather than throwing at the birds themselves (`3SATCeA3KaU`) — the
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md) cast is spent on a fish, not on a
bird.

**Surface iron and swimbaits both produce, at the same time.** The afternoon
calico bite ran on both, with the plastics fished weedless and the whole crew on
them by the end of the trip (`3SATCeA3KaU`); rigging is in
[soft-plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md) and the retrieve in
[swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md).

**A plug worked tight to shore is the shallow-water yellowtail play.** The
"monster" fish around the point ate a plug in shallow water and were landed on
heavy line with hard drag pressure — "pull hard… pay drag" — and the crew called
doing it in shallow water "not easy to do" (`3SATCeA3KaU`). Line-class
parameters are in
[line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md), and the zone's wider 40/50 versus
80/100 argument is on
[the zone page](cedros-island.md#doctrine--conflicts).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a tide, moon or current window for this
island, how long to give a stop, or how the ground fishes outside a
mothership/skiff program. The one session's timing statements are a clock — an
afternoon bite and a first-light push — never a tide window.
