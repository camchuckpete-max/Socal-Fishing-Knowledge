# Punta Perico

**[Baja only]** — the **dorado ground** of the
[Loreto & Puerto Escondido](loreto.md) island cluster: a shoreline point up the
coast where the bait vendor placed [dorado](../species/dorado.md) being caught
**close to shore**, under diving birds and around weed (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). It is
the only dorado ground in this zone that resolves to a named place; the islands'
record here is a shallow-rock and troll program.

## Getting there

**It is a long run up the coast out of Puerto Escondido Marina, and nobody in
the corpus has completed it.** The point was called *"kind of a long run up
there"* on the morning it was chosen, and the departure for it is described as
*"a shot up to the north end of Carmen… about 20 miles, probably an hour"*
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`). What the run actually produced was a **sheared shift pin** at
the start of it, a radio call and a tow home behind a marina boat — the zone's
standing lesson that a breakdown here is a rescue, not a self-repair
([the zone page](loreto.md#getting-there)).

**Buy bait first, and ask the vendor where the fish are — this ground is his
report, not a plotter mark.** The sardines and the dorado report both came from
**Antonio**, the bait vendor who meets boats out front of Puerto Escondido
Marina after the dock order is missed (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). See
[the zone page](loreto.md#getting-there) and
[making bait](../bait/making-bait.md).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): **"this ground is his report" is the
disputed half of that line.** Buying bait from Antonio and asking him where the
fish are is verbatim on the record — *"I got some sardines now to fish for the /
Dorado and then we're gonna ask him / where people have been catching the /
Dorado at"*, *"there he is / got the bait guy right here Antonio he / said he
was out front the marina"*, *"Antonio's teaching me / the ways of the Dorado
here"* (`n4nMCLLW3tY`) — as are the rigging advice and the catch times below.
What the source does not settle is that the ground he named was **this** one;
see the flag at the top of this page and
[the zone page](loreto.md#structure--bathymetry), which records the vendor's
steer as *desante* and reads *"close to the shore in Punta Perico"* as the
speaker's line rather than the vendor's. Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

**It was picked over the ground the locals were talking about.** Word that
morning pointed at *"desante"* / *"the Sante"*, but the crew's read was that the
report was not holding up, so they went to their own history here instead
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`). That other ground resolves to no charted place in the KB and is
already flagged on [the zone page](loreto.md#spots).

## Structure & bathymetry

**A point, a shoreline and floating weed — that is the whole description.** The
fish are placed *"close to the shore in Punta Perico"*, with *"the birds diving
and then the seaweed or whatever"* as the sign (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). Nothing names a
reef, a ledge, a corner or a bottom type, and no depth is given anywhere on this
ground.

**The sign here is floating, not on the bottom.** Weed plus working birds is
what the vendor's read hangs on, which makes this a surface-and-shoreline
program rather than a structure one — the same thing
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md) do for dorado elsewhere, read
alongside [bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md).

⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): **how far off the beach the fish were is lost in
the caption.** The vendor gives a distance from shore — rendered *"while miles
from the side"* — which the transcript cannot resolve into a number
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`), so "close to shore" is as precise as this page gets.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: depth, bottom composition, relief, how far the
point runs out, which side of it to work, or any sounder picture from this
ground ([electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)). The
zone's **5–10 ft** and **30 ft** soundings were taken against island rock
elsewhere and do not belong to this point
([the zone page](loreto.md#structure--bathymetry)).

## How it fishes

**Live sardine, rigged the vendor's way, is the documented dorado program.** A
**small-to-medium hook through the nose and eyes** so the bait keeps swimming,
fished with **the weight underneath rather than the bait on top**
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`). The same crew's flylined sardine went out on a circle hook that
morning, although it fished the drift home rather than this point
([the zone page](loreto.md#how-it-fishes)). Execution is in
[fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md) and
[flyline](../techniques/flyline.md).

⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): the vendor's advice appears to pair the flylined
bait with **chumming the same sardines**, but the instruction survives only as a
broken auto-caption (`n4nMCLLW3tY`); the crew did chum sardines for dorado later
that day, on the tow back, which is consistent with it without confirming it.

**Late morning is the window on the record.** The reported catches ran
**10:20–11:00**, and the crew's plan was built around getting up here for it
rather than around first light (`n4nMCLLW3tY`) — the opposite shape to the
zone's evening yellowtail programs
([the zone page](loreto.md#how-it-fishes)).

⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): **the window survives as three unpunctuated caption
tokens.** The whole exchange is *"what time did you catch them early no / maybe
in it / 10 20 11. okay / Maybe"* (`n4nMCLLW3tY`), so *"early no"* makes
late-morning the right direction, but **10:20–11:00** is one reading of *"10 20
11."* and the *"maybe"* hedge is dropped where the times are set in bold here
and on [the zone page](loreto.md#whats-there). Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

**Find the weed and the birds, then work in.** Diving birds over floating weed
close to the beach is the entire search picture given for this ground
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`); method is in
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md),
[search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md) and
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).
