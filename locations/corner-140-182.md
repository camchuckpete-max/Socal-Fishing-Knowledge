# The Corner / 140 / 182

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is a four-mark piece of the numbered
offshore-bank grid west of Point Loma — **140**, **182**, **166** and **The
Corner** (cameron), spanning **5.6 minutes of latitude** (32°37.600'N to
32°43.167'N) and **7.0 minutes of longitude** (117°42.400'W to 117°49.400'W).
Every pair sits inside **7.0 nm** of every other, so unlike most bank zones on
this ladder **nothing here was attached on distance**: all four clustered on
their own coordinates.

**One of the four carries the corpus, and it carries three fisheries.** The
**182** is the KB's worked example of how a bank gets its name, a named member
of the June–July below-Clemente search box (`Blh2BA-7Ono`), a stop on the
October warmest-water yellowfin path (`OYOda6T3f-8`), and a named break-day
swordfish spot (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`). The **140** and the **166** appear nowhere in
the corpus but the coordinate library, and **The Corner** has a single probable
mention. So this page is a real zone with one documented mark and three that are
position-only.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The four marks and what separates them.** The 140 sits at **32°43.167'N
117°47.167'W**, the 182 at **32°41.300'N 117°42.400'W**, the 166 at
**32°39.000'N 117°43.700'W** and The Corner at **32°37.600'N 117°49.400'W**
(cameron). The tightest pair is **182 to 166 at 2.5 nm**; the widest is **182 to
The Corner at 7.0 nm** (cameron). Both extremes are inside the census's **12 nm**
zone-diameter cap, which is the clustering rule that grouped them, and the
whole zone fits inside a little over half that cap.

**Three of the four names are readable depths.** A bank is an isolated high spot
rising out of otherwise deep water, named for the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point** as read on the sounder, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way the **140** tops
out at **140 fathoms — about 840 ft**, the **166** at **166 fathoms — about
996 ft**, and the **182** at **182 fathoms — about 1,092 ft**.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the front-matter `depth_band` field puts

under one `Rf1HKJG-SDg` cite, but the transcript never says 140, never states a
range, and performs no foot conversion of any fathom figure — only "depth is
measured in fathoms and six feet is in a fathom" (@00:06:40) is sourced; the
840/996/1,092 ft numbers are this page's own arithmetic. The convention itself is

"it's not a fish finder the machine is called a fedometer" — where this note
writes "as read on the sounder".

**The 182 is the convention's own worked example.** Water 2,000–3,000 ft deep
runs out flat, then comes up to 182 fathoms at one spot and levels off again on
the far side; that highest part is what earns the name and the word *bank*, and
there are places all over the ocean at 182 fathoms that are not banks
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms the 140 or the 166 as
charted depths, or describes relief, high-spot shape or bottom composition at
any of the four.

## How it fishes

**This zone sits on the complex side of the search split, which decides how you
budget the day.** The below-Clemente complex is worked as one search box — you
do not "check the 43", you plan **hours of looking**, glassing and running
contour between features, treating the named banks as reference points inside a
single hunting zone. The opposite type is the compact spot — 302, 371,
west-of-Catalina — a small definable piece of structure you idle up to, grade on
the meter, and either commit to or leave (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). The 182 is named inside the complex.

## Spots

Four charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. All four clustered on their
own coordinates — no member was attached to this zone on distance:

- **The 140** — the northern mark, and the closest in the zone to the next
  grouping north (5.9 nm to the 138). Coordinates only; the corpus never names
  it.
- **The 182** — the eastern mark, and the only position in this zone the corpus
  carries. It is the worked example of the bank-naming convention
  (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), a member of the below-Clemente search box (`Blh2BA-7Ono`),
  part of the fall "181/182 Ridge" (`OYOda6T3f-8`) and a break-day swordfish spot
  (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`).
- **The 166** — the middle mark, 2.5 nm from the 182 and the closest position to
  the zone centre. Coordinates only; the corpus never names it.
- **The Corner** — the southwest mark, the only one without a fathom number and
  the only name in the zone that collides with other uses of the word in the KB.
  Its single probable corpus mention is the October yellowfin clause above, and
  that reading is uncertain.

Charted positions for all four are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position and, for the 182, the four cites above, so each spot page is
coordinates plus its parent zone until more sources arrive.
