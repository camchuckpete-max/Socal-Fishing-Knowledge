# The Corner / 140 / 182

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is a four-mark piece of the numbered
offshore-bank grid west of Point Loma — **140**, **182**, **166** and **The
Corner** (cameron), spanning **5.6 minutes of latitude** (32°37.600'N to
32°43.167'N) and **7.0 minutes of longitude** (117°42.400'W to 117°49.400'W).
Every pair sits inside **7.0 nm** of every other, so unlike most bank zones on
this ladder **nothing here was attached on distance**: all four clustered on
their own coordinates.

**One of the four carries the corpus, and it carries three fisheries.** The
**182** is the KB's worked example of how a bank gets its name, a named member
of the June–July below-Clemente search box (`Blh2BA-7Ono`), a stop on the
October warmest-water yellowfin path (`OYOda6T3f-8`), and a named break-day
swordfish spot (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`). The **140** and the **166** appear nowhere in
the corpus but the coordinate library, and **The Corner** has a single probable
mention. So this page is a real zone with one documented mark and three that are
position-only.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run distance, heading, timing or fuel
figure to this zone from any port, and no source names a port these banks are
fished out of.

**This is offshore water, well outside the nearshore bank line.** North 9 Mile
Bank is named for sitting **9 statute miles** off the coast rather than for a
fathom depth (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), and it lies **14.2–19.7 nm east** of the four
marks here (cameron) — this zone is further offshore again. The nearest charted
coastal positions the spot library offers are **Widow Rock** in
[La Jolla](la-jolla.md) at **20.6 nm** and the **Point Loma Pipe** at
**24.1 nm** from the zone centre, with the Mission Bay artificial reefs at
**25.2–25.7 nm** (cameron). For scale, the [Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md)
run **~13 nm from the San Diego bay entrance** (cameron), so this zone sits
roughly twice that distance out.

**Deep water starts close in here, so distance offshore is not the same as
depth.** A mile to a mile-and-a-half off the beach from Dana Point through
Newport out to Catalina is already 2,000–3,000 ft (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`,
[Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)); what makes these four marks worth
the run is relief off that plain, not the distance itself.

2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The four marks and what separates them.** The 140 sits at **32°43.167'N
117°47.167'W**, the 182 at **32°41.300'N 117°42.400'W**, the 166 at
**32°39.000'N 117°43.700'W** and The Corner at **32°37.600'N 117°49.400'W**
(cameron). The tightest pair is **182 to 166 at 2.5 nm**; the widest is **182 to
The Corner at 7.0 nm** (cameron). Both extremes are inside the census's **12 nm**
zone-diameter cap, which is the clustering rule that grouped them, and the
whole zone fits inside a little over half that cap.

**Three of the four names are readable depths.** A bank is an isolated high spot
rising out of otherwise deep water, named for the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point** as read on the sounder, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way the **140** tops
out at **140 fathoms — about 840 ft**, the **166** at **166 fathoms — about
996 ft**, and the **182** at **182 fathoms — about 1,092 ft**.

**The 182 is the convention's own worked example.** Water 2,000–3,000 ft deep
runs out flat, then comes up to 182 fathoms at one spot and levels off again on
the far side; that highest part is what earns the name and the word *bank*, and
there are places all over the ocean at 182 fathoms that are not banks
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms the 140 or the 166 as
charted depths, or describes relief, high-spot shape or bottom composition at
any of the four.

**The Corner carries no number, so the convention yields nothing for it.**
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what *Corner* denotes as a structure term
here, or what it is a corner of. Two other senses of the word are already in the
KB and neither is this spot — **Northwest Corner** is a separate charted mark in
La Jolla at **32°50.630'N 117°18.460'W** (cameron,
[Northwest Corner](northwest-corner.md)), and *corner* is also the kelp-bed
sense in the current-selection rule (downhill current → northwest corner of the
bed, uphill → southeast corner;
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)).

**The corpus pairs the 182 with the 181, and the coordinates put them in
different zones.** The below-Clemente area is enumerated as "the 43, the 181,
182, 289, the entire Clemente Ridge, all the way up to Mackerel Bank"
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`), and the fall yellowfin path names "the 181/182 Ridge"
(`OYOda6T3f-8`) — both treating 181 and 182 as one breath. The 181 is charted at
**32°54.000'N 117°53.000'W**, **15.5 nm northwest of the 182** and **15.1 nm**
from this zone's centre (cameron), past the 12 nm cap, so the census placed it in
a different cluster. This page carries the tension rather than resolving it,
because the zone list is set by the census and not by this page. ⚠ Flagged gap —
no corpus source states whether "181/182" names two separate banks or one ridge
feature running between them.

**The corpus's search box is bigger than any one zone on this ladder.** The
below-Clemente complex reads as **one ~50–60 square-mile search box**
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`), and its named members fall across four separate census
clusters: the 182 here, the 43 at **10.8 nm** from this centre, the 181 at
**15.1 nm**, the 289 at **23.0 nm** and Mackerel Bank at **38.7 nm** (cameron).
Treat this zone as one corner of that box on a June–July bluefin plan, not as
the whole of it.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron): the **138** at **9.0 nm** from
the zone centre, the **91** at **9.6 nm**, the **51** at **9.8 nm**, the **43**
at **10.8 nm**, the **226** at **12.4 nm**, the **300** at **13.3 nm**, the
**178** at **13.9 nm**, and then the **181**, **North 9 Mile Bank** and **302
(Kidney Bank)** at **15.1–16.7 nm**. ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: none
of those neighbouring bank groupings has a page on this ladder, so there is
nothing to hand a fallback plan off to.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: preferred directions, drift or current behaviour on any of these four.

## What's there

| species | season | what this zone does for them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) | June–July | The 182 is a named member of the below-Clemente complex, which reads as one ~50–60 sq mi search box rather than a set of spot-checks (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [June–July](../seasonal/june-july.md)) |
| [yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) | October, once fall fragmentation sets in | The biggest concentrations track the warmest water on the coast — the West End of San Clemente, down through Mackerel Bank to the 181/182 Ridge and on "down there towards the corner" (`OYOda6T3f-8`) |
| [swordfish](../species/swordfish.md) | late September–December, peak October–mid-November | "The 138/182 area on break days" is a named working spot ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md), `yMiBtZ7k8-w`); the 182 and 166 crests read inside the **150–350 fathom** band the fishery works, the 140 reads shallower than it |

**The yellowfin mention is the only corpus sentence that may name The Corner,
and it is not certain.** The October path runs "down to the 181 182 Ridge down
there towards the corner, that's the warmest water on the coast, and that's
where all that yellowfin is" (`OYOda6T3f-8`, asr-uncertain on *the corner*). The
auto-caption leaves that word lowercase and unpunctuated while capitalising the
proper nouns around it in the same sentence (*West End of Clementi*, *Ridge*), so
it may name the charted spot at 32°37.600'N 117°49.400'W — **7.0 nm southwest of
the 182** (cameron) — or may be a generic corner of the Bight. The
[yellowfin router](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) and
[San Clemente front side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md) both carry the
earlier part of the same sentence without the clause.

**The swordfish pick was made on camera off a chart, not from a logbook.** On an
SST/chlorophyll plot the 138 was chosen first, and the 182 named next because it
showed mixing water and heavy current flow — the reasoning stated for it is that
converging waters form at any edge, temperature or chlorophyll (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`).
That makes the 182 a **break-position spot** rather than a standing destination:
you check whether the break is on it before you go
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)).

## How it fishes

**This zone sits on the complex side of the search split, which decides how you
budget the day.** The below-Clemente complex is worked as one search box — you
do not "check the 43", you plan **hours of looking**, glassing and running
contour between features, treating the named banks as reference points inside a
single hunting zone. The opposite type is the compact spot — 302, 371,
west-of-Catalina — a small definable piece of structure you idle up to, grade on
the meter, and either commit to or leave (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). The 182 is named inside the complex.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source places the 140, the 166 or The Corner on
either side of that split, so whether the four are a search box of their own or
four spot-checks is unstated.

**The June–July read pairs the search box with the moon.** Full moons drive the
night bite in that window, and the plan is hours of looking rather than a
spot-check ([bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md),
[June–July](../seasonal/june-july.md), [moon](../conditions/moon.md)).

**The swordfish read is a conditions read before it is a location read.** It is
a fair-weather fishery — **>15 kt is a no-go**, comfortable at **10–13 kt**,
drift **≤ ~2–2.5 kt** — and the water you want is an SST plus chlorophyll break
where masses converge ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md),
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)). Because the 182 was picked off
break position rather than history (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`), the break-aging and
advection rules in
[report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)
decide whether this zone is on the plan at all on a given day.

**No mark here is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions for this zone are
pulled per charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the four are run as one circuit, which end to
work first, or what tide, light and current do here.

## Spots

Four charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. All four clustered on their
own coordinates — no member was attached to this zone on distance:

- **The 140** — the northern mark, and the closest in the zone to the next
  grouping north (5.9 nm to the 138). Coordinates only; the corpus never names
  it.
- **The 182** — the eastern mark, and the only position in this zone the corpus
  carries. It is the worked example of the bank-naming convention
  (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), a member of the below-Clemente search box (`Blh2BA-7Ono`),
  part of the fall "181/182 Ridge" (`OYOda6T3f-8`) and a break-day swordfish spot
  (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`).
- **The 166** — the middle mark, 2.5 nm from the 182 and the closest position to
  the zone centre. Coordinates only; the corpus never names it.
- **The Corner** — the southwest mark, the only one without a fathom number and
  the only name in the zone that collides with other uses of the word in the KB.
  Its single probable corpus mention is the October yellowfin clause above, and
  that reading is uncertain.

Charted positions for all four are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position and, for the 182, the four cites above, so each spot page is
coordinates plus its parent zone until more sources arrive.
