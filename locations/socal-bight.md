# Southern California Bight

**The character rung.** The Bight runs from Point Conception to the US border
and carries the Channel Islands, Catalina, San Clemente, the nearshore banks
and the outer banks at Tanner and Cortez
([regions & waters](regions.md)). Deep water starts close in — a mile to a
mile-and-a-half off the beach from Dana Point through Newport and Marina del
Rey out to Catalina is already 2,000–3,000 ft (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`) — so one run off
a mainland ramp reaches island, bank and open-ocean water. It is the only
region under [US waters](us-waters.md), which makes CDFW and the USCG the
authorities for everything on this page, and its year runs as four water-state
regimes rather than twelve months
([water regimes](../conditions/water-regimes.md)).

## The fishery

**Bottom relief is the whole game, because there is so little of it.** Against
mostly deep, featureless bottom a **bank is an isolated high spot** rising out
of the surrounding depth, and it is named for the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point** — 182 tops out at 182 fathoms, 209 at 209, 43 at 43. The
exceptions are named for distance instead: Nine Mile Bank sits **9 statute
miles** off the coast and carries many closely spaced high spots rather than
one dominant peak, and "the 14" sits **14 miles** off the beach
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Huntington is the one mainland stretch that stays shallower
longer, because of the flats there. Full naming conventions and the
search-box sizing that goes with them are in
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md).

**Three structure types, and each fishes a different number of current
directions.** Land-attached perpendicular ridges are two-directional and want
parallel current; standalone banks fish any direction but each has its own
preferred ones; coastal deep spots default to downhill (N→S) current
([island structure](island-structure.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). At the islands the
**fathom curve is itself structure** — Catalina's ~500-fathom curve compresses
the uphill summer counter-current up the slope, spins eddies, and aggregates
both bait and kelp paddies along the line (`HnqiE05vdXs`).

**Inshore, the region is kelp, rock and tidal bays.** Coastal kelp lines,
breakwalls, jetties and riprap
([break walls, jetties and riprap](breakwalls-jetties-riprap.md),
[bass structure](bass-structure.md)) run down the mainland, and the bay
systems — San Diego Bay, Mission Bay, Newport, Huntington Harbour, the
LA/Long Beach complex — are tidal engines with eelgrass, channel edges, docks
and riprap ([bays and harbors](bays-and-harbors.md)).

**Kelp paddies are not spread evenly across the region.** The best paddy water
is **south of San Clemente Island / Catalina** and along the coastal strip from
**Dana Point to Oceanside and southward**; paddies thin out and hold fewer fish
**north of Dana Point** (`HzE4FHHcvTk`). Mechanics are
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

**A NW blow does not hit the region evenly.** The northern Channel Islands and
the LA/Ventura coastal shelf get crushed — a strong NW event rolls surface
temperature down **5–8 °F** there — while **south of Newport** is less prone to
wind-wave-driven rollover, and the Coronados, the tuna grounds, Catalina and
San Clemente Island held their water through one such event (`YZtX1MiT0y8`).
The **Catalina Eddy** can shield the inner San Diego banks and tuna grounds
while it blows outside, so an ugly offshore forecast does not automatically
close the inner zones ([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md),
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)).

**The species mix spans bay bass to billfish in one region:**

| water | what the Bight offers |
| --- | --- |
| open-ocean | [bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md), [yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md), [dorado](../species/dorado.md), [skipjack](../species/skipjack-tuna.md), [striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md), [swordfish](../species/swordfish.md), [opah](../species/opah.md) |
| island, bank | [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md), [white seabass](../species/white-seabass.md), [calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md), [ocean whitefish](../species/ocean-whitefish.md), [sheephead](../species/sheephead.md), [rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md) |
| nearshore-coast | [calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md), [sand bass](../species/sand-bass.md), [barracuda](../species/barracuda.md), [bonito](../species/bonito.md), [California halibut](../species/california-halibut.md), [California spiny lobster](../species/california-spiny-lobster.md) |
| bay-harbor | [spotted bay bass](../species/spotted-bay-bass.md), [California halibut](../species/california-halibut.md), [sand bass](../species/sand-bass.md) |

[Wahoo](../species/wahoo.md) reach the Bight only as a warm-water range
extension, and the [Pacific crevalle jack](../species/pacific-crevalle-jack.md)
has no dated Bight record in the corpus — both are exceptions, not part of the
standing mix.

## Season shape

Priors, not current intel: they weight zones before live
[BightSST](../planning/day-plan-protocol.md) conditions are pulled. In an
anomalous year the water state overrides the calendar
([water regimes](../conditions/water-regimes.md)).

| regime | typical months | how the Bight fishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Winter / upwelling | mid-December–March ([February–March](../seasonal/february-march.md), back half of [November–December](../seasonal/november-december.md)) | A structure-and-water-quality fishery, not a tuna fishery: [sand bass](../species/sand-bass.md) hold deep structure, crankbaits work suspended bait balls over pipes all winter, [calico](../species/calico-bass.md) go off first when runoff dirties the inshore |
| Warming transition | April–May ([April](../seasonal/april.md), [May](../seasonal/may.md)) | Leading-edge fish ahead of the volume — bluefin creep into US waters in May with the first paddy yellows; April bluefin are a deep sonar-school and night-jig game mostly out of skiff range; squid spawns concentrate island fish |
| Warm stable | June–September ([June–July](../seasonal/june-july.md), [August](../seasonal/august.md), [September](../seasonal/september.md)) | The offshore season proper. Full moons drive the island [white seabass](../species/white-seabass.md) bite and the bluefin night bite (`DnSqw4r7A1s`, `-JZpyWLdKlk`); August is peak dispersal across the whole Bight and the peak of the paddy program; the September "volume square" — Santa Barbara Island, San Nicolas, San Clemente, Catalina — holds the bigger bluefin (`5to3Q5P7w90`) |
| Fall fragmentation | October–December ([October](../seasonal/october.md), front half of [November–December](../seasonal/november-december.md)) | The chart runs the plan: "conditions become pretty important… look 100 % at your temp chart and chlorophyll" (`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`). Breadth of species, low density of any one; Santa Ana events flatten the NW outer islands and open Santa Rosa / San Miguel for shallow rockfish and lings (`OYOda6T3f-8`, `Z3rZqy2Pi8E`) |

Two geographic patterns run across the year and decide where to look rather
than what to throw: the **wind-rollover map** (which zones survive a NW event)
and the **fall warm-band path** (the arc the warm water retreats along, ending
in a condensation onto the outer banks). Both are in
[bight geography](bight-geography.md). The
[year-anniversary prior](../seasonal/year-anniversary-prior.md) is a location
prior inside the fall regime, not a date rule.

**July offshore geography splits into two kinds of water and is planned
differently.** The below-Clemente complex — 43, 181, 182, 289 and Clemente
Ridge up to Mackerel Bank — reads as **one ~50–60 square-mile search box** that
costs hours of looking, while compact spots such as 302, 371 and the west end
of Catalina are a spot-check — idle up, grade the meter, commit or leave
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`). Sizing the box and budgeting the time — a compact spot reads
in 20–30 minutes, a complex is a half-day commitment — is
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md); filling the hours is
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).

## Zones

Forty-one zones, derived from the coordinates in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) rather than from mention counts.
The tables below are the character map; the complete linked index is at the
foot of this section and fills itself in as the zone pages land.

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: CDFW for marine protected areas
and take restrictions; US Navy for security zones. Checked as of 2026-08.
Several boundaries travel with the waypoint itself in the spot labels (e.g.
`stay W of MPA at 118 29.300` at Ship Rock). Verify current boundaries with
CDFW before you fish — MPA lines and closures move year to year.**

## Access & range

**Everything in the region is reached from a mainland port, and the bait comes
first.** The San Diego Bay and Mission Bay receivers and the Dana Point barge
are the buy side of the day ([making bait](../bait/making-bait.md)); receiver
refills are trackable when bait is short
([fleet intelligence](../planning/fleet-intelligence.md)). At Dana Point the
sportboat fleet's **6:00 a.m.** departure is also when the private-boat crowd
converges on the barge, so leaving the dock at **5:00 a.m.** — or 4:30 — beats
the line entirely, while 7:00 a.m. is too late
(`ztpj1Xll0-U`, `7WapaxdtjQg`). Barge etiquette
and load sizing are [making bait](../bait/making-bait.md) and
[bait tanks](../bait/bait-tanks.md).

**Range is short to structure and long to the outer banks.** Deep water starts
a mile to a mile-and-a-half off the beach across the LA-to-Dana-Point stretch
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), and two of the region's banks carry their distance in their
name — Nine Mile Bank at 9 statute miles off the coast, "the 14" at 14 miles
off the beach (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). The outer banks at Tanner and Cortez and the
NW-corner seamounts are the far end of the same region and are not a
short-range proposition.

**Trip format buys range.** A San Diego one-day trip runs overnight, fishes
roughly half a day and returns the following evening; a 3-day trip fishes two
full days dawn to dusk and can reach **up to 200 miles** if needed, and 4-to-5
day trips buy range and time in the zone rather than a different daily rhythm
(`jU7qi40WTzQ`). The stated reason to buy the longer format is that offshore
tuna often bite late in the afternoon, when a one-day schedule has already
turned for home ([trip-length selection](../planning/trip-length-selection.md)).

**Weather decides which end of the range is open.** Weight zones by the
wind-rollover map before a NW event and check the Catalina Eddy state when the
outside forecast is bad — the inner San Diego banks can be fishable while it
blows outside ([island structure](island-structure.md),
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

**The neighbouring region is 13 nm away and needs different paperwork.** The
Coronado Islands sit about **13 nm** from the San Diego bay entrance (cameron)
but are Mexican water and belong to `baja-pacific-north`
([regions & waters](regions.md)); the papers for this side of the line are
[US waters](us-waters.md).
