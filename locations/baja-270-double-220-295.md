# Baja - 270 / Double 220 / 295

**A zone is a run grouping.** Baja - 270 / Double 220 / 295 is an east–west
string of the numbered offshore-bank grid in open water west of Ensenada —
three charted positions (cameron) inside **2 minutes of latitude**
(31°34.000'N to 31°36.000'N) but spread across **13 minutes of longitude**
(117°26.000'W to 117°39.000'W). All three clustered on coordinates with no
attached outlier, and the widest pair in the zone spans **11.1 nm** against the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap — this is a run grouping that only just
holds together, and it holds together along a parallel rather than around a
centre.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these three marks.** No source
names the Baja - 270, the Double 220 or the 295, and the nearest documented
fishing is a 2020 bluefin corridor whose southern anchor sits **18.3 nm** north
of the zone's nearest mark (`49joKHD7Umc`, cameron). The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered grid — fifty-one charted positions over seventeen bank zones with no
depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
spot names claim, and what is missing.

**Two of the spot library's naming rules run through this one zone**, which is
what makes its marks easy to confuse with water **70–140 nm** away (cameron). The
**landmass-prefix** rule produced *Baja - 270*, and the *distinct, not
collisions* rule kept **Double 220** separate from the plain **220**
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). Both counterparts are real spots somewhere
else, and both are covered below.

are close but not the same.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The zone is a line along one parallel.** The 295 sits at **31°36.000'N
117°26.000'W**, the Baja - 270 at **31°34.000'N 117°29.000'W** and the Double 220
at **31°35.000'N 117°39.000'W** (cameron) — every mark within a minute of
**31°35.000'N**, which is also the latitude of the zone's computed centre. The
295 and the Baja - 270 are the tight pair at **3.2 nm**; the Baja - 270 to the
Double 220 is **8.6 nm**; the 295 to the Double 220 is **11.1 nm** and is what
sets the zone's span. From the centre the marks lie **2.2 nm** (Baja - 270),
**4.7 nm** (295) and **6.5 nm** (Double 220) out (cameron).

**The eastern end looks outward as well as inward.** Past its zone-mate the
Baja - 270 at 3.2 nm, the 295's nearest charted neighbours sit outside this zone:
the **480** at **31°42.000'N 117°18.000'W** is **9.1 nm** away and the **385**
**9.4 nm**, both closer than its other zone-mate the Double 220 at 11.1 nm, and
both parented to
[385 / 238 / 475](385-238-475.md) (cameron). The complete-linkage rule is what
keeps the two zones apart — the 480 to the Double 220 is **19.2 nm**, well past
the 12 nm cap, so no merge is available — but it means the water off this zone's
eastern end is continuous bank ground split across two pages. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: whether the 295 is fished with the Baja - 270 or with the 385 and
the 480.

**The western end is near-equidistant between two zones.** The Double 220 sits
**11.1 nm** from the 295 at its own zone's far end and **11.6 nm** from the
**1010 Trench**, with the **1067 Knuckle** **12.1 nm** off — both marks in
[1010 Trench / 378 / 213](1010-trench-378-213.md) (cameron). Off the zone's
northern side the **Upper 500** in
[Upper 500 / Hidden Bank](upper-500-hidden-bank.md) is the nearest charted mark
of any zone to this one's centre at **11.1 nm**, ahead of the 480 at **13.3 nm**
and the 385 at **14.0 nm** (cameron). Nothing about this stretch of grid is
isolated; the zone boundaries here are a clustering cap, not a break in the
bottom.

**The names are the only depth claims in the zone, and they land in a band the
KB actually works.** A bank takes the **fathom depth of its shallowest point** as
its name, at six feet to a fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
`Rf1HKJG-SDg`):

| mark | name read as depth | at six feet to a fathom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Double 220 | 220 fathoms | about 1,320 ft |
| Baja - 270 | 270 fathoms | about 1,620 ft |
| 295 | 295 fathoms | about 1,770 ft |

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): `Rf1HKJG-SDg` states the naming
convention as a tendency, not a rule — "most of them are … called after the
highest part" — and names its own exceptions in the same breath (the 9 Mile and
14 Mile banks are named for miles off the beach, and "there are places all over
the ocean that are 182 but they're not a bank"). It is also describing SoCal
banks throughout; nothing in it scopes the convention to the Baja numbered grid,
so the three readings in this table are an off-region rule of thumb.

**All three fall inside the 150–350 fathom contour band** that the swordfish
router gives for contour lines, thumbs, notches and humps
([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)) — a **75-fathom** spread across the whole
zone, and no name here reads below that band. For contrast, the neighbouring
[385 / 238 / 475](385-238-475.md) names read **238–480 fathoms**, and the Upper
500 next door reads about 3,000 ft. **That
band is stated for SoCal water, not for this coast** — nothing in the corpus
applies the swordfish contour program to a Baja bank, and SoCal daytime
deep-drop baits average **800–1,100 ft**, above the bottom these names claim
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)). Read
literally these are marks you would drift baits *in*, not bottom you would fish.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms any of the three numbers as a charted
depth, and none says what **Double** denotes in a spot name — whether a doubled
peak, a second mark at the same fathom reading, or something else.

**The general read on a bank is the only structure doctrine available here.**
Against otherwise deep, featureless bottom a bank is an isolated high spot —
a submerged mountain where bait stages for shelter and gamefish follow the bait
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). Standalone structure like this
fishes in **any** direction, but each individual bank still has its own
**preferred directions** learned over repeat trips
([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
bottom composition, relief, contour, high-spot shape, surrounding depth,
preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at any of these three marks.
The `depth_band` is unknown for that reason, not because the water is shallow.

**Both name-collision counterparts are far from here, and the distances are the
point.** The plain **220** at **30°30.350'N 116°23.200'W** is **91.6 nm** south
of the Double 220, in the 179 / 220 zone; the **LA - 270** at
**33°41.000'N 118°34.500'W** is **138.5 nm** northwest of the Baja - 270; and
[The 270 rockfish area](270-rockfish-area.md) in the La Jolla zone is
**73.0 nm** north of it (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says what
relationship, if any, the Double 220 has to the 220 beyond the shared number —
the library records them as distinct names, not a collision
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).

**Searching the corpus on the bare number returns gear, not geography.** Outside
the spot library, *220* appears in the corpus only as a jig weight, a
lure size, a called column depth and a video-series volume number
([knife jigging](../techniques/knife-jigging.md),
[knife jigs](../lures/knife-jigs.md), [DTX minnow](../lures/dtx-minnow.md)) —
never as this bank. A search for these marks has to be anchored on the full
name, which is the reason the census scores a numbered spot only on an anchored
form and never on the bare digits.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states

**The only program the corpus scopes to this water is the port's, and it is
chart-first rather than spot-first.** The stated Ensenada offshore plan is to run
**20–30 mi due west** to a temperature break, then continue north or west off it
by sea conditions, with a fallback search **20–30 mi north**; trolling cedar

stated method for locating fish, and not part of the offshore plan quoted here.

a break found at that distance is the one documented heading that would carry a

## Spots

Three charted spots (cameron), listed east to west. They group as a tight
eastern pair plus one long western leg:

- **The eastern pair — the 295 and the Baja - 270**, **3.2 nm** apart — the
  second-tightest pair of charted banks in the library's Baja offshore list,
  behind only the 179 and the plain 220 at **2.7 nm** (cameron). The 295 is the
  easternmost mark in the zone and the closest of the three to Ensenada at
  **39.3 nm** from Punta San Miguel; it is also closer to the 480 and the 385 in
  the next zone east than it is to the Double 220 in its own.
- **The Double 220** — the western end, **8.6 nm** from the Baja - 270 and
  **11.1 nm** from the 295, which is the pair that sets the zone's span against
  the census's 12 nm cap. It is the furthest mark from Ensenada at **49.8 nm**,
  the shallowest-reading name in the zone at 220 fathoms, and near-equidistant
  between the 295 and the 1010 Trench in the zone west of it (cameron).

Charted positions for all three are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
