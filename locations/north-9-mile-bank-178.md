# North 9 Mile Bank / 178

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is two charted marks in the water off San
Diego — **North 9 Mile Bank** at **32°38.000'N 117°26.000'W** and the **178** at
**32°42.000'N 117°29.317'W** (cameron). They lie **4.9 nm** apart, the 178
bearing **325°** from North 9 Mile Bank and North 9 Mile Bank **145°** back, so
the whole zone spans well under half the census's **12 nm** diameter cap.

**This is the inshore end of the region's bank grid, and the bank's name is what
says so.** Nine Mile Bank is the worked example of a bank named for **distance
rather than depth** — it "has so many high spots all over this thing" at similar
depths, coming up "almost as high as 600 feet" in places, so no single fathom
number describes it, and the name records that it sits **9 statute miles off the
beach** rather than in 9 fathoms (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). Measured against
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md), the two marks sit **7.2 nm** and
**7.9 nm** from the nearest charted coastal position (cameron) — closer to the
beach than any other bank in the library except **La Jolla Canyon**, which is a
canyon on the shelf, and **South 9 Mile Bank**, which is the next mark down the
same complex.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the two named exceptions are not the only
ones. Measuring every entry in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)'s
offshore-banks section against its nearest charted coastal position puts five
more banks inside these figures — the **97** at **1.7 nm** off Punta
Salsipuedes, **Todos Santos Island** at **3.1 nm**, **Banda Bank** at **4.3 nm**,
the **60** at **5.7 nm** and the **330** at **6.7 nm** — all of them Baja marks
against Baja coastal positions. The claim holds only if it is silently scoped to
US/SoCal water, which it does not say.

**Both of this zone's marks read into one fishery's working-spot list.** The
daytime deep-drop swordfish list — "the ridge or the 9 or the 178 or the 302 …
or even the 43" — names the **178** outright and names "the 9," which is this
bank complex under its short name (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`,
[swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says
which of the library's three Nine Mile waypoints "the 9" means, so only the 178
is pinned to a position. The corpus also puts a rockfish run on the bank
(`yn1suHH6b1Y`) and one yellowfin day on "the upper nine" (`ouBrIdO7d4k`), and
**North 9 Mile Bank is a BightSST eval target** (cameron,
[eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)) — so unlike most bank zones, its
conditions come off a scored spot rather than a bare position pull.

What the corpus does not give is a program: no run, no start mark, no depth
sounding, no season for anything but the swordfish. Those gaps are flagged below
and aggregate in [the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run distance, heading, time or fuel figure
to this zone from any port, and no source names the port either mark is fished
out of.

**The bank's own name is the corpus's only distance statement, and the library
cannot check it exactly.** Nine Mile Bank is named for sitting **9 statute
miles** off the coast (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Measured to the nearest charted positions
the library carries: the **Point Loma Pipe** is **7.9 nm** — **9.1 statute
miles** — at **073°** from North 9 Mile Bank, and **Widow Rock** in
[La Jolla](la-jolla.md) is **7.2 nm** — **8.3 statute miles** — at **057°** from
the 178 (cameron). Both of those are themselves offshore fishing marks rather
than shoreline positions, so the figures bracket the name without confirming it.

[The zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md) writes it "9 (statute) miles", parenthesising

**The bay entrance is the useful reference, and it is inside 14 nm.** The
**Zuniga Jetty** at the San Diego Bay entrance is **10.9 nm** at **078°** from
North 9 Mile Bank and **13.5 nm** at **097°** from the 178 (cameron). For scale,
the [Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) run **~13 nm** from that same
entrance (cameron, [Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)) — this zone is
the same order of run, on a heading that stays in US water.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states a run time, sea-state envelope or

**This is the water an ugly outside forecast does not automatically close.** The
**Catalina Eddy** can shield the **inner San Diego banks** and tuna grounds while
it blows outside, so the eddy state is the thing to check when the offshore
forecast is bad ([island structure](island-structure.md),
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md),
[Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)). Separately, a strong NW event
crushes the northern Channel Islands and the LA/Ventura shelf hardest while
**south of Newport** is less prone to wind-wave rollover, and the Coronados,
Catalina and San Clemente held their water through one such event
(`HzE4FHHcvTk`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md)). Both marks lie south of
Newport and inshore of every zone in that comparison (cameron).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The two positions.** North 9 Mile Bank is at **32°38.000'N 117°26.000'W** and
the 178 at **32°42.000'N 117°29.317'W** (cameron) — **4.0 minutes of latitude**
and **3.317 minutes of longitude** apart, **4.9 nm** on a northwest–southeast
line.

**One mark is a complex, the other is a number, and the corpus explains the
difference.** A bank is an isolated high spot rising out of otherwise deep water
and takes the **fathom depth of its shallowest point** as its name, six feet to
a fathom — 182 tops out at 182 fathoms, 209 at 209, 43 at 43
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). Nine Mile Bank is the stated
**exception**: it carries many closely spaced high spots of similar depth rather
than one dominant peak, so no single fathom figure describes it, and it takes its
distance off the beach as a name instead (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read the two marks that
way:

| spot | what the name states | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North 9 Mile Bank | 9 statute miles off the coast — a distance, not a depth; high spots up to roughly 600 ft in places, exact figure uncertain in the source audio (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`) | about 600 ft at the shallowest reported |
| 178 | read by the naming convention, 178 fathoms | about 1,068 ft |

**Those two figures put the zone across a fishery boundary.** The bank's
~**600 ft** crown sits inside the **300–900 ft** deep band the rockfish router
works ([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)), while **178
fathoms** falls inside the **150–350 fathom** contour band the SoCal daytime
swordfish fishery works ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md),
[deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)). Both fisheries
show up in the corpus for this zone, and the depth spread is why.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms 178 as a charted sounding, or gives
relief, bottom composition or surrounding depth at either mark.

**"North" is a position on a bank, not a bank of its own.** The library carries
three Nine Mile waypoints — **North 9 Mile Bank**, **South 9 Mile Bank**
(**7.3 nm** at **144°**) and **Lower 9 Mile Bank** (**7.2 nm** at **139°**) — and
the three span only **7.3 nm**, inside the **12 nm** clustering cap (cameron).
The complex is nonetheless split across **three** zones on this ladder: the two
marks here, a South 9 Mile Bank / 439 grouping, and Lower 9 Mile Bank inside the
[Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) zone. What pushes the group past the cap
is not the bank but its neighbours — the **178** is **12.2 nm** from South 9 Mile
Bank and the **439** is **13.3 nm** from the 178 (cameron). ⚠ Flagged stub — no
corpus source yet: the South 9 Mile Bank / 439 zone has no page, so the nearest
hand-off out of this zone, **9.8 nm** from the centre, has nothing to hand off
to.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the stub is stale —
[South 9 Mile Bank / 439](south-9-mile-bank-439.md) exists, and it already

**The contour edge is the feature, not the crown.** On the banks this fishery
works — "the ridge or the 9 or the 178 or the 302 … or even the 43" — the
preferred drift is **down and in along the contour edges** where the fish dwell
and feed (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`). That is the corpus's only statement about how the
bottom here is worked, and it points at the edges rather than the high spots.
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat trips
([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
high-spot count, shape, preferred directions or current behaviour at either mark.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first**, measured from the zone centre (cameron):
**South 9 Mile Bank** at **9.8 nm** southeast, **Lower 9 Mile Bank** at **9.6
nm** southeast, the **439** at **10.9 nm** south-southeast, the **182** at
**12.5 nm** west, the **226** at **13.5 nm** southwest, the **166** at **13.5
nm** west, the **302 (Kidney Bank)** at **14.9 nm** south-southwest,
**[La Jolla Canyon](la-jolla-canyon.md)** at **15.9 nm** northeast, the **140**
at **16.7 nm** west-northwest, **The Corner** at **18.5 nm** west, the **138**
at **20.2 nm** west-northwest and the **51** at **20.7 nm** west-northwest. Of
those groupings, [The Corner / 140 / 182](corner-140-182.md),
[Coronados - 230 / 302 (Kidney Bank) / 226](coronados-230-302-226.md) and
[51 / 181 / 138](51-181-138.md) have pages; the 9 Mile marks southeast do not.
The **14 Mile Bank** — the other distance-named bank, and the reason the census
refused to merge the two — is **51.5 nm** northwest
([279 / 267 / 14 Mile Bank](279-267-14-mile-bank.md), cameron).

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the two are run as one circuit, which end to work
first, or what tide, light or swell do here.

**The one program the corpus attaches to these marks is the deep-drop drift.**
On the banks "where we usually fish … the ridge or the 9 or the 178 or the 302 …
or even the 43," the preferred set-up is a **down-and-in drift along the contour
edges** where the fish dwell and feed (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`). The operator's wind
envelope on a traditional straight NW wind is **10 to 12 or 13 kt** as the
maximum (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`); the species router
states the same fishery as a fair-weather one — **>15 kt = no-go**, comfortable
**10–13 kt**, drift **≤ ~2–2.5 kt**
([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md),
[deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)).

**Life on arrival is the read this zone is named in.** Rolling out to the spot —
"whether that's the 9 mile bank or the 14 or the 43" — and finding whales,
dolphins and birds is a good sign even though all of it sits a thousand feet
above where the bait is presented, because the reason the mammals and birds are
there is food (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`). The corpus's one field note from this water is a
bird: a probable **black-footed albatross** east of the 9 Mile Bank in August
2026, called unusually close to shore (cameron,
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md)) — inshore of these marks rather
than on them, and a sighting rather than a bite. Method is in
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md) and
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md); grading what the
sounder shows over the edges is
[electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).

**Bring a jig stick even when the day is a bottom trip.** The gear call for a run
"to the 9-mile bank fishing cods" is to carry a **jig-stick class** rod because
"you don't know what's going to jump out of the water" (`yn1suHH6b1Y`) — the
zone's mixed shallow-crown-and-deep-edge structure showing up as a tackle
decision. Classes are in [gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md); the rod
itself is [surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md), and the bottom program
is [rockfish deep-dropping](../techniques/rockfish-deep-dropping.md).

**Conditions for this zone come off a scored spot, which is unusual.** North 9
Mile Bank is one of the BightSST eval targets — the original set listed it as
"9 Mile Bank (San Diego)" and the expansion splits it into **North + South**
(cameron, [eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). Cameron's
turnover doctrine is that fishing is best **right before turnover** — the sharp
SST drop plus chlorophyll spike — so the output to read for this zone is the
approach, not the event. The **178** is not an eval target and is pulled per
position (cameron).

**Which of the two zone types this is depends on which mark you are on.** The
Bight's split is between a **~50–60 square-mile** complex you plan hours of
looking across and the compact spot you idle onto, grade on the meter, and either
commit to or leave inside **20–30 minutes** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). The bank is explicitly the many-high-spots kind
of ground (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), which reads as the search type; the 178 is a single
number **4.9 nm** away (cameron), which reads as the compact type.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): only the first half of that split is in
`Blh2BA-7Ono`. The transcript gives the big-box figure — "50 miles 60 square
miles to look … you should expect to spend quite a bit of time driving around" —
and scopes it to one specific group of banks below San Clemente on 2022-07-21,
not to a general Bight typology. It says **nothing** about a compact spot, about
idling on and grading the meter, or about a **20–30 minute** commit-or-leave
read; the only "20 30" in it describes birds moving "about 20 30 feet."
[The zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md) cites the video on its complex bullet only
and leaves the compact-spot bullet uncited — this page puts both halves under the
one cite.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source places either mark on that split.

**Report coverage is the one thing this zone should have.** These are inner banks
inside 14 nm of the bay entrance (cameron), so a quiet week here is more likely
to be real than at an outer bank nobody ran to — but ageing and advection still
apply to anything read off a report
([report reading](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)).

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron), listed northwest to southeast:

- **The 178** — the northwestern mark and the zone's inshore corner toward
  [La Jolla](la-jolla.md): **7.2 nm** from Widow Rock, the closest any bank in

  itself and South 9 Mile Bank down the same complex (cameron). Its name read by

  fathoms**, about 1,068 ft, which lands inside the **150–350 fathom** swordfish
  band — and it is named outright in the daytime deep-drop working-spot list

  describes the bottom.
- **North 9 Mile Bank** — the southeastern mark, the north end of the

  (`yn1suHH6b1Y`), the "the 9" of the swordfish list read against the library,

  the Mexican line down the complex (cameron).
