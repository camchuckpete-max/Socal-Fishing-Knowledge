# Two Harbors

The harbour at Catalina's isthmus, on the island's
[front side](catalina-island-front-side.md). In the corpus it is a **base, not a
bite**: it is the named tournament headquarters for the island's calico events,
where the dockside briefing is given and the fish are weighed (`iQLyBzhOSi8`,
`PKf7G3uL4io`, `IATPg9110CE`), and the departure point for runs to the outer
islands. No source fishes the harbour itself — the charted marks that ring it
are their own pages: [Ship Rock](ship-rock.md),
[Isthmus Reef](isthmus-reef.md) and [Bird Rock Reef](bird-rock-reef.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a charted position for the harbour itself.
[The spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) carries the reefs outside it but
not Two Harbors, so this page publishes no coordinates.

## Getting there

**The crossing is a zone-level figure, not a spot-level one.** The corpus gives
the run to Catalina's front side as roughly a **30 mi run** and about **2.5
hours** under way, neither tied to a named departure port
([Catalina Island — Front Side](catalina-island-front-side.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: distance, run time or sea-state envelope from
Long Beach, San Pedro, Newport or [Dana Point](dana-point.md) to this harbour,
and nothing on moorings, ramps, fuel or a bait receiver here.

**Outer water is measured from just outside this harbour.** Ship Rock, the
charted position off the isthmus, is the reference other pages run their
distances from (cameron) — Landing Cove **27.0 nm**, Kidney Bank **27.0 nm** and
the 175 **24.8 nm** (cameron,
[Kidney Bank (63) / 175](kidney-bank-63-175.md)).

**Santa Barbara Island is a short hop from the dock.** A private tournament boat
left Two Harbors and was on the island in **about an hour and 15–20 minutes**,
arriving in the dark to start on the beach at first light (`PKf7G3uL4io`,
[Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)).

**The outer islands are all reachable in a tournament day from here.** One team
ran from this base all the way to [San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md),
fished about three hours until the bite died, and ran back to Santa Barbara
Island the same day (`IATPg9110CE`); another crossed to San Clemente Island and
landed at [Northwest Harbor](northwest-harbor.md), taking the crossing despite
"some really big swells" (`iQLyBzhOSi8`).

## Structure & bathymetry

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: depth, bottom composition, shelter or holding
ground inside the harbour. What the corpus and the spot library do fix is what
sits immediately outside it — [Isthmus Reef](isthmus-reef.md) with a shoal
caution, plus [Ship Rock](ship-rock.md) and
[Bird Rock Reef](bird-rock-reef.md), all three within about six-tenths of a
minute of latitude of one another (cameron).

**All three of those neighbours carry the same closure edge**: stay **west of
the MPA at 118 29.300** (cameron). California / CDFW, as recorded in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) as of 2026-08 — verify current MPA
boundaries and take rules with CDFW before fishing.

## How it fishes

**This is a planning surface, and the corpus's clearest example of one.** The
dockside briefing given here named a stack of live variables without resolving
them — a full moon, "pretty big tides," "pretty extreme temperatures" and heavy
squid around the island, with the crew stating outright "we don't have a game
plan just yet" (`iQLyBzhOSi8`). Swell was tracked across the whole event window
from this dock: **5–7 ft** the day before day one, **4–6 ft** on day one, **4–6
ft** forecast for day two and **3–5 ft** for the following Sunday — an easing
trend — with a small craft advisory mentioned in passing for part of the field
(`iQLyBzhOSi8`). See [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md),
[moon](../conditions/moon.md) and
[report reading and forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md).

**The briefing's hazard framing is the island's, not the harbour's.** The local
islands were called a combination of thick kelp, razor-sharp boiler rocks and
big waves that "can ruin the day," and anglers were warned to be careful of the
big swell (`iQLyBzhOSi8`).

**The decision this base sets up is stay or run.** From this dock on the same
morning the field split three ways: stay at Catalina and grind the front side
(`PKf7G3uL4io`), run out to Santa Barbara Island (`IATPg9110CE`), or cross to
San Clemente Island (`iQLyBzhOSi8`). The front side the harbour opens onto is
also the island's shelter — the side a boat retreats to when the West End and
backside blow out (`PKf7G3uL4io`,
[Catalina Island — Front Side](catalina-island-front-side.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: current, wind or tide behaviour in and around
the harbour itself, and how the isthmus reefs outside it fish through a day.
