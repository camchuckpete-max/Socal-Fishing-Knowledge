# 81 / 381

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is three charted marks in the open water
southwest of San Clemente Island — the **81**, the **381** and the **267 (The
Condom)** (cameron) — spanning **13.2 minutes of latitude** (32°30.000'N to
32°43.167'N) and **16.6 minutes of longitude** (118°24.367'W to 118°41.000'W).
The 81 and the 381 clustered on their own coordinates **10.4 nm** apart; the 267
was attached on distance and, at **19.2 nm** from the 81, it is what sets the
zone's diameter.

**One mark here carries the corpus, and it carries one sentence.** In the
2022-10-20 Bight report the bluefin volume had slid out to Tanner and Cortez, and
the water being worked ran "from the 381 off the east end back side of Clementi
out to Tanner and Cortez," all of it slightly cooler (`OYOda6T3f-8`). That makes
the **381** the inshore anchor of a named fall search band. The **81** and the
**267** appear nowhere in the corpus but the coordinate library, and the corpus's
other "267" references are a different bank entirely.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run distance, heading, timing or fuel
figure to this zone from any port, and no source names a port these three marks
are fished out of.

**San Clemente Island is what this zone hangs off.** The 81 sits **2.4 nm** south
of Desperation Reef and **2.8 nm** south of the 29 Fathom Spot at the southeast
end of [the back side](san-clemente-island-back-side.md), and **5.9 nm** from
Pyramid Cove on [the front side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md); the 381 is
**11.0 nm** west-southwest of Desperation Reef, and the 267 **20.8 nm**
southwest of it (cameron). The island itself is an overnight-range destination
rather than a full-day run (`LTCFjqtSI8g`), described as roughly **60 miles
straight off the beach** (`XyKF34C5iB4`), so the reach to these marks is that
crossing plus a short leg — not a figure any source states.

**The mainland is a long way further.** The nearest charted mainland position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) is **Widow Rock** in
[La Jolla](la-jolla.md), **52.4 nm** from the 81 and **60.9 nm** from the zone
centre, with the **Point Loma Pipe** at **64.8 nm** from the centre (cameron).
The library carries no charted positions on the Los Angeles or Long Beach coast,
so those figures are a floor on the run, not a run distance from the ports a boat
would actually leave from. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: distance and run
time from any named port, and the sea-state envelope the crossing demands.

**The island end of this zone sits against a Navy range.** San Clemente Island is
a U.S. Navy training range rather than open coastline, and which areas are open
or closed rotates with the Navy's schedule
([island structure](island-structure.md#san-clemente-island--navy-controlled-access),
`XyKF34C5iB4`). That rule belongs to the island, not to these three offshore
marks, but the 81 lies **2.4 nm** off its back side (cameron). **Verify current
access with the Navy before working the island end of this zone.**

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries, restricted areas or
depth-based rockfish rules stated for any position in this zone. The access
advisories the spot library records nearby attach to San Clemente Island
shoreline spots, not to these three offshore marks (cameron).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three positions.** The 81 sits at **32°43.167'N 118°24.367'W**, the 381 at
**32°41.000'N 118°36.500'W** and the 267 (The Condom) at **32°30.000'N
118°41.000'W** (cameron). The 81 and the 381 are **10.4 nm** apart, inside the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, which is the clustering rule that grouped
them.

**The 267 was attached, not clustered.** It lies **11.6 nm** from the 381 but
**19.2 nm** from the 81, so a three-mark group would have had a **19.2 nm**
diameter — past the cap — and it did not cluster. The census attached it as the
nearest grouping it could be fished with, at **15.0 nm** from the 81/381 pair's
midpoint and inside the **25 nm** attach limit, against **19.2 nm** to the next
nearest grouping, the W. Butterfly / 157 / Mushroom marks to its east-southeast
(cameron). It is carried here as queued rather than re-parented, because the zone
list is set by the census and not by this page.

**All three marks lie on or beside the axis the corpus draws.** The October band
runs from the 381 out to Tanner and Cortez (`OYOda6T3f-8`); those two banks are
charted **26.5 nm** and **30.2 nm** from the 381 and **15.6 nm** from each other,
so the band is a corridor about **27 nm** long from the 381 to their midpoint
(cameron). The corridor bears **256°** from the 381. The **81** sits **0.4 nm**
off that line — but **10.4 nm** back beyond the 381's end of it, on the island
side. The **267** sits **9.7 nm** off the same line to the south-southeast,
**6.4 nm** — about a quarter — along it. So the zone is the band's inshore end
plus one mark just inshore of it and one off its southern flank.

**The names are the only depth claims in the zone.** A bank is an isolated high
spot rising out of otherwise deep water and takes the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way:

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 81 | 81 fathoms | about 486 ft |
| 267 (The Condom) | 267 fathoms | about 1,602 ft |
| 381 | 381 fathoms | about 2,286 ft |

Read as a set that is an **1,800 ft spread across 19.2 nm**, and the order does
not run with geography: the shallowest name is the mark closest to San Clemente,
the deepest is the middle mark, and the southern mark reads between them
(cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source confirms any of the three numbers as a charted depth, or says whether "The
Condom" describes the shape of the 267's high spot.

**The corpus's "267" is a different bank, and reading it onto this zone would be
wrong.** Both times a 267 is named it is grouped with the San Diego local banks —
"high spots in the middle of the ocean that are 267 fathoms deep, 181 fathoms
deep, 209 fathoms deep" (`m-M0iwX8DjA`), and a warm pocket lying "between the
14 M Bank, the 209, the 267" (`-bw1KDfDjv4`). The spot library charts a separate
**279 / 267** at **33°17.800'N 117°49.150'W**, **64.7 nm northeast** of the 267
(The Condom) (cameron), which is the mark those two passages fit. This is the
collision Cameron's landmass-prefix naming rule exists for
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source names the 267
(The Condom) at all, so it has no fathom confirmation, no shape and no fishing
record here.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: bottom composition, relief, high-spot shape, surrounding depth, preferred
directions, drift or current behaviour at any of these three marks.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron): **Desperation Reef** and the
**29 Fathom Spot** at **2.4–2.8 nm** from the 81, the **157** at **13.3 nm** from
the 267, **Mackerel Bank** at **19.0 nm** from the 81, the **289** at **20.3 nm**
from the 81, **W. Butterfly** at **21.1 nm** from the 267, the **43** at
**22.2 nm** from the 81, **Tanner Bank** at **26.5 nm** from the 381 and **Cortes
Bank** at **30.2 nm** from the 381. Of those, only the two San Clemente Island
sides are built
as pages on this ladder. ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: the
W. Butterfly / 157, Tanner Bank / Cortes Bank and E. Butterfly / San Salvador
Knoll groupings that surround this zone are unbuilt, so a fallback plan out of
here has nothing to hand off to.

## How it fishes

**The fall program here starts on the chart, not on a mark.** The October regime
is to "look 100% at your temp chart and chlorophyll," with bluefin on the **cool**
side of a break when the water is warm and the warm side when it is cold
(`OYOda6T3f-8`, [October](../seasonal/october.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)). In the documented October that read
pointed at the slightly cooler band, and this zone was its inshore end.

**Without a break to work, the banks are what the fish hold to — but not
exclusively.** Across that big area, absent any differentiation in temperature the
fish are most likely associating with structure like the banks, while an
open-water school is possible anywhere in the same water (`OYOda6T3f-8`). The
zone-level consequence: the three marks are where the meter time goes, and the
water between them still earns glassing
([search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md),
[electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**The band is in motion, so a report ages against you.** The corpus describes this
water as a corridor the fish were moving along rather than a spot they were on
(`OYOda6T3f-8`), which is exactly the case the break-aging and advection rules in
[report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md) are
for: check where the cooler band has gone before pointing the boat at the 381.

**Which side of the search-box split this zone sits on is unstated.** The Bight's
two zone types are the complex — a **~50–60 square-mile** box you plan hours of
looking across — and the compact spot you idle onto, grade on the meter, and
either commit to or leave (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). The
one corpus line here describes a corridor **27 nm** long (cameron), which reads
like the first type, but ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source places the 81, the 381
or the 267 on either side of that split.

**The seasonal frame is the outbound leg of the fall condensation.** September
holds the bigger bluefin in the volume square — Santa Barbara Island, San
Nicolas, San Clemente, Catalina — and as the coast cools and fragments the fish
condense outward toward Tanner and Cortez
([September](../seasonal/september.md), [October](../seasonal/october.md),
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)). This zone is charted between the square's
San Clemente corner and that Tanner/Cortez terminus (cameron), which is the
geometry the one corpus line here describes.

**No mark here is a BightSST eval target.** Cortes Bank, at the far end of the
same band, is one; the 81, the 381 and the 267 are not
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)), so conditions for this zone are
pulled per charted position rather than read off a scored spot.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the three are run as one circuit, which end to
work first, or what tide, light and current do here.

## Spots

Three charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. Two clustered on their own
coordinates and one was attached on distance:

- **The 81** — the island end of the zone, and the closest mark in the spot
  library's offshore-banks list to any charted San Clemente Island position:
  **2.4 nm** off Desperation Reef, against **9.0 nm** for the next nearest,
  Mackerel Bank (cameron). Its name reads as the shallowest crest here,
  81 fathoms. Coordinates only; the corpus never names it.
- **The 381** — the middle mark and the only position in this zone the corpus
  carries: the inshore anchor of the October 2022 band running out to Tanner and
  Cortez (`OYOda6T3f-8`).
- **The 267 (The Condom)** — the southern mark, attached to this zone rather than
  clustered into it, and the closest position here to the W. Butterfly / 157
  grouping (**13.3 nm**). Coordinates only; the corpus's "267" mentions are the
  San Diego 279 / 267, **64.7 nm** away.

Charted positions for all three are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position and, for the 381, the single cite above, so each spot page is
coordinates plus its parent zone until more sources arrive.
