# Ensenada

**A zone is a run grouping.** Ensenada is the working port of
[Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md) and the one place in the region
where the corpus documents a whole day, bait to bottom. Four charted positions
(cameron) bracket the coast either side of it — **Punta San Miguel** at
**31°54.110'N 116°44.980'W** in the north, **Punta Morro** at **31°52.185'N
116°41.260'W**, **San Miguel Reef** at **31°51.200'N 116°48.100'W**, and **Punta
Banda** at **31°45.430'N 116°45.330'W** in the south, whose library label
carries a boiler caution. End to end that is about **8.7 nm**, computed from the
library's own positions.

**What is fished here is mostly not those four marks.** The corpus attaches its
Ensenada material to distances from the marina rather than to charted names: a
bait stop just outside it, a high spot **7–8 mi** out (`CKq0Z6ExVs4`), a reef
and point a short run down the coast with a rock drop-off to about **300 ft**
outside it (`0dIwWiOc1NY`), and offshore grounds **20–50 mi** west
(`0bcDBGzQnGw`, `Ix0gG0-l3v0`). Those unnamed positions are the zone's real
program; the offshore grounds are a separate run into the numbered-bank zones
and are treated as such below.

## Getting there

**Ensenada is a drive-to port, worked out of the Hotel Coral marina.** Bait is
made just outside the marina and the trips in this corpus depart from it
(`0dIwWiOc1NY`, `CKq0Z6ExVs4`, `0bcDBGzQnGw`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
the road route, drive time or border-crossing detail to get there, the ramp,
hoist, fuel or haul-out situation, or any second port on this stretch of coast.

**The distance ladder out of the marina is the zone's planning spine:**

| step | distance from the marina | source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bait | just outside the marina | `0dIwWiOc1NY` |
| The cresting high spot | 7–8 mi | `CKq0Z6ExVs4` |
| The offshore temperature break | 20–30 mi due west | `0bcDBGzQnGw` |
| A floating object holding fish | about 35 mi | `0bcDBGzQnGw` |
| Yellowfin reported showing | 30–40 mi out | `0dIwWiOc1NY` |

| The August 2024 tournament bank | 42–43 mi | `Ix0gG0-l3v0` |

One tournament day totalled roughly **180 mi** run, with about **40 mi** still
to get in at the point that figure was called out (`U1AgwmlY5bI`) — the honest
measure of what an offshore day out of this port costs in fuel and hours.

**Punta Banda breaks.** The library labels the southern waypoint *caution —
boilers* (cameron): rock that stands up and breaks when the swell is up, on the
headland a boat passes running south out of the zone
([island structure](island-structure.md) carries the general read on boilers).

**The port has also been a fly-out point.** An earlier Cedros access route ran a
small-plane charter out of Ensenada with the Mexican fishing permits obtained in
Tijuana (`xIUKmH9ccgQ`), so which airport a Cedros operator uses and who handles
the paperwork are per-operator questions ([Cedros Island](cedros-island.md)).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
closures, protected areas, restricted zones or local rules anywhere in this
zone, including whether anything applies inside the bay or at Punta Banda.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the sea-state envelope this coast demands,
which winds shut the zone down, or how a small private boat should time the run
out and back.

## Structure & bathymetry

**Three headlands and one reef, and only one of them sits where its name
suggests.** Punta San Miguel, Punta Morro and Punta Banda are named points;
**San Miguel Reef** is the library's only named reef here and is charted
**3.9 nm southwest** of the point it shares a name with, which puts it off the
coast rather than on it (cameron). Punta Morro at **116°41.260'W** is the
easternmost position in the zone and San Miguel Reef at **116°48.100'W** the
westernmost — about **6.9 minutes of longitude** apart. The four positions
enclose water rather than tracing a straight shoreline: Punta San Miguel to
Punta Morro is **3.7 nm**, Punta Morro to Punta Banda **7.6 nm**, and Punta
Banda back up to San Miguel Reef **6.2 nm**, all computed from the library's
charted positions.

**The high spot is the zone's one measured piece of structure, and it crests
hard.** A mark **7–8 mi** off the marina comes up to about **25 ft from roughly
200 ft** (`CKq0Z6ExVs4`) — the sharpest relief the corpus records on this coast,
and the reason the region page reads coastal structure here as points and high
spots. It is not one of the four charted positions.

**Outside the reef and point structure the bottom drops to about 300 ft over
rock.** A short run down the coast from the marina, the sounder showed rocky
bottom at that depth and it fished as the day's bottom stop (`0dIwWiOc1NY`).

**Neighbouring charted water sits close on both sides.** Punta Salsipuedes, the
southern end of [La Fonda / Bajamar / Salsipuedes](la-fonda-bajamar-salsipuedes.md),
is **6.0 nm** north of Punta San Miguel, and La Bufadora, the northern end of
[Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md), is **2.6 nm** south of
the Punta Banda waypoint (cameron) — the two coastal zones this one hands off
to. The numbered bank **the 97** lies **6.8 nm** northwest of Punta San Miguel.

**Todos Santos Island is charted inside this zone's footprint but assigned
elsewhere.** Its position, **31°48.000'N 116°47.400'W** (cameron), sits **3.5 nm**
from the centre of these four spots — closer in than Punta Banda itself — yet
the coordinate census groups it with Banda Bank as its own zone. The grouping is
left as queued rather than changed here. ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet:
the Banda Bank / Todos Santos Island zone has no page, so the island in the
middle of this bay has nothing to hand off to.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: charted depth, bottom composition, kelp extent
or relief at any of the four charted positions, and no position at all for the
7–8 mi high spot or the 300 ft rock drop-off — both are described by distance
and depth only.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the bay itself. Bahía de Todos Santos and the
harbour water inside it carry no fishing content in the corpus, which is why the
region page carries no `bay-harbor` water term despite a sand bass taken at the
bait stop (`0dIwWiOc1NY`).

this port** — the temperature break, the floating object and the tournament bank
sit 20–50 mi west, in the numbered-bank zones, not on these four marks.

## How it fishes

**The documented day is a ladder, and it moves outward as each step dies.**
Make mackerel just outside the marina, run a short way down the coast to a point
with reef structure for yellowtail, and when that goes quiet slide out to the
**300 ft** rock for bottomfish (`0dIwWiOc1NY`). Nothing in that day was bought,
booked or guessed — the guide located structure, the crew made their own bait,
and the fallback was one move offshore rather than a run home.

**Bait is made, not bought, and there are two of it.** Mackerel spawn close to
shore and spread out in late spring and early summer, which is when bait is easy;
the "Spanish jack" is a slower swimmer that stays alive in the tank for hours,
the "greenback" is smaller, flashier and more energetic (`0dIwWiOc1NY`,
[making bait](../bait/making-bait.md)).

**Gray light is the gate on the inshore yellowtail bite.** Yellowtail feed
hardest in the gray light early in the morning; the reef/point stop that
produced none was fished after that window with the tide called "off"
(`0dIwWiOc1NY`, [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md#feeding-triggers)). Plan
the point for first light and the bottom for the middle of the day, not the
other way round.

**Two presentations run at once on the point rather than one at a time.**
Slow-troll a couple of live mackerel off the stern while casting jigs from the
bow at the same structure — the stated logic is that the surface splashes from
the cast jigs pull fish into the area alongside the trolled bait rather than
competing with it (`0dIwWiOc1NY`, [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md),
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)).

**The bottom stop is also split between two methods.** Half the crew fished
[slow-pitch jigging](../techniques/slow-pitch-jigging.md) — drop to bottom, slow
half-turns and gentle lifts so the jig flutters down like a wounded baitfish —
while the rest fished a classic dead-bait dropper rig, a couple of hooks above a
weight reeled up off the rocks and left to soak; both produced steadily
(`0dIwWiOc1NY`, [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md)).

**On the high spot, reset the drift instead of fishing it blind.** The captain
repositioned mid-session when the boat's drift reversed from what the first pass
had shown, setting up **up-drift** of the structure to get a clean window to drop
onto it (`CKq0Z6ExVs4`,
[electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**The offshore day out of this port is chart-first, not spot-first.** The stated
plan is to run **20–30 mi due west** to a temperature break, then continue north
or west off it by sea conditions, with a fallback search **20–30 mi north**; the
crew deliberately worked away from the rest of the fleet sitting 45–50 mi out
(`0bcDBGzQnGw`, [water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). Trolling is used to
locate — cedar plugs around a large dolphin school — and the conversion when the
school broke into a full foamer was to stop trolling and work
[flylined sardine](../techniques/flyline.md) plus
[chunked](../techniques/chunking.md) cut bait into it (`0bcDBGzQnGw`).

**The tournament program on the bluefin bank is troll-and-glass, then commit.**
Two [Mad Macs](../lures/mad-mac.md) staggered at roughly an **80 count** and a
**50 count** so they would not cross, glassing from the tower with
gyro-stabilized binoculars, a zone abandoned after about **45 minutes** with no
marks or surface sign, a three-bar
[spreader-bar](../lures/spreader-bar.md) spread deployed once bait finally showed
scattered rather than bunched, and the [kite](../techniques/kite-fishing.md)
saved for the final day on the best sign (`U1AgwmlY5bI`, `Ix0gG0-l3v0`,
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)).

**Moons mark arrivals here, not just bites.** New waves of bluefin were observed
arriving out of the west on the moon into this zone (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[moon](../conditions/moon.md)).

## Spots

Four charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. They are the coastal frame
around the port rather than the ground the corpus fishes:

- **The north headland** — Punta San Miguel, the zone's northernmost position and
  the handoff to [La Fonda / Bajamar / Salsipuedes](la-fonda-bajamar-salsipuedes.md)
  6.0 nm further up the coast.
- **The eastern corner** — Punta Morro, the easternmost charted position here,
  3.7 nm southeast of Punta San Miguel.
- **The outside reef** — San Miguel Reef, the only reef the library names in this
  zone and its westernmost position, charted 3.9 nm southwest of the point it is
  named for.
- **The south headland** — Punta Banda, logged *caution — boilers* (cameron). The
  coastal zone immediately south is named for this headland even though the
  waypoint itself is charted in the Ensenada section — a naming overlap to expect
  when reading [Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md).

The bait ground just outside the marina, the 7–8 mi high spot cresting to 25 ft
(`CKq0Z6ExVs4`), and the reef and point a short run down-coast with the 300 ft
rock drop-off outside it (`0dIwWiOc1NY`) are all fished ground the library does
not name as spots; they stay in
[the evidence file](evidence/ensenada.md) rather than becoming pages. Charted
positions for all four are in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md); the
per-spot pages carry them.
