# Honeymoon Bay

**[Baja only]** — the **windy-day option** in the
[Loreto & Puerto Escondido](loreto.md) island cluster: a bay called *"pretty
sheltered"* from the boat, with a sailboat lying at anchor in it, and with
*"that is good yellowtail on the corner down there"* (`ikiXx9sUsoA`). It is the
one place in the zone the corpus names for **shelter** rather than for a fish
that came out of it.

**Nothing was fished here on camera.** The whole record is one passing sighting
on the way out of Puerto Escondido for an evening troll — the crew named the
bay, looked at it, and carried on across the bay and around the island
(`ikiXx9sUsoA`). Read the page as a place identified, not as a fishery
described.

⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): **which island this bay lies against is never
stated.** The KB carries it as Isla Carmen ([the zone page](loreto.md#structure--bathymetry),
[the spot harvest](../sources/spot-harvest.md)), but in the same passage the
speaker points out *"that's Carmen far in the back over there"* while the bay is
in nearer view (`ikiXx9sUsoA`). Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
That library stops at San Quintín on the Pacific side and holds nothing on this
coast, so a conditions pull has to run against
[the zone](loreto.md) rather than against this bay.

## Getting there

**You arrive in the zone's program, and this bay is something you pass.** The
documented trip flew in through **CBX** into the Tijuana airport, lived aboard a
**300–308 ft** mothership and fished off an **18 ft** Grady White towed behind
it, with **Puerto Escondido Marina** as the working base (`ikiXx9sUsoA`). The
access, the operators and the day shape are all on
[the zone page](loreto.md#getting-there).

**The sighting was made outbound on the first afternoon**, after fuelling and
running out to look for the evening bite; from there the boat went *"all the way
across that bay"* and around behind the island rather than into it
(`ikiXx9sUsoA`).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md), [the zone page](loreto.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**Two properties are on the record, and that is all: it is protected, and it
has a corner.** The bay is called *"pretty sheltered"*, which is what makes it
the option when the wind is up, and the ground named inside it is *"the corner
down there"* (`ikiXx9sUsoA`). Neither the corner's aspect, its bottom, nor its
depth is given.

**The zone's shallow soundings do not belong to this bay.** The **30 ft** a boat
looked down and found, and the **10 ft** called on the sounder with structure
visible through the water, were both recorded on a bird chase elsewhere in the
zone, not here (`ikiXx9sUsoA`) — they sit on
[the zone page](loreto.md#structure--bathymetry) and are not transferable to
this ground.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: depth, bottom composition, relief, the extent
of the bay, what the corner is made of, or any sounder picture from inside it
([electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

## How it fishes

**Shelter is the reason to come here, and it is the only read the corpus
gives.** The bay is the named answer to wind in a zone where wind protection
otherwise comes off the island itself — a sierra on the same trip came off rock
*"nice and protected from the wind"* (`ikiXx9sUsoA`). See
[sea state & wind](../conditions/sea-state.md) and
[the zone page](loreto.md#how-it-fishes) for the wider windy-day picture.

**No technique is documented at the corner.** The zone's programs — the troll
between stops, running at working birds, burning surface baits when fish are up
— are what the crews did elsewhere in these islands
([trolling](../techniques/trolling.md),
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md),
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)); nothing in the corpus says which
of them the corner wants, or from which side to work it.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: tide, current, moon or time-of-day window for
this bay; how long to give the corner; the line class, lure or approach for it;
or how it fishes for anyone not on a mothership tender.
