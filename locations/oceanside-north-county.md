# Oceanside / North County

The mainland coastal strip of San Diego's north county: **18 charted spots** in
the spot library (cameron), running from **San Onofre Kelp (33°20.880'N
117°34.050'W)** at the Camp Pendleton end south to **Flat Rock (32°54.755'N
117°15.755'W)** off Del Mar. It is a **kelp-and-pipe coast** — standing kelp
beds, three named outfall pipes, four hard-bottom rockfish areas and a canyon
head — and it is the neighbour of, not the same fishery as, the built-reef
[North County artificial reefs](north-county-artificial-reefs.md) that sit on
the same stretch of water. The strip is also the north end of the Bight's best
[kelp paddy](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md) water (`HzE4FHHcvTk`).

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the ports and launch ramps this zone is run
from, run distance or time to any spot, and the sea-state envelope the run
demands. The one departure the corpus records is a charter that left **Dana
Point** — the zone to the north — and worked south down the coast to the
Camp Pendleton end (`HMdrP4-i9MM`).

**Oceanside is a named break wall**, listed among the SoCal walls worth
fishing in their own right and reachable without a boat
(`XyKF34C5iB4`) — read it as
[break walls, jetties and riprap](breakwalls-jetties-riprap.md), not as one of
the 18 offshore-of-the-beach spots below.

**One MPA edge is charted in this zone.** The spot library carries a single
boundary note on the strip: at **Encinitas rockfish / Devil's Rock North**,
stay **north of 33°03.000'N** to stay out of the SMCA (cameron). No other spot
here carries a boundary note, and latitude alone does not establish where a
boundary runs — several spots south of that line sit at different points along
the coast. California / CDFW — verify current MPA boundaries, closures and take
rules with CDFW before fishing (as of 2026-08).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether the Camp Pendleton stretch at the
north end carries a military or restricted-area rule of its own.

## Structure & bathymetry

The zone is a **north–south ribbon of mainland structure**, about 26 minutes of
latitude long and never far off the beach, and it sorts into four families
(cameron):

- **Kelp beds — five.** San Onofre Kelp, Barn Kelp, Terramar Kelp, Solana Beach
  Kelp and Powerhouse Kelp. **San Onofre is the corpus's canonical example of a
  named SoCal coastal kelp bed**, used as the stand-in for "the kelp bed" in
  inshore instruction alongside La Jolla and Point Loma (`xI9tPJFXbUM`,
  `kzD0kSnnVPw`) and as the coastal counterpart to San Clemente Island
  (`BdRX4b8Fo5w`).
- **Outfall pipes — three.** Buccaneer Pipe, Encina Powerplant Pipe and
  Anderson Pipe. Sewer and outfall pipes run **all up and down this coast** and
  are deep-water structure that aggregates bait and the bass that follow it;
  the **Buccaneer Pipe** is named on camera among the *larger*-structure
  examples, as opposed to an isolated wreck or small reef (`Kf5wk_TFgTc`).
- **Hard-bottom rockfish areas — four.** Box Canyon, Leucadia, Encinitas
  rockfish / Devil's Rock North, and Del Mar rockfish area — the library's own
  labels for the zone's rock (cameron).
- **A canyon head — one.** Carlsbad Canyon (33°07.500'N 117°21.400'W), sitting
  immediately inshore-adjacent to the Encina Powerplant Pipe and Terramar Kelp
  at nearly the same latitude (cameron).

The remaining five — **Clam Beds**, **Golf Ball**, **Ponto**, **Beacons** and
**Flat Rock** — are named local spots the library carries by position only.

**The one measured bottom in the corpus:** off the Camp Pendleton stretch,
**hard bottom with light kelp growth that does not reach the surface** — kelp
riding roughly **20 ft off the bottom** in **~40–42 ft** of water
(`HMdrP4-i9MM`). That is a sub-surface kelp signature a boat looking for
standing canopy will run straight past.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: charted depths, relief and bottom
composition for the named kelp beds, pipes, rockfish areas and Carlsbad
Canyon; the depth of the canyon head; and what "Golf Ball", "Clam Beds",
"Ponto" and "Beacons" actually are.

**Naming collision.** The corpus's only other "Flat Rock" is a **Palos Verdes**
kelp spot named beside "shark fin" and "below Rocky" (`0KQ--N5TjqE`,
`OYOda6T3f-8`) — a different place from this zone's Flat Rock off Del Mar. Do
not read Palos Verdes reports onto this spot.

## What's there

| [Calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md) | year-round; bass bite through the winter, with water temperature setting how active they are (`Kf5wk_TFgTc`) | Calico relate to the kelp and shallow reef here, and were taken over hard bottom with sub-surface kelp in ~40–42 ft in October, grading to **5 lb** fish repeatedly and a **9 lb** best (`HMdrP4-i9MM`) |
| [Sand bass](../species/sand-bass.md) | year-round, and **the reliable winter pattern** on this coast (`Kf5wk_TFgTc`) | Sand bass sit mostly on deep-water structure and are rarely in the kelp — which is what the zone's three pipes and its hard-bottom areas are for (`Kf5wk_TFgTc`); they mixed into the October calico catch off Camp Pendleton (`HMdrP4-i9MM`) |

**The paddy fish are outside the spots, not on them.** The best kelp-paddy
water in the Bight runs **from Dana Point to Oceanside and southward**;
paddies north of Dana Point are scarcer and hold fewer fish, and the closer to
a dock and the more traffic, the less willing they are (`HzE4FHHcvTk`). That
is open water offshore of this strip rather than a property of the 18 coastal
spots — see [kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md) and
[August](../seasonal/august.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether white seabass, halibut, bonito or
sheephead are targeted anywhere on this strip, and no month-by-month season is
scoped to this zone for any species.

## How it fishes

**Read the current before you pick a corner of the kelp.** At a named kelp bed
— San Onofre is the worked example — check which way the current runs at that
spot first: **downhill current → work the northwest corner; uphill current →
work the southeast corner** (`kzD0kSnnVPw`). Standing in front of the San
Onofre bed, fronds all laid pointing toward San Diego reads as downhill
current, and the **front (up-current) edge** is where the bigger fish stack
once the current turns on, because that is where food is being delivered
(`sIoNELGlxmk`). **Anchor the spot; do not drift through the bed** — drifting
through gives up the edge-holding pattern entirely (`sIoNELGlxmk`). Full
mechanism in [current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md), and the
structure read in [bass structure](bass-structure.md).

**Bait capacity is the program.** Whether the day is San Clemente Island or the
coast at San Onofre, the lever is raising the **prey-density level** in the
water until fish that were not feeding come over and eat (`BdRX4b8Fo5w`); a
small boat that cannot carry sportboat-scale bait gets the same effect from a
chum bucket tied off (`xI9tPJFXbUM`). Execution is
[chunking](../techniques/chunking.md) and
[fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md).

**When the kelp goes off, go to the pipes.** Calico relating to kelp and
shallow reef are the first to shut down when runoff dirties the inshore water,
while the deep marks stay fishable — the winter retreat is the deep structure,
and a crankbait ground through bait balls suspended over a pipeline works all
cold season ([February–March](../seasonal/february-march.md),
[inshore crankbaits](../techniques/inshore-crankbaits.md)). Structure size
picks the method: an **isolated** wreck or small reef is fished vertically,
because the bass are either on it or they are not; a **larger** piece like one
of the coast's pipes or a big artificial-reef system holds bass scattered
across it, so you cast around and move to find them (`Kf5wk_TFgTc`). The
vertical build is a sinking slug-style bait or a
[slow-pitch jig](../techniques/slow-pitch-jigging.md) fished on the fall; the
covering-water build is [swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md) and
[inshore crankbaits](../techniques/inshore-crankbaits.md).

**On the iron, dial the retrieve tighter here than at the islands.** The
smaller mainland schools from **Del Mar down to Imperial Beach** run markedly
more finicky than a big Coronados school on the same gear, especially on
bigger-grade fish (`VpW91AKOFVQ`) — see
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md).

**The worked coastal day in the corpus** (October, hard bottom with sub-surface
kelp in ~40–42 ft): [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) straight down
for anglers who cannot cast, and [flylined](../techniques/flyline.md) live
sardine for everyone else, producing mixed calico and sand bass
(`HMdrP4-i9MM`).

## Spots

Eighteen charted spots (cameron), listed north to south, grouped by what the
structure is rather than by how close they sit:

- **The Pendleton end** — San Onofre Kelp, Box Canyon rockfish area, Barn Kelp.
  The northernmost and westernmost water in the zone, and the stretch the
  corpus's one worked coastal day was fished on (`HMdrP4-i9MM`).
- **The Oceanside stretch** — Clam Beds and Golf Ball (a tight pair about
  1 minute of longitude apart), Buccaneer Pipe.
- **The Carlsbad stretch** — Encina Powerplant Pipe, Terramar Kelp, Carlsbad
  Canyon, Anderson Pipe, Ponto. Pipe, kelp and canyon head sitting within a few
  minutes of each other.
- **The Encinitas / Leucadia stretch** — Leucadia rockfish area, Beacons,
  Encinitas rockfish / Devil's Rock North (the zone's one charted MPA edge).
- **The south end** — Solana Beach Kelp, Powerhouse Kelp, Del Mar rockfish
  area, Flat Rock. The stretch the finicky-mainland-iron doctrine starts from
  (`VpW91AKOFVQ`).

Charted positions for all eighteen are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No individual spot in the zone
carries corpus detail beyond its position.
