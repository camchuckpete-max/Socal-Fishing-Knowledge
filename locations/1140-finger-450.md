# 1140 Finger / 450

**A zone is a run grouping.** 1140 Finger / 450 is the piece of the numbered
offshore-bank grid that lies southwest of Punta Banda, off the Santo Tomás
coast — three charted positions (cameron) stepping down **16.7 minutes of
latitude** (31°27.200'N to 31°10.500'N) inside **8.5 minutes of longitude**
(117°02.500'W to 117°11.000'W). The **450** and the **1140 Finger** are
**10.4 nm** apart and are the pair the zone is named for; the **Lower 500**
further southeast was attached to them by the census as the nearest grouping it
could be fished with.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these three marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid — fifty-one charted positions over seventeen bank zones with
no depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
spot names claim, and what is missing.

**Two things make it worth reading anyway.** It carries the **deepest-reading
name in the spot library** — nothing else in the library numbers higher than the
1140 — and it is the zone where the documented Ensenada offshore day *runs out*:
its northern mark sits inside the corpus's stated distance bands and its southern
mark sits past every one of them.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The zone is a dogleg, not a line and not a cluster.** The 450 at
**31°27.200'N 117°05.250'W** lies **10.4 nm** from the 1140 Finger at
**31°18.000'N 117°11.000'W** on **208°**, and the 1140 Finger lies another
**10.4 nm** from the Lower 500 at **31°10.500'N 117°02.500'W** on **136°**
(cameron) — two equal legs with a **72°** turn between them. End to end the 450
and the Lower 500 are **16.9 nm** apart, past the census's **12 nm** zone-diameter
cap, which is why only the first leg clustered and the Lower 500 was attached.
For contrast [Upper 500 / Hidden Bank](upper-500-hidden-bank.md) to the north is
a straight meridian run and [385 / 238 / 475](385-238-475.md) is a tight
triangle; this zone is neither.

**The names are the only depth claims here, and one of them reads deeper than
anything else in the library.** A bank takes the **fathom depth of its shallowest
point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the transcript states this as a tendency,
not a rule — "most of them are the banks are called after the highest part of the
bank" — and names distance-named exceptions in the same passage: "now the nine
mile bank that's not nine miles deep that's nine miles off the coast" and "14 is
14 miles off the beach". Its examples are all SoCal banks (182, 209, 43, the 9
and 14 Mile) and nothing extends the convention to the Baja numbered grid. Six
feet to a fathom verifies verbatim. Same defect ledgered on
[483/500 / 437](483-500-437.md) and
[1010 Trench / 378 / 213](1010-trench-378-213.md).

| mark | name read as depth | at six feet to a fathom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 450 | 450 fathoms | about 2,700 ft |
| Lower 500 | 500 fathoms | about 3,000 ft |
| 1140 Finger | 1,140 fathoms | about 6,840 ft |

**Read literally, two of the three sit level with the surrounding plain rather
than above it.** The lexicon puts the deep, mostly featureless bottom inside the
Bight at **2,000–3,000 ft**, against which a bank is an isolated high spot rising
out of that depth ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the 2,000–3,000 ft figure is scoped to
inshore SoCal in its source — "from dana point newport marina del rey you are in
two to three thousand feet of water", framed as "most of the ocean off of
southern california", with the Bight never named — so applying it as the
surrounding plain for banks 32–46 nm off Punta San Miguel, 130+ nm down-coast,
is an out-of-region comparison. The 450 at
about 2,700 ft and the Lower 500 at about 3,000 ft land inside that band, so as
depth readings the names describe crests that barely stand up. Every one of the
three also sits far below the deepest working depth the KB records: SoCal daytime
deep-drop baits average **800–1,100 ft**
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)), the swordfish
contour is **150–350 fathoms** ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)), and the
deep rockfish game runs **300–900 ft**
([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)). Nothing here reads as
structure fished on the bottom the way the [Colonet](colonet.md) crests at
**28 and 41 fathoms** are (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms
any of the three numbers as a charted depth, and nothing in the KB says whether
the fathom convention extends to four-digit names at all — the same unresolved
question the **1010 Trench** and **1067 Knuckle** raise on
[1010 Trench / 378 / 213](1010-trench-378-213.md).

**Two naming families run through this zone and neither has its counterpart in
it.** The **Lower 500** is one half of an upper/lower pair whose other half, the
**Upper 500** at **31°46.000'N 117°30.000'W**, is **42.5 nm** away on **326°**
and belongs to [Upper 500 / Hidden Bank](upper-500-hidden-bank.md); a third
name, the **483/500**, sits west of both
([483/500 / 437](483-500-437.md)). The **1140 Finger** carries a *Finger*
qualifier whose only charted relatives are the **Upper Finger Bank** at
**32°06.800'N 117°04.400'W**, **49.1 nm** north, and the five depth-labelled
spots on it in [Finger Bank rockfish](finger-bank-rockfish.md) (cameron).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says what *Finger* denotes as a structure term,
whether the 1140 Finger is part of the same feature as the Upper Finger Bank or

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: bottom composition, relief, contour, high-spot shape, surrounding depth,
preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at any of these three marks.
The `depth_band` is unknown for that reason, not because the numbers are large.

**The 450's nearest charted neighbour is in another zone, and it is closer than
its own zone partner.** The **238** at **31°33.000'N 117°11.000'W** is
**7.6 nm** from the 450 and the **475** is **8.8 nm**, against **10.4 nm** to the
1140 Finger (cameron) — both belong to [385 / 238 / 475](385-238-475.md), which
flags the same overlap from its side. Joining the two zones would breach the
diameter cap, so the complete-linkage rule split continuous bank ground across
two pages. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether the 450 is fished with the

**The zone's charted neighbours are mostly unbuilt.** Baja - 230 / Peanut Bank /

**11.8 nm** from the Lower 500, the closest foreign mark to any spot here —
[385 / 238 / 475](385-238-475.md) is **19.0 nm** north,
[Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md) **26.5 nm** east on the
coast and Baja - 270 / Double 220 / 295 **27.0 nm** northwest (cameron). The
Colonet high spots lie **32.3 nm** southeast of the Lower 500 (cameron), which
makes this the nearest numbered-bank water to the northern end of the Colonet

## Spots

Three charted spots (cameron). They group as a pair plus one attachment:

- **The pair — the 450 and the 1140 Finger**, **10.4 nm** apart on **208°** and
  the only part of this zone that clustered on its own. The 450 is the northern
  and inshore end, **32.0 nm** from Punta San Miguel and the closest mark in the

  (cameron), which is what left it out of the cluster and inside the attach

  pair split across two zones.
