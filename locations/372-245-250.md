# 372 / 245 / 250

**A zone is a run grouping.** 372 / 245 / 250 is the far southwestern corner of
the numbered offshore-bank grid — four charted positions (cameron) spanning
**25 minutes of latitude** (31°16.900'N to 31°42.000'N) and **12 minutes of
longitude** (118°20.750'W to 118°33.000'W) in open water. Three of them — the
**372**, the **245** and the **250** — sit inside 11 nm of each other and are
the cluster the zone is named for; the **Dumping Grounds** to the north is a
lone mark the census attached here as the nearest grouping it could be fished
with.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these four marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid — fifty-one charted positions over seventeen bank zones with
no depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
spot names claim, and what is missing.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three-bank core is what makes this one zone.** The 372 at **31°22.717'N
118°32.283'W**, the 250 at **31°18.733'N 118°20.750'W** and the 245 at
**31°16.900'N 118°28.500'W** (cameron) sit **6.7 nm** (372 to 245), **6.9 nm**
(245 to 250) and **10.6 nm** (372 to 250) apart — every pair inside the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, which is the clustering rule that grouped
them.

**The Dumping Grounds was attached, not clustered.** It lies at **31°42.000'N
118°33.000'W** (cameron), **17.3 nm** from the zone's midpoint and **19.3 nm**
from the 372, its nearest neighbour in the zone — past the 12 nm cap, and far
enough from the 245 (**25.4 nm**) and the 250 (**25.5 nm**) that it is a
separate run rather than another stop on the same circuit. The census flags it
as a parent-distance outlier for that reason. It is carried here as queued
rather than re-parented, because the zone list is set by the census and not by
this page.

**The zone line runs between two marks no further apart than the core.** The
**West 400** sits **7.1 nm** from the 245 — the same spacing as the core-three
pairs — but clustered into the Sniffer / West 400 / 300 (The Rampart) grouping
to the south, so it is not a spot on this page. Anyone fishing the 245 is
within a short run of it. ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: that zone has
no page to hand off to.

**The names are the only depth claims in the zone, and one name makes none.** A
bank takes the **fathom depth of its shallowest point** as its name, at six
feet to a fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that
way the three numbered marks are all deep-water banks rather than anything
fished on the surface of the structure:

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 372 | 372 fathoms | about 2,232 ft |
| 250 | 250 fathoms | about 1,500 ft |
| 245 | 245 fathoms | about 1,470 ft |

For comparison, SoCal daytime swordfish baits average **800–1,100 ft**
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)), which is
the deepest working number in the KB. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms
any of the three numbers as a charted depth.

**The 245 and the 250 are two marks whose names nearly collide.** Read as
fathoms they differ by **5 fathoms — 30 ft** while the positions are **6.9 nm**
apart, so the numbers cannot be treated as rounding of one another. The lexicon
keeps similar-looking names distinct rather than merging them ("475" vs "475
Knuckle", "300" vs "Bell Bank (300)"), and this pair follows that rule. Neither
name repeats anywhere else in the spot library, so the landmass-prefix
collision rule is not triggered in this zone (cameron).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what the **Dumping Grounds** is named for.
It is the only name in the zone outside the numeric convention, it carries no
depth claim, and nothing in the KB defines the term or says what is on the
bottom there.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bottom composition, relief, contour, high-spot
shape, surrounding depth or current behaviour anywhere in this zone. The
`depth_band` is unknown for that reason, not because the water is shallow.

**The zone's charted neighbours are mostly unpaged.** The Sniffer / West 400 /
300 (The Rampart) grouping sits about **19 nm** south of the midpoint, the
483/500 / 437 grouping about **20 nm** east,
[1010 Trench / 378 / 213](1010-trench-378-213.md) about **37 nm** northeast and
[The Pistol / Bell Bank (300)](pistol-bell-bank-300.md) about **40 nm**
northwest (cameron). ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: neither of the two
nearer groupings carries corpus material or a page.

## Spots

Four charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. They group as a core plus
one attachment:

- **The core three — the 372, the 250 and the 245**, inside 11 nm of each other
  (cameron) and the only part of this zone that clustered on its own. The 372
  is the northwest corner of the triangle, the 250 the east corner and the 245
  the south corner.
- **The Dumping Grounds** — the northern outlier, 17.3 nm off the zone's
  midpoint, the only non-numeric name in the zone and the mark closest to US
  water.

Charted positions for all four are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
