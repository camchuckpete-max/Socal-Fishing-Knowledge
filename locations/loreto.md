# Loreto & Puerto Escondido

**The island end of [Cortez South](cortez-south.md): a fishery worked around a
cluster of big islands rather than off a mainland shore.** Isla Carmen and Isla
Monserrat are the runs, and **Puerto Escondido**, a marina a little south of
Loreto, is the base boats leave from (`ikiXx9sUsoA`, `n4nMCLLW3tY`). The water
against those islands is shallow — cabrilla come from **5–10 ft** and a boat
chasing birds sat in **30 ft** (`n4nMCLLW3tY`, `ikiXx9sUsoA`) — so the day is a
shallow-structure and surface program, not a deep one.

[Yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) and
[cabrilla](../species/cabrilla.md) run underneath here the same way they do at
[Bahía de los Ángeles](bahia-de-los-angeles.md), but **roosterfish, sierra,
[dorado](../species/dorado.md) and sailfish are named targets rather than
luck**: at Bahía de los Ángeles roosterfish are described as migratory, a fish
you get lucky on, and more reliable further south in the Sea of Cortez
(`P36VGPPf120`) — and this is where a trip trolls one up before lunch
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`, `ikiXx9sUsoA`). The species mix, the access and the year are
all different enough that a Bahía de los Ángeles program does not transfer, and
neither does its season table.

## Getting there

**You fly in.** One trip reached the zone through **CBX**, the Cross Border
Xpress bridge into the Tijuana airport, rather than driving (`ikiXx9sUsoA`) —
the practical break from [Cortez North](cortez-north.md), which is drive-only.

**Boats leave from three kinds of place:**

- **Puerto Escondido Marina**, south of Loreto, is the documented launch — a
  trip's third fishing day started there (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **A mothership at anchor.** One operation runs a **300–308 ft** vessel with
  an **18 ft** Grady White towed behind on a custom cleat-to-cleat rope; the
  crew lives aboard the mothership, fishes off the Grady White, and the tow is
  what gets the small boat out to the islands (`ikiXx9sUsoA`, `n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **Off a sandy beach.** A party fishing local pangas split into two boats one
  day and four the next, running from a beach where the rest of the group
  stayed and where the catch was cooked afterwards (`Re56xUHOu6A`).

**Order bait before the morning you need it.** Sardines are bought from a local
bait vendor who meets boats in front of the marina; one crew missed the vendor
at the dock and only got bait because he was still working out front
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`). See [making bait](../bait/making-bait.md) for the alternative.

**A breakdown here is a radio call, not a self-rescue.** A shift-linkage pin
sheared on a small boat about to start a 20-mile run to the north end of Isla
Carmen; the crew paddled and swam the boat back toward the marina while a
second boat was called in over the radio to tow it, and the day ended at the
mechanic (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)).**

## Structure & bathymetry

**The fishery works off the islands, and the runs between them are the day.**

- **Isla Monserrat** is the outside run. Local word on it is specific: *"Monserrat
  north end is the best"* (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **Six Mile Reef** is a high spot fished on the way out to Monserrat, marked on
  the plotter and worked to the bottom; a yellowtail came off it on an earlier
  trip, and triggerfish, skipjack and Pinto bass came off it on the day it was
  filmed (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **Isla Carmen** carries the sheltered water. **Honeymoon Bay** is called out
  as protected, which is what makes it the windy-day option, and *"that is good
  yellowtail on the corner down there"* (`ikiXx9sUsoA`). The **north end** of
  Carmen is about **20 miles** from Puerto Escondido, roughly **an hour** in a
  small boat (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **Punta Perico** is a shoreline point up the coast that produced a yellowtail
  on an earlier trip and that the bait vendor named as where dorado were being
  caught close to shore (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).

**Everything documented here is shallow.** Cabrilla came from **5–10 ft** of
water against island rock (`n4nMCLLW3tY`); a boat working birds looked down and
was in **30 ft**, with **10 ft** called out on the sounder while structure was
visible through the water on the same run (`ikiXx9sUsoA`); a yellowtail and
roosterfish session was fished with the boat in rock (`s5PaKJX3VDo`); and a
shoreline program was worked close enough in that the crew noted *"you could
have surf cast for this fish"* (`0b0PDLVh-T4`). Wind protection comes from the
island itself — a sierra came off rock that was *"nice and protected from the
wind"* (`ikiXx9sUsoA`).

## How it fishes

**Birds decide where you stop, and the first stop can be the day.** One trip's
first bird stop leaving the dock produced wide-open surface fish and effectively
ended the search (`s5PaKJX3VDo`); crews break off the troll and run at working
birds as the standing move (`n4nMCLLW3tY`, `ikiXx9sUsoA`). Method is in
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md) and
[search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).

**Trolling is what covers the water between stops.** The spread is
[feathers](../lures/tuna-feathers-and-skirts.md) — a green-and-yellow "candy
bar" pattern and a pink-and-blue — [cedar plugs](../lures/cedar-plug.md), and
[Halco](../lures/halco-laser-pro.md) hard baits, with UV-finish lures called out
as the preference; the roosterfish came on the Halco (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). Local
pangas troll *plumas*, big squid-skirt lures run right at the surface so they
push water as they swim (`Re56xUHOu6A`). Trolling continues even on a tow back
to the marina (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). Execution is in
[trolling](../techniques/trolling.md) and, for a small boat with a crew,
[panga team trolling](../techniques/panga-team-trolling.md).

**When they are up, burn it.** Surface fish here were taken on spooks, a Pickle
Kick swimbait, [surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md) and bass gear, all
worked fast: *"you got to burn it, can't let them look at it"*, with about
**20 followers on every cast** and fish sitting beside the boat (`s5PaKJX3VDo`).
Casting mechanics are in
[surface iron casting](../techniques/surface-iron-casting.md) and
[foamer casting](../techniques/foamer-casting.md).

**Cast [jerkbaits](../lures/jerkbaits.md) at the shallow rock for cabrilla** — a
Shimano suspending jerkbait in **5–10 ft** is what produced them
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`), the same shallow-structure program the
[cabrilla](../species/cabrilla.md) router carries.

**Live sardine is the dorado bait, and the local rig is specific:** a small to
medium hook through the nose and eyes so the bait keeps swimming, fished with
the weight underneath rather than the bait on top (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). Flylining the
same bait is what one angler put out while adrift (`n4nMCLLW3tY`); a circle hook
was used. See [fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md) and
[flyline](../techniques/flyline.md).

**Set the drag for the fish you might get, not the one you just landed.** A
roosterfish was hooked on a drag left tight from a sierra, and the angler had to
back it off mid-fight (`ikiXx9sUsoA`).

**The day is short and the light matters.** A sunset troll was worked until the
crew called it too dark to keep fishing and ran north for shelter overnight
(`ikiXx9sUsoA`); a charter day out of the beach ran **2 hours** (`Re56xUHOu6A`);
the vendor's dorado window was late morning (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).

**Sierra and cabrilla come off the boat as dinner.** Sierra are bled at the
gills on the deck and made into ceviche; the catch is cleaned at the dock and
cooked at the hotel or on the beach, including as sashimi (`ikiXx9sUsoA`,
`n4nMCLLW3tY`, `Re56xUHOu6A`). General handling is in
[bleeding & general care](../fish-care/dorado-and-general.md).

## Spots

**No spot in this zone has a charted position in the KB, so no spot pages hang
below it.** The named grounds recorded are:

- **Isla Monserrat**, north end — the far run, and the end of the island the
  locals rate (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **Six Mile Reef** — the high spot on the way out to Monserrat, fished to the
  bottom, with a yellowtail taken there on an earlier trip (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **Isla Carmen**, north end — about 20 miles and an hour from Puerto Escondido
  (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **Honeymoon Bay** (Isla Carmen) — sheltered water, with yellowtail on the
  corner (`ikiXx9sUsoA`).
- **Punta Perico** — a shoreline point that produced a yellowtail, and the
  vendor's dorado ground (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **"The Sante"** — a ground the locals first pointed a crew at for dorado
  before they chose Punta Perico instead; passed again on the tow home
  (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): the name is rendered "desante",
  "the Sante" and "la Sante" across one transcript and resolves to no charted
  place in the KB.
- **Puerto Escondido Marina** — the launch, not a fishing spot (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).

None of these carries a public name plus a charted position, which is what a
spot page needs, so they are logged in
[the spot harvest](../sources/spot-harvest.md) for the gazetteer rather than
written up here.
