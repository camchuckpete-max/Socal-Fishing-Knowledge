# Loreto & Puerto Escondido

**The southern Sea of Cortez island fishery — roughly 250 miles south of
[Bahía de los Ángeles](bahia-de-los-angeles.md), and a different mix of fish.**
Yellowtail and cabrilla are still here, but **roosterfish, sierra, dorado, and
sailfish** are on the table in a way they are not up north, and the fishing is
built around a cluster of big islands rather than a mainland shoreline.

Kept separate from BOLA deliberately: the species table, the seasons, and the
access are all different, and lumping them would let a BOLA plan offer a Loreto
program.

## Getting there

**You can fly.** One documented trip flew out of **CBX** (Cross Border Xpress,
the San Diego–Tijuana airport bridge) rather than making the long drive
(`ikiXx9sUsoA`). That is the practical difference from BOLA, which is a
drive-only destination.

## The water

The fishery works off the islands rather than the beach:

- **Isla Monserrat** — the run-to island on one trip; *"Monserrat north end is
  the best"* was the local word (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **Six Mile Reef** — a high spot fished on the way out to Monserrat.
- **Isla Carmen** and **Honeymoon Bay** — Honeymoon called out as sheltered
  water, which matters on a windy day (`ikiXx9sUsoA`).
- **Puerto Escondido** — a bit south of Loreto, used as a base.
- **Shoreline is fishable too** — one session was worked close enough in that
  the crew noted *"you could have surf cast for this fish"* (`0b0PDLVh-T4`).

Water can get very shallow around the islands — one boat was in **30 ft**
while working birds, and cabrilla were taken in **5–10 ft** (`n4nMCLLW3tY`,
`ikiXx9sUsoA`).

## What's here

| Species | How it showed up |
| --- | --- |
| **Roosterfish** | Taken **on the troll**, on a Halco, in shallow water off an island (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). This is the real difference from BOLA, where roosters are a matter of luck. |
| **Yellowtail** | The stated main target on a Loreto/Puerto Escondido trip (`ikiXx9sUsoA`); "good yellowtail on the corner" was the local read. |
| **Cabrilla** | Shallow, **5–10 ft**, on a suspending jerkbait. More than one cabrilla species turns up — one fish was called out on camera as "a different type of cabrilla" (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). |
| **Sierra** | Reported on the troll (`ikiXx9sUsoA`). |
| **Dorado** | Caught on a Loreto trip targeting them directly (`Re56xUHOu6A`). |
| **Sailfish** (*pez vela*) | Actively targeted; a Spanish-language segment describes fish "up to 300 lb" and fights of **four to five hours** (`Re56xUHOu6A`) — treat the weight as an on-camera claim, not a verified figure. |
| **Snapper / pargo** | Taken from the shoreline program (`0b0PDLVh-T4`) and named in the dinner rundown. |
| **Triggerfish, skipjack, Pinto bass** | Constant bycatch. Skipjack kept for chunk bait. |

## How it's fished

- **Trolling is the locate method.** Feathers (a green-and-yellow "candy bar"
  pattern, a pink-and-blue), cedar plugs, and **Halco** hard baits — the
  roosterfish came on a Halco. UV-finish lures were called out as a preference.
- **Birds are the search sign** — crews break off and run to working birds, the
  same read as the SoCal program. See
  [bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md).
- **Jerkbaits shallow for cabrilla** — a Shimano suspending jerkbait in 5–10 ft
  of water, consistent with the
  [cabrilla](../species/cabrilla.md) shallow-structure program.
- **Surface and topwater when they're up.** One trip found wide-open surface
  fish on the first bird stop leaving the dock, taken on **spooks, a Pickle Kick
  swimbait, surface iron, and bass gear** — with a burn retrieve: *"you got to
  burn it, can't let them look at it"* (`s5PaKJX3VDo`). Fish followed lures to
  the boat in numbers ("20 followers on every cast").
- **Boats:** local pangas, and at least one operation running a mothership with
  an 18-ft Grady White towed behind to fish from (`ikiXx9sUsoA`).

## Notes

- **Season data is thin.** One trip is dated on camera to **2023-07-31**
  (`n4nMCLLW3tY`) and one is a September shoreline session (`0b0PDLVh-T4`);
  nothing in this corpus lays out a month-by-month Loreto calendar. Do not
  borrow BOLA's seasonal table for Loreto — it is a different latitude and a
  different species mix.
- All five sources are unregistered channels and mostly trip vlogs, so this
  note is `medium` throughout and carries no doctrine, only what was done and
  what it produced.
- **"The Baja Catch"** — a long-standing Baja fishing book — is referenced on
  camera as the crew's own planning reference (`ikiXx9sUsoA`). Not a corpus
  source; noted because it points at material this KB does not have.
- Operators named: **Stella June Sportfishing** (`s5PaKJX3VDo`).
