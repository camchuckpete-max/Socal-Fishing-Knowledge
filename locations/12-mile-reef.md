# 12 Mile Reef

**A zone is a run grouping.** This one is a single charted mark — the
**12 Mile Reef** at **34°13.000'N 119°41.017'W** (cameron), at the north end of
the [Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md), which places it in the Santa
Barbara Channel. The position in the infobox is the charted mark itself, not an
average of anything: a zone of one has no centre to compute.

**No corpus source names it.** "12 Mile Reef" appears in no transcript, no
knowledge note and no evidence file; the only places it occurs in the repo are
its coordinate line in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) and the
flagged stubs on neighbouring pages ([Hidden Reef / 170](hidden-reef-170.md),
[311 (Trask Knoll)](311.md), [The Boot (504) / 307](boot-504-307.md),
[Hancock Bank](hancock-bank.md)). The zone exists on coordinates (cameron), so
this is a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the mark is, what water it
sits in, what its name does and does not claim, and what a source would have to
supply. The [region page](socal-bight.md) already carries this zone as a flagged
stub and nothing here lifts that stub. The flags below aggregate in
[the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

**This is a zone of one because nothing else is within reach of it.** The census
clusters banks under a **12 nm** zone-diameter cap and then attaches a leftover
lone bank to the nearest zone inside **25 nm** (cameron). The nearest charted
position in the whole spot library is the **170**, **35.8 nm** away on a bearing
of **144°** — past both thresholds by a wide margin — so this mark clustered with
nothing and attached to nothing, and minted a zone in its own name. Only **three**
charted positions in the library lie within **50 nm** of it: the 170, **Hidden
Reef** at **39.1 nm** and **311 (Trask Knoll)** at **46.3 nm** (cameron).

**It is the region's northern end of the line.** Of the 391 positions in the spot
library exactly one lies north of this mark — **Arguello Canyon**, **69.2 nm**
west-northwest at **278°**, which the census attached to the
[Davis Knoll / San Miguel Gap / Rodriguez Seamount](davis-knoll-san-miguel-gap-rodriguez-seamount.md)
zone out on the region's northwest corner (cameron). Everything else charted is
south or southeast. This mark and that corner are the two ends of the region's
top edge, **61.7 nm** apart at the nearest members, with nothing charted between
them.

**The name is a distance, and here the chart cannot read it back.** A bank is
normally named for the **fathom depth of its shallowest point**, but SoCal names
some marks for distance instead — Nine Mile Bank sits **9 statute miles** off the
coast and "the 14" is **14 miles off the beach** (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). On [279 / 267 / 14 Mile Bank](279-267-14-mile-bank.md)
that reading checks out against the chart, because a mainland position sits
**14.0 statute miles** off the mark. Here there is nothing to check it against:
the nearest mainland-side position in the library is **98.9 nm** away (cameron).

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a stated distance, heading, run time, fuel
figure or sea-state envelope to this mark from any port, and no source names a
port, ramp or landing this zone is fished out of. Nothing in the corpus records

**The corpus's nearest port framing is Ventura and Oxnard, and it is stated for
the islands rather than for this mark.** A Channel Islands day starts "just
outside of Ventura," makes a tank of squid on the way, and jets out to the
islands (`6-8KfjEg0x8`); a Channel Islands tackle guide opens on essentials "if
you're going to be fishing out of Ventura or Oxnard ... going out to the Channel
Islands" (`nRIAgz5G_Bc`). Sportboats out of the Channel Islands landings also run
as far as San Nicolas ([San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md)). Those are
the ports on this end of the region; ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source connects
any of them to this position.

**The wind map the corpus draws for this water is the harshest in the region.** A
strong NW event rolls surface temperature down **5–8 °F** on the **northern
Channel Islands** and the **LA / Ventura coastal shelf**, while south of Newport
is "less prone to wind-wave-driven rollovers" and the Coronados, Catalina and San
Clemente held their water through the same blow (`YZtX1MiT0y8`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). Those two
crushed waters are the two this zone sits between, which makes a NW forecast the
first thing to check before pointing a boat up here — ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source scopes that map to this position, or states a wind or swell number that
turns this zone off.

**A calm dock reading is a trap at this end of the region, but the rule is
stated for water west of here.** W and NW wind blows around Point Conception at
night and quits in the morning, so the go/no-go is **2 or more consecutive days**
of no wind or Santa Anas before committing to a Santa Rosa / San Miguel crossing
(`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`), which is why a **12–24 h wind hindcast** for the route belongs in
every conditions pull alongside the forward forecast
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md#wind-history-not-just-wind-forecast-1224-h-hindcast)).
That crossing runs west of this mark and past the northern islands.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states the Conception night-wind behaviour for
the Santa Barbara Channel itself.

**Check the swell upstream, not just the point forecast.** Swell arrives days
after and hundreds of miles from the wind that made it — a system off **Oregon**
put San Nicolas from **4 ft** to over **12 ft** by the Monday after a locally calm
weekend, with the swell tracking down past Point Conception (`zKmZ4zql2ws`). Scan
the synoptic wind field up-coast over the prior **48–72 h**
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md#upstream-fetch-check-swell-arrives-after-the-wind-that-made-it)).
This is the northernmost zone in the region, so it is the first water that swell
train reaches.

## Structure & bathymetry

**One position, and the name is all there is.** The mark sits at
**34°13.000'N 119°41.017'W** (cameron). The name says *reef*; the
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md) defines **bank** — an isolated high spot rising
out of otherwise deep water, where bait stages for shelter and gamefish follow
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`) — and defines nothing else. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
what **reef** denotes as a structure term in this fishery, and whether this mark
is a high spot, a ridge, a rock pile or hard bottom on a shelf.

**No depth is claimed or stated.** The fathom-depth convention only speaks
through numbers, at six feet to a fathom (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)), and this name carries a distance instead — the
same reason the 14 Mile Bank makes no depth claim
([279 / 267 / 14 Mile Bank](279-267-14-mile-bank.md)). The `depth_band` is
unknown for that reason. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states a sounded depth,
relief, high-spot shape, bottom composition, kelp, surrounding depth or current
set at this mark.

**The water around it is uncharted in this library, in every direction.**
Nearest charted positions, nearest first (cameron): the **170** at **35.8 nm**
southeast, **Hidden Reef** at **39.1 nm** southeast — both in
[Hidden Reef / 170](hidden-reef-170.md) — **311 (Trask Knoll)** at **46.3 nm**
southwest, **Kidney Bank (63)** at **51.0 nm** and the **175** at **51.4 nm**
([Kidney Bank (63) / 175](kidney-bank-63-175.md)), **Arch Reef** on
[Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) at **53.9 nm**, **The Boot (504)**
at **56.8 nm** east-southeast ([The Boot (504) / 307](boot-504-307.md)) and
**Blockhouse** on [San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md) at **57.0 nm**. On
the nearest-outside-mark measure this is the region's **second-most isolated
zone**, behind only the Davis Knoll grouping at **36.5 nm** and ahead of San Juan
Seamount at **30.9 nm**, Hancock Bank and [311](311.md) at **23.0 nm** (cameron).

**Between this mark and the shore it is named for, the library holds nothing.**
The **northern Channel Islands** — Santa Cruz, Santa Rosa, San Miguel — carry no
coordinates in the spot library at all and so mint no zone, even though the
corpus names them repeatedly ([Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)), and
the library's mainland coverage stops at Crystal Cove, **98.9 nm** southeast
(cameron). ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: the northern Channel Islands
and the Santa Barbara Channel mainland shore need spot coordinates before either
can carry a page, which leaves this zone with no built neighbour inside **35 nm**
in any direction and nothing at all to hand off to on the shore side.

**Standalone structure with no land attached fishes in any direction.** Structure
rising off the plain has no lee to work against, and the face to bias toward is
the up-current one, where current forced against the high spot drives the
upwelling that stacks the food chain
([current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md),
[island structure](island-structure.md)) — though each individual mark still has
its own preferred directions learned over repeat trips. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: preferred directions, drift behaviour or current set here.

**The corpus does describe the fishery of the water this mark sits in, at the
islands rather than on the reef.** A Ventura-departure Channel Islands day names
**yellowtail, white seabass and halibut** as the main goals, with "a lot of bass,
barracuda, bonito, the three B's around," and finishes on shallow-water rockfish
and lingcod (`6-8KfjEg0x8`); a Ventura/Oxnard Channel Islands tackle guide names
the same three primary targets (`nRIAgz5G_Bc`). Routers:
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md),
[white seabass](../species/white-seabass.md),
[California halibut](../species/california-halibut.md),
[calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md),
[barracuda](../species/barracuda.md), [bonito](../species/bonito.md),
[rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md).

**Shallow rockfish and lings are the corpus's named northern-islands target, and
they are weather-gated.** The northern islands fish a **100–250 ft** shallow
regime where a private boat gets variety — reds, blue bass, bocaccio and lingcod
off a single drift over one rock — and that island variety is a northern thing
you do not get in San Diego (`6-8KfjEg0x8`,
[rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)). **Santa Ana events flatten
the northwest outer islands** and are the trigger to run them for shallow
rockfish and lings in late fall (`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`, `OYOda6T3f-8`); a Santa Ana is an
offshore-blowing northeast pattern and a seasonal norm
([November–December](../seasonal/november-december.md)).

**Seasonal marks the corpus does place on this shore.** Barracuda are dated to
Santa Cruz and Anacapa in early August (`CMQkHQMxbXM`, `-JZpyWLdKlk`,
[barracuda](../species/barracuda.md)); bonito to the Ventura coast and the
islands in October (`OYOda6T3f-8`, `5p6gu14ZC4w`,
[bonito](../species/bonito.md)). Bluefin scatter across the whole Bight at peak
August dispersal — **Catalina to San Nicolas to Ventura**, "showing up in weird
places, gone the next day" (`CMQkHQMxbXM`, [August](../seasonal/august.md),
[bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md)) — which is the one seasonal window
whose stated extent reaches this end of the region.

**The fall arc starts on this shore and runs away from it.** As the coast cools
and the warm band fragments, the warm water tracks a repeatable path — backside
Santa Cruz / Rosa → Santa Barbara Island → backside Catalina → hooking to the
499 / Clemente (`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md), [October](../seasonal/october.md)). Its
first station is the island backsides **50-odd nm** south of this mark and every
later one is further away (cameron), so the fall pattern moves the fishery off
this end of the region rather than onto it.

**The paddy band the corpus names is nowhere near here.** The best kelp-paddy
water in the Bight is **south of San Clemente Island / Catalina** and along the
coastal strip from Dana Point to Oceanside and southward; north of Dana Point
paddies thin out and hold fewer fish (`HzE4FHHcvTk`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md), [kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md)).
Every position in that band is over **95 nm** from this mark (cameron).

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states an
approach, a starting depth, a drift, or what tide, light or current do here.

**A single mark reads as a compact spot-check rather than a search box, but no
source says so.** The Bight's two zone shapes are the complex — a
**~50–60 square-mile** box you plan hours of looking across — and the compact
spot you idle onto, grade on the meter, and either commit to or leave in
**20–30 minutes** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). One charted position
with nothing else inside **35 nm** (cameron) is the shape of the second, and with
no fallback mark inside that radius a blank here ends the day rather than moving
it along.

**The island program on this shore is a squid program.** The Ventura-departure day
makes a tank of squid before running out, then covers the column with a
[dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) and a
[sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md) rig — the sliding sinker
described as a way to cover "every level of the water column" from the surface to
the bottom, and especially good when fishing squid at the Channel Islands
(`6-8KfjEg0x8`). Making and keeping live squid is in
[making bait](../bait/making-bait.md) and [bait tanks](../bait/bait-tanks.md).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether squid is made, held or fished
anywhere near this mark.

**Current is the gate at the islands, and the meter alone will fool you.** A spot
with no current will meter fish that do not bite (`6-8KfjEg0x8`,
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)) — so grade the water
moving over the structure before grading the marks on the screen
([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**With no corpus program, the general layers are what a plan has.** Break
position and break age decide whether this zone is on a given day's plan
([report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)).
From October the regime flips to reading **100 % the temperature chart and
chlorophyll**, and across a big area with no temperature differentiation the fish
are most likely on structure like the banks (`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`,
[water regimes](../conditions/water-regimes.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)). That last rule is the only general
doctrine that argues for pointing a boat at an unfished mark, and it is not scoped
here.

**This mark is not a BightSST eval target, and the nearest one is most of a day
away.** Conditions here are pulled per charted position rather than read off a
scored spot ([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)); the closest scored spot is
the **14 Mile Bank** at **97.3 nm** and the closest after that **Pyramid Cove** at
**106.3 nm** (cameron), both far enough that neither is usable as a proxy. The
per-position pull is the only honest conditions read for this zone.
