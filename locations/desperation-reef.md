# Desperation Reef

A charted high spot at **32°45.520'N 118°24.520'W** (cameron), at the southeast
end of the
[San Clemente Island — Back Side (N→S)](san-clemente-island-back-side.md) zone
and the island's southernmost charted position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) (cameron). It is the corpus's
anchor spot on this side of the island: the boat sits on the bank over staged
bait squid rather than working an edge of the shoreline (`Rb5I2ljAqeE`).

## Getting there

**Plan it as an overnight, not a day trip.** San Clemente Island sits at
overnight range, and the crossing is described as roughly **60 miles** straight
off the beach without a named port (`XyKF34C5iB4`,
[the zone page](san-clemente-island-back-side.md#getting-there)). Reaching this
mark is that crossing plus the leg down to the southeast end of the back side.

**Check the Navy schedule before you leave.** The island is a training range
with rotating closures, and the access check is a standard pre-trip item —
statement and as-of date in
[San Clemente Island — Front Side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md#getting-there)
(`XyKF34C5iB4`). **Verify current access with the Navy before running the
crossing.**

**The one charted run in the corpus reaches it from offshore, not from the
beach.** From the [209](209-312.md) the run to here is **33.7 nm** at **233°**
(cameron) — a jump a fast boat makes off an offshore first stop rather than a
departure leg.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: run time and distance from any named port,
whether the approach rounds the northwest or the southeast end onto this side,
and the sea-state envelope the crossing demands.

## Structure & bathymetry

**This is a bank read, not a shoreline read.** It is worked as a high spot to
anchor on, and the reason given for fish holding here is that bait squid were
staged on the bank — nothing about the island's shoreline orientation
(`Rb5I2ljAqeE`).

**Name discrepancy, carried as recorded.** The source that anchored here calls
the ground the "West End tuna zone" while
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) charts Desperation Reef at the
**southeast** end of the back side's N→S run (`Rb5I2ljAqeE`, cameron). ⚠ The
two namings are not reconciled; the charted position is what this page is built
on.

**A second Desperation name sits at this island.** The corpus separately names a
**Desperation Point**, with a 2016-11-29 trip ending about **six miles** off the
island outside it in **1,200–1,300 ft** of water (`AIHvJj-paoo`, via
[the 209 / 312](209-312.md)) — deep water off the island, not this charted
high spot. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says whether the point and the reef
name the same ground; they are carried as two names.

**Charted neighbours** (cameron): the [29 Fathom Spot](29-fathom-spot.md) is the
next mark north on the same run, with [The Caves](caves.md) and
[China Point](china-point.md) completing the zone's southeast-end group. Off the
island, the [81](81-381.md) is **2.4 nm** south — the closest mark in the spot
library's offshore-banks list to any charted San Clemente Island position — with
the 381 at **11.0 nm** west-southwest and the 267 at **20.8 nm** southwest; the
[157](w-butterfly-157.md) is **23.2 nm** south and the W. Butterfly **24.2 nm**;
the [E. Butterfly](e-butterfly-san-salvador-knoll.md) is **33.1 nm** off, which
makes this the nearest charted island position to that mark, and the San
Salvador Knoll **37.0 nm**. The [289 and the 284](289-284.md) sit abeam the
island to the north — this position is **10.5 nm** and **9.0 nm** south of them
in latitude.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: charted depth, bottom composition, relief or
high-spot shape, the surrounding depth it rises out of, or which side of it to
anchor on a given current.

## How it fishes

**The squid is the read, not the mark.** The stated reason to anchor here was
that bait squid were staged on the bank (`Rb5I2ljAqeE`) — which makes this a
forage call: the zone's rule is that a squid bite going quiet is a relocation
problem rather than an absence problem, so follow the squid on the meter before
writing the stop off (`YZtX1MiT0y8`,
[electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**Dead squid still gets bit here.** Leftover squid fished "fresh dead" on a
[flyline](../techniques/flyline.md#fishing-it) drew repeat bluefin bites off the
anchor (`Rb5I2ljAqeE`) — the bait did not have to be live to work this stop.

**With current running against the wind, weight the flyline lightly.** The crew
departed from the flyline's no-weight rule and added an unusually light
**half-ounce** weight to keep the bait swinging naturally instead of letting the
current straighten it out (`Rb5I2ljAqeE`,
[flyline](../techniques/flyline.md#fishing-it)).

**On the anchor, watch the bow.** Anchored at San Clemente Island, chum gets
pushed up-current toward the bow by the fish themselves — birds locking on up
there mark the school, and the surface iron goes under them (`DnSqw4r7A1s`,
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). The source describes
anchoring on the island's **front** side; it is carried here as an island-level
anchoring read, the corpus having no back-side version, and applies to the one
anchoring spot documented on this side.

**Check the current at the mark, not at the last stop.** Two named San Clemente
Island spots close enough to be a short run apart can show opposite current
directions on the same day, framed as routine at this island (`sIoNELGlxmk`,
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)).

**Gear for the grade.** The jig stick runs a **50 lb** top shot at this island
rather than the 40 lb used closer in, because an overnight-range island carries
a trophy shot at any moment (`LTCFjqtSI8g`,
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a tide, moon or current window for this mark,
where on the bank to set the anchor, or how long to give the stop before moving.
