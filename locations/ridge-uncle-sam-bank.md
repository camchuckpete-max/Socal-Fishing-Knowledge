# The Ridge / Uncle Sam Bank

**[Baja only]** — The standing alternative to
[Alijos Rocks](alijos-rocks.md) on the San Diego long-range fleet's seven-day
trips: a trip books "one or the other," not both (`_r08B4bItAY`). "The Ridge"
is resolved on camera as running to an upper high spot named **Uncle Sam
Bank**, "famous for yellowtail and black sea bass and grouper and yellowfin
tuna especially during the time frame that we're down there" — the fall, late
September into early October (`_r08B4bItAY`). The whole ground sits in
[Baja Pacific — South](baja-pacific-south.md), which the census gives no zone
of its own, so this page hangs directly off the region rung.

**It carries no charted position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)**, whose sections stop at San
Quintín. A day plan cannot pull per-spot conditions here; the run distance is
the only geographic figure the corpus gives.

**Name collision — "the Ridge" is three different places in this corpus.**
This ground is the long-range Baja bank. A separate "the Ridge" is one of
[Magdalena Bay](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md)'s two offshore banks, the
longer run out of the boca, and a third is a San Diego deep-drop
[swordfish](../species/swordfish.md) ground on the local contour edge
(`yMiBtZ7k8-w`). [Clemente Ridge](zone-lexicon.md) in the Bight is a fourth
use of the word. ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): whether the Magdalena-area Ridge
and this one are the same feature — one long-range series works the
Magdalena-Bay-to-Alijos-Rocks corridor and names "the ridge" on it
(`3tQ1_xiqwVU`), and nothing on camera resolves the two names against each
other. The **180 ft, 10 ft-off-the-bottom** yellowtail from that trip is
carried on the Magdalena Bay page, not here.

## Getting there

**It is a two-day run each way from San Diego and nothing shortens it.** The
figure given is **450–460 mi** to the upper end where Uncle Sam Bank is, a
**two-day run** (`_r08B4bItAY`) — the same clip that puts Alijos Rocks at
**488 mi** and a **45-hour run**, leaving around 11:00 with bait taken aboard
the next morning (`_r08B4bItAY`; [Alijos Rocks](alijos-rocks.md#getting-there)
carries the departure pattern). ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): a second
account puts the same grounds "roughly 400 miles" south of San Diego
(`97clKtVsEOs`); the 88 mi spread is unreconciled in the corpus and the
450–460 mi figure here rides on the same source as the larger Alijos number
([fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md)).

**The trip lengths that reach it are six, seven and eight days.** Fall trips
are timed to fish Alijos Rocks and the Ridge for wahoo from October onward
(`tjBeR9tWd4s`), and the bomb doctrine is written for "seven-day-and-longer"
trips reaching one ground or the other (`ETw_3AFxEcM`). Seven- and eight-day
boats leave as early as **July** to be first on the grounds when nothing is
biting locally, which works only if the water is already warm enough
(`4uNPLknRAQg`).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a sea-state or weather envelope for this
ground, a bait stop on the way, or any access other than a San Diego
long-range boat.

**Regulatory — Mexico.** Mexican entry and sport-fishing paperwork applies to
every trip that reaches here: see [Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md) and the
region's [paperwork gate](baja-pacific-south.md#access--range). Checked as of
2026-08; **verify current requirements before you fish**. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: the Mexican (CONAPESCA) bag limit, size gauge, gear rule or

## Structure & bathymetry

**The corpus names the shape and never measures it.** What is stated is that
it is a ridge with an upper end, and that the upper end is a named high spot
called Uncle Sam Bank (`_r08B4bItAY`). Nothing on camera gives a sounding, a
bottom composition, a rise-from depth, or the ridge's length or orientation.

**Two depth signals exist and they point different ways.** The ground is
grouped with Alijos Rocks, Colonet and Guadalupe as "real deep water" — the
tier where the yo-yo pick steps up to a 7X-heavy iron rather than the 6X
fished at **120–150 ft** or the 6X Jr fished shallower (`hteLeDIy9Qs`,
[yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md#jig-size--depth)). Against that, the
wahoo-bomb sink-count rule uses roughly **90–100 ft** of water as its worked
example and warns that letting the bomb reach bottom "on structure like the
ridge" buys a triggerfish or whitefish instead of a wahoo (`4uNPLknRAQg`) —
which also says the bottom here holds those species. ⚠ Fact-check
(unverifiable): the two figures are never stated as the same water, and the
ridge plausibly carries both a shallow high-spot crest and deep flanks; no
source says so.

**More rock than anything north of it, by implication of the tackle.** The
stated reason for stepping the yellowtail yo-yo outfit up at Alijos Rocks and
"the ridge" is bigger, meaner fish with more rock underneath
(`RNRFrfepiW0`); the rig itself is in
[How it fishes](#how-it-fishes).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any sonar or meter signature for this
ground, what the sounder shows against the ridge, or how a boat searches it on
arrival. General method: [electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)
and [search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).

## How it fishes

**Arrive and get a spread out.** The stated first move at a dedicated wahoo
stop — Alijos Rocks or the Ridge — is to start the boat's
[trolling](../techniques/wahoo-trolling.md) spread and "get the lay of the
land" before switching to cast or live-bait presentations (`4uNPLknRAQg`).
Two sizes of the Nomad DTX are named for this ground and Alijos Rocks as the
long-range trolling kit, chosen because the lure swims deeper in the water than
most trolling lures (`it_YYh_8Z-w`;
[DTX Minnow](../lures/dtx-minnow.md#wahoo-trolling-application-dtx-200)).

**The bomb is the cast presentation, and speed is the non-negotiable.** On
seven-day-and-longer trips reaching this ground, the wahoo bomb — with a small
spinner or without, angler's choice — must be retrieved at the absolute
highest rate of speed possible (`ETw_3AFxEcM`). Count **20 or 30** on the
sink before engaging, deliberately shorter than a yo-yo drop, because wahoo
are not fished near the bottom: in roughly **90–100 ft** the target zone is
about **60 ft** up to the surface, and the crew calls the day's depth so
anglers know how long to let it sink (`4uNPLknRAQg`;
[wahoo bomb & Raider jig casting](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md)).

**Yellowtail here are a bottom job, not a surface one.** The documented
program is a [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) on **80 lb** — or
80 to 100 lb test on the heavier four-outfit build — to a **16 oz** sinker on
the bottom with a live sardine or mackerel (`uWGGeDOprsE`, `MPC_OQjvO-o`). The
same outfit interchanges between that and trolling for wahoo, which is why a
seven-day trip carries it as one rod rather than two
([Searcher five-outfit quiver](../tackle/searcher-alijos-rocks-ridge-7-day-quiver.md),
[Searcher 6-to-8-day heavy outfit](../tackle/searcher-6-to-8-day-heavy-outfit.md)).
On [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) the ground sits in the heavy
tier: a 7X-heavy iron (`hteLeDIy9Qs`) on a two-speed Penn Fathom 40 Narrow,
**80 lb** braid to a **60 lb** monofilament top shot and a shorter, more
powerful **30–80 lb** rod — stepped up for big fish, wind, or trouble getting
the jig down (`RNRFrfepiW0`).

**Terminal tackle for the trip.** Match the fluorocarbon leader to the running
line's class or step one size above — the worked example is **40 lb**
monofilament running line to a **50 lb** fluorocarbon leader (`uWGGeDOprsE`;
[line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md#connections-parameters--steps-are-in-the-rigging-notes)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: current, tide, moon, wind or light-window
doctrine at this ground, how long a stop lasts, or which end of the ridge is
worked in what conditions. Every stated gate on this page is trip length and
season.
