# North Point

**[Baja only]** — the north end of Cedros Island, in the
[Cedros / San Benitos](cedros-island.md) zone, and the ground the crew names
when the target is a bigger grade of [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md):
"we're going to go to North Point, and that's where the bigger 30 plus yellows
are at" (`wdbqTio1SQU`). The same crew framed the following morning as running
for **30–40 lb** fish "on the north end" (`wdbqTio1SQU`), so the name and the
end of the island are one ground in the corpus.

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
The single source names it by name only, so a conditions pull has to be run
against the zone rather than against this point.

## Getting there

**The documented pattern is a lodge panga day that starts with bait.** The stated
sequence is: fish for bait in the morning, then run to North Point for the bigger
grade (`wdbqTio1SQU`). The bait is mackerel, made on the spot and held in the
lodge boats' bait tanks — tanks the crew noted were working on this trip where
they had not been on earlier visits (`wdbqTio1SQU`); see
[making bait](../bait/making-bait.md) and [bait tanks](../bait/bait-tanks.md).
The boats are large pangas with a casting platform (`wdbqTio1SQU`).

[The zone page](cedros-island.md#spots) groups this point with the Cedros front
side as the default water off the lodge dock; the zone's two access modes — the
Cedros fly-in chain and the San Diego long-range boat — are on
[the zone page](cedros-island.md#getting-there), and neither is written around a
run to this end of the island.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: distance, heading, run time or a sea-state
envelope for the run to North Point, or how the ground is reached by anyone not
on the lodge program.

**Regulatory — Mexico.** Mexican entry and sport-fishing paperwork, and the
zone's operator-level **100% catch-and-release** agreement for
[calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md), grouper (cabrilla) and black seabass,
are recorded on [the zone page](cedros-island.md) and
[Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md). That agreement is an operator agreement,
not a federal or state regulation. Recorded on or before 2023-05-24; the Mexican
paperwork requirements were checked as of 2026-08; **verify current status with
your operator before fishing**
([regulatory claims](../sources/regulatory-claims.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The corpus places this ground and never describes it.** The only structural
statement is positional — it is the north point / north end of Cedros Island,
reached as a run from the lodge water (`wdbqTio1SQU`). Nothing is said on camera
about relief, rock, kelp, sand or the depth being fished.

The zone's recorded soundings — the **28–30 ft** resident rock (`QSmE3mdEL28`),
the **60–68 ft** surface-iron band (`IdMJnlg_rZk`), the ridge cresting near
**60 ft** (`BvT560Nblqo`) — all attach to other Cedros grounds and are on
[the zone page](cedros-island.md#structure--bathymetry), not to this point.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: soundings, bottom composition, kelp extent, the
shape of the point, or how far off it the boats work.

## How it fishes

**Bait and iron are both in play on the same session, and the iron produces.**
The one confirmed hookup called on camera at this ground came on the jig, not the
bait — "was that on bait or jig? Jigging. Yo-yo." (`wdbqTio1SQU`) — so
[yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) works alongside the live mackerel the
crew ran up for. Doubles were called on the same morning, though the footage
does not separate them from the mackerel bait stop (`wdbqTio1SQU`).

**Precision on the trip's opening fish:** the trip's first yellowtail, taken
within the opening ten minutes on [surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)
while the crew was "running and gunning," came on the arrival-afternoon session
before the North Point run was made (`wdbqTio1SQU`) — it is a Cedros fish, not a
North Point fish. The zone's surface-iron program is on
[the zone page](cedros-island.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a tide, moon, current, wind or water-colour
read for this point, what depth the yo-yo was worked to, how long to give the
stop, or whether it fishes differently on any other trip. The single session's
timing statements are a clock — a morning bait stop then a run — never a tide
window.
