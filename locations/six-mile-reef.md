# Six Mile Reef

**[Baja only]** — the **stop on the way out** in the
[Loreto & Puerto Escondido](loreto.md) island cluster: a high spot held as a
mark on the plotter, fished to the bottom on the run from Puerto Escondido out
to [Isla Monserrat](isla-monserrat-north-end.md) (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). It is the one
ground in the zone the corpus fishes **down** rather than along a shoreline or
behind a trolled spread.

**It is a mark, not a destination.** The day's plan named it as the first stop
before the real run — *"stop at Six Mile Reef first, we got a mark here on the
way"* — and the crew were back under way to the island inside the same sequence
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`). Read the page as a waypoint that has produced, not as a
fishery worked for its own sake.

⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): **the name is the only position this reef has.**
"Six Mile Reef" states a distance without a reference point — the source never
says six miles from what, and gives no bearing, run time or sounding to fix it
— and the reef resolves to no charted position in the KB (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
Queued in [the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
That library stops at San Quintín on the Pacific side and holds nothing on this
coast, so a conditions pull has to run against
[the zone](loreto.md) rather than against this reef.

## Getting there

**You pass it, so the run is the zone's run.** The reef sits on the outbound
leg from **Puerto Escondido Marina** toward Isla Monserrat, and the day on
record went to it first because the boat already held the mark (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).
The zone's access — flights in through CBX, a mothership at anchor with an
**18 ft** Grady White towed behind it, the marina as the working base, and
sardines ordered from the bait vendor who meets boats out front — is on
[the zone page](loreto.md#getting-there).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): **the access summary restates a claim the
zone page already flags.** `n4nMCLLW3tY` carries only the custom cleat-to-cleat
tow rope for *"towing the Grady White behind the Odyssey"* — no length, no CBX,
and the word "anchor" appears nowhere in it. The **18 ft** figure and CBX reach
this page from `ikiXx9sUsoA`, where both verify; the *mothership at anchor*
framing does not — neither transcript says "mothership" or "anchor", the vessel
is named *"the Odyssey"*, and [the zone page](loreto.md#getting-there) carries a
standing `contradicted-by-source` flag on that framing which this page restates
unflagged. Queued in [the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

**Nothing in the corpus times the leg.** The only distance the source states is
to a different ground on a later day of the same trip: the north end of Isla
Carmen at **about 20 miles, roughly an hour** from Puerto Escondido
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`). That figure belongs to
[Isla Carmen](loreto.md#structure--bathymetry) and does not transfer here.

## Structure & bathymetry

**One property is on the record: it is a high spot you can sit on top of.** The
crew held it as a plotter mark, fished it, and then repositioned — *"I'll go
back over to the high spot"* — which is what makes it a discrete piece of
relief rather than a stretch of bottom (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).

**It is fished all the way down.** The drops here go to the bottom and the
crew check each other on it — *"have you hit bottom yet"* and *"all the way
down huh"* (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). That is the only depth information the reef has:
deep enough that hitting bottom is worth
asking about, against a zone whose every other documented ground is **5–30 ft**
([the zone page](loreto.md#structure--bathymetry)).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): **one of the two depth quotes lands
before the boat gets there.** *"all the way down huh"* is spoken at 03:36,
sixty-seven seconds ahead of the on-camera arrival *"all right we made it to Six
Mile Reef"* at 04:43; only the *"have you hit bottom yet"* / *"no just the
trigger"* exchange at 05:09 is unambiguously on the mark (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). Queued
in [the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the reef's depth, its relief, its bottom
composition, how big it is, or any sounder picture of it — the mark is used as
a plotter position, and nothing is ever read off the screen
([electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)). The zone
page carries the same gap a rung up.

## How it fishes

**Get on the mark, drop, and reposition when it goes quiet.** The whole
documented program is arriving on a saved plotter position, fishing down to the
bottom, and moving back over the high spot when the drops stop connecting
(`n4nMCLLW3tY`). No anchoring, no drift line and no chum is described.

**It is a short stop inside a longer day.** The reef was fished on the way out
and the crew carried on to the island — *"we're out here at Monserrat"* follows
directly (`n4nMCLLW3tY`) — so what it buys is a few fish and a look at the
water, not a session. The zone's trolling programs run between stops like this
one ([the zone page](loreto.md#how-it-fishes),
[trolling](../techniques/trolling.md)).

**A worn jig was swapped for a fresh one on the approach** — *"no more paint
left"* and a fresh Megabait put on for the colour (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). It is the
only tackle detail attached to this ground.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: which lure, line class or retrieve took which
fish here; whether the drops were jigged or baited; tide, current, moon, wind or
time-of-day window for this reef; or how long to give it before running on.
Bottom-jig execution generally is in [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md)
and [knife jigging](../techniques/knife-jigging.md), but **nothing in the
corpus says either was used here.**
