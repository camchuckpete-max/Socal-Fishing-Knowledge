# La Jolla

The San Diego coastal zone at the head of La Jolla Canyon: **14 charted spots**
in the spot library (cameron) — the thirteen of the library's own La Jolla
section plus La Jolla Canyon itself, carried in the offshore-banks section. Two
fisheries share the zone. The north end is a nearshore reef and high-spot
program for [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) in 5–10 fathoms
(`yLpDI8jnizU`); to the south the shelf hard bottom is drifted at 180–210 ft for
[rockfish](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md) (`_ZThckj2TIM`). **Presence is not
catchability here** — some yellowtail are in La Jolla year-round and half-day
boats can go months between fish (cameron).

## Getting there

The zone is inside **morning-half-day range** for a San Diego charter: a boat
can fish it, run in and offload, and be back on the same spots the same
afternoon (`VpW91AKOFVQ`).

**Three of the fourteen spots carry an MPA edge** in the spot library
(cameron):

| spot | stated boundary |
| --- | --- |
| The Cove | stay **west of 117°16.400'W** |
| Windansea | stay **north of 32°49.573'N** |
| Crystal Pier | stay **south of 32°47.945'N** |

The three run north to south down the beach side of the zone, so the inshore
line is the part of La Jolla with boundary rules on it and the offshore
structure is not. Latitude or longitude proximity alone does not establish
where a boundary runs. California / CDFW — verify current MPA boundaries,
closures and take rules with CDFW before fishing (as of 2026-08).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the ports and launch ramps this zone is run
from, run distance or time to any spot, and the sea-state envelope the run
demands.

## Structure & bathymetry

The zone sorts into four families, all charted by position in the spot library
(cameron):

- **The canyon and its shelf.** La Jolla Canyon (**32°54.000'N
  117°18.800'W**), with the La Jolla Canyon Shelf rockfish area (**32°52.500'N
  117°19.000'W**) and La Jolla Shelf hardbottom (**32°51.300'N 117°19.250'W**)
  inshore of it — the only three spots in the zone the library names for what
  the bottom is rather than for a landmark.
- **The north-end reef and high spots.** The Cove, The Bump, The Half and the
  Northwest Corner, inside about 2 minutes of latitude of each other. The
  **Northwest Corner** is a reef with an **incline** running into it: the
  sounder shows yellowtail as big arcs at **5–10 fathoms** with bait around
  them, and holding fish down at **90 ft** on the incline itself
  (`yLpDI8jnizU`).
- **The beach spots.** Marine Street, Windansea and Crystal Pier — the
  southern two carrying the MPA edges above.
- **The rockfish ground.** 32 Fathom Spot rockfish, The 270 rockfish area,
  The Airplane rockfish area and Widow Rock, the zone's southern and western
  corner. Three are labelled "rockfish area" outright. The one measured bottom
  in the corpus is a **rock pile / high spot in 35 fathoms — 180–210 ft** —
  with schools hanging off the structure on the sounder, though which of the
  named marks it was is not stated (`_ZThckj2TIM`).

**Hard-meets-soft is the seam that matters.** Bottom discrimination read while
running this zone shows where hard bottom meets soft, and those transitions are
the travel-and-feeding highways — worth more than the top of the high spot
itself (`yLpDI8jnizU`). Method in
[electronics and the sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).

## How it fishes

**Run the north end on the meter before you fish it.** Work a split screen —
chart on one half, sounder on the other — across the Northwest Corner reef
looking for big arcs at 5–10 fathoms with bait around them; when you run over
fish, use the sounder's history playback to drop a waypoint on the mark you
already passed, turn back over it, and cast a jig at it (`yLpDI8jnizU`).
Full method in
[electronics and the sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md) and
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).

**Winter yellowtail here are an iron fishery, both irons.**
[Surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md) and
[yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) both produced on the same January
morning, the yo-yo out-fishing the surface jig 3 fish to 1 (`VpW91AKOFVQ`).
Dial the retrieve tighter than an island trip needs: the smaller mainland
schools from Del Mar to Imperial Beach are finicky about jig action,
especially on bigger-grade fish (`VpW91AKOFVQ`).

**At a kelp bed, current direction picks the corner.** La Jolla is one of the
named beds this rule is taught on: **current running downhill → work the
northwest corner; current running uphill → work the southeast corner**, and
the same logic carries to rock structure (`kzD0kSnnVPw`). Mechanism in
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md).

**Rockfish here are drifted, not anchored.** At 35 fathoms the depth rules out
anchoring: set up up-wind and up-current of the mark and let the drift carry
the baits across it (`_ZThckj2TIM`). Execution — weights by depth, dropper-loop
build, descending devices — is
[rockfish deep-dropping](../techniques/rockfish-deep-dropping.md).

**On the deep bass structure, stay off the top of it.** Cast the slug to the
side of the spot rather than sitting on top of the mark, on a **1 oz** lead
head; colour is not the variable (`-eaaWPN5Fxk`). Retrieve in
[lead-head swimbait retrieve](../techniques/leadhead-swimbait-retrieve.md).

**The Northwest Corner is a conditions bellwether for the whole Bight.** It is
one of the original eight [BightSST eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)
(cameron), so per-spot SST and chlorophyll here are modelled and scored — and
the operative read is time-to-turnover, since the bite is best right *before*
turnover ([upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)).

## Spots

Fourteen charted spots (cameron), north to south, grouped by what the structure
is rather than by how close they sit:

- **The canyon** — La Jolla Canyon, the deepest and northwesternmost position
  in the zone, carried in the library's offshore-banks section rather than its
  La Jolla section.
- **The shelf** — La Jolla Canyon Shelf rockfish area and La Jolla Shelf
  hardbottom, stacked inshore of the canyon on nearly the same longitude.
- **The north-end reef** — The Cove, The Bump, The Half, Northwest Corner. The
  Northwest Corner is the zone's one spot with corpus detail beyond its
  position, and the zone's yellowtail mark (`yLpDI8jnizU`).
- **The beach** — Marine Street, Windansea, Crystal Pier. Windansea and
  Crystal Pier are the MPA-edge spots.
- **The rockfish ground** — 32 Fathom Spot rockfish, The 270 rockfish area,
  Widow Rock, The Airplane rockfish area.

Charted positions for all fourteen are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).
