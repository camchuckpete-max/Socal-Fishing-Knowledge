# Kidney Bank (63) / 175

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is two charted marks in open water directly
north of Santa Barbara Island — **Kidney Bank (63)** at **33°34.467'N
119°00.850'W** and the **175** at **33°36.850'N 118°57.117'W** (cameron). They
sit **3.9 nm** apart: the 175 bears **053°** from Kidney Bank, and Kidney Bank
bears **233°** from the 175. At **3.9 nm** end to end this is one of the tightest
zones the census cut out of the offshore-banks list, far inside its **12 nm**
diameter cap — a two-stop circuit, not a search area.

**No corpus source names either mark.** Neither "Kidney Bank (63)" nor the "175"
appears in a transcript, a knowledge note or an evidence file; the zone exists on
coordinates from [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) (cameron). What
makes it different from the Bight's other position-only bank pages is **what it
is next to**: [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) is **5.6 nm**
south, and it is one of the better-documented island zones in the corpus.
Everything this page can offer beyond position is borrowed from that neighbour
and labelled as borrowed.

**The borrowed lead is a bluefin one, and it is worth checking on the water.**
The bluefin ground worked off the island in November 2024 and October 2025 sat
**"just northeast of Santa Barbara Island"** (`usHl-4SfqDA`, `mDmbGdQAy-4`,
[Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)) — and this zone *is* the water
north and northeast of the island by position: Kidney Bank bears **008°** from
Landing Cove at **5.6 nm** and the 175 bears **026°** at **8.8 nm** (cameron).
⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): no source names a mark, a bank or a number on that
ground, so the overlap is a coordinate coincidence until a report confirms it.
Treat it as the first place to look, not as a documented spot.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run distance, heading, timing, fuel figure
or sea-state envelope to this zone from any port, and no source names a port
either mark is fished out of.

**From Catalina's west end this zone is not "past" Santa Barbara Island — it is
the same distance.** A private tournament boat left **Two Harbors** and was on
the island in **about an hour and 15–20 minutes**, arriving in the dark to start
at first light (`PKf7G3uL4io`,
[Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)). Measured from Ship Rock, a
charted position just off that harbour, Landing Cove is **27.0 nm**, Kidney
Bank is **27.0 nm** and the 175 is **24.8 nm** (cameron) — the outer mark of this
zone is the *closer* of the three. The island run figure is therefore a
reasonable first estimate for this water rather than a floor to add to.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states a run to either mark directly.

**From the mainland it is a long way, and the library's coast positions are all
south of the useful ones.** The nearest charted mainland position is **Pelican
Point** in [South Orange County](south-orange-county-crystal-cove.md),
**54.8 nm** from the 175 and **57.9 nm** from Kidney Bank, with the Dana Point
kelp at **62.2 nm** and **65.0 nm** (cameron) — all bearing roughly **090–099°**,
back east down the coast. The library carries no charted positions on the Los
Angeles, Ventura or Santa Barbara shore, which is the shore this zone sits off,
so the run that would actually matter to a boat coming from the north is not
computable from the corpus.

**Plan it as overnight range.** The jig-stick top-shot rule puts Santa Barbara
Island in the same class as San Clemente Island — **50 lb** mono rather than the
40 lb run at the full-day-accessible islands, because those spots are overnight
range with a trophy shot at any moment (`LTCFjqtSI8g`,
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)). The sportboat pattern at the
island is to run overnight out of the mainland, "get there in the dark, get
anchored up and be sitting on the numbers" (`SGbynqaiHdY`,
[Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)). This zone is at the same range
as that island from every port the library can measure (cameron).

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries or restricted areas
stated for either position. The one MPA line the corpus knows in this part of the
Bight is the Cave Canyon instruction on Santa Barbara Island's east end — **stay
north of 33 28.500** — and both marks here lie north of that latitude by
**6.0** and **8.4 minutes** (cameron,
[Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)); that is a latitude comparison,
not a boundary reading, and the line's east–west extent is not in the corpus. The
corpus also names the **Cow Cod Conservation Area** as an offshore closure with
its own depth limit and its own as-of date
([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)) but gives no boundary for
it, so whether either mark falls inside it is unverified — **check the CCCA
boundary against your position with CDFW/NOAA before dropping.** Boundary
compliance is positional, not depth-based ([US waters](us-waters.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**Two positions, 3.9 nm apart on a southwest–northeast line.** Kidney Bank (63)
sits at **33°34.467'N 119°00.850'W**, the 175 at **33°36.850'N 118°57.117'W** —
**2.38 minutes** of latitude and **3.73 minutes** of longitude apart, which works
out to **3.9 nm** on the **053°/233°** line between them (cameron).

**The names are the only depth claims here, and they disagree violently.** A bank
is an isolated high spot rising out of otherwise deep water and takes the
**fathom depth of its shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way:

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kidney Bank (63) | 63 fathoms | about 378 ft |
| 175 | 175 fathoms | about 1,050 ft |

**That is a 112-fathom spread across 3.9 nm**, and it is what makes this zone
interesting on paper: two marks a short hop apart whose names claim depths a
factor of nearly three apart (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms
either number as a charted reading, or gives relief, bottom composition or
surrounding depth at either mark.

**If the 63 is real, it is the shallowest high spot in this stretch of the outer
Bight.** Every other fathom-named mark within **25 nm** of the zone centre reads
far deeper — the **172** at **8.9 nm**, the **125** at **15.3 nm**, **The Boot
(504)** at **16.1 nm**, the **170** at **16.3 nm**, the **286** at **19.2 nm**,
**LA - 270** at **21.1 nm** and the **499** at **22.7 nm** (cameron). Under the
naming convention that makes Kidney Bank the most pronounced rise on this side of
the island group, which is exactly the sort of structure bait stages on and
gamefish follow (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).
⚠ Fact-check (single-source): the ranking rests entirely on reading names as
soundings; it is not a bathymetric claim.

**Where the two readings land against the corpus's working bands.** **378 ft** is
below the shallow **100–250 ft** Channel Islands band the rockfish router uses
and above the **75-fathom (450 ft)** mainland figure it cites for the depth-based
closure line — and that router's own lesson is that the line runs at different
depths in different places, so a chart app and a position, not a depth reading,
decide legality ([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)). **1,050
ft** on the 175 sits inside the **800–1,100 ft** band SoCal daytime swordfish
baits are set in and inside the **150–350 fathom** contour band that fishery works
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md),
[swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source targets
either species on either mark.

**"Kidney Bank" is a name and a number at once, and the corpus explains neither.**
Nine entries in the library carry both a word name and a fathom figure, this one
among them (cameron). The number is the depth claim; the word is unexplained
anywhere in the corpus. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says what the name
describes.

**The name also collides, and Cameron has already ruled on it.** "Kidney Bank
(63)" and **"302 (Kidney Bank)"** are logged as colloquially ambiguous but
distinct spots (cameron, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). They are **99.3 nm**
apart: this one is north of Santa Barbara Island, the other is a compact bank in
the [Coronados - 230 / 302 (Kidney Bank) / 226](coronados-230-302-226.md) zone
down at the border (cameron). Confirm which Kidney a report means before you plot
it — the two are on opposite ends of the Bight and are fished for different
things.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: high-spot shape, preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at
either mark.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron, measured from Kidney Bank unless
noted): **Landing Cove** and **Webster Point** on
[Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) at **5.6 nm**, the **Sutil Island
squid / yellowtail zone** at **6.9 nm**, the **172** at **8.6 nm** from the 175,
**Hidden Reef** at **11.9 nm** northwest, **Osborn Bank** at **12.9 nm** south —
which the census attaches to the Santa Barbara Island zone rather than to a bank
grouping — **The Boot (504)** at **14.1 nm** from the 175, the **170** at
**15.7 nm**, the **125** at **16.2 nm**, the **286** at **17.7 nm** from the 175,
and the **West End Humps** on [Catalina](catalina-island-front-side.md) at
**18.8 nm** from the 175. Every direction out of this zone has a page:
[Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) south, [172 / 125](172-125.md)
southeast, [Hidden Reef / 170](hidden-reef-170.md) northwest,
[The Boot (504) / 307](boot-504-307.md) northeast and
[LA - 270 / 286](la-270-286.md) east.

**This zone sits immediately off the volume square's northern corner, not inside
it.** The **SBI – Nick – Clemente – Catalina** box is where the bigger bluefin
were in September 2022 (`5to3Q5P7w90`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[September](../seasonal/september.md)). Kidney Bank lies **5.3 minutes** of
latitude and the 175 **7.7 minutes** north of Webster Point, the northernmost
charted position on Santa Barbara Island (cameron) — a few miles outside the
corner rather than deep outside the box, which is a different situation from the
banks further northwest.

**The fall warm-band arc arrives from this side.** In fall the arc runs
**backside Santa Cruz / Rosa → Santa Barbara Island → backside Catalina → the 499
and Clemente** (`OYOda6T3f-8`, `HzE4FHHcvTk`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)). Santa Barbara Island is a named station
on it, and this zone sits on the leg the band comes *from* — north of the island
(cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source places either mark on or off the arc,
and the northern Channel Islands carry no coordinates in the spot library
([Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)), so the leg cannot be drawn. Pull
temperature and chlorophyll per position and decide on the water
([water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)).

**This zone is in the scarce half of the paddy split.** The best paddy water in
the Bight is **south of San Clemente Island / Catalina** and the coastal strip
from **Dana Point to Oceanside and southward**; north of Dana Point paddies thin
out and hold fewer fish (`HzE4FHHcvTk`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)). Kidney Bank sits **5.0 minutes** and the
175 **7.4 minutes** of latitude north of the northernmost charted Dana Point
position (cameron), above that line. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source describes a
paddy over either position either way; mechanics are in
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the two are run as one circuit, which end to work
first, or what tide, light, current or swell do here.

**The geometry reads as two compact spot-checks, not a search box.** The Bight's
two zone types are the complex — a **~50–60 square-mile** box you plan hours of
looking across — and the compact spot you idle onto, grade on the meter, and
either commit to or leave in **20–30 minutes** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). Two marks **3.9 nm** apart is among the
shortest hops between charted positions in any bank zone on this ladder
(cameron): both are readable inside an hour of a morning, which is the compact
pattern.

**In practice this is an extension of the Santa Barbara Island run, not a
destination.** That zone is two fisheries on one trip — a shoreline program of
bass, squid yellowtail and rockfish, and a **separate** tuna run on the water off
the island with separate gear ([Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)).
These two marks lie on the tuna side of that split by position (cameron), which
makes the realistic plan "island plus a look north," not "run to the 63."
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source describes anyone running this zone at all.

**The nearest documented bank program is Osborn Bank's, 12.9 nm south.** There
the bite is a **graylight** one starting around **4:30–5:00 a.m.** and building,
and the bank behaves as a **temperature edge** — **70 °F** right at the edge about
a mile and a half off the boat against **72 °F** where it sat, with the fish in
the cooler water and the yellowtail and yellowfin southeast in the warmer
(`i3qIAHW-SJc`, [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). That is the closest
analogue the corpus offers for how an isolated bank works in this water, and it
is an analogue, not a description of these marks.

**If the borrowed bluefin lead is live, the tackle answer is already written.**
On the ground northeast of the island, foamers in October 2025 refused both a
trolled spreader bar and cast plugs because the fish were locked onto saury,
anchovy and microbait; what converted was a **dead flying fish under the kite**
(`mDmbGdQAy-4`, [kite fishing](../techniques/kite-fishing.md),
[bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md#situations--techniques)), and the fight
style on that ground is the beltless, rail-locked "railroading" method
(`usHl-4SfqDA`, `mDmbGdQAy-4`,
[fighting big bluefin](../techniques/fighting-big-bluefin.md)).

**Wind is the risk this zone cannot answer for itself, and its neighbours argue
it is exposed.** A big NW event rolls the **northern Channel Islands** and the
**LA / Ventura coastal shelf** hardest — **5–8 °F** off the surface temperature —
while the Coronados, Catalina and San Clemente held their water through the same
event (`HzE4FHHcvTk`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). A single
August 2022 wind event was forecast to blow out the Channel Islands, San
Clemente, San Nicolas, Santa Barbara Island and the tuna zone off Catalina in one
go (`CMQkHQMxbXM`, [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)). Both marks
sit **5.6 nm and 8.8 nm** north of the island's own shoreline positions
(cameron) — outside its lee on the weather side. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
classifies this water on the rollover map; weight it down when a NW event is
forecast and pull the water per position ([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

**Report coverage out here will be thin, and thin is not empty.** A quiet week
from Santa Barbara Island is a read on how many boats went, not on how many fish
are there (`5p6gu14ZC4w`, [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md),
[report reading](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)). These two marks
are a further few miles out with **zero** corpus mentions (cameron), so the same
correction applies harder.

**Neither mark is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions for this zone are
pulled per charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)). The nearest eval target is the
**14 Mile Bank**, **49.4 nm** east-southeast of the 175 (cameron) — far enough
that its score says nothing about this water. General search method is in
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md) and
[electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md); neither is
scoped here.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron), listed inner to outer — inner meaning closer to
Santa Barbara Island, which is the only thing in this water with a documented
fishery:

- **Kidney Bank (63)** — the southwestern and inner mark, **5.6 nm** due north of
  Landing Cove on **008°** and the same **5.6 nm** from Webster Point (cameron).
  It is the zone's shallow claim: **63 fathoms**, about **378 ft**, read off the
  naming convention (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), which would make it the shallowest
  fathom-named mark within 25 nm. It is also the half of the name collision that
  is *not* the Coronados bank. Coordinates only; the corpus never names it.
- **The 175** — the northeastern and outer mark, **3.9 nm** from Kidney Bank on
  **053°**, and the closer of the two to everything east: **49.4 nm** to the
  14 Mile Bank, **54.8 nm** to Pelican Point, **8.6 nm** to the 172 in
  [172 / 125](172-125.md) and **14.1 nm** to The Boot (504) in
  [The Boot (504) / 307](boot-504-307.md) (cameron). Its name reads as
  **175 fathoms**, about **1,050 ft**. Coordinates only.

Charted positions for both are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
