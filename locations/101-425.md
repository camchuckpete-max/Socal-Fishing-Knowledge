# 101 / 425

**A zone is a run grouping.** 101 / 425 is a two-mark piece of the numbered
offshore-bank grid lying south of the Coronado Islands and roughly ten miles off
the Rosarito–Descanso coast. The two charted positions (cameron) share an
**exact latitude — 32°15.000'N** — and sit **6.9 nm** apart on a bearing of
**270°**, so the zone is a single due east–west line rather than a cluster with
a shape: the **101** at **32°15.000'N 117°15.000'W** is the eastern, inshore
end and the **425** at **32°15.000'N 117°23.200'W** is the western, offshore
end.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing either mark.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid — dozens of charted positions over seventeen bank zones with
no depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
two names claim, how it sits against the water around it, and what is missing.

**What makes it worth reading anyway is the company it keeps.** Most of the
numbered grid is deep water a long way from anything the corpus describes. This
zone is not: the 101 sits **5.0 nm** from the Coronado Islands' outermost
charted mark and **3.4 nm** from the nearest depth-labelled rockfish spot in
[Finger Bank rockfish](finger-bank-rockfish.md) (cameron). It is the numbered
bank water that lies closest to the most-fished Mexican-side day in the KB, and
the corpus still says nothing about it.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run, heading, timing or fuel figure to

**The nearest access frame the corpus offers belongs to the Coronado Islands,
not to this zone.** The chain runs **~13 nm from the San Diego bay entrance**
(cameron) and is the closest island fishing to a major US port
([Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md)). Measured against the library's
charted US marks, this zone is a longer reach on the same axis: **17.7 nm** from
the **Imperial Beach Pipe** and **25.4 nm** from the **Point Loma Pipe** to the
101, **20.2 nm** and **25.9 nm** to the 425 (cameron). Both marks bear
**356–031°** from those pipes — very nearly due south — which is the same line
a Coronados day already runs.

**The Baja coast is closer than either US pipe.** Punta Descanso lies
**11.1 nm** east of the 101 and the Rosarito Flats **9.4 nm** northeast of it
(cameron; [Rosarito / Descanso](rosarito-descanso.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: any ramp, hoist, fuel dock or bait receiver on that coast, and
nothing in the KB describes a trip departing from it — the region page flags the
whole stretch between the border and Punta Banda as unsourced.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.

this zone, which lies further south than the islands do. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: closures, protected areas or restricted zones anywhere in this
zone, and no source states where the maritime border runs relative to these
marks.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The zone is a line, and a short one.** The 101 and the 425 lie **6.9 nm**
(about **8.0 statute miles**) apart at **270°** (cameron) — inside the census's
**12 nm** zone-diameter cap with room to spare, which is why the two clustered
on their own. There is no third mark and no dogleg: everything in this zone is
one east–west leg, and a boat working from one to the other never changes
latitude.

**The names are the only depth claims here.** A bank takes the **fathom depth
of its shallowest (highest) point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`):

| mark | name read as depth | at six feet to a fathom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 101 | 101 fathoms | about 606 ft |
| 425 | 425 fathoms | about 2,550 ft |

**Read literally the two marks are not the same kind of water.** The 101 at
about 606 ft falls inside the KB's **deep rockfish band of 300–900 ft**
([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)) — one of very few
numbered-grid names anywhere in the region that reads as a workable bottom
depth. The 425 at about 2,550 ft sits below the swordfish contour band of
**150–350 fathoms** ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)) and inside the
**2,000–3,000 ft** deep, mostly featureless plain the lexicon describes a bank
as rising *out of* ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`) — so as a
depth reading the 425 describes a crest that barely stands up. ⚠ Flagged gap —
no corpus source confirms either number as a charted depth, and no source states
whether these two marks sit on one feature or on two unrelated ones that happen
to share a latitude.

**The mileage reading does not apply here.** The lexicon's one documented
exception to the fathom convention is the **9 Mile Bank**, named for sitting
nine statute miles off the coast rather than for a depth
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Neither 101 nor 425 matches a
distance from any port or headland the corpus charts — the marks are 9.4–25.9 nm
from every reference position in the library's San Diego and Rosarito sections
(cameron) — so nothing supports reading these numbers as miles.

**The 101's nearest charted neighbours are in another zone, and they are closer
than its own zone partner.** The three depth-labelled Upper Finger Bank rockfish
marks lie **3.4–4.1 nm** east of the 101, against **6.9 nm** west to the 425
(cameron), and they belong to [Finger Bank rockfish](finger-bank-rockfish.md) —
a `##` section of the spot library, so a boundary the fishery drew rather than
one the census inferred. The **Rockpile** at **32°17.620'N 117°10.030'W**, the
Coronados' outlying southeastern mark, is **5.0 nm** from the 101 and
**South Kelp Ridge** **7.2 nm** (cameron; [Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether the 101 is fished on a Coronados day,
worked with the Finger Bank rockfish ladder, or run as its own destination with
the 425.

**The 425's second neighbour also sits across a zone line.** The **371** at
**32°16.200'N 117°32.800'W** is **8.2 nm** west of the 425 and only **1.2
minutes of latitude** north of it, yet it parents to
[Coronados - 230 / 302 / 226](coronados-230-302-226.md), a zone the census
labels `socal-bight` while this one is labelled `baja-pacific-north` (cameron).
The two marks are neighbours on the water and are filed on opposite sides of a
region boundary. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states where that boundary
lies, so nothing in the KB resolves which label is right for this band of
latitude.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure not attached to land **fishes in any direction**, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: bottom composition, relief, contour, high-spot shape, surrounding
depth, preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at either mark. The
`depth_band` is unknown for that reason, not because the names are absent.

**The 101's charted position is coarser than most of the library's.** It is
given as round whole minutes on both axes — 15.000 and 15.000 — where
neighbouring entries carry tenths and hundredths of a minute (cameron). A
position stated at that resolution describes an area rather than a pinpoint;
the 425 is charted to the tenth in longitude and shares the same round latitude.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a more precise position for either mark, or
any statement of how the library's positions were recorded.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states

**The one structural thing that can be said about working it is geometric.**
The two marks sit on the same latitude, so the run between them is due east–west
— a drift or troll from one to the other crosses whatever current is setting
along the coast rather than running with it. General reading layers are
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md) and
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md); neither is scoped to this water,
and no source describes the current here at all.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron), listed east to west. They are the whole zone:

- **The 101** — the eastern, inshore mark, and the one that gives this zone its
  only claim on real fishing water: **5.0 nm** from the Coronados' Rockpile,
  **3.4 nm** from the nearest Upper Finger Bank rockfish mark, **9.4 nm** from
  the Rosarito Flats (cameron), and the shallower of the two names by a factor
  of four if the numbers are fathoms.
- **The 425** — the western, offshore mark, **6.9 nm** out from the 101 on the
  same latitude and the deeper name. It is the zone's closest approach to the
  numbered banks lying west, with the **371** **8.2 nm** further on.

Charted positions for both are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
