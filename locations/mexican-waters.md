# Mexican waters

Everything south of the border needs paperwork a US angler does not carry by
default, and the checks are real rather than theoretical. This page is the
paperwork rung of the geographic ladder: what to buy, what to print, and what
to have to hand. It covers every Mexican region the KB holds — `baja-pacific-north`,
`baja-pacific-south`, `cortez-north` and `cortez-south`
([regions & waters](regions.md); region pages land with the geo phase) — and it
says nothing about how to fish. That is the regions and zones below it.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: INM for entry, CONAPESCA for fishing.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you travel: these
change, and this is a foreign jurisdiction.**

## Papers you need

1. **FMM — the tourist permit.** Filled out online in advance; needs passport
   details, point of entry and length of stay, and produces a printout.
   **Print it.** One angler reported being checked three times on a single
   trip (`P36VGPPf120`).
2. **Mexican fishing licence (CONAPESCA).** Bought online. The licence moved to
   a *new* website as of 2026, so search fresh rather than trusting an old
   bookmark (`P36VGPPf120`). Carry your passport when you buy.
3. **Passport.**
4. **Vehicle insurance — separate, and mandatory in practice.** US policies do
   not carry over. **Baja Bound** is the named recommendation; a 4-day trip ran
   about **$90** for a 2021 car and about **$130** for a newer Tacoma
   (`P36VGPPf120`).

corpus does not answer it.

## Bringing fish home

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: US customs declaration for fish caught in
Mexico, transport and limits at the border.

Species and bag limits set by Mexican law are regulations, not fishery
knowledge, and are recorded per-species with their jurisdiction and as-of date;
see [regulatory claims](../sources/regulatory-claims.md). A Baja limit that
differs from a CDFW limit is two jurisdictions, never a doctrine conflict.

## Regions

Four regions sit on this side of the line: the Pacific coast of Baja, split
north and south at the BC/BCS state line, and the Sea of Cortez, split the same
way ([regions](regions.md)). The paperwork on this page applies to all four;
how they fish does not — a Cortez day and a Pacific-coast day share only the
permit.
