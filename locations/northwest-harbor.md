# Northwest Harbor

The harbor at the northwest end of San Clemente Island, charted at
**33°02.000'N 118°35.250'W** and labelled a naval security zone (cameron). It
is the northern-most mark on the
[front side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md) run N→S and the only front-side
spot the corpus fishes by name away from the Pyramid end: two sessions work a
shallow-rock calico program inside it (`KCcEqHSZ84k`,
`eNcltRh-shc`) and a tournament team made its landing here before working back
north and down the front side (`iQLyBzhOSi8`).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the spot library charts this harbor as a naval
security zone while the corpus records boats fishing inside it. Both records
are carried as stated, and the question is already queued for Cameron in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md) against the
[zone page](san-clemente-island-front-side.md). Jurisdiction: U.S. Navy, as of
2026-08 — verify the current security-zone boundary before fishing it.

## Getting there

**Plan it as an overnight, and check the Navy schedule first.** Northwest
Harbor inherits the zone's access terms: San Clemente Island sits at overnight
range rather than day range, and it is a U.S. Navy training range whose zones
open and close on a rotating training schedule, with the crossing given as
roughly **60 miles** straight off the beach (`XyKF34C5iB4`). **Jurisdiction:
U.S. Navy, as of the 2025-04-19 seminar — verify current access before running
the crossing.** The full access picture is on
[San Clemente Island — Front Side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md).

**It is the corner of the island, not a stop along the front side.** Measured
off the charted positions in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md), the
harbor sits about **11.3 nm** northwest of the next front-side mark,
White Rock, while the back-side spots are just around the end — Castle Rock
about **1.4 nm** and [9 Fathom Spot](9-fathom-spot.md) about **2.1 nm** away
(cameron). A day that starts here is at the top of the N→S run, which is how
the tournament team fished it: land at the harbor, then work back north and
down this side (`iQLyBzhOSi8`). Castle Rock, the neighbouring back-side mark,
is charted with the same naval-security-zone label (cameron,
[back side](san-clemente-island-back-side.md)).

**The crossing is the part that gets chosen against.** The team that landed
here ran to San Clemente Island rather than staying at Catalina while citing
"some really big swells" for that crossing, on an event window tracked at
**5–7 ft** the day before, **4–6 ft** on day one and **3–5 ft** forecast four
days later (`iQLyBzhOSi8`, [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The documented structure is boiler rock, and it is very shallow.** A boat
anchors tight to island rock in about **6 ft of water** on a feature named on
camera as "Coiler Rock" — asr-uncertain, the name is not independently
confirmed — and fishes sardines over it (`KCcEqHSZ84k`). A second session
worked another shallow rock inside the same harbor, named on camera as "boiler
Rock" and possibly the same feature, again with fish taken very shallow and
tight to the rock (`eNcltRh-shc`). Break-offs on the rock are the recurring
failure mode there, so constant re-tying is part of the program
(`eNcltRh-shc`). That matches the island group's general framing as a hazard
stack of thick kelp, razor-sharp boiler rocks and big waves (`iQLyBzhOSi8`,
[island structure](island-structure.md)).

**Water clarity here swings hard between visits.** One session found the
harbor unusually calm and clear for the island at **30–40 ft visibility**
(`eNcltRh-shc`); the tournament landing found it **dirty and washed out**, with
conditions described as coming from "a different direction than we normally
get" and a little current running along the front side (`iQLyBzhOSi8`,
[water color](../conditions/water-color.md)).

composition, kelp extent, how the named rocks sit relative to each other, or
whether the two on-camera rock names are one feature or two.

## How it fishes

**Fish it as an anchored shallow-rock spot on bait.** The working program is to
anchor tight to the rock in about 6 ft and fish sardines over it, with chum
(`KCcEqHSZ84k`, [chunking](../techniques/chunking.md),
[fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md)).

**Fight with the tip high and never pump.** Hooked this shallow and this tight
to rock, hold the rod tip as high as possible and wind as fast as possible
without ever pumping the rod — a dropped tip gives the fish the leverage to
bury its head in the rock and root the leader off. Line for that scenario is
**65 lb braid straight to a 30 lb fluorocarbon top shot** (`KCcEqHSZ84k`,
[chunking](../techniques/chunking.md)). Expect to re-tie constantly; break-offs
on the rock are the recurring problem here (`eNcltRh-shc`).

**A working plastics cadence here is bottom-up, then free-spool back.** Once a
producing spot was located the coaching was to drop straight to the bottom,
wind **10 or 15 turns** up, free-spool back down and repeat — the lure type is
not named on camera (`iQLyBzhOSi8`, [swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md)).

**Read the water before committing the morning to it.** The same harbor shows
30–40 ft visibility on one visit and dirty, washed-out water on another
(`eNcltRh-shc`, `iQLyBzhOSi8`); on the dirty day the team left and worked back
north and down the front side rather than grinding it out (`iQLyBzhOSi8`,
[water color](../conditions/water-color.md)).

**Check the current at the spot.** Two San Clemente Island spots a short run
apart can show opposite current directions on the same day, so a read carried
down from the last stop does not transfer here
([front side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). A little current was
noted running along the front side on the tournament day (`iQLyBzhOSi8`).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: how the anchor is set and where, what wind
and tide do to the harbor, and how long to give it before moving.
