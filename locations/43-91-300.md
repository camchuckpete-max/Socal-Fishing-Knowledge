# 43 / 91 / 300

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is a three-mark piece of the numbered
offshore-bank grid — the **43**, the **91** and the **300** (cameron) — spanning
**3.6 minutes of latitude** (32°35.750'N to 32°39.333'N) and **4.2 minutes of
longitude** (117°56.350'W to 118°00.500'W). The whole zone is **4.0 nm** across,
a third of the census's 12 nm zone-diameter cap and the **tightest three-mark
bank zone in the Bight grid**; all three marks clustered on their own
coordinates, so nothing here was attached on distance.

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the census's 12 nm figure is a
spot-to-zone-CENTRE distance, not a zone diameter and not a cap on pair
separation — `sources/geo-census.txt` reads "339 spot pages assigned; 18 sit
further than 12 nm from their zone centre". Both this sentence and "the 12 nm
cap that is the clustering rule which grouped them" in Structure & bathymetry
measure a different quantity. Read as the census means it, the three marks sit
**2.0 nm** (43), **1.8 nm** (91) and **2.3 nm** (300) from the zone centre
(cameron), so the "tightest three-mark bank zone" finding still holds — the
tightest rivals are 51 / 181 / 138 at 6.1 nm and 172 / 125 at 6.8 nm — and only
the "zone-diameter cap … clustering rule" reading fails. The same misreading is
already ledgered on `locations/pistol-bell-bank-300.md` and
`locations/372-245-250.md`.

**The 43 carries the zone — every corpus mention here is of that one mark.** It
is the source's worked example of a bank as a mountain in a desert, and the
numbered mark whose crest depth that source states outright (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`); it
is the first-named member of the June–July below-Clemente search box
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`); it is a named working spot in the daytime swordfish list
(`yMiBtZ7k8-w`); it is the stated heading of an offshore run in the corpus
(`A6DJoXbID4c`); and it is the one numbered bank in the KB that a named closure
moved from one target species to another (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). The **91** and the
**300** appear nowhere but the coordinate library.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three positions.** The 43 sits at **32°39.333'N 117°58.417'W**, the 91 at
**32°36.800'N 117°56.350'W** and the 300 at **32°35.750'N 118°00.500'W**
(cameron). The tightest pair is **43 to 91 at 3.1 nm**; the widest is **43 to
300 at 4.0 nm**, with **91 to 300 at 3.7 nm** — every spacing inside a third of
the **12 nm** cap that is the clustering rule which grouped them. No mark is
more than **2.3 nm** off the centre of the three, so the whole zone is a
single search radius rather than a circuit.

**A bank is named for the fathom depth of its shallowest point**, at six feet to
a fathom — an isolated high spot rising out of otherwise deep water, with places
all over the ocean at the same reading that are not banks
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md#bank-naming--the-fathom-depth-convention),
`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way the **43** tops out at **43 fathoms — about
258 ft**, the **91** at **91 fathoms — about 546 ft**, and the **300** at
**300 fathoms — about 1,800 ft**.

**The 43's crest is stated directly by a source, and it is the shallowest
numbered bank in the Bight grid.** The convention's own source
gives it as "43 fathoms deep" and says **43 fathoms is the highest it comes to**
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`) — no other bare-numbered mark in the
[Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md) grid reads shallower, the next
being the 51 at **51 fathoms** in [51 / 181 / 138](51-181-138.md) (cameron).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms the 91 or the 300 as charted depths.

**Why the 43 holds fish is stated as a bottom fact, not a conditions one.** The
surrounding water is "a desert — there's just nothing around", then a little
mountain comes up under the water; bait congregates around it **for safety**,
the chain reaction occurs, and the big fish arrive. Current, tide and water
temperature all matter, but structure is what they act on (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`;
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). The same segment
names the 43 in a list of banks with "209, 181, 182" and notes that a sounder
does not see fish spread everywhere — it sees them **on the high spots**
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`, [electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`: "structure is what they act
on" is an added mechanism. The transcript states a requirement, not a relationship:
"current tide water temperature all that matters yes but you still got to have
structure" (@00:03:08). The captain never says the other three factors operate
through structure.

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`: the desert /
bait-congregating / chain-reaction sequence is stated of the **209**, not the 43 — "the 209 is
another mountain in the middle of what you would call a desert there's just
nothing around so then all of a sudden here's a little mountain that's going to
have the bait congregate around the mountain for safety and then… the chain
reaction just occurs and then all of a sudden here's the big fish" (@00:02:46).
What the source says of the 43 is the shorter "it's a high spot in the middle of
a giant desert… where the mountain comes up under the water and all the bait
hangs out around the edges of it" (@00:03:50) — no safety motive and no chain
reaction attached to this mark.

**The zone spans 257 fathoms of named crest inside 4.0 nm.** From **258 ft** at
the 43 to **1,800 ft** at the 300 is the widest depth spread of any bank zone
this tight on the ladder, and the numbers read as three separate mountains off
one plain rather than one feature and its flanks. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: relief, high-spot shape, bottom composition or contour orientation at

**Three different banks in the library are called some form of "300", and this

**300 (The Rampart)** as distinct names rather than a collision
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md));
Bell Bank (300) lies **75.7 nm** south in
[The Pistol / Bell Bank (300)](pistol-bell-bank-300.md) and 300 (The Rampart)
**97.8 nm** south, both off Baja (cameron). **No corpus mention of "the 300"

depth in feet. Treat the mark as position-only.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron): **The Corner** at **7.6 nm**

pair for the tightest spacing between any two of the Bight's offshore-bank zones

## How it fishes

**This zone sits on the complex side of the search split, which is what decides
the day's budget.** The below-Clemente complex is worked as one search box — you
do not "check the 43", you plan **hours of looking**, running contour and
glassing between features and treating the named banks as reference points
inside a single hunting zone. The opposite type is the compact spot — the 302,
the 371, the ground off the West End of Catalina — a small definable piece of
structure you idle up to, grade on the meter, and either commit to or leave
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). The 43 is the first
mark named inside the complex, so a trip out here is a half-day commitment to
looking, not a stop.

**The stated search method inside that box is a bank-to-bank sweep with a timing
rule attached.** Run bank to bank looking for **colour changes, temperature
changes and bird life — anything that looks different than it did before** — and
if a piece of water reads fishier than the rest, be back in it on a **late
morning slack tide after 09:30**, which is named as the best shot at finding
fish (`Blh2BA-7Ono`; [water colour](../conditions/water-color.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md),
[tide & slack](../conditions/tide-and-slack.md)). At **4.0 nm** across, this
zone is one leg of that sweep rather than the sweep itself.

**The June–July read pairs the search box with the moon.** Full moons drive the
night bite in that window, and the plan is hours of looking rather than a
spot-check ([bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md),
[June–July](../seasonal/june-july.md), [moon](../conditions/moon.md)).

**If a paddy turns up on the way out, the trip that ran this heading fished it
from off the edge.** You do not have to fish tight against the kelp — stand
roughly **100 yards off**, because the tuna sit outside the piece rather than
directly under it — and keep watch through the full 360° around the boat rather
than fixating on the paddy (`A6DJoXbID4c`;
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md)). The same session's approach on a
travelling school was to read the terns for direction, swing **out and around**
so the boat comes to rest ahead of the school, shut down and let the fish come
into casting range, then match the hatch of the bait they are on
(`A6DJoXbID4c`; [foamer casting](../techniques/foamer-casting.md),
[tuna poppers & stickbaits](../lures/tuna-poppers-and-stickbaits.md)). Bait for
that trip was fresh mackerel loaded off Shelter Island before the run
([making bait](../bait/making-bait.md)).

**The swordfish program here is a contour drift, and the 43's crest is not where
the bait goes.** The preferred set-up on the banks that list names is a **"down
and in" drift along the contour edges** the fish dwell and feed at, fished up to
about **11–13 kt** of wind with **10 lb of weight** before scope becomes a
problem, on a drift of **1 to 2.5 kt** (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`;
[deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md),
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)). The fishery works **150–350 fathoms**
with baits averaging **800–1,100 ft** ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)),
which the 43's **258 ft** crest sits far above — so the drift is set on the
edges off the high spot, not over it. The seminar makes the same point from the
surface: rolling out to a spot like the 43 and finding whales, dolphins and
birds is a good sign even though that life is **a thousand feet above where the
bait will be presented**, because the reason it is there is food (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`).

## Spots

Three charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. All three clustered on
their own coordinates — no member was attached to this zone on distance, and no
pair is more than **4.0 nm** apart:

- **The 43** — the northern mark, the shallowest numbered bank in the Bight grid
  at **43 fathoms**, and the only position in this zone the corpus carries. It
  is the worked example of a bank as a mountain in a desert and the mark whose
  crest a source states outright (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), the first-named member of the
  below-Clemente search box (`Blh2BA-7Ono`), a named daytime swordfish spot
  (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`), the stated heading of the one corpus run out of Shelter
  Island toward this water (`A6DJoXbID4c`), and the only mark in the KB whose
  fishery was moved from rockfish to bluefin by a closure (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`).
- **The 91** — the eastern mark, and the closest position in the zone to the
  next grouping east (**5.9 nm** to The Corner). Coordinates only; the corpus
  never names it.
- **The 300** — the southwestern mark and the deepest name in the zone at
  **300 fathoms**, the only one of the three reading inside the swordfish band.
  Coordinates only; the two other library banks sharing the number are Baja
  marks **75.7 nm** and **97.8 nm** south (cameron).

Charted positions for all three are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither the 91 nor the 300 carries
corpus detail beyond its position, so those spot pages are coordinates plus
their parent zone until sources arrive.
