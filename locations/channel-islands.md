# Channel Islands

**The island group at the north end of the Bight, fished out of Ventura and
Oxnard.** "The Channel Islands" is how the corpus names this water on camera —
a destination and a program rather than a mark — and most of what is stated
about it is scoped to the group, not to one island
([Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)). The trip it describes is a
**squid-and-variety day**: make a tank of squid on the way out, then work the
shallow island high spots for
[white seabass](../species/white-seabass.md),
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) and
[California halibut](../species/california-halibut.md) with
[bass, barracuda and bonito](../species/barracuda.md) filling the hours and
[rockfish and lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md) behind it
(`6-8KfjEg0x8`, `nRIAgz5G_Bc`).

**This page is the group, not a rung.** The islands named inside it carry no
coordinates in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md), so the group mints
no zone and this page hangs off the region the same way
[Anacapa Island](anacapa-island.md) does — a gap the
[region page](socal-bight.md) already flags (cameron). Where a claim is scoped
to one island it is said so below; everything else is group-level by the
source's own framing. [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) and
[San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md) are separate zones offshore and
south, and the [12 Mile Reef](12-mile-reef.md) is the one charted mark built in
the Santa Barbara Channel.

## Getting there

**Ventura and Oxnard are the stated ports.** A Channel Islands day starts "just
outside of Ventura," makes a tank of squid on the way out and runs to the
islands (`6-8KfjEg0x8`); the tackle briefing for the same water opens on what to
carry "if you're going to be fishing out of Ventura or Oxnard ... going out to
the Channel Islands" (`nRIAgz5G_Bc`). Making and holding that squid is in
[making bait](../bait/making-bait.md) and [bait tanks](../bait/bait-tanks.md).

**These are the full-day-accessible islands, and the line class says so.** The
schoolie-grade [surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md) build runs **40 lb
monofilament** top shot for the local, full-day-accessible islands — the
Coronados, the Channel Islands and Catalina — and steps up to **50 lb** for the
overnight-range destinations, San Clemente and Santa Barbara Island, where a
trophy yellowtail is a live possibility at any moment (`LTCFjqtSI8g`). The
halibut program that scales from bay water to here is framed the other way, as
"the outfit for an overnight or two-day trip" — a **30 lb bait-class** boat rod
with 30 lb mono (`a5u8BaYzw8c`,
[California halibut — live-bait drift](../species/california-halibut-live-bait-drift.md)).
Those two framings are not reconciled anywhere in the corpus and are kept side
by side: they turn on the trip being sold, not on the run itself.

**A NW blow hits this end of the region hardest, and it is the first gate.** A
strong NW event rolls surface temperature down **5–8 °F** on the northern
Channel Islands and the LA/Ventura coastal shelf, while south of Newport is less
prone to wind-wave rollover and the Coronados, Catalina and San Clemente held
their water through one such event (`YZtX1MiT0y8`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). Inside the
group the order of what stays fishable is repeatable in the two 2022 reports
that state it: with wind up the northern islands for the weekend after
2022-07-21, Santa Cruz and Anacapa were called "plenty fishable" while Santa
Rosa "might be an issue for private boats" (`Blh2BA-7Ono`), and the April 2022
event put the worst of it on Miguel, Rosa and the front side of Santa Cruz
(`YZtX1MiT0y8`).

**A Santa Ana is the window for the outer end of the group.** Santa Ana events
flatten the northwest outer islands and are the stated trigger to run them for
shallow rockfish and lings in late fall (`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`, `OYOda6T3f-8`). West and
northwest wind blows around Point Conception at night and quits in the morning,
so a Santa Rosa / San Miguel crossing wants **two or more consecutive days** of
no wind or Santa Anas before it is committed to (`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`,
[rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)) — which is why a
**12–24 h wind hindcast** belongs in the conditions pull beside the forward
forecast
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md#wind-history-not-just-wind-forecast-1224-h-hindcast)).

**Private-boat access is the difference between a report and a trip.** On the
2022-08-03 week barracuda were reported here, the zone was reachable only aboard
a boat (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). Read that alongside August's coverage bias: with the
fleet offshore the islands lose reporting, which is missing coverage rather than
a dead island (`CMQkHQMxbXM`, [August](../seasonal/august.md),
[report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)).

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md);
season and depth rules for the bottom targets are in
[rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**Two depth bands carry the group's fishery, and both are shallow.** The bottom
variety program runs the **100–250 ft** island high spots, where a private boat
gets reds, blue bass, bocaccio and lingcod off a single drift over one rock —
island variety explicitly named as a northern thing you do not get in San Diego
(`6-8KfjEg0x8`, [rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)). The one
stop given a number on camera is a rock at roughly **175 ft** (`6-8KfjEg0x8`).
Above that, [white seabass](../species/white-seabass.md) sit along kelp edges,
hard structure and squid nests and beds in **60–100+ ft** at the islands
(`Eo_bA8IWvyU`, `Xr4nURK-Z48`).

**Hard bottom and hard-to-sand seams are the reason fish are there.**
[Ocean whitefish](../species/ocean-whitefish.md) come off exactly those seams on
the same shallow island high spots that produce the rockfish variety bag, and
[sheephead](../species/sheephead.md) come off the same shallow island rock
(`6-8KfjEg0x8`). The general reads — which face of an island is worth running to
is a wind-and-swell decision made per island and per spot, and each mark has its
own preferred conditions — are in [island structure](island-structure.md) and
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md).

**Squid nests are structure here, not just bait.** The leadhead program is
described as fishing squid nests around the Channel Islands, with the head
pinned through the mantle to mimic a spawn (`F-vOTerdulU`,
[leadhead mods & rigging](../rigging/leadhead-mods.md)) — see
[making bait](../bait/making-bait.md) for the squid itself.

## How it fishes

**Current is the gate, and the meter alone will fool you.** A spot with squid
but no current meters fish that do not bite: at the islands current is "just as
important as the bait you're fishing," and the conditions make or break the day
out there (`6-8KfjEg0x8`, [current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)).
Grade the water moving over the structure before grading the marks on the screen
([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**The program is a squid program, fished top to bottom.** Cover the column with
a [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) and a
[sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md) — the sliding sinker described
as the way to fish "every level of the water column" from surface to bottom and
especially good with squid at the Channel Islands, and the pair specifically
called for when marks are scattered (`6-8KfjEg0x8`,
[rockfish deep-dropping](../techniques/rockfish-deep-dropping.md)). The squid
version of the slider runs a small egg sinker straight to an offset octopus
hook, sinker preferably **glow** to match the squid and add a light attractant
on the common night bite (`apyGy3XKlss`).

**Weights are set by current and by how high the fish are riding.** For the
white seabass dropper drop the torpedo runs **6 oz up shallow to 12–16 oz**,
scaled to how fast the drop has to get down to capitalize on a bite
(`nRIAgz5G_Bc`, `wl27BWAWpq0`,
[dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md)); the slider goes to **1/4 oz or
1/2 oz** when the fish are higher in the column (`nRIAgz5G_Bc`,
[white seabass — sliding sinker](../species/white-seabass-sliding-sinker.md)).
On squid nests the leadhead runs **1/8 oz up to 1 oz**, heavier for more current
and lighter for less, cast out and jigged slowly back through the column above
the nest — or hung from the rod holder on a lighter head and left to waft
mid-column (`F-vOTerdulU`). The light-gauge **banana head** covers the same job
at **1/2 oz to 1 oz** on fresh dead or live squid (`PnAnAS6QoG8`, `nRIAgz5G_Bc`,
[leadhead mods & rigging](../rigging/leadhead-mods.md)). A soft-plastic fluke
splits the same way — **3/4 oz** when fish are higher in the column, **2 oz** to
keep it on the bottom or to target halibut (`nRIAgz5G_Bc`,
[soft-plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md)) — and the
open-water version of that cast, off a drifting boat over a sonar mark or a
squid nest, is its own retrieve family
([leadhead swimbait retrieve](../techniques/leadhead-swimbait-retrieve.md),
`hjZ3hgq0Bzg`).

**Hooks are sized to the bait, not to the target.** Owner Flyliner **size 2** is
the first grab for the white seabass / halibut / yellowtail trip out of
Ventura/Oxnard, down to **size 4** on anchovies and up to **1 or 1/0** for bigger
bait; the Owner Aki Twist runs **4/0 to 6/0** for the same trip (`nRIAgz5G_Bc`,
`wl27BWAWpq0`, [hooks](../tackle/hooks.md)).

**When bluefin foam here, it is a small-heavy-lead-bait cast.** On the 2023-08-02
fleet trip the picks were a Daiwa SK jig, a Shimano Colt Sniper and RW Peanuts in
**80 g** and **100 g** — small, heavy profile being the point rather than the
model (`42A8Owhc8fw`, [foamer casting](../techniques/foamer-casting.md)).

**The fall arc starts on this shore and runs away from it.** As the coast cools
and the warm band fragments, the water tracks a repeatable path — backside Santa
Cruz / Rosa → [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) → backside
Catalina → hooking to the 499 and Clemente (`OYOda6T3f-8`, `XLVUhV8DW64`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md), [October](../seasonal/october.md)). Its
first station is these island backsides and every later one is further south, so
the fall pattern moves the fishery off this group rather than onto it.
