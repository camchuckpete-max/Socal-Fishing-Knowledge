# Magdalena Bay Estuary Channel

**[Baja only]** — the middle depth band of
[Magdalena Bay](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md): the **25–30 ft** channel
running past the mangrove edge, between the 2–15 ft root program inside the
mangroves and the 40–50 ft boca at the bay's mouth (`isXJONlpUP4`,
`EcQK5elTPJE`). It is the water a lodge day falls back to when offshore is
blown out, and it fishes as a troll-and-cast program along the mangrove edge
rather than as a mark you sit on (`isXJONlpUP4`). It carries no charted
position.

The whole KB record of this water is **two consecutive episodes of one October
2020 lodge trip** (`isXJONlpUP4`, `OVwqUKim9Pc`), which is why most of what
follows is a flagged gap rather than doctrine.

from a different crew (`isXJONlpUP4`, `OVwqUKim9Pc`). Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

**No coordinates are published here.**

plan has to pull conditions against

## Getting there

**It is a panga day out of the lodge, not a run.** The estuary days on record
were fished from **Mag Bay Lodge** with a local panga guide named on camera as
**Enrique** (`isXJONlpUP4`). Zone-level access — the flight into
[Loreto](loreto.md), the drive across the peninsula, the dock and the panga
fleet — is on
[the zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md#getting-there).

**The channel sits inside the mouth the offshore boats read on their way out.**
An offshore day out of the same lodge begins by idling past the estuary mouth
to look at the wave state and picking the day's bank off what it shows
(`OVwqUKim9Pc`), so the channel is passed rather than run to.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md),
[the zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**One depth and one edge is the whole of what is on record.** The guide called
the channel depth near the mangroves at roughly **25–30 ft**, cued by his
"already it's so shallow" as the boat closed to within **50–100 m** of the
mangrove edge (`isXJONlpUP4`). That puts this water between the two bands the
zone page documents either side of it: the mangrove interior at **12–15 ft**
falling to about **2 ft** in the roots, and the boca at **40–50 ft**
(`EcQK5elTPJE`).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the **50–100 m** figure is not a closing
distance. The captions run it the other way — "hit it past the channel put it
close to the mangroves / when you move it probably 100 meters 50 meters already
it's so shallow" (`isXJONlpUP4`, @00:05:04) — so it reads as the distance over
which the water shoals, not a range the boat closed to. The **25–30 ft** itself
IS verbatim, and hedged ("how deep enrique is it here is probably 25 30 foot",
@00:04:56), but it is sounded *before* the move toward the mangroves, so "near
the mangroves" — also in the `depth_band` infobox — is the KB's placement. Same
finding already ledgered on [the zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md);
queued in [the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the **12–15 ft** → about **2 ft**
gradient is two unlinked statements roughly 32 minutes apart in the seminar —
"the fishing in 12 to 15 ft of water is blind boggly" (@00:14:07) and "I'm
fishing in 2 ft of water because if you look at the mangrove Roots they're all
oysters" (@00:46:07), the second a chosen casting depth rather than a bottom
reading. `EcQK5elTPJE` states nothing between 2 ft and 40 ft, so it cannot
bracket this channel from below. Already ledgered on
[species/snook.md](../species/snook.md).

**The bottom is fishable, not root-choked.** A jig fished on the bottom and
lifted off it took a snook here (`OVwqUKim9Pc`) — the presentation that hangs
instantly in the root zone a few feet shallower
([bucktail jigs](../lures/bucktail-jigs.md),
[flyline](../techniques/flyline.md)).

## How it fishes

**It is the answer to a blown-out offshore day, not a second choice.** The
crew had fished offshore for striped marlin the day before and "got a little
beat up" by wind and chop, so the lodge switched the plan to the estuary rather
than run out again (`isXJONlpUP4`); the trip's last day made the same swap
(`OVwqUKim9Pc`). See [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md) and the zone's
[four-fisheries weather logic](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md#how-it-fishes).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): "the trip's last day made the same swap"
is not what the second episode shows. No wind, swell or weather reason is given
for that day's call, and the crew SPLIT rather than switched — "some of the
boys are going marlin fishing / we're going in the estuary" (`OVwqUKim9Pc`,
@00:11:29). The blown-out-offshore reason belongs to the first day alone, where
it is verbatim ("we got a little beat up yesterday so we're taking a break
because it's still windy it's still kind of choppy out there", `isXJONlpUP4`,
@00:02:02; that video also says only "marlin", never **striped** marlin). The
doctrine the bullet carries is untouched — it just rests on one day, not two.

**Tide direction moves the fish, it does not switch them off.** The
falling/low tide produced most of the day's fish, and once the tide started
flooding the open-channel bite slowed — but the guide's read is that the fish
move **inside** the mangroves rather than stop, so the response is to work
tight along the edge rather than quit (`isXJONlpUP4`). That corroborates the
zone's ebb-window doctrine from a separate crew
([zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md#how-it-fishes)); see
[tide & slack](../conditions/tide-and-slack.md).

**Work the edge, at the edge.** The boat fishes within **50–100 m** of the
mangroves (`isXJONlpUP4`) — close enough that a hooked fish reaches the roots,
which is where the day's biggest fish was lost (`isXJONlpUP4`). Inside the
estuary a hooked fish "normally is going to [run] straight to the mangroves"
(`isXJONlpUP4`), the inverse of the boca read where a rising fish is how the
crew tell a snook from a grouper
([snook](../species/snook.md#landing--handling)).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): two problems in this bullet. The
**50–100 m** standoff is the shoaling distance, not a range held — see the
Structure flag above. And the quoted run is a cleaned-up caption: the transcript
reads "but that one is running to the mangroves / like there's no normally it's
going to stress to the mango" (`isXJONlpUP4`, @00:08:30), so "straight" is an
unbracketed substitution for "stress" — phonetically sound, but the note marks
only `[run]` as edited. The doctrine is corroborated elsewhere in the same video
("yeah he wants those mangroves dude", @00:13:42).

**Three presentations are on record, and two of them are the same drift.**

- **Troll small lures tight to the mangrove edge** — the prospecting program,
  and what the palometa come on
  ([trolling](../techniques/trolling.md#magdalena-bay-baja--inshore-estuarymangrove-troll-and-cast-windchop-fallback-from-offshore-marlin),
  `isXJONlpUP4`).
  ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): "small" lures. Already ledgered against
  this video on [species/snook.md](../species/snook.md) and
  [the zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md) — the trolled lures are never
  called small on camera, and the only size given (the ¾ oz Kastmaster) was
  cast, not trolled. The trolling itself is verbatim ("no we're just trolling
  these for paulus", @00:09:04; "making another pass down the mangroves",
  @00:08:46), and the palometa/troll link is circumstantial rather than stated
  ("shoved it in neutral and all of a sudden i had one on", @00:07:48).
- **Put the trolling rods down and cast at anything showing on top** — light
  spinning tackle and a **3/4 oz** casting spoon at a surfaced pargo school
  (`isXJONlpUP4`).
  ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): **spinning** is nowhere in the
  transcript. The gear is called only "this light lightweight caster"
  (@00:18:31) and "light tackle" (@00:18:57), and the one reel named in the
  episode is a garbled "the cavalla" (@00:10:43) — a lever-drag conventional if
  it resolves to Cavalla or Calcutta, which would invert the reel class the note
  states. The **¾ oz** and the lure are verbatim, though as a brand rather than
  a category: "threw in a three-quarter ounce / cast master" (@00:18:46);
  "spoon" is never spoken. Matters because reel class is what a day plan
  resolves against [gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md).
- **Fish the bottom of the channel** — a white
  [bucktail jig](../lures/bucktail-jigs.md) popped up off the bottom, on what
  is described only as a light rig (`OVwqUKim9Pc`).
