# Evidence — Point Loma

The observation layer behind [Point Loma](../point-loma.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `Rb5I2ljAqeE` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-05-05, San Clemente Island West End
  tuna zone): the crew framed that season's bluefin push as running from the
  West End "all the way down to... 10 miles off Point Loma," fish reported 50
  to almost 300 lb — Point Loma used as the southern mainland bearing point
  for an offshore range, with no fishing on the zone itself. Unregistered
  channel — medium confidence. Harvested from
  [bluefin tuna](../../species/bluefin-tuna.md).
- `0E9REoTjKrY` (SearcherSportfishing / Steve Clarkson, 2020-03-19, filmed at
  the Fred Hall Show): a 315 lb bluefin credited to a 320 g glow flat-fall
  class jig, the catch placed 59 mi off Point Loma and dated on/around January
  2020 — again the landmark as an offshore distance reference, not a spot in
  this zone. Unregistered channel — medium confidence. Harvested from
  [knife jigs](../../lures/knife-jigs.md) and the
  [Searcher big-tuna rig ladder](../../tackle/searcher-big-tuna-rig-ladder.md).

## Structure & bathymetry

- `xI9tPJFXbUM` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, 2019-08-30, SoCal
  inshore): Point Loma named alongside San Onofre and La Jolla as the stand-in
  "the kelp bed" when teaching inshore fishing — a mention of the zone as a
  type example, with no Point Loma-specific structure detail. Registered
  voice.
- `Kf5wk_TFgTc` (BDOutdoors / Erik Landesfeind, Bight report, 2025-02-14):
  sewer and outfall pipes described as running all up and down the SoCal coast
  and as the winter deep-structure option, and as *larger* structure as
  opposed to an isolated wreck or small reef. Not scoped to the Point Loma
  Pipe on camera — the pipe is a charted spot in this zone (cameron).
  Registered voice. Harvested from
  [February–March](../../seasonal/february-march.md).
- `XyKF34C5iB4` (Time On The Water, saltwater bass seminar panel, 2025-04-19):
  the Zuniga Jetty (San Diego) named on camera in the panel's list of SoCal
  walls worth fishing, beside the Long Beach federal breakwater, the Seal
  Beach and Surfside walls, Marina del Rey and Oceanside — a name in a list,
  with no description of this wall's own structure. The panel's stated
  philosophy is that a wall does not need a famous name to hold fish.
  Unregistered channel — medium confidence. Harvested from
  [break walls, jetties and riprap](../breakwalls-jetties-riprap.md).

## What's there

- `OpcKQPA3vAI` (BDOutdoors / Erik Landesfeind, "Finding Coastal and Island
  Halibut," Academy Ep. 15, 2024-02-09): the San Diego Bay shipping-lane and
  breakwater-mouth walls named as a major "thumb over the hose" among the
  bay-and-harbor halibut cases, fished on channel edges on a strong tide. The
  zone's charted structure at that entrance is the Zuniga Jetty (cameron).
  Registered voice. Harvested from
  [California halibut](../../species/california-halibut.md).
- `VpW91AKOFVQ` (Capt. Scotty, Brothers Sportfishing, via Roman Castro,
  2020-01-28): the smaller mainland-coast schools from Del Mar down to
  Imperial Beach run markedly more finicky on the surface iron than a big
  Coronado Islands school on the same gear, especially on bigger-grade fish.
  Point Loma is not named on camera; the zone's charted latitudes fall inside
  that stretch (cameron). Registered voice. Harvested from
  [surface iron](../../techniques/surface-iron.md).

## How it fishes

- `kzD0kSnnVPw` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, 2019-04-07): at a
  named kelp bed — Point Loma listed with San Onofre, La Jolla, San Clemente
  Island and Catalina — current direction picks the corner: downhill current →
  the northwest corner, uphill → the southeast corner, with the same logic
  carried to rock structure. Registered voice, single mention of this framing.
  Harvested from
  [current structure](../../conditions/current-structure.md).
