# Evidence — 289 / 284

Provenance for [289 / 284](../289-284.md). **Every observation below names the
289; none names the 284.** The four Bight reports fall in consecutive months of
2022 — 2022-07-21, 2022-08-03, 2022-09-14 and 2022-10-12 — and each of them
places the 289 inside a larger unit rather than describing the mark itself.

## Getting there

- `CMQkHQMxbXM` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-04 upload, Bight report of 2022-08-03,
  below-Clemente banks): on the Friday before the report the presenter went as
  deckhand on a charter out of Newport, clearing the harbour about 08:00. Bait was
  hard to catch — sardines, Spanish mackerel and small bonito instead of the
  mackerel wanted in case of marlin or swordfish — and the plan was tuna on lures
  with the tide around 11:00 or noon. Knowing there were fish off the east end of
  Catalina but being too early for them, the boat ran out to Mackerel Bank, saw
  plenty of boats and no sign of actual fish, then ran down to the 289, where "it
  got less fishy looking the further down we went"; it bent back north to the 152
  off Catalina's east end (nothing), up to the Avalon Bank (nothing), then down
  toward the 14 Mile Bank, crossing the end of the shipping lanes where a quick
  moving spot of 60–80 lb bluefin had been seen weeks earlier, and got casts on
  surface lures into small spots of fish that would not bite before wind chop shut
  down spotting. The same report notes most of the overnight boats out of Newport
  and Dana Point were fishing offshore rather than San Clemente Island, that most
  of the private effort was on the banks below Clemente, and that the coming
  weekend was "not gonna be good weather to go out there on your own boat anyway."

## Structure & bathymetry

- `Rf1HKJG-SDg` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2020-12-15, SoCal outer banks): the naming
  convention behind both marks — a bank is an isolated high spot rising out of
  otherwise deep water, named for the fathom depth of its shallowest point at six
  feet to a fathom, with places all over the ocean reading the same number without
  being banks. Neither the 289 nor the 284 is named in this source; it is the rule
  their names are read against.

## What's there

- `Blh2BA-7Ono` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-21, below San Clemente): bluefin below San
  Clemente were not getting coverage, so the area was framed as one search — "the
  43, the 181, 182, 289, the entire Clemente Ridge, all the way up to Mackerel
  Bank" treated as one section of water, roughly 50–60 square miles to look at,
  explicitly contrasted with running to the 302, the 371 or the stuff off the West
  End of Catalina, and with the instruction to expect to spend a lot of time
  driving around rather than driving to a spot.
- `5to3Q5P7w90` (BDOutdoors, 2022-09-15 upload, Bight report of 2022-09-14, SoCal
  Bight): the September spread of dorado and bluefin ran from the east end of
  Catalina out to the east end of San Clemente and down Clemente Ridge to the 289,
  with more yellowfin the further south you went; a private trip that week ran
  into schools of 30–150 lb bluefin everywhere it went while looking for dorado.
  The 289 is the far end of the stated spread. Carried from the same report at
  [474 / 711](../474-711.md) and [dorado](../../species/dorado.md).
- `XLVUhV8DW64` (BDOutdoors, 2022-10-13 upload, Bight report of 2022-10-12, SoCal
  Bight): with bluefin still on the back side of San Clemente and yellowfin on the
  front side "all the way into Newport Harbor", the better shot was framed as
  getting out to the outer banks and ridges, with Mackerel Bank and the 289 named
  in that clause. asr-uncertain("macrobank" for Mackerel Bank, and the words
  around the number are garbled) — the bare number 289 is clear in the captions,
  the sentence it sits in is not.

## How it fishes

- `Blh2BA-7Ono` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-21, below San Clemente): the complex-versus-
  compact distinction that governs how this zone is worked — a complex is hours of
  looking across one box, a compact spot (302, 371, west of Catalina) is a
  20–30 minute read. This is the source behind
  [zone lexicon](../zone-lexicon.md); the zone page applies it rather than
  restating it.

Three further sources the zone page cites carry general Bight doctrine rather
than an observation on these marks, and their provenance lives with their home
notes: `HzE4FHHcvTk` (wind-rollover and paddy geography,
[bight geography](../bight-geography.md)), `OYOda6T3f-8` (the fall
warmest-water × cleanest-water read, same note) and `yMiBtZ7k8-w` (structure
plus current edge as the trigger,
[current & structure](../../conditions/current-structure.md)).
