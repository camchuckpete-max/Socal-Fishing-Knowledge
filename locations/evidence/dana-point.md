# Evidence — Dana Point

The observation layer behind [Dana Point](../dana-point.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `ztpj1Xll0-U` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, PCS Show live
  seminar, 2023-03-02, Dana Point): the Dana Point sportboat fleet's own 6:00
  a.m. departure is also when the private-boat crowd converges on the bait
  barge; leaving the dock around 5:00 a.m. (or 4:30) beats the line, 6:00 means
  waiting in it, and 7:00 a.m. is "all gone." Registered voice, single mention.
  Harvested from [bait tanks](../../bait/bait-tanks.md).
- `bM2vUS1B-yQ` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, PCS Show live
  seminar, 2023-03-03): the same rush named as charter, half-day and 3/4-day
  boats all converging by 6:00 a.m. with a line forming by a quarter to six,
  and his own target stated as "at least five o'clock in the morning, if not
  four thirty." Second dated mention by the same voice. Harvested from
  [making bait](../../bait/making-bait.md).
- `7WapaxdtjQg` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, 2022-01-04, Dana
  Point): the Dana Point barge used as the worked example for barge etiquette —
  the barge marks its tie-up side with a cone or arrow (here a cone on the
  ocean side), hail on VHF channel 11 with the barge name twice plus your own,
  never board the barge, ask for the load light, and avoid the 6:00 a.m. rush
  by arriving at 5:00 or 7:00 or loading mid-day. Registered voice. Harvested
  from [making bait](../../bait/making-bait.md).
- `m-M0iwX8DjA` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, 2024 Fred Hall Show
  live seminar, 2024-01-27): his own 21 ft Wellcraft, based in Dana Point, runs
  a 60-gallon bait tank. Registered voice, single mention of the figure.
  Harvested from [bait tanks](../../bait/bait-tanks.md).
- `Rf1HKJG-SDg` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, 2020-12-15): the
  inshore mainland water a mile to a mile-and-a-half off the beach from Dana
  Point through Newport and Marina del Rey out to Catalina is already
  2,000–3,000 ft deep, which is the backdrop against which a bank counts as an
  isolated high spot. Registered voice, single mention. Harvested from
  [zone lexicon](../zone-lexicon.md).
- `NC3-3pJDEgo` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2020-11-18, Avalon / Catalina): during
  an active Santa Ana, a boat caught at Avalon faces a multi-hour beat back to
  the mainland ports — Newport and Dana Point named — fighting swell the whole
  way, because the offshore NE wind pushes water back against the prevailing
  onshore motion and stacks the sea. Harvested from
  [sea state](../../conditions/sea-state.md).
- `HMdrP4-i9MM` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-10-11): an annual Okuma-employee
  kids/family trip aboard a Dana Wharf Sportfishing charter departed Dana Point
  and worked south down the coast to the Camp Pendleton end — Dana Point used
  as the start line, with the fishing done in the neighbouring zone.
  Unregistered channel — medium confidence. Harvested from
  [calico bass](../../species/calico-bass.md).
- `LqW32NSkObY` (Fisher Newb, Fred Hall Show seminar, 2019-03-13 — Tommy Ponce,
  kayak guide, Dana Point home waters): wind direction picks the day's stretch
  of coast before launch, with a windy day pushing the choice south from Dana
  Point so the paddle/pedal home has the wind at your back; guided trips are
  planned around the group's ability to cover the crossing, with an example run
  of roughly 8 miles. Unregistered channel — medium confidence. Harvested from
  [kayak bass fishing](../../techniques/kayak-bass-fishing.md).

## Structure & bathymetry

- `M6U_FVdosr4` (StokedOnFishing, 2013-07-04, Dana Point kelp bed, Sun Fun out
  of Dana Wharf Sportfishing — "Dana Wharf Kids Club Sunday's," a recurring
  Sunday second-half-day trip): the bed was described on camera as carrying "a
  little current" running through it, and the boat anchored rather than
  drifting. Unregistered channel — medium confidence. Harvested from
  [calico bass](../../species/calico-bass.md).
- `LqW32NSkObY` (Fisher Newb / Tommy Ponce, 2019-03-13): the depths most
  commonly worked in his Dana Point water are 0–8 ft and 20–100 ft,
  occasionally a little past 100 ft; he is skeptical of a cited FishBase figure
  of calico to ~275 ft, saying he has not personally seen them that deep.
  Unregistered channel — medium confidence. Harvested from
  [calico bass](../../species/calico-bass.md).

## What's there

- `M6U_FVdosr4` (StokedOnFishing, 2013-07-04, Dana Point kelp): that morning's
  first trip tallied 5 sand bass and 3 calico bass, every one caught on a strip
  of squid, which is why crew called squid over a sardine strip for the
  afternoon; mid-session "barracuda are coming in," and the day's wrap named
  "some short barracuda flopping around in the kelp" plus bass, with the fish
  "actually biting the smaller sardines today, which was a surprise." The word
  "short" is the only trace of a legal minimum size in the source; no release
  and no gauge is stated on camera. Unregistered channel — medium confidence.
  Harvested from [barracuda](../../species/barracuda.md) and
  [calico bass](../../species/calico-bass.md).
  ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the place label on this line is not
  on camera. The transcript names the Dana Point kelp bed only for the
  **afternoon** second-half-day trip ("We have a little current Dana Point
  right here in the kelp bed", @00:01:47); the 5-and-3 tally is "this morning"
  (@00:01:15) with no place attached. 2013-07-04 is the upload date — no date
  is spoken. Same finding as the parent note's calico and sand bass rows.
- `poqjnb1r1zk` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2019-07-02, Dana Point): the trip was
  called on the strength of "a lot of Bonita around right now." The presenter
  is not named on camera; the channel's registered voice is `dave-hansen`, so
  attribution is likely but unconfirmed for this clip. Harvested from
  [bonito](../../species/bonito.md).
- `ouBrIdO7d4k` (SD Fish and Sips, 2025-03-08, booth interview at the 2025
  Pacific Coast Sportfishing Show): a SoCal charter captain, not confidently
  named, credits his porpoise-associated yellowfin trolling program to friends
  fishing cheap King Buster lures around Dana Point roughly 10–12 years prior
  (circa the mid-2010s) and getting bit on yellowfin — the origin story he then
  rebuilt with a tungsten 1/2 oz head. Unregistered channel/speaker, single
  mention — medium confidence. Harvested from
  [tuna feathers and skirts](../../lures/tuna-feathers-and-skirts.md) and
  [trolling](../../techniques/trolling.md).
- `HzE4FHHcvTk` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-17, Bight): the best paddy fishing ran
  south of San Clemente Island or Catalina and along the beach below Dana Point
  toward Oceanside; further north the paddies were fewer and held less, and
  paddies close to a dock and heavy traffic were the least willing. A read on
  the open water offshore of this strip, not on its coastal spots. Registered
  voice. Harvested from [kelp paddies](../../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

## How it fishes

- `poqjnb1r1zk` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2019-07-02, Dana Point): a 5:45 a.m.
  skiff start; "a pretty slow morning" fishing south of Dana Point Harbor, then
  working north of the harbour, with small feathers rigged as a short daisy
  chain trolled at roughly 5.5 kt and a small sardine-profile hard bait
  (Rapala-class diver) cast and twitched behind the boat, including while still
  trolling. Harvested from [bonito](../../species/bonito.md).
- `LqW32NSkObY` (Fisher Newb / Tommy Ponce, 2019-03-13): a personal-observation
  calico temperature preference of roughly 65–68°F, with fish going more
  lethargic and dropping to cooler water past about 70°F though still willing
  to hit a top-water bait; rain runoff named as the local driver of dirty water
  in these home waters, after which bass hunker on the bottom and largely go
  off the bite, while bright, sunny, clear days warm the surface and open a
  top-water window; murkier water calls for more flash and brighter colour,
  with a 1 oz+ spinnerbait-class blade cited. Spots are relocated by lining up
  two shore landmarks at a fixed offset rather than on electronics, and a piece
  of kelp is used as an improvised anchor.
  ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the offset runs off a **single** mark
  — "I'll use uh buoys. Just I know that 100 yd south of that buoy, I know
  there's a rock pile over there" (@00:08:26) — with the lifeguard station and
  tower listed as alternative landmark types, not a second leg of a bearing;
  and he separately says "I always have a fish finder on there" (@00:22:45).
  Same finding as the parent note. Unregistered channel — medium
  confidence. Harvested from [calico bass](../../species/calico-bass.md),
  [water color](../../conditions/water-color.md) and
  [kayak bass fishing](../../techniques/kayak-bass-fishing.md).
