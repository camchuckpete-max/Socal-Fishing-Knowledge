# Evidence — Gonzaga Bay

Trip reports and per-source provenance behind
[Gonzaga Bay](../gonzaga-bay.md). Two records: a trailer-boat, beach-camp trip
filmed across two StokedOnFishing episodes, and the last morning of a San
Felipe mothership trip. Observations, not doctrine.

## Getting there

- `_Wb4z4ammoM` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-10-29, Gonzaga Bay; "Fishing Cabrilla
  and Barred Snapper at Gonzaga Bay, Mexico | Part 1"): the drive called on
  camera as about 3 h to the border, 2.5 h Mexicali to San Felipe and 2 h more
  to the destination, with a military checkpoint 30 minutes short of San
  Felipe, a fuel-and-tacos stop there to fill the gas cans, Puertecitos about
  60 miles further and Gonzaga about 40 miles beyond that; the trip is called
  "Baja 2014" on camera while the episode uploads 2015-10-29.
  asr-uncertain(Puertecitos rendered "PTO cedos"); sponsor-heavy (Simrad,
  Okuma); unregistered channel.
- `_Wb4z4ammoM`: the crew tow their own boats down and camp on the beach at a
  campo, picking a cabaña before fishing, with Papa Fernández and a second camp
  offering a hotel, bar, Wi-Fi and AC as the non-camping option; Isla Willard
  named from the road. asr-uncertain(second camp rendered "Alano's").
- `_Wb4z4ammoM`: trip tips given to camera — travel a first time with someone
  experienced, because the value is in knowing the right turns, safe camping
  spots and which launch ramps are accessible at high and low tides; the roads
  to Gonzaga Bay called "fantastic"; carry plenty of water for washing gear and
  for the boat.
- `A6s-A1NARuA` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-10-28, Gonzaga Bay; part 2): a
  lower-than-normal tide sent the crew to check a second launch spot, then a
  small ramp took a couple of tries to get a trailer boat in — "it's just a
  little tricky… once you're in, you're in," called "standard procedure" for
  Mexico. unregistered channel.
- `ts9rJgxjmSg` (Dirty Hookers, San Felipe mothership, "Ep. 8"): the boat
  relocating a full day's run to reach Gonzaga Bay for the next morning,
  planned as a half day of "Pargo, grouper, possible more cabrilla" with the
  anglers choosing the split; the same angler's trip-to-date cabrilla count was
  about six over 10 lb with a 14.5 lb best. unregistered channel.
- `P36VGPPf120` (Strictly Irons, 2026-08-06): Gonzaga as the drive's midpoint
  and a stop "everybody basically stops at" for the scenic view and a restroom
  break, 2 h to 2 h 15 short of Bahía de los Ángeles, with no cell service from
  the desert through Gonzaga.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `_Wb4z4ammoM`: the pitch for the place is local reefs and offshore islands
  with "not another fishing boat around," fished by a group that had been
  coming for eight years; day one ran north to islands visible from the road,
  fished in 10–12 ft, which the crew called very hard fishing and expected to
  cost tackle all day. asr-uncertain(the island named "St Louise Island";
  outer points rendered "Puna finel" and "Puna buo").
- `A6s-A1NARuA`: the country introduced as "the enchanted islands in the Sea of
  Cortez," fished inshore for calico bass, pargo and grouper from trailered
  center-consoles; a 20-mile run from the launch put the crew on ground they
  had never fished — "20 miles, so this is all new territory… and it looks
  excellent."
- `A6s-A1NARuA`: a worked stretch of shoreline read as granite with bait
  everywhere along it, the fish coming more toward the outer edges.
- `0dNfsle54Wo` (Dirty Hookers, upload 2023-06-23, San Felipe mothership,
  "Ep. 9: Chasing Foam"): hard bottom under the deep drop — a hooked grouper
  reached structure instantly — and a mid-morning stop called "kind of a boiler
  spot" with sierra and mackerel bait on it. unregistered channel.

## What's there

- `0dNfsle54Wo`: the trip's last morning at Gonzaga Bay opened deep-dropping
  for pargo, grouper and cabrilla with sierra already showing beside the boat;
  a golden grouper rocked the angler instantly and broke 130 lb after he left
  it in free spool until he felt it kick and got three turns on the handle; a
  live yellowtail went down as bait; a sea lion worked the stop.
- `0dNfsle54Wo`: about 30 cabrilla foaming on the surface "like they were a
  school of carp," every fish over 5 lb, mostly 5–10 with some 15s and one the
  angler put at 20 with its back out of the water; first bite a 7–8 lb fish
  that launched clear of the water on a jerkbait; boobies crashing sierra
  schools marked the spots; sierra in the same water "big ones, like six
  pounders."
- `0dNfsle54Wo`: two more foam stops at 10:20 produced the trip's best fish —
  17 lb on a spring scale, the first over the 15 lb mark for the boat — taken
  in "deep, deep water, wide open, away from the rocks," called perfect
  conditions. This is the day whose grade [cabrilla](../../species/cabrilla.md)
  currently files under Bahía de los Ángeles; the video names Gonzaga Bay.
- `_Wb4z4ammoM`: day one on the island ground produced sierra (one bit through
  a connection), a triggerfish the crew called huge, cabrilla, a small
  yellowtail with a bigger one seen following, grouper wolf-packing behind a
  lure five or six at a time, and a "toad" barred pargo — the biggest the boat
  had seen — that took line against a buttoned-down drag and broke a hook; a
  sea turtle and a whale shark came to the boat.
- `A6s-A1NARuA`: a roosterfish raising its fins on a mullet in boils on a point
  at first light, the first the crew had seen in this bay, which never bit;
  a wide-open "Mexican sea bass" bite fished to doubles and released; Humboldt
  squid moving in within seconds of that bite ending and eating anything on
  light tackle; a second whale shark cruising, which the presenter swam with.
  asr-uncertain(the roosterfish weight call rendered "five thirty pounds").

## How it fishes

- `_Wb4z4ammoM`: a wire leader on 65 lb spectra rigged for "the old rocks and
  toothy critters," described as "real similar to Calico fishing right… except
  you're using wire," on the stated reasoning that these fish run bigger than
  Cal bass, to 15+ lb; no wire gauge or leader length given. Lures that got bit
  on the island ground were a homemade Alabama rig, a chrome jig and a surface
  iron; the search was pelicans, boobies, cormorants and gulls working bait
  just off the beach, with yellowtail expected wherever the birds were.
- `A6s-A1NARuA`: the gear rundown to camera — 7–8 ft heavy and extra-heavy
  rods, 6:1 reels for fast retrieve, 65 lb spectra mandatory minimum with most
  of the crew on 80 lb to a 3–4 ft 80 lb mono and/or fluorocarbon top shot, a
  6 in suspending jerkbait in smelt and a solid 7X surface iron in mint, worked
  near and far around the rocky points and edges.
- `A6s-A1NARuA`: the deep step called out on camera — "put a little bit deeper,
  starting like 80, 90 feet of water, dropping down the biggest baits you got,
  wire leader, buttons drags," fished openly expecting break-offs; grouper
  wolf-packing on the points, "just like calicoes," a big one following out of
  a shadow with the sun on the rock.
- `0dNfsle54Wo`: the half day's shape — deep drop first, a change of scenery at
  about 08:40 onto a couple of sierra spots, then roughly three hours running
  bird schools for foaming cabrilla before the noon cutoff; a surface iron
  thrown into a foam stop was refused, "they wanted the jerk bait on that
  stop"; sierra taken on a Colt Sniper.
