# Evidence — US waters

The observation layer behind [US waters](../us-waters.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Papers you need

- `LsFMBCa9DOQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2025-02-05, San Diego landing, Ranger 85
  3-day): crew collects California fishing licences from anglers as they come
  down the gangway; an angler without one is told on camera he is not getting
  on the boat.
- `E9YtH56Dngo` (SearcherSportfishing, 2020-12-17, San Diego): a December
  bluefin-tackle rundown closes by telling viewers to stock up on a fishing
  licence for the coming season — the licence framed as a Christmas present.
- `Qs9oEsh3b_w` (Roman Castro, 2020-10-17, San Diego, hoop-net build): GO ID
  located on the licence one line below the barcode, a 10-digit number, written
  on the buoy in marker or on securely-attached tape.
- `b19_AJjYCok` (BDOutdoors, 2019-11-13, SoCal, spiny-lobster tips): buoys must
  carry name and GO ID, with the GO ID found on the fishing permit.

## On the water

- `EGKesj7V64M` (Roman Castro, 2019-06-18, San Diego bays): tip six of a
  spotted-bay-bass list is knowing the zones and rules of the area — hook
  counts, rod counts, takeable species, seasonal species and bag limits — with
  the framing that a game warden issues the ticket regardless of whether the
  angler knew.
- `iAr6sbRC384` (Fisher Newb seminar, 2020-03-16, Fred Hall Show): the
  presenter recounts private boats being boarded and cited over the rockfish
  closure line, and recommends loading the boundary into a chart app (he names
  Navionics) so position rather than depth decides legality. Depth figures from
  the same source are logged with the rockfish note; portions of the
  auto-captions around this passage are heavily garbled (asr-uncertain), so
  only the boarding/boundary-tool point is carried here.
- `9hEa3sGTh40` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2019-08-20): Ch 16 is for hailing and
  distress; the stated doctrine is not to treat monitoring it as a duty — that
  is the Coast Guard's or vessel assist's job — except for an emergency
  visible in front of you.
- `shZCjX2-fkI` (SearcherSportfishing, 2021-01-15, San Diego): the boat's
  annual maintenance period is described as the regulatory period — Coast
  Guard, American Bureau of Shipping, FCC, fire system. Corroborated by
  `ACL2BD6gbkE` (2010-06-01), which describes the same annual Coast Guard and
  American Bureau of Shipping inspection at haul-out.
