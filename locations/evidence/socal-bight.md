# Evidence — Southern California Bight

The observation layer behind
[Southern California Bight](../socal-bight.md). One line per observation.
Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## The fishery

- `HnqiE05vdXs` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-31, Catalina): after a spot of bluefin
  came up an hour and a half before the tide, the boat stayed on the
  500-fathom curve about three miles off the island instead of running on to
  Clemente or Mackerel Bank, and raised a second spot five minutes later for
  two smaller fish; kelps lined up along the curve were noted as usually
  empty.
- `YZtX1MiT0y8` (BDOutdoors, 2022-04-21, Bight-wide): after the NW blow, San
  Miguel, Santa Rosa and the front side of Santa Cruz were in the low 50s and
  a beach reading of 56 °F was called seven or eight degrees off pre-wind,
  while backside Santa Cruz, Clemente, Catalina and the Coronados/tuna
  grounds held; south of Newport read fine under patchy cloud.
- `Rf1HKJG-SDg` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2020-12-15, SoCal banks): the 43 is
  described as the rockfish bank the fleet ran to from the 1940s through the
  1990s, and as now drawing boats for giant bluefin since the rockfish area
  closed.

## Season shape

- `Blh2BA-7Ono` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-21, below Clemente): fish below Clemente
  were getting little coverage, four-pack and charter boats were running that
  direction, and finding a 23-foot patch in the 60-square-mile area was named
  as the problem — run bank to bank on color changes, temperature changes and
  bird life, and try to be on it at a late-morning slack after 09:30.
- `DnSqw4r7A1s` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-14, Bight): the week's bite was called
  100 % related to the buck moon, with boats taking two-day limits in one day
  and the stated expectation that it returns to normal once the moon passes.
- `-JZpyWLdKlk` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-11, northern Channel Islands): fishing
  was slow earlier in the week, then the Mirage took 46 white seabass coming
  into the 11 August full moon.
- `5to3Q5P7w90` (BDOutdoors, 2022-09-14, Bight): the volume of fish sat in the
  square drawn between Santa Barbara Island, San Nicolas, San Clemente and
  Catalina, where "a lot of the bigger ones are"; marlin were coming by the
  Boot on a tournament weekend.
- `OYOda6T3f-8` (BDOutdoors, 2022-10-20, Bight): with the warm water condensing
  down to nothing, the week's advice was to run on the temp chart and
  chlorophyll, and a Santa Ana window was named as the time to run to Santa
  Rosa or San Miguel for shallow-water rockfish, with a sea-bass shot on the
  squid up that way. Corroborated by `XLVUhV8DW64` (2022-10-12), which tells
  viewers to make educated guesses off the chart, SST, chlorophyll and
  structure against where fish have been historically.
- `Z3rZqy2Pi8E` (BDOutdoors, 2022-11-23, northern Channel Islands): a Santa
  Ana weekend laid the water down and boats out of Santa Barbara landing ran
  to Santa Rosa and San Miguel for limits of reds and lings, with a yellowtail
  caught and released on an artificial; the reporter's own plan was to trailer
  up and pick Rosa or Miguel on sea conditions for shallow-water rockfishing.

## Zones

- `YZtX1MiT0y8` (BDOutdoors, 2022-04-21, San Clemente Island back side, east
  end): the Freedom got nice yellowtail fishing squid — high teens to high
  30s, a 20–25 lb average — with anglers losing fish for being under-geared;
  the bite dried up after the first hit and was read as a squid-zone bite that
  had moved.
- `5p6gu14ZC4w` (BDOutdoors, 2022-10-26, Catalina / Santa Barbara Island):
  Farnsworth Bank named as the fall yo-yo call where only yellowtail and
  bonito may be kept; squid was around at several islands and on the Long
  Beach barge, and the Freelance had about 40 yellowtail at Catalina.
- `HzE4FHHcvTk` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-17, Bight): paddy fishing ran up to San
  Clemente Island and the inside of Catalina, but further up the paddies were
  fewer and less holding; the best of it was south of Clemente or Catalina and
  along the beach below Dana Point toward Oceanside, with paddies close to a
  dock likely already driven over.

## Access & range

- `ztpj1Xll0-U` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2023-03-02, Dana Point): the seminar's
  worked example puts the angler at the bait barge at 05:00 rather than 06:00
  because the sister sportboat schedule has every Dana Point boat leaving at
  06:00; 04:30 also works and 07:00 means the bait is gone.
- `7WapaxdtjQg` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-01-04, Dana Point): the same
  doctrine demonstrated at the barge — do not arrive at 06:00, get there at
  05:00 and you never see a line, because by 06:00 the barge is three-quarters
  full of boats.
- `jU7qi40WTzQ` (SearcherSportfishing, 2010-06-01, San Diego): the boat's own
  pitch for 3-to-7-day trips — a one-day boards at 22:00, travels overnight,
  fishes half a day and is back at the dock the next evening; a 3-day gives
  two dawn-to-dusk days and 200 miles of range if needed; a 7-day reaches the
  rocks.
