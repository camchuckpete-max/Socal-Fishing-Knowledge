# Evidence — Imperial Beach

The observation layer behind [Imperial Beach](../imperial-beach.md). One line
per observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `Ec2GaNpiOHI` (Strictly Irons / Dan Wade Q&A, 2019-03-19): a boyhood
  yellowtail story set aboard a sportboat named the *City of Imperial Beach* —
  fish up and showing but refusing the jig until an older angler demonstrated
  a speed-up-slow-down "little perky jerky" retrieve, which is where Wade
  dates his own jig cadence from. The only corpus trace of boats sailing from
  this stretch, and it is a boat name rather than a stated port —
  `asr-uncertain`: the captions render the departure point twice
  ("out of I out of the pyramid Oh Beach") and neither rendering is clean.
  Registered voice (`dan-wade`). Harvested from
  [surface iron](../../techniques/surface-iron.md).

## Structure & bathymetry

- `kwMIgkCtFUE` (BDOutdoors / SoCal Bight Fishing Academy Ep. 5, "Breaking
  Down Bass Structure," 2023-01-27): Point Loma described as a much bigger
  piece of structure than anglers imagine — underwater rock reaching a good
  distance off the visible kelp and running almost down to the Coronados —
  with the aside that "there's also more structure down off Imperial Beach,"
  and the conclusion that with that much structure the bass cannot be
  everywhere on it. The zone's four charted marks are not named on camera.
  Registered voice.
- `Kf5wk_TFgTc` (BDOutdoors / Erik Landesfeind, Bight report, 2025-02-14):
  sewer and outfall pipes described as running all up and down the SoCal coast
  and as *larger* structure as opposed to an isolated wreck or small reef,
  with "the Imperial pipe down San Diego" named in the list of larger pipes.
  `asr-uncertain` — "I think it's the buccaneer pipe" follows in the same
  breath, and the Buccaneer Pipe is a separate charted spot in Oceanside /
  North County (cameron), so it is not certain whether the two names attach to
  one pipe. The same asr uncertainty is recorded from the other side in
  [Oceanside / North County evidence](oceanside-north-county.md). Registered
  voice.

## What's there

- `VpW91AKOFVQ` (Capt. Scotty, Brothers Sportfishing, via Roman Castro,
  2020-01-28): the smaller mainland-coast schools from Del Mar down to
  Imperial Beach run markedly more finicky on the surface iron than a big
  Coronado Islands school on the same gear, especially on bigger-grade fish.
  Imperial Beach is named as the south end of that stretch, with no catch or
  spot reported in the zone. Registered voice (`capt-scotty-brothers`).
  Harvested from [surface iron](../../techniques/surface-iron.md).

## How it fishes

- `kwMIgkCtFUE` (BDOutdoors / SoCal Bight Fishing Academy Ep. 5, 2023-01-27):
  the deep-structure search method taught over the Point Loma-to-Imperial
  Beach structure field — work the face turned into the current (the west side
  on a downhill current, where the rocky reef drops to the bottom), look along
  that edge for bait feeding on the upwelling and its zooplankton, treat a
  solid wall of bait as probably holding no bass and a tight, broken,
  inconsistent bait picture as the one to drop on, and cover the ground with
  the fish finder rather than cast by cast because the area is a mile long.
  Registered voice.
- `Kf5wk_TFgTc` (BDOutdoors / Erik Landesfeind, Bight report, 2025-02-14):
  large structure is fished by casting around and moving rather than stopping
  on it; deeper structure is the most reliable cold-season bass bite on the
  coast while kelp and shallow reef shut down; top-hook swimbait and slug
  builds for casting over large structure, plus a crankbait run over suspended
  bait above the pipes. Not scoped to this zone on camera beyond the pipe
  mention. Registered voice.
