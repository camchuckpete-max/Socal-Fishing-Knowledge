# Evidence — International Artificial Reef

The observation layer behind
[International Artificial Reef](../international-artificial-reef.md). One line
per observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

**No corpus source names this reef.** Every line below is program-level
material about SoCal artificial reefs generally, carried here because it is
what the zone page applies; none of it was observed at International Reef A–F
or the missile tower.

## Getting there

- `iAr6sbRC384` (Fisher Newb seminar, 2020-03-16, Fred Hall Show): private
  boats boarded and cited over the rockfish closure line, with the fix stated
  as loading the boundary into a chart app so position rather than depth
  decides legality. Stated for rockfish depth closures, not for the
  international boundary. Harvested from [US waters](../us-waters.md).

## What's there

- `f4qYtHACGyk` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, 2020-11-07, SoCal
  artificial reefs, no individual reef named on camera): reported catches off
  chummed artificial reefs on the anchored-chum program — sargo up to 5–6 lb,
  big white seabass, big sheephead, calico bass, and yellowtail; the frenzy
  makes the catch mixed and unpredictable. Harvested from
  [chunking](../../techniques/chunking.md).
- `bM2vUS1B-yQ` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, PCS Show seminar,
  2023-03-03): stage account of the SoCal artificial-reef system being built
  for sport fishermen in the 1950s–70s on sportfishing money, with the reef
  species mix listed; registered voice, single mention, no reef named.
  Harvested from [chunking](../../techniques/chunking.md).

## How it fishes

- `f4qYtHACGyk` (Dave Hansen): the light-line finesse rig and the stated #1
  mistake — anglers fishing way too heavy on reefs that are shallow, close to
  harbors and fished for years. Harvested from
  [chunking](../../techniques/chunking.md).
- `bM2vUS1B-yQ` (Dave Hansen): the worked anchored-chum example — bucket of
  ground sardines, mackerel or anchovies tied off at the bow, No. 4–No. 6
  hooks. Harvested from [chunking](../../techniques/chunking.md).
