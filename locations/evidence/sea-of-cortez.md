# Evidence — Sea of Cortez

The observation layer behind [Sea of Cortez](../sea-of-cortez.md), a
nine-episode San Felipe mothership series plus a companion tackle-bag video.
One line per observation. Observations never change the note's stated
doctrine. Dates below are upload dates unless a trip date is stated on
camera.

## Getting there

- `aT-fL7vaavY` (2023-04-12, San Felipe mothership, "Ep. 6: Yellowtail Fishing
  the Tony Reyes Mothership"): boat relocated overnight in an 8–9 hour run
  through 12–15 ft rollers to get back to the day-one zone; pangas held on
  deck on arrival, crew fished the bow sport-boat style instead.
- `BSNGeduOHYM` (2023-04-20, San Felipe mothership, "Ep. 7: Wide Open Early
  Morning Bite!"): only three pangas launched on a marginal-weather morning,
  rotating three anglers per boat in two-hour sessions until the wind died.
- `4Dn7yChmhk0` (2023-11-22, San Felipe mothership, "Ep. 6: Wide Open Cabrilla
  Fishing! Bahía de Los Ángeles!"): title and on-camera narration disagree on
  the ground fished — "Bahía de Los Ángeles" in the title, "aboard the Tony
  Reyes out of San Felipe" and "Bay of LA" on camera. A second, differently
  dated "Ep. 6" from the same series as `aT-fL7vaavY`.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `8IRONtdkceM` (2023-03-09, San Felipe mothership, "Ep. 2: Big Shallow
  GROUPER vs BASS RODS!"): cabrilla taken in six feet of water over boiler
  rock; crew reasons the shallow, snaggy structure is why they fish 80 lb.

## What's there

- `8IRONtdkceM` (as above): six or seven cabrilla landed inside an hour on one
  panga.
- `0dNfsle54Wo` (2023-06-23, San Felipe mothership / Gonzaga Bay, "Ep. 9:
  Chasing Foam + UPDATE!"): grade held 12–15 lb with the crew hoping to break
  15; the trip's last morning deep-dropped Gonzaga Bay for pargo and grouper
  with sierra showing beside the boat.
- `4Dn7yChmhk0` (as above): two golden-morph cabrilla landed within the first
  hour.

## How it fishes

- `ts9rJgxjmSg` (2023-04-27, San Felipe mothership, "Ep. 8: Double Grouper on
  Jerk Bait!"): pause-next-to-the-rock jerkbait retrieve draws a reaction
  strike; spare split rings needed mid-session.
- `81YHbZQFzgk` (2023-03-03, San Felipe mothership, "Ep.1 GROUPER on BASS
  GEAR!"): spare split-ring/hook replacement comes up again on this episode;
  trip framed around fishing grouper/cabrilla on bass tackle.
- `ZYAILGyrkRk` (2023-03-13, San Felipe mothership, "Ep. 3: Tony Reyes
  Cabrilla Fishing!"): surface iron worked on cabrilla with the standard
  wind-don't-swing hookset, then was dropped for faster-recovery lures on the
  high-volume bite; a Wax Wing got bit every cast; yellowtail followed lures
  without committing.
