# Evidence — Loreto & Puerto Escondido

The observation layer behind
[Loreto & Puerto Escondido](../loreto.md). One line per observation.
Observations never change the note's stated doctrine. Dates are upload dates
unless a trip date is stated on camera.

All five Loreto sources are trip vlogs from channels that are not in
`sources/source-registry.md`, which caps the zone at `medium` confidence; none
carries doctrine, only what was done and what it produced.

## Getting there

- `ikiXx9sUsoA` (SD Fish and Sips, uploaded 2022-11-30, Loreto / Puerto
  Escondido, November 2022): the trip flew out through CBX, the Cross Border
  Xpress bridge into the Tijuana airport, at the invitation of a host with
  30-plus years of Sea of Cortez trips; the crew lived aboard a 300–308 ft
  British-flagged mothership registered in Georgetown and fished from an 18 ft
  Grady White towed behind it; unregistered channel,
  asr-uncertain(vessel length given as "300 feet, 308").
- `n4nMCLLW3tY` (SD Fish and Sips, trip dated on camera 2023-07-31 and
  2023-08-01, Loreto): day three launched from Puerto Escondido Marina; a
  sardine order was missed at the dock and filled by the bait vendor, Antonio,
  waiting out front of the marina; the shift pin on the outboard sheared at the
  start of a 20-mile run to the north end of Isla Carmen, and the boat was
  paddled and swum back while a marina boat was called in by radio to tow it on
  a custom cleat-to-cleat rope normally used to tow the Grady White behind the
  mothership; unregistered channel.
- `Re56xUHOu6A` (Marquez Fishing, uploaded 2026-08-10, Loreto): a birthday
  party fished from a sandy beach, two boats the first day and four the second,
  with part of the group staying on the beach and local cooks preparing the
  catch there; unregistered channel, part Spanish-language.
- `ikiXx9sUsoA`: "The Baja Catch" — a Baja fishing book — is read on camera as
  the crew's planning reference, and the Honeymoon Bay yellowtail read is
  attributed to it; not a corpus source.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `n4nMCLLW3tY` (SD Fish and Sips, 2023-07-31, Loreto): the day's plan was to
  run further than the day before, stopping at Six Mile Reef on a plotter mark
  on the way out to Isla Monserrat, where local word was "Monserrat north end
  is the best"; the reef had produced a yellowtail on an earlier trip and gave
  up triggerfish, a Pinto bass and a skipjack on this one; the north end of
  Isla Carmen was called "about 20 miles, probably an hour to get there" from
  Puerto Escondido; cabrilla came from 5–10 ft against the island, the crew
  calling the water "super shallow"; unregistered channel.
- `ikiXx9sUsoA` (SD Fish and Sips, November 2022, Isla Carmen): Honeymoon Bay
  named on camera as "pretty sheltered" with "good yellowtail on the corner
  down there"; a boat chasing birds looked down and was in 30 ft; 10 ft was
  called on the sounder with structure visible through the water; a sierra came
  off rock "nice and protected from the wind"; unregistered channel.
- `s5PaKJX3VDo` (Bryan Timm, uploaded 2023-11-10, Loreto): the surface session
  was fished with the boat in rock — "keep it tight, we're in rock";
  unregistered channel.
- `0b0PDLVh-T4` (Pacific Outdoor Sports, September session, uploaded
  2025-10-14, Loreto): a shoreline day worked close enough in that the crew
  noted "you could have surf cast for this fish", with one stop moved deeper;
  unregistered channel, heavily music-scored with sparse narration.

## What's there

- `n4nMCLLW3tY` (SD Fish and Sips, 2023-07-31, Isla Monserrat): a roosterfish
  came on the troll on a Halco in shallow water off the island; cabrilla came
  from 5–10 ft on a Shimano suspending jerkbait, one of them called on camera
  "a different type of cabrilla"; a houndfish chased lures; a skipjack was kept
  for chunk bait; unregistered channel.
- `n4nMCLLW3tY` (SD Fish and Sips, 2023-08-01, Puerto Escondido): the bait
  vendor's dorado report placed fish close to shore at Punta Perico under
  diving birds and around weed, caught roughly 10:20–11:00; the crew chose
  Punta Perico over a ground the locals named, rendered variously "desante" /
  "the Sante", where the report was said not to be holding up; asr-uncertain
  (spot name, distance offshore).
- `ikiXx9sUsoA` (SD Fish and Sips, November 2022, Loreto): yellowtail were the
  stated main target with dorado, cabrilla and sierra as the rest of the list;
  a barracuda came on the troll and was called from the fight — no yellowtail
  head shakes, long profile; a first-ever roosterfish was landed on the
  afternoon troll and released; a large sierra was bled at the gills and made
  into ceviche; another crew's report that morning had sierra on the troll, and
  the local fleet was "doing work on Sierra" in the afternoon chop;
  unregistered channel.
- `s5PaKJX3VDo` (Bryan Timm, uploaded 2023-11-10, Loreto, with Stella June
  Sportfishing): the first bird stop leaving the dock found wide-open surface
  fish; yellowtail were released, cabrilla came out of the rock, and the crew
  rolled up on a foamer they took for yellowtail that turned out to be
  roughly 30 lb roosterfish taken on jigs; unregistered channel.
- `Re56xUHOu6A` (Marquez Fishing, uploaded 2026-08-10, Loreto): a large dorado
  was landed on day one; day two ran to an island and targeted mahi-mahi and
  *pez vela*, with the captain sighting a marlin; a Spanish-language segment
  describes sailfish "up to 300 lb" with fights of "cuatro o cinco horas" and
  says several were seen in the same area at the time — the single-source basis
  for the fact-check row on that claim; cabrilla, a brown *vaqueta*, a grouper
  and white snapper are named separately in the dinner rundown; unregistered
  channel.
- `0b0PDLVh-T4` (Pacific Outdoor Sports, September session, uploaded
  2025-10-14, Loreto): the shoreline program produced snapper — "very good
  snapper" — plus a red fish called "rojo" and a small tuna, framed as a
  species slam; unregistered channel.
- `P36VGPPf120` (Strictly Irons, 2026-08-06, Bahía de los Ángeles trip
  planning): roosterfish at Bahía de los Ángeles are described as migratory and
  a matter of luck, and more reliable further south in the Sea of Cortez — the
  contrast this zone's lead rests on; cited here for that comparison only, the
  rest of the video sits in the
  [Bahía de los Ángeles evidence file](bahia-de-los-angeles.md).

## How it fishes

- `n4nMCLLW3tY` (SD Fish and Sips, 2023-07-31 and 2023-08-01, Loreto): the
  troll spread ran feathers — a green-and-yellow "candy bar" and a
  pink-and-blue — cedar plugs and Halco Laser Pros, with UV-finish lures called
  out as the preference and the same candy-bar pattern remembered from Gonzaga
  Bay in March; the crew broke off the troll to chase working birds and dropped
  a Megabait through them; the bait vendor's sardine rig was a small-to-medium
  hook through the nose and eyes so the bait lives, fished with the weight
  underneath rather than the bait on top; a flylined sardine on a circle hook
  was left out while the disabled boat drifted, and the trollers were left out
  during the tow home; unregistered channel.
- `s5PaKJX3VDo` (Bryan Timm, uploaded 2023-11-10, Loreto): surface fish were
  taken on a spook, a Pickle Kick swimbait, surface iron and bass gear, with the
  retrieve instruction "burn that thing faster… you got to burn it, can't let
  them look at it" and "20 followers on every cast"; a "300" jig is credited on
  camera with several fish (asr-uncertain: the jig is never identified beyond
  the number); unregistered channel.
- `ikiXx9sUsoA` (SD Fish and Sips, November 2022, Isla Carmen): a roosterfish
  was hooked on a drag left tight from the previous sierra and the angler had
  to back it off during the fight; the sunset troll was called off as too dark
  and the boat ran north for shelter and a crossing; the afternoon session
  fished the same area as the morning; unregistered channel.
- `Re56xUHOu6A` (Marquez Fishing, uploaded 2026-08-10, Loreto): the panga
  trolled *plumas* — large squid-skirt lures that work right at the surface and
  push water as they swim — and switched to live bait the moment the captain
  sighted a marlin; the fight coaching was pump-and-reel with constant tension;
  the charter fished 2 hours; unregistered channel.
- `0b0PDLVh-T4` (Pacific Outdoor Sports, September session, uploaded
  2025-10-14, Loreto): the shoreline day included trolling from the small boat
  alongside the cast program; unregistered channel.
