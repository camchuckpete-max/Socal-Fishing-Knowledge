# Evidence — Cortez South

The observation layer behind [Cortez South](../cortez-south.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## The fishery

- `mG8ZZLFGlT8` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2016-12-22, Buena Vista Beach Resort,
  East Cape; "Stoked On Hotel Buena Vista Beach Resort, Part 3," the resort's
  first annual Stoked On Fishing Fiesta): an estimated 400–500 lb blue marlin
  hooked ~5 mi offshore, lost, re-hooked on a second pass, lost again, and
  connected on a third pass ~40 minutes later — landed on a captain-plus-angler
  relay, the captain bringing it to leader four times in roughly the first
  hour, ~1.5 hours combined fight time; the tournament weigh-in ran 37 lb,
  39.4 lb and a fish corrected on stage from 41 lb to 46 lb; one boat recapped
  five tuna, another four; a young angler landed his first striped marlin;
  unregistered channel, asr-uncertain(second species in the day recap, heard
  as "Grotto").
- `tcso7Lpm_Xs` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2018-03-09, Buena Vista Beach Resort,
  East Cape): jigs in the water ~15 minutes before the first hookup; one
  morning produced tuna, roughly 15–16 dorado (most released), roughly 15–20
  skipjack and an unconfirmed marlin; a second morning's spread produced
  multiple wahoo; the school-holding tactic stated as leaving a hooked dorado
  in the water while a second bait goes out; the resort framed on camera as
  offering offshore trolling and inshore/beach fishing out of the same trip;
  unregistered channel, asr-uncertain(wahoo count, heard as "free wahoo").
- `6j7V34GYzzw` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2018-03-12, Buena Vista Beach Resort,
  East Cape; "Part 2"): a long free-spooled run on the offshore troll landed
  and released with a circle hook credited on camera for the hookup, species
  never named — logged as an unconfirmed gamefish; the promised rooster session
  ran as a panga slow-troll of a pink skirt with repeated strikes and several
  fish released, plus an African pompano identified boatside; unregistered
  channel, asr-uncertain(grounds name heard as "Kabul Eidos", read as Cabo
  Pulmo).
- `84XPJAeH0Rw` (StokedOnFishing, 2019-02-10, East Cape; Buena Vista Beach
  Resort trip): a jack crevalle came in a mixed morning catch alongside four
  tuna; a hand-fought tuna was coached through two more boatside circles with
  the cue for the gaff being no more line going out; the day's tuna recapped as
  mostly 60s–70s lb; a detour of "a couple of minutes" to cast at a small patch
  of surface activity, rather than continuing to a pack of working boats, paid
  off; unregistered channel.
- `GptrotE0x5M` (StokedOnFishing, presenter Shay, 2019-02-13, East Cape; Buena
  Vista Beach Resort trip): the day's points-race weigh-in recorded yellowfin
  at 72 lb, 63 lb and 63 lb; a fish fought on a 30 lb mono top shot over
  spectra with both thumbs on the braid, drag bumped up only late; tuna fed on
  chummed squid no more than 50 ft down, taken on 40 lb and circle hooks; an
  estimated 60 lb fish broke the gaff boatside, went back in, and was landed in
  a 40-minute fight with the lever drag pushed down and the spool thumbed,
  credited on camera to the circle hook; drag run at 30 lb, the rod called "a
  little light" for the 60–80 lb grade; unregistered channel, sponsor-heavy
  (Simrad, Okuma).
- `_c6UI3lGBVg` (StokedOnFishing, 2019-02-18, East Cape; final day of the same
  Fiesta trip): fishing less than 2 mi off shore in 52 ft of water, with an
  on-camera interview voice calling the tuna bite "consistent all year round...
  along the coast" and the fish moving between locations through the year; the
  day's grade ran 50–70 lb class; unregistered channel, attribution of the
  year-round claim unresolved (voice not diarized).
- `BQ2U1PqxWi8` (StokedOnFishing, presenter Shay, upload 2020-04-01, East Cape;
  the resort's fifth annual Fiesta): a marlin released on camera on the trip's
  first day, with an earlier on-camera claim of five already aboard whose
  species split is not stated; unregistered channel, asr-uncertain.
- `APsnsunT4gM` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2020-04-08, East Cape; fifth annual
  Fiesta): the first day's troll produced "solid grade A" yellowfin plus wahoo,
  including a personal-best wahoo on a circle hook described as the resort
  fleet's first wahoo of the season; one marlin boated and a second spooled the
  angler; a dedicated bait day was called because the offshore bait supply had
  "been kind of poor lately", and a ~20 lb dorado ate a live sardine ~100 yards
  off the beach in ~30 ft on 50 lb braid, a 30 lb fluorocarbon top shot and a
  #2 circle hook; unregistered channel, sponsor-heavy (Okuma),
  asr-uncertain(first-wahoo-of-the-season phrasing).
- `mL4Ph7t0WcQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-12-16, Lighthouse Point beach, East
  Cape): a local captain described Lighthouse Point as a beach spot with a drop
  very close to shore holding sharks, roosterfish, jacks, dorado and even
  yellowfin, fishable by surf-casting; the surf program ran a ~30 ft cast held
  stationary and thumbed on the spool, with a 10-count on a circle hook before
  winding tight rather than swinging; unregistered channel,
  asr-uncertain(a further species name heard as "kuas").
- `ORC1A68cEeM` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2023-10-25, La Paz; Baja Rancheros
  fleet, fleet captain Pedro and local guide Sergio; the channel's first La Paz
  trip): a live-bait slow-troll for jurel in 12 ft of water against visible
  rock, 40 lb line, circle hooks and a counted 3-second pause before engaging
  the reel, producing doubles close to 30 lb; the morning ran a two-species
  bait program — green mackerel jigged around floating structure against a
  guide target of 20–40 per boat, roughly a dozen landed, plus sardines bought
  from local vendors for chum and light bait; unregistered channel,
  asr-uncertain(the target named on camera as "Carell", read as jurel).
- `qM7iOO7fOBw` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2023-11-03, La Paz; day 2 of the same
  trip — day 1 windy with strong current and slow fishing): flylined live
  mackerel at a crowded shared high spot produced yellowtail, cabrilla, dorado
  and a large pargo, with the crew deliberately fishing outside the pack rather
  than anchoring in it; a yellowtail school mixed with jack was spotted by bait
  "shattering" on the surface; a hooked dorado was deliberately left in the
  water because it "attracts more"; unregistered channel, asr-uncertain(both
  anglers' line-setup figures, given over crosstalk).
- `92y14x33etQ` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2023-11-09, La Paz; guide Felipe,
  final video of the trip, framed as wrapping up a two-week stay): a
  snapper/cabrilla bottom stop at rock structure off Isla Espíritu Santo went
  quiet — "they just were not chewing... no current whatever" — before the
  fleet ran to "El Bajo", called the area's most famous high spot, shallowest
  point roughly 50 ft on low tide and no more than 80 ft on high; baits dropped
  to the bottom, then slow-trolled mackerel lifted off bottom by the troll
  speed, on 65 lb braid to a 60 lb fluorocarbon top shot; that morning's
  bait-making ran in 15–20 mph wind out of the north; the crew released the
  rest of the yellowtail once three bags had gone through the dock's
  clean/fillet/vacuum-seal/freeze service; unregistered channel,
  asr-uncertain(spot name also heard as "El Elito"/"El Bajito").
- `1nK7vSPl2sg` (StokedOnFishing, presenter Shay McKinty, captain Andrew
  Pereira, 2019-10-25, La Ventana; six-pack charter aboard a 35 ft Everglades
  out of a La Ventana-area resort): a local captain ran to a high spot rising
  from roughly 200 ft to 80 ft, described as holding a lot of fish; a bait let
  swim freely about seven seconds got bit on a 30 lb fluorocarbon top shot and
  a 3/0 circle hook, producing a large amberjack against a stated target list
  of tuna, dorado, wahoo and marlin, framed on camera as a possible La Ventana
  record; registry caps this presenter at medium,
  asr-uncertain(hook size, reel model).
- `n4nMCLLW3tY` (StokedOnFishing-adjacent trip vlog, trip dated on camera
  2023-07-31, Loreto): local word was "Monserrat north end is the best"; a boat
  working birds sat in 30 ft, cabrilla came from 5–10 ft on a suspending
  jerkbait, and a roosterfish was taken on the troll on a Halco; one fish was
  called on camera "a different type of cabrilla"; unregistered channel.
- `ikiXx9sUsoA` (Loreto/Puerto Escondido trip vlog): the trip flew in through
  CBX rather than driving; yellowtail were the stated main target with sierra
  reported on the troll; Honeymoon Bay called out as sheltered water for a
  windy day; the operation ran a mothership with an 18 ft Grady White towed
  behind to fish from; unregistered channel.
- `s5PaKJX3VDo` (Loreto trip vlog, Stella June Sportfishing): the first bird
  stop leaving the dock found wide-open surface fish taken on spooks, a Pickle
  Kick swimbait, surface iron and bass gear with a burn retrieve — "you got to
  burn it, can't let them look at it" — and "20 followers on every cast";
  unregistered channel.
- `Re56xUHOu6A` (Loreto trip, Spanish-language segment): sailfish actively
  targeted, described as fish up to 300 lb with fights of four to five hours —
  an on-camera claim, not a verified figure; dorado caught on a Loreto trip
  targeting them directly; unregistered channel.
- `0b0PDLVh-T4` (Loreto, September shoreline session): a session worked close
  enough in that the crew noted "you could have surf cast for this fish", with
  snapper/pargo taken from the shoreline program; unregistered channel.
