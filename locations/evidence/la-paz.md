# Evidence — La Paz

The observation layer behind [La Paz](../la-paz.md). One line per observation.
Observations never change the note's stated doctrine. Dates are upload dates
unless a trip date is stated on camera.

One source group carries this zone: StokedOnFishing's first trip to La Paz,
filmed as three episodes off the Baja Rancheros fishing fleet during a stated
two-week stay. The channel is not in `sources/source-registry.md`, which caps
the zone at `medium`; each episode carries sponsor reads for tackle, apparel,
outboards and the channel's own charter business, and the operator segment in
`92y14x33etQ` is a promotional interview, capped `low`.

Day order across the three videos: `ORC1A68cEeM` is day 1 (shallow-rock
slow-troll), `qM7iOO7fOBw` is day 2 (shared high spot, after a windy day 1),
`92y14x33etQ` is the trip's last day (Espíritu Santo, then El Bajo).

## Getting there

- `ORC1A68cEeM` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-10-25, La Paz, BCS): the party landed at
  the San José del Cabo airport — named on camera as the gateway for Cabo San
  Lucas, the East Cape and La Paz — was met by the fleet's people and driven
  across, the ride called "very pleasant"; check-in was at a waterfront hotel
  within walking distance of the malecón, with dinner at 6:30 and yellowtail and
  grouper sashimi on the menu. One traveller in the party had been coming to La
  Paz for close to 15 years; the fleet itself was described as formed in 2021
  out of an existing group of local captains.
- `qM7iOO7fOBw` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-03, La Paz, BCS): the trip is
  described as flying south and fishing the very next day, with the fleet
  handling airport pickup and hotel check-in.
- `92y14x33etQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-09, La Paz, BCS): the operator
  interview at the end states the fleet works 365 days a year, that all fish are
  cleaned, processed, vacuum-packed and frozen in its own office, and that
  anglers need only bring coolers; non-fishing activities named were UTVs,
  paddleboards, whale watching and seasonal whale-shark swims (promotional
  segment, `low`). The same segment claims the Sea of Cortez holds "over 435
  different varieties of fish" — an operator marketing figure, not carried into
  the zone page.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `ORC1A68cEeM` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-10-25, La Paz shallow rock): the troll
  started in 12 ft — "it's really really shallow, we started in 12 ft of water"
  — with fish reaching rock immediately; a neighbouring boat was 0 for 3 early
  on, the crew unsure whether the fish were going into the rocks, breaking off
  or being missed. Grass and weed were on the same ground, with one fish worked
  out of it at the net. Later in the session the boat moved to water the crew
  called deeper with "no rocks nothing", where bigger fish were fought without
  cutoff risk.
- `92y14x33etQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-09, Isla Espíritu Santo and El Bajo):
  the island stop was fished on rock close to the island for snapper, with mullet
  snapper and cabrilla named as the hoped-for fish; the guide then called El
  Bajo the most famous high spot in La Paz, shallowest point "on low tide has
  like 50 ft, on high tide no more than 80", fished by working around that rock.
  The spot name is heard as "El elito", "El bajo" and "El bajito" within the same
  transcript. ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): "El bajito" does not occur
  in the transcript; the renderings actually present are "the LEL grounds",
  "El elito", "El bajo Elbo" and "El bajo elb", and the "most famous high spot"
  line attaches to "El elito", not to "El bajo".
- `qM7iOO7fOBw` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-03, La Paz shared high spot): the
  ground is called "this uh little high spot" with the fleet's other boats on
  it; the bottom under the boat was called sand mid-session, and kelp was in the
  water at the net.
- `ORC1A68cEeM` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-10-25, La Paz bait grounds): mackerel
  were jigged "around these containers" — floating structure near the port —
  before the run out.

## What's there

- `ORC1A68cEeM` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-10-25, La Paz): the fleet captain's
  morning plan named jurel (yellowtail, captions render "Carell") and cabrilla,
  both "running pretty well the last few days". The session produced shallow-
  water yellowtail including a double the crew called "close to 30... 25, 30,
  30 lb", one of them off a sardine and one off a mackerel; a jack came off the
  deeper stop, and a hooked fish was lost at the boat to end the day.
- `qM7iOO7fOBw` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-03, La Paz shared high spot): by the
  mid-session update the boat had two yellowtail, several grouper/cabrilla and a
  dorado; the standout was a large pargo the crew called "a moose", after which
  the call was that any further pargo "go home" rather than being released. A
  surface school breaking bait was identified on camera as yellowtail mixed with
  jack. Dolphins passed behind a hooked fish, and a needlefish was called out.
- `92y14x33etQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-09, La Paz): the island stop produced
  a couple of cabrilla and a nice snapper with bigger fish visibly refusing, and
  small toothy snapper/grouper on the bottom; El Bajo then produced repeated
  yellowtail. A dedicated roosterfish attempt at a beach — laps run with a
  launched mackerel — drew no bite, and the only thing found there was a
  spawning aggregation of bat rays leaping beside the boat.
- `ORC1A68cEeM` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-10-25, La Paz): a cast surface iron drew
  frigatebirds during a boil rather than yellowtail — "I got the frigs going
  after my surface iron, but no yellowtail".

## How it fishes

- `ORC1A68cEeM` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-10-25, La Paz): the fleet captain's
  stated plan was to make bait first — 20 to 40 green mackerel per boat — then
  pick up sardines for chumming, with the action expected "on and rolling by
  10:00"; the crew got about a dozen mackerel and bought sardines from local
  vendors. On the water the crew's stated ritual was "always ask the captain to
  test your drags"; the troll ran 40 lb line with circle hooks and a counted
  "give them 3 seconds, 1, 2, 3, put it in gear" before engaging. Sardines were
  thrown at a boil that popped up off the corner of the boat. A short bite was
  diagnosed on camera as the angler not holding the rod still through the pause.
- `qM7iOO7fOBw` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-03, La Paz shared high spot): the crew
  flylined mackerel while one angler cast a stickbait; the deliberate call was
  "we're fishing outside of everybody else", and the bite was described as going
  "0 to 100 fast" when fish moved through. Current was noted as moving
  mid-session. A dorado was deliberately left in the water rather than boated
  "cuz we're trying to chum a few more in — if we leave them in the water it
  attracts more". Line setups compared on camera in one boat: roughly 65 lb
  braid to 60 lb with about a 40 lb top shot, against 40 lb to 40 lb
  fluorocarbon.
- `92y14x33etQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-09, La Paz): bait-making ran in 15–20
  mph wind out of the north. At El Bajo the presentation was described as
  throwing the baits down on the bottom with a weight, then slow-trolling the
  same mackerel — "when it hits the bottom, boom, with the same troll it was
  going to lift it up" — with a sardine fished "way out the back in the high
  column"; the rig ran 65 lb braid to a 60 lb fluorocarbon top shot. The captain
  motoring the boat away from structure to pull a hooked fish off it was called
  out approvingly — "good job Captain, you pulling them off... away from the
  brick". A cast hardbait came back with its trebles straightened by a big fish
  on braid, the stated fix being heavier hooks. The crew stopped keeping fish at
  three bags — "when you got enough you got enough" — and released the rest.
- `qM7iOO7fOBw` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-03, La Paz): day 1 of the trip is
  recapped as windy with super strong current and not the best fishing, with day
  2 fished at a different spot and described as completely different.
- `92y14x33etQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-09, Isla Espíritu Santo): the island
  stop was abandoned on a stated current read — "we fish the island and they
  just were not chewing as simple as that, maybe there no current whatever" —
  and the boat ran to El Bajo.
- `92y14x33etQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-09, La Paz): the trip's only
  conditions numbers are air in the high 80s to mid-90s °F and water at 75 °F,
  read out beside an apparel sponsor segment and beside the word "springtime",
  which does not fit an October–November trip. Flagged on the zone page and in
  `sources/fact-check-ledger.md`.

## Spots

- `92y14x33etQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-09, La Paz): "El Bajo" / "El elito" /
  "El bajito", the high spot, and Isla Espíritu Santo, named on camera by the
  guide, with the island's name translated on camera as Holy Spirit Island. The
  roosterfish beach is described only as an "absolutely beautiful" white-sand
  beach and never named.
- `qM7iOO7fOBw` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-11-03, La Paz): the day's ground is given
  only as "this little high spot" with no name and no position.
- `ORC1A68cEeM` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-10-25, La Paz): the day's spot is never
  named — the crew state on camera they have no idea where they are fishing and
  are following a local guide.
