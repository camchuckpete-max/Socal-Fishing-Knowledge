# Evidence — East Cape

The observation layer behind [East Cape](../east-cape.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine. Dates are
upload dates unless a trip date is stated on camera.

One source group carries this zone: StokedOnFishing's annual trip to the Hotel
Buena Vista Beach Resort, filmed across four years as ten episodes. The channel
is not in `sources/source-registry.md`, which caps the zone at `medium`; every
episode is built around the channel's own tournament event at the resort and
carries sponsor reads, so destination framing here is promotional and the
fishing content is on-the-water observation.

Episode groups: December 2016 is the first event (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ` beach and
inshore, `YijeuGOYoVQ` wahoo and the beach sight fishery, `mG8ZZLFGlT8` the blue
marlin and the weigh-in); March 2018 is the offshore/panga pair (`tcso7Lpm_Xs`,
`6j7V34GYzzw`); February 2019 is the squid-and-flyline trip (`84XPJAeH0Rw`,
`GptrotE0x5M`, `_c6UI3lGBVg`); April 2020 is the fifth event (`BQ2U1PqxWi8`,
`APsnsunT4gM`).

Auto-generated captions garble several place names in this zone. Every
unresolved rendering is carried verbatim rather than corrected: "Kabul Eidos"
and "Kabul movie" (`6j7V34GYzzw`, `_c6UI3lGBVg`), "rink on area"
(`84XPJAeH0Rw`), "ciraolo island" (`APsnsunT4gM`), "Kon Lucas" for the arrival
airport (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`).

## What's there

- `mG8ZZLFGlT8` (2016-12-22): an estimated 400–500 lb blue marlin hooked ~5 mi
  offshore, lost, re-hooked on a second pass, lost again, connected on a third
  pass ~40 minutes later, and landed on a captain-plus-angler relay — the
  captain bringing it to leader four times in roughly the first hour, ~1.5 hours
  combined fight time; the tournament weigh-in ran 37 lb, 39.4 lb (after a
  stated three-hour day) and a fish corrected on stage from 41 lb to 46 lb; one
  boat recapped five tuna, another four, a third three good-size yellowfin; a
  young angler landed his first striped marlin; the day was called "probably the
  best day I've ever had in the East[ Cape]"; asr-uncertain(a second species in
  the day recap heard as "Grotto").
- `YijeuGOYoVQ` (2016-12-20): the day was run as a wahoo trip — "we're targeting
  more Wahoo than anything else" — and produced an angler's first wahoo ever;
  the radio carried "a very good blue marlin bite today, there's three boats
  hooked up right now", one already released; a striped-marlin-versus-sailfish
  identification debate is left unresolved on camera; from the beach, a
  roosterfish was sighted at "at least 50 lb" with another at "half his size
  probably 25 30 lb", three or four fish seen in a session, some cruising five
  feet off the shore; ladyfish/needlefish also taken; asr-uncertain(the billfish
  identification, which the corpus leaves open rather than resolving).
- `tcso7Lpm_Xs` (2018-03-09): one morning produced tuna, roughly 15–16 dorado
  (most released), roughly 15–20 skipjack, the biggest snapper the angler had
  seen, and a marlin hookup one angler said "did not look like a striped marlin"
  — logged unconfirmed; the biggest tuna was about 45 lb; a second day's spread
  produced multiple wahoo, the running count ASR-garbled ("free wahoo", most
  likely three boated with a fourth hooked); the school-holding tactic stated as
  leaving a hooked dorado in the water while a second bait goes out.
- `84XPJAeH0Rw` (2019-02-10): a jack crevalle came in a mixed morning catch
  alongside four tuna, with about six jacks and a "big one" called at 65 —
  ASR does not resolve whether that weight is the jack or a tuna, so it is not
  carried as a species weight; the day's tuna recapped as "many seventies,
  sixties" plus football-grade fish on light tackle; triggerfish came repeatedly;
  the search sign was flying fish scattering and bait breaking in "puddles".
- `GptrotE0x5M` (2019-02-13): the day's points-race weigh-in recorded yellowfin
  at 72 lb, 63 lb and 63 lb, framed as bigger-grade fish starting to show for
  multiple boats.
- `_c6UI3lGBVg` (2019-02-18): the boat was "hooked up pretty much every drift"
  with "every fish that 50 to 70 pound class", and the narration notes "the
  better grade yellowfin tuna have showed up"; an on-camera interview voice
  (not diarized) called the bite "consistent all year round tuna bite along the
  coast" with the fish moving "from different locations"; attribution of that
  claim is unresolved.
- `BQ2U1PqxWi8` (2020-04-01): a marlin released on camera on the trip's first
  day, with an earlier claim of five already aboard whose species split is not
  stated; the coaching grade was "forties fifties grade, take your time", with
  one fish felt as a 15–20 lb-er; a yellowfin estimated at about 50 lb came
  "flying out of the water five feet out of the water" chasing a flying fish,
  read as feeding activity rather than a hooked fish; the trip is billed as a
  mix of inshore and offshore with dorado, snapper and big yellowfin;
  asr-uncertain.
- `APsnsunT4gM` (2020-04-08): the first day's troll produced yellowfin plus
  wahoo, including a personal-best wahoo on a circle hook described as the resort
  fleet's first wahoo of the season and as the biggest tuna the resort had seen
  that year; one marlin was boated and a second spooled the angler; a ~20 lb
  dorado ate a live sardine ~100 yards off the beach in ~30 ft; the trip's recap
  names snappers and pargos biting and roosterfish that "charged the beach in
  crazy numbers"; a release call was made on an inshore fish — "you gotta let
  these go man... there's not many of them left around here" — an ethic, not a
  regulation; asr-uncertain(the first-wahoo-of-the-season phrasing),
  sponsor-heavy (Okuma).
- `6j7V34GYzzw` (2018-03-12): an African pompano identified boatside on the
  panga rooster troll, plus needlefish; the inshore framing given as "when you

- `mL4Ph7t0WcQ` (2016-12-16): a snapper taken on a plastic and a grouper off the

## How it fishes

- `mL4Ph7t0WcQ` (2016-12-16): squid bought from local pangeros who "catch these
  every morning before everyone goes out, early early early at night with Nets";
  the skipper's stated plan was to take squid, fish close to shore for tuna, then
  go for roosters afterward; the presentation was flylined squid "slowly drift
  this squid back", likened on camera to a chunk bait; a bait was fished at a
  called-out 30 ft and thumbed there; circle-hook coaching given as "give him a
  10 count, at least 10 seconds" then "wind, just wind wind wind", with the note
  that "it's pretty tough to snag on a circle hook"; the day was fished at 20 lb,
  called "light Tackle fishing at the finest", on a medium-heavy rod the crew
  themselves called way too light; the beach retrieve was "slow rolling it back
  in, creeping it back in"; the fish "biting the Spectra" is noted.
- `YijeuGOYoVQ` (2016-12-20): the beach program is a sight fishery run off rented
  ATVs — "it's sight fishing, so cruising, we're not going to fish until we see
  something", looking for "the shadow" rather than the fish; the start time rule
  is light, not feeding: "the idea of coming out after 9 is because you need
  light to see the fish"; the rooster retrieve is "the more erratic and splash
  they can make, it's the best for rooster"; surf kit given as a 12 ft rod
  throwing an ounce and a half to 2 oz, plus a 3 oz skipped lure; some anglers
  come "strictly for surf fishing" and spend three or four days on the beach;
  the wahoo fight instruction was "no pumping... just going to keep going forward
  to keep the tension" with a "steady reel, nothing fancy"; hot pink is singled
  out as the colour, with the standing line "you just got to find that magic
  lure".
- `84XPJAeH0Rw` (2019-02-10): the crew ran "one rod with 30 pound, another one
  with 40, another one with 25 — and that's how we kind of play and see where it
  more often gets bit, and then we'll pick that", with the framing "what worked
  yesterday is completely different today"; hooks sized small for sardines and up
  to a "four, five" on fluorocarbon for the mackerel bait; squid, live sardines
  and live mackerel all fished together; a fight was coached as "circle him up...
  you're probably gonna get two more circles out of them", with the gaff cue that
  no more line is going out; a detour of "a couple of minutes" to cast at a small
  patch of surface activity, rather than continuing to the pack of working boats,
  paid off — "it never hurts to take a shot"; a small popper thrown at bait
  breaking the surface blew up immediately.
- `GptrotE0x5M` (2019-02-13): with the boat parked over feeding tuna the crew
  chummed squid pieces continuously while hook-baiting squid on one of the
  smaller hooks, on 40 lb and circle hooks; a fish was fought on a 30 lb mono top
  shot over spectra with both thumbs on the braid, pulling only when the fish was
  not pulling, drag bumped up only late; an estimated 60 lb fish broke the gaff
  boatside, went back in, and was landed in a 40-minute fight with the lever drag
  pushed down and the spool thumbed; drag run at 30 lb, the rod called "a little
  light" for the 60–80 lb grade; asr-uncertain(the parted-leader word rendered
  "GFF").
- `_c6UI3lGBVg` (2019-02-18): squid presentation given as "you want your squid
  floating down" with chunks thrown alongside; fight coaching "if you have to
  hand off, hand off... these fish are big and they beat you down", on fights
  put at about an hour, with "you can't horse these things in"; a small
  sardine-sized hook pulled out of a big tuna mid-fight, given as the reason the
  crew kept steering anglers back to a circle hook.
- `BQ2U1PqxWi8` (2020-04-01): the program was a slow troll with big scad mackerel
  while an angler dropped a flat-fall under the boat and ripped it — "we're going
  slow enough to where Ryan can cast and drop down" — with the captain "seeing
  him on the meter and just kind of calling it out"; the spot was chosen because
  the boat they got the baits from had already taken tuna there; other boats were
  chumming squid the same day; slack from a fish swimming toward the boat was
  first read as a lost fish, and winding down showed it still on; a topwater
  stickbait thrown at boils was eaten on the second cast; gear called out as a
  Komodo 450 on an eight-foot rod, and a fish that "ripped off about 100 yards".
- `APsnsunT4gM` (2020-04-08): a whole day was given to gathering sardines at an
  island because the bait situation had "been kind of poor lately"; the inshore
  rig was 50 lb spectra to a 30 lb fluorocarbon top shot and a #2 circle hook
  chosen "in case we get any roosters", on a 7'6" heavy rod, with a second rig
  running the same braid to a 40 or 30 lb top shot; asr-uncertain(the bait island
  rendered "ciraolo island", unresolved).
- `6j7V34GYzzw` (2018-03-12): a long free-spooled run on the offshore troll was
  landed and released with a circle hook credited on camera for the hookup,
  species never named — logged as an unconfirmed gamefish; the rooster session
  ran as a panga slow-troll of a pink skirt with repeated strikes and several
  fish released — "here we go on the pinky, pinky does strike again";
  asr-uncertain(the grounds name heard as "Kabul Eidos").
- `tcso7Lpm_Xs` (2018-03-09): jigs were in the water roughly 15 minutes before
  the first hookup; the fight instruction on wahoo was again no pumping, "bringing
  them in slow"; asr-uncertain(the wahoo count, heard as "free wahoo").
- `mG8ZZLFGlT8` (2016-12-22): back on the lighthouse beach on live bait, the
  coaching was to "just take your time" rather than rush the hookset, and once
  fish were located the bite window was fast — a hookup "within seconds" to at
  most "a couple minutes".
