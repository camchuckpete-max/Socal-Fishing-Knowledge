# Evidence — Guadalupe

The observation layer behind [Guadalupe](../guadalupe.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `_r08B4bItAY` (Capt. Art, SearcherSportfishing, "Fall, 2009 on Searcher,"
  2009-08-25 upload; `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel, a trip-preview
  interview rather than the numbered instructional format — medium): the run
  distances (215 mi north end, 235 mi south end), the ~11:00 departure and the
  09:00–10:00 / ~noon day-two arrival times, September five- and six-day trips
  as the Guadalupe window, and the "always a chance to go to Cedros or San
  Benitos" alternate stop. Same upload date as `Sx40JvCXFuA` and read as the
  same underlying interview split across two uploads, so the two are not
  independent sightings.
- `shZCjX2-fkI` (SearcherSportfishing, Tackle Talk Live, 2021-01-14;
  `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel — medium, single mention):
  answering a viewer asking whether the boat would be fishing Guadalupe, the
  operator states it holds a valid permit but has not been told whether the
  reserve will be open; in 2020 the health department in Ensenada closed the
  reserve for public-health reasons and the port of Ensenada — where the boat
  clears in and out — was closed too, so there was no access that year. The
  same segment notes the boat generally does not start fishing until the end of
  June. Booking/contact detail in the same passage is promotional and not
  carried.
- `_jDXMtCrUZg` (Steve Carson, Penn/SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday
  Vol. 59 (Fly line outfit)," 2019-10-16): filmed mid-summer against three-day
  trips headed to Guadalupe Island — the shortest trip format in the corpus that
  names the island.
- `gqEjWrPpa48` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 66 (Heavy
  2-Speed)," 2019-09-19): a heavy two-speed recommended for anglers headed out
  on a fall ultra-limited-load trip, cited for "landing one of those nice big
  tuna at Guadalupe the island." The reel giveaway that closes the clip is
  promotional and not carried.
- `zuAuk-Kfa1Y` (Captain Arch, SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol.
  158 (Essential Outfits)," 2021-10-07): the four-outfit quiver's Outfit 4 is
  framed around "the trips that we're on right now" — Guadalupe Island — placing
  the video in an October Guadalupe window.
- `WxlDxFjB8oQ` (Gary Gillingham, Accurate Fishing Products, via
  SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 80 (7day Trip)," 2019-12-26;
  sponsored segment — low on the product picks): dockside before the trip, the
  presenter names the destination himself as an expectation — "I think our
  destination is going to be Guadalupe Island" — and sources the 30–40 lb grade
  and the "very very line shy" read to "my buddies in the grapevine," which is
  also where the 20 lb fluorocarbon drop comes from.

## What's there

- `Sx40JvCXFuA` (Capt. Art, SearcherSportfishing, "2009 fishing season comments
  by Capt Art," 2009-08-25 upload; a season-recap clip — medium): the operator's
  first trip of 2009 to Guadalupe, run after receiving the Mexican permits,
  produced yellowfin graded 40–70 lb and yellowtail to 25 lb, called
  "one of the best fishing trips to Guadalupe Island that I've experienced in my
  career." No technique or tackle reasoning on camera, so recorded as an outcome.
- `ETw_3AFxEcM` (Steve Carson, SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol.
  189," 2022-07-14): Guadalupe yellowfin graded 50–150 lb on the six-day trips,
  fished on the same flyline rig used for SoCal yellowtail yo-yo work.
- `NJ9ZLAU3sls` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 68 (Chunking),"
  2019-10-03; speaker not named on camera — medium): the chunk-fishery grade
  called out at 100, 120, 130 and 140 lb, species named only as "tuna."
- `EfaxxszOYFI` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 72 (Halloween
  Edition)," 2019-10-31; speaker not identified — medium): Guadalupe Island and
  "the ridge" named together as a light-line tuna fishery, with a 45 lb
  yellowfin landed on size 2 and 4 hooks described as "frightfully tough."
- `Ec2GaNpiOHI` (Strictly Irons, "Q & A with Dan Wade," 2019-03-19; `dan-wade`
  is a registered voice — high on the man, single occurrence for this figure):
  asked for his yellowtail personal best on the surface iron, Wade names a 68 lb
  fish at Guadalupe on 40 lb line.
- `RgtkbmBFUXI` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-03-27, "Guadalupe Island Yellowtail vs
  Man"; unregistered channel — medium): the crew nickname "water buffalo," three
  fish snapped off into the rocks inside one sequence, the 120–250 ft drift down
  the drop-off, 100 lb leader on the bottom on a dropper loop, and the angler's
  own "I lose about eight out of 10" — "you bite right through it." Framed on
  camera as routine, not as a bad day: "it happens every day here."
- `FXWOIB0TPfE` (SearcherSportfishing, Tackle Talk Live, 2020-04-08;
  `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel — medium, single mention): a viewer
  question opening "sharks are part of the game at Guadalupe" and asking how to
  improve the odds against them; the answer is fight-mechanics (two-speed reel,
  work the rail, let the boat pull, ask for relief, keep steady pressure), logged
  in [fighting a big bluefin](../../techniques/fighting-big-bluefin.md). A
  separate passage in the same session places longliners catching albacore
  400–600 mi outside Guadalupe Island — out of this zone, carried here only so
  the mention is not lost.

## How it fishes

- `_jDXMtCrUZg` (Steve Carson, Penn/SearcherSportfishing, Vol. 59, 2019-10-16):
  the 40/50 lb-class flyline outfit for these trips — two-speed reel, 65 lb
  braid, 8 ft 30–60 lb rod, 1/0 or 2/0 circle hook, bait cast into the current —
  and the rubber-band sinker rig run to roughly 120 ft on the same trips.
- `MPC_OQjvO-o` (SearcherSportfishing, "6 to 8 day trip rod & reel
  recommendations," sponsored/product-led — low on the picks): one heavy outfit
  covering four applications, two of them Guadalupe-specific — an 80 lb flyline
  for large tuna, and a personal kite outfit on 100 lb spectra with a short
  leader that the boat will rig.
- `zuAuk-Kfa1Y` (Captain Arch, SearcherSportfishing, Vol. 158, 2021-10-07): the
  Guadalupe outfit's three applications — 60 or 80 lb flyline for 80–100 lb
  yellowfin, a bottom dropper loop with a 16 oz sinker, 6/0 or 7/0 hook and
  nose-hooked sardine, and a troll rig if the trip runs to "the ridge" — plus
  the aside that the same outfit doubles for night flat-fall jigging if bigger
  bluefin are a chance.
- `rFmWrp-Vndo` (SearcherSportfishing; cut-loop surgeon's loop segment — medium):
  named on camera as "really good for fishing yellowtail at Guadalupe" (captions
  render it "guadaloop"; resolved from channel context, not stated cleanly), with
  loop size scaled up for these trips — "they like a bigger loop," no length
  given.
- `hteLeDIy9Qs` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 121 (Yo-Yo
  Fishing)"): Guadalupe named with Colonet, "the ridge" and Alijos Rocks as the
  "real deep" ground calling for a 7X-heavy iron.
- `rziFyx7SRGI` (presenter introduced as "Fred," affiliation captioned "secret
  fishing rods" and flagged `asr-uncertain`; SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip
  Thursday Vol. 63 (Long Rod)," 2019-08-29): a 23-second clip pitching the jig
  stick for Guadalupe Island — "a lot of guys leave their jig sticks at home" —
  with no jig weight, colour or retrieve given. The dated charter-booking
  announcement that closes the clip is promotional and not carried.
- `fgTmUq78ofQ` (Art Taylor/Mike, SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol.
  147 — Live at Turner's Outdoorsman," 2021-05-14): the two-dropper 200 lb rig
  called "double treble" on camera, 3/0 or 4/0 circle hooks, baits
  shoulder-hooked so they "dance around the surface," named as "one of our
  favorite ways to catch yellowfin at Guadalupe."
- `yr6z3DmWY4s` (BD Outdoors, serving a fluorocarbon leader into hollow-core
  braid): the served leader named for stealth bait fishing at Guadalupe Island,
  with dedicated 50, 60 and 80 lb rods each pre-served so nothing is retied on
  the water.
- `48ZFXnCTTQE` (Cesar, Eric's Tackle, via Dirty Hookers, 2023-11-01 upload;
  the session recounted is dated ~2019–2020 from "three years ago, four years
  ago" against that upload date; Guadalupe Island): a morning bite on 50–60 lb
  test went dead by mid-morning, the presenter dropped to 30 lb test — a nearby
  skipper independently doing the same — got bit within two minutes and landed a
  130 lb fish in 45 minutes.
