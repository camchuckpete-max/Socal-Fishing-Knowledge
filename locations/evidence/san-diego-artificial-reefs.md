# Evidence — San Diego Artificial Reefs

The observation layer behind
[San Diego Artificial Reefs](../san-diego-artificial-reefs.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## What's there

- `AH4Eiya1Hn0` (Pablo Fernandez, Fernandez Charters, 2024-03-09, Mission
  Bay / La Jolla): named the Ingram Street Bridge as a standout Mission Bay
  spotted-bay-bass spot alongside jetties and docks generally, with activity
  picking up from March through fall and fish available year-round;
  unregistered speaker, single mention. The spot library spells the same
  structure "Ingraham St Bridge Rubble" (cameron). The only corpus mention
  that names a waypoint in this zone. Harvested from
  [spotted bay bass](../../species/spotted-bay-bass.md).
  ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): "the same structure" is the open
  question, not a settled spelling variant. The bridge named on camera is a road
  bridge inside Mission Bay (`AH4Eiya1Hn0`); the library waypoint sits at
  32°45.930'N 117°16.278'W (cameron), on the offshore Pacific Beach AR longitude
  band rather than in the bay, and reads as rubble *from* that bridge placed
  offshore as reef material.
  ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): "picking up from March through fall" is
  a conversion of the speaker's relative "from now till a fall" against the
  2024-03-09 show date. What he says is "you can get them year round the best
  times obviously spring summer fall" (`AH4Eiya1Hn0`), which is the parent
  note's rendering; [spotted bay bass](../../species/spotted-bay-bass.md)
  carries the March version under the same cite.

## How it fishes

- `f4qYtHACGyk` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, 2020-11-07, SoCal
  artificial reefs, no individual reef named on camera): reported catches off
  chummed artificial reefs on the anchored-chum program — sargo up to 5–6 lb,
  big white seabass, big sheephead, calico bass, and yellowtail; the frenzy
  makes the catch mixed and unpredictable. Not scoped to the San Diego
  series specifically. Harvested from
  [chunking](../../techniques/chunking.md).
- `bM2vUS1B-yQ` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, PCS Show seminar,
  2023-03-03): stage account of the SoCal artificial-reef system being built
  for sport fishermen in the 1950s–70s on sportfishing money, with the reef
  species mix listed; registered voice, single mention, and no San Diego reef
  named. Harvested from [chunking](../../techniques/chunking.md).
- `AH4Eiya1Hn0` (Pablo Fernandez): sabiki whatever small baitfish is around
  the dock or bridge, then fish that same bait live on the spot — "every
  single one gets eaten." A bay-structure method, not reef-chum. Harvested
  from [spotted bay bass](../../species/spotted-bay-bass.md).
