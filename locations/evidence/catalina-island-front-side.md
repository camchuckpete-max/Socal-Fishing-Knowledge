# Evidence — Catalina Island, Front Side

The observation layer behind
[Catalina Island — Front Side](../catalina-island-front-side.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `PKf7G3uL4io` (StokedOnFishing, "California Offshore Challenge, part 2,"
  event ~early November 2014 per the 2014-11-11 upload, Catalina, day two): a
  team started day two running the West End, found it "just blowing," found it
  worse the further back along the backside they went, turned around and
  retreated to shelter on the front side; no swell direction given on camera;
  unregistered channel.
- `MUpvP-Yl2R0` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-11-23 upload, front side of Catalina):
  with first-timers aboard the captain picked the front side specifically for
  calmer conditions after phoning a local fish-report line the night before;
  the run was called roughly 30 mi with live squid — "the candy" — aboard;
  sponsor-heavy Navico/Simrad demo; unregistered channel.
- `aFb221LUoD0` (Your Saltwater Guide, Capt. Dave Hansen, 2020-10-20, Catalina
  game-plan process): swell direction read first — a big west swell sends
  anglers to the east end rather than the front side, backside or middle,
  while a south swell tears up the east end; built from FishDope charting for
  chlorophyll/temperature and Buoy Weather for swell; registered voice, single
  mention.
- `iQLyBzhOSi8` (StokedOnFishing, "California Offshore Challenge, part 1,"
  Two Harbors, Catalina; on-camera slate reads 2012 fourth-annual COOC against
  a 2014-11-11 upload date, dating unresolved): dockside briefing named a full
  moon, big tides, extreme temperatures and heavy squid around the island;
  swell tracked 5–7 ft the day before day one, 4–6 ft day one, 4–6 ft forecast
  day two, 3–5 ft the following Sunday, with a small craft advisory mentioned;
  the local islands framed as a hazard stack of thick kelp, razor-sharp boiler
  rocks and big waves; unregistered channel.
- `Y2bXn44lfqo` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-08-30, Catalina Island): a hatchery
  broodstock crew fished a Catalina MLPA area described on camera as closed to
  public fishing; the side of the island is not stated; registered in
  [regulatory claims](../../sources/regulatory-claims.md) as of 2024;
  unregistered channel.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `HGyL7pXy3Ts` (Your Saltwater Guide, Capt. Dave Hansen, 2019-05-20, Catalina,
  anchoring on calico spots): running onto a close-range rock the meter read
  9 fathoms, converted on the fly to 54 ft, and the boat settled into 48 ft
  over a rock whose crest sat about 8 ft below the surface; the side of the
  island is not stated; registered voice.
- `HnqiE05vdXs` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-31, Catalina): the ~500-fathom curve
  described as structure in its own right — the uphill summer counter-current
  compresses climbing the slope, spins eddies and aggregates bait and kelp
  paddies along the curve; full worked example in
  [island structure](../island-structure.md).

## What's there

- `MUpvP-Yl2R0` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-11-23, front side of Catalina): anchored
  and chumming live squid, multiple yellowtail inside about the first half
  hour, plus calico bass (one close-call legal-size fish), bonito to the rail,
  and two California halibut — one short and released, one measured 24 1/4 in
  against a crew-stated 22 in legal minimum and kept; a hard-fighting fish
  called "a big halibut" mid-fight surfaced as a yellowtail, a species-
  misidentification instance; unregistered channel.
- `zUFbCIWZZMw` (StokedOnFishing, 2014-11-03 upload, Bird Rock / Two Harbors,
  Catalina): an anchored spot near Bird Rock called "very fishy" produced a
  landed yellowtail on live squid alongside 3–4 lb calico bass and barracuda;
  the same trip's tender worked the west end for yellowtail and white seabass
  on an expected afternoon bite and produced a bat ray then a leopard shark;
  sponsor-heavy Simrad demo; unregistered channel.
- `5p6gu14ZC4w` (BDOutdoors, 2022-10-26, Catalina / Santa Barbara Island): the
  Freelance had about 40 yellowtail at Catalina, with yellowtail called
  catchable while water temperatures held, though the zone by then was "mostly
  against Bonita, bass and Rockfish"; Farnsworth Bank named separately as the
  fall yo-yo call with a keep-list limited to yellowtail and bonito.
- `oadK6zIYyCo` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-12-23 upload, Catalina, family day at
  anchor): a yellowtail landed on live squid first cast; a small calico came up
  on the sardine rather than the squid with both soaking; a barracuda took a
  Tady 45 sunk about 30 ft before winding; a sheephead landed incidentally and
  called "Poor Man's Lobster" on camera; side of the island not stated;
  sponsor-heavy dealership-ad episode; unregistered channel.
- `S2L3KLSQ6Is` (BDOutdoors, 2022-05-26, Catalina Island): weekly Bight report
  placing barracuda at the island in late May.
- `M7BtON4GZgQ` (StokedOnFishing, trip dated August 2020, 2021-02-03 upload,
  west end of Catalina, 85 ft El Dorado, Long Beach Sportfishing): bluefin seen
  in the zone "every day" for several days prior, coming back from the islands
  a handful at a time rather than as one body; marks called at 120 ft and then
  a "really good mark... solid" at 150 ft with jigs sent down; unregistered
  channel.
- `HzE4FHHcvTk` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-17, Bight): paddy fishing ran up to San
  Clemente Island and the inside of Catalina, but paddies were fewer and less
  holding further up; the best of it was south of Clemente or Catalina.
- `YQsbwfQ4wzY` (Your Saltwater Guide, Capt. Dave Hansen, 2019-11-26): hoop
  netting Catalina's shallow structure in 20 ft and 30 ft off a 65 ft Hatteras,
  shallower than the general 40–50 ft rope band; registered voice.

## How it fishes

- `MUpvP-Yl2R0` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-11-23, front side of Catalina): pre-bait,
  the crew called out a size 4 hook on a slider sinker weighted at roughly
  1/4 oz as "perfect for squid," fished on live squid at anchor with chum;
  en route the crew read a buoy overlay off the plotter at 3 ft / 17 s swell
  plus a 2 ft / 7 s wind wave, and used the plotter's SST shading to help pick
  the day's zone; radar bird mode painted a close-in bird school at a 1/2 mile
  range; sponsor-heavy, product claims not carried; unregistered channel.
