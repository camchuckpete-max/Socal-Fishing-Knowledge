# Evidence — Desperation Reef

The observation layer behind [Desperation Reef](../desperation-reef.md). One
line per observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `Rb5I2ljAqeE` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-05-05 upload, "Top Gun 80 Epic 5 Day,
  part 1," a spot the crew names "desperation reef," called on camera the West
  End tuna zone): the boat anchored on the high spot reasoning the bite held
  there because bait squid were staged on the bank; the on-camera zone name sits
  against the spot library charting Desperation Reef at the southeast end of the
  back-side run — flagged, not reconciled; unregistered channel.
- `AIHvJj-paoo` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-11-29 trip): a run from the 209 out to
  San Clemente Island ended about six miles off the island outside **Desperation
  Point** in 1,200–1,300 ft with fish boiling around the boat — a second
  Desperation name at this island, in deep water rather than on the charted high
  spot; unregistered channel.

## What's there

- `Rb5I2ljAqeE` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-05-05 upload, desperation reef): session
  grade 40–90 lb bluefin with the best "probably close to 90" and the angler's
  biggest bluefin yet, plus baits suspended under a balloon for 30–40 lb tuna;
  the same anchor stop picked up yellowtail at 18–25 lb on a sardine flyline,
  the crew calling the landed yellowtail "Home Guard"; the trip framed that
  season's bluefin push as running from the West End down to about 10 miles off
  Point Loma with fish reported 50 to almost 300 lb; unregistered channel.

## How it fishes

- `Rb5I2ljAqeE` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-05-05 upload, desperation reef): flylined
  dead squid (leftover bait fished "fresh dead") drew repeat bites in
  current-against-wind conditions, with an unusually light half-ounce weight
  added to the flyline to keep the bait swinging naturally rather than let the
  current straighten it — a stated departure from the technique's no-weight
  rule; unregistered channel.
