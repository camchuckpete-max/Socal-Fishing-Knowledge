# Evidence — Baja Pacific — South

The observation layer behind
[Baja Pacific — South](../baja-pacific-south.md). One line per observation.
Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## The fishery

- `97clKtVsEOs` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2015-10-29, Alijos Rocks; "Intrepid
  Style 8 Day, part 2," Capt. Kevin Osborne): the rocks described on camera as
  structure running from the surface all the way to the bottom and unfished
  for about five days beforehand, run specifically to troll for wahoo — only
  one wahoo bit and the crew moved on rather than grind; a scouting pass on
  the Ridge on the same trip was abandoned because the water was too warm;
  sponsor-heavy, unregistered channel.
- `6kpWn2sXokI` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2012-05-08, Alijos Rocks; "part 3"
  aboard the long-range Intrepid): closing a couple-day stop, yellowfin graded
  70 lb up to a couple around 80 lb in a mixed bag with yellowtail as the
  standout, a few scattered wahoo and dorado starting to pick around — "not
  wide open, but... good fishing"; an angler who yo-yoed most of the trip got
  bit, framed as a persistence game at this bank; unregistered channel.
- `3tQ1_xiqwVU` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2012-05-01, the Ridge near Magdalena
  Bay; "Intrepid Long Range Sportfishing | Part 2"): a yellowtail hooked
  yo-yoing at 180 ft, 10 ft off the bottom, working back up on the Ridge after
  the crew left Thetis Bank overnight because the current was opposite; the
  stated plan for the next stop was kite, helium balloon and flyline on 50 lb
  for yellowfin graded 60–90 lb with a few over 100 lb; unregistered channel.
- `UuyqTE21-kc` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2015-01-18, lower banks near Magdalena
  Bay): a helium balloon rig floated a flying-fish bait downwind of the boat to
  keep it riding lifelike on the surface, with multiple hookups on flyers, on
  giant yellowfin graded 150 to 300-plus lb; unregistered channel.
- `RNRFrfepiW0` (Steve Carson, SearcherSportfishing, 2023-04-27, "Tackle Tip
  Thursday Vol. 219"): Alijos Rocks and the Ridge named as where yellowtail run
  bigger and meaner with more rocks, the stated trigger for stepping up to a
  two-speed Penn Fathom 40 Narrow with 80 lb braid and a 60 lb top shot on
  a 30–80 lb rod, plus a heavier/longer Tady 14A when the fish are big or wind
  makes the bottom hard to reach; registered channel, product picks capped low.
- `hteLeDIy9Qs` (Fred, Secret Fishing Rods, via SearcherSportfishing,
  2020-10-15, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 121"): three yo-yo jig sizes by depth
  band, with Alijos and "the ridge" in the deep tier calling for a 7X heavy —
  stated reasoning is more time in the strike zone on the straight
  up-and-down; registered channel.
- `uWGGeDOprsE` (SearcherSportfishing, 2009-08-25, "Tackle Tips for Alijos
  Rocks and The Ridge"): the five-outfit quiver for a 7-day trip to these two
  grounds, including a 50/60 lb two-speed for 100 lb-class yellowfin on a
  fly-lined sardine and an 80 lb outfit interchanging between wahoo trolling
  and a 16 oz dropper loop for yellowtail; registered channel.
- `EcQK5elTPJE` (Capt. Dave Hansen and Larry Hansen, Your Saltwater Guide,
  2024-01-27, 2024 Bart Hall Show, Lopez Mateos / Magdalena Bay): four
  fisheries in one bay — mangrove interior at 12–15 ft with a snook program
  down to 2 ft, the boca 5 minutes from the dock in 40–50 ft with grouper to
  100 lb and snook to at least 42 lb, mid-range tuna 10–12 mi out over 200–300
  ft at 20–50 lb, and offshore banks named only as the 23 and the 38 fished
  alongside San Diego long-range boats; Larry Hansen's voice is unregistered
  and caps the block at medium.
- `gimFbgN5Jjk` (Benny Ortiz, 2025 PCS Show "Slow Pitch to Speed Jigging"
  seminar, upload 2025-03-14, Magdalena Bay): a personal best day of 72 marlin
  with the boat capable of over a hundred the same day, and the bottom/jig
  fishery called virtually untapped because the October–November crowd comes
  for marlin — one jig day produced 14–15 species including Colorado snappers,
  broom tail and gulf groupers, and yellowtail; unregistered panel.
- `Cobp85UvHmM` (StokedOnFishing, 2018-02-07, La Bocana; "Stoked On La Bocana,
  part 2"): the crew ran 25 mi offshore past what they called the barracuda
  grounds onto tuna grounds, trolling tuna feathers and Rapala-class hardbaits
  for tuna, marlin and wahoo, then finished the day back on inshore rock
  structure taking yellowtail on surface iron; unregistered channel.
- `qri15R3caYE` (StokedOnFishing, 2018-02-01, La Bocana; "Stoked On La Bocana,"
  part 1): jigs fished to roughly 80 ft on the inshore rock structure drew a
  hookup every single drop, in a mixed bag of yellowtail, sand bass, barracuda,
  calico bass and some big bonito; unregistered channel.
- `ll7r4A6atno` (Capt. Dave Hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, 2021-01-19, Cabo San
  Lucas): small hoochies with a quarter-ounce slider in the head, on wire,
  slow-trolled along the surf line at roughly 6 kt for sierra — best early
  morning or right at sunset, and better once the surf water turns dirty
  lime-green, with a dorado taken incidentally on the same gear; registered
  voice, single mention of this rig.
- `dgauGbNxP84` (Capt. Dave Hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, 2021-01-28, Cabo San
  Lucas): a current break identified visually as a whitewater/foam line running
  against a slicker patch angling offshore, then trolled back and forth across
  rather than down one side, producing skipjack.
- `XH-Hrfet6To` (StokedOnFishing, 2014-12-02, dockside at Melton Tackle ahead
  of a Cabo San Lucas charter; "Stoked On Cabo San Lucas, part 1"): the shop
  steered the trip toward medium-to-larger rather than giant trolling lure
  heads on tournament-scoring reasoning, and described Cabo's offshore water as
  flat in the morning and building choppy through the afternoon as heat off the
  land drives wind; unregistered channel, product claims capped low.

## Season shape

- `9IhmYstB8sA` (SearcherSportfishing / Fisherman's Landing, 2019-11-14,
  "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 74"): October and November named on camera as the
  best time of year to catch wahoo, with no destination attached to the claim;
  registered channel.
- `tjBeR9tWd4s` (Carl, Fisherman's Landing / Team Searcher, 2022-10-20, "Tackle
  Tip Thursday Vol. 201"): filmed in October, the long-range boats including
  the Searcher about to start their 6-, 7- and 8-day trips fishing Alijos Rocks
  and the Ridge for wahoo; registered channel.
- `4uNPLknRAQg` (SearcherSportfishing, 2020-05-06, "Tackle Talk Live Recap
  (Wahoo 5.5.20)"): fall named best — late September, October, early November —
  with Capt. Mike Tahr's second-week-of-November-2019 trip cited as producing
  tremendous catches; some seven/eight-day trips leave as early as July to be
  first at Alijos Rocks or the Ridge if the water is warm enough, on the stated
  mechanism that water warms incrementally through summer and can carry a
  hurricane-driven warm push, with 72 °F or warmer the standing condition;
  registered channel.
- `_r08B4bItAY` (Capt. Art, SearcherSportfishing, 2009-08-25, "Fall, 2009 on
  Searcher"): previewing the late-September/early-October seven-day trips,
  Alijos Rocks and the Ridge given as the two options, "one or the other," with
  a wahoo chance at the rocks or on the ridge or on kelp paddies down in that
  area, a 100 lb yellowfin shot, and a nice big dorado off the paddies; the
  Ridge resolved as running to an upper high spot named Uncle Sam Bank, famous
  for yellowtail, black sea bass, grouper and yellowfin in that window;
  registered channel.
- `_f5QmWSUUx8` (SearcherSportfishing, upload 2011-02-10, "Jim Preciado, Alijos
  Rocks, 09/10" — trip resolved to September 2010 from companion uploads in the
  same series): an angler cast a wire-leadered Raider-class jig on his first
  stop at the rocks, with a dorado boil sighted at the surface and two apparent
  hookups called; neither landing nor species is confirmed in the captions, so
  it stands as gear corroboration only; registered channel, raw footage.
- `jH0q4UPjwC0` (SearcherSportfishing, upload 2011-03-29, filmed September 2010
  per the title, Alijos Rocks): a crew member described adding a 2–4 oz sliding
  sinker to a kite-flown bait on others' advice that it keeps the bait in the
  water better, framed as untested by him; registered channel, raw footage —
  medium.
- `4t_Z75shK_E` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2020-09-25, Magdalena Bay; presenter
  Shea with lure-company owner/guide Toby): the bay framed as loaded with
  striped marlin plus a healthy wahoo and dorado population come fall; roughly
  three days of wind had just settled and blew again the night before, so no
  boats had been out for a couple of days; a dead bait's mouth was tied closed
  before slow-trolling to keep it streamlined; unregistered channel, the
  transit-lure claim promotional and capped low.
- `isXJONlpUP4` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2020-10-07, Magdalena Bay, Mag Bay
  Lodge): after getting beaten up offshore for marlin the previous day the crew
  switched to the estuary and mangroves, working an estuary channel at roughly
  25–30 ft within 50–100 m of the mangroves; palometa came fast on small
  trolled lures, a visible pargo school was cast to with a 3/4 oz Cast Master,
  and the guide's read was that a flooding tide moves the fish inside the
  mangroves rather than shutting them off; unregistered channel.
- `OVwqUKim9Pc` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2020-10-14, Magdalena Bay, Mag Bay
  Lodge): before committing the crew idled past the estuary mouth to read the
  wave state — the Ridge if it looked manageable, the closer "Modesto Main" if
  not — and the day's offshore run for wahoo turned into a full-speed dorado
  bite; the final day went back to the estuary for a multi-fish snook bite;
  unregistered channel.
- `Mwx5AAXNMvE` (StokedOnFishing, 2014-12-03, offshore Cabo San Lucas; "Stoked
  On Cabo San Lucas, part 2"): two big blue marlin struck trolled lures and did
  not stay hooked, the lure names too garbled in the captions to carry;
  unregistered channel.
- `UuyqTE21-kc` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2015-01-18, lower banks near Magdalena
  Bay): dated on camera as October, the big fish "finally showed" on the lower
  banks — narrated as the area from Mag Bay down toward Cabo San Lucas, where
  giant tuna of 130s to 350 lb migrate up every fall to breed and spawn, fished
  a few days before the boat worked back up toward the Ridge; unregistered
  channel.
- `ASitOLYzFEA` (StokedOnFishing, on-camera trip date November 25 with the year
  unresolved, "lower banks," Baja; "Intrepid 300lb dock day"): the boat
  searched a couple of days, locked onto a single school of giant tuna and
  anchored on that one spot for four days without moving, with wahoo swimming
  through the school; unregistered channel.

## Access & range

- `_r08B4bItAY` (Capt. Art, SearcherSportfishing, 2009-08-25): Alijos Rocks put
  at 488 mi from San Diego, a 45-hour run leaving around 11:00 with bait
  obtained the next morning; the Ridge and Uncle Sam Bank at 450–460 mi to the
  upper end, a two-day run.
- `97clKtVsEOs` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2015-10-29): the same rocks described
  on camera as roughly 400 mi south of San Diego — an 88 mi disagreement with
  the figure above, unreconciled in the corpus.
- `3tQ1_xiqwVU` (StokedOnFishing, upload 2012-05-01): the transit from near
  Magdalena Bay toward Alijos Rocks given as 160 mi and about 16 hours.
- `EcQK5elTPJE` (Dave Hansen and Larry Hansen, Your Saltwater Guide,
  2024-01-27): access to Lopez Mateos by flying into Loreto and driving through
  Ciudad Constitucion with a dirt-road final stretch; inside the bay the boca
  is about 5 minutes from the dock and the 10–12 mi tuna grounds are a half-day
  with the boat back by 10:00; when it blows outside the inside stays flat —
  wind chop, no swell — so consecutive fishable days are routine by moving
  between zones.
