# Evidence — Coronado Islands

The observation layer behind [Coronado Islands](../coronado-islands.md). One
line per observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `D5DR7Kx42_A`: the Coronados stated as Mexican water with CONAPESCA/INM
  jurisdiction rather than CDFW.
  ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the transcript's only jurisdictional
  statement is geographic — "approximately 13 miles from San Diego off the
  coast of Mexico." CONAPESCA, INM and CDFW are never named and no season,
  limit, licence or paperwork is mentioned anywhere in the video.

## How it fishes

- `HTowqnwAMeA`: the troll-search circuit — figure-8s around the islands plus a
  Middle Grounds pass — with trolling framed as prospecting that happens to
  hook fish rather than as the presentation.
  ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): only the prospecting framing is in
  the transcript ("trolling is an excellent way of prospecting a zone quickly
  and effectively"); "figure-8", "island", "Middle Grounds" and "Coronado"
  appear nowhere in it. The trip is a mid-August local coastal day "above the
  border" on a rock "20, 30 ft across," fished anchored first and then moving
  ("we picked up the anchor, started moving around… slow troll a little bit"),
  and the same video treats trolling as a real presentation too (colour, depth
  and vibration selection on a dedicated trolling outfit).
