# Evidence — Bays and Harbors

Per-source provenance backing [Bays and Harbors](../bays-and-harbors.md).

## Structure & bathymetry

- `AxLlx2Ug-rs` (Capt. Benny Florentino, StokedOnFishing, 2012-03-29, location
  unspecified): fish held tight to the pilings, using them to ambush bait; the
  crew kept casts as close to the pilings as possible rather than working the
  open water around them. Species not specified in source.
- `usHl-4SfqDA` (StokedOnFishing, 2024-11-13, Long Beach Harbor; unregistered
  channel — medium confidence): working dock pilings for bass (calico, sand
  bass, spotted bay bass mixed), the crew favored old, weathered pilings over
  new ones — old pilings carry established growth and crustaceans that new
  pilings haven't grown yet, and that growth is what holds fish; a new piling
  can still hold a fish but is judged distinctly worse water. A quick visual
  tell: old pilings look noticeably different (worn, colonized) from a
  freshly installed piling nearby.
