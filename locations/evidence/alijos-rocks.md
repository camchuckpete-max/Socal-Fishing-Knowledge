# Evidence — Alijos Rocks

The observation layer behind [Alijos Rocks](../alijos-rocks.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `_r08B4bItAY` (Capt. Art, SearcherSportfishing, 2009-08-25, trip preview for
  the boat's fall seven-day trips): Alijos Rocks given as 488 mi from San Diego
  on a 45-hour run leaving around 11:00 with bait obtained the next morning at
  a stop captioned "Vallejos" (asr-uncertain, not resolved to a place and not
  carried as a destination); the Ridge/Uncle Sam Bank 450–460 mi to its upper
  end on a two-day run; a seven-day trip books "one or the other"; registered
  channel.
- `97clKtVsEOs` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-10-29, Alijos Rocks; "Intrepid Style 8
  Day, part 2," aboard the 116 ft *Intrepid*, Capt. Kevin Osborne): the rocks
  placed "about 400 miles south of San Diego", called out in the distance at 10
  mi on the approach and described as massive; sponsor-heavy episode (Simrad,
  Okuma, Davey's Locker, a dealership), unregistered channel.
- `3tQ1_xiqwVU` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-05-01, transiting from near Magdalena
  Bay toward Alijos Rocks aboard the *Intrepid*; "Intrepid Long Range
  Sportfishing | Part 2"): the corridor run given as 160 mi and about 16 hours;
  the destination is captioned "Leo's Rocks"/"Allejo's Rocks"
  (asr-uncertain, read as Alijos Rocks from the stated transit and context);
  unregistered channel.
- `tjBeR9tWd4s` (Carl, Fisherman's Landing / Team Searcher, 2022-10-20,
  "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 201"): filmed in October, long-range boats
  "including the Searcher" about to start their 6-, 7- and 8-day trips fishing
  Alijos Rocks and the ridge for wahoo; registered channel.
- `J3FGJj5zYPE` (Logan, Searcher Sportfishing / Fisherman's Landing,
  2023-09-14, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 227"): filmed ahead of an eight-day
  trip to Alijos Rocks; unregistered channel.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `97clKtVsEOs` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-10-29, Alijos Rocks): asked how deep it
  is, the answer on camera is that the rocks go "from the surface all the way
  to the bottom"; no depth figure given anywhere in the segment.
- `RNRFrfepiW0` (Steve Carson, SearcherSportfishing, 2023-04-27, "Tackle Tip
  Thursday Vol. 219"): Alijos Rocks (captioned "Ali host rocks"/"Elio's rocks",
  asr-uncertain, read as Alijos Rocks) and "the ridge" named as bigger water
  with more rocks and bigger, meaner yellowtail; registered channel, product
  picks capped low.
- `hteLeDIy9Qs` (Fred, Secret Fishing Rods, via SearcherSportfishing,
  2020-10-15, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 121"): three yo-yo jig tiers by depth,
  with "Alejos" (asr-uncertain, read as Alijos Rocks), "the ridge", Colonet and
  Guadalupe in the deep tier calling a 7X heavy, on the stated reasoning of
  more time in the strike zone; registered channel.

## What's there

- `6kpWn2sXokI` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-05-08, Alijos Rocks aboard the
  *Intrepid*; "part 3"): closing a couple-day stop at the rocks, yellowfin
  graded 70 lb up to a couple around 80 lb, yellowtail called the standout and
  "really good", a few scattered wahoo, and dorado "starting to pick around"
  on the crew's self-described second day — "not wide open, but... good
  fishing"; no jig weight, colour or depth given for the yellowtail;
  unregistered channel.
- `_r08B4bItAY` (Capt. Art, SearcherSportfishing, 2009-08-25): the fall
  seven-day trips put a 100 lb yellowfin chance at Alijos Rocks alongside a
  wahoo chance "at the rocks or on the ridge or on kelp paddies down in that
  area" and "a nice big dorado" off those paddies; registered channel.
- `4uNPLknRAQg` (SearcherSportfishing, 2020-05-06, "Tackle Talk Live Recap
  (Wahoo 5.5.20)"): fall named best — "late September, October, early
  November" — with 72 °F or warmer as the standing wahoo condition and
  incremental summer warming plus a possible hurricane-driven warm push as the
  stated mechanism; some seven- and eight-day trips leave as early as July to
  be first at Alijos Rocks or the Ridge if the water is warm enough; Capt. Mike
  Tahr's second-week-of-November-2019 trip cited as producing "tremendous
  catches"; registered channel/series.

## How it fishes

- `97clKtVsEOs` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-10-29, Alijos Rocks): the boat left a
  Benito's Island yellowtail leg to run here specifically to troll for wahoo,
  arriving on ground nobody had fished for five days — "the Wahoo ought to be
  ready to bite" — and only one wahoo bit all day, after which the crew chose
  not to grind, bailed out and ran to the Ridge, where the water was "just too
  warm", before returning to Benito's; the trolled bomb was picked over a
  jig-and-reel for hookup ratio on wahoo's back-half strike ("we got nothing
  but hook there"), fished on a two-speed reel named as an Okuma Andros 12
  prototype with 40 lb Okuma Soft Steel Ultra, and the fight was coached as
  continuous winding with no pumping; sponsor-heavy (Okuma is an on-air
  sponsor, so the reel/line model claims stay low), unregistered channel.
- `ETw_3AFxEcM` (Steve Carson, SearcherSportfishing, 2022-07-14, "Tackle Tip
  Thursday Vol. 189"): on the Searcher's seven-day-and-longer trips reaching
  Alijos Rocks or "the Ridge", wahoo taken on a bomb with or without a small
  spinner, retrieved at the absolute highest rate of speed possible;
  unregistered channel.
- `J3FGJj5zYPE` (Logan, Searcher Sportfishing / Fisherman's Landing,
  2023-09-14): wahoo target a variety of fish moving through the structure, so
  the lure colour that gets bitten is set by what they are feeding on at the
  moment — carry pink, blue and green rather than one confidence colour;
  unregistered channel.
- `it_YYh_8Z-w` (Logan, SearcherSportfishing, 2022-09-22, "Tackle Tip Thursday
  Vol. 197"): two sizes of the Nomad DTX named for any long-range trip and
  called out by name at Alijos Rocks and "the Ridge", on the stated mechanism
  that it swims deeper than most trolling lures; the "out-fish other lures four
  to one" claim is a product endorsement with no basis given and stays low.
- `uWGGeDOprsE` (SearcherSportfishing, 2009-08-25, "Tackle Tips for Alijos
  Rocks and The Ridge"): the five-outfit quiver for a seven-day trip to these
  two grounds — a 50/60 lb two-speed for 100 lb-class yellowfin on a fly-lined
  sardine, a 6 ft 6 in high-speed 50/60 lb outfit for the wahoo bomb, and an 80
  lb outfit interchanging between wahoo trolling and a 16 oz dropper loop with
  live sardine or mackerel for yellowtail; the circle-hook trigger given as 60
  to 80 lb fish biting only 40 or 50 lb test; speaker not identified on camera;
  registered channel.
- `3tQ1_xiqwVU` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-05-01, transiting toward Alijos Rocks):
  the crew narrated the plan to fish the kite, helium balloon rigs and the
  flyline on 50 lb for "quality" yellowfin graded 60–90 lb, possibly a few over
  100 lb, at the next stop — a stated plan, not shown executed in that footage;
  unregistered channel.
- `jH0q4UPjwC0` (SearcherSportfishing, upload 2011-03-29, filmed September 2010
  per the title, Alijos Rocks): an unidentified crew member interviewed
  dockside described adding a 2–4 oz sliding sinker to a kite-flown bait
  because "that's what they were saying" keeps the bait in the water better,
  framing it as untested for himself; raw on-the-water footage rather than the
  Tackle Tip/Tackle Talk series, so it stays medium and single-mention.
- `_f5QmWSUUx8` (SearcherSportfishing, upload 2011-02-10, "Jim Preciado, Alijos
  Rocks, 09/10" — resolved to September 2010 from companion clips in the same
  upload series): an angler on his first stop at "the rocks" fished a
  wire-leadered jig captioned "Rider jig" (read as the Raider jig); a dorado
  boil was sighted at the surface and an apparent strike and a second hookup
  were called, but the captions never confirm a fish landed or name a species —
  carried as gear corroboration only.
- `6kpWn2sXokI` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-05-08, Alijos Rocks): an angler who had
  been yo-yoing "pretty much most of the trip" got bit, framed on camera as a
  persistence game at this bank — fish enough drops and a bite eventually
  comes; a garbled line near the hookup ("trying to add 40 50 lb absolute
  must") is too uncertain to carry as a parameter; unregistered channel.
- `RNRFrfepiW0` (Steve Carson, SearcherSportfishing, 2023-04-27): the step-up
  rig for this ground — two-speed Penn Fathom 40 Narrow filled three-quarters
  with 80 lb braid under a 60 lb mono top shot on a shorter, more powerful
  30–80 lb rod, with a jig captioned "Tady 14a" (asr-uncertain) called "the key
  to your big Baja yellowtail"; registered channel.

## Spots

- `97clKtVsEOs`, `6kpWn2sXokI`, `_f5QmWSUUx8` (StokedOnFishing and
  SearcherSportfishing, 2011–2015 uploads, Alijos Rocks): all three describe
  the ground as "the rocks" without naming a high spot, face, end or corner,
  and none gives a position — the basis for this zone publishing no spot pages.
- `3tQ1_xiqwVU` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-05-01): Thetis Bank left overnight
  because the current was opposite, the crew working back up on the Ridge
  instead — a separate ground on the same corridor, not part of this zone.
