# Evidence — San Clemente Island, Back Side

The observation layer behind
[San Clemente Island — Back Side](../san-clemente-island-back-side.md). One line
per observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `YZtX1MiT0y8` (BDOutdoors, Erik Landesfeind Bight report, 2022-04-21): a big
  northwest event crashed the northern Channel Islands and the LA/Ventura
  coastal shelf 5–8 °F while the Coronados, the tuna grounds, Catalina and San
  Clemente Island held their water — a same-event contrast, not a claim that
  this side is always sheltered.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `Z3rZqy2Pi8E` (BDOutdoors, 2022-11-24 report of a 2022-11-20 trip, San
  Clemente Island east end back side): about 8 mi off the island a 63 → 61.5 °F
  break inside roughly 1 mi carried sign on the edge with the fish deep and
  sonar-dependent.
- `Rb5I2ljAqeE` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-05-05 upload, "Top Gun 80 Epic 5 Day,
  part 1," a spot the crew names "desperation reef," called on camera the West
  End tuna zone): the boat anchored on the high spot reasoning the bite held
  there because bait squid were staged on the bank; the on-camera zone name sits
  against the spot library charting Desperation Reef at the southeast end of the
  back-side run — flagged, not reconciled; unregistered channel.

## What's there

- `YZtX1MiT0y8` (BDOutdoors, 2022-04-21): a squid fishery on the back side /
  east end with yellowtail at a 20–25 lb average, high teens to high 30s; when
  the squid drops deep or moves the bite dies but the fish have not left —
  relocate the forage on the meter before writing the zone off.
- `Z3rZqy2Pi8E` (BDOutdoors, 2022-11-24 report of a 2022-11-20 trip, San
  Clemente Island east end back side): after patterning bass on off-colour water
  with current running in toward the island, "a bunch of Bonita come up outside
  of the kelp… they're boiling around, jumping"; the fish came on DD100s and
  small chrome jigs on bass gear at two to three pounds, best "maybe six or
  seven pounds."
- `9xNhdu2aBqE` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-10-28, San Clemente Island West End): a
  green-stained, near-opaque water colour at roughly 5–10 ft visibility was
  called the preferred read for kelp-edge bass — the stain gives fish "a place to
  hide" and makes them more comfortable feeding up in the open — paired with
  moving current read off whether the kelp lies down under the surface or floats
  slack on top; the West End is charted in both this zone and the front-side
  zone's spot runs, so the side is not resolved by the source; unregistered
  channel.
- `r4J5nP5Bkl4` (StokedOnFishing, trip framed on camera as "this past June,"
  resolved from the 2024-04-26 upload to June 2023, backside San Clemente
  Island): a day-and-a-half El Dorado charter under Captain TJ — kelp-paddy
  dorado (roughly a half dozen on a picky, free-swimmer bite drawn bow-to-corner
  with live-bait chum) and mixed bluefin through the afternoon, a "sundown barn
  burner" to 100 lb, then a night jig session over a school stacked about
  198–330 ft; anglers geared for 50–70 lb fish were into 70–90 lb on 30 and 40 lb
  test, with multiple casualties including a leader broken boatside; unregistered
  channel.
- `Rb5I2ljAqeE` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-05-05 upload, desperation reef): flylined
  dead squid drew repeat bites in current-against-wind conditions on a half-ounce
  weight, session grade 40–90 lb bluefin with the best close to 90 lb; the crew
  also suspended flylined baits under a balloon for 30–40 lb tuna, and the same
  trip picked up yellowtail at 18–25 lb on a sardine flyline; unregistered
  channel.
- `sHnqSIOjTdM` (StokedOnFishing, August trip, year not stated on camera,
  2026-04-24 upload, San Clemente Island): an early-morning dropper-loop bite on
  40 lb at 100 ft took yellowtail on both live squid and sardine with squid
  called out as outfishing sardine, and the soak repeatedly picked up white
  seabass bycatch, each released because the boat had already reached its trip
  limit; the side of the island is not stated on camera; unregistered channel.

## How it fishes

- `sIoNELGlxmk` (dave-hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, PCS Show live seminar,
  2023-03-04): at San Clemente Island two named spots close enough to be a short
  run apart showed opposite current directions on the same day — one downhill
  toward San Diego, the other uphill — with no explained cause, framed as routine
  at this island; the specific landmark names were garbled beyond recovery by
  auto-captions and are not carried — asr-uncertain(spot names).
- `DnSqw4r7A1s` (BDOutdoors): anchored at San Clemente Island, chum gets pushed
  up-current toward the bow by the fish themselves — birds locking on up there
  mark the school and the surface iron goes under them; the side of the island is
  not stated.
