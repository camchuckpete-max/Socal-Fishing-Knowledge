# Evidence — The Boot (504) / 307

Provenance for [The Boot (504) / 307](../boot-504-307.md). **One source in the
corpus names anything in this zone, and it names the Boot.** Nothing names the
307; it is carried on its charted position alone. The other entries below are
general doctrine the zone page reads against these two positions, not
observations made on them.

## What's there

- `5to3Q5P7w90` (BDOutdoors, 2022-09-15 upload, Bight report of 2022-09-14,
  SoCal Bight): boats fishing the Masters marlin tournament over the preceding
  weekend "got a lot of marlin uh in by the boot," and another tournament was
  running the weekend that followed. The same report put the volume of fish in
  the square drawn between Santa Barbara Island, San Nicolas, San Clemente and
  Catalina, "where a lot of the bigger ones are" — a separate piece of water,
  spoken of separately from the marlin ground. No depth, bait, lure, spread,
  water temperature or colour is given for the marlin fishing.

## How it fishes

- `5to3Q5P7w90` (BDOutdoors, 2022-09-15 upload, Bight report of 2022-09-14,
  SoCal Bight): the fleet-read rule stated in the same segment — boats with
  outriggers out driving back over themselves in slow circles are marlin boats,
  not a tuna stop, so do not crash the pack and start trolling a Mad Mac through
  it: "if you're up that way it's probably the marlin guys." Carried as doctrine
  in [striped marlin](../../species/striped-marlin.md) and
  [October](../../seasonal/october.md); it is the only on-the-water instruction
  in the corpus that attaches to this water.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `Rf1HKJG-SDg` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2020-12-15, SoCal outer banks): a bank is
  an isolated high spot rising out of otherwise deep water and is named for the
  fathom depth of its shallowest point, at six feet to a fathom; the inshore
  mainland water from Dana Point through Newport and Marina del Rey out to
  Catalina is already 2,000–3,000 ft deep, the featureless bottom banks are
  contrasted against. Used here to read the 307 and the Boot's alias 504, neither
  of which the source itself names.
- `Blh2BA-7Ono` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-21, SoCal Bight report): the split between a
  50–60 square-mile complex you plan hours of looking across and the compact spot
  you idle onto, grade and leave inside 20–30 minutes. Neither the Boot nor the
  307 is named in that source; the zone page reads the pair's 9.3 nm separation
  against the split and flags the placement as unstated.

## Getting there

- `HzE4FHHcvTk` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-17, SoCal Bight report): the paddy-water
  split — best paddy water south of San Clemente Island and Catalina and along
  the coastal strip from Dana Point to Oceanside and southward, thinning north of
  Dana Point. Carried in full at
  [bight geography](../../locations/bight-geography.md); the zone page uses it
  only to place these two marks on the scarce side of the line, with no paddy
  observed over either.
