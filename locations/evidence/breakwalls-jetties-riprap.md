# Evidence — Break Walls, Jetties and Riprap

Per-source provenance backing [Break Walls, Jetties and Riprap](../breakwalls-jetties-riprap.md).

## Structure & bathymetry

- `bydQzE3F9yM` (BDOutdoors / SoCal Bight Fishing Academy Ep. 9, Erik
  Landesfeind, 2023-02-24): showed a photo of an unnamed riprap zone where
  uneven, out-jutting piles of concrete change the current; called a locally
  known big-bass zone, though the spot itself is not named on camera.

## What's there

- `RUuPNpK0x-s` (n6yun, Fred Hall calico bass panel discussion part 2,
  unregistered channel — medium confidence): a panelist described night
  sessions producing double-digit ("teen[s]" lb) calico bass at "that
  breakwater," against very few fish over roughly 4 lb caught there by day —
  called "night and day." Bait was doctored with a scent additive ("hot
  sauce"); the bait itself renders in captions only as "betty's baits," an
  ASR-garbled name not confidently resolved, so the live/cut-vs-artificial
  distinction is unconfirmed. The wall's kelp growth, not just the bare rock,
  was also credited with holding fish and producing bites after dark. No
  specific wall or region named beyond "that breakwater."
  ⚠ Fact-check (single-source): verified verbatim ("teen jumbos caught on
  that breakwater at night and I don't know a very many over four that have
  been caught during the day"; "the nighttime fishing on the brake wall is
  night and day") but the specific weight figures are corroborated nowhere
  else in this note's citations.
- `ZoIzpyyS9xw` (n6yun, Fred Hall calico bass panel discussion part 1,
  2013-03-09, unregistered channel, surname uncertain — `asr-uncertain` —
  medium confidence): the panelist ("Jack") who fishes this wall a lot at
  night names it specifically as the Long Beach federal breakwater —
  presumably the same session/wall as the `RUuPNpK0x-s` account above, though
  the two mentions are not explicitly cross-referenced on camera. He runs a
  5.5 in bait at night there — roughly half the size of a bigger bait he
  names in the same breath (brand garbled in the ASR, not confidently
  resolved) — calling the smaller size the one that "really works great" on
  the federal breakwater at night.
  ⚠ Fact-check (single-source): verified verbatim ("they've got one, it's
  about half, that's five-five inch, it really works great on that with
  federal breakwater") but the 5.5 in figure is corroborated nowhere else in
  this note's citations.

## How it fishes

- `XyKF34C5iB4` (Time On The Water panel, Pacific Coast Sportfishing seminar,
  2025-04-19, unregistered channel — medium confidence): one panelist credits
  a tip from a scuba/freediving contact about a wall's underwater composition
  with pointing him toward walls holding "really big" calico bass he would
  not otherwise have picked — one instance of the broader "network with
  divers/anglers for structure intel" habit rather than fishing every wall
  cold.
