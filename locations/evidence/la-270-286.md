# Evidence — LA - 270 / 286

Provenance for [LA - 270 / 286](../la-270-286.md). **No source in the corpus
names LA - 270 or the 286.** The entries below describe the water this zone sits
in — the LA-side bluefin ground off Catalina, worked out of Long Beach and San
Pedro — or are general doctrine the zone page reads against these two charted
positions. None of them is an observation made on either mark.

## What's there

- `CMQkHQMxbXM` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-04 upload, Bight report of 2022-08-03,
  SoCal Bight): the week's guaranteed-bluefin move, stated in hindsight after a
  trip that took mixed bluefin and yellowfin elsewhere, was to have launched out
  of Long Beach and run up to the area that had been biting "up by santa barbara
  and north west of catalina." The same segment's weather note listed the water
  a forecast wind event was expected to blow out: "the channel island, san
  clemente island, nick, sbi, the tuna zone off cat and sbi." Neither LA - 270
  nor the 286 is named; the description is by port and direction.
- `5to3Q5P7w90` (BDOutdoors, 2022-09-15 upload, Bight report of 2022-09-14,
  SoCal Bight): the Channel Islands bluefin were called out as a separate body of
  fish from the LA-side fishery — "those aren't the same fish they're fishing
  down out of long beach and san pedro either there's just more fish in between
  here and there scattered out." The same report placed the volume of bigger fish
  in the square between Santa Barbara Island, San Nicolas, San Clemente and
  Catalina. No position is given for the Long Beach / San Pedro fish.

## Getting there

- `CMQkHQMxbXM` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-04 upload, Bight report of 2022-08-03,
  SoCal Bight): the port advice attached to that bluefin zone — it "is pretty far
  from san diego so if you want to if you live up towards long beach or l.a. …
  i'd look for a boat that's got running up here that's going there because you
  have a lot less time in the car and you have a shorter boat ride."
- `DnSqw4r7A1s` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-14, SoCal Bight report): the boats expected
  to work San Nicolas that weekend were "some of the overnight boats out of uh
  out of long beach or san pedro may run up that way" — a forecast of fleet
  movement, not a report of one. Cited on the zone page only as evidence of
  which ports work this half of the Bight.
- `S2L3KLSQ6Is` (BDOutdoors, 2022-05-26, SoCal Bight report): an exploratory
  offshore look for early-season tuna is "an expensive run out there out of long
  beach or newport to go look," with the advice to do it before the wind started.
  Again a port-level statement, with no mark named.
- `zKmZ4zql2ws` (BDOutdoors, SoCal Bight Fishing Academy ep. 6, marine
  forecasts): where current accelerates over a ridge or shoal the swell stands up
  locally — the worked example is the ridge off Catalina's east end, rough for a
  few miles and laying back down three or four miles past it, with the
  instruction to look at the chart for underwater topography when it turns rough
  unexpectedly. Carried in full at [sea state](../../conditions/sea-state.md);
  the zone page uses it only to say that two isolated high spots in this corridor
  are the kind of feature to annotate on a transit.
- `MUpvP-Yl2R0` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-11-23, Catalina Island) and
  `L_FD-UzvEio` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-02-04, bait management): the Catalina
  crossing as roughly a 30 mi run and about 2.5 hours under way, neither tied to
  a named departure port. Carried at
  [Catalina front side](../catalina-island-front-side.md); used here only as an
  unverified ceiling, because these marks lie north of the island rather than
  beyond it.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `Rf1HKJG-SDg` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2020-12-15, SoCal outer banks): a bank is
  an isolated high spot rising out of otherwise deep water and is named for the
  fathom depth of its shallowest point, at six feet to a fathom; the inshore
  mainland water from Dana Point through Newport and Marina del Rey out to
  Catalina is already 2,000–3,000 ft deep, the mostly featureless bottom banks
  are contrasted against. That corridor is the water these two marks sit in, at
  its Catalina end. Used to read the names 270 and 286 as fathoms; the source
  names neither mark.
- `Blh2BA-7Ono` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-21, SoCal Bight report): the split between a
  50–60 square-mile complex you plan hours of looking across and the compact spot
  you idle onto, grade and leave inside 20–30 minutes, with 302, 371 and
  "west-of-Catalina" named as compact. Neither mark here is named, and both sit
  north of the West End rather than west of the island, so the page reads the
  6.6 nm separation against the split and flags the placement as unstated.

## How it fishes

- `YZtX1MiT0y8` (BDOutdoors, 2022-04-21, Bight report with Erik Landesfeind):
  the NW wind-rollover map — the Channel Islands took the most wind and the
  coast above Newport, Long Beach, Marina del Rey and the Ventura shelf "got hit
  pretty hard," water temp down into the 50s and "seven maybe eight degrees off
  from where it was," while Clemente, Catalina and the Coronados tuna grounds
  "held up pretty well." Carried in full at
  [bight geography](../bight-geography.md); the zone page uses it only to place
  these two marks relative to the line, with nothing observed at either.
- `HzE4FHHcvTk` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-18 upload, Bight report of 2022-08-17,
  SoCal Bight): the paddy-water split — best south of San Clemente Island or
  south of Catalina and along the beach below Dana Point towards Oceanside,
  with paddies "fewer and further between" and "not always holding" further up.
  Carried in full at [bight geography](../bight-geography.md); the zone page
  uses it only to place these two marks relative to that line.
- `OYOda6T3f-8` (BDOutdoors, 2022-10-20, Bight report): the fall warm-band arc,
  backside Santa Cruz / Rosa to Santa Barbara Island to backside Catalina and on
  to the 499 and Clemente. Carried at
  [bight geography](../bight-geography.md); the zone page measures the 286
  against the arc's Catalina leg and its 499 terminus without claiming either
  mark sits on it.
