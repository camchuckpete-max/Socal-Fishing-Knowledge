# Evidence — Channel Islands

Provenance for [Channel Islands](../channel-islands.md). **One on-the-water day
and a run of dated weekly Bight reports reach this island group in the corpus**;
the rest is tackle-briefing material scoped to the trip out of Ventura and
Oxnard. Every entry here is also carried under its species or technique, and is
repeated rather than moved.

## Getting there

  More," 2026-07-23, Channel Islands out of Ventura; Channel Islands Yacht Club
  / Freedom Boat Club charter): the day starts just outside Ventura, makes a
  tank of squid on the way out and runs to the islands; unregistered channel,
  single mention — medium confidence.

## What's there

- `6-8KfjEg0x8` (BDOutdoors, 2026-07-23, Channel Islands out of Ventura): a
  squid tank made before running out produced "monster-sized whitefish to start
  it off" on the shallow stop, which also lost a pair of sheephead; the boat then
  moved to one ~175 ft rock for reds, blue bass, bocaccio and short lingcod. The
  day's stated goals were yellowtail, white seabass and halibut, with "a lot of
  bass, barracuda, bonito, the three B's around." Blue bass is named as an
  islands fish you do not get in San Diego. Also carried in
  [ocean whitefish](../../species/ocean-whitefish.md),
  [sheephead](../../species/sheephead.md) and
  [rockfish & lingcod](../../species/rockfish-lingcod.md).
- `HnqiE05vdXs` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-31, Channel Islands): the weekly report
  opens here — "some sign of sea bass on this new moon we're having right now —
  the Aloha Spirit had 22 fish up that way the other day," hedged in the same
  segment as "could be a one-off, we'll have to wait and see if the fish bite
  again" — then "nice warm water up that way plenty of bass body and stuff like
  that probably find a marlin up that way if you're gonna look for one." Carried
  in [white seabass](../../species/white-seabass.md) and
  [striped marlin](../../species/striped-marlin.md).
- `DnSqw4r7A1s` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-14, Channel Islands / Santa Monica Bay /
  open beach): island anglers getting calicos and "nice barracuda"; carried in
  [barracuda](../../species/barracuda.md), and the same report's full-moon island
  white seabass line in [white seabass](../../species/white-seabass.md).
- `-JZpyWLdKlk` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-11, Channel Islands): slow fishing early in
  the week produced barracuda and occasional yellowtail; heading into the full
  moon the report listed rockfish, "a few barracudas," and white seabass that
  "might bite a couple more days on this moon" — the moon claim attached to the
  seabass, not the barracuda.
- `CMQkHQMxbXM` (BDOutdoors — Erik Landesfeind, SoCal Bight report, 2022-08-03):
  "still some barracuda caught" at Santa Cruz and Anacapa on a week the zone was
  reachable only aboard a private boat, with the fleet offshore and island
  reporting thinning out; the same report carries August bluefin dispersal from
  Catalina to San Nicolas to Ventura.
- `Blh2BA-7Ono` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-21): bluefin scattering off the counts
  "might be sliding up to the backside of Anacapa and Santa Cruz islands," and
  with wind forecast up the northern islands for the coming weekend, Santa Cruz
  and Anacapa were called "plenty fishable" while Santa Rosa "might be an issue
  for private boats" — stated as one weekend's read with the advice to check the
  weather before leaving the dock, not as a standing rule.
- `5to3Q5P7w90` (BDOutdoors, 2022-09-14): with yellowfin sliding north and
  bluefin foaming in 76 °F water, "you might be going bluefin fishing on the
  front side of Anacapa Island next ... they've certainly been up there in the
  past."

## How it fishes

- `6-8KfjEg0x8` (BDOutdoors, 2026-07-23): "we did not have much current, which at
  the islands is just as important as the bait you're fishing, really — the
  conditions make or break it out here," said after the shallow stop; the stated
  answer is combining a dropper loop with a sliding sinker to cover the whole
  column when marks are scattered. Carried in
  [rockfish deep-dropping](../../techniques/rockfish-deep-dropping.md).
- `42A8Owhc8fw` (Ray Sharifi, Dirty Hookers, 2023-08-02): a Channel Islands fleet
  trip where the full-day boats were not yet connecting on the bluefin foamers —
  the small-lead-bait picks were a Daiwa SK jig, a Shimano Colt Sniper and RW
  Peanuts in 80 g and 100 g; `ray-sharifi` registered, SoCal-capped — medium.
  Carried in [foamer casting](../../techniques/foamer-casting.md).
