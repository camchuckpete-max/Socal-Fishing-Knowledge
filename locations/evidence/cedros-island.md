# Evidence — Cedros / San Benitos

The observation layer behind
[Cedros / San Benitos](../cedros-island.md). One line per observation.
Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## How it fishes

- `IdMJnlg_rZk` / `2cxeg_AE418` / `_MygUHw19q8` (Strictly Irons, 2024 four-day
  trip): the half-day arrival, the run south on middle days, the 05:20/08:30
  departure-morning window with four fish taken in it; the crew moved their
  start earlier after seeing another boat hooked up on a 43 lb fish at
  07:00–08:00 and had about five fish hanging by 06:30; the 17:40 late
  school; the mid-day 5.5 in mini-iron calico filler — "it's raining calicos
  out here"; locked drag and the leverage call; the soft take; the jig-size and
  flat-profile argument; "don't look at the videos and think it's easy."
- `6EDQtQHEwFE` (StokedOnFishing, October 2024 trip, 2025-03-07 upload, Cedros
  Island, part 1): the trip's surface-iron bite was called among the best the
  crew had had at the island and was fished start-to-finish on the iron alone,
  with multi-year regulars of 8–10 years rating it some of the best fishing
  they had ever had; repeated sight-casts to individually spotted fish
  ("spotted him, pointed at him, casted on him") under working birds; a hooked
  fish grabbed through the gut by a seal boatside; a roughly 30-minute closing
  blitz under gulls including a triple hookup after the day had been called
  done; unregistered channel.
- `ILBl12Jm7-0` (StokedOnFishing, October 2024 trip, 2025-03-15 upload, part 2;
  the crew's fifth consecutive mid-October trip): day two opened running and
  gunning between fresh boils with boats hooking up within minutes of a stop
  and a "mob of forktails" called some of the trip's most exciting fishing; by
  midday the wind had picked up significantly and pushed the crew off the
  morning grounds onto a calico point; the straight-braid fatigue argument, a
  mono/fluoro top shot broken off in kelp twice, and a casting-past-the-knot
  thumb slice are logged in
  [line & leader](../../tackle/line-and-leader.md); unregistered channel.
- `u0scEBby7nA` (StokedOnFishing, October 2024 trip, 2025-03-22 upload, part 3):
  day two closed with surface iron and yo-yo both working, the crew nearly
  limiting in about two hours at one stop, and a 44 lb personal best on a
  custom iron called "The Lucky Dave"; by session's end surface iron, dropper
  loop and yo-yo all produced simultaneously — "you name it, they're biting";
  unregistered channel.
- `QSmE3mdEL28` (StokedOnFishing, 2021-10 trip, 2022-06-05 upload, Cedros
  Island): a shallow-water program on resident fish — a rock cresting at 28 ft
  called the day's "perfect" biting depth, fish also worked down to 30 ft — run
  on slow-trolled live mackerel and circle hooks, with the crew fielding an
  80–100 lb line class as standing gear; a ledger row is filed on that bracket
  as a single-crew build; unregistered channel.
- `I-QBxuV2p7M` (StokedOnFishing, 2021-10 trip, 2022-06-15 upload, day two
  continuing `QSmE3mdEL28`): the morning bait run targeted bigger mackerel and

  mackerel and go back to their hole"; the troll worked over roughly 30 ft and
  produced zero yellowtail bites all day at the same spot that had fired the
  day before — "every single day is different here" — and the crew diverted to

  overhead, wide-open on surface iron, "catching them pretty much every cast";
  unregistered channel.
- `Zo92MG459gQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-05-24 upload, Cedros Island, continuing
