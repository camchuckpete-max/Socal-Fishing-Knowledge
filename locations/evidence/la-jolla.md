# Evidence — La Jolla

The observation layer behind [La Jolla](../la-jolla.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `VpW91AKOFVQ` (Capt. Scotty, Brothers Sportfishing, via Roman Castro,
  2020-01-28, La Jolla): the boat ran an open-party half-day a.m. charter on
  the zone, came in, and went straight back out on an afternoon session to the
  same spot and zone — the whole day inside a charter's normal half-day
  turnaround. No port, ramp or run time named on camera. Registered voice
  (`capt-scotty-brothers`).

## Structure & bathymetry

- `yLpDI8jnizU` (BDOutdoors / Capt. Duane Diego Mellor, Pinnacle Sportfishing,
  2020-07-29, La Jolla — the Northwest Corner): running up to the spot on a
  split GPS/sounder screen, yellowtail expected as big arcs at five to ten
  fathoms mid-screen with bait around them; the pass over the spot marked a
  little bit of yellowtail down at 90 ft "on the increment into the reef,"
  which is what the boat then turned back on to cast at. Bottom
  discrimination was demonstrated on the same run — a picture of gravel, rock
  or sand directly below, used to find the highways where hard bottom meets
  soft reef and pinnacles. Registered voice (`duane-diego-mellor`). Harvested
  from
  [electronics and the sounder](../../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).
- `_ZThckj2TIM` (Capt. Scotty, Brothers Sportfishing, via Roman Castro,
  2020-04-04, La Jolla): the day's ground was 180–200 ft, called at 35 fathoms
  / 210 ft when the drift was set up; the sounder (Raymarine, 50 kHz) showed
  bottom rising into a high spot with a rock pile and fuzz — schools — hanging
  off the structure, then dropping off again. Registered voice
  (`capt-scotty-brothers`). Harvested from
  [rockfish deep-dropping](../../techniques/rockfish-deep-dropping.md).
- `xI9tPJFXbUM` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, 2019-08-30, SoCal
  inshore): La Jolla named alongside San Onofre and Point Loma as the stand-in
  "the kelp bed" when teaching inshore fishing — a mention of the zone as a
  type example, with no La Jolla-specific structure detail. Registered voice.

## What's there

- `VpW91AKOFVQ` (Capt. Scotty, Brothers Sportfishing, via Roman Castro,
  2020-01-28, La Jolla): the morning half-day hooked five yellowtail and
  boated three — one bite on the surface iron that pulled, three landed on the
  yo-yo, one more lost on the yo-yo — weighed at 15 lb, 17 lb and about 18 lb,
  with two of the three taken on a red-crab-pattern iron; the captain's own
  read was that the angler mattered more than the jig colour that day. The
  afternoon session was run specifically to try for one on the surface iron.
  Registered voice. Also logged at
  [surface iron — colour](../../techniques/surface-iron-color.md).
- `_ZThckj2TIM` (Capt. Scotty, Brothers Sportfishing, via Roman Castro,
  2020-04-04, La Jolla): the April drift produced a mixed bag on squid —
  bocaccio ("salmon grouper") early on the light bite, vermilion and other
  reds once baits were put on the rock, and a sculpin handled carefully for
  its venomous spines, with an undersized fish sent back down on a
  wire descending device. Registered voice. Harvested from
  [rockfish & lingcod](../../species/rockfish-lingcod.md).
- `-eaaWPN5Fxk` (BDOutdoors / SoCal Bight Fishing Academy Ep. 7, 2023-02-10,
  San Diego deep structure): the slug-off-the-side approach is introduced with
  "down in San Diego you take a slug down to those artificial reefs, La Jolla,
  and, uh, or whatever that's called down there" — asr-uncertain(the phrasing
  runs the San Diego artificial reefs and La Jolla together in one breath, so
  it is not clear whether the presenter means the built reefs, the La Jolla
  hard bottom, or both). Kept scoped to "San Diego deep-structure bass ground"
  on the zone page for that reason. Registered voice.

## How it fishes

- `kzD0kSnnVPw` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, 2019-04-07): at a
  named kelp bed — La Jolla listed with San Onofre, Point Loma, San Clemente
  Island and Catalina — current direction picks the corner: downhill current →
  the northwest corner, uphill → the southeast corner, with the same logic
  carried to rock structure. Registered voice, single mention of this framing.
  Harvested from
  [current structure](../../conditions/current-structure.md).
- `yLpDI8jnizU` (BDOutdoors / Capt. Duane Diego Mellor, 2020-07-29, La Jolla
  Northwest Corner): the Furuno TZtouch history-playback workflow demonstrated
  on this spot — swipe the screen back, tap the marks that already scrolled
  past, create a waypoint, turn around on autopilot and re-run them — followed
  by casting a jig at the re-found fish. Registered voice.
- `Ec2GaNpiOHI` (Strictly Irons / Dan Wade Q&A, 2019-03-19): asked whether his
  local surface-iron personal best came from "La Jolla, Coronado," the answer
  moves to casting position and to North Island landmarks at the Coronados
  without giving a La Jolla figure — logged so the mention is not read as La
  Jolla data. Unregistered channel — medium confidence.
