# Evidence — Oceanside / North County

The observation layer behind
[Oceanside / North County](../oceanside-north-county.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `XyKF34C5iB4` (Time On The Water panel, 2025-04-19, SoCal walls): Oceanside
  named on camera in the list of fishable SoCal break walls beyond the Long
  Beach federal breakwater, in a segment aimed at anglers without boat access;
  no detail given on the Oceanside wall itself. Unregistered channel — medium
  confidence. Harvested from
  [break walls, jetties and riprap](../breakwalls-jetties-riprap.md).

## Structure & bathymetry

- `HMdrP4-i9MM` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-10-11, off North County San Diego near
  Camp Pendleton): hard bottom carrying light kelp growth that did not reach
  the surface — kelp riding roughly 20 ft off the bottom in ~40–42 ft of
  water. Unregistered channel — medium confidence. Harvested from
  [calico bass](../../species/calico-bass.md).
- `Kf5wk_TFgTc` (BDOutdoors / Erik Landesfeind, Bight report, 2025-02-14):
  sewer and outfall pipes described as running all up and down the SoCal
  coast and as the winter deep-structure option, with the Buccaneer Pipe named
  among the *larger*-structure examples. asr-uncertain(the two pipe names
  spoken either side of it render as "San An pipe" and "Imperial pipe," and it
  is not clear from the captions which pipe the name "Buccaneer" is attached
  to) — the Buccaneer Pipe is a charted spot in this zone (cameron), the
  Imperial Beach Pipe is not. Registered voice.
- `xI9tPJFXbUM` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, 2019-08-30, SoCal
  inshore): San Onofre named alongside La Jolla and Point Loma as the stand-in
  "the kelp bed" when teaching inshore fishing. Registered voice.
- `BdRX4b8Fo5w` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, 2021-02-04, bait tanks):
  the coast at San Onofre used as the coastal counterpart to San Clemente
  Island when arguing for maximum bait capacity. Registered voice. Harvested
  from [chunking](../../techniques/chunking.md).
- `0KQ--N5TjqE` (BDOutdoors / SoCal Bight Fishing Academy, Palos Verdes) and
  `OYOda6T3f-8` (BDOutdoors, 2022-10-20, Palos Verdes): both name a "Flat
  Rock" beside "shark fin" / "below Rocky" in the Palos Verdes outside kelp —
  a different place from this zone's Flat Rock off Del Mar. Logged here so the
  collision is not silently read across. Harvested from
  [barracuda](../../species/barracuda.md).

## What's there

- `HMdrP4-i9MM` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-10-11, near Camp Pendleton): an annual
  Okuma-employee kids/family trip aboard a Dana Wharf Sportfishing charter out
  of Dana Point, working south down the coast; calico bass graded up through
  the session — repeated 5 lb fish "one after another" and a best of 9 lb
  against a hoped-for double-digit — with sand bass mixed in, including one
  angler's first fish ever. The crew's pre-trip game plan also flagged
  barracuda and 25–30 lb "green fish" (yellowtail) recently seen on the same
  stretch of coast, neither of which the day produced.
  ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): no bass weight is spoken in
  `HMdrP4-i9MM` — its only size talk is short-fish legal checks ("12 and a
  half… a little short", "he's got to be 14") — and the repeated 5 lb "one
  after another" plus a 9 lb best against a hoped-for double-digit belongs to
  `qri15R3caYE`, the StokedOnFishing **La Bocana, Baja** trip logged in
  `species/evidence/calico-bass.md`. Unregistered channel —
  medium confidence. Harvested from
  [calico bass](../../species/calico-bass.md) and
  [sand bass](../../species/sand-bass.md).
- `HzE4FHHcvTk` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-17, Bight): the best paddy fishing ran
  south of San Clemente Island or Catalina and along the beach below Dana
  Point toward Oceanside; further north the paddies were fewer and held less,
  and paddies close to a dock were likely already driven over. A read on the
  open water offshore of this strip, not on its coastal spots. Registered
  voice. Harvested from
  [kelp paddies](../../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

## How it fishes

- `kzD0kSnnVPw` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, 2019-04-07): at a
  named kelp bed — San Onofre given first among his examples, with Point Loma,
  La Jolla, San Clemente Island and Catalina — current direction picks the
  corner: downhill current → the northwest corner, uphill → the southeast
  corner, and the same logic carries to rock structure. Registered voice,
  single mention of this framing. Harvested from
  [current structure](../../conditions/current-structure.md).
- `sIoNELGlxmk` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, PCS Show live
  seminar, 2023-03-04): the same doctrine walked through live at the San
  Onofre bed — fronds laid pointing toward San Diego read as downhill current
  — with a trout-stream analogy for why the front, up-current edge holds the
  bigger fish once current runs, plus an explicit "anchor the spot, don't
  drift through it" call for kelp. Second dated mention by the same voice.
  Harvested from [current structure](../../conditions/current-structure.md).
- `VpW91AKOFVQ` (Capt. Scotty, Brothers Sportfishing, via Roman Castro,
  2020-01-28): the smaller mainland-coast schools from Del Mar down to
  Imperial Beach run markedly more finicky on the surface iron than a big
  Coronado Islands school on the same gear, especially on bigger-grade fish.
  Registered voice. Harvested from
  [surface iron](../../techniques/surface-iron.md).
- `Kf5wk_TFgTc` (BDOutdoors / Erik Landesfeind, 2025-02-14): winter bass
  taxonomy — kelp vs deep structure vs shallow coast — with sand bass mostly
  on deep structure and rarely in the kelp, and the deeper structure called
  the most reliable cold-season bite; isolated structure (a wreck, a small
  reef) is fished vertically, larger structure (a coastal pipe, a big
  artificial-reef system) is covered by casting around and moving. The
  vertical builds shown were an 8 in slug on a spring-head lead with
  free-swinging assist hooks and a slow-pitch jig, both fished on the fall
  with a burn-and-drop follow-up. Registered voice. Harvested from
  [February–March](../../seasonal/february-march.md).
- `HMdrP4-i9MM` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-10-11, near Camp Pendleton): crew set
  the kids on a dropper loop specifically to avoid casting — drop straight
  down and reel — over the ~40–42 ft hard bottom, with live sardine and squid
  or sardine strip; a straight flylined sardine was called out on camera as
  producing a kid's fish. Unregistered channel — medium confidence. Harvested
  from [dropper loop](../../techniques/dropper-loop.md) and
  [flyline](../../techniques/flyline.md).
