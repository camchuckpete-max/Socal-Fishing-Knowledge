# Evidence — San Quintin

The observation layer behind [San Quintin](../san-quintin.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## What's there

- `shZCjX2-fkI` (SearcherSportfishing, Art and Armando, 2021-01-14, "Tackle
  Talk Live: Bluefin Tuna," San Martin Island): boats on long trips just
  outside San Martin Island had giant schools of big bluefin about ten days
  before the 2021-01-14 taping — the fish "didn't bite all that well" but stuck
  with the boat, giving opportunities; framed as the bluefin having moved a
  little further south and as the reason the operation expected to start its
  spring trip season earlier than the scheduled 1 April readiness date; the
  63–65 °F figure given in the same conversation is for that January presence;
  registered channel.
- `fgTmUq78ofQ` (Art Taylor and Mike, SearcherSportfishing via Turner's
  Outdoorsman, 2021-05-14): with the fleet working one area west of San Diego
  and two west of Ensenada, nothing was running to Colonet or San Quintín
  because the closer grounds were producing; both captains describe the water
  structure at Colonet/San Quintín as historically productive early in the
  modern SoCal bluefin fishery and expect it to still hold fish if the
  structure persists; the wider resident-stock reading in the same seminar is
  flagged single-source on [bluefin tuna](../../species/bluefin-tuna.md);
  registered channel.
- `L3tkGVu516A` (StokedOnFishing, 2014-11-13, San Martin Island then Dono,
  "MC Swimbaits Skiff Trip part 1"): the crew checked San Martin first thing,
  passed on it — conditions "didn't look all that great" for staying — and ran
  down the coast to Dono and Sacramento Reef, explicitly after **bigger**
  calico, noting a lot of spots along that coast hold calico but smaller fish;
  the first three-hour skiff rotation at Dono, on slug and weedless swimbait,
  gave "three personal bests" at 7 lb → 8 lb → 9 lb plus a 9.5 lb fish on the
  scale, with a "biggest" restated as 10.2 lb in a garbled second debrief
  (ASR-uncertain), and the stop called "fishing where world records live";
  the bite pattern was first drifts "a little scratchy," then every cast once
  the skiff came around the corner at the upper part of the island where the
  current ran down the side; a yellowtail on a plastic "basically spooled" a
  200-size reel and was lost; a sheephead came on a very slow wind — "as slow
  as I can turn the handle" — and one fish came unbuttoned with kelp slid all
  the way down the weedless hook's shank; the trip ran off the 90-ft mothership
  *Shogun* with skiffs and a 20-ft center console, six days inshore;
  unregistered channel.
- `_r_qKX_7080` (StokedOnFishing, 2014-11-13, "MC Swimbaits Skiff Trip part 2"):
  the crew left after "two days of awesome calico bass fishing at Dono" to
  slide down the beach to Chester's Rock, giving Dono as 188 miles from San
  Diego and Chester's Rock a further 112, "300 miles from San Diego" — the only
  distance figures in the corpus for this stretch, units never stated;
  unregistered channel.
