- `0KQ--N5TjqE`: "Shark Fin" — a rock outjet the narrator also calls "Flat

- `0KQ--N5TjqE`: Rat's Beach — usually the most stable, coolest water in the
  bay through summer, gaining another half-to-one degree in the transitional
  months; current tends to go slack there.
- `0KQ--N5TjqE`: back near the original Rocky Point-area bite — current that
