# Evidence — San Clemente Island, Front Side

The observation layer behind
[San Clemente Island — Front Side](../san-clemente-island-front-side.md). One
line per observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `iQLyBzhOSi8` (StokedOnFishing, "California Offshore Challenge, part 1," an
  on-camera slate reading 2012 against the 2014-11-11 upload date used elsewhere
  in this KB for the same tournament, Catalina and San Clemente Island): with
  swell tracked at 5–7 ft the day before day one, 4–6 ft on day one and 3–5 ft
  forecast for the following Sunday, and a small craft advisory over part of the
  field, one team ran for San Clemente Island rather than staying at Catalina
  while citing "some really big swells" for that crossing; unregistered channel.

## Structure & bathymetry

- `KCcEqHSZ84k` (dave-hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, 2019-06-13, Northwest
  Harbor, San Clemente Island): anchored tight to island rock in about 6 ft of
  water on a feature named on camera as "Coiler Rock" — asr-uncertain, the name
  is not independently confirmed — fishing sardines, with calico to 8 lb taken
  on that pattern; a 2019-05-26 session at another shallow rock inside the same
  harbor, named on camera as "boiler Rock" and possibly the same feature, found
  unusually calm, clear water for the island at 30–40 ft visibility, with fish
  taken very shallow and constant re-tying needed because break-offs on the rock
  were the recurring problem.
- `sIoNELGlxmk` (dave-hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, PCS Show live seminar,
  2023-03-04): at San Clemente Island two named spots close enough to be a short
  run apart showed opposite current directions on the same day — one downhill
  toward San Diego, the other uphill — with no explained cause, framed as
  routine at this island; the landmark names were garbled beyond recovery by
  auto-captions and are not carried — asr-uncertain(spot names).

## What's there

- `Blh2BA-7Ono` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-21, San Clemente Island front side, per a
  Thunderbird captain's report): the front-side yellowtail bite is "a morning
  thing" that "kind of slows down, becomes a pick-o day, bass, barracuda stuff
  like that in the mix of bonita" — get there early if yellowtail is the target;
  the same report sizes the below-Clemente complex (43, 181, 182, 289, Clemente
  Ridge up to Mackerel Bank) as one ~50–60 sq mi search box against compact
  spot-check structure such as the 302, 371 and west-of-Catalina.
- `yuXr3IJ8ybg` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-06-09, Pyramid Head, San Clemente
  Island): a sponsor-heavy Okuma gear demo — live squid produced a mixed
  calico/bonito/yellowtail bite, a bonito school boiling around the boat was
  called "a good sign" with yellowtail joining shortly after, the crew's
  call-the-species-by-feel tells are undercut by the same footage and are not
  carried as an ID method, no white seabass was landed despite squid and a
  stated seabass target, and one hooked yellowtail was lost boatside to a seal
  working the boat; unregistered channel.
- `nsUdT-zXI8s` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-12-07 upload, front side of San Clemente
  Island): a free overnight charity charter for 15 military veterans aboard the
  El Dorado — picking at yellowtail plus short bass on flylined sardine, "a lot
  of Bonita" through the day, day's baits called the right size for roughly a
  2/0–3/0 hook on 30 lb line with anglers going heavier or lighter by
  preference, and the crew's "famous" resident sea lion took one good-size
  yellowtail that morning, leaving them "in the negative" on catch-to-hook;
  unregistered channel.
- `Ow3an9lSVh4` (StokedOnFishing, 2014-11-03, off Pyramid Head, east end of San
  Clemente Island, "Stoked on Simrad, part 3"): a two-day trip found tuna on
  temperature breaks in open water on day one and a better grade of yellowfin
  right off the island's east end the next morning, fished alongside a fleet of
  long-range boats the presenter says normally work 150–200 miles south of the
  border — their presence in SoCal water that trip was attributed on camera to
  "this El Niño," a dated 2014 regional pattern rather than a repeated rule;
  sponsor-heavy, unregistered channel.
- `Klfb433I3Uk` (StokedOnFishing, 2019-10-04, San Clemente Island): a barracuda
  caught on a yellowtail charter, called on camera before the fish was
  identified; the side of the island is not stated; unregistered channel.
- `iQLyBzhOSi8` (StokedOnFishing, "California Offshore Challenge, part 1,"
  San Clemente Island): the team's landing put them at Northwest Harbor, where
  the water was dirty and washed out with conditions described as coming from
  "a different direction than we normally get" and a little current noted
  running along the front side; the team then worked back north from the harbor
  and down the front side of the island; no visibility or current-strength
  figure is given and the "different direction" remark is not tied to current
  rather than swell or wind; unregistered channel.

## How it fishes

- `KCcEqHSZ84k` (dave-hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, 2019-06-13, Northwest
  Harbor): once a calico is hooked this shallow and this tight to rock, hold the
  rod tip as high as possible and wind as fast as possible without ever pumping
  the rod — a dropped tip lets the fish bury its head into the rock and root the
  leader off; line was 65 lb braid straight to a 30 lb fluorocarbon top shot,
  lighter than the 40–65 lb fluoro logged for kelp swimbait work and kept beside
  it rather than reconciled.
- `iQLyBzhOSi8` (StokedOnFishing, "California Offshore Challenge, part 1,"
  Northwest Harbor / front side, San Clemente Island, day one): once a producing
  spot was located, the angler was coached to drop straight down to the bottom,
  wind 10 or 15 turns up, then free-spool back down and repeat; the lure type is
  not named on camera, and the turn count sits beside — not merged into — the
  10-crank yo-yo cadence logged for yellowtail on iron; unregistered channel.
- `Ow3an9lSVh4` (StokedOnFishing, 2014-11-03, off Pyramid Head): on the second
  day, with a better grade of yellowfin wanting the sardine deeper than the day
  before, the crew added a half-ounce to three-quarter-ounce rubber-core and/or
  sliding sinker to flylined sardines to get them down, and butt-hooked the
  fly-lined baits, called out on camera as making the bait "swim down deep";
  sponsor-heavy, unregistered channel.
- `V_ONnegk95M` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-05-04, San Clemente Island): the
  captain's pre-trip briefing called the evening bite window at about 5:00–5:45
  pm and told anglers to start soaking baits then, with stated tackle of 30 lb
  test to about a 3/0 circle hook on 30 lb fluorocarbon, drags set before
  fishing, and bait picked for intact scales, good colour and slime; the side of
  the island is not stated on camera; unregistered channel.
