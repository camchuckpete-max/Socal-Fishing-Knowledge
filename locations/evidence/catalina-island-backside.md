# Evidence — Catalina Island, Backside

The observation layer behind
[Catalina Island — Backside](../catalina-island-backside.md). One line per
observation. Observations never change the note's stated doctrine.

## Getting there

- `PKf7G3uL4io` (StokedOnFishing, "California Offshore Challenge, part 2," event
  ~early November 2014 per the 2014-11-11 upload, Catalina, day two): a team
  started day two running the West End, found it "just blowing," found it worse
  the further back along the backside they went, turned around and retreated to
  shelter on the front side; no swell direction given on camera; unregistered
  channel.
- `aFb221LUoD0` (Your Saltwater Guide, Capt. Dave Hansen, 2020-10-20, Catalina
  game-plan process): swell direction read first — a big west swell sends
  anglers to the east end rather than the front side, backside or middle, while
  a south swell tears up the east end; built from FishDope charting for
  chlorophyll/temperature and Buoy Weather for swell; registered voice.

## How it fishes

- `aFb221LUoD0` (Your Saltwater Guide, Capt. Dave Hansen, 2020-10-20, Catalina):
  in a southeast wind a backside spot he calls "Freddy's Beach" on camera stops
  producing (name unverified against the spot library, and no orientation or
  facing detail is given for it), while spots where the boat lies stern-toward
  the island's west end turn on — Salta Verde and Catalina's China Point named
  as southeast-wind producers; the spot list also carries a distinct San
  Clemente Island "China Point," and this claim is about Catalina's; registered
  voice.
- `mXu8vJ8yr4Q` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-09-15, Catalina Island backside): a
  weedless-rigged swimbait fished repeatedly through kelp boilers — the crew's
  fourth or fifth trip that same week on the same lead head — was said to "run
  out of paint on the head before you'll lose it," coming through the kelp clean
  without popping off, and the angler reported never losing a fish to how much
  the weedless hook flexes; unregistered channel.
- `ouBrIdO7d4k` (SD Fish and Sips, 2025-03-08 upload, filmed 2025-03-06, Pacific
  Coast Sportfishing Show booth interview; the SoCal charter captain speaking is
  not identifiable from the undiarized transcript and is logged unnamed):
  recapping half-day charters run mostly around Catalina, he described a
  standing method for dolphin-associated yellowfin on the backside during the
  lobster opener, dated on camera as "first week of October, end of September" —
  a King Buster-style finesse trolling lure re-headed with a tungsten 1/2 oz
  head and a small Yamashita stainless double hook, trolled on 60 or 80 lb
  straight to the lure at 7–9 mph, worked by getting well out in front of the
  pod rather than cutting through it, with the yellowfin on the flanks and up to
  ~50 yd in front of a feeding pod; 60–70 lb fish cited as the result;
  unregistered channel, single mention.

## What's there

- `_KldpqPPT1c` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2019-05-23, outside Cat Harbor,
  Catalina): anchored just outside Cat Harbor rather than inside the harbor
  itself and found a steady, not-wide-open kelp bite, with hooked fish
  repeatedly running into the kelp; no current, water-color or tide detail given
  beyond the location; registered voice.
- `sIoNELGlxmk` (Your Saltwater Guide, Capt. Dave Hansen, PCS Show live seminar,
  2023-03-04): anchoring West Cove in 32 ft of water at 3 p.m. offered as the
  afternoon white seabass play — told as a worked "game plan" example rather
  than a dated report, so a lean rather than a confirmed bite; registered voice,
  single mention.
- `5p6gu14ZC4w` (BDOutdoors, 2022-10-26, inside Catalina and Farnsworth Bank):
  Farnsworth Bank named as the fall yo-yo call, in a closure where only
  yellowtail and bonito may be kept; yellowtail are catchable at Catalina while
  water temperatures hold but "you're mostly against Bonita, bass and Rockfish
  this time of year."
- `5to3Q5P7w90` (BDOutdoors, 2022-09-14 report): by the late warm-stable phase
  dorado are in open-water schools below and on the backside of Catalina rather
  than on the beach.
