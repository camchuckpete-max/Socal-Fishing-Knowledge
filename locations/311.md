# 311 (Trask Knoll)

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is two charted marks in open water on the
western edge of the [Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md) — **311 (Trask
Knoll)** at **33°37.000'N 120°16.000'W** and **Patton Ridge** at **33°30.000'N
120°30.000'W** (cameron). They lie **13.6 nm** apart: Patton Ridge bears
**239°** from the 311 and the 311 bears **059°** back, a southwest–northeast
line with **7.0 minutes** of the separation in latitude and **14.0 minutes** in
longitude.

**No corpus source names either mark.** Neither "Trask Knoll" nor "Patton
Ridge" appears in the transcript corpus, in a knowledge note or in an evidence
file, and the bare token "311" appears in the corpus only as a fish weight — a
**311 lb** personal-best callout on a long-range trip (`ASitOLYzFEA`,
[yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md)), not as a bank. The zone exists
on coordinates from [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) (cameron), so
this is a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the two marks are, what
their names do and do not claim, what water they sit in, and what a source
would have to supply. The [region page](socal-bight.md) already carries this
zone as a flagged stub, and nothing here lifts that stub. The flags below
aggregate in [the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

**The pair is wider than the clustering rule allows, which is why it exists as
a zone at all.** At **13.6 nm** the two marks are **past** the census's
**12 nm** zone-diameter cap, so they never clustered: the 311 was left over as
a lone bank too far from any grouping to join one and minted a zone of its own,
and Patton Ridge then attached to it under the **25 nm** rule that puts a lone
bank in the nearest zone it could realistically be fished with (cameron). This
is a zone by attachment, not by cluster — the same mechanism that carries
Arguello Canyon in
[Davis Knoll / San Miguel Gap / Rodriguez Seamount](davis-knoll-san-miguel-gap-rodriguez-seamount.md).
That page names Patton Ridge as its own nearest charted neighbour and records
that the census minted no zone in Patton Ridge's name; the ridge sits here
instead, as this zone's attached member.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a stated distance, heading, run time, fuel
figure or sea-state envelope to either mark from any port, and no source names
a port, ramp or landing this zone is fished out of. Nothing in the corpus

**The nearest charted land in the spot library is San Nicolas Island, and it is
a long way off.** [Blockhouse](blockhouse.md) on the island's east half lies
**43.8 nm** at **117°** from the 311 and **52.3 nm** from Patton Ridge, with
[Airplane Rock](airplane-rock.md) **45.3 nm** from the 311 (cameron); the whole
[San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md) zone sits east-southeast of this
water. **Every mainland position in the library is over 120 nm away** — the
nearest is Pelican Point off Crystal Cove at **120.5 nm** (cameron) — but read
that as a fact about the library rather than about the water: the spot library
carries no mainland coordinates north of Orange County, so the coast this zone
actually faces is uncharted here. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any
Santa Barbara, Ventura or Channel Islands departure point with a position, or
any run stated to this end of the region.

**The doctrine that governs the outside islands is the closest thing to a
crossing rule this water has, and it is not scoped to these marks.** At San
Nicolas — **43.8 nm** inshore of the 311 (cameron) — weather rather than the
bite decides whether anyone goes: a July 2022 white seabass bite there was
written off as unreachable under **15–20 kt gusting 25** rather than over
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`), and an August 2022 wind event blew out the Channel Islands,
San Clemente, San Nicolas, Santa Barbara Island and the tuna zone off Catalina
together (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). This zone is further out than any of them.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source extends that pattern to the 311 or Patton
Ridge.

**Check the swell upstream, not just the point forecast.** The worked example
in the forecast curriculum clicks the **West End of San Nicolas Island** and
finds a benign local wind picture hiding a swell that climbs from **4 ft on a
Thursday afternoon to over 12 ft by Monday**, generated by a system up off
Oregon days earlier (`zKmZ4zql2ws`) — which is why a **48–72 h** upstream fetch
scan belongs in front of any run to the outside
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md#upstream-fetch-check-swell-arrives-after-the-wind-that-made-it)).
Nothing here is sheltered by land in any direction.

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries, military schedules
or restricted areas stated for either position. The corpus names the **Cow Cod
Conservation Area** as an offshore closure with its own depth limit and its own
as-of date ([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)) but gives no
boundary for it, so whether either mark falls inside it is unverified — **check
the CCCA boundary against your position with CDFW/NOAA before dropping.**

## Structure & bathymetry

**The two positions.** The 311 sits at **33°37.000'N 120°16.000'W** and Patton
Ridge at **33°30.000'N 120°30.000'W** (cameron) — **13.6 nm** apart on a
**239°/059°** line. Working both is one southwest–northeast leg with open water
between them, and no third mark to break the run.

**This is the western edge of the charted region.** Only five positions in the
whole spot library lie further west than Patton Ridge's **120°30.000'W** —
Arguello Canyon, Rodriguez Seamount, the San Miguel Gap, San Juan Seamount and
Davis Knoll, all of them northwest of here (cameron). The 311 is also north of
every charted Catalina and San Nicolas position by latitude, so this zone sits
outside the Bight's inshore geography on both axes at once.

**One name claims a depth and the other claims nothing.** A bank is an isolated
high spot rising out of otherwise deep water, named for the **fathom depth of
its shallowest point**, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`):

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 311 (Trask Knoll) | 311 fathoms | 1,866 ft |
| Patton Ridge | no number in the name | — |

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms that read for this mark. The
convention is stated for the 182, the 209 and the 43
([Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`), all of them
inshore banks, and no source states a sounded depth, relief, high-spot shape,
bottom composition or surrounding depth at either position here. The
`depth_band` is unknown for that reason.

**The 311 carries a number-plus-proper-name form, which is the library's
pattern for a mark that is known two ways.** It is recorded the way Sverdrup
Bank (126), Kidney Bank (63) and The Boot (504) are — number and name together,
so a report using either one resolves (cameron), and the naming conventions
behind that are in [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source says which name the fleet actually uses on the radio here, or who Trask
and Patton were.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what **knoll** and **ridge** denote as
structure terms. The lexicon defines *bank* — an isolated high spot rising out
of surrounding depth — and nothing else in this zone's vocabulary; the same
**knoll** question is open on
[Davis Knoll / San Miguel Gap / Rodriguez Seamount](davis-knoll-san-miguel-gap-rodriguez-seamount.md).
It matters here because the two words imply different shapes: a ridge is a line
to work along and a knoll is a point to work around, and nothing in the corpus
licenses that reading.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: preferred directions, drift behaviour or current set at either mark.

**Almost nothing charted is near this zone.** No position in the spot library
lies within **23.0 nm** of either mark. Nearest first (cameron): **Sverdrup
Bank (126)** at **23.0 nm** south-southeast of Patton Ridge, **Potato Bank** at
**31.1 nm** southeast of the 311 — which is parented to
[San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md) rather than to anything out
here — **San Juan Seamount** at **35.5 nm** southwest of Patton Ridge, the
[San Miguel Gap](san-miguel-gap.md) at **36.5 nm** northwest of it, and
**Albatross Knoll** at **42.2 nm**. On that measure this is the region's
fifth-most isolated zone, and the four ahead of it are the region's other
outer-water zones: the Davis Knoll grouping (**36.5 nm** to its nearest outside
mark), 12 Mile Reef (**35.8 nm**), San Juan Seamount (**30.9 nm**) and Hancock
Bank (**23.0 nm**, ahead of this zone by hundredths of a mile — call it a tie).
Two of those four are this zone's own neighbours — the Davis Knoll grouping and
San Juan Seamount, both reached off Patton Ridge; 12 Mile Reef sits **46.2 nm**
off and Hancock Bank **68.9 nm** (cameron), so neither is a fallback from here. ⚠ Flagged stub —
no corpus source yet: Sverdrup Bank (126), San Juan Seamount, Albatross Knoll
and Hancock Bank are charted positions only, none has a page on this ladder
yet, and none carries fishing doctrine to hand off to. **There is no built
fallback zone within 30 nm of this water.**

**The paddy band the corpus names is a long way inshore and south of here.**
The best kelp-paddy water in the Bight is **south of San Clemente Island /
Catalina** and along the coastal strip from Dana Point to Oceanside and
southward; north of Dana Point paddies thin out and hold fewer fish
(`HzE4FHHcvTk`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md)). Both marks lie north of
every charted Catalina position by latitude and far west of all of it
(cameron), which puts this zone outside that band on both counts. That is a
statement about where the named water is, not about these banks:
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source describes a paddy, bait, squid, temperature or
water colour over either position. Paddy mechanics are in
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

**The fall condensation runs east of this zone, not through it.** As the coast
cools and fragments, the bluefin condense outward toward **Tanner and Cortez**
along an arc that runs backside Santa Cruz / Rosa → Santa Barbara Island →
backside Catalina → the 499 / Clemente (`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md), [October](../seasonal/october.md)).
Cortes Bank is **90.8 nm** and Tanner Bank **79.2 nm** from the 311 (cameron),
and the arc's inshore leg passes well east of this water. Whatever that band is
doing, no source puts it here.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the two are run as one southwest–northeast
circuit, or what tide, light, current or drift do here.

**The geometry reads as two spot-checks with a transit between them, but no
source says so.** The Bight's two zone types are the complex — a
**~50–60 square-mile** box you plan hours of looking across — and the compact
spot you idle onto, grade on the meter, and either commit to or leave in
**20–30 minutes** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). Two isolated marks
**13.6 nm** apart with open water between them (cameron) is the shape of a pair
of spot-checks, not of a box — but a ridge is not a point, and nothing in the
corpus says how much of Patton Ridge there is to work.

**With no corpus program, the general layers are what a plan has.** SoCal fish
orient to structure and then to current edges, and the banger days come when
the two line up; there is enough water on this coast that current edges get
fished as much as structure does (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`,
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). With nothing
recorded here, break position and break age decide whether this zone is on a
given day's plan
([report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)),
and the meter decides once you are there
([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)). From
October the regime flips to reading **100 % the temperature chart and
chlorophyll** (`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`,
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)). None of those layers is scoped
to this water.

**The NW-blow map is stated for the water inshore of this zone.** A strong NW
event pulled **5–8 °F** off the northern Channel Islands and the LA / Ventura
shelf while the Coronados, San Clemente and Catalina held their water
(`YZtX1MiT0y8`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). This zone
is west of the cooled end of that map and unnamed in it, so treat the
temperature question as open here and the exposure question as decided: open
ocean this far out is [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md) first.

**Neither mark is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions here are pulled per
charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)). The nearest eval target is Cortes
Bank at **90.8 nm** (cameron) — far too distant to stand in for either mark,
which makes the per-position pull the only conditions read this zone has.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron), listed northeast to southwest — the axis this zone
is drawn on:

- **311 (Trask Knoll)** — the northeastern mark and the one the zone is named
  for, the only member whose name claims a depth, and the closer of the two to
  everything east: **31.1 nm** to Potato Bank, **43.8 nm** to Blockhouse at San
  Nicolas Island, **79.2 nm** to Tanner Bank (cameron). Coordinates only; the
  corpus never names it.
- **Patton Ridge** — the southwestern mark, **13.6 nm** at **239°** from the
  311, attached to this zone rather than clustered with it, and the closer of
  the two to everything west and south: **23.0 nm** to Sverdrup Bank (126),
  **35.5 nm** to San Juan Seamount, **36.5 nm** to the
  [San Miguel Gap](san-miguel-gap.md). It is a *ridge* rather than a high spot
  by name alone. Coordinates only.

Charted positions for both are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive. The spot rung here needs a disambiguated filename for
311 (Trask Knoll), whose slug collides with this zone page's own — a gazetteer
question, not one this page resolves.
