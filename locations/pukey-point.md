# Pukey Point

The northern end of the [Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) chain, at
**32°26.850'N 117°18.000'W** (cameron). It is fished as part of the islands
circuit rather than as a destination of its own.

**This is a minimum spot page.** The corpus carries the position and the parent
zone and nothing further yet; the flagged gaps below are what a source would
