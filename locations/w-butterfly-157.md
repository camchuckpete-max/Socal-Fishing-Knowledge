# W. Butterfly / 157

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is three charted marks in open water southwest
of San Clemente Island — the **157**, the **W. Butterfly** and the **Mushroom**
(cameron) — spanning **18.5 minutes of latitude** (32°04.000'N to 32°22.500'N)
and **12.2 minutes of longitude** (118°17.833'W to 118°30.000'W). The 157 and the
W. Butterfly clustered on their own coordinates **8.6 nm** apart on an almost
due-east line; the Mushroom was attached on distance and, at **20.7 nm** from the
W. Butterfly, it is what sets the zone's diameter.

**No corpus source names any of the three.** Neither Butterfly, the 157 nor the
Mushroom appears anywhere in the transcript corpus, in a knowledge note or in an
evidence file — the whole zone exists on coordinates from
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) (cameron). This is therefore a
**position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the spot names
claim, what it sits between, and what is missing. The
[region page](socal-bight.md) already carries it as a flagged stub, and this
page does not lift that stub.

**What it is is the water off San Clemente's southwest shoulder.** Every mark
here is closer to the [San Clemente Island back side](san-clemente-island-back-side.md)
than to any other charted place in the library, and the zone runs south from
there toward the maritime boundary the census draws (cameron).

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run distance, heading, timing or fuel
figure to this zone from any port, and no source names a port these three marks
are fished out of.

**San Clemente Island is what this zone hangs off.** The 157 sits **23.2 nm**
south of Desperation Reef and **25.3 nm** south of China Point on
[the back side](san-clemente-island-back-side.md) and **26.8 nm** from Pyramid
Cove on [the front side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md); the W. Butterfly is
**24.2 nm** from Desperation Reef and the Mushroom **41.8 nm** (cameron). The
island itself is an overnight-range destination rather than a full-day run and
sits roughly **60 miles straight off the beach**
([81 / 381](81-381.md), [island structure](island-structure.md#san-clemente-island--navy-controlled-access)),
so the reach to these marks is that crossing plus a leg of **23–42 nm** further
out — not a figure any source states.

**The mainland is a long way further.** The nearest charted mainland position in
the library is **Widow Rock** in [La Jolla](la-jolla.md), **52.7 nm** from the
W. Butterfly, **60.2 nm** from the 157 and **71.0 nm** from the Mushroom, with the
**Point Loma Pipe** at **62.5 nm** from the zone centre (cameron). The library
carries no charted positions on the Los Angeles or Long Beach coast, so those
figures are a floor on the run rather than a distance from the ports a boat would
actually leave from. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: run time from any named
port, and the sea-state envelope the crossing demands.

**The Navy rule belongs to the island, not to these marks.** San Clemente Island
is a U.S. Navy training range and which areas are open rotates with the Navy's
schedule
([island structure](island-structure.md#san-clemente-island--navy-controlled-access)).
The nearest mark here lies **23.2 nm** off its back side (cameron), so that rule
governs the transit past the island rather than the fishing on this zone.
**Verify current access with the Navy before working the island end of the run.**

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries or restricted areas
stated for any position in this zone. The corpus names the **Cow Cod Conservation
Area** as an offshore closure with its own depth limit and its own as-of date
([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)) but gives no boundary for
it, so whether any mark here falls inside it is unverified — **check the CCCA
boundary against your position with CDFW/NOAA before dropping**. Boundary
compliance is positional, not depth-based: a boat can be legal at one position
and over the line at a similar depth a short distance away
([US waters](us-waters.md)).

**The southern end of this zone is close to the border.** The census places the
Mushroom in [the Bight](socal-bight.md) and the **60 Mile Bank (53 Fa.)** —
**12.9 nm** to its east at the same latitude — in
[Baja Pacific North](baja-pacific-north.md), so its approximation of the maritime
boundary runs between the two (cameron). That line is a modelling approximation,
not a charted border. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source gives the maritime
boundary's position, so **confirm which side of it you are on before fishing the
Mushroom** — the paperwork on the far side is an FMM plus a CONAPESCA licence
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)), not a CDFW licence.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three positions.** The 157 sits at **32°22.500'N 118°28.000'W**, the
W. Butterfly at **32°22.000'N 118°17.833'W** and the Mushroom at **32°04.000'N
118°30.000'W** (cameron). The 157 and the W. Butterfly are **8.6 nm** apart on a
bearing of **093°** — a near-due-east pair, and well inside the census's **12 nm**
zone-diameter cap, which is the clustering rule that grouped them.

**The Mushroom was attached, not clustered.** It lies **18.6 nm** from the 157 and
**20.7 nm** from the W. Butterfly (cameron), so a three-mark group would have had
a **20.7 nm** diameter — past the cap — and it did not cluster. The census attached
it as the nearest grouping it could be fished with, at **19.2 nm** from the
157/W. Butterfly midpoint on a bearing of **198°** and inside the **25 nm** attach
limit. It is carried here as queued rather than re-parented, because the zone list
is set by the census and not by this page.

**The Mushroom's own nearest neighbour went to a different zone by 1.5 nm.** The
60 Mile Bank (53 Fa.) is **12.9 nm** from the Mushroom — closer than the Mushroom
is to either mark in its own zone — but it was attached first, and it measured
**18.0 nm** to the Upper Cross cluster's centre against **19.5 nm** to this zone's
pair, so it is parented to
[Upper Cross / 421 / 390](upper-cross-421-390.md) across the boundary line
(cameron). The practical consequence for a day plan: the water immediately east of
the Mushroom is continuous bank ground split across two pages and two
jurisdictions.

**The two Butterflies are a charted east/west pair split across two zones.** The
W. Butterfly's nearest charted neighbour of all is the **E. Butterfly**, **11.5 nm**
away on a bearing of **119°** (cameron) — inside the 12 nm cap on its own, but
joining the two would have put the 157 **19.6 nm** from the E. Butterfly and
breached it, so the pair sits either side of a zone line with the E. Butterfly
parented to E. Butterfly / San Salvador Knoll. The same east/west naming runs on
**East 400** and **West 400** in the Baja grid, where the library records it as the
exception to the landmass-prefix collision rule
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says
what *Butterfly* denotes, whether the two marks are one structure or two, or which
of them the name was given to first.

**Only one of the three names claims a depth.** A bank is an isolated high spot
rising out of otherwise deep water and is named for the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point**, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way:

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 157 | 157 fathoms | about 942 ft |
| W. Butterfly | no number in the name | — |
| Mushroom | no number in the name | — |

Two shape names to one number is unusual on this grid — the numbered-bank zones
either side of this one are numbers throughout — and it leaves the zone with a
single depth claim, unconfirmed. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms the
157 as a charted depth, or gives any depth at the W. Butterfly or the Mushroom.

**Read as a depth, the 157 lands inside two working bands and outside a third.**
The SoCal swordfish fishery works the **150–350 fathom** contour
([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)), and the 157's name read at six feet to a fathom — about **942 ft** (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`)
— sits just inside the shallow end of it; the same figure is close to the
**~950 ft** DSL depth the fishery prefers ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md))
and inside the **800–1,100 ft** band SoCal deep-drop baits average
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)).
The deep rockfish game runs **300–900 ft** ([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)),
and 942 ft reads just past it. All three are readings of a name against doctrine
stated for other water, not a claim that any of it is fished here.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: bottom composition, relief, high-spot shape, surrounding depth, preferred
directions, drift or current behaviour at any of these three marks.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron): the **E. Butterfly** at
**11.5 nm** from the W. Butterfly, the **60 Mile Bank (53 Fa.)** at **12.9 nm**
from the Mushroom, the **267 (The Condom)** at **13.3 nm** from the 157, the
**300** at **20.1 nm** from the W. Butterfly, the **81** at **20.9 nm** from the
157, **Cortes Bank** at **33.9 nm** from the 157 and **Tanner Bank** at
**39.0 nm**. Two of those are already built on this ladder:
[81 / 381](81-381.md) carries both the 81 and the 267, and
[43 / 91 / 300](43-91-300.md) carries the 300. ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus
source yet: the E. Butterfly / San Salvador Knoll grouping immediately east and
the Tanner Bank / Cortes Bank grouping to the west are unbuilt, so the closest
hand-off out of this zone has nothing to hand off to.

**The one Bight-wide pattern that does scope to this water is paddy geography.**
The best paddy water in the Bight is **south of San Clemente Island / Catalina**,
and along the coastal strip from Dana Point to Oceanside and southward; north of
Dana Point paddies thin out and hold fewer fish (`HzE4FHHcvTk`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)). Every mark in this zone lies **23–42 nm**
south of the San Clemente back side (cameron), which puts the whole zone inside
that named band. That is a statement about the water, not about the banks:
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source describes a paddy over any position here. Paddy
mechanics are in [kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md), and the species
that ride them are [bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md),
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md), [yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md)
and [dorado](../species/dorado.md).

**The fall condensation runs past this zone rather than over it.** In the
2022-10-20 Bight report the bluefin volume had slid out to Tanner and Cortez, the
water being worked running "from the 381 off the east end back side of Clementi
out to Tanner and Cortez" (`OYOda6T3f-8`, [October](../seasonal/october.md)). That
corridor runs about **27 nm** from the 381 out to Tanner Bank, and this zone sits
south of its inshore end — **19.9 nm** from the 381 at the 157, **24.7 nm** at the
W. Butterfly and **37.4 nm** at the Mushroom (cameron) — alongside the band, never
on it. The terminal outer banks that band ends on are
**33.9 nm** (Cortes) and **39.0 nm** (Tanner) from the 157 (cameron,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)), so this
zone is inshore-and-south of the fall endpoint rather than part of it.

**The below-Clemente search box is a different piece of water too.** That complex
— 43, 181, 182, 289, Clemente Ridge up to Mackerel Bank — reads as one
**~50–60 square-mile** box (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)), and
its named members sit **30.1–43.2 nm** northeast and north of the 157 (cameron).
This zone is outside it.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states

circuit, whether the Mushroom is worth the extra **19 nm** leg, or what tide,

**Which side of the search-box split this zone sits on is unstated, and the
geometry argues both ways.** The Bight's two zone types are the complex — a
**~50–60 square-mile** box you plan hours of looking across — and the compact spot
you idle onto, grade on the meter, and either commit to or leave (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). The 157 and the W. Butterfly are two isolated
marks **8.6 nm** apart with open water between them, which reads like a pair of
compact spot-checks; the **19.2 nm** leg down to the Mushroom is a transit, not a
box (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source places any of the three on either
side of that split.

**With no corpus program, the general layers are what a plan has.** Structure is
where bait stages and gamefish follow ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), and reading the day out here falls to
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md) and
[electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md). None of them is
scoped to this water.

**No mark here is a BightSST eval target.** Cortes Bank, **33.9 nm** west-northwest
of the 157 (cameron), is one; the 157, the W. Butterfly and the Mushroom are not
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)), so conditions for this zone are pulled
per charted position rather than read off a scored spot.

## Spots

Three charted spots (cameron). They group as a pair plus one attachment, listed
north to south:

- **The 157** — the western mark of the pair and the only position in the zone
  whose name reads as a depth, 157 fathoms. It is also the closest mark here to
  the outer banks, **33.9 nm** from Cortes Bank, and to the 267 (The Condom) in
  [81 / 381](81-381.md) at **13.3 nm** (cameron). Coordinates only; the corpus
  never names it.
- **The W. Butterfly** — the eastern mark of the pair, **8.6 nm** due east of the
  157, the closest mark in the zone to the mainland at **52.7 nm** from Widow
  Rock, and **11.5 nm** from its charted counterpart the E. Butterfly in the
  adjacent unbuilt zone. Coordinates only.
- **The Mushroom** — the southern mark, attached to this zone rather than
  clustered into it, **19.2 nm** from the pair's midpoint and the closest position
  here to the census's maritime boundary line and to the 60 Mile Bank (53 Fa.),
  **12.9 nm** east in [Upper Cross / 421 / 390](upper-cross-421-390.md).
  Coordinates only.

Charted positions for all three are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
