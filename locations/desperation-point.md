# Desperation Point

A name carried at San Clemente Island, in the
[San Clemente Island — Back Side (N→S)](san-clemente-island-back-side.md) zone,
and in the corpus a **landmark rather than a mark fished**: the one recorded
session here was "just about 6 miles off the island outside of desperation
point" in **1,200–1,300 ft** of water, with tuna boiling around the boat
(`AIHvJj-paoo`). The name is what places the water; nothing in the corpus is
fished on the feature itself. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says what the
feature is — a shore point, a high spot, or another name for the charted reef —
and the one source that names it hedges the name on camera ("outside of
desperation point I think", `AIHvJj-paoo`).

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
The library's only Desperation name is
[Desperation Reef](desperation-reef.md) at the southeast end of the back-side
run (cameron), which is why this page sits in that zone. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source says which end of the island this point is on, or whether the
point and the reef name the same ground; they are carried as two names. A
conditions pull therefore has to be run against the zone, or against the reef's
charted position, rather than against this point.

## Getting there

**Plan it as an overnight, not a day trip.** San Clemente Island sits at
overnight range and the crossing is described as roughly **60 miles** straight
off the beach without a named port (`XyKF34C5iB4`, `LTCFjqtSI8g`,
[the zone page](san-clemente-island-back-side.md#getting-there)).

**Check the Navy schedule before you leave.** The island is a training range
with rotating closures, and the access check is a standard pre-trip item —
statement and as-of date in
[San Clemente Island — Front Side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md#getting-there)
(`XyKF34C5iB4`). **Verify current access with the Navy before running the
crossing.**

**The one recorded leg reaches it from offshore, not from the beach.** The 2016
session arrived as a fast boat's jump off an earlier offshore stop — "we just
made a jump, went from the 209 to out San Clemente area," with the crew noting
it is "nice to have a fast boat" (`AIHvJj-paoo`,
[the 209 / 312](209-312.md#how-it-fishes)). The charted version of that leg runs
**33.7 nm** at **233°** from the [209](209-312.md) to Desperation Reef
(cameron) — the nearest charted equivalent, not a measured run to this point.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: distance, heading or run time from any named
port, which end of the island the approach rounds, or a sea-state envelope for
the crossing.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The corpus names it and describes only the water outside it.** The
single positional statement is about **six miles off the island** in
**1,200–1,300 ft** (`AIHvJj-paoo`) — deep water with no bottom relationship
stated, which is why this page carries `open-ocean` alongside `island`. Nothing
is said on camera about the feature itself: no relief, rock, kelp or sand, and
no statement that it is a shoreline feature at all.

**The charted Desperation name sits elsewhere.**
[Desperation Reef](desperation-reef.md) is a high spot charted at
**32°45.520'N 118°24.520'W** at the southeast end of the back-side run
(cameron) — a bank read in far shallower water than the 1,200–1,300 ft fished
outside this point (`AIHvJj-paoo`). ⚠ The two Desperation names are carried
unreconciled; neither page claims they are the same ground.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any sounding, bottom composition, kelp line
or relief for the point, how the deep water outside it relates to the island's
shelf edge, or how far off the boats work.

## How it fishes

**Downsize the leader when the bite gets picky.** On a picky private-boat bite
here the on-camera call was a **25 lb fluorocarbon leader** with a lighter
**thin-wire size 2 circle**, reasoned as letting the bait swim better, with
**20–25 lb** fluorocarbon running on multiple rods aboard the same day
(`AIHvJj-paoo`). That is one of three recorded stepping-off points for the same
scaled-down flyline bite; all three, with the decision frame — take the lightest
step that gets bit, then climb back — are on
[yellowfin — flyline](../species/yellowfin-tuna-flyline.md#rigs--gear).

**When the sport boats pull off, chum with the private boats that stay.** The
advice to the cluster left on the school was to join forces and chum
off-and-on together, which holds the school the way a party boat's steady chum
line does, and to give neighbouring boats room, since these tuna take long runs
and a hooked fish saws off on a close neighbour's line (`AIHvJj-paoo`,
[chumming with live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md#chumming-with-live-bait)).

**Check the current at the mark, not at the last stop.** Two named San Clemente
Island spots close enough to be a short run apart can show opposite current
directions on the same day, framed as routine at this island (`sIoNELGlxmk`,
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)) — worth doing on a
stop reached as a jump off another zone.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a tide, moon, current or wind window for this
water, how deep the bait was fished, how long the stop was given, or whether it
fishes at all on any other trip. The zone-level program is on
[San Clemente Island — Back Side (N→S)](san-clemente-island-back-side.md#how-it-fishes).
