# Punta Banda / Santo Tomas

The coastal stretch immediately south of Ensenada, from La Bufadora down to
Punta Santo Tomás, and **the densest coastal spot list in
[Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md)** — twelve charted waypoints
(cameron), more than any other mainland zone in the region. It is a run of
named points, rocks and bluffs with a line of fathom-named high spots outside
them. What the library does not carry is anyone fishing it: the corpus
describes Ensenada to the north and San Quintín to the south in detail and says
almost nothing about the water between.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: how this zone fishes at all — no trip, no
program, no species-by-season pattern scoped to it. Everything below the
coordinates is either regional doctrine that spans this stretch or a flagged
gap.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the port, ramp or launch this zone is run
from, the distance and run time to any of its twelve spots, and the sea-state
envelope that run demands. The nearest working port in the corpus is Ensenada,
whose marina, bait and offshore departures are covered on the
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md); nothing states that this zone is fished
out of it.

**Boilers mark the north end of the run.** The library labels the Punta Banda
waypoint at **31°45.430'N 116°45.330'W** *caution — boilers* (cameron) — rock
that breaks. That waypoint is charted in the Ensenada zone, on the headland
just north of this zone's northernmost spot, so a boat coming down from
Ensenada passes it on the way in.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)).** ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
closures, protected areas or restricted zones anywhere on this stretch,
including whether La Bufadora or Bahía Soledad carry any local rule.

## Structure & bathymetry

**Two kinds of mark, and the labels say which.** Seven of the twelve waypoints
are named coastal features — La Bufadora, White Rock, El Retiro, Billy's Bluff,
Bahía Soledad, Isolete and Punta Santo Tomás — and five carry a fathom figure
instead of a name: the 15 Fa Ridge and the 33, 38, 47 and 48 fathom spots
(cameron).

**The fathom labels are a depth convention.** A mark named for a number is
named for the fathom depth of its shallowest point as read on the sounder, at
six feet to a fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read
that way the five spots span **15 to 48 fathoms — roughly 90 to 288 ft** — with
the 15 Fa Ridge the shallowest and the 48 the deepest. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source confirms the charted depth of any of these five marks
independently of its label, and none of the seven named coastal spots carries a
depth at all.

**The zone runs north-to-south along the coast with the deep spots outside.**
The charted positions run from **31°43.440'N 116°43.380'W** at La Bufadora down
to **31°29.000'N 116°42.800'W** at the 33 fathom spot, and the two westernmost
positions in the section are the **47 fathom spot (116°46.500'W)** and the
**48 fathom spot (116°49.000'W)** — the offshore end of the group (cameron).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bottom composition, kelp, relief or contour
detail for this coast — whether these points fish as kelp edges, clean rock or
sand-and-rock transitions ([bass structure](bass-structure.md) carries the
general typology, not this zone's).

## Spots

Twelve charted waypoints (cameron), in two groups:

- **The coastal run, north to south** — La Bufadora, White Rock, El Retiro,
  Billy's Bluff, Bahía Soledad, Isolete and Punta Santo Tomás: named points,
  rocks and bluffs down the shore between the Punta Banda headland and Punta
  Santo Tomás.
- **The fathom-named high spots** — the 15 Fa Ridge, and the 33, 38, 47 and 48
  fathom spots: the marks outside the coastal run, the 47 and 48 the furthest
  offshore of the twelve.

Charted positions for all twelve are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md); no waypoint carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page will be coordinates plus its parent zone
until sources arrive.
