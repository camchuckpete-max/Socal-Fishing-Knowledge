# Chester's Rock

**[Baja only]** — the shallow rock at the south end of the
[Cedros / San Benitos](cedros-island.md) complex, "historically a good big
yellowtail spot" for **40–50 lb** fish and one corner of the "Yellow Tail
triangle" the crews draw between Cedros Island, the Benitos Islands and Punta
Eugenia (`_r_qKX_7080`). It fishes as a **12 ft** rock with kelp on its outside
edges, worked from skiffs off a mothership, and it is the far end of the
Geronimo Island stretch of "endless miles" of calico water (`3SATCeA3KaU`).

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
Every source names it by name only, so a conditions pull has to be run against
the zone rather than against this mark.

## Getting there

**The only stated route is a boat run down the beach, and it is long.** The crew
put the rock at roughly **300 miles** from San Diego — a **188 mile** leg to the
Dono stop plus a further **112 miles** — and reached it as the closing leg of a
multi-day mothership trip, running skiffs off the 90-ft *Shogun*
(`_r_qKX_7080`, `3SATCeA3KaU`). A separate October 2020 charter arrived here as
the last stop of a **"macro banks" → Benitos Island → Chester's Rock** rotation
(`yjwIGFzWO8I`). The zone's two access modes — the Cedros fly-in program and the
San Diego long-range boat — are on
[the zone page](cedros-island.md#getting-there); neither is written around a run
to this specific rock.

**Wind decides whether you get to stay.** Wind blew the skiffs off the rock on
the documented 2014 stop, and the crew's fallback was the lee of another island
near Rose Island / Dondo Ridge — both names asr-uncertain — where a "puff of
current" bite ran to **40 lb** (`_r_qKX_7080`). Plan the stop with that lee in
mind rather than as a fixed anchorage. Sea-state judgment is
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md).

**Regulatory — Mexico.** The zone's operator-level **100% catch-and-release**
agreement for [calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md), grouper (cabrilla) and
black seabass, and the Mexican entry and sport-fishing paperwork, are recorded
on [the zone page](cedros-island.md) and
[Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md). That agreement is an operator agreement,
not a federal or state regulation. Recorded on or before 2023-05-24; **verify
current status with your operator before fishing**
([regulatory claims](../sources/regulatory-claims.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a port, launch, fuel stop, heading, run time
or sea-state envelope for reaching this rock, or how it is approached by anyone
not travelling on a mothership.

## Structure & bathymetry

**It is a shallow rock, and the numbers are the reason it eats tackle.** Fishing
depth is **12 ft** right on the rock, dropping to **40–60 ft** a short distance
out, and the captain's tip on the stop pointed at the **kelp on the outside
edges** rather than at the rock top (`_r_qKX_7080`). That is the whole
bathymetric record for this mark.

**The kelp is thick enough to be the design constraint on a bait.** A weedless
plastic rigged on a **3/8 oz** keel-weighted hook was called out on camera as
running clean through "the thick of the heel [kelp] grass" with no fouling
(`_r_qKX_7080`) — the fouling problem is what a bait has to solve here, not an
incidental. Rigging parameters are in
[soft-plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md) and
[swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md).

**It sits at the far end of one continuous stretch, not on its own.** The ground
from **Geronimo Island to Chester's Rock** is worked as a single piece of calico
water — "endless miles" of it — and the same stretch turns into a home-guard
yellowtail brawl (`3SATCeA3KaU`). Anything read about one end applies down the
stretch unless a source says otherwise.

**The corpus places the rock two different ways, and does not resolve it.** One
crew logs it at the **south end of Cedros Island** (`_r_qKX_7080`,
`3SATCeA3KaU`); the October 2020 charter logs it on the **mainland side near
Cedros**, arriving there after the Benitos (`yjwIGFzWO8I`). Both are label
differences on a name-only mark, not competing doctrine — ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source gives a charted position that would settle which side of the
channel this rock is on. Do not plan a run off either framing without a chart.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bottom composition, relief, the shape of the
rock, its extent, or how deep the water is between it and Geronimo Island.

**Grade here is a big-fish grade, and the calico are big enough to be in the
way.** On the morning after the brawl the deliberate yellowtail push was
repeatedly broken up by them — "pesky 5-pound calicoes… putting off my 50-pound
yellow" (`3SATCeA3KaU`). Treat a running calico bite as a sign you are on the
right ground rather than as the day's target.

## How it fishes

**Anchor and fan-cast when there is no current to read.** On the 2014 morning
stop the crew anchored and found "no real condition, there's a slight current
going down the rock," so they worked it as fan-casting and circling — fishing
hard over the whole piece rather than setting up on a defined current edge
(`_r_qKX_7080`). That is the documented answer to a slack stop here; the general
read is [current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md) and
[tide & slack](../conditions/tide-and-slack.md).

**The weedless plastic is the working bait on the rock.** Both the 7 lb calico
morning and the first-cast **5–6 lb** kelp fish came on weedless swimbaits
(`_r_qKX_7080`, `yjwIGFzWO8I`), with an MC-brand jerk bait also producing on the
October 2020 stop (`yjwIGFzWO8I`,
[soft-plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md)). The kelp is what
forces the weedless rig — see
[swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md) for the retrieve and the hookset.

**Birds over the ground are the entry to the iron, not fish showing on top.**
Down the Geronimo-to-Chester's stretch, "birds flared up, came out here, threw
the jig" is the stated search cue (`3SATCeA3KaU`,
[calico bass — surface iron](../species/calico-bass-surface-iron.md)); the
discipline on the first-light yellowtail push at the same ground was to **hold
the cast until the yellows actually showed** under the birds rather than
throwing at the birds themselves (`3SATCeA3KaU`). Bird work is
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md) and
[search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).

**Line class is decided by the rock, and the corpus gives a clean result.**
Anglers on **40–50 lb** landed their fish while several on lighter line lost
theirs in the shallow, rocky water (`_r_qKX_7080`); the 45 lb fish hooked on the
iron went straight into the rocks and was lost anyway. Expect a rock fight from
the hookup, not an open-water fight. Parameters are in
[line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md); the zone's wider 40/50 versus
80/100 argument, and what it turns on, is on
[the zone page](cedros-island.md#doctrine--conflicts).

**Wind is the stop's clock.** The 2014 session ended when wind blew the skiffs
off the rock; the crew moved to the lee near Rose Island / Dondo Ridge (both
asr-uncertain) and found a "puff of current" bite up to **40 lb** on surface
iron, [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) and
[flyline](../techniques/flyline.md), plus more calico on a light current under
working birds (`_r_qKX_7080`). The lee is the documented continuation of the
day, not an abandonment of it.

**A mothership changes what "moving" means here.** When the *Shogun* hooked
yellowtail while the skiffs were on the rock, the skiff group came aboard and
fished from her rather than running to new ground (`_r_qKX_7080`).
