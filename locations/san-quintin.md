# San Quintin

**A zone is a run grouping.** San Quintín is the island-and-coast grouping
around **San Martin Island** — eight charted positions (cameron): the island
anchorage and its kelp, the boiler rock at Roca Ben, a 6.5 and a 15 fathom
spot, Breakers Reef, Tranquillo Kelp off to the southeast, and the numbered
bank **304** that the census assigns here on distance. It is the southern end
of the mainland-coast ladder in
[Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md); below it the spot library stops
and [Cedros / San Benitos](cedros-island.md) picks up as a corpus-only zone.

Two fisheries are documented here and they do not overlap. The water **outside**
San Martin Island held giant schools of big [bluefin](../species/bluefin-tuna.md)
in January 2021 (`shZCjX2-fkI`), and the coast **from the island south** is
big-[calico](../species/calico-bass.md) country fished off a mothership
(`L3tkGVu516A`). Nobody in the corpus fishes the charted marks in between.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any port, ramp, fuel dock, bait receiver or
launch for San Quintín. The [region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the
same gap for the whole coast south of Ensenada, so a private-boat plan for this
zone has no supported staging point in the KB.

**Both documented ways in came from somewhere else.** San Diego long-range
boats worked the water just outside the island in early January 2021 on trips
already headed down the line (`shZCjX2-fkI`), and a 2014-11-13 trip fished this
stretch off the 90-ft mothership *Shogun* with skiffs launched from her, a
six-day inshore program with a 20-ft center console along
(`L3tkGVu516A`). The one distance in the corpus belongs to that trip: **188
miles from San Diego** to its first stop at Dono, with Chester's Rock a further
112 miles beyond it (`_r_qKX_7080`) — the units are never stated on camera.

**Two of the eight spots are named for water that breaks.** Roca Ben carries
the library's *caution — shoals/boilers* label at **30°26.300'N
116°07.000'W**, and Breakers Reef is named for the same behaviour at
**30°20.100'N 116°06.700'W** (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
describes the swell size or direction that stands either up, or how wide to
give them.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)).** ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
closures, protected areas or restricted zones anywhere in this zone, including
whether San Martin Island itself carries a local rule.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The island is the anchor.** Two of the eight positions sit on San Martin
Island — the anchorage at **30°29.180'N 116°06.150'W** and the Natural Jetty
Kelp at **30°29.060'N 116°05.910'W** (cameron) — an island with an anchorage
and a kelp edge on a lee, which is the pattern
[island structure](island-structure.md) describes generally. ⚠ Flagged gap —
no corpus source: the island's relief, its kelp extent, or which side fishes in
which wind.

**South of the island the marks run down the coast.** Roca Ben, then the 6.5
Fathom Spot at **30°25.600'N 116°09.100'W**, the 15 Fathom Spot at
**30°22.000'N 116°05.000'W**, and Breakers Reef as the southern end of the
charted run (cameron).

**The fathom labels are a depth convention.** A mark named for a number is
named for the fathom depth of its shallowest point as read on the sounder, six
feet to a fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that
way the two spots are **6.5 fathoms — about 39 ft — and 15 fathoms — 90 ft**,
both shallow enough to be structure you fish over rather than water you drop
into. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms either charted depth
independently of its label, and none of the four named marks — the anchorage,
the kelp, Roca Ben, Breakers Reef — carries a depth at all.

**Two positions sit apart from the rest.** Tranquillo Kelp at **30°17.000'N
115°49.000'W** is the zone's southeast outlier, **15.8 nm** from the zone
centre in the geographic census — carried here as queued rather than re-parented,
because the zone list is set by the census and not by this page. The numbered
bank **304** at **30°18.000'N 116°17.000'W** comes from the library's offshore-
banks section and was attached to this zone as the nearest one it could be
fished with. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: anything at all about 304 or
Tranquillo Kelp beyond their positions.

**Two fished stops are not in the library.** Dono and Sacramento Reef are
worked down-coast of San Martin on the 2014-11-13 trip (`L3tkGVu516A`) and sit
roughly 112 miles up-coast of Chester's Rock (`_r_qKX_7080`), which places them
in this grouping rather than at [Cedros](cedros-island.md). Neither carries a
charted position anywhere in the corpus, so neither gets a spot page.

## What's there

| species | season | what this zone does for them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) | documented in January; named as structure that produced early in the modern fishery | Giant schools of big bluefin held just outside San Martin Island in early January 2021, in **63–65 °F** water — they stuck with the boat but "didn't bite all that well" (`shZCjX2-fkI`). San Quintín with Colonet is named as water structure that was productive early in the modern SoCal bluefin fishery and is expected to still hold fish, simply unfished as of 2021-05 because grounds closer to San Diego and Ensenada were producing (`fgTmUq78ofQ`) |
| [Calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md) | documented in November | The coast from the island south is where a crew goes for **size** rather than count: a lot of spots along this stretch hold calico but smaller ones, so the 2014-11-13 trip ran past them to Dono, where a three-hour skiff rotation produced personal bests at **7, 8 and 9 lb** and a **9.5 lb** fish on the scale, and two days were fished there (`L3tkGVu516A`, `_r_qKX_7080`) |
| [Yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) | ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source gives a window for this zone | Baja coastal structure from Ensenada southward holds yellowtail more or less year-round with the grade improving the further south you go (cameron); the one fish logged here took a swimbait at Dono and "basically spooled" a 200-size reel before it was lost (`L3tkGVu516A`) |

rockfish, halibut, white seabass, barracuda or bonito is attached to any

## How it fishes

**The island gets looked at, not automatically fished.** The 2014-11-13 crew
checked San Martin first thing in the morning, decided conditions "didn't look
all that great" for staying, and ran down the coast instead (`L3tkGVu516A`).
That decision is the corpus's only zone-level program note: the island is the
first look on the way down, and the coast below it is the fallback that gets
fished when the look is bad.

**Current on a corner made the calico bite.** At Dono the first drifts were
"a little scratchy" until the skiff came around the corner at the upper part of
the island, where the current ran down the side — after that it was a fish
every cast on weedless swimbait and slug (`L3tkGVu516A`). That is the general
current-on-structure read in
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)
applied on the water; the retrieve itself — including the very slow wind that
was working that day — belongs to [swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md).

**Presence is not catchability for the bluefin here.** The January 2021 schools
were big, visible and stayed with the boat while biting poorly
(`shZCjX2-fkI`); the [bluefin router](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) carries what
to do about that, and nothing in the corpus scopes a bluefin program to this
zone specifically.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: how the eight charted marks fish — no
technique, tide, light or current call for the anchorage, the kelp, Roca Ben,
either fathom spot, Breakers Reef, Tranquillo Kelp or 304.

## Spots

Eight charted positions (cameron), in three groups:

- **San Martin Island** — the anchorage and the Natural Jetty Kelp: the island
  that names the zone, the first look on the way down the coast, and the mark
  the bluefin schools were sighted outside of.
- **The coastal run south of the island** — Roca Ben (boilers), the 6.5 and 15
  Fathom Spots, and Breakers Reef: shallow rock and reef down the beach, two of
  it labelled for breaking water and two for their sounder depth.
- **The outliers** — Tranquillo Kelp to the southeast and the numbered bank 304
  to the west: both assigned to this zone by the census on distance, neither
  carrying a word of corpus detail.

Dono and Sacramento Reef, the two stops this zone is actually fished at in the
corpus, have no charted position and so are not spots here — they are covered
in [What's there](#whats-there) and the
[evidence file](evidence/san-quintin.md).
