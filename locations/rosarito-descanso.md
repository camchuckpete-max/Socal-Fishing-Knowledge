# Rosarito / Descanso

**A zone is a run grouping.** Rosarito / Descanso is the **northernmost Baja
coastal zone** — six charted positions (cameron) strung down the open mainland
coast from the **Bull Ring** at **32°31.500'N 117°08.600'W** to **Sugarloaf
Rock** at **32°13.365'N 116°57.814'W**, about **18 minutes of latitude** of
beach, points and flats.

It is one of the two halves the spot library's **Northern Baja** section splits
into. That section ran a single heading of roughly **33 nm** from the Bull Ring
down to Punta Salsipuedes, which is too long to fish in one trip, so coordinate
clustering cut it at Sugarloaf Rock; the southern half is the La Fonda /
Bajamar / Salsipuedes zone. ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: the La Fonda
/ Bajamar / Salsipuedes page does not exist, so the coast south of Sugarloaf
Rock has no page to hand off to.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these six marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap for the whole
coastal stretch between the border and Punta Banda — eleven charted positions
across this zone and the one below it, and no doctrine attached to any of them.
This page is therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone
is, what the spot labels claim, and what is missing. Everything a plan would
need beyond the coordinates is flagged below.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any port, ramp, hoist, fuel dock or bait
receiver serving this stretch of coast, and no run distance or time from
anywhere. There is no supported staging point in the KB for a private-boat trip

**The zone starts within sight of the international boundary.** The Bull Ring
is the northernmost position in the whole Baja spot library at **32°31.500'N**
(cameron), sitting just south of — and about **6 minutes of longitude inshore
of** — the US-side
[International Artificial Reef](international-artificial-reef.md) complex,
whose waypoints run **32°32.495'N to 32°32.717'N** around **117°14.8'W**
(cameron). A boat working the Bull Ring is in Mexican water; a boat on the
International Reef is not.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)). Because the zone's north end sits so
close to the line, the paperwork question is live on the shortest possible trip
here, not only on a long run south. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures,
protected areas, restricted zones or local rules anywhere in this zone,
including whether anything applies at the Bull Ring itself.

## Structure & bathymetry

The six positions sort into three families by their labels (cameron):

- **The north end — Bull Ring** (32°31.500'N 117°08.600'W). The zone's
  **parent-distance outlier**: it lies **14.0 nm** from the zone's own centre
  (`sources/geo-census.txt`), far enough that a trip fishing the Bull Ring and
  a trip fishing the Descanso marks are not the same trip. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
  corpus source: what the Bull Ring actually is — rock, reef, a kelp bed or a
  shore mark.
- **The flats — Rosarito Flats** (32°21.000'N 117°06.500'W), logged in the
  library as a **big area**, so the position is the centre of a search box and
  not a mark to run to (cameron). It sits roughly midway down the zone.
- **The Descanso cluster — four spots inside two minutes of latitude.** Punta
  Descanso (32°15.880'N 117°01.890'W), **Descanso rockfish 1** (32°14.836'N
  117°00.876'W), **Descanso rockfish 2** (32°14.447'N 116°58.562'W) and
  Sugarloaf Rock (32°13.365'N 116°57.814'W). This is the zone's only tight
  grouping — a point, two rockfish marks and a named rock, all fishable on one
  drop-down-the-coast plan, and the reason the zone exists as a run grouping at
  all.

**The two rockfish marks are the only spots in the zone whose label names what
is fished there** (cameron), and even they carry no depth. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: charted depth, relief, bottom composition, kelp extent or
current behaviour at any of the six. The zone's `depth_band` is unknown for
that reason, not because the spots are shallow.

**What sits offshore of this coast is charted and this coast is not.** The
[Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) share the zone's latitudes a few
minutes of longitude to the west — the Coronados' southeast mark, the Rockpile,
is at **32°17.620'N 117°10.030'W** (cameron), effectively abeam Rosarito Flats
and Punta Descanso. ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: the Finger Bank
rockfish zone lies directly offshore of the Descanso cluster and has no page
either.

## Spots

Six charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. They group as one outlier
plus one workable run:

- **Bull Ring** — alone at the north end, 14.0 nm off the zone's centre
  (`sources/geo-census.txt`) and the closest charted Baja position to the
  border. Fished as its own trip, or as the first or last stop on a crossing.
- **Rosarito Flats** — the midpoint, and a search area rather than a mark
  (cameron).
- **The Descanso run** — Punta Descanso, the two Descanso rockfish marks and
  Sugarloaf Rock, all within two minutes of latitude of one another at the
  south end, and the only part of the zone that reads as a normal
  spot-to-spot day.

Charted positions for all six are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus
detail beyond its position and, for two of them, the word *rockfish* in the
label.
