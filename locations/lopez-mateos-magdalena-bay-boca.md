# The Boca (Magdalena Bay Entrance)

**[Baja only]** — the mouth of
[Magdalena Bay](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md) and the outermost of the
bay's three inside depth rungs: **40–50 ft**, about **five minutes** by boat
from the Lopez Mateos dock, fished on the bottom at a known mark for
[snook](../species/snook.md) to at least **42 lb** mixed with grouper to
**100 lb** (`EcQK5elTPJE`). It is the grade program — the run you make when
the target is a **20 lb-plus** snook rather than the small fish the mangrove
roots give up — and it is also the gate an offshore day reads on the way out,
because the wave state outside the mouth is what picks the day's bank
(`OVwqUKim9Pc`).

**"The boca" is a description, not a proper name** — it is the ordinary
Spanish word for a bay mouth, and no other name for this entrance is on
record.

**No coordinates are published here.**
[The spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) stops at San Quintín, so a day
plan has to pull conditions against
[the zone](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md) rather than against this mark.

## Getting there

**It is the shortest run in the bay.** The boca sits about **5 minutes** by
boat from the dock at Lopez Mateos, which is what makes it a half-day or a
weather-window program rather than a committed run (`EcQK5elTPJE`).
Zone-level access — the flight into [Loreto](loreto.md), the drive across the
peninsula through Ciudad Constitución, the dock and the panga fleet — is on
[the zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md#getting-there).

**Bait is a precondition, arranged a day ahead.** Live shrimp is the named
snook bait here and no dockside receiver stocks it — the guide has it caught
overnight, so it is ordered the day before; live sardine is the other bait
named for this program (`EcQK5elTPJE`). See
[making bait](../bait/making-bait.md).

**The mouth is where the offshore decision gets made.** An offshore day out
of the same dock starts by idling past the estuary mouth to look at the wave
state, and the bank is picked off what that look shows — the longer run to
[the Ridge](the-ridge.md) if it is manageable, the closer Modesto Main if it
is not (`OVwqUKim9Pc`). See [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md),
[the zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**One depth is the whole of what is measured: 40–50 ft** (`EcQK5elTPJE`).
That puts the boca at the bottom of the bay's inside ladder — the mangrove
interior at **12–15 ft** falling to about **2 ft** in the roots, the
[estuary channel](magdalena-bay-estuary-channel.md) at **25–30 ft**, and this
mark at **40–50 ft** (`EcQK5elTPJE`, `isXJONlpUP4`).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the **25–30 ft** rung and the joint cite
behind the ladder. `EcQK5elTPJE` never states 25–30 ft — the only depths in it
are 40–50 ft, 12–15 ft and 2 ft. In `isXJONlpUP4` the figure is a hedged
sounding under the panga ("how deep enrique is it here is probably 25 30 foot")
that the same transcript then distinguishes from the channel ("hit it past the
channel"), which is how both
[the estuary channel page](magdalena-bay-estuary-channel.md) and
[the snook router](../species/snook.md#where--when) cite it —
`isXJONlpUP4` alone. `EcQK5elTPJE`'s **40–50 ft** is also spoken in one breath
with the estuary ("40 50 ft water the est[uary] there right outside the boka,
boka's the mouth"), so narrowing it to the boca alone is the KB's. Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

**There is hard structure down there and it is what costs you fish.** A
single-speed reel means "breaking off all day" against the structure at this
mark, and a hooked grouper heads straight back for the rock and snaps you off
there (`EcQK5elTPJE`). The build that answers it — a locked drag and a
five-second turn — is on
[snook — sliding sinker](../species/snook-sliding-sinker.md).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): whether the boca is open bottom or rock.
The same source supplies both the break-off structure above and the reasoning
carried on the [snook router](../species/snook.md#landing--handling) that a
hooked snook rises here because "there is nothing to rock a leader against in
open water at the boca." One of the two readings is wrong, and no source
describes the bottom directly. Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the bottom composition, the width or extent

located on the water

## What's there

**No season is stated for the boca itself.** Snook bite year-round in this bay
with no month-by-month pattern recorded, and the inside fishing generally is
called "phenomenal" through the winter (`EcQK5elTPJE`); neither claim is
narrowed to this mark.

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): both halves, and the "no pattern"
reading. In `EcQK5elTPJE` the year-round line is said of the bay's fishery as a
whole — "there is no time that it doesn't bite", "fish b[ite] there all year
long" — never of snook; "phenomenal" attaches to grouper specifically ("the
grouper fishing is phenomenal in the winter"), not to inside fishing generally;
and the same talk does give a pattern, which "no month-by-month pattern
recorded" denies: "the best time [of] year to go guys is from about mid
September till about the end of December". Already flagged on
[the zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md) and carried on
[the snook router](../species/snook.md). Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

## How it fishes

**It is a drop-on-the-mark program.** The bait goes down to the bottom in the
40–50 ft at the entrance and is fished there (`EcQK5elTPJE`); no visual sign,
bird read or sonar signature is on record for the boca. That is the opposite
of the inside program a few minutes
away, which is precision-cast to visible roots
([snook — finding them](../species/snook.md#finding-them-sign--sonar)).

**One technique is on record: a Carolina rig on the bottom.** Live shrimp or
sardine on a [sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md) build —
**80 lb** braid to **80 lb** fluorocarbon, sinker, swivel, about **6 ft** of
**100–130 lb** leader, a 4X hook sized to the bait, on a **4/0-size two-speed**
reel with the drag locked as tight as it goes (`EcQK5elTPJE`). The full build,
including why a single-speed reel is disqualifying, is on
[snook — sliding sinker](../species/snook-sliding-sinker.md).

**The fight is decided in about five seconds.** Either the fish is turned off
the structure immediately or it is gone (`EcQK5elTPJE`). The leader spec was
reached by losing fish, not by reasoning: the first six snook hooked here all
broke off, and the leader was walked up through **60, 80, 100 and finally
130 lb** before fish were landed (`EcQK5elTPJE`). **4X** hooks have been
straightened out by fish at the top of this grade (`EcQK5elTPJE`); see
[hooks](../tackle/hooks.md) and
[line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the progression is not stated as the
origin of the **100–130 lb** spec, and no fish is said to be landed at 130 lb.
In `EcQK5elTPJE` the 60→80→100→130 lb anecdote (~00:16:23) sits about 26
minutes before the 6 ft / 100–130 lb rig spec (~00:42:38–00:43:23), the source
never ties the two together, and the spec passage instead reports still
breaking off on "100 to 130 lb test". The straightened **4X** hooks are also
never attributed to a fish size in the transcript, so "at the top of this
grade" is the KB's. Already flagged on
[snook — sliding sinker](../species/snook-sliding-sinker.md) and
[the snook router](../species/snook.md). Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

**Which fish you have is readable off the strike.** A snook comes **up**
toward the boat and starts taking drag against the locked setting once it is
off the bottom; a grouper dives straight back into the rock
(`EcQK5elTPJE`). That read is how the two are told apart before the fish is
up, and it decides how the fight is played.

**The mouth is the weather gate for everything outside it.** The wave state
here is what an offshore crew reads before committing to a bank
(`OVwqUKim9Pc`), and the bay's standing answer to bad outside weather is to
move inside rather than stand down — the inside stays flat in a blow, wind
chop only, no swell (`EcQK5elTPJE`). See
[the zone's four-fisheries weather logic](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md#how-it-fishes).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a tide, current, moon or light window for
