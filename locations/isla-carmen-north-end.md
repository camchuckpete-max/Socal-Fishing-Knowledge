# Isla Carmen — north end

**[Baja only]** — the **far end of Isla Carmen** in the
[Loreto & Puerto Escondido](loreto.md) cluster: *"about 20 miles probably an
hour to get there"* from Puerto Escondido in a small boat (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). It
is the run a crew picked as the first move of the day, not a ground the corpus
describes.

**Nothing was fished here on camera, and the one documented run never
arrived.** On 2023-08-01 a crew left Puerto Escondido Marina with fresh
sardines for dorado, passed on the ground the locals had named — *"they were
telling me the Sante, but it doesn't seem like that's the case"* — and called
the shot up to the north end of Carmen; the outboard's shift-linkage pin
sheared at the start of the run, and the day ended being towed back to the
marina and at the mechanic (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). Read the page as a destination
named with a distance attached, not as a fishery described.

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
That library stops at San Quintín on the Pacific side and holds nothing on this
coast, so a conditions pull has to run against
[the zone](loreto.md) rather than against this ground.

## Getting there

**About 20 miles and roughly an hour from Puerto Escondido Marina**, in the
18 ft Grady White a mothership operation tows behind it (`n4nMCLLW3tY`,
`ikiXx9sUsoA`). The figure is the zone's only stated run time; it is given as
miles from the marina with the unit never specified, and never as a heading.
Access to the zone itself — the CBX fly-in, the mothership, the pangas and the
beach launches — is on [the zone page](loreto.md#getting-there).

**The one attempt at this run needed a tow home.** It broke down at the start
of the run and got back only because a second boat was called in over the radio
to tow on a custom cleat-to-cleat rope; the crew paddled and swam the boat back
toward the marina in the meantime (`n4nMCLLW3tY`). Twenty miles is a long way
to paddle — plan it with a radio and a second boat in reach.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md), [the zone page](loreto.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**Nothing about this ground is on the record underwater.** It is named as *"the
North End of Carmen"* and reached for as a destination, with no bottom, relief,
high spot, kelp or point attached to it (`n4nMCLLW3tY`).

**The zone's soundings belong to other grounds.** The shallow depths recorded in
this island cluster — the cabrilla water, the bird-chase sounding and the
sounder call with structure visible through the water — were all taken elsewhere
and sit on [the zone page](loreto.md#structure--bathymetry); none of them is
transferable here. The one structural read the corpus attaches to Isla Carmen at
all is [Honeymoon Bay](honeymoon-bay.md), a sheltered bay with a fishable corner
— and which island that bay lies against is itself never stated on camera (the
flag is on that page), so nothing in it places structure at this end.

## How it fishes

**No technique, conditions read or timing window is documented at this ground.**
The zone's programs — trolling between stops, breaking off to run at working
birds, burning surface baits when fish are up, casting
[jerkbaits](../lures/jerkbaits.md) at shallow rock — are what the crews did
elsewhere in these islands
([trolling](../techniques/trolling.md),
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md),
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)); nothing says which of them the
north end wants.

**The one thing the record does say is about time.** An hour out plus an hour
back is the price of the trip, and the one crew that called it spent the first
move of the day on the run rather than on the closer grounds (`n4nMCLLW3tY`) —
the same short-day arithmetic that runs the rest of the zone
([zone page](loreto.md#how-it-fishes)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: tide, current, moon, wind direction or
time-of-day window for this ground; which side of the north end to work; or the
line class, lure or approach for it.
