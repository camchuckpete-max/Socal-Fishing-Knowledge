# Hidden Reef / 170

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is two charted marks in open water north of
Santa Barbara Island — **Hidden Reef** at **33°43.800'N 119°09.733'W** and the
**170** at **33°43.800'N 119°16.000'W** (cameron). They sit on **exactly the same
charted latitude**, **6.267 minutes of longitude** apart, which works out to
**5.2 nm** on a due east–west line: Hidden Reef bears **090°** from the 170, and
the 170 bears **270°** from Hidden Reef. At **5.2 nm** end to end this is one of
the tightest zones the census cut out of the offshore-banks list, well inside its
**12 nm** diameter cap.

**No corpus source names either mark.** Neither Hidden Reef nor the 170 appears
in a transcript, a knowledge note or an evidence file — the whole zone exists on
coordinates from [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) (cameron). This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the two
names claim, what it sits between, and what is missing. Everything below that a
source would have to supply is flagged, and those flags aggregate in
[the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

**The one thing the position tells you is what this zone is near.** It is the
northern-outer corner of the Bight's bank ground: **Santa Barbara Island is
15.7 nm south**, the unbuilt **Kidney Bank (63) / 175** pair is **11.9 nm**
southeast, and [The Boot (504) / 307](boot-504-307.md) is **22.8 nm** due east
(cameron). Santa Barbara Island is the corpus-rich neighbour — a squid,
[calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md) and
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) island with
[bluefin](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) ground off it
([Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)) — and every planning hook this
page has comes from being close to it, not from anything documented here.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run distance, heading, timing, fuel figure
or sea-state envelope to this zone from any port, and no source names a port
either mark is fished out of.

**The spot library's charted positions are all on the wrong end of the coast for
this water.** The nearest is **Pelican Point** in
[South Orange County](south-orange-county-crystal-cove.md), **65.9 nm** from
Hidden Reef and **71.1 nm** from the 170, with the Dana Point hundred-fathom
curve at **70.3 nm** and **75.5 nm** (cameron) — both bearing roughly **098°**,
back east down the coast. The library carries no charted positions on the Los
Angeles, Ventura or Santa Barbara shore, which is the shore this zone sits off,
so the distance that would actually matter to a boat running here is not
computable from the corpus. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: distance and run
time from any northern-Bight port.

**Santa Barbara Island is the nearest land, and it is behind you to the south.**
Webster Point is **15.7 nm** at **159°** from Hidden Reef and Arch Reef is
**18.1 nm** at **146°** from the 170 (cameron). The only run figure the corpus
attaches to that island is a private tournament boat leaving **Two Harbors,
Catalina** and arriving in about **an hour and 15–20 minutes**
(`PKf7G3uL4io`, [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)); this zone is a
further **15.7 nm** beyond it on a different bearing (cameron), so that figure is
a floor for the island, not a number for this water.

**Treat it as overnight range until a source says otherwise.** The jig-stick
top-shot rule puts Santa Barbara Island in the same class as San Clemente Island
— **50 lb** mono rather than the 40 lb run at the full-day-accessible islands —
because those spots are overnight range with a trophy shot at any moment
(`LTCFjqtSI8g`, [surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)). This zone lies
**beyond** that island from every charted port in the library (cameron).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states the range class of this water directly.

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries or restricted areas
stated for either position. The one MPA line the corpus knows in this part of the
Bight cuts Santa Barbara Island's east end — stay north of **33 28.500** — and
attaches to that island's Cave Canyon entry, not to these two offshore marks
(cameron, [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)). The corpus also names
the **Cow Cod Conservation Area** as an offshore closure with its own depth limit

gives no boundary for it, so whether either mark falls inside it is unverified —
**check the CCCA boundary against your position with CDFW/NOAA before dropping.**
Boundary compliance is positional, not depth-based ([US waters](us-waters.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The two positions, and the line they sit on.** Hidden Reef is at
**33°43.800'N 119°09.733'W** and the 170 at **33°43.800'N 119°16.000'W**
(cameron) — the same latitude to the thousandth of a minute, **5.2 nm** apart
east to west. The line does not stop at the zone edge: **The Boot (504)**, in the
[next zone east](boot-504-307.md), sits at **33°43.833'N**, within **0.03 minutes
of latitude** of both, so three charted marks lie on essentially one parallel
spanning **28.0 nm** end to end (cameron). The **12 nm** clustering cap is what
split them into two pages; the water does not know about the split.

**The names are the only depth claim in the zone, and only one of them makes
one.** A bank is an isolated high spot rising out of otherwise deep water and
takes the **fathom depth of its shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a
fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way:

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 170 | 170 fathoms | about 1,020 ft |
| Hidden Reef | not a number — no depth claim | — |

**The 170's figure lands inside a working band.** **170 fathoms** falls in the
lower half of the **150–350 fathom** contour band the SoCal daytime swordfish
fishery works ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md),
[deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)), and it is
deeper than the **100–250 ft** shallow Channel Islands band the rockfish router
uses ([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source confirms the number as a charted reading, or gives relief, bottom
composition or surrounding depth at either mark.

**"Reef" is the odd word in an offshore-banks list.** The spot library catalogues
Hidden Reef in its **Offshore banks (N→S)** section alongside the 170 (cameron),
but the corpus's naming layer defines only "bank" — an isolated high spot rising
off the plain (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`) — and never defines "reef". ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source says whether Hidden Reef is a bank in that sense, a shallower rock
reef, or something the fishery names for another reason entirely; the word is the
only clue, and a name is not a sounding.

**The name also collides across the library, and the collision rules do not cover
it.** Three entries carry "Hidden": **Hidden Reef** here in `socal-bight`, and
**[Hidden Bank](hidden-bank.md)** and
**[Upper Hidden Bank](upper-hidden-bank.md)** in `baja-pacific-north`, both in
[Upper 500 / Hidden Bank](upper-500-hidden-bank.md), **138.2 nm** and
**132.2 nm** south of this zone (cameron). Cameron's landmass-prefix collision
rule and his list of look-alike-but-distinct names do not name this trio
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)), so the separation currently rests on
Reef-versus-Bank alone. Confirm which "Hidden" a report means before you plot it.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: high-spot shape, preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at
either mark.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron, measured from Hidden Reef unless
noted): **Kidney Bank (63)** at **11.9 nm** and the **175** at **12.6 nm**
southeast, **Webster Point** on
[Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) at **15.7 nm**, the **172** at
**21.0 nm**, **The Boot (504)** at **22.8 nm** due east, **Osborn Bank** at
**23.0 nm** — which the census attaches to the Santa Barbara Island zone rather
than to a bank grouping — the **125** at **27.1 nm**, **Blockhouse** on
[San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md) at **29.1 nm** from the 170, **LA -
270** at **29.5 nm**, the **499** at **34.5 nm** and the
**12 Mile Reef** at **39.1 nm** northwest. ⚠ Flagged stub — no
corpus source yet: the **Kidney Bank (63) / 175** zone southeast and the
**12 Mile Reef** zone northwest are both unbuilt, so
the nearest hand-off out of this zone — the one **11.9 nm** away — has nothing to
hand off to.

**The species that would be candidates are the ones documented 15.7 nm south.**
Santa Barbara Island carries [calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md) on its
shoreline, squid-gated [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md),
[rockfish and lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md) as the stated fallback,
and [bluefin](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) in the water off it, with
[yellowfin](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) incidental on the warm side of the
Osborn Bank temperature edge ([Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)).
Of those, only the offshore ones could transfer to two open-water banks with no
kelp or shoreline documented — and none of it is scoped to this zone.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether bluefin, yellowfin, yellowtail,
[dorado](../species/dorado.md), [striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md),
[swordfish](../species/swordfish.md) or rockfish are targeted on either mark, and
nothing describes bait, squid, temperature or water colour over them.

**This zone sits north of the September volume square, not inside it.** The
**SBI – Nick – Clemente – Catalina** box is where the bigger bluefin were in
September 2022 (`5to3Q5P7w90`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[September](../seasonal/september.md)). Both marks here lie north of *every*
charted position on Santa Barbara Island and Catalina in the library — by
**14.7 nm** and **15.1 nm** of latitude respectively (cameron) — so the zone is
outside the box, off its northern edge.

**Whether the fall warm-band arc passes over this water is the open question
worth answering.** In fall the arc runs **backside Santa Cruz / Rosa → Santa
Barbara Island → backside Catalina → the 499 and Clemente** (`OYOda6T3f-8`,
`HzE4FHHcvTk`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md)). This zone sits **north of
Santa Barbara Island**, on the side the arc arrives from, which makes it
geometrically plausible as water on the first leg. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: whether either mark sits on or off that arc. The northern Channel Islands
carry **no coordinates** in the spot library
([Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)), so the leg cannot even be drawn. Pull the temperature and
chlorophyll per position and decide on the water, not on this paragraph
([water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)).

**This zone is in the scarce half of the paddy split.** The best paddy water in
the Bight is **south of San Clemente Island / Catalina** and the coastal strip
from **Dana Point to Oceanside and southward**; north of Dana Point paddies thin
out and hold fewer fish (`HzE4FHHcvTk`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)). Both marks sit **14.3 nm** north of the
northernmost charted Dana Point position (cameron), which puts them above that
line. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source describes a paddy over either position
either way; mechanics are in [kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the two are run as one circuit, which end to work
first, or what tide, light, current or swell do here.

**The geometry reads as two compact spot-checks on one leg, and no source says
so.** The Bight's two zone types are the complex — a **~50–60 square-mile** box
you plan hours of looking across — and the compact spot you idle onto, grade on
the meter, and either commit to or leave in **20–30 minutes** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). Two isolated marks **5.2 nm** apart on one
east–west line is a short enough hop to read both inside a single morning
(cameron), which is the compact pattern rather than the search-box one.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source places either mark on either side of that split.

**Wind is the risk this zone cannot answer for itself, and its neighbours argue
it is exposed.** A big NW event rolls the **northern Channel Islands** and the
**LA / Ventura coastal shelf** hardest — **5–8 °F** off the surface temperature —
while the Coronados, Catalina and San Clemente held their water through the same
event (`HzE4FHHcvTk`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). Separately, a
single August 2022 wind event was forecast to blow out the Channel Islands, San
Clemente, San Nicolas, Santa Barbara Island and the tuna zone off Catalina in one
go (`CMQkHQMxbXM`, [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)). This zone
sits **15.7 nm** north of Santa Barbara Island and **29.1 nm** north-northeast of
San Nicolas (cameron), outside every island's lee. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source classifies this water on the rollover map; treat it as an open question,
weight it down when a NW event is forecast, and pull the water per position.

**With no corpus program, the general layers are what a plan has.** Structure is
where bait stages and gamefish follow (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)), and reading a day out here falls to
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md),
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md) and
[electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md). None of them is
scoped to this water.

**Report coverage out here will be thin, and thin is not empty.** A quiet week
from Santa Barbara Island is a read on how many boats went, not on how many fish
are there (`5p6gu14ZC4w`, [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md),
[report reading](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)). These two marks
are a further **15.7 nm** out with **zero** corpus mentions (cameron), so the same
correction applies harder.

**Neither mark is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions for this zone are
pulled per charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)). The nearest eval target is the
**14 Mile Bank**, **61.5 nm** east-southeast of Hidden Reef (cameron) — far
enough that its score says nothing about this water.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron), listed west to east along the parallel they share:

- **The 170** — the western mark and the only position in the zone whose name
  reads as a depth, **170 fathoms**, about 1,020 ft. It is the zone's outer end
  in every direction that matters: furthest from the mainland at **71.1 nm** from
  Pelican Point, furthest from Santa Barbara Island at **18.1 nm** from Arch
  Reef, and the closer of the two to the **12 Mile Reef** at
  **35.8 nm** northwest (cameron). Coordinates only; the corpus never names it.
- **Hidden Reef** — the eastern mark, **5.2 nm** due east of the 170 on the same
  charted latitude, the closer of the two to everything inshore and east:
  **11.9 nm** from Kidney Bank (63), **15.7 nm** from Webster Point on
  [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) and **22.8 nm** due east to The
  Boot (504) in [The Boot (504) / 307](boot-504-307.md) (cameron). It is also the
  one name in the zone that is a word rather than a number, and the one that
  collides with the two Baja "Hidden" banks. Coordinates only.

Charted positions for both are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
