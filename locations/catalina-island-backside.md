# Catalina Island — Backside

The ocean-facing side of Catalina, running W→E from Eagle Rock at the West End
to Seal Rocks at the east end, with 21 charted spots (cameron). It is the
island's **weather side**: SoCal's prevailing wind, current and swell default out
of the west, so this shore takes the sea the front side is sheltered from
(`aFb221LUoD0`), and when the West End is blowing the backside gets worse the
further along it runs (`PKf7G3uL4io`). Three of its charted spots sit in
partial-take closures, including the standalone **Farnsworth Bank** (cameron).
It is also a station on the fall warm-band path, which is where the offshore
species show up on this side
([bight geography](bight-geography.md), [October](../seasonal/october.md)).

## Getting there

**Pick the side before you pick the spot.** Read swell direction first, then
choose east end against front side, backside or middle: a big west swell is the
trigger to send anglers down to the east end rather than the backside, and a
south swell reverses the pattern and tears up the east end specifically
(`aFb221LUoD0`). When the West End is blowing, the further back along the
backside a boat runs the worse it gets, and the front side is the shelter it
retreats to (`PKf7G3uL4io`).

Rounding the island's **east end** onto this side crosses a bathymetric slop
hotspot: the ridge off the east end stands the swell up and is rough for a few
miles, then lays down 3–4 mi past it — one bad patch on the transit, not a read
on the whole zone ([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

The shore alternates rock points, offshore rocks, coves and kelp: Eagle Rock,
Ribbon Rock, Whale Rock, Pedestal Rock and Sentinel Rock; the points at Cape
Cortes, Ben Weston Point and China Point; the coves and harbors at West Cove,
Lobster Bay, Cat Harbor and Little Harbor; the canyon-and-bluff run of Silver
Canyon and Palisades; and the east-end rocks at Church Rock, East End Light and
Seal Rocks (cameron).

**Kelp boilers** are the working feature of the shallow shore here — submerged
rock with kelp growing off it, fished by running a bait through it repeatedly
rather than around it (`mXu8vJ8yr4Q`,
[soft plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md)).

**Farnsworth Bank is a standalone bank, not shoreline.** Standalone banks rise
off the plain and fish on current from any direction, but each one has its own
preferred directions ([current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md),
[island structure](island-structure.md)). That makes it the one spot in this
zone whose read does not start from the island's shoreline orientation.

**Structure typology gates the spot before conditions do.** A ridge running out
perpendicular from an island is a two-directional spot: it produces only on
current running parallel to the shoreline, uphill or downhill; current straight
into the beach is marginal and straight off the beach is dead
([island structure](island-structure.md)). At Catalina's **~500-fathom curve**
the uphill summer counter-current compresses as it climbs the slope, spins
eddies and aggregates bait and kelp paddies along the curve (`HnqiE05vdXs`) — a
line to hunt rather than a point, and the offshore edge of this zone's water.

**No charted rockfish pinnacles are assigned here.** The spot library's Catalina
rockfish list carries backside place names — Salta Verde Cod 1–8 and the Salta
Verde Cod Pinnacle, 3rd V Cod 1–2 — but the census assigns that whole group to
[the front side zone](catalina-island-front-side.md), and the split is queued
rather than re-drawn here (cameron).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: charted depths, bottom composition, kelp
extent and working depth bands for the individual backside spots.

## How it fishes

**This is the wind-and-swell side, so the program starts with which wind is
blowing.** Individual spots have a preferred wind, not just a preferred current
direction, and a wind already blowing from the wrong quarter takes the planned
spot off the list before the boat reaches it. In a **southeast wind** the spots
where the boat lies with its **stern toward the island's west end** turn on —
**Salta Verde** and Catalina's **China Point** are named as southeast-wind
producers — while a backside spot called "Freddy's Beach" on camera stops
producing; that name is unverified against the spot library and is carried
flagged rather than confirmed (`aFb221LUoD0`,
[island structure](island-structure.md)).

**Then read current against the spot's typology, not against the calendar.**
Current direction is coherent across a region, so once the day's set is known
every point and reef on the list can be pre-judged — the perpendicular ridges
only on parallel current, and the standalone Farnsworth on whichever direction
it prefers ([current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)).

**Shallow work is anchor-and-boiler work.** Anchor **outside** Cat Harbor rather
than inside it for the kelp bite (`_KldpqPPT1c`); an anchored shallow set at West
Cove in **32 ft** is the corpus's other worked anchoring depth on this side
(`sIoNELGlxmk`). Through the boilers, a weedless-rigged swimbait on a
keel-weighted hook comes back clean pass after pass (`mXu8vJ8yr4Q`,
[soft plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md),
[swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md)).

**Offshore of the shore, the fish are found on dolphin pods.** Get well out in
front of a moving pod and never cut through it; for yellowfin specifically the
fish sit on the **edges — up in front and on the flanks** of a feeding pod, and
can foam out as much as **50 yd** ahead of it. The lure is a small
tungsten-headed finesse trolling lure pulled on **60 or 80 lb** straight to the
lure at **7–9 mph** (`ouBrIdO7d4k`) — full execution in
[trolling](../techniques/trolling.md#socal-backside-of-catalina--porpoise-associated-yellowfin-finesse-trolling).

## Spots

Twenty-one charted spots, grouped as the run reads W→E:

- **West End** — Eagle Rock, West Cove, Iron Bound, Ribbon Rock, Whale Rock.
  This is the end that blows first and hardest (`PKf7G3uL4io`); West Cove is the
  corpus's named shallow anchoring example (`sIoNELGlxmk`).
- **Cape Cortes to Cat Harbor** — Cape Cortes, Lobster Bay, Cat Harbor. The
  bass program anchors outside Cat Harbor, and the harbor itself holds a
  partial-take closure (`_KldpqPPT1c`, cameron).
- **Mid-backside** — Pedestal Rock, Little Harbor, Sentinel Rock, Ben Weston
  Point. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: how any of these four fish.
- **Standalone bank** — Farnsworth Bank, off the shoreline and inside a
  partial-take closure with a yellowtail-and-bonito keep list (`5p6gu14ZC4w`).
- **China Point to the 3 V's** — China Point, Salta Verde, Silver Canyon,
  Palisades, 3 V's. China Point and Salta Verde are the named southeast-wind
  producers (`aFb221LUoD0`).
- **East end** — Church Rock, East End Light, Seal Rocks. The east end is the
  side-selection escape valve on a big west swell and the ground a south swell
  tears up (`aFb221LUoD0`), and the ridge off it is a transit slop hotspot
  ([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

Charted positions for all twenty-one spots are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md); the per-spot pages carry them.
