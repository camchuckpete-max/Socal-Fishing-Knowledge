# E. Butterfly / San Salvador Knoll

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is two charted marks in open water at the
southern edge of the Bight — the **San Salvador Knoll** at **32°19.000'N
117°54.000'W** and the **E. Butterfly** at **32°16.417'N 118°06.000'W**
(cameron). They lie **2.6 minutes of latitude** and **12.0 minutes of longitude**
apart, which works out to **10.5 nm**: the Knoll bears **076°** from the
E. Butterfly, and the E. Butterfly bears **256°** back. It is a nearly due
east–west pair, and the whole zone is that one line.

**No corpus source names either mark.** Neither the E. Butterfly nor the San
Salvador Knoll appears anywhere in the transcript corpus, in a knowledge note
or in an evidence file — the zone exists on coordinates from
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) (cameron). This is therefore a
**position-and-ladder page**: it says where the two marks are, what their names
do and do not claim, what water they sit in, and what a source would have to
supply. The [region page](socal-bight.md) already carries this zone as a
flagged stub, and nothing here lifts that stub. The flags below aggregate in
[the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

**The pair only just held together.** At **10.5 nm** the two are inside the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap with **1.5 nm** to spare, and neither was
attached on distance — both clustered on their own coordinates (cameron). But
the Knoll's nearest charted neighbour of all is not the E. Butterfly: it is
**Coronados - 230** at **10.1 nm**, in
[Coronados - 230 / 302 (Kidney Bank) / 226](coronados-230-302-226.md). The
E. Butterfly's nearest neighbour outside the zone is its charted counterpart the
**W. Butterfly** at **11.5 nm**, in [W. Butterfly / 157](w-butterfly-157.md).
Read that as a warning about the seams: each of these two marks has a mark in
an adjacent zone about as close as its own zone partner.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a stated distance, heading, run time or fuel
figure to either mark, from any port, and no source names a port this zone is
fished out of.

**The two marks look to different land, which is unusual for a two-spot zone.**
The nearest charted island position in the library to the **E. Butterfly** is
**Desperation Reef** on
[San Clemente Island — Back Side](san-clemente-island-back-side.md), **33.1 nm**
away, with **Pukey Point** in the [Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) a
further **8.8 nm** off at **41.9 nm**. For the **San Salvador Knoll** the order
reverses: **Pukey Point** at **31.4 nm** and Desperation Reef at **37.0 nm**
(cameron). The E. Butterfly is Clemente-facing water and the Knoll is
Coronados-facing water, **10.5 nm** apart.

**The mainland is a long run either way.** The nearest charted mainland-coast
position in the library is the
[International Artificial Reef](international-artificial-reef.md) off Imperial
Beach at **35.7 nm** from the Knoll and **46.1 nm** from the E. Butterfly, with
the **Point Loma Pipe** off [Point Loma](point-loma.md) at **37.8 nm** and
**47.8 nm** and **Widow Rock** in [La Jolla](la-jolla.md) at **38.1 nm** and
**47.4 nm** (cameron). Those are straight-line figures from charted marks,
not distances from the ports a boat actually leaves. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: run time from any named port, or the sea-state envelope this crossing
demands.

**The Navy rule belongs to San Clemente Island, not to these marks.** San
Clemente Island is a U.S. Navy training range and which areas are open rotates
with the Navy's schedule
([island structure](island-structure.md#san-clemente-island--navy-controlled-access)).
The nearer of these two marks lies **33.1 nm** off its back side (cameron), so
that rule governs a transit past the island rather than the fishing here.
**Verify current access with the Navy before working the island end of such a
run.**

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries or restricted areas
stated for either position. The corpus names the **Cow Cod Conservation Area**
as an offshore closure with its own depth limit and its own as-of date
([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)) but gives no boundary for
it, so whether either mark falls inside it is unverified — **check the CCCA
boundary against your position with CDFW/NOAA before dropping.** Boundary
compliance is positional, not depth-based ([US waters](us-waters.md)).

**Mexican-region water lies close on three sides, so the border question is
live here.** The census assigns this zone to the Bight, but the **421** —
**11.1 nm** from the Knoll — and **Upper Cross**, **390** and the **60 Mile
Bank (53 Fa.)** at **14.5–19.7 nm** are all parented to
[Upper Cross / 421 / 390](upper-cross-421-390.md) in
[Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md), and the
[Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) **31.4 nm** east of the Knoll are on the
Mexican side too (cameron, [regions & waters](regions.md)). The census's split
is a modelling approximation, not a charted border. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source gives the maritime boundary's position, so **confirm which side of it you
are on before you fish** — the paperwork on the far side is an FMM plus a
CONAPESCA licence ([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)), not a CDFW licence.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The two positions.** The San Salvador Knoll sits at **32°19.000'N
117°54.000'W** and the E. Butterfly at **32°16.417'N 118°06.000'W** (cameron) —
**10.5 nm** apart on a **076°/256°** line, with **12.0 minutes** of the
separation in longitude and only **2.6 minutes** in latitude. Reading both is a
single east–west leg, not a box.

**Neither name claims a depth, and that is what sets this zone apart from the
numbered grid around it.** A bank is an isolated high spot rising out of
otherwise deep water, named for the **fathom depth of its shallowest point**, at
six feet to a fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Every
*numbered* mark within **20 nm** of this pair follows that convention — the
**421**, the **390**, the **300**, the **371**, the **302**, the **226**, the
**157** (cameron) — and neither mark here carries a number to read. *Butterfly* is
a shape name and *Knoll* is a landform word:

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| San Salvador Knoll | no number in the name | — |
| E. Butterfly | no number in the name | — |

The practical consequence is that this zone gives a planner **nothing to
sound against** before the trip. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any depth,
relief, high-spot shape, bottom composition or surrounding depth at either
mark. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says what *Butterfly* denotes, what a
**knoll** is as distinct from a bank or a seamount, or who *San Salvador* was —
the same **knoll** question is open on
[Davis Knoll / San Miguel Gap / Rodriguez Seamount](davis-knoll-san-miguel-gap-rodriguez-seamount.md).

**The two Butterflies are a charted east/west pair split across two zones.**
The E. Butterfly's counterpart, the **W. Butterfly**, is **11.5 nm** away at
**299°** — inside the 12 nm cap on its own — but joining the two would have put
the **157** at **19.6 nm** from the E. Butterfly and breached the cap, so the
pair sits either side of a zone line with the W. Butterfly parented to
[W. Butterfly / 157](w-butterfly-157.md) (cameron). That page states the same
split from its own side. The same east/west naming runs on **East 400** and
**West 400** in the Baja grid, recorded as the exception to the
landmass-prefix collision rule ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), cameron).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says whether the two Butterflies are one
structure or two, or which of them was named first. The working consequence:
**a report that says "the Butterfly" without a prefix does not tell you which
mark, or which of two zone pages to plan off.**

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: preferred directions, drift behaviour or current set at either mark.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron): **Coronados - 230** at
**10.1 nm** from the Knoll, the **421** at **11.1 nm** from the Knoll, the
**W. Butterfly** at **11.5 nm** from the E. Butterfly, the **60 Mile Bank
(53 Fa.)** at **14.5 nm** from the E. Butterfly, the **390** at **14.7 nm** from
the Knoll, **Upper Cross** at **17.2 nm** from the E. Butterfly, the **226** at
**17.2 nm**, the **300** at **17.6 nm**, the **302 (Kidney Bank)** at
**17.8 nm** and the **371** at **18.2 nm** from the Knoll, and the **157** at
**19.6 nm** from the E. Butterfly. Three of those groupings are
already built on this ladder —
[Coronados - 230 / 302 (Kidney Bank) / 226](coronados-230-302-226.md),
[W. Butterfly / 157](w-butterfly-157.md) and
[43 / 91 / 300](43-91-300.md) — and
[Upper Cross / 421 / 390](upper-cross-421-390.md) carries the 421, the 390,
Upper Cross and the 60 Mile Bank across the census's boundary line. **This zone
is the best-surrounded thin page on the ladder**: it has no corpus of its own,
but a fallback plan out of it has four built neighbours inside **20 nm** in
four different directions.

**The one Bight-wide pattern that does scope to this water is paddy geography.**
The best paddy water in the Bight is **south of San Clemente Island / Catalina**
and along the coastal strip from Dana Point to Oceanside and southward; north of
Dana Point paddies thin out and hold fewer fish (`HzE4FHHcvTk`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)). Both marks lie **26.5–29.1 nm** of
latitude south of San Clemente Island's southernmost charted position (cameron),
which puts the whole zone well inside that named band — and unlike the marks
abeam the island further north, there is no ambiguity about which side of the
line it is on. That is a statement about the water, not about the banks:
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source describes a paddy over either position. Paddy
mechanics are in [kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md), and the species
that ride them are [bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md),
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md),
[yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) and
[dorado](../species/dorado.md).

**The fall condensation runs well west of this zone.** In the 2022-10-20 Bight
report the bluefin volume had slid out to Tanner and Cortez, the water being
worked running "from the 381 off the east end back side of Clementi out to
Tanner and Cortez" (`OYOda6T3f-8`, [October](../seasonal/october.md)). This zone
sits southeast of that corridor's inshore end and a long way inshore of its
outer end — **53.2 nm** from Cortes Bank and **58.3 nm** from Tanner Bank at the
E. Butterfly, further still from the Knoll (cameron). Whatever that band is
doing, it is not doing it here.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the two are run as one east–west circuit, or
what tide, light, current or swell do here.

**The geometry reads as two spot-checks rather than a search box, but no source
says so.** The Bight's two zone types are the complex — a **~50–60 square-mile**
box you plan hours of looking across — and the compact spot you idle onto, grade
on the meter, and either commit to or leave in **20–30 minutes** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). Two isolated marks
**10.5 nm** apart with open water between them (cameron) is the shape of a pair
of spot-checks with a short transit, not of a box. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source places either mark on either side of that split.

**With no corpus program, the general layers are what a plan has.** SoCal fish
orient to structure and then to current edges, and the banger days come when the
two line up; there is enough water on this coast that current edges get fished
as much as structure does (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`,
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). With nothing
recorded here — and no depth even claimed by the names — break position and
break age decide whether this zone is on a given day's plan
([report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)),
and the meter decides once you are there
([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)). From
October the regime flips to reading **100 % the temperature chart and
chlorophyll** (`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)), crossing the warmest water available
against a chlorophyll chart for the cleanest (`OYOda6T3f-8`,
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)). None of those layers is scoped
to this water.

**The wind map is stated for the neighbours, not for these two marks.** South of
Newport is "less prone to wind-wave-driven rollovers", and the Coronados, the
tuna grounds, Catalina and San Clemente Island all held their water through a NW
event that pulled **5–8 °F** off the northern Channel Islands and the LA /
Ventura shelf (`YZtX1MiT0y8`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). Two of those
named holders bracket this zone — the Coronado Islands **31.4 nm** east of the
Knoll and San Clemente Island **33.1 nm** north of the E. Butterfly (cameron) —
so the pattern surrounds it without naming it. Exposure is a separate question
from temperature, and open water this far out is
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md) first.

**Neither mark is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions here are pulled per
charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)). The nearest eval target is the
**302**, **17.8 nm** east-northeast of the Knoll and **28.2 nm** from the
E. Butterfly (cameron) — close enough to be the reference for the Knoll end of
the pair, too far off to stand in for the E. Butterfly.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron), listed west to east — the axis this zone is drawn
on. Both clustered on their own coordinates; neither was attached on distance:

- **The E. Butterfly** — the western mark, and the eastern half of a charted
  east/west pair whose other half, the **W. Butterfly**, is **11.5 nm** away in
  [W. Butterfly / 157](w-butterfly-157.md). It is the closer of the two to San
  Clemente Island (**33.1 nm** to Desperation Reef), to the outer banks
  (**53.2 nm** to Cortes Bank) and to the Mexican-region marks southwest
  (**14.5 nm** to the 60 Mile Bank (53 Fa.)). Coordinates only; the corpus never
  names it.
- **The San Salvador Knoll** — the eastern mark, **10.5 nm** at **076°** from
  the E. Butterfly, and the closer of the two to everything inshore: **10.1 nm**
  to Coronados - 230, **31.4 nm** to Pukey Point in the Coronado Islands and
  **37.8 nm** to the Point Loma Pipe (cameron). Its nearest charted neighbour is
  in another zone, **0.4 nm** closer than its own zone partner. Coordinates
  only.

Charted positions for both are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
