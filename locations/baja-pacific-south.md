# Baja Pacific — South

**The character rung.** The Pacific side below the BC/BCS state line at 28°N —
Bahía Asunción, Magdalena Bay, Alijos Rocks, round to Cabo San Lucas
([regions & waters](regions.md)). Nothing here is a day trip from a US port.
The corpus reaches this region three ways: on the San Diego long-range fleet's
six-, seven- and eight-day trips (`tjBeR9tWd4s`), on a flight into Loreto and a
drive across to Magdalena Bay (`EcQK5elTPJE`), and on charters out of Cabo San
Lucas (`ll7r4A6atno`). What it offers is warm-water fishing — wahoo, 100 lb
yellowfin, striped marlin in numbers, dorado — over banks **450–488 mi** south
of San Diego (`_r08B4bItAY`), plus one bay complex that fishes like four
separate fisheries stacked in the same place. The paperwork rung is
[Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md).

## The fishery

**The region is a long-range destination first.** Alijos Rocks is rock
structure running "from the surface all the way to the bottom"
(`97clKtVsEOs`), fished as a dedicated stop on the fleet's six-, seven- and
eight-day trips (`tjBeR9tWd4s`). "The Ridge" is the alternative on the same
trip type — a seven-day trip books "one or the other" — and is resolved on
camera as running to an upper high spot named **Uncle Sam Bank**
(`_r08B4bItAY`). The tackle a seven-day trip to those two grounds calls for is
its own note: [Searcher five-outfit quiver](../tackle/searcher-alijos-rocks-ridge-7-day-quiver.md).

**Wahoo is what this region is known for.** October and November are named as
"the best time of year to catch wahoo" (`9IhmYstB8sA`), restated as fall —
"late September, October, early November" (`4uNPLknRAQg`) — and the boats'
six-, seven- and eight-day trips fish Alijos Rocks and the Ridge for them
(`tjBeR9tWd4s`). Magdalena Bay holds a healthy wahoo population "come fall
time" (`4t_Z75shK_E`), where the stated key is getting out early in the
morning (`EcQK5elTPJE`). Execution is in [wahoo](../species/wahoo.md),
[wahoo trolling](../techniques/wahoo-trolling.md) and
[wahoo bomb & Raider jig casting](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md).

**The grade is heavier than anything north of here, and it changes the rig.**
Alijos Rocks carries a **100 lb** [yellowfin](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md)
chance (`_r08B4bItAY`); one stop graded the catch **70 lb up to a couple
around 80 lb** (`6kpWn2sXokI`) and another leg was planned for **60–90 lb,
possibly a few over 100 lb** (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`), while the "lower banks" near
Magdalena Bay ran giant yellowfin at **150 to 300-plus lb** (`UuyqTE21-kc`).
[Yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) at Alijos Rocks and the Ridge run
bigger and meaner with more rock underneath, which is the stated trigger for
stepping up from the base yo-yo rig to a two-speed Penn Fathom 40 Narrow,
**80 lb braid** under a **60 lb** top shot on a **30–80 lb** rod
(`RNRFrfepiW0`).

**Deep water sets the jig.** Alijos Rocks and the Ridge sit in the deepest of
three [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) tiers, where the pick is a
Salas 7X heavy rather than the 6X or 6X Jr fished at 60–150 ft — more time in
the strike zone on the straight up-and-down (`hteLeDIy9Qs`). A yellowtail came
off the Ridge yo-yoing at **180 ft, 10 ft off the bottom** (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`).

**Magdalena Bay is four fisheries in one bay** (`EcQK5elTPJE`): a mangrove
interior at **12–15 ft** whose [snook](../species/snook.md) program works down
to **2 ft** tight to oyster-covered roots; the boca, about **5 minutes** by
boat from the dock in **40–50 ft**, holding grouper to **100 lb** and snook to
at least **42 lb**; a mid-range tuna program **10–12 mi** out over **200–300
ft** for **20–50 lb** fish; and offshore banks named on camera only as "the 23
and the 38". Full chain: [Lopez Mateos & Magdalena
Bay](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md).

**Striped marlin in numbers is that bay's headline.** The bay is described as
"loaded with stripe marlin... they're everywhere" in fall (`4t_Z75shK_E`), and
one presenter's own best day there was **72 marlin**, with the boat capable of
"over a hundred" the same day (`gimFbgN5Jjk`). Because the October–November
visitors come for that, the bottom and jig fishery in the same water is
"virtually untapped" — a single day of jig fishing produced **14–15 species**,
including Colorado snappers, "broom tail" and gulf groupers, and yellowtail
(`gimFbgN5Jjk`). Route: [striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md).

**The inshore edge is a different fishery again.** At La Bocana the inshore
rock structure is worked as a distinct "Barracuda grounds" band that a boat
leaves behind on the **25 mi** run out to the tuna grounds (`Cobp85UvHmM`);
jigs fished to roughly **80 ft** on that structure drew a hookup "every single
drop", in a mixed bag with yellowtail, bonito, sand bass and calico bass
(`qri15R3caYE`). At Cabo San Lucas the surf line itself is trolled — small
hoochies on wire at roughly **6 kt** for sierra, best early morning or right at
sunset, and better in dirty lime-green water than in clear (`ll7r4A6atno`).
Cabo's offshore water is characteristically flat in the morning and builds
choppy through the afternoon as heat off the land mass drives wind
(`XH-Hrfet6To`), and its current breaks show as a whitewater/foam line running
against a slicker patch angling offshore, worked back and forth rather than
from one side (`dgauGbNxP84`;
[current diagnostics](../conditions/current-diagnostics.md)).

**The species mix is warm-water pelagic over bank, plus a bay:**

| water | what this region offers |
| --- | --- |
| open-ocean | [wahoo](../species/wahoo.md), [yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md), [dorado](../species/dorado.md), [striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md), [skipjack](../species/skipjack-tuna.md) |
| bank | [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md), [wahoo](../species/wahoo.md), [yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) |
| nearshore-coast | [barracuda](../species/barracuda.md), [bonito](../species/bonito.md), [calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md), [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md), [snook](../species/snook.md) |
| bay-harbor | [snook](../species/snook.md), [California halibut](../species/california-halibut.md), grouper, pargo/snapper |

Sierra, sand bass at La Bocana, grouper, pargo/snapper, palometa and black sea
bass are all named in the corpus for this region but carry no router note of
their own — single thin mentions, recorded here as context rather than linked.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the coast between Punta Eugenia and
Magdalena Bay apart from La Bocana. Bahía Asunción appears in the region
vocabulary ([regions & waters](regions.md)) and nowhere else in the corpus.

## Season shape

Priors, not current intel, and the `seasonal/` calendar these normally rest on
is written for the [Bight](socal-bight.md). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a
month-by-month prior for the Baja Pacific south — the rows below are dated
observations, not a calendar.

| window | what is dated here |
| --- | --- |
| Winter (Dec–Feb) | Cabo runs an inshore-and-offshore January program: a surf-line sierra troll that also drew a dorado (`ll7r4A6atno`, 2021-01-19) and skipjack trolled back and forth across a current break (`dgauGbNxP84`, 2021-01-28). Two big blue marlin struck trolled lures without staying hooked on a December 2014 Cabo charter (`Mwx5AAXNMvE`). La Bocana was fished in **February 2018** — inshore rock structure for barracuda, bonito and yellowtail, then a 25 mi run offshore for tuna, marlin and wahoo (`qri15R3caYE`, `Cobp85UvHmM`). Gray whales arrive at Magdalena Bay "about mid-January" (`EcQK5elTPJE`) |
| Spring (Mar–May) | Thin, and what exists carries upload dates rather than fishing dates: an Intrepid long-range series working the Magdalena-Bay-to-Alijos-Rocks corridor (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`, uploaded 2012-05-01; `6kpWn2sXokI`, uploaded 2012-05-08) covering a Thetis Bank stop, the Ridge, and a couple of days at the rocks. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any spring pattern for this region with a stated fishing date |
| Summer (Jun–Aug) | Some seven- and eight-day trips leave as early as **July** to be the first boat at Alijos Rocks or the Ridge, if the water is warm enough (`4uNPLknRAQg`). At Magdalena Bay more of the big-ocean species — marlin, tuna, wahoo, dorado — show up through the summer (`EcQK5elTPJE`) |
| Fall (Sep–Nov) | The region's season. The Searcher's seven-day trips to Alijos Rocks or the Ridge run late **September**/early **October**, with a wahoo chance "at the rocks or on the ridge or on kelp paddies down in that area" alongside 100 lb yellowfin and dorado (`_r08B4bItAY`); a **September 2010** Alijos trip is dated on camera (`_f5QmWSUUx8`, `jH0q4UPjwC0`). Capt. Mike Tahr's wahoo trip in the **second week of November 2019** closed that season with "tremendous catches" (`4uNPLknRAQg`). Magdalena Bay's marlin numbers are an **October–November** draw (`gimFbgN5Jjk`), and the fall 2020 Mag Bay footage runs across three trips (`4t_Z75shK_E`, `isXJONlpUP4`, `OVwqUKim9Pc`). Giant yellowfin of **130 to 350 lb** move up into the Magdalena-Bay-to-Cabo "lower banks" area every fall to breed and spawn, with one **October** arrival dated on camera (`UuyqTE21-kc`). A **November 25** stop on the "lower banks" anchored on one giant-tuna school for four days without moving — the year is unresolved on camera (`ASitOLYzFEA`) |

**Two claims about Magdalena Bay's year sit side by side** (`EcQK5elTPJE`).
Dave Hansen: there is no time of year it doesn't bite at all — the inside
grouper fishing is "phenomenal" through winter, and more of the big-ocean
species arrive through summer. Larry Hansen, as a stated personal preference:
**mid-September through the end of December** is the pick for the best
combination of variety and volume. The choice turns on what you are travelling
for rather than on which claim is right — the first says the bay always has
something open, the second ranks the windows by variety and volume — and the
order here is not a ranking.

**Warm water, not the calendar, is the gate offshore.** **72 °F or warmer** is
the standing condition for a wahoo chance, and the stated mechanism for fall
being best is that the water warms incrementally through summer and can carry
a hurricane-driven warm push by then (`4uNPLknRAQg`) — which is why the July
trips run only if the water has come up. Too warm closes ground as well: a
scouting pass on the Ridge was abandoned because "the water was just too warm"
(`97clKtVsEOs`). See [water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md).

## Zones

**Three zones, and none of them come from coordinates.** The charted sections
of [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) stop at San Quintín, so every
zone on this page is derived from corpus depth instead of from a position
list. The consequence is concrete: this region publishes no coordinates at
all, so a day plan cannot pull per-spot conditions here the way it can in the
[Bight](socal-bight.md) or [Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md). Zone
pages land with the zone phase, so unlinked names below have no page yet.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. CDFW rules do not apply on this side of the line, and a
Mexican limit differing from a California one is two jurisdictions, not a
conflict. Verify current requirements before you fish
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)).** ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
Mexican closures, protected areas or depth restrictions anywhere in this
region.

| zone | spots | character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alijos Rocks | corpus zone, no charted spots in the library | The dedicated long-range stop: rock running surface-to-bottom (`97clKtVsEOs`), a wahoo troll and a 100 lb-yellowfin flyline in the same day (`_r08B4bItAY`), and the deepest yo-yo tier in the KB (`hteLeDIy9Qs`) |
| [Lopez Mateos & Magdalena Bay](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md) | corpus zone, no charted spots in the library | Four fisheries in one bay — mangrove interior, boca, mid-range tuna, offshore banks — and the one place in this region that stays fishable when it blows (`EcQK5elTPJE`) |
| Cabo San Lucas | corpus zone, no charted spots in the library | The inshore/offshore charter end of the region: surf-line sierra trolling and dorado on the beach side (`ll7r4A6atno`), marlin on trolled lure heads offshore (`XH-Hrfet6To`, `Mwx5AAXNMvE`) |

**Named grounds the census derives no zone for** — logged rather than
omitted, because silence would read as "nothing there":

- **"The Ridge" / Uncle Sam Bank** — ⚠ Flagged stub. The standing alternative
  to Alijos Rocks on the same seven-day trips, "famous for yellowtail and
  black sea bass and grouper and yellowfin tuna" in that window
  (`_r08B4bItAY`), and one of the two grounds the fall wahoo trips fish
  (`tjBeR9tWd4s`).
- **Thetis Bank** — ⚠ Flagged stub. Left overnight because the current was

- **"Modesto Main"** — ⚠ Flagged stub. Magdalena Bay's closer fallback bank,

  mouth looks rough (`OVwqUKim9Pc`).
- **Bank 23 and Bank 38** — ⚠ Flagged stub. Magdalena Bay's own offshore
  banks, fished in company with San Diego long-range boats (`EcQK5elTPJE`).
- **The "lower banks"** — ⚠ Flagged stub. The giant-yellowfin ground, named on
  camera as the area running from Magdalena Bay down toward Cabo San Lucas,
  150 to 300-plus lb (`UuyqTE21-kc`, `ASitOLYzFEA`).
- **La Bocana** — ⚠ Flagged stub. Inshore rock structure plus a 25 mi offshore
  troll (`Cobp85UvHmM`, `qri15R3caYE`);
  [barracuda](../species/barracuda.md) already carries a zone-guide stub for
  it.

## Access & range

**Every ground here is a multi-day commitment, and the run distances are the
reason.** Alijos Rocks is **488 mi** from San Diego, a **45-hour run** leaving
around 11:00 with bait obtained the next morning; the Ridge and Uncle Sam Bank
are **450–460 mi** to the upper end, a **two-day run** (`_r08B4bItAY`). A
separate source places Alijos Rocks "roughly 400 mi south of San Diego"
(`97clKtVsEOs`). The two figures are 88 mi apart and nothing in the corpus
reconciles them — the larger one comes with a stated run time and a departure
hour, the smaller one with neither, which is what to weigh if you are planning
off them. Inside the region, the transit from near Magdalena Bay to Alijos
Rocks is **160 mi, about 16 hours** (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`). Trips are six, seven and
eight days out of San Diego (`tjBeR9tWd4s`, `4uNPLknRAQg`).

**Magdalena Bay is reached from the Cortez side, not by boat from the north.**
The route is a flight into [Loreto](loreto.md), then a drive through Ciudad
Constitución with a dirt-road final stretch into Lopez Mateos (`EcQK5elTPJE`).
Once there the distances collapse: the boca is about **5 minutes** by boat from
the dock, and the mid-range tuna grounds **10–12 mi** out are a half-day
program with the boat back at the dock by **10:00** (`EcQK5elTPJE`).

**That bay is the region's weather answer.** When it is blowing outside, the
inside stays flat — wind chop only, no swell — so a blown-out offshore day is
still fishable inside, and multiple consecutive fishable days are described as
routine through a run of bad outside weather by moving between zones
(`EcQK5elTPJE`). Two dated instances show the pattern working: no boats had
been out of Magdalena Bay for a couple of days after a run of wind
(`4t_Z75shK_E`), and a crew that got beaten up offshore chasing marlin
switched the next day to the estuary and mangroves rather than run out again
(`isXJONlpUP4`). The bank choice itself is made by idling out past the estuary
mouth to read the wave state before committing (`OVwqUKim9Pc`;
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

**The paperwork gate applies to every one of these.** FMM, CONAPESCA licence
and passport ([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: what a long-range passenger out of San Diego must carry for these
grounds, or how a fly-in through Loreto differs.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: ports, ramps, fuel and haul-out anywhere in
this region — nothing on the Cabo San Lucas marina or its run distances
despite several sourced Cabo days, and nothing on La Bocana's beach access
beyond the 25 mi offshore run.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: sea-state and weather geography outside
Magdalena Bay — which grounds a blow shuts down, and how far up the coast
Cabo's morning-flat/afternoon-chop pattern (`XH-Hrfet6To`) extends.
