# 483/500 / 437

**A zone is a run grouping.** 483/500 / 437 is a piece of the numbered
offshore-bank grid in open water west of Ensenada — three charted positions
(cameron) strung roughly **north–south along the 118°W meridian**, from the
**437** at 118°12.000'W down to **East 400** at 118°00.750'W, spanning
**18.4 minutes of latitude** against **11.25 minutes of longitude**. The same
meridian carries **Upper Cross** 35.8 nm north of the zone's centre and **Lower
Cross** 25.0 nm south of it ([Upper Cross / 421 / 390](upper-cross-421-390.md)),
so this zone is a middle segment of a line of charted marks rather than a
compact cluster.

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the parenthetical attributes both Cross
marks to [Upper Cross / 421 / 390](upper-cross-421-390.md), but that zone's
children are the 390, the 421, the 60 Mile Bank and Upper Cross only. Lower
Cross carries its own `type: zone` page (`locations/lower-cross.md`), parented
straight to the region. The 35.8 nm and 25.0 nm figures both recompute
exactly; the zone the second one is filed under does not.

**The zone is a tight pair plus an attached outlier.** The 437 and the 483/500
are **7.9 nm** apart and clustered on coordinates inside the census's **12 nm**
zone-diameter cap; **East 400** joined as a lone bank, **17.5 nm** from that
pair's midpoint and inside the **25 nm** attach radius the census allows a
bank with no cluster of its own. The result is a zone **20.7 nm** corner to
corner — wider than the cap that built its core — though from the finished
three-spot centre every mark falls inside 12 nm (**3.7**, **9.2** and
**11.7 nm**). **East 400 is closer to a mark in another zone than to either of
its zone-mates**: **13.6 nm** to Lower Cross on a bearing of **177°**, against
**14.6 nm** to the 483/500 and **20.7 nm** to the 437 (cameron). That is left as
a queued question for Cameron, not a re-parenting — the zone list is the
census's.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these three marks.** No source
names the 483/500, the 437 or East 400, and the nearest documented fishing is a
2020 bluefin corridor whose southern anchor sits **38.9 nm** north of the zone's
nearest mark (`49joKHD7Umc`, cameron). The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered grid — fifty-one charted positions over seventeen bank zones with no
depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
spot names claim, and what is missing.

**Two of the library's naming problems meet here.** The **483/500** is the only
**slash-numbered name in the entire spot library** — every other mark carries a
single number, a word name, or a number in parentheses
([the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)). And **East 400** is one half of
the library's single stated exception to the landmass-prefix collision rule,
paired with **West 400** ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). Both are covered
below.

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the 483/500 is not the only slash-numbered
name in the library — [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) also lists
**279 / 267** at **33°17.800'N 117°49.150'W**, in the same slash form and also
carrying two numbers, so neither this superlative nor the "only name in the
library carrying two numbers" line in *Structure & bathymetry* below holds.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The zone is a dogleg down one meridian.** The 437 sits at **31°32.000'N
118°12.000'W**, the 483/500 at **31°28.000'N 118°04.000'W** and East 400 at
**31°13.633'N 118°00.750'W** (cameron). The 437 to the 483/500 runs **7.9 nm** on
**120°**; the 483/500 to East 400 runs **14.6 nm** on **169°** — east-southeast,
then very nearly due south. The 437 to East 400 is **20.7 nm** and is what sets
the zone's span. Measured from the centre of the
three, the marks lie **3.7 nm** (483/500), **9.2 nm** (437) and **11.7 nm**
(East 400) out.

**The zone's charted neighbours sit close on three sides.** The **250** is
**14.2 nm** west-southwest of the centre on **246°**, parented to
[372 / 245 / 250](372-245-250.md); the **1067 Knuckle** is **16.4 nm** northeast
on **041°** and **The Airplane** **25.5 nm** north on **357°**, both in
[1010 Trench / 378 / 213](1010-trench-378-213.md); the **245** is **21.0 nm** and
the **372** **22.9 nm** west; **West 400** and the **Sniffer** are **24.4** and
**24.9 nm** southwest, and **Lower Cross** **25.0 nm** south (cameron). Nothing
about this stretch of grid is isolated — the zone boundaries here are a
clustering cap, not a break in the bottom. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: no
source says which of these marks are fished together, or whether the 118°W line
is run as a line at all.

**The names are the only depth claims in the zone, and they read deeper than
anything the KB fishes.** A bank takes the **fathom depth of its shallowest
point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
`Rf1HKJG-SDg`):

| mark | name read as depth | at six feet to a fathom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| East 400 | 400 fathoms | about 2,400 ft |
| 437 | 437 fathoms | about 2,622 ft |
| 483/500 | 483–500 fathoms | about 2,898–3,000 ft |

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): `Rf1HKJG-SDg` states the naming convention
as a tendency, not a rule — "most of them … are called after the highest part of
the bank on the fish finder" — and names its own exceptions in the same breath:
"the nine mile bank that's not nine miles deep, that's nine miles off the coast"
and "14 is 14 miles off the beach". It also adds a second condition this table
drops, that depth alone does not make a bank ("there are places all over the
ocean that are 182 but they're not a bank"). The six-feet-to-a-fathom half is
verbatim. The source is describing SoCal banks throughout and never scopes the
convention to the Baja numbered grid.

**All three read below the deepest band the corpus works.** The swordfish router
gives **150–350 fathoms** for the contour lines, thumbs, notches and humps worth
fishing ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)), and SoCal daytime deep-drop baits
average **800–1,100 ft** ([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md))
— above the bottom every name here claims, the shallowest of them by more than
1,300 ft. That band is stated for SoCal water, not for this coast. Read
literally these are marks you would drift baits *over*, not bottom you would
fish. For contrast, the neighbouring [372 / 245 / 250](372-245-250.md) and
[Baja - 270 / Double 220 / 295](baja-270-double-220-295.md) names read 220–372
fathoms, inside or near that band; this zone starts where they end. ⚠ Flagged
gap — no corpus source confirms any of these numbers as a charted depth, and
nothing in the KB says whether the fathom convention holds at all for names in
the four hundreds.

**The slash is undefined.** ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says what
**483/500** denotes — two soundings on one mark, two peaks fished as one spot, a
range across the high spot, or a mark recorded twice and merged. It is the only
name in the library carrying two numbers, and the library's "distinct, not
collisions" list — which does resolve *475* against *475 Knuckle* and *220*
against *Double 220* — does not address it ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): "the only name in the library carrying two
numbers" is the same superlative flagged in the introduction above — the library
also lists **279 / 267**.

**There are three 500s in the library and no rule covering them.** **Upper 500**
at **31°46.000'N 117°30.000'W** lies **34.1 nm** northeast of the 483/500 on
**058°** ([Upper 500 / Hidden Bank](upper-500-hidden-bank.md)), and **Lower 500**
at **31°10.500'N 117°02.500'W** **55.4 nm** east-southeast on **108°**; the two
named halves of that pair are **42.5 nm** apart, with the 483/500 sitting west of
both rather than between them (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says
whether the 483/500 belongs to the Upper/Lower 500 family or is an unrelated mark
that shares a number.

**The 400 pair checks out, with one wrinkle.** The library records East 400 and
West 400 as "both Baja offshore, roughly **44 km** apart east–west at the same
latitude," the stated exception to the landmass-prefix rule
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), cameron). The charted positions give
**23.9 nm — 44.2 km** — on a bearing of **081°** west to east, so the distance is
exact. The latitudes are close rather than identical: East 400 at
**31°13.633'N** against West 400 at **31°09.800'N**, **3.8 minutes** apart
(cameron). West 400 is parented to a different zone, so the pair sits either side
of a zone line the way the two Butterflies do
([W. Butterfly / 157](w-butterfly-157.md)).

**The general read on a bank is the only structure doctrine available here.**
Against otherwise deep, featureless bottom a bank is an isolated high spot —
a submerged mountain where bait stages for shelter and gamefish follow the bait
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). Standalone structure like this
fishes in **any** direction, but each individual bank still has its own
**preferred directions** learned over repeat trips
([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
bottom composition, relief, contour, high-spot shape, surrounding depth,
preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at any of these three marks.
The `depth_band` is unknown for that reason, not because the water is shallow.

**Searching the corpus on the bare numbers returns gear, not geography.** Outside
the spot library, *500* appears as a reel size — "the 500" is a presenter's
personal pick of an [Accurate Tern 2](../tackle/tackle-express-accurate-tern-2-reel.md)
— as a jig weight in grams ([knife jigging](../techniques/knife-jigging.md)), and
as Catalina's **~500-fathom curve**, which compresses the uphill summer
counter-current and aggregates bait and paddies along the line (`HnqiE05vdXs`;
[SoCal Bight](socal-bight.md)); *437*
and *East 400* return nothing outside the library and
[Cameron's spot file](../profiles/cameron/spots.md). The census scores a numbered
spot only on an anchored form for exactly this reason, and every hit it recorded
for this zone is one of those three false positives.

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the Catalina aside drifts from
`HnqiE05vdXs` in three ways — the source's word is an "uphill current" ("the
current was running offshore or up uphill like crazy"), never a *counter*-current;
the account is a single day's conditions in a report dated 08/31/22, not a stated

million kelp … they don't usually have fish on them". The ~500-fathom figure and
the thumb-over-the-hose compression mechanism are verbatim, though the source's
own depths are internally inconsistent ("it goes from 3000 feet to 500 feet",
where 500 fathoms *is* 3,000 ft, waved off with "it doesn't matter the depth").
The same wording sits unflagged in [SoCal Bight](socal-bight.md).

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the 118°W line is worked north to south as one
troll, whether the 14.6 nm leg down to East 400 is worth taking, or what current,
tide, light or swell do here.

**The only program the corpus scopes to this water is the port's, and it is
chart-first rather than spot-first.** The stated Ensenada offshore plan is to run
**20–30 mi due west** to a temperature break, then continue north or west off it
by sea conditions, with a fallback search **20–30 mi north**; trolling cedar
plugs locates fish and the crew deliberately worked away from the fleet
(`0bcDBGzQnGw`; [Ensenada](ensenada.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). Continuing **west** off
a break found at that distance is the one documented heading that trends toward
this zone — but the marks here sit **53–59 statute miles** beyond the outer edge
of that 20–30 mi band, on a west-southwest to southwest line (cameron). These are
**positions a boat would arrive at by following water**, not destinations the
corpus sends anyone to, which is the honest reading until a source names one.
General reading layers are
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md) and
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md); neither is scoped to this water.

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the cedar plugs are spliced in from
elsewhere in `0bcDBGzQnGw`. Their only appearance is at 03:03, on an earlier
segment — "slow troll and some cedar plugs right now around this big dolphin
school and hoping that some tuna around it" — trolling worked around sign already
spotted, not a method for locating fish and not part of the offshore plan quoted
here. The plan's own stated locating doctrine is "the name of the game is finding
life" (03:11). The 20–30 mi west run, the north-or-west continuation by sea
conditions, the 20–30 mi north fallback and the work-away-from-the-fleet goal all
verify at 11:17–11:43.

**No bottom program in the corpus reaches these depths.** The deepest tier of the
[yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) jig-size ladder — where the pick goes
to a heavy 7X for more time in the strike zone — is named against Colonet, the
