# San Juan Seamount

**A zone is a run grouping.** This one is a single charted mark — the
**San Juan Seamount** at **33°03.800'N 120°58.600'W** (cameron), on the western
edge of the [Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md). The position in the
infobox is the charted mark itself, not an average of anything: a zone of one
has no centre to compute.

**No corpus source names it.** "San Juan Seamount" appears in no transcript, no
knowledge note and no evidence file; the only places it occurs in the repo are
its coordinate line in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) and the
flagged stubs on neighbouring pages ([311 (Trask Knoll)](311.md),
[Sverdrup Bank (126)](sverdrup-bank-126.md), [Hancock Bank](hancock-bank.md),
[12 Mile Reef](12-mile-reef.md)). The two places the words *San Juan* do occur
in the transcripts are a Baja tournament stop, Puerto San Juan (`T3cEQUqiH8E`),
and Old San Juan in a Puerto Rico episode (`vdgf_C1-P08`) — neither is this
mark, and neither is in this region. The zone exists on coordinates (cameron),
so this is a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the mark is, what water
it sits in, what its name does and does not claim, and what a source would have
to supply. The [region page](socal-bight.md) already carries this zone as a
flagged stub and nothing here lifts that stub. The flags below aggregate in
[the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

**This is a zone of one because nothing else is within reach of it.** The census
clusters banks under a **12 nm** zone-diameter cap and then attaches a leftover
lone bank to the nearest zone inside **25 nm** (cameron). The nearest charted
position in the whole spot library is **Sverdrup Bank (126)**, **30.9 nm** away
at **082°** — past both thresholds — so this mark clustered with nothing and
attached to nothing, and minted a zone in its own name. Only **three** charted
positions in the library lie within **50 nm** of it: Sverdrup, **Patton Ridge**
at **35.5 nm** and **311 (Trask Knoll)** at **48.7 nm** (cameron). This zone and
[12 Mile Reef](12-mile-reef.md) are the region's only two drawn from a single
mark.

**It is the western edge of the charted region, and the water west of it is
empty in this library.** Of the 391 positions in the spot library only three lie
further west — Arguello Canyon, Rodriguez Seamount and the San Miguel Gap, all
of them **52.8 nm** or more to the north in the
[Davis Knoll / San Miguel Gap / Rodriguez Seamount](davis-knoll-san-miguel-gap-rodriguez-seamount.md)
zone (cameron) — which makes this the westernmost charted position anywhere
south of that corner. In the arc from **125°** clockwise through south and west
round to **350°**, nothing charted lies inside **104.9 nm** of it; the first
mark to appear is Elephant, in northern Baja, at **133°** (cameron). Read that
as a fact about the library rather than about the ocean, but read it: every
fallback this zone has is east or north.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a stated distance, heading, run time, fuel
figure or sea-state envelope to this mark from any port, and no source names a
port, ramp or landing this zone is fished out of. Nothing in the corpus records
anyone fishing here at all.

**Every run the corpus states stops well short of this mark.** The furthest
water any source puts a distance on is Cortez Bank — **96 mi** southwest of San
Pedro and **111 mi** west of Point Loma (`QSvzVHW9UMk`), **~110–115 mi** from
Fisherman's Landing in San Diego and "a full overnight sportboat run"
(`UfuiWFVvz2E`), with the fuller table of stated runs on
[Tanner Bank](tanner-bank.md). Cortes Bank lies **100.4 nm** east-southeast of
this seamount and Tanner Bank **95.4 nm** (cameron), so this mark sits the better
part of another overnight leg beyond the furthest water the corpus describes
anyone reaching. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states that leg.

**One trip format is stated to reach this far out, and not to this mark.** A
3-day sportboat trip fishes two full days dawn to dusk and can reach **up to 200
miles** "if we need to," while a one-day trip runs overnight, fishes roughly
half a day and turns for home (`jU7qi40WTzQ`,
[trip-length selection](../planning/trip-length-selection.md)). That is the only
range envelope in the corpus long enough to put this longitude inside it, and it
is stated as a general capability rather than as a run anyone made out here.

**The nearest charted land is San Nicolas Island, and the mainland is far past
it.** Dutch Harbor on the island lies **75.4 nm** at **083°** from the seamount
and [Blockhouse](blockhouse.md) **75.9 nm** (cameron); the whole
[San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md) zone sits due east of this water and
is the nearest zone in any direction carrying corpus fishing doctrine at all.
**Every mainland-side position in the library is over 155 nm away** — the
nearest is Pelican Point off Crystal Cove at **159.4 nm** (cameron), the longest
such gap of any zone reviewed on this ladder — but that is a fact about the
library: it carries no mainland coordinates north of Orange County, so the coast
this zone actually faces is uncharted here. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
any Santa Barbara, Ventura or Point Conception departure point with a position,
or any run stated to this end of the region.

**The weather doctrine that governs the outside is stated at the islands, and
this mark is further out than all of them.** At San Nicolas — **75.4 nm**
inshore of here (cameron) — weather rather than the bite decides whether anyone
goes: a July 2022 white seabass bite there was written off as unreachable under
**15–20 kt gusting 25** rather than fished (`Blh2BA-7Ono`), and an August 2022
wind event blew out the Channel Islands, San Clemente, San Nicolas, Santa
Barbara Island and the tuna zone off Catalina together (`CMQkHQMxbXM`).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source extends that pattern to this position, or
states a wind or swell number that turns this zone off.

**A calm dock reading is a trap at this end of the region.** W and NW wind blows
around Point Conception at night and quits in the morning, so the go/no-go for a
Santa Rosa / San Miguel crossing is **2 or more consecutive days** of no wind or
Santa Anas before committing (`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`), which is why a **12–24 h** wind
hindcast for the route belongs in every conditions pull alongside the forward
forecast
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md#wind-history-not-just-wind-forecast-1224-h-hindcast)).
That rule is drawn for the crossing to the northern islands, whose water the
spot library does not chart at all. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states the
Conception night-wind behaviour this far offshore.

**Check the swell upstream, not just the point forecast.** The worked example in
the forecast curriculum clicks the West End of San Nicolas Island and finds a
benign local wind picture hiding a swell that climbs from **4 ft** on a Thursday
afternoon to over **12 ft** by Monday, generated by a system up off Oregon days
earlier (`zKmZ4zql2ws`) — which is why a **48–72 h** upstream fetch scan belongs
in front of any run to the outside
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md#upstream-fetch-check-swell-arrives-after-the-wind-that-made-it)).
That example is drawn at the island **75.4 nm** inshore of this mark (cameron),
and nothing out here is sheltered by land in any direction.

2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**One position, and the name is all there is.** The mark sits at
**33°03.800'N 120°58.600'W** (cameron), the fourth-westernmost of the 391
positions in the spot library and the only one of the four that is not up on the
region's Point Arguello corner.

**The corpus uses *seamount* loosely and never defines it.** Cortez Bank is
called "a shallow seamount in the North Pacific Ocean" (`QSvzVHW9UMk`), and the
region page groups the northwest-corner marks under the same word
([Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)) — but the
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md) defines only **bank**, an isolated high spot
rising out of otherwise deep water where bait stages for shelter and gamefish
follow (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what **seamount**
denotes as a structure term in this fishery, whether it implies more relief,
deeper surrounding water or a different working shape than a bank, and how
shallow this one tops out. The same question is open on
[Davis Knoll / San Miguel Gap / Rodriguez Seamount](davis-knoll-san-miguel-gap-rodriguez-seamount.md)
and [Sverdrup Bank (126)](sverdrup-bank-126.md), and it matters because the word
may imply a shape to work that nothing in the corpus licenses reading into it.

**No depth is claimed or stated.** A bank is named for the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point**, at six feet to a fathom (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)) — but the convention only speaks through
numbers, and this is a proper-named mark like Tanner, Cortez, Hancock and
Northeast Bank rather than a numbered one like the 182 or the 43
([Hancock Bank](hancock-bank.md)). The `depth_band` is unknown for that reason.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states a sounded depth, relief, high-spot
shape, bottom composition, surrounding depth or current set at this mark, or
names a rock, pinnacle or sub-feature on it.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: who or what the mark is named for. The
library's own naming conventions — the closest-landmass prefix and the
collisions it exists to prevent — are in [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), and
none of them reaches a proper-named mark this far from any landmass.

**Nothing charted is near it.** Nearest positions in the spot library, nearest
first (cameron): **Sverdrup Bank (126)** at **30.9 nm** east,
**Patton Ridge** at **35.5 nm** northeast — parented to
[311 (Trask Knoll)](311.md) — **311** itself at **48.7 nm**, **Albatross Knoll**
at **52.1 nm** east ([Sverdrup Bank (126)](sverdrup-bank-126.md)), the
**San Miguel Gap** at **52.8 nm** due north, **Potato Bank** at **58.8 nm** and
**Rodriguez Seamount** at **59.1 nm**. Seven charted positions inside **60 nm**,
three inside **50 nm**, none inside **30 nm**. On the nearest-outside-mark
measure this is the region's **third-most isolated zone**, behind the Davis
Knoll grouping at **36.5 nm** and [12 Mile Reef](12-mile-reef.md) at **35.8 nm**
and ahead of [311](311.md), [Sverdrup](sverdrup-bank-126.md) and
[Hancock Bank](hancock-bank.md) at **23.0 nm** (cameron). ⚠ Flagged stub — no
corpus source yet: Sverdrup, Patton Ridge, the 311, Albatross Knoll and the San
Miguel Gap all carry coordinates and no fishing doctrine, so there is nothing on
any of them to hand off to. **The nearest zone that does carry doctrine is San
Nicolas Island, 75.4 nm east.**

**Standalone structure with no land attached fishes in any direction.**
Structure rising off the plain has no lee to work against, and the face to bias
toward is the up-current one, where current forced against the high spot drives
the upwelling that stacks the food chain
([current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md),
[island structure](island-structure.md)) — though each individual mark still has
its own preferred directions learned over repeat trips. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: preferred directions, drift behaviour or current set here.

**The fall condensation runs east of here and ends 95 nm away.** As the coast

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states an
approach, a starting depth, a drift, or what tide, light or current do here.

**A single mark reads as a compact spot-check rather than a search box, but no
source says so.** The Bight's two zone shapes are the complex — a **~50–60
square-mile** box you plan hours of looking across — and the compact spot you
idle onto, grade on the meter, and either commit to or leave in **20–30
minutes** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). One charted position
with nothing else inside **30.9 nm** (cameron) is the shape of the second, and
the consequence is harsher here than anywhere else in the region: the nearest
fallback mark is that far off and is itself corpus-silent, so a blank here ends
the day rather than moving it along.

**With no corpus program, the general layers are what a plan has.** Break
position and break age decide whether this zone is on a given day's plan
([report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)),
and the meter decides once you are there
([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)). From
October the regime flips to reading **100 % the temperature chart and
chlorophyll**, and across a big area with no temperature differentiation the
fish are most likely on structure like the banks (`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`,
[water regimes](../conditions/water-regimes.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)). That last rule is the only
general doctrine that argues for pointing a boat at an unfished mark, and it is
not scoped here.

**The NW-blow map is drawn inshore and north of this zone.** A strong NW event
pulled **5–8 °F** off the northern Channel Islands and the LA / Ventura shelf
while the Coronados, San Clemente and Catalina held their water (`YZtX1MiT0y8`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). This mark
sits west of everything named in that map, so treat the temperature question as
open here and the exposure question as decided: open ocean this far out is
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md) first.

**This mark is not a BightSST eval target, and the nearest one is most of a day
away.** Conditions here are pulled per charted position rather than read off a
scored spot ([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)); the closest scored spot
is Cortes Bank at **100.4 nm** and the next Pyramid Cove at **131.4 nm**
(cameron), both far enough that neither stands in for this water. The
per-position pull is the only honest conditions read this zone has.
