# Sverdrup Bank (126)

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is two charted marks in open water on the
outer edge of the [Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md) — **Sverdrup Bank
(126)** at **33°08.000'N 120°22.000'W** and **Albatross Knoll** at
**32°58.000'N 119°56.833'W** (cameron). They lie **23.3 nm** apart: Albatross
Knoll bears **115°** from Sverdrup and Sverdrup bears **295°** back, an
east-southeast/west-northwest line with **10.0 minutes** of the separation in
latitude and **25.2 minutes** in longitude.

**No corpus source names either mark.** Neither "Sverdrup" nor "Albatross
Knoll" appears in a transcript, a knowledge note or an evidence file, and the
one place the word *albatross* does appear in the corpus is a bird — a probable
black-footed albatross seen east of the 9 Mile Bank in August 2026 (cameron,
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md)), **128.4 nm** from this knoll and
nothing to do with it. The zone exists on coordinates from
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) (cameron), so this is a
**position-and-ladder page**: it says where the two marks are, what their names
do and do not claim, what water they sit in, and what a source would have to
supply. The [region page](socal-bight.md) already carries this zone as a flagged
stub, and nothing here lifts that stub. The flags below aggregate in
[the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

**This is a zone by attachment, not by cluster, and it is the widest one on the
ladder drawn from only two marks.** At **23.3 nm** the pair is nearly double the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, so the two never clustered: Sverdrup was
left over as a lone bank too far from any grouping to join one and minted a zone
of its own, and Albatross Knoll then attached to it under the **25 nm** rule
that puts a lone bank in the nearest zone it could realistically be fished with
(cameron). Every zone in the ladder with a longer span carries three or more
spots. The mechanism is the same one that carries Patton Ridge in
[311 (Trask Knoll)](311.md) and Arguello Canyon in
[Davis Knoll / San Miguel Gap / Rodriguez Seamount](davis-knoll-san-miguel-gap-rodriguez-seamount.md).

**Albatross Knoll's nearest charted neighbour is not in this zone.** Potato Bank
sits **18.0 nm** north of it — closer than Sverdrup — but Potato Bank is
catalogued under [San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md), and the attach
rule measures to the centre of a zone's members rather than to its nearest one:
the knoll sits **27.3 nm** off the centre of that ten-spot island group, past
the **25 nm** rule, and **23.3 nm** off Sverdrup, inside it (cameron). Read the
pairing as an arithmetic outcome, not as a claim that the two fish alike.

**The doctrine that governs the outside islands is the closest thing to a
crossing rule this water has, and it is not scoped to these marks.** At San
Nicolas — **27.6 nm** inshore of Albatross Knoll (cameron) — weather rather than
the bite decides whether anyone goes: a July 2022 white seabass bite there was
written off as unreachable under **15–20 kt gusting 25** rather than fished
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`), and an August 2022 wind event blew out the Channel Islands, San
Clemente, San Nicolas, Santa Barbara Island and the tuna zone off Catalina
together (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). Both marks here are further out than any of them.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source extends that pattern to Sverdrup or Albatross
Knoll.

**Check the swell upstream, not just the point forecast.** The worked example in
the forecast curriculum clicks the **West End of San Nicolas Island** and finds
a benign local wind picture hiding a swell that climbs from **4 ft** on a
Thursday afternoon to over **12 ft** by Monday, generated by a system up off
Oregon days earlier (`zKmZ4zql2ws`) — which is why a **48–72 h** upstream fetch
scan belongs in front of any run to the outside
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md#upstream-fetch-check-swell-arrives-after-the-wind-that-made-it)).
That example is drawn at the island this zone sits outside of, and nothing here
is sheltered by land in any direction.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The two positions.** Sverdrup Bank sits at **33°08.000'N 120°22.000'W** and
Albatross Knoll at **32°58.000'N 119°56.833'W** (cameron) — **23.3 nm** apart on
a **115°/295°** line. Working both is one long east-southeast leg with open
water and no third charted mark between them.

**This is the western edge of the charted region.** Only six positions in the
whole spot library lie further west than Sverdrup's **120°22.000'W** — Arguello
Canyon, Rodriguez Seamount, the San Miguel Gap, San Juan Seamount, Davis Knoll
and Patton Ridge, all of them northwest of here (cameron). Albatross Knoll,
**25.2 minutes** of longitude east of Sverdrup, is the member that sits back
toward the island geography.

**One name claims a depth and the other claims nothing.** A bank is an isolated
high spot rising out of otherwise deep water, named for the **fathom depth of
its shallowest point**, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`):

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sverdrup Bank (126) | 126 fathoms | 756 ft |
| Albatross Knoll | no number in the name | — |

**Sverdrup carries the name-plus-number form, which is the library's pattern for
a mark that is known two ways.** It is recorded the way Kidney Bank (63), The
Boot (504) and 311 (Trask Knoll) are — name and number together, so a report
using either one resolves (cameron), and the naming conventions behind that,
including the collisions they exist to prevent, are in
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says which
name the fleet actually uses on the radio here, or who Sverdrup was.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what **knoll** denotes as a structure term.
The lexicon defines *bank* — an isolated high spot rising out of surrounding
depth — and nothing else in this zone's vocabulary; the same question is open on
[311 (Trask Knoll)](311.md) and
[Davis Knoll / San Miguel Gap / Rodriguez Seamount](davis-knoll-san-miguel-gap-rodriguez-seamount.md).
It matters because the two words may imply different shapes to work, and nothing
in the corpus licenses a reading either way.

**Neither mark is attached to land, which sets how each would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, and the
face to bias toward is the up-current one, where current forced against the high
spot drives the upwelling that stacks the food chain
([current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md),
[island structure](island-structure.md)) — but each individual bank still has
its own preferred directions learned over repeat trips. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: preferred directions, drift behaviour or current set at either
mark.

**Almost nothing charted is near this zone.** No position in the spot library
lies within **23.0 nm** of either mark. Nearest first (cameron): **Patton
Ridge** at **23.0 nm** north-northwest of Sverdrup, **Potato Bank** at
**18.0 nm** north of Albatross Knoll — which is parented to
[San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md) rather than to anything out
here — **Hancock Bank** at **27.0 nm** southeast of the knoll, **Cherry Banks**
at **27.6 nm** east of it, **311 (Trask Knoll)** at **29.4 nm** north of
Sverdrup and **San Juan Seamount** at **30.9 nm** west of it. On the
nearest-outside-mark measure only three zones in the region are more isolated
than this one — the Davis Knoll grouping (**36.5 nm**), 12 Mile Reef
(**35.8 nm**) and San Juan Seamount (**30.9 nm**), the last two of which have no
page on this ladder yet — and this zone ties [311](311.md) and Hancock Bank at
**23.0 nm**. Sverdrup is
the mark all three of those ties run through: it is 311's nearest outside
position and San Juan Seamount's as well (cameron). ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus
source yet: Patton Ridge, Potato Bank, Hancock Bank, Cherry Banks and San Juan
Seamount carry no fishing doctrine scoped to them to hand off to. **The nearest
zone that does is San Nicolas Island, 27.6 nm from the eastern mark and 44.5 nm
from the western one.**

**The paddy band the corpus names is a long way inshore and east of here.** The
best kelp-paddy water in the Bight is **south of San Clemente Island /
Catalina** and along the coastal strip from Dana Point to Oceanside and
southward; north of Dana Point paddies thin out and hold fewer fish
(`HzE4FHHcvTk`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md)). The nearest charted
Catalina position is **73.0 nm** from Albatross Knoll and **89.8 nm** from
Sverdrup, the nearest San Clemente Island position **66.6 nm** and **87.6 nm**,
and the nearest position in that coastal strip over **113 nm** (cameron), which
puts this zone outside that band by a wide margin. That is a statement about
where the named water is, not about these banks: ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus

position. Paddy mechanics are in
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

**The September volume square stops short of this water, and the fall
condensation runs east of it.** The square that holds the bigger bluefin in
September is drawn Santa Barbara Island – San Nicolas – San Clemente – Catalina
(`5to3Q5P7w90`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md#the-volume-square)); Albatross
Knoll lies **27.6 nm** west of its nearest corner and Sverdrup **44.5 nm**
(cameron). Then, as the coast cools and fragments, the bluefin condense outward
toward **Tanner and Cortez** along an arc that runs backside Santa Cruz / Rosa →
Santa Barbara Island → backside Catalina → the 499 / Clemente (`XLVUhV8DW64`,
`OYOda6T3f-8`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[October](../seasonal/october.md)). Tanner Bank is **44.0 nm** and Cortes Bank
**51.9 nm** from Albatross Knoll (cameron), and every leg of that arc passes
east of this zone. Whatever that band is doing, no source puts it here.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the two are run as one leg, or what tide, light,
current or drift do here.

**The geometry reads as two separate spot-checks rather than one box, but no
source says so.** The Bight's two zone types are the complex — a **~50–60
square-mile** box you plan hours of looking across — and the compact spot you
idle onto, grade on the meter, and either commit to or leave in **20–30 minutes**
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). Two isolated marks
**23.3 nm** apart with open water between them (cameron) is not the shape of a
box: the run between them is longer than the whole diameter of most zones on
this ladder, so committing to the second mark is a transit decision made before
you leave the first.

**With no corpus program, the general layers are what a plan has.** Break
position and break age decide whether this zone is on a given day's plan
([report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)),
and the meter decides once you are there
([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)). From
October the regime flips to reading **100 % the temperature chart and
chlorophyll** (`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`,
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)). None of those layers is scoped to
this water.

**The NW-blow map is stated for the water inshore of this zone.** A strong NW
event pulled **5–8 °F** off the northern Channel Islands and the LA / Ventura
shelf while the Coronados, San Clemente and Catalina held their water
(`YZtX1MiT0y8`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). This zone
sits west of everything named in that map, so treat the temperature question as
open here and the exposure question as decided: open ocean this far out is
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md) first.

**Neither mark is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions here are pulled per
charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)). The nearest eval target is Cortes
Bank at **51.9 nm** from Albatross Knoll and **74.9 nm** from Sverdrup
(cameron) — far too distant to stand in for either mark, which makes the
per-position pull the only conditions read this zone has.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron), listed west to east — the axis this zone is drawn
on:

- **Sverdrup Bank (126)** — the western mark and the one the zone is named for,
  the only member whose name claims a depth, and the closer of the two to
  everything north and west: **23.0 nm** to Patton Ridge, **29.4 nm** to the
  311, **30.9 nm** to San Juan Seamount (cameron). Coordinates only; the corpus
  never names it.
- **Albatross Knoll** — the eastern mark, **23.3 nm** at **115°** from Sverdrup,
  attached to this zone rather than clustered with it, and the closer of the two
  to everything east and south: **18.0 nm** to Potato Bank, **27.0 nm** to
  Hancock Bank, **27.6 nm** to Dutch Harbor at San Nicolas Island, **44.0 nm**
  to Tanner Bank (cameron). It is a *knoll* rather than a named bank, and it
  carries no number at all. Coordinates only.

[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive. The spot rung here needs a disambiguated filename for Sverdrup
Bank (126), whose slug collides with this zone page's own — a gazetteer
