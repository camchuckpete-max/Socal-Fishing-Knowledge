# Cabo San Lucas

**[Baja only]** — The charter end of
[Baja Pacific — South](baja-pacific-south.md), at the cape itself. Boats clear
the harbor, pass **El Arco** and are on the fishing grounds without a long run
(`XH-Hrfet6To`). Two programs are documented and they are a few hundred yards
apart: a **surf-line troll** worked along the beach for sierra, with
[dorado](../species/dorado.md) as the incidental (`ll7r4A6atno`), and an
**offshore troll** for [striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md), blue
marlin and dorado over water with no bottom structure named in any source
(`XH-Hrfet6To`, `Mwx5AAXNMvE`, `pX6mV3O0L_E`). This is a fly-in, hire-a-boat
zone in the corpus — every documented day is fished off a local charter or a
local's boat, none from a vessel run down from the United States.

Every day the KB has here falls in **November, December or January**
(`pX6mV3O0L_E`, `XH-Hrfet6To`, `Mwx5AAXNMvE`, `ll7r4A6atno`, `dgauGbNxP84`), so
the zone is documented as a winter fishery and nothing more.

The zone carries **no charted position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)**, whose sections stop at San
Quintín, so no spot pages hang below it and a day plan cannot pull per-spot
conditions here. Paperwork for every Mexican trip is
[Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md).

## Getting there

**You fly in and fish off someone else's boat.** The documented approach is a
**two-hour** flight from John Wayne Airport in Orange County, California, into
Cabo, with a hotel base and a charter booked on the water (`Mwx5AAXNMvE`).

**The run to the grounds is short.** Boats clear the harbor and pass **El
Arco**, the rock arch at the cape, and reach the fishing grounds quickly after
that (`XH-Hrfet6To`). The surf-line program is shorter still — it is fished
along the beach itself (`ll7r4A6atno`).

**Live bait comes off the bait boats, and the window is early.** The stated
trip tip is to get to the bait boats early enough to get live bait
(`Mwx5AAXNMvE`) — the dropback that produced this zone's billfish hookups runs
on that bait, so missing the bait boats changes the day's technique, not just
its bait. See [fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. CDFW rules do not apply on this side of the line, and a
Mexican limit differing from a California one is two jurisdictions, not a
conflict. Verify current requirements before you fish
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)).**

## Structure & bathymetry

**Nothing in the corpus describes the bottom here.** No depth, sounding,
fathom curve, bank, high spot, canyon edge or reef is named in any Cabo source.
The two grounds are described entirely by their surface water: the **surf
line**, trolled along the beach in water shallow enough that the lures run in
the breaking swell (`ll7r4A6atno`), and **open water** off the cape, where the
feature that is fished is a current break rather than structure
(`dgauGbNxP84`).

**The current break is the offshore structure.** It shows as a line of
whitewater or foam running against a noticeably slicker patch of water,
angling offshore, and it holds the bait the fish are on (`dgauGbNxP84`;
[current diagnostics](../conditions/current-diagnostics.md#current-break-visual-id)).

**Offshore water is flat in the morning and builds choppy through the
afternoon, as heat off the land mass drives wind** (`XH-Hrfet6To`) — the
selection consequence is that a lure has to run clean in calm morning water and
still hold in the afternoon chop. One December day on the same water ran
against the pattern: **85 °F**, blue and clear, with **long swell** and no wind
to speak of, called on camera in the middle of the day (`XH-Hrfet6To`).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the afternoon-chop pattern and the calm
mid-day water call both come from `XH-Hrfet6To`, the first as a tackle shop's
general description of Cabo, the second as an on-camera reading of that day.
The KB has no second source for either, and this is the only sea-state prior
the zone carries.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any charted position, depth band or
bathymetry for this zone. [The spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) stops at
San Quintín and carries nothing at the cape, so a day plan cannot pull per-spot
BightSST conditions here the way it can in the [Bight](socal-bight.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether the Pacific side and the Sea of
Cortez side of the cape fish differently, and where the corpus's "offshore"
here sits relative to either. Every Cabo source treats the water off the cape
as one undifferentiated ground.

## How it fishes

**Two programs, and which one you fish is a beach-or-offshore decision made at
the dock.** Nothing in the corpus describes running both in one day beyond a
single move: when the beach did not produce, the boat swung outside and looked
for working birds, which is where the dorado came from (`ll7r4A6atno`).

### The surf line

**The beach is trolled, not cast.** Small hoochies are dragged along the surf
line at roughly **6 knots**, with rubber bands looped on the stern cleats to
hold them down in the water instead of skipping on the surface, and a short
piece of wire ahead of each hoochie because of the sierra's teeth
(`ll7r4A6atno`). Full execution, colors and hook configuration:
[Cabo surf-line sierra trolling](../techniques/trolling.md#cabo-san-lucas--surf-line-slow-trolling-for-sierra);
single-strand wire termination is [haywire twist](../rigging/haywire-twist.md).

**The bite window is early morning or right at sunset** (`ll7r4A6atno`).

**Dirty water is the cue to fish it.** Lime-green, off-color surf water
improves the sierra bite; the mechanism given is eyesight — sierra see well and
go bite-shy in clear, calm shallow water, and feed more readily once the water
turns (`ll7r4A6atno`). That is a predator-vision read, not the bait-holding
mechanism behind the Bight's [water color](../conditions/water-color.md)
doctrine, and it does not transfer.

### Offshore

**The search is eyes, birds and sonar together, run from the troll.** With the
sonar working, the crew watches for birds, porpoise and any sign of life while
the spread is out (`XH-Hrfet6To`;
[search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md),
[electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**Once a current break is found, work it and stay on it.** Troll back and forth
across the break — inside side, then outside — rather than making a single
straight pass, because fish shift from one side to the other with the bait; the
stated rule is to stay with the break rather than leave it to search elsewhere
(`dgauGbNxP84`;
[running the spread](../techniques/trolling.md#running-the-spread--sweeps-not-straight-lines)).

**A billfish that rises to the lures gets a dropback.** The striped marlin
landed here came up on the trolled lures and was hooked after a dropback
(`XH-Hrfet6To`), and the dropback was named as the trip's best bet for solid
hookups, rigged **80 lb** mono to a **#9 circle hook** (`Mwx5AAXNMvE`;
[bait and switch](../techniques/bait-and-switch.md)). The contrast on the same
trip is the two blue marlin that struck trolled lures and did not stay pinned
(`Mwx5AAXNMvE`).

**Marlin lure heads here are medium-to-large, not giant, rigged with a single
big hook** — a medium head is described as covering the whole size range of
fish, and the reasoning given is tournament scoring rather than what the fish
prefer (`XH-Hrfet6To`). Size logic, the named lure makers and the afternoon-chop
selection criterion are in
[Cabo offshore marlin lure selection](../techniques/trolling.md#cabo-san-lucas--offshore-marlin-lure-size-selection-and-afternoon-chop-rigging).

## Spots

**No place in this zone has a public name plus a charted position in the KB, so
no spot pages hang below it.** The named places on record are:

- **El Arco** — the rock arch at the cape, cleared on the way out of the harbor
  and the zone's only landfall reference; a landmark, not a ground
  (`XH-Hrfet6To`).
- **The petroglyph** — the beach landmark the surf-line troll started in front
  of, and the only named position in the inshore program (`ll7r4A6atno`).
  ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): the name is given on camera as a beach feature
  only and resolves to no charted place in the KB.
- **The bait boats** — where live bait is bought before the run out, a stop
  rather than a spot (`Mwx5AAXNMvE`).

The current break the offshore program fishes is a moving water feature, not a
mark, and no source gives a position for where it set up on any day
(`dgauGbNxP84`).
