# The Ridge (Magdalena Bay)

**[Baja only]** — the longer of the two offshore banks a
[Magdalena Bay](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md) day picks between, run when
the wave state outside the boca allows it and given up for the closer Modesto
Main when it does not (`OVwqUKim9Pc`). San Diego long-range boats work the
same name from the other end of the Magdalena-Bay-to-[Alijos
Rocks](alijos-rocks.md) corridor, where it fished as a deep yellowtail bank
alongside yellowfin and jumbo bull dorado (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`). It carries no
charted position and no settled depth.

**The name covers three different grounds in this KB.** This page is
Magdalena Bay's bank. The long-range
[Ridge / Uncle Sam Bank](ridge-uncle-sam-bank.md) — the seven-day-trip
alternative to Alijos Rocks — and a San Diego deep-drop
[swordfish](../species/swordfish.md) ground are the other two; that page
carries the full collision.

**It carries no charted position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)**, whose sections stop at San
Quintín, so a conditions pull has to run against
[the zone](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md) rather than against this bank.

## Getting there

**The run out is the decision, and it is made at the mouth.** The offshore
day out of Lopez Mateos starts by idling past the estuary mouth to look at the
waves, and the bank is picked off that look — the Ridge if the swell allows
the long boat ride, the closer Modesto Main "if the waves look a little
rough" (`OVwqUKim9Pc`). One angler on that trip was up at about **02:00** to
make the day (`OVwqUKim9Pc`). Zone-level access — the flight into
[Loreto](loreto.md), the drive across the peninsula, the lodge and panga fleet
— is on [the zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md#getting-there).

**Nothing times the leg out.** No distance, bearing or run time from the boca
to the bank is stated anywhere. The only measured leg attached to it runs the
other way: **160 mi**, a **16-hour run**, from the Ridge on to
[Alijos Rocks](alijos-rocks.md) for an **08:00** arrival the next morning
(`3tQ1_xiqwVU`).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md),
[the zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**It is named for its shape and sounded twice, inconsistently.** The plan out
of the boca names it as the ridge with **23 fathoms** (`OVwqUKim9Pc`). The one
hookup with a depth on it came yo-yoing at **180 ft, 10 ft off the bottom**,
which puts the bottom under that drift near **190 ft** (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`).

⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): the 23-fathom figure. The auto-captions render it
"23 fetus" and neither the number nor the unit is recoverable from the corpus;
the reading is published here beside the flag rather than as a bare depth
(`OVwqUKim9Pc`). Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): whether the bank the Magdalena Bay panga and
charter fleet calls the Ridge and the bank a San Diego long-range boat calls
the ridge in front of Mag Bay are one ground. Two crews eight years apart use
the name with no position from either, and the two depth figures sit roughly
**9 fathoms** apart — which is either relief along one ridge or two different
banks (`OVwqUKim9Pc`, `3tQ1_xiqwVU`). Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

**The water outside it holds drifting structure.** Kelp paddies and floating
logs are what the afternoon off the bank is worked for (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`); see
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

## What's there

One stop with fish on it, and it is undated. A San Diego long-range boat
worked back up on the bank between a Thetis Bank stop abandoned overnight and
the run to Alijos Rocks (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`). The October 2020 Magdalena Bay trip
names the bank as one of two candidates for its offshore day and never says
which it fished, so that day's dorado bite is carried on
[the zone page](bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md#whats-there), not here
(`OVwqUKim9Pc`).

## How it fishes

**Deep iron is the only documented presentation.** The yellowtail came on a
[yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) drop fished **10 ft off the bottom
in 180 ft** (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`). No jig weight, colour, line class or retrieve is
recorded for this ground.

**It is the fallback when the ground offshore of it goes off.** The crew that
fished it had come from Thetis Bank and left overnight because "the current
and the conditions were opposite," working back up on the Ridge instead
(`3tQ1_xiqwVU`) — a current read that moves a boat between banks rather than
ending the day. See [current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md).

**Work the water outside it, not only the bank.** The afternoon program off
the stop is a look outside the ridge for kelp paddies or floating logs and
wahoo off them (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`);
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md) is the read and
[wahoo trolling](../techniques/wahoo-trolling.md) the execution.

**Getting here at all is a sea-state call.** The wave state outside the boca
is what buys or cancels the long ride (`OVwqUKim9Pc`); see
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: tide, moon, wind or light-window doctrine at
this bank, how long a stop lasts, or which end of it is worked in what
conditions.
