# Alijos Rocks

**[Baja only]** — The San Diego long-range fleet's dedicated wahoo stop, and the
outermost zone of [Baja Pacific — South](baja-pacific-south.md). Rock breaks the
surface here and runs "from the surface all the way to the bottom" in open
ocean **488 mi** south of San Diego, a **45-hour run** (`97clKtVsEOs`,
`_r08B4bItAY`). Six-, seven- and eight-day trips book it, and the alternative
on the same trip type is "the Ridge" — a seven-day trip takes "one or the
other" (`_r08B4bItAY`, `tjBeR9tWd4s`). The draw is a fall
[wahoo](../species/wahoo.md) troll and a **100 lb**
[yellowfin](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) flyline in the same day
(`_r08B4bItAY`), over more rock — and bigger, meaner
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) — than anything north of here
(`RNRFrfepiW0`).

The zone carries **no charted position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)**, whose sections stop at San
Quintín, so no spot pages hang below it and a day plan cannot pull per-spot
conditions here. Paperwork for every Mexican trip is
[Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md).

## Getting there

**It is a two-day run each way and nothing shortens it.** From San Diego the
rocks are **488 mi**, a **45-hour run** departing around **11:00** with bait
taken aboard the next morning at a stop the captions do not resolve
(`_r08B4bItAY`). Inside the region, the transit from near Magdalena Bay is
**160 mi, about 16 hours** (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`). Trips reaching here are **six,
seven and eight days** out of San Diego (`tjBeR9tWd4s`, `4uNPLknRAQg`,
`J3FGJj5zYPE`), and the same seven-day booking buys either these rocks or "the
Ridge", not both (`_r08B4bItAY`).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the run distance is given as **488 mi**
with a stated 45-hour run time and departure hour (`_r08B4bItAY`) and as
"roughly **400 mi** south of San Diego" with neither (`97clKtVsEOs`). The two
are 88 mi apart and nothing in the corpus reconciles them; the figure with the
run time attached is the one to plan off.

**You see the rocks long before you fish them.** They are called out in the
distance at **10 mi** on the approach and described on camera as massive
(`97clKtVsEOs`) — the only landfall reference the corpus gives for this ground.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. CDFW rules do not apply on this side of the line, and a
Mexican limit differing from a California one is two jurisdictions, not a
conflict. Verify current requirements before you fish
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)).**

## Structure & bathymetry

**The whole zone is one rock mass with no shelf under it.** Asked how deep it
is, the answer on camera is that "the Rocks go from the surface all the way to
the bottom" (`97clKtVsEOs`) — the rock breaks the surface and drops away
without an intervening bank top, which is why the fishing here is worked
vertically against a wall rather than across a high spot.

**There is more rock here than on the northern grounds, and it changes the
yellowtail rig.** Alijos Rocks and the Ridge are named together as where
yellowtail run bigger and meaner with more rock underneath, which is the stated
trigger for stepping up from the base yo-yo outfit
(`RNRFrfepiW0`; [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md)).

**It sits in the deepest of the three yo-yo depth tiers.** Where 60–100 ft
calls a small jig and 120–150 ft a Salas 6X, this ground — with Colonet, "the
Ridge" and [Guadalupe](guadalupe.md) — calls a **7X heavy**, on the stated
reasoning that a heavier jig spends more time in the strike zone on the
straight up-and-down (`hteLeDIy9Qs`).

**Warm water, not the calendar, is the gate.** **72 °F or warmer** is the
standing condition for a wahoo chance, and the stated mechanism for fall being
best is that the water warms incrementally through summer and can carry a
hurricane-driven warm push by then (`4uNPLknRAQg`;
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md)). That is also why some
seven- and eight-day trips leave as early as **July** to be the first boat here
or on the Ridge — but only if the water has come up (`4uNPLknRAQg`).

## How it fishes

**Treat it as a stop, not a destination you sit on.** A dedicated wahoo stop
that does not produce is left rather than ground out, and the boat moves to the
next option on the itinerary (`97clKtVsEOs`). The corollary is that the
alternative gets checked on the same trip: the Ridge is the standing
seven-day-trip alternative to these rocks (`_r08B4bItAY`), and a scouting pass
there on one trip was abandoned because "the water was just too warm"
(`97clKtVsEOs`).

**Fishing pressure is a stated input and not a guarantee.** Arriving on ground
nobody had fished for five days is called out on camera as a reason the wahoo
"ought to be ready to bite" (`97clKtVsEOs`) — the read is recorded, the result
that day is in the [evidence file](evidence/alijos-rocks.md).

### Wahoo — the program the trip is built around

- **Bomb, trolled or cast.** A bomb-style lure is the standard, available with
  or without a small spinner, and the one non-negotiable is that a cast bomb is
  **retrieved at the absolute highest rate of speed possible**
  (`ETw_3AFxEcM`). The trolled version and the cast version are both documented
  at these rocks — see [wahoo trolling](../techniques/wahoo-trolling.md) and
  [wahoo bomb & Raider jig casting](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md).
- **Carry a colour spread, not a confidence colour.** The colour a wahoo bites
  is set by what it is feeding on at the moment, so **pink, blue and green**
  are carried together rather than one favourite (`J3FGJj5zYPE`).
- **High-speed plugs are the alternative to a bomb.** Two sizes of the Nomad
  DTX are named for this ground and the Ridge, on the stated mechanism that the
  plug swims deeper than most trolling lures (`it_YYh_8Z-w`;
  [DTX Minnow](../lures/dtx-minnow.md)).
- **Wind continuously — never pump.** Pumping the rod puts slack in the line
  and a wahoo uses it to throw the hook; the instruction is to keep winding and
  let the fish slide underneath the boat (`97clKtVsEOs`).

### Yellowfin — flyline and kite

The **100 lb**-class fish here are fished on a **50 or 60 lb** two-speed outfit
with a fly-lined sardine, and the two-speed is called key for exactly that
combination (`uWGGeDOprsE`). **When the grade and the line class do not
match — 60 to 80 lb fish biting only 40 or 50 lb — the answer is a circle
hook** (`uWGGeDOprsE`; [hooks](../tackle/hooks.md)). The kite, helium balloon
rigs and a flyline on **50 lb** are the stated plan for a stop here on quality
yellowfin graded **60–90 lb** (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`;
[kite fishing](../techniques/kite-fishing.md)). One kite variant recorded at
these rocks adds a **2–4 oz sliding sinker** to the kite-flown bait to keep it
in the water better — stated as advice the angler had been given and had not
yet tested (`jH0q4UPjwC0`), and it sits beside, not inside, the
bare-flyer doctrine in that note.

### Yellowtail — the deep yo-yo

Yo-yoing here is a persistence game: fish enough drops at this bank and a bite
comes (`6kpWn2sXokI`). The jig is a **7X heavy** for the depth (`hteLeDIy9Qs`),
and the outfit steps up to a two-speed Penn Fathom 40 Narrow filled
three-quarters with **80 lb braid** under a **60 lb** mono top shot on a
shorter, more powerful **30–80 lb** rod, because the fish are bigger and there
is more rock to be cut off on (`RNRFrfepiW0`). The bottom-fishing alternative
on the same trip is an **80 lb** outfit running a
[dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) with a **16 oz** weight and a
live sardine or mackerel — the same rod interchanging with the wahoo troll
(`uWGGeDOprsE`).

**The full quiver for a seven-day trip to these rocks and the Ridge** — five
outfits, terminal tackle, wire, crimps and jig assortment — is
[Searcher five-outfit quiver](../tackle/searcher-alijos-rocks-ridge-7-day-quiver.md).

## Spots

**Nothing at these rocks carries a public name plus a charted position in the
corpus, so no spot pages hang below this zone.** The ground is fished and
described as one thing — "the rocks" — with no named high spots, faces, ends or
corners distinguished on camera in any source (`97clKtVsEOs`, `6kpWn2sXokI`,
`_f5QmWSUUx8`).

**Not in this zone:** "the Ridge" and its upper high spot **Uncle Sam Bank**,
**450–460 mi** from San Diego on a two-day run — the standing alternative
booking on the same seven-day trip, "famous for yellowtail and black sea bass
and grouper and yellowfin tuna" in the fall window (`_r08B4bItAY`). **Thetis
Bank**, left overnight on one trip because the current was opposite
(`3tQ1_xiqwVU`), is a separate ground on the same corridor. Both are carried as
flagged stubs on [Baja Pacific — South](baja-pacific-south.md); the yellowtail,
black sea bass and grouper material from those grounds belongs to them, not
here.
