# Anacapa Island

Anacapa is one of the **north Channel Islands**, at the Santa Barbara Channel
end of the [Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md). The corpus names it a
handful of times and never describes it. The one landed-fish line is
**early-August barracuda**, "still some barracuda caught" at Santa Cruz and
Anacapa, on a week the zone was not doable except aboard a private boat
(`CMQkHQMxbXM`, `-JZpyWLdKlk`, [barracuda](../species/barracuda.md)); two 2022
bluefin lines put fish on its front side and its backside without describing
either, and one wind line calls it fishable when Santa Rosa is not
(`5to3Q5P7w90`, `Blh2BA-7Ono`). No position, no structure and no program is
stated for this island itself; the rest of this page is island-group or
region-level material that is labelled as such.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the boat type is inverted — see the
full finding under **Private-boat access** in [Getting there](#getting-there).
The barracuda quote itself verifies (`CMQkHQMxbXM` @00:02:06, ASR "santa cruz
nana capitol islands").

**The zone rung above this page is missing, so it hangs off the region.** The
north Channel Islands carry no coordinates in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) and therefore mint no zone, even
though they are named repeatedly — a gap the
[region page](socal-bight.md) already flags (cameron). Until a coordinate lands
here, `parent` points at the region rather than at a zone.

## Getting there

**Ventura and Oxnard are the stated ports for this island group.** A Channel
Islands day starts "just outside of Ventura," makes a tank of squid on the way
out and runs to the islands (`6-8KfjEg0x8`); the tackle framing for the same
water opens on what to carry "if you're going to be fishing out of Ventura or
Oxnard ... going out to the Channel Islands" (`nRIAgz5G_Bc`).

**Private-boat access is the difference between a report and a trip.** On the
2022-08-03 week the barracuda were reported, the zone was reachable only aboard
a boat (`CMQkHQMxbXM`, [barracuda evidence](../species/evidence/barracuda.md)).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the source says the opposite, and it is
a **weather** call, not an access one. `CMQkHQMxbXM`: "uh but this went this
week and i don't really think it's gonna be uh doable up there on unless you're
on a sport but maybe not even then so like i said check the weather before
heading out" (@00:02:10) — i.e. not doable *unless you are on a sport boat*,
and maybe not even then, which shuts **private** boats out rather than making
them the way in. The string "private boat" does not occur anywhere in
`CMQkHQMxbXM`. The lead paragraph carries the same inversion. The second cite
used for it in the lead, `-JZpyWLdKlk`, does contain "if you're on a sport boat
or you got a yacht you want to go drift around at night and drop jigs that'd be
your zone up there" (@00:03:11) — but that is the offshore **northern bluefin
zone**, not the north Channel Islands.
Read that alongside August's coverage bias: with the fleet offshore, island
reports thin out, which is missing coverage rather than a dead island
(`CMQkHQMxbXM`, [August](../seasonal/august.md),
[report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the cite is misapplied. The
coverage-bias line in `CMQkHQMxbXM` is scoped to **San Clemente Island**, not
to the north Channel Islands — "not a lot of coverage at that island right now
most of the overnight boats at newport and dana porn [Dana Point] are fishing
offshore" (@00:04:38). The general island-wide form is in the note's other
August source, `-JZpyWLdKlk` — "an island report because there's not a lot to
talk about at the islands themselves because most people are out looking
around" (@00:04:12) — which is not cited here.

**A NW blow hits this end of the region hardest.** A strong NW event rolls
surface temperature down **5–8 °F** on the northern Channel Islands and the
LA/Ventura coastal shelf, while south of Newport is less prone to wind-wave
rollover and the Coronados, Catalina and San Clemente held their water through
one such event (`YZtX1MiT0y8`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). A NW
forecast is therefore the first gate on a run up here.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): neither the **5–8 °F** range nor the
**NW** direction is in `YZtX1MiT0y8`. Its only degree figure is a hedged
single-site reading of Long Beach harbour taken off a friend's video — "it
looked like it was like 56 degree water which is i think **seven maybe eight**
degrees off from where it was before this wind along the beach" (@00:05:15) —
while for the islands it states an absolute rather than a delta, "hardest hit
was the northern channel islands miguel rosa the front side of santa cruz
island they all that stuff dropped dramatically they're you know they're in the
low 50s in some of those areas" (@00:01:14). "Northwest"/"NW" never occurs; the
wind is only "it's been blown outside all week" and the mechanism named is
"wind wave driven rollovers and upwelling" (@00:02:04). The held zones verify
verbatim (@00:01:33), and the source also puts the **backside** of Santa Cruz
and the lee in the held group (@00:01:26). Scoped to this page's citation:
[bight geography](bight-geography.md) owns the map and Cameron adjudicated
2026-08-26 that **the map stands** and is to be backed by BightSST rollover
statistics rather than re-sourced; the identical flag already sits on
[socal-bight](socal-bight.md), [12 Mile Reef](12-mile-reef.md) and roughly a
dozen other zone pages.

**Within the north group this island is the fishable end of a blow.** With wind
forecast up the northern Channel Islands for the weekend following 2022-07-21,
Santa Cruz and Anacapa were called "plenty fishable" while Santa Rosa "might be
an issue for private boats" (`Blh2BA-7Ono`) — the same ordering the April 2022
event produced, where Miguel, Rosa and the front side of Santa Cruz took the
worst of it (`YZtX1MiT0y8`). It is stated as one weekend's read with the advice
to check the weather before leaving the dock, not as a standing rule
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`).

**A calm dock reading is a trap in this channel, but the rule is stated for the
water west of here.** West and northwest wind blows around Point Conception at
night and quits in the morning, so a Santa Rosa / San Miguel crossing needs
**two or more consecutive days** of no wind or Santa Anas before it is
committed to (`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`, [rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)),
which is why a **12–24 h wind hindcast** for the route belongs in the conditions
pull beside the forward forecast
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md#wind-history-not-just-wind-forecast-1224-h-hindcast)).
That crossing runs west of Anacapa; ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states the
Conception night-wind behaviour for Anacapa or for the Santa Barbara Channel
itself.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the bolded **two or more consecutive
days** is harder than the cite. `Z3rZqy2Pi8E` says "i don't really look for **a
couple days** of continuous no wind or santa anas before i plan a trip up
there" (@00:02:54) — an unquantified "a couple", and the auto-caption's "don't"
inverts the evident sense of the sentence, so the clause's polarity is a
caption artifact rather than stated doctrine. The night-wind mechanism and the
morning-calm trap verify near-verbatim in the same passage ("West Wind or
northwest wind that blow around conception at night but stop in the morning",
"a lot of times it may look very calm in the morning because Wind Blows at
night up there"), as does the Rosa/Miguel destination (@00:01:44).

## Structure & bathymetry

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any charted position, depth, kelp line,
boiler rock, pinnacle, bottom composition or shoreline orientation at Anacapa.
The island is named as a place fish are found and never described as
structure, and it carries no coordinates in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) (cameron) — which is why this page
publishes none.

**The general island read is all a plan has here.** Which face of an island is
worth running to is a wind-and-swell decision made per island and per spot, and
each mark has its own preferred conditions learned over repeat trips
([island structure](island-structure.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). Anacapa is listed by
name alongside Catalina, San Clemente, the Coronados, Santa Cruz and Santa
Barbara as islands that are "affected in different ways" by swell, west versus
south (`YPhc0zr7oBs`, `aFb221LUoD0`).

**A front side and a backside are named, and neither is described.** Bluefin are
placed on "the front side of Anacapa Island" (`5to3Q5P7w90`) and as possibly
sliding up to "the backside of Anacapa and Santa Cruz islands" (`Blh2BA-7Ono`),
so the two faces exist in the corpus as labels only. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source says what either face looks like underwater, which swell or wind
direction favours which one, or names a single spot on this island.

**The nearest built pages sit around it, not on it.** The
[12 Mile Reef](12-mile-reef.md) is the region's charted Santa Barbara Channel
mark and the only built page in this water; [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)
and [San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md) are the nearest built island
zones, both offshore and south. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source gives a
bearing or distance between Anacapa and any of them.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: an approach, a starting depth, a drift, or
what tide, light or current do at Anacapa. Nothing below is scoped closer than
the island group.

**The island-group program is a squid program.** Squid is made on the way out,
then the column is covered with a [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md)
and a [sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md) — the sliding sinker
described as the way to fish "every level of the water column" from surface to
bottom, and especially good with squid at the Channel Islands (`6-8KfjEg0x8`).
Making and holding live squid is in [making bait](../bait/making-bait.md) and
[bait tanks](../bait/bait-tanks.md).

**Current is the gate, and the meter alone will fool you.** A spot with no
current meters fish that do not bite (`6-8KfjEg0x8`,
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)) — grade the water
moving over the structure before grading the marks on the screen
([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): `6-8KfjEg0x8` says nothing about
metering fish that will not bite. Its line is "we did not have much current,
which at the islands is just as important as the bait you're fishing, really.
Uh the conditions make or break it out here" (@00:02:43) — and that same
low-current day still produced the bag ("We got some whitefish. We lost a
couple sheephead"). The meter half appears nowhere in it; the transcript's only
sonar reference precedes a bite ("some kind of scratchy marks coming through
throughout the column… And he's on. And we're bit.", @00:01:17). The same
finding already stands on [12 Mile Reef](12-mile-reef.md) and
[rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md) against the same cite.

**The fall arc starts on this shore and runs away from it.** As the coast cools
and the warm band fragments the water tracks a repeatable path — backside Santa
Cruz / Rosa → Santa Barbara Island → backside Catalina → hooking to the 499 /
Clemente (`XLVUhV8DW64`, `OYOda6T3f-8`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md), [October](../seasonal/october.md)). Its
first station is the neighbouring island backsides and every later one is
further south, so the fall pattern moves the fishery off this island rather
than onto it.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the chain belongs to `OYOda6T3f-8`
alone. `XLVUhV8DW64` carries **none** of it — no Santa Cruz, no Rosa, no 499,
and Santa Barbara Island only as the abbreviation "SBI" — and its bluefin track
is a different one: "that stuff was up off the West End of cat West into
Clementi at the Nick and SBI for a while… it kind of moved down off the east
end back side of Clementi" (@00:03:29). `OYOda6T3f-8` does carry the whole
sequence in order (@00:03:07 → @00:04:09), but it makes Clemente and the 499
**one** endpoint ("it hooks around to Clementi and that's at 499 Zone"), not
the two the "499 / Clemente" slash implies, and it describes one October 2022
SST-chart geometry rather than a "repeatable path". Same finding already
ledgered from [12 Mile Reef](12-mile-reef.md).

**The paddy program is not a reason to come here.** The best kelp-paddy water
is south of San Clemente Island / Catalina and along the coastal strip from
Dana Point to Oceanside southward; north of Dana Point paddies thin out and hold
fewer fish (`HzE4FHHcvTk`, [kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md),
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the second half overstates the cite in
two ways. `HzE4FHHcvTk` says the **paddies** get scarcer, not that they hold
fewer fish — "when you get further up the patties are fewer and further between
and they're not always holding" (@00:03:30) — and its threshold is the vague
"when you get further up", not a **Dana Point** line; Dana Point appears only
on the positive side, "your best bet is i would say for that type of action is
south of san clemente island or south of catalina or along the beach below dana
point um towards the ocean side" (@00:03:36). The only fewer-fish mechanism in
the source is **pressure, not latitude** ("the more pressure the patties get
less fish they're", @00:01:05; "the closer the patties are to a dock the more
likely they are to get hit", @00:03:46).
[socal-bight](socal-bight.md) already routes the wording fix to the cluster
pass.

**Conditions come from a per-position pull, and there is no position.** Anacapa
is not a scored spot ([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)) and has no
charted coordinate in this repo, so a day plan cannot pull SST or chlorophyll
for it. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a public position for Anacapa Island;
one coordinate would make this island plannable and would mint the north
Channel Islands zone the [region page](socal-bight.md) is waiting on.
