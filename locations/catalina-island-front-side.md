# Catalina Island — Front Side

The mainland-facing side of Catalina, running W→E from Johnson Rock at the West
End to The Slide at the east end, with 22 charted shoreline spots, 17 charted
rockfish pinnacles and one attached offshore bank (cameron). It is the island's
lee in the prevailing west wind and swell, and the side a boat retreats to when
the West End and backside blow out (`PKf7G3uL4io`). The program here is squid:
anchor on shallow rock, chum, and soak live squid for yellowtail, calico bass
and bonito (`MUpvP-Yl2R0`). Nine of its charted spots carry a marine-protected-area
boundary note, so the closure edge is part of nearly every spot decision
(cameron).

## Getting there

**Pick the side before you pick the spot.** SoCal's prevailing wind, current and
swell default out of the west, and island structure and fish orientation are
conditioned to that pattern; read swell direction first, then choose east end
against front side, backside or middle (`aFb221LUoD0`). A big west swell is the
trigger to send anglers down to the east end rather than to the front side; a
south swell reverses the pattern and tears up the east end specifically
(`aFb221LUoD0`). When the West End is blowing and the backside gets worse the
further along it runs, the front side is the shelter (`PKf7G3uL4io`), and it is
the side picked outright for calmer conditions on a trip carrying first-timers
(`MUpvP-Yl2R0`). Call a local fish-report line the night before to set the side
and the spot ([report reading and forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md),
`MUpvP-Yl2R0`).

The crossing shows up in the corpus as roughly a **30 mi run** (`MUpvP-Yl2R0`)
and about **2.5 hours** under way (`L_FD-UzvEio`), neither tied to a named
departure port. Trailering to a closer ramp trades a shorter tow against a
longer crossing (cameron).

A Catalina MLPA area closed to public fishing is named on camera as the context
for a hatchery broodstock crew fishing it under permit; the source does not say
which side of the island it sits on (`Y2bXn44lfqo`). California / CDFW, as of
2024 — verify current closures with CDFW before fishing. The Farnsworth Bank
closure, where only yellowtail and bonito may be kept, is charted in the
island's backside section, not here (`5p6gu14ZC4w`, cameron).

**Navigation cautions** carried in the spot library: Johnson Rock is charted
with a boiler-rock caution and Isthmus Reef with a shoal caution (cameron). The
local islands in general are framed as a hazard stack of thick kelp,
razor-sharp boiler rocks and big waves (`iQLyBzhOSi8`).

## Structure & bathymetry

The shoreline alternates rock points, reefs, kelp and coves: boiler rock at
Johnson Rock; rock points at Stony Point, Arrow Point, Yellowtail Point and Long
Point; reefs at Eagle Reef, Isthmus Reef and Bird Rock Reef; named kelp at Hen
Rock Kelp and Can Dump Kelp; and coves and landings at Parson's Landing, Empire
Landing and Toyon Bay (cameron).

**Scale of the shallow rock.** Running onto a close-range rock at Catalina the
meter reads **9 fathoms**, converted to **54 ft**, and the boat settles into
**48 ft** as it slides over a rock whose crest sits about **8 ft below the
surface** (`HGyL7pXy3Ts`) — the size of feature the anchored program is set up
over ([electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**Charted rockfish pinnacles, 31–48 fathoms** (cameron): the Salta Verde Cod
group runs **40–48 fa** across eight numbered spots plus the Salta Verde Cod
Pinnacle; the 3rd V Cod pair runs **31–36 fa**; the East End Rockfish trio runs
**46–48 fa**. West End Humps 1–3 carry no charted depth. The Salta Verde Cod and
3rd V Cod pinnacles share names with Salta Verde and 3 V's, which the spot
library charts in the **backside** section (cameron) — the census assigns the
whole rockfish group to this zone, and the split is queued rather than
re-drawn here.

**Structure typology gates the spot before conditions do.** A ridge running out
perpendicular from an island is a **two-directional** spot: it produces only on
current running parallel to the shoreline, uphill or downhill. Current straight
into the beach is marginal; straight off the beach is dead
([island structure](island-structure.md)). At Catalina's **~500-fathom curve**
the uphill summer counter-current compresses as it climbs the slope, spins
eddies, and aggregates both bait and kelp paddies along the curve
(`HnqiE05vdXs`) — a line to hunt rather than a point, and the offshore edge of
this zone's water.

**Kelp paddies.** Paddy fishing reached "the inside of Catalina" in August 2022,
but paddies were fewer and less holding that far up the Bight; the best paddy
water was south of San Clemente Island or Catalina (`HzE4FHHcvTk`). This side is
the upper edge of the paddy program, not its core
([kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md)).

## How it fishes

**The default program is anchor, chum, soak squid.** Anchor on shallow rock,
chum live squid, and fish it on a light slider — a **size 4 hook** on a
roughly **1/4 oz** sliding sinker was called perfect for squid and produced
multiple yellowtail (`MUpvP-Yl2R0`,
[sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md)). At Bird Rock an anchored
stop produced a yellowtail on live squid alongside 3–4 lb calicos and barracuda
(`zUFbCIWZZMw`). Squid is the gate on the whole side: when squid is around the
stops fire, and with both squid and sardine soaking the bait that gets bit
changes stop to stop (`oadK6zIYyCo`,
[fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md)). Run the crossing with the
tank lid off so the bait stays visible, because a dead pump discovered at the
island costs the whole tank (`L_FD-UzvEio`); load squid without packing them
densely, which shortens how long they last (`xFS3MW4GpDU`,
[bait tanks](../bait/bait-tanks.md)).

**Read current against the spot's typology, not against the calendar.** Because
current direction is coherent across a region, once you know which way the
water runs you can pre-judge every point and reef on the day's list — the
perpendicular ridges only on parallel current
([island structure](island-structure.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). Individual spots also
have a preferred **wind**, not just a preferred current: spots are worth
annotating for whether they bite in a northwest or a southeast wind, and a wind
already blowing from the wrong quarter takes the planned spot off the list
before the boat reaches it. A spot that fished on downhill current and a west
wind can be dead 24 hours later on no current and flat wind (`aFb221LUoD0`).

**Line class for a full-day island.** The schoolie-grade surface-iron outfit
runs **40 lb** monofilament for full-day-accessible islands including Catalina,
against **50 lb** reserved for overnight-range islands and Baja (`LTCFjqtSI8g`,
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md),
[gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md)).

## Spots

Forty charted spots, grouped as the run reads W→E:

- **West End** — Johnson Rock (boiler-rock caution), Stony Point, Parson's
  Landing, and West End Humps 1–3.
- **Arrow Point to the Isthmus** — Arrow Point, Indian Rock, Eagle Reef Buoy,
  Lions Head, Ship Rock, Isthmus Reef, Bird Rock Reef. This is the MPA-edge
  stretch: every spot in it carries a boundary rule (cameron). Two Harbors is
  the corpus's named tournament base for the island (`iQLyBzhOSi8`).
- **Mid-island** — Yellowtail Point, Empire Landing, Red Bluff, Little Gibraltar
  Main Big Rock, Long Point, Hen Rock Kelp.
- **Toyon to the east end** — Toyon Bay, Gallaghers, Can Dump Kelp, East End
  Quarry, Jewfish Point, The Slide.
- **Charted rockfish pinnacles** — Salta Verde Cod 1–8 and the Salta Verde Cod
  Pinnacle, 3rd V Cod 1–2, East End Rockfish 1–3, at 31–48 fathoms.
- **Attached offshore bank** — Avalon Bank, charted in the library's
  offshore-banks list and assigned to this zone as its nearest run grouping
  (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: how Avalon Bank fishes.

**Name collision, unresolved.** "The Slide" names both a Catalina front-side
shoreline spot and a separate offshore bank; both are closest to Catalina, so
the landmass prefix rule cannot separate them and the resolution is an open item
(cameron, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).

Charted positions for all forty spots are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md); the per-spot pages carry them.
