# 385 / 238 / 475

**A zone is a run grouping.** 385 / 238 / 475 is the inshore corner of the
numbered offshore-bank grid southwest of Ensenada — four charted positions
(cameron) inside **9 minutes of latitude** (31°33.000'N to 31°42.000'N) and
**13 minutes of longitude** (117°05.000'W to 117°18.000'W). Three of them —
the **385**, the **238** and the **475** — sit inside 9 nm of each other and are
the cluster the zone is named for; the **480** to the northwest is a lone bank
the census attached here as the nearest grouping it could be fished with.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these four marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid — fifty-one charted positions over seventeen bank zones with
no depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
spot names claim, and what is missing.

**What separates it from the rest of the grid is reach.** Every other numbered
zone but one sits further off the Ensenada coast than this one, and the corpus's
own offshore distance ladder out of that port brackets these four positions
rather than falling short of them. That does not make this zone documented — it
makes it the one place in the grid where the documented Ensenada offshore day
and the charted marks occupy the same water.

## Getting there

**Ensenada is the working port this water sits outside of.** Bait is made just
outside the marina and the trips in this corpus depart from it (`0dIwWiOc1NY`,
`0bcDBGzQnGw`; [Ensenada](ensenada.md)). The library gives the port no charted
position of its own, so distances here are measured from **Punta San Miguel** at
**31°54.110'N 116°44.980'W**, the northern headland of that zone (cameron) —
the corpus's own offshore figures are stated from the port, so the two are close
but not the same reference point.

**The corpus's Ensenada distance ladder brackets this zone.** Read against the
charted positions, the documented offshore day runs through this water rather
than past it:

**None of those figures names a bank, so none of them is attributed to a mark

the tournament bank 42–43 mi out is the one *bank* the corpus places offshore of
Ensenada, and it is further out than anything in this zone (`Ix0gG0-l3v0`,
`U1AgwmlY5bI`). The overlap is a distance coincidence worth knowing when
planning, not evidence that these positions were the ones fished.

**This zone sits far closer to Mexico than to the United States.** The Point
Loma Pipe at **32°40.350'N 117°17.000'W** is **58.4–67.5 nm** from the four
marks (cameron), roughly twice the reach from Punta San Miguel — the reverse of
[Upper Cross / 421 / 390](upper-cross-421-390.md) to the north, where the US
positions are the closer ones. A boat leaving the United States crosses the
border to fish here.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three-bank core is what makes this one zone.** The 385 at **31°36.600'N
117°15.000'W**, the 238 at **31°33.000'N 117°11.000'W** and the 475 at
**31°36.000'N 117°05.000'W** (cameron) sit **5.0 nm** (385 to 238), **5.9 nm**
(238 to 475) and **8.5 nm** (385 to 475) apart — every pair well inside the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, which is the clustering rule that grouped
them. This is the tightest bank core in the Baja grid the review has written so
far: the whole triangle spans **8.5 nm**, well under half the **20.0 nm** across
[1010 Trench / 378 / 213](1010-trench-378-213.md).

**The 480 was attached, not clustered, and only just.** It lies at
**31°42.000'N 117°18.000'W** (cameron), **7.1 nm** from the zone centre and
**6.0 nm** from the 385 — closer to a core mark than the 385 and the 475 are to
each other — but **12.6 nm** from the 475, which is what put the four-mark
diameter past the 12 nm cap and left it a lone bank for the clustering to
attach. It is carried here as queued rather than re-parented, because the zone
list is set by the census and not by this page.

**One charted bank outside the zone sits closer to the three-bank core's centre
than the 480 does.** The 450 at **31°27.200'N 117°05.250'W** is **9.1 nm** off
that centre against the 480's **9.4 nm**, and **7.6 nm** from the 238 and
**8.8 nm** from the 475 (cameron), yet it is parented to
1140 Finger / 450 to the south. That is the complete-linkage rule working as
designed — the 450 is 18.4 nm from the 480, so joining the two zones would
breach the cap — but it means the water between this zone's southern edge and
that one is continuous bank ground split across two pages. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: whether the 450 is fished with the 238 and the 475 or with the
1140 Finger.

**The names are the only depth claims in the zone, and every one of them reads
deep.** A bank takes the **fathom depth of its shallowest point** as its name,
at six feet to a fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read
that way the 238 tops out at **238 fathoms — about 1,428 ft**, the 385 at
**385 fathoms — about 2,310 ft**, the 475 at **475 fathoms — about 2,850 ft**
and the 480 at **480 fathoms — about 2,880 ft**. The 238 is the shallowest name
in the zone and still sits below the deepest working depth in the KB: SoCal
daytime swordfish baits average **800–1,100 ft**
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)). Nothing
here reads as structure fished on the bottom the way the
[Colonet](colonet.md) crests at **28 and 41 fathoms** are (cameron).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms any of the four numbers as a charted
depth.

**The 475 and the 475 Knuckle are different spots and the charts agree.** The
library records the pair as distinct names rather than a collision
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)), and the two charted positions are **28.1 nm**
apart — the 475 Knuckle at 32°04.000'N 117°07.000'W belongs to the 475 Knuckle /
Upper Finger Bank zone well to the north (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source says what *Knuckle* denotes, or whether the two names describe related
structure at all.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bottom composition, relief, contour, high-spot
shape or current behaviour anywhere in this zone. The `depth_band` is unknown for
that reason, not because the water is shallow.

**The zone's charted neighbours are mostly unbuilt.** Baja - 270 / Double 220 /
295 sits **16.4 nm** northwest of the zone centre, 1140 Finger / 450 **19.0 nm**
south, Banda Bank / Todos Santos Island **20.3 nm** northeast and Upper 500 /
Hidden Bank **22.7 nm** north (cameron). The nearest zone that has a page is
[Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md) on the coast, **25.2 nm**
to the east, with its **48 Fa. Spot** the closest charted mark in it at **13.6 nm**
from the 475. ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: none of the four nearer
bank zones carries corpus material or a page to hand off to.

**The species the corpus puts in this water are placed by distance, not by
mark.** Yellowfin were reported showing **30–40 mi** out of Ensenada with the
crew choosing closer structure instead (`0dIwWiOc1NY`); skipjack and yellowfin
were seen around a floating object about **35 mi** out, and a striped marlin
jumped at the boat on a dolphin-school foamer the same trip, working temperature
breaks west of the port (`0bcDBGzQnGw`); and
the **August 2024** tournament bluefin were breezing and kite-raised on a bank
**42–43 mi** out, ~40 boats landing **4 fish on day one and 7 on day two**
(`Ix0gG0-l3v0`, `U1AgwmlY5bI`). The first two bands cover this zone's distance
range and the third is outside it, but no bearing narrow enough to place any of

[striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md) and

**Where the bluefin are is a fleet-position question here, not a calendar one.**
Three areas were being worked as of **2021-05** — one west of San Diego and two
west of Ensenada — with nothing running further down the coast to Colonet or San
Quintín because the closer grounds were producing (`fgTmUq78ofQ`). No
coordinate, distance or bank name is given for either Ensenada-side area, so
none of it is attributed to this zone.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the tight three-bank core is worked as one
circuit, whether the 480 is worth the extra leg, or what current, tide, light or
swell do here.

**The only program the corpus scopes to this water is the port's, and it is
chart-first rather than spot-first.** The stated Ensenada offshore plan is to run
**20–30 mi due west** to a temperature break, then continue north or west off it
by sea conditions, with a fallback search **20–30 mi north**; trolling cedar
plugs locates fish and the crew deliberately worked away from the fleet
(`0bcDBGzQnGw`; [Ensenada](ensenada.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). Applied here that
makes the four charted positions **things you would pass through on a break
search**, not destinations the corpus sends you to — which is the honest reading
until a source names one. General reading layers are
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md) and
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md); neither is scoped to this water.

## Spots

Four charted spots (cameron). They group as a core plus one near-miss
attachment:

- **The core three — the 385, the 238 and the 475**, spanning 5.0 to 8.5 nm and
  the only part of this zone that clustered on its own. The 385 and the 238 are
  the tightest pair at 5.0 nm; the 475 is the easternmost mark in the zone and
  the closest of the four to the coast, 24.9 nm from Punta San Miguel (cameron).
- **The 480** — the northwestern mark, 7.1 nm off the zone centre and 6.0 nm
  from the 385, but 12.6 nm from the 475, which is what kept it out of the core
  cluster. It is also the deepest-reading name here and the furthest of the four
  from the 450 to the south.

Charted positions for all four are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
