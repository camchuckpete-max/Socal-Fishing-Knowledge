# Pyramid Head

The southeast tip of San Clemente Island at **32°48.920'N 118°21.250'W**
(cameron), the last mark on the
[front-side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md) run N→S and the corpus's
producing end of that side. Two trips in this corpus fish it by name: a June
live-squid session that produced a mixed calico/bonito/yellowtail bite
(`yuXr3IJ8ybg`) and a November morning that found a better grade of yellowfin
right off the island's east end (`Ow3an9lSVh4`).

## Getting there

**Plan it as an overnight, and check the Navy schedule first.** Pyramid Head
inherits the zone's access terms: San Clemente Island sits at overnight range
rather than day range, and it is a U.S. Navy training range whose zones open and
close on a rotating training schedule, with the crossing given as roughly
**60 miles** straight off the beach (`XyKF34C5iB4`). **Jurisdiction: U.S. Navy,
as of the 2025-04-19 seminar — verify current access before running the
crossing.** The full access picture, including the front side's naval security
zone, is on
[San Clemente Island — Front Side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md).

**It is the island mark the offshore banks are measured against.** Pyramid Head
is the nearest charted land to the below-Clemente complex: **14.8 nm** from the
284 and **14.9 nm at 242°** from the [289](289-284.md), **21.5 nm** from the
[43](43-91-300.md), **24.3 nm** from the 181 and **33.6 nm** from the 182
(cameron). That geometry is what makes an "out to the 43, then San Clemente" day
a run that keeps going west rather than a turn for home
([43 / 91 / 300](43-91-300.md)).

**The offshore leg here is fishable water, not just transit.** The
2014-11-03 trip that ended off Pyramid Head spent its first day on open-water
temperature breaks on the way out, and its trip-tips segment came off that leg:
be patient on a kelp paddy or offshore structure, because a paddy that looks
loaded with skipjack can have the tuna roll in and push the skippies out
(`Ow3an9lSVh4`, [skipjack tuna](../species/skipjack-tuna.md),
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**It is the head itself — the island's southeast corner.** The corpus places the
November yellowfin "right off the island's east end" (`Ow3an9lSVh4`), and the
spot library charts it as the southernmost of the three Pyramid marks (cameron).
The other two sit close by: [Pyramid Reef](pyramid-reef.md) is about **0.2 nm**
north-northeast and [Pyramid Cove](pyramid-cove.md) about **1.5 nm** west, so
the three read as one end-of-island group rather than three separate runs
(cameron). Pyramid Cove is also one of the BightSST evaluation targets
([BightSST eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md), cameron), which makes it the
nearest point a conditions pull is already tuned for.

The island group in general is framed as a hazard stack of thick kelp,
razor-sharp boiler rocks and big waves
([San Clemente Island — Front Side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md),
[island structure](island-structure.md)).

or the shape of the drop off the head — the two documented sessions describe the
bite, not the ground.

## How it fishes

**Squid is what the documented bite ran on.** The June session's mixed
calico/bonito/yellowtail bite came on **live squid** (`yuXr3IJ8ybg`,
[fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md)), which matches the island-wide read that yellowtail
here follow squid presence rather than the calendar
([yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md)).

**Read a bonito boil as the opener, not the event.** With bonito boiling around
the boat the call on camera was "a good sign," and yellowtail joined the bite
shortly after (`yuXr3IJ8ybg`) — stay on the school rather than running off it.

**Weight the flyline when the grade wants it deeper.** Off Pyramid Head a better
grade of yellowfin wanted the sardine below the surface, and the crew added a
**½ oz to ¾ oz** rubber-core or sliding sinker to the flyline to get it there,
butt-hooking the baits so they swam down — a conditions-driven exception to the
no-weight rule, not a replacement for it (`Ow3an9lSVh4`,
[flyline](../techniques/flyline.md),
[sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md)).

**Do not call the species off the fight here.** On the squid trip the crew's
by-feel tells — sustained side-to-side runs as bonito, a "head nod" as
yellowtail — were undercut by the same footage, with a hookup called calico,
then bonito, landed as a yellowtail. In a mixed bite this is crew chatter, not
an ID method (`yuXr3IJ8ybg`).

**Expect pinnipeds on hooked fish.** A hooked yellowtail was lost boatside to a
seal working the boat (`yuXr3IJ8ybg`); the front side also carries a resident
sea lion that has taxed a trip's catch
([front side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md)).

**Current is a spot-level read at this island.** Two San Clemente Island spots a
short run apart can show opposite current directions on the same day, so check
the current here rather than carrying a read down from the last stop
([front side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)).
