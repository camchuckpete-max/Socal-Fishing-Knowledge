# Finger Bank rockfish

**A zone is a run grouping.** Finger Bank rockfish is a five-mark cluster of
depth-labeled bottom spots (cameron) running from **32°16.360'N 117°10.470'W**
down to **32°14.000'N 117°07.100'W** — about **2.4 minutes of latitude** — and
it is **the only Baja bank zone in the library whose spot labels name what is
fished there**. Three of the five say *rockfish* outright; the other two are
bare fathom marks.

It is its own `##` section of the spot library rather than a coordinate cluster
carved out of the offshore-banks catch-all, so the zone's boundary is the one
the fishery drew, not one the census inferred.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these five marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid. This is therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says
where the zone is, what the labels claim, what the label depths imply against
the rest of the KB, and what is missing. Everything a plan would need beyond
the coordinates is flagged below.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any port, ramp, hoist, fuel dock or bait
receiver used for this zone, and no run distance or time from anywhere. There
is no supported staging point in the KB for a trip here.

**The zone's north end sits just south of the Coronado Islands' southeast
corner.** The Coronados' southernmost charted mark, the **Rockpile**, is at
**32°17.620'N 117°10.030'W** (cameron) — about **1.3 minutes of latitude**
north of this zone's 180 ft spot, at effectively the same longitude — and the
Coronados run **~13 nm from the San Diego bay entrance** (cameron). That is the
closest thing the corpus offers to an access frame. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source states that this zone is fished on a Coronados day, run from San Diego,
or reached from any Baja port.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)). This matters more here than on most bank
pages because the zone is a **rockfish** zone in **Mexican** water: the
depth-based closure lines, seasons and bag limits documented in the
[rockfish & lingcod router](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md) are
**California / CDFW** rules and describe US water only. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: Mexican rockfish seasons, depth restrictions, bag limits or
closures applying to this zone.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The five spots sort into a tight western group of four plus one shoreward
outlier** (cameron):

- **The western group** — the *180ft*, *240ft* and *300ft* rockfish marks and
  the *71 Fathom Spot*, spread across about **1.1 minutes of longitude**
  (117°10.260'W to 117°11.330'W) and **1.9 minutes of latitude** (32°16.360'N
  to 32°14.480'N). Four marks inside a box that small is one drift-to-drift
  program, not four separate trips.
- **The shoreward outlier — the 27 Fathom Spot** at **32°14.000'N
  117°07.100'W**, about **3.2 minutes of longitude east** of the western
  group's nearest mark and the southernmost position in the zone. It is also
  the shallowest label, which is what you would expect from the most
  shoreward mark. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether the 27 Fathom Spot
  is worked on the same day as the western four.

**The labels give the zone a complete depth ladder, and nothing else does.**
At **six feet to a fathom** ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`),
the five labels read: **27 fathoms (162 ft)**, **180 ft**, **240 ft**,
**300 ft**, **71 fathoms (426 ft)**. Within the western group the labels
**deepen from north to south** — 180 ft at the top (32°16.360'N), 240 ft and
300 ft in the middle (both near 32°15.1'N), 71 fathoms at the bottom
(32°14.480'N) — and the deepest mark is also the westernmost. The bottom
falling away to the west and south is what the label set implies; ⚠ Flagged
gap — no corpus source confirms that reading, or gives relief, bottom
composition, pinnacle count or current behaviour anywhere in this zone.

**The zone's name and the charted Upper Finger Bank do not sit in the same
place.** The library's offshore-banks list charts the **Upper Finger Bank** at
**32°06.800'N 117°04.400'W** and the **475 Knuckle** at **32°04.000'N
117°07.000'W** (cameron) — a separate zone, roughly **7 to 10 minutes of
latitude south** of these five marks and **3 to 7 minutes of longitude east**
of them. So the five spots here carry the Upper Finger Bank name while lying
well north and west of the position charted under that name. ⚠ Flagged gap —
no corpus source: whether these marks sit on a northern extension of the same
bank feature — a *finger* would suggest exactly that — or whether the label is
inherited loosely from the nearest named bank.

**Directly inshore, at the same latitudes, is the Descanso cluster.** Punta
Descanso (**32°15.880'N 117°01.890'W**) and *Descanso rockfish 1*
(**32°14.836'N 117°00.876'W**) in
[Rosarito / Descanso](rosarito-descanso.md) share this zone's band of latitude
about **6 to 10 minutes of longitude east** (cameron). Those coastal marks
carry the same *rockfish* label with **no depth**; this zone carries the depths
with **no coastline**. Together they are the only two zones in the region whose
labels name rockfish, and neither has a source describing a trip.

## Spots

Five charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. They group as one workable
cluster plus one shoreward mark:

- **The western four — the 180 ft, 240 ft and 300 ft rockfish marks and the
  71 Fathom Spot** (cameron), inside about a minute of longitude and two of
  latitude, and the reason the zone exists as a run grouping: a single depth
  ladder from 180 ft down to 426 ft that a boat can walk in one session.
- **The 27 Fathom Spot** — the zone's shallowest label and its only eastern
  position, sitting toward the Descanso coast rather than with the group.

Charted positions for all five are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus
detail beyond its position and the depth in its own name, so each spot page is
coordinates plus its parent zone until sources arrive.
