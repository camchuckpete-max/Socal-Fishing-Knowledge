# Point Loma

The San Diego coastal zone on the ocean face of the Point Loma peninsula and
around the corner into the bay entrance: **12 charted spots** in the spot
library (cameron). Six of them run in a near-straight north–south line down the
Sunset Cliffs face; the rest are the Point Loma Pipe, three marks south of the
point, and — east of the point — the Zuniga Jetty and the Hotel Del hard
bottom. Two things make the zone matter beyond its own spots. Point Loma is one
of the corpus's named SoCal kelp beds, used as a stand-in for "the kelp bed"
when inshore fishing is taught (`xI9tPJFXbUM`, `kzD0kSnnVPw`); and the
peninsula is the swell block that decides whether the San Diego Bay entrance is
protected or rough (cameron).

## Getting there

**The peninsula is the swell block for the whole bay entrance.** The San Diego
Bay entrance is open to swell from roughly **160°–186°**, with the measured
channel seaward axis at **186°**. Baja blocks swell coming from east of
**~160°**, and **Point Loma blocks swell from west of the channel axis**
(cameron). That makes the zone's own landmass the variable in whether the run
out and the return through the channel are rough — read the swell direction
against those two numbers before committing to a day here
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

The zone's east end **is** that entrance: the Zuniga Jetty sits at
**32°40.300'N 117°13.400'W** (cameron), and the Hotel Del hard bottom
(**32°40.100'N 117°11.280'W**) lies further east again, outside the Coronado
beach.

**No spot in this zone carries a boundary note in its library label**
(cameron) — unlike the neighbouring [La Jolla](la-jolla.md) zone, where three
spots do. California / CDFW — verify current MPA boundaries, closures,
restricted areas and take rules with CDFW before fishing (as of 2026-08).

**Point Loma is also the corpus's mainland reference point for offshore
distances, and none of that water is in this zone.** The 2017 bluefin push was
framed as running from the San Clemente Island West End down to **10 mi off
Point Loma**, fish 50 to almost 300 lb (`Rb5I2ljAqeE`), and a **315 lb**
bluefin was taken **59 mi off Point Loma** (`0E9REoTjKrY`). Both are offshore
positions named for this landmark, not fishing on it — see
[bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md).

## Structure & bathymetry

The twelve sort into four families by charted position (cameron):

- **The Sunset Cliffs face — six.** Hill Street (**32°43.790'N
  117°16.660'W**), College Area / Sunset Cliffs (**32°43.180'N 117°16.630'W**),
  Green Tank (**32°42.610'N 117°16.740'W**), The Lab (**32°41.450'N
  117°16.310'W**), New Hope Rock (**32°41.140'N 117°15.990'W**) and 5 Tanks
  (**32°40.870'N 117°16.160'W**) — a near-straight north–south line down the
  ocean face of the peninsula, spanning **2.9'** of latitude inside **0.75'**
  of longitude (cameron).
- **The pipe — one.** Point Loma Pipe (**32°40.350'N 117°17.000'W**), the
  zone's westernmost position.
- **South of the point — three.** The Dropoff (**32°39.150'N 117°15.720'W**),
  Buoy 3 Hardbottom (**32°38.250'N 117°14.500'W**) and Whistler Buoy
  (**32°37.470'N 117°14.720'W**), stepping south and east to the zone's
  southern limit.
- **The entrance and Coronado — two.** Zuniga Jetty (**32°40.300'N
  117°13.400'W**) and Hotel Del Hardbottom (**32°40.100'N 117°11.280'W**).

**Kelp.** Point Loma is one of the five named beds the corpus teaches kelp
fishing on, listed beside San Onofre, La Jolla, San Clemente Island and
Catalina (`kzD0kSnnVPw`), and one of the three named as the stand-in "the kelp
bed" in inshore instruction (`xI9tPJFXbUM`). Which of the twelve charted marks
sit in the bed is not stated anywhere in the corpus.

**The pipe is deep-water structure, and it is *large* structure.** Sewer and
outfall pipes run all up and down the SoCal coast, aggregate bait and the bass
that follow it, and are the cold-season deep option; a coastal pipe is
explicitly the *larger*-structure case rather than an isolated wreck or small
reef (`Kf5wk_TFgTc`).

**Two of the twelve are named for the bottom itself** — Buoy 3 Hardbottom and
Hotel Del Hardbottom (cameron). Hard bottom, and specifically the hard-to-soft
transition where reef meets sand, is the bass-holding feature
([bass structure](bass-structure.md)).

**The Zuniga Jetty is riprap, and riprap is a current structure.** It is named
on camera among the SoCal walls worth fishing (`XyKF34C5iB4`). A wall does not
fish uniformly: the points, pockets, corners and angle changes are the "thumb
over the hose" deviations that concentrate fish, and a wall's ends and corners
are where the deviation is largest
([break walls, jetties and riprap](breakwalls-jetties-riprap.md)).

Charted positions for all twelve are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).

## How it fishes

**At the kelp, current direction picks the corner.** Point Loma is one of the
named beds this rule is taught on: **current running downhill → work the
northwest corner; current running uphill → work the southeast corner**, and
the same logic carries to rock structure (`kzD0kSnnVPw`). Mechanism in
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md).

**Structure size picks the method.** An isolated wreck or small reef is fished
vertically, because the bass are either on it or they are not; a larger piece
like one of the coast's pipes holds bass scattered across it, so you cast
around and move to find them (`Kf5wk_TFgTc`). The vertical build is a sinking
slug-style bait or a [slow-pitch jig](../techniques/slow-pitch-jigging.md)
worked on the fall; the covering-water build is
[swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md) and
[inshore crankbaits](../techniques/inshore-crankbaits.md).

**Winter moves the zone off the beach and onto the deep marks.** Kelp and
shallow reef are the first to shut down when runoff dirties the inshore water
while the deeper structure stays fishable, and that deeper structure is the
most reliable cold-season bite on this coast (`Kf5wk_TFgTc`) — here that means
the pipe and the two hard-bottom marks rather than the cliffs face. Seasonal
frame in [February–March](../seasonal/february-march.md).

**On the iron, dial the retrieve tighter than an island trip needs.** The
smaller mainland schools from Del Mar down to Imperial Beach are finicky about
jig action compared with a big Coronados school on the same gear, especially
on bigger-grade fish (`VpW91AKOFVQ`) — see
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md).

**On the jetty, fish the deviations and skip the straightaways.** Approach from
the up-current side so the presentation sweeps down onto the deviation the way
food arrives, and position so a hooked fish comes away from the rock rather
than into it ([break walls, jetties and riprap](breakwalls-jetties-riprap.md)).
Birds parked on one stretch of a wall mark the deviation for you
([bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md)).

## Spots

Twelve charted spots (cameron), grouped by what the structure is rather than by
how close they sit. Only two of the twelve are named for the bottom, one for a
pipe, one for a jetty, one for a rock and one for a buoy; the rest carry
landmark names the corpus never explains.

- **The Sunset Cliffs face** — Hill Street, College Area / Sunset Cliffs, Green
  Tank, The Lab, New Hope Rock, 5 Tanks. The zone's shallow, kelp-and-reef end.
- **The pipe** — Point Loma Pipe, the westernmost mark and the zone's one piece
  of *large* deep structure.
- **South of the point** — The Dropoff, Buoy 3 Hardbottom, Whistler Buoy.
- **The entrance and Coronado** — Zuniga Jetty, the zone's riprap and the one
  spot a source names on camera (`XyKF34C5iB4`), and Hotel Del Hardbottom.

No spot in the zone carries corpus detail beyond its position except the Zuniga
Jetty, and that mention is a name in a list of SoCal walls rather than a
description of this wall (`XyKF34C5iB4`).
