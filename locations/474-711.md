# 474 / 711

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is two charted marks in the open water between
the islands — the **474** at **33°09.500'N 118°55.000'W** and the **711** at
**33°08.000'N 119°03.000'W** (cameron). They lie **1.5 minutes of latitude** and
**8.0 minutes of longitude** apart, which works out to **6.9 nm** on a near due
east–west line: the 474 bears **077°** from the 711, the 711 bears **257°** from
the 474.

**This zone is interior water of the September volume square.** The square drawn
"from the Santa Barbara Island to Nick to Clemente to Cat" is where the volume of
bluefin sits in September and "that's where a lot of the bigger ones are"
(`5to3Q5P7w90`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md#the-volume-square)). Both marks
fall inside it on the spot library's own positions — north of every charted San
Clemente Island position, south of every Santa Barbara Island position, east of
every San Nicolas position and west of every Catalina position (cameron). Of the
offshore-bank zones the census cut out of this part of the Bight, this is the one
that sits in the middle of that box rather than outside its edge.

**The 711 is a named corner of the July 2022 search block.** The water above San
Clemente was drawn "from cat to the osborne bank to nick to that 7-eleven outside
of clementi" (`Blh2BA-7Ono`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states which
charted mark "7-Eleven" names; the 711 is the only position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) carrying that number, and it sits
offshore of San Clemente's back side, which is where the report puts the corner.
The **474** appears nowhere in the corpus but the library (cameron).

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run distance, heading, timing or fuel
figure to this zone from any port, and no source names a port either mark is
fished out of.

**Four islands surround this zone and none of them is close.** From the 474 the
nearest charted land is the **9 Fathom Spot** on
[San Clemente Island's back side](san-clemente-island-back-side.md) at
**16.1 nm** on **115°**, then Sutil Island at
[Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) at **19.4 nm** on **341°** and
the West End Humps on [Catalina](catalina-island-backside.md) at **23.8 nm** on
**039°**. From the 711 the nearest is the **East End Buoy** at
[San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md), **18.3 nm** on **287°** (cameron).
Every one of those legs is longer than the **6.9 nm** between the two marks, so
this is open water first and island water second.

**The corpus's shape for this water is a slide-off from an island program.** In
July 2022 boats fishing sea bass at San Nicolas or yellows at San Clemente could
slide off the islands and add bluefin to the mix — one trip took bluefin, sea
bass, halibut and yellowtail on the same day (`Blh2BA-7Ono`). The long-range
fleet worked the same block as a destination, with the boats that had the time to
look expected to find scattered fish again (`Blh2BA-7Ono`). Both patterns start
from an island, not from the beach.

**The mainland is a long way off.** The nearest charted mainland position in the
spot library is **Pelican Point** in
[South Orange County](south-orange-county-crystal-cove.md) at **58.8 nm** from
the 474 and **65.5 nm** from the 711, with the Dana Point hundred-fathom curve at
**60.2 nm** and **67.0 nm** (cameron), both bearing east-northeast. ⚠ Flagged gap
— no corpus source: distance and run time from Dana Point, Newport, Long Beach or
San Pedro, and the sea-state envelope the crossing demands. Trip length against
that kind of leg is in
[trip-length selection](../planning/trip-length-selection.md).

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries or restricted areas
stated for either position in this zone. The two **naval security zones** the
spot library records nearby — Northwest Harbor and Castle Rock — attach to San
Clemente Island shoreline positions **16.9–18.2 nm** east of the 474, not to
these offshore marks (cameron,
[back side](san-clemente-island-back-side.md)). The corpus also names the **Cow
Cod Conservation Area** as an offshore closure with its own depth limit and its
own as-of date ([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)) but gives
no boundary for it, so whether either mark falls inside it is unverified —
**check the CCCA boundary against your position with CDFW/NOAA before dropping.**
Boundary compliance is positional, not depth-based ([US waters](us-waters.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The two positions and what separates them.** The 474 sits at **33°09.500'N
118°55.000'W** and the 711 at **33°08.000'N 119°03.000'W** — **6.9 nm** apart
(cameron), comfortably inside the census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, which is
the clustering rule that grouped them. Neither is closer to any other mark in the
library than it is to the other, which is what makes this a run grouping rather
than a pair of attachments.

**The names are the only depth claims in the zone.** A bank is an isolated high
spot rising out of otherwise deep water and takes the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way:

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 474 | 474 fathoms | about 2,844 ft |
| 711 | 711 fathoms | about 4,266 ft |

**Neither reading behaves like a high spot.** The naming convention describes
banks as rising out of a **2,000–3,000 ft** featureless plain (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`);
the 474's figure lands at the bottom of that range and the 711's is **well
deeper** than the plain the convention contrasts banks against. Both are also
below the **800–1,100 ft** band SoCal daytime swordfish baits are set in
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)) and below the
**150–350 fathom** contour band that fishery works
([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms
either number as a charted depth, says whether either name is a depth at all, or
gives relief, bottom composition or surrounding depth at either mark. The library
records the naming exception that some marks are named for **distance off the
beach** rather than depth ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`), and
nothing states which convention these two follow.

**The nearest structure in any direction belongs to another zone.** The **499**
lies **11.2 nm** from the 474 on **051°** and is parented to
[172 / 125](172-125.md); **Osborn Bank** lies **13.6 nm** from both marks, almost
due north, and is parented to [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md)
(cameron). Both are closer than any charted land. The practical consequence for a
day plan: a boat that has read this zone out has two named marks inside a short
run, on two different pages.

**The zone sits on the southeast corner of the July 2022 above-Clemente block.**
The block's four named corners — Catalina's West End, Osborn Bank, San Nicolas
and the 711 (`Blh2BA-7Ono`) — enclose roughly **263 square nm** on the library's
charted positions, with edges of **22.1 nm**, **19.6 nm**, **18.3 nm** and
**29.5 nm** (cameron). The 711 is the corner itself; the 474 sits **3.4 nm**
outside the 711-to-Catalina edge, on the San Clemente side, one-fifth of the way
along it. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states the block as a boundary rather
than as four named corners, so whether the 474 is inside it is not stated.

**The zone also lies on the mid-October 2022 movement line.** By mid-October 2022
the bluefin had left San Nicolas for the **east end and back side** of San
Clemente, with Nick named as water they could already be back on without the
coverage to know (`XLVUhV8DW64`, [October](../seasonal/october.md)). The direct
line from the East End Buoy at San Nicolas to the northernmost charted position
on San Clemente's back side runs **40.2 nm**, and the **711 sits 0.5 nm off it**,
**45%** of the way across; the 474 sits **2.7 nm** off the same line. Drawn
instead to the **east-end** back-side positions the report names, the line runs
**55–57 nm**, the 711 stays inside **4 nm** of it and the 474 falls **7–8 nm**
off (cameron) — so the 711 is on the crossing whichever back-side end you draw
to, and the 474 only on the near-end reading. ⚠ Flagged gap —
no corpus source places fish on either mark during that move; the line is
arithmetic on the library, not a reported track.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: high-spot shape, preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at the
474 or the 711.

## What's there

| --- | --- | --- |
| [bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) | July–October, all documented in 2022 | **July:** fish that had been outside San Clemente slid in between San Clemente and Catalina and joined fish that had come up off Catalina's West End, and the long-range fleet did "really well" on them before they scattered; the search block drawn afterwards carries the 711 as a corner (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [June–July](../seasonal/june-july.md)). **September:** this is interior water of the volume square where "a lot of the bigger ones are," and in that week the fish had been in the same place for three weeks, "in between the islands on the [fathom] curves on the edges of the banks and ridges up there" (`5to3Q5P7w90`, [September](../seasonal/september.md)). **October:** the fish moved off San Nicolas for the back side of San Clemente, a line that runs through this zone (`XLVUhV8DW64`, cameron) |
| [white seabass](../species/white-seabass.md) / [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) | ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source | Both are named at the islands either side of this water rather than on these marks: in July 2022 the boats were fishing sea bass at San Nicolas and yellows at San Clemente and sliding **off** the islands to add bluefin, not the reverse (`Blh2BA-7Ono`) |
| everything else | ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source | Nothing in the corpus names the 474 at all, and the 711 is named once, as a corner of a search block |

**The September grade claim is about the box, not about these two marks.** The
volume square is stated as a region — "if you make draw a square in there, that's
where a lot of the bigger ones are" (`5to3Q5P7w90`) — and the same report puts
the fish on the fathom curves and bank edges inside it rather than on any named
position. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source names the 474 or the 711 as one of
those bank edges.

**The dorado water named that week is elsewhere.** September 2022 dorado were in
**open-water schools below Catalina**, and the fish spread from the east end of
Catalina out to the east end of San Clemente and down Clemente Ridge to the 289
(`5to3Q5P7w90`, [dorado](../species/dorado.md),
[September](../seasonal/september.md)) — the east side of the islands, not this
water. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source describes
[dorado](../species/dorado.md), [yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md),
[striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md),
[swordfish](../species/swordfish.md) or
[rockfish and lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md) on either mark, and
nothing describes bait, squid, temperature or water colour over them. The silence
is a gap in the sources, not a statement that the zone is empty.

**This zone sits outside the named paddy band.** The best paddy water is **south
of San Clemente Island / Catalina** and the coastal strip from **Dana Point to
Oceanside and southward** (`HzE4FHHcvTk`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)). Both marks lie north of every charted San
Clemente position in the library and west of Catalina (cameron), which is between
the islands rather than below them. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source describes a
paddy over either position either way; mechanics are in
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

## How it fishes

**The one program statement the corpus makes for the inside of this square is a
contour instruction.** In September 2022 the fish had held for three weeks "in
between the islands on the [fathom] curves on the edges of the banks and ridges
up there" (`5to3Q5P7w90`). That puts the boat on the **edges** of a bank rather
than over its high spot, which is what
[island structure](island-structure.md) and
[electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md) describe
working; it is the only corpus line that tells a plan where to put the boat once
it is in this water. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source gives the depth of those
curves, which side of a bank to run, or a drift direction.

**Grade comes off a different technique set than volume.** In the same week the
bigger tuna were being caught **at night**, or by four-pack boats, or by boats
**flying kites and trolling spreader bars and Mad Macs** — "same stuff, same
areas" (`5to3Q5P7w90`). Selection between them by wind, grade and conditions is
in [bluefin trolling](../species/bluefin-tuna-trolling.md); execution is in
[kite fishing](../techniques/kite-fishing.md), and the running parameters are on
[the spreader bar](../lures/spreader-bar.md) and
[the Mad Mac](../lures/mad-mac.md).

**The search box is far bigger than the zone.** The above-Clemente block is
"another big area" whose fish "aren't really holding hard to structure" and can
move **10–15 miles overnight**, which is why boats that had been on them lose
them (`Blh2BA-7Ono`). At roughly **263 square nm** on the library's corner
positions (cameron) it is several times the **~50–60 square-mile** complex the
same report sizes as a half-day of looking
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). Budget hours against
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md), and age yesterday's
report against that overnight displacement with
[report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)
before pointing the boat at either mark.

**The report's big-area method is bank-to-bank, sign-first.** Run bank to bank
and look for **colour changes, temperature changes and bird life** — anything
that looks different from before — then commit on what you find
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [water colour](../conditions/water-color.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md)). Two marks **6.9 nm** apart with
the 499 **11.2 nm** further on (cameron) is a bank-to-bank line of exactly that
shape. ⚠ Flagged gap — the method is stated for the below-Clemente complex in
that report; no corpus source restates it for the block this zone sits in, which
the same report calls another big area with the same problem.

**Timing is the late-morning slack.** When an area looks fishier than the rest,
be back in it at the slack tide, and specifically at a **late-morning slack after
09:30** where the day offers one (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[tide and slack](../conditions/tide-and-slack.md)). The cost of skipping it shows
up in the same corpus: a September 2022 charter found early sign off the fathom
curve on Catalina's back side, did not want to wait for the slack, and moved off
(`5to3Q5P7w90`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source gives a tide or current window
for these two marks specifically.

**A warm reading is not a reason to leave.** Bluefin were seen foaming on the
surface in **76 °F** water in September 2022, above the usually assumed ceiling
(`5to3Q5P7w90`, [September](../seasonal/september.md)); treat warm SST as soft
evidence against bluefin, not a gate
([water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md)).

**In fall the chart decides whether this zone is on the plan.** The regime flips
to reading temperature and chlorophyll, and the warm band tracks backside Santa
Cruz/Rosa → Santa Barbara Island → backside Catalina → hooks to the 499 and San
Clemente, with the fish condensing outward toward Tanner and Cortez as the coast
cools ([bight geography](bight-geography.md), `OYOda6T3f-8`,
[October](../seasonal/october.md)). Both ends of that arc pass this zone: the 499
is **11.2 nm** off the 474, and Tanner Bank is **26.4 nm** off the 711 (cameron).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source places the band over either mark.

**Wind geography does not resolve here.** A big NW blow rolls the northern
Channel Islands and the LA/Ventura shelf hardest while **Catalina and San
Clemente held their water** through the same event
([bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). This zone
sits in open water between the two islands that held (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap —
no corpus source says whether the open water between them rolls or holds; treat
it as an open question and pull conditions per position
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

**Neither mark is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions for this zone are
pulled per charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)). The nearest eval target is **Pyramid
Cove**, **33.8 nm** southeast of the 474 (cameron) — an island cove on the far
side of San Clemente, far enough that its score says nothing about this water.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron), listed west to east:

- **The 711** — the western mark and the only position in the zone the corpus
  carries: the "7-Eleven outside of Clemente" corner of the July 2022
  above-Clemente search block (`Blh2BA-7Ono`). It is also the mark that sits on
  the October 2022 San Nicolas–to–San Clemente line, **0.5 nm** off it, and the
  closer of the two to San Nicolas (**18.3 nm**) and to Tanner Bank
  (**26.4 nm**).
- **The 474** — the eastern mark, closest in the zone to San Clemente Island
  (**16.1 nm**), to the 499 (**11.2 nm**) and to the mainland (**58.8 nm**).
  Coordinates only; the corpus never names it.

Charted positions for both are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position and, for the 711, the block-corner cite above, so each spot
page is coordinates plus its parent zone until sources arrive.
