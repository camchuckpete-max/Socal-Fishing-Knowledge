# Davis Knoll / San Miguel Gap / Rodriguez Seamount

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is the northwest corner of the
[Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md) — four charted positions (cameron)
offshore of Point Arguello and San Miguel Island, at the outer edge of the
region's stated limit of Point Conception to the US border
([regions & waters](regions.md)). Three of them — **Davis Knoll**, **Rodriguez
Seamount** and the **San Miguel Gap** — sit inside **11.3 nm** of each other and
are the cluster the zone is named for; **Arguello Canyon**, **15.1 nm** off the
zone centre, is a lone position the census attached here as the nearest grouping
it could be fished with (cameron).

**The only doctrine the corpus scopes to this water is weather doctrine.** This
is the water a Santa Ana is needed to lay down (`OYOda6T3f-8`, `Z3rZqy2Pi8E`),
and the crossing to it is gated on a wind rule that a morning forecast will not
show. How the seamounts themselves fish is uncovered, so this is a
**position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what it takes to get
there, and what is missing.

## Getting there

**W and NW wind blows around Point Conception at night and quits in the
morning, so a calm 6 a.m. reading is a trap** — the crossing to this corner can
be extremely rough under a forecast that looks flat at the dock (`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`).
The go/no-go rule is **2 or more consecutive days of no wind or Santa Anas**
before committing to the crossing (`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`), which is why a **12–24 h wind
hindcast** for the route belongs in every conditions pull for this zone
alongside the forward forecast ([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

**A Santa Ana is the one wind that lays this corner down.** Santa Ana events
flatten the northwest outer islands and are the trigger to run Santa Rosa and
San Miguel (`OYOda6T3f-8`, `Z3rZqy2Pi8E`). A Santa Ana is an offshore-blowing
**northeast** pattern and a recurring seasonal norm rather than an anomaly,
typically running through Thanksgiving (`NC3-3pJDEgo`); the seasonal framing is
in [November–December](../seasonal/november-december.md).

**Check the up-coast fetch, not just the local forecast.** Swell arrives days
after and hundreds of miles from the wind that made it — a system off Oregon put
San Nicolas from **4 ft to 12 ft** by the Monday after a locally calm weekend,
with the swell tracking down past Point Conception (`zKmZ4zql2ws`). Scan the
synoptic wind field up-coast over the prior **48–72 h**.

/ San Miguel crossing, which passes inshore of this zone.

**Santa Barbara Landing is the only departure the corpus attaches to this
water.** Boats out of it ran to Santa Rosa and San Miguel on a Santa Ana
weekend, and a trailer boat launched there picking Rosa or Miguel on sea
conditions (`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: run distance,
heading, timing or fuel from Santa Barbara or any other port to any of these four
marks, and nothing states that these marks are fished out of anywhere at all.
The region page carries the same gap for launch ramps, port-to-zone distances
and outer-bank fuel planning ([Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)).

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md);
season and depth rules for the one target the corpus names at this end of the
region are in [rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)). ⚠ Flagged
gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries or restricted areas anywhere in
this zone.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three-high core is what makes this one zone.** Davis Knoll at
**34°07.200'N 120°55.200'W**, Rodriguez Seamount at **34°02.800'N 121°03.000'W**
and the San Miguel Gap at **33°56.600'N 121°00.000'W** (cameron) sit **6.7 nm**
(Rodriguez to San Miguel Gap), **7.8 nm** (Davis Knoll to Rodriguez) and
**11.3 nm** (Davis Knoll to San Miguel Gap) apart — every pair inside the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, which is the clustering rule that grouped
them.

**Arguello Canyon was attached, not clustered.** It lies at **34°22.000'N
121°04.000'W**, **15.1 nm** from the zone centre and **16.5 nm**, **19.2 nm** and
**25.6 nm** from Davis Knoll, Rodriguez Seamount and the San Miguel Gap
respectively (cameron) — the widest span in the zone and well past the cap. It
is carried here as queued rather than re-parented, because the zone list is set
by the census and not by this page.

**The names give no depth here.** A bank takes the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`) — the convention that makes the
182, the 209 and the 43 readable off their names alone. None of these four names
carries a number, so there is nothing to read. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
charted depth, relief, high-spot shape, bottom composition, kelp, or current
behaviour at any of the four. The `depth_band` is unknown for that reason.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what **knoll**, **seamount**, **gap** and
**canyon** denote as structure terms. The lexicon defines *bank* — an isolated
high spot rising out of surrounding depth, where bait stages for shelter and
gamefish follow ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)) — and nothing else in this
zone's vocabulary.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** that you learn over
repeat trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: the preferred directions, or any drift, on any of these four.

**This is the most isolated grouping in the region.** No other charted position
in the spot library lies within **36.5 nm** of any member — the nearest is
**Patton Ridge**, 36.5 nm off the San Miguel Gap and **45.0 nm** from the zone
centre, which the census minted no zone for at all. Then come **311 (Trask
Knoll)** at **47.8 nm** from the centre, **San Juan Seamount** at **63.4 nm**,
**12 Mile Reef** at **66.1 nm** and **Sverdrup Bank (126)** at **67.3 nm**
(cameron). This is not water you drop into on the way somewhere else, and there
is no adjacent zone to fall back on if it does not produce. ⚠ Flagged stub — no
corpus source yet: each of those four neighbours is charted position only, none
has a page yet, and none carries fishing doctrine to hand off to.

**The northern Channel Islands mint no zone, so this page is the region's only
charted ladder entry for its northwest corner.** Santa Cruz, Santa Rosa and San
Miguel carry no coordinates in the spot library even though the corpus names
them repeatedly ([Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)) — which is why the
Conception crossing doctrine above, stated for a Rosa/Miguel run, has no island
page of its own to live on.

**The target the Santa Ana window is run for is an island-shallow target, not an
offshore-high one.** The Rosa/Miguel trigger is **shallow rockfish and lingcod**
(`OYOda6T3f-8`, `Z3rZqy2Pi8E`), with lings pulling onto shallow high spots in
late fall and water as shallow as **20–30 ft** named as fishable
(`Z3rZqy2Pi8E`); see [rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md).
Nothing extends that pattern out to these offshore highs.

**The nearest fish claim in the corpus at this latitude is scoped to the island,
not to these marks.** Pacific bluefin off SoCal and northern Baja are claimed as
a resident stock ranging Point Conception to Cedros Island, with a **2019**
sighting of a school of **100–300 lb** fish jumping just south of San Miguel
Island (`fgTmUq78ofQ`); the claim is carried and flagged single-source in the
[bluefin router](../species/bluefin-tuna.md), and no coordinate is attached to
it.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the three-high core is worked as one circuit,
whether Arguello Canyon is worth the extra 16–26 nm leg, or what current, tide,

**What is known is what the water does to the water, not how to fish it.** A
strong NW blow crushes this end of the region hardest — San Miguel, Santa Rosa
and the front side of Santa Cruz dropped into the **low 50s °F** after one
event, a **5–8 °F** rolldown, while Clemente, Catalina and the Coronados held
(`YZtX1MiT0y8`). Plan a temperature read on this corner as the first casualty of
a NW event, not the last ([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md),
[upwelling & turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md),
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
extends that rolldown offshore to these four positions; it is stated for the
islands inshore of them.

## Spots

Four charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. They group as a core plus
one attachment:

- **The core three — Davis Knoll, Rodriguez Seamount and the San Miguel Gap**,
  inside 11.3 nm of each other (cameron) and the only part of this zone that
  clustered on its own. Davis Knoll is the inshore-most of the three by
  longitude; the San Miguel Gap is the southern end.
- **Arguello Canyon** — the northern outlier, 15.1 nm off the zone centre and
  the region's northernmost charted position west of the Santa Barbara Channel
  (cameron). A canyon head rather than a high spot, by its name alone.

Charted positions for all four are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
