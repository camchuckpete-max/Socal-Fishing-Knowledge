# San Benito Islands

**[Baja only]** — the island group in the
[Cedros / San Benitos](cedros-island.md) zone, fished on the same long-range
routes as Cedros and named with it as one corner of the "Yellow Tail triangle"
the crews draw between Cedros Island, the Benitos and Punta Eugenia
(`_r_qKX_7080`). It is the zone's **deep, squid-keyed**
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) ground: the fish sit **30–40 fathoms**
down feeding right off the bottom rather than up top (`JaKSGkZ6CAc`), over a
jagged rocky bottom that takes terminal tackle off anyone who leaves it down
(`ilINTeknKB4`). Capt. Art of the *Searcher* named the San Benitos beside Cedros
as among his favourite places to fish [surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)
for yellowtail (`lYyi_Yh3S5g`).

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
The corpus names the group and gives working depths, never a mark, so a
conditions pull has to be run against the zone rather than against this island.
The captions render the name several ways — "Benito's," "Benitos," "San Bonitas
Island," "Bonito Island" — all asr variants read as San Benito.

## Getting there

**The documented access is a San Diego long-range boat, not the zone's fly-in
program.** On one trip aboard the *Top Gun 80* out of H&M Landing the boat
fished bluefin and yellowfin roughly **170 mi** south of the border on day one,
then ran a further **~150 mi** south to San Benito for day two's yellowtail
(`JaKSGkZ6CAc`). A second charter, aboard the *Intrepid* under Capt. Kevin
Osborne, worked the island across an 8-day run (`97clKtVsEOs`, `ilINTeknKB4`).
Cedros and the San Benitos also come up as an optional extra leg on San Diego
five- and six-day trips primarily bound for [Guadalupe](guadalupe.md) — "always a
chance to go to Cedros or San Benitos" (`_r08B4bItAY`). The zone's charter-flight
program is written around the Cedros lodge, not this island
([the zone page](cedros-island.md#getting-there)).

**The island works as a base inside a longer itinerary rather than a
destination on its own.** The *Intrepid* left a Benito's yellowtail leg to run to
[Alijos Rocks](alijos-rocks.md) specifically to troll for wahoo, scouted
[the Ridge](ridge-uncle-sam-bank.md) and left it because "the water was just too
warm," then came back to Benito's for another morning (`97clKtVsEOs`). One
multi-stop Cedros Outdoor Adventures trip ran the other way —
"macro banks" → Benitos → mainland and [Chester's Rock](chester-s-rock.md)
(`yjwIGFzWO8I`).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a port, heading, run time, nautical-mile
distance or sea-state envelope for reaching the San Benitos, an anchorage, or
how the group is reached by a private boat.

**Regulatory — Mexico.** The Mexican entry and sport-fishing paperwork for this
water is recorded on [Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md) and
[the zone page](cedros-island.md). The zone's **100% catch-and-release**
agreement for [calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md), grouper (cabrilla) and
black seabass is an **operator agreement adopted by the fishing companies at
Cedros**, not a federal or state rule; recorded on or before 2023-05-24, and
whether it is applied at the San Benitos is not stated in the corpus. **Verify
current status with your operator before fishing**
([regulatory claims](../sources/regulatory-claims.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: the Mexican (CONAPESCA) bag limit, size gauge or season for the
rockfish and lingcod taken here — California rockfish rules do not apply in
Mexican waters.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The bottom is jagged rock at about 200 ft, and it eats terminal tackle.** On a
rockfish drift the captain warned the rail to get sinkers and iron up off the
bottom the moment they touched down or lose them (`ilINTeknKB4`). That is the
same rock-cutoff pattern the crews report across the triangle
(`_r_qKX_7080`), sharpened here by depth.

**The yellowtail depth is a bait depth, not a structure depth.** The fish were
out on squid sitting **30–40 fathoms** down — called "30, 40, 50 fathoms" and
later "30, 35" in the same segment, preserved as spoken — and feeding right off
the bottom; the squid had moved out of the shallows because small mackerel had
taken over there, and the yellowtail moved out with them (`JaKSGkZ6CAc`). Read
the depth off where the squid are, not off a fixed contour.

**Kelp grows over the rock, and the two combine against you.** A hooked
yellowtail ran into kelp, the braid was cut off in it, and the fish then dragged
the frayed, kelp-wrapped line down into the rocks where it parted (`9D3Oiy0ASzg`).
Crew narration generalizes the risk past kelp: a deep-diving fish is "looking for
anything that's structure, a piece of weed, anything to rock you off on"
(`SH7zOA9ZF3o`).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a charted position, soundings other than the
30–40 fathom bait band and the ~200 ft rockfish bottom, the number and names of
the individual islands in the group, kelp extent, or where the group sits
relative to Cedros in miles or bearing.

**Forage is squid, and it is confirmed from the fish.** Nearly every yellowtail
boated had **fresh squid in its stomach** (`JaKSGkZ6CAc`) — the forage call and
the depth call are the same read. Small mackerel had taken over the shallow
water the squid vacated (`JaKSGkZ6CAc`).

**One tackle recap filmed here is not about here.** The *Intrepid* trip's closing
tips summary, shot at Benito's Island on the final morning, covers the whole
8 days — its bluefin build (40 lb mono main line to a **4 ft** top shot of 40 lb
fluorocarbon on sardine) belongs to the trip's earlier northern legs, not to this
island (`ilINTeknKB4`; [bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md)).

## How it fishes

**Three outfits work here, as they do across the zone.** The day's yellowtail
bit on bait, surface iron and the yo-yo through most of the day (`SH7zOA9ZF3o`),
and a second morning again produced them on surface iron with more on the yo-yo
(`97clKtVsEOs`). For the deep squid-keyed fish the rigs are the
[dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) and
[yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) (`JaKSGkZ6CAc`).

**The dropper loop is fished on the bottom on a 100 lb line minimum.** The crew
framed it as "dropper loop on the bottom, 100 lb line minimum" for the day's
30 lb-and-better grade in 30–40+ fathoms (`JaKSGkZ6CAc`); one angler's first-ever
yellowtail came dropped straight to the bottom and worked by hand rather than off
a rod. Line-class context is in [line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md).

**The yo-yo cadence taught here is bottom-up in stages.** A Salas 6X Jr is
free-spooled to the bottom, cranked hard ("reel like the devil") about halfway
up, dropped out of gear back to the bottom, cranked hard about halfway again,
dropped back a second time, then reeled all the way up to finish the cycle
(`SH7zOA9ZF3o`). A first-time yo-yo angler on the same trip described his version
as "put it on the bottom, keep it up, give it about three or four shots, start it
over again — and you get bit" (`JaKSGkZ6CAc`). A later hookup came on a 6X Junior
in a dorado colour pattern (`ilINTeknKB4`).

**A dead bite at anchor with fish still on the meter means drift, not leave.** A
surface-iron bite went quiet once the boat anchored — "they wouldn't respond on
the anchor very well" — while fish still marked on the sounder; getting above the
marks and drifting over them brought the bite back (`97clKtVsEOs`). The general
read is in [electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md#anchor-vs-drift-call-off-a-live-sounder-read).

**Presentation is chosen by where in the column the fish are sitting.** The
trip's closing recap split it three ways: a **mint** surface iron for fish up in
the column, a **scrambled egg** pattern for fish down deep, and a **half-oz
sliding sinker with a nose-hooked sardine** for suspended fish (`ilINTeknKB4`;
[surface iron colour](../techniques/surface-iron-color.md),
[sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md)).

**One crew called a repeat noon window at the same spot.** On the third morning a
crew member expected the bite around noon "like they did the other day… yeah,
timing thing" — a same-spot, day-to-day timing data point, not a general rule
(`ilINTeknKB4`).

**Expect to lose fish to the bottom.** Hooked fish dive for structure of any kind
(`SH7zOA9ZF3o`), kelp over rock has taken a braid-fished fish outright
(`9D3Oiy0ASzg`), and the drift over the 200 ft rock costs sinkers and iron to any
pause on the bottom (`ilINTeknKB4`). The zone's wider 40/50 versus 80/100
line-class argument is on
[the zone page](cedros-island.md#doctrine--conflicts).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a tide, moon, current or wind window for the
San Benitos, how long to give a stop, or how the group fishes outside a
long-range charter program. Every timing statement here is a clock time.
