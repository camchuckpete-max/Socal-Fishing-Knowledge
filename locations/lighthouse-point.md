# Lighthouse Point

**[Baja only]** — the beach point under the lighthouse on the
[East Cape](east-cape.md) shore of the Sea of Cortez, and the zone's one
properly named ground. A local captain calls it *"one of the main fishing spots
that we have here"* and the beach *"world famous"* for roosterfish
(`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`); a later episode calls it *"the worldclass Lighthouse"*
(`YijeuGOYoVQ`). What makes it a spot rather than a stretch of sand is a drop
very close to shore — the crew's phrase is that *"everything's right here on
this Edge"* — putting sharks, roosterfish, jacks, cabrilla and dorado within
casting range of the beach, with the further claim that *"even the yellowfin
tuna you can catch it from the Beach here"* (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`). The lighthouse
structure itself is dated to the **1980s** (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`).

**It carries no charted position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)**, which stops at San Quintín on
the Pacific side and holds nothing on this coast. A day plan therefore has to
pull conditions against the zone rather than against this point.

**Every documented day here is one resort's tournament week** — the Hotel Buena
Vista Beach Resort trips of December 2016 (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`, `YijeuGOYoVQ`,
`mG8ZZLFGlT8`) and April 2020 (`APsnsunT4gM`). Read the page as that operation's
beach program, not as the point's full fishery. Paperwork for the trip is
[Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md).

## Getting there

**This is the walk-and-ride half of an East Cape trip, not a run.** The resort
sits directly on the beach ([East Cape](east-cape.md#getting-there)), and the
lighthouse beach is worked from the sand: anglers rent **ATVs** and cruise the
beach, and the lighthouse is the ground that program works (`YijeuGOYoVQ`).
Some guests fish it to the exclusion of everything else, spending **three or
four days on the beach** (`YijeuGOYoVQ`).

**The clock rule is about light, not feeding.** Start **after 9:00**, because
the sun has to be up high enough to see into the water at all (`YijeuGOYoVQ`) —
the beach program is a sight fishery and cannot start before it can see. The
point is also fished in the **afternoon and at night** (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. CDFW rules do not apply on this side of the line, and a
Mexican limit differing from a California one is two jurisdictions, not a
conflict. Verify current requirements before you fish
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md),
[regulatory claims](../sources/regulatory-claims.md)).**

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: how far the lighthouse is from the resort, in
which direction, whether the beach is public, whether an ATV is required or
merely convenient, and what an ATV rental costs. The parent zone carries the
same gap for the trip as a whole.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The whole spot is one edge, and the corpus never sounds it.** The captain's
description is *"a drop very close to Shore"* with *"everything's right here on
this Edge"* (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`) — that single close-in drop is the reason the point
holds a full inshore assemblage inside a surf cast, and it is also the only
structural statement anyone makes about it. No depth, bottom composition,
relief or extent is given.

**The one shallow-water figure in the zone is not confidently this ground.** A
beach session on the April 2020 trip fished roughly **100 yd off the sand in
about 30 ft** (`APsnsunT4gM`), and that is the nearest thing to a sounding for
any East Cape beach. ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): the same episode states the
plan as roosters, jacks and snappers *"at the lighthouse"* (`APsnsunT4gM`) but
never says the 100 yd / 30 ft session was fished there rather than off the beach
in front of the resort, so [the zone page](east-cape.md#spots) keeps the two
apart and so does this one.

**The lighthouse is a landmark, not the structure.** It is a shore building
dated to the **1980s** (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`); nothing in the corpus connects it to a
reef, rock or point of hard bottom below the waterline — the fish are on the
drop, and the light is what names it.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the depth of the drop, how far off the sand it
sits, the bottom it runs over, how long the fishable stretch is, or where along
the beach to set up relative to the light.

## How it fishes

**Two programs run off the same sand: sight-cast lures, and a soaked bait.**
They differ in everything except the beach.

**Program one — sight fishing on wheels.** Nothing is cast until a fish is
seen: *"it's sight fishing, so cruising, we're not going to fish until we see
something,"* and what you are looking for is **the shadow** rather than the fish
itself (`YijeuGOYoVQ`). Roosters show close — one was **five feet off the
shore** (`YijeuGOYoVQ`). The surf kit is a **12 ft** rod throwing **1.5–2 oz**,
up to a **3 oz** skipped lure, and the retrieve is deliberately loud: the
**more erratic and splashy the better** (`YijeuGOYoVQ`).

**Program two — the short bait soak, and it is the opposite discipline.** The
cast is short — about **30 ft** — then held stationary and thumbed on the spool
(`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`). The terminal is a **circle hook**, and the coaching that goes
with it is the whole technique: let the fish take it and run, *"give him a 10
count, at least 10 seconds,"* then simply **wind** rather than swing
(`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`; [hooks](../tackle/hooks.md),
[fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md)). The same instruction is
repeated on the return session as *"just take your time"* (`mG8ZZLFGlT8`).

**Finding fish is the slow part; the bite is not.** Once fish are located the
window is fast — a hookup **within seconds**, at most a couple of minutes
(`mG8ZZLFGlT8`). Budget the day for searching, not for waiting on a rod.

**Bait comes from the boat program.** The live sardines the beach bait sessions
run on are made inshore first — one April 2020 day gave up **the whole day** to
gathering them because bait had been poor (`APsnsunT4gM`;
[making bait](../bait/making-bait.md)). The beach bait rig the zone documents —
50 lb braid to a 30 lb fluorocarbon top shot, a **#2** circle hook sized for the
sardine, on a 7'6" heavy rod — is recorded on
[the zone page](east-cape.md#how-it-fishes) against the resort beach session
rather than against this point.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: tide, swell, wind, moon or water temperature
at this point. The only condition anyone states is the after-9:00 light window,
and that governs visibility, not the bite — so the page cannot say what surf,
stage of tide or direction of wind makes the drop fish. This is the same hole
the parent zone carries.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what lure the sight-casters actually throw.
The weight range and the retrieve are on record; the lure type, colour and
leader are not.
