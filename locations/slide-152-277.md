# The Slide / 152 / 277

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is the **ridge that runs off the East End of
Catalina** (`3qSY328fFYo`) — three charted marks, The Slide, the 152 and the
277, strung over **9.7 nm** along a single northwest–southeast axis (cameron).
It is close enough to Catalina to be a short run off an Orange County port and
far enough offshore to be blue-water ground: the corpus fishes it for
[striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md) in the fall and runs across
[bluefin](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) foamers on it in August.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): `HzE4FHHcvTk` puts the foamer "just a few
miles past the 152 ridge" on the run back out toward Mackerel Bank, not on the
zone's three marks, and the two bites taken on the zone itself were never
identified as bluefin ("maybe a smaller tuna that just came off").

**The Slide here is the offshore bank, not the Catalina shoreline spot of the
same name.** The two lie **3.4 nm (6.4 km)** apart (cameron), both closest to Catalina, so
the landmass-prefix rule cannot separate them; the collision is an open item
awaiting Cameron ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). The shoreline mark is
[The Slide (Catalina)](slide.md) in the
[Catalina Island — Front Side](catalina-island-front-side.md) zone.

## Getting there

**Navigate by the shipping lanes — both corpus trips do.** The tanker lanes run
across the top of this water and are the landmark for where the jigs go in. One
plotted track ran **19.2 miles at 193°** from an unnamed home port to the bottom
of the tanker lanes, roughly **17 miles out**, dropped the spread there, then
carried on toward the 277 — a further **~35-minute run** (`3qSY328fFYo`).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): in `3qSY328fFYo` "19.2 miles at 193°" is
the first cursor measurement to a mark on the shipping lane, after which the
target is moved and the stated run becomes "so we got uh 18 miles traveling
out"; the ~17 miles is the same leg guessed by eye ("about I don't know 17 miles
probably"), not a separate distance offshore. The
other trip metered its bait concentration "toward the end of the shipping
lanes" (`HzE4FHHcvTk`). That second boat launched at **Newport**
(`HzE4FHHcvTk`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the port the 2014 trip left
from, the traffic-separation rules for crossing or trolling near the lanes, or
the sea-state envelope the run demands.

**This is the zone you fall back to when the outside run blows out.** A
tournament trip launched at **Newport** intending the back side of the 152 ridge
or a run out to Mackerel Bank; a morning **west wind** made getting that far out
tough, so the crew aborted and fished their way up to the 152 ridge instead,
then ran back out once the wind laid down about **11:00** (`HzE4FHHcvTk`). The
zone fished in conditions that shut down the longer run past it. Wind reading is
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three marks are one ridge, and the charted positions say so.** Running
down the line the bearings are **124°** from The Slide to the 152 and **119°**
from the 152 to the 277 — the same axis within five degrees, over **9.7 nm** end
to end, with the 152 sitting **1.0 nm** off the midpoint and only **0.2 nm** off
the straight line between the two ends (cameron):

| mark | position | to the next mark down the ridge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Slide (offshore) | 33°17.000'N 118°14.667'W | 3.9 nm at 124° to the 152 |
| 152 | 33°14.800'N 118°10.833'W | 5.8 nm at 119° to the 277 |
| 277 | 33°12.000'N 118°04.800'W | — (lower end) |

**Extended northwest, that axis lands on Catalina's East End.** From The Slide
the reciprocal runs **304°**, and the East End shoreline marks bear **302–308°**
at 3.4–3.8 nm (cameron) — the charted geometry matches the on-camera
description of the 277 as sitting "at the bottom of the ridge that runs off the
East End of Catalina" (`3qSY328fFYo`). Extended the other way the same line
continues to the **209**, 12.2 nm off the 277 at **121°** (cameron), past the
12 nm grouping cap that made this zone three marks and not four.

**The corpus calls the middle mark "the 152 ridge" and gives it a back side.**
A trip's stated destination was "the back side of the 152 ridge"
(`HzE4FHHcvTk`) — so the feature is worked as a two-sided piece of relief rather
than a pin. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: which side is the back side, or
which way the ridge faces.

**The names are the only depth claims here.** A Bight bank is an isolated high
spot rising out of otherwise deep bottom and is named for the **fathom depth of
its shallowest point**, at six feet to a fathom (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): `Rf1HKJG-SDg` hedges the convention —
"**most of them** are... called after the highest part of the bank" — and names
distance-based exceptions on camera, including the 14 Mile Bank listed in this
note's neighbour table ("14 is 14 miles off the beach") and the Nine Mile Bank
("that's not nine miles deep that's nine miles off the coast"), which
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md) carries but this sentence drops; the source
names neither the 152 nor the 277.
Read that way the 152 tops out at **152
fathoms (about 912 ft)** and the 277 at **277 fathoms (about 1,662 ft)** — deep
banks, not structure fished on its surface. The Slide breaks the convention: it
carries a name, not a number, and therefore no depth claim at all. ⚠ Flagged gap
— no corpus source confirms either number as a charted sounding, and nothing
describes bottom composition, relief or contour anywhere in the zone. The
`depth_band` is unknown for that reason.

**Deep water starts close on this side of the Bight.** From Dana Point through
Newport and Marina del Rey out to Catalina the bottom is already **2,000–3,000
ft** (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`) — the featureless deep these three marks rise out of.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): `Rf1HKJG-SDg` scopes that depth to "a mile
mile and a half off the beach" and excepts Huntington explicitly ("except for up
there in huntington... because we have the flats there"); the mile-off-the-beach
onset is kept in [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md) and dropped here. That
is the region-level generalisation read onto the zone by position, not a
sounding taken on any of them.

**Bait sits in the off-colour water and the marlin sit outside it.** On the 152
there was a lot of bait, and it was holding in **dirtier water — not filthy, just
not the blue offshore water you would expect**; trolling from that edge into the
clear water where marlin live, the bait disappeared (`HzE4FHHcvTk`). That split
is the zone's most useful fact and it is why the troll is laid on the seam.
Reading it is [water colour](../conditions/water-color.md).

**A [kelp paddy](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md) sat in the productive water on
the 2014 stop.** Moved onto the temp break, the boat started metering bait,
trolled past a kelp paddy with the graph lit up with bait underneath it, and was
bit immediately after (`3qSY328fFYo`) — the only paddy the corpus puts over this
zone. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: how often paddies hold here, what the
zone does outside August through November, or how it fishes in any other water
regime ([water regimes](../conditions/water-regimes.md)).

## How it fishes

**The program the corpus supports, in order.**

1. **Start at the lanes, work down the ridge.** Jigs in at the bottom of the
   tanker lanes about 17 miles out, then troll southeast toward the 277
   (`3qSY328fFYo`). The 152 is the middle stop and the one both trips name.
2. **Hunt the temperature before the jigs go in.** The target for the marlin
   stop was **68 °F**, looked for specifically rather than assumed
   (`3qSY328fFYo`). Mid-morning the boat crossed a break of about
   **two-tenths of a degree**; a nearby boat reported the bite on the **colder**
   side, and that side was worked afterwards.
3. **Lay the spread on the bait/colour seam.** Put the marlin jigs on the edge
   where bait still shows, then troll into the clean water
   (`HzE4FHHcvTk`) — bait in the dirty side, marlin in the blue.
4. **Time it to the slack.** Marlin are slack-tide biters, and the highest-
   percentage spot wants to be under you **an hour before the tide change**
   (`6TBxHnkYXI0`). On the 2014 stop jig strikes were said to land about an hour
   either side of the change; the day's high was **7:30** and its low was given
   on camera first as roughly **11:30–12:00** and later restated as **11:15**,
   kept side by side rather than reconciled
   ([tide and slack](../conditions/tide-and-slack.md)).
5. **Keep the meter working under way.** The console sounder ran down to
   **120 ft** so bait could be picked up while trolling, cross-checked against
   surface sign (`3qSY328fFYo`,
   [electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).
6. **Convert what shows.** Execution lives in the technique notes: the full
   spread, the troll-downhill sun rule and the jig-watching discipline in
   [trolling — SoCal, Catalina tanker lanes/277 bank](../techniques/trolling.md#socal-catalina--tanker-lanes277-bank-marlin-troll),
   the dropback in [bait-and-switch](../techniques/bait-and-switch.md), and the
   foamer read — jacuzzi-tight versus spread-out — in
   [foamer casting](../techniques/foamer-casting.md).

**Sizing the day.** This zone is three marks on one axis inside 9.7 nm, so it is
a run-the-contour zone rather than a spot-check, but it is not the 50–60 square
mile hunt that the below-Clemente complex is
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states how
long to give any mark before moving, or which of the three to start on when the
lanes are not the plan.

## Spots

Three charted spots (cameron), listed down the ridge from northwest to
southeast. They are one run, not three destinations:

- **The Slide (offshore)** — the upper end, and the mark nearest Catalina at
  3.4 nm off the East End shoreline. It is the only name in the zone outside the
  numeric convention, so it carries no depth claim, and it is one half of the
  unresolved "The Slide" name collision ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).
  ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source names this mark; it is carried on its charted
  position alone.
  ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the zone lists three spots, but only
  [152](152.md) and [277](277.md) have child pages — the offshore Slide at
  33°17.000'N 118°14.667'W is in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)
  with no page of its own, and [The Slide (Catalina)](slide.md) is the shoreline
  mark this note explicitly separates it from.
- **The 152** — the middle mark and the busiest name in the zone. The corpus
  calls it **"the 152 ridge"**, gives it a back side, holds bait on it, and pulls
  both marlin jigs and a Mad Mac across it (`HzE4FHHcvTk`).
- **The 277** — the lower end, described on camera as sitting **at the bottom of
  the ridge that runs off Catalina's East End**, and the destination of the
  tanker-lanes marlin troll (`3qSY328fFYo`).

The **tanker / shipping lanes** cross the top of this water and are how both
trips navigate it, but they are a traffic feature rather than a fishing mark and
get no spot page. Charted positions for all three spots are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).
