# La Fonda / Bajamar / Salsipuedes

**A zone is a run grouping.** La Fonda / Bajamar / Salsipuedes is the stretch of
open Baja mainland coast between Descanso and Ensenada — four charted coastal
positions (cameron) from **Punta Mesquite** at **32°09.580'N 116°54.800'W** down
to **Punta Salsipuedes** at **31°58.385'N 116°50.010'W**, about **12 minutes of
latitude** of points and beach, with one numbered offshore bank, **the 97**,
sitting just outside the south end.

It is the southern of the two halves the spot library's **Northern Baja**
section splits into. That section ran one heading from the Bull Ring on the
border down to Punta Salsipuedes — too long to fish in one trip — so coordinate
clustering cut it at Sugarloaf Rock; the northern half is
[Rosarito / Descanso](rosarito-descanso.md).

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these five marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap for the whole coastal
stretch between the border and Punta Banda — eleven charted positions across
this zone and the one above it, and no doctrine attached to any of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
spot labels claim, and what is missing. Everything a plan would need beyond the
coordinates is flagged below.

## Structure & bathymetry

**Four of the five positions are coastal labels with no structure attached, and
the fifth is a bank.** The coastal four — Punta Mesquite, La Fonda, Bajamar and
Punta Salsipuedes (cameron) — carry no depth, no fathom figure and no bottom
description. Two are named points; two are place names off the shore they sit
on.

**The coastal four sit in two pairs, with an eight-minute gap between them:**

- **The north pair** — Punta Mesquite (32°09.580'N 116°54.800'W) and La Fonda
  (32°08.460'N 116°54.150'W), about **1.1 minutes of latitude** apart and the
  tightest pairing in the zone.
- **The south pair** — Bajamar (32°00.500'N 116°52.650'W) and Punta Salsipuedes
  (31°58.385'N 116°50.010'W), about **2.1 minutes of latitude** apart.
- **Between them, nothing.** Roughly **8 minutes of latitude** of coast between
  La Fonda and Bajamar carries no charted position at all. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
  corpus source: whether that stretch is featureless or merely unrecorded in
  this library.

**The 97 is the zone's only offshore mark, and its only depth claim.** It is
charted at **31°57.600'N 116°51.817'W** (cameron) — under a minute of latitude
south of Punta Salsipuedes and about **1.8 minutes of longitude** west of it —
and comes from the spot library's *Offshore banks* list rather than the coastal
section, attached here because this is the run grouping it falls inside.
Under the bank-naming convention a bank takes the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`), which would put the 97's high
spot at **97 fathoms — roughly 582 ft**. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
confirms the 97's charted depth, relief or bottom independently of its label, or
says anything about fishing it.

**Nothing else in the library sits offshore of this coast at these latitudes
except the Upper Finger Bank group.** The Upper Finger Bank (32°06.800'N
117°04.400'W) and the 475 Knuckle (32°04.000'N 117°07.000'W) lie roughly **10 to
13 minutes of longitude** west of the zone's north pair (cameron). ⚠ Flagged
stub — no corpus source yet: that bank zone has no page either, and neither does
the Finger Bank rockfish zone north of it.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bottom composition, relief, kelp extent,
contour or current behaviour anywhere in this zone. The `depth_band` is unknown
for that reason, not because the water is shallow. General structure typology is
[bass structure](bass-structure.md), which is not scoped to this coast.

**The region's one year-round coastal claim explicitly starts south of this
zone.** Baja coastal structure **from Ensenada southward** holds
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) more or less year-round, with the grade
improving the further south you go (cameron). This zone lies **north** of
Ensenada, so that frame does not reach it and is not asserted here.

## Spots

Five charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. They group as two coastal
pairs plus one bank:

- **The north pair — Punta Mesquite and La Fonda**, about a minute of latitude
  apart at the top of the zone, immediately south of the Rosarito / Descanso
  handoff at Sugarloaf Rock.
- **The south pair — Bajamar and Punta Salsipuedes**, about two minutes apart at
  the bottom of the zone, and the closest charted water in this zone to
  Ensenada.
- **The 97** — the zone's only offshore position and only numbered bank, sitting
  just outside Punta Salsipuedes, carried in the library's offshore-banks list.

Charted positions for all five are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
