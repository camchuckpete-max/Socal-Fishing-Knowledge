# The Pistol / Bell Bank (300)

**A zone is a run grouping.** The Pistol / Bell Bank (300) is the **western
frontier of the numbered Baja bank grid** — four charted positions (cameron) in
open ocean roughly due west of Ensenada, spanning **20.7 minutes of latitude**
(31°36.300'N to 31°57.000'N) and **32.2 minutes of longitude** (118°55.800'W to
119°28.000'W). All four sit **further west than any other position south of
32°N in the spot library** (cameron): the **Elephant** at 119°28.000'W is the
westernmost Baja-side mark on the list, and the next-westernmost position on
the Baja side — 300 (The Rampart) at 118°37.000'W — sits outside the zone
entirely, 37 nm to the south (cameron).

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these four marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid — no depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to a
single position on it. This is therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says
where the zone is, what the four names claim, how far outside the corpus's
documented range it sits, and what is missing.

For scale on the other end, the [Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) — the
closest island fishing to a major US port — run **~13 nm from the San Diego bay

## Structure & bathymetry

**This zone is four near-lone banks, not a tight cluster.** Measured between the
charted positions (cameron), only one pair falls inside the census's **12 nm**
zone-diameter cap:

| pair | separation |
| --- | --- |
| The Pistol ↔ Bell Bank (300) | **11.2 nm** |
| The Pistol ↔ The Worm | 12.7 nm |
| The Worm ↔ Bell Bank (300) | 20.9 nm |
| The Pistol ↔ Elephant | 21.6 nm |
| The Worm ↔ Elephant | 25.1 nm |
| Elephant ↔ Bell Bank (300) | **31.6 nm** |

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the census's 12 nm figure is a
spot-to-zone-CENTRE distance, not a zone diameter or a cap on pair separation —
`sources/geo-census.txt` reads "18 sit further than 12 nm from their zone
centre". Read that way two of the four marks are inside it (The Pistol 3.5 nm,
The Worm 11.5 nm) and two are the outliers the next paragraph names; the table
above measures a different quantity.

**The Pistol is the anchor** — it is the only mark within 13 nm of two others,
and it sits **3.5 nm** from the zone centre (cameron). The other three hang off it: The
Worm **11.5 nm** from centre, **Bell Bank (300) 14.6 nm** and the **Elephant
18.6 nm**, the last two both flagged as parent-distance outliers in the census.
Corner to corner the zone measures **31.6 nm**, so it is a grouping of banks
that share a stretch of far-western water rather than a circuit a boat works in
an afternoon. The zone list is set by the census and not by this page, so all
four are carried here as queued.

**Three of the four names are shapes, not depths — which is unusual on this
grid.** A bank is conventionally named for the **fathom depth of its shallowest
point**, six feet to a fathom ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), and most of the Baja grid is bare numbers. **The Pistol**, **The
Worm** and the **Elephant** carry no number at all. Only **Bell Bank (300)**
does, and read by the convention it would top out at **300 fathoms — about
1,800 ft**. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms that number as a charted
depth, and nothing says what the three shape names describe — bottom outline,
sounder trace or something else.

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): `Rf1HKJG-SDg` hedges the naming rule —
"**most** of them are the banks are called after the highest part of the bank
on the fish finder fedometer" (@00:06:04) — and gives distance-named exceptions
in the same passage ("the nine mile bank that's not nine miles deep that's nine
miles off the coast", "14 is 14 miles off the beach"). Every worked example in
the source is three digits or fewer (182, 209, 43 fathoms), while this page
reads a four-figure depth off the rule. The six-feet-to-a-fathom half verifies

**The zone sits on a westward axis the corpus does describe, but no source puts
a bank on it.** New waves of bluefin were observed arriving into the Ensenada
zone **"out of the west on the moon"** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[moon](../conditions/moon.md)), and this zone lies roughly due west of Ensenada
at the same latitude band. As of **2021-05** three areas were being worked — one
west of San Diego and two west of Ensenada — with nothing running further down
the coast because the closer grounds were producing (`fgTmUq78ofQ`). Neither
source gives a coordinate, distance or bank name, and both describe the fleet
working **closer** water, so none of it is attributed to this zone. The
[bluefin router](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) carries that material at region

## Spots

Four charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. They group as an anchor
plus three long legs off it:

- **The Worm** — the northern mark, 12.7 nm from The Pistol (cameron).
- **Elephant** — the far western outlier, 18.6 nm off the zone centre, 31.6 nm
  from Bell Bank, and the westernmost charted position south of 32°N in the
  whole spot library.
- **The Pistol** — the anchor and the zone's namesake, 3.5 nm off the zone
  centre and the only mark close to two others.
- **Bell Bank (300)** — the southern mark, 11.2 nm from The Pistol and the only
  spot in the zone carrying a number; one of the library's three distinct
  "300" names.

Charted positions for all four are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
