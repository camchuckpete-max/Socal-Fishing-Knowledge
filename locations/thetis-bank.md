# Thetis Bank

**[Baja only]** — a named offshore bank on the Magdalena-Bay-to-[Alijos
Rocks](alijos-rocks.md) corridor, worked as a stop by San Diego long-range
boats. The KB's only record of it is a stop that was **given up**: a crew went
into the bank after finishing at [the Ridge](the-ridge.md), *"didn't really
like what we had there,"* found the current and the conditions opposite, and
left in the middle of the night (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`). It is a separate ground from
both the Ridge and Alijos Rocks, and
[Baja Pacific — South](baja-pacific-south.md) derives no zone for it, so this
page hangs directly off the region rung.

**Nothing was caught here on camera, and that is the page.** Every fish on the
trip that names this bank came after the crew left it — on the Ridge and then
at the rocks (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`). What this page can teach is a conditions
decision, not a fishery.

**It carries no charted position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)**, whose sections stop at San
Quintín, so a day plan cannot pull per-spot conditions here; the region
([Baja Pacific — South](baja-pacific-south.md)) is the coarsest-but-only
handle.

## Getting there

**You reach it as a stop on a corridor, not as a destination.** The one
recorded visit was a leg of a San Diego long-range trip aboard the *Intrepid*
running the Magdalena-Bay-to-Alijos-Rocks corridor: the Ridge was finished the
day before, the boat *"went into the Thetus Bank,"* and after the overnight
departure it was fishing in front of Mag Bay (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`).
That is a sequence, not a distance — no mileage, bearing, run time or
sea-state envelope for reaching the bank is stated, and a night's steam is not
a position.

**The only timed leg on that trip belongs to another ground.** The **160 mi**,
**16-hour** run for an **08:00** arrival is the Ridge on to Alijos Rocks
(`3tQ1_xiqwVU`); it does not transfer here. Corridor access generally — the
San Diego long-range departure pattern and trip lengths — is on
[Alijos Rocks](alijos-rocks.md#getting-there) and
[the Ridge / Uncle Sam Bank](ridge-uncle-sam-bank.md#getting-there).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md),
[the region page](baja-pacific-south.md#access--range)).

## How it fishes

**The one lesson here is a leaving decision.** Opposite current and conditions
were enough to write the bank off overnight rather than wait it out or work it
differently — the boat left in the middle of the night and had a bite going on
another ground by morning (`3tQ1_xiqwVU`). On a long-range trip with multiple
banks in reach, a bad current read moves the boat rather than ending the day;
[the Ridge](the-ridge.md#how-it-fishes) carries the same event from the
receiving end, and [current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md) is
the read itself.

**Nothing else about fishing it is on record.** No technique, presentation,
jig, bait, line class, depth, drift or anchor is described at this bank, and
no source says what it fishes *like* when the current is right
(`3tQ1_xiqwVU`). What the corridor's neighbouring grounds fish on —
[yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) deep for yellowtail on the Ridge,
[wahoo trolling](../techniques/wahoo-trolling.md) and the bomb at the rocks —
is **not** attributed here and should not be assumed onto it.
