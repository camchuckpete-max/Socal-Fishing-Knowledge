# Mackerel Bank

An offshore bank northeast of San Clemente Island at **33°02.200'N
118°23.600'W** (cameron), charted in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)'s offshore-banks list and carried by
the [front side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md) zone as its one attached
offshore mark. Its documented role is positional: it is the **northern limit
of the below-Clemente complex** — the 43, 181, 182, 289 and Clemente Ridge up to
Mackerel Bank — which reads as **one ~50–60 square-mile search box** worked as
hours of looking rather than a spot-check (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). It is a boundary name in two other senses as
well: the **southern tuna zone** is everything from the middle of Clemente and
Mackerel Bank down to the Mexican border (`-JZpyWLdKlk`), and the bank is the
inside/outside line a small boat runs to on a windy weekend — fish the inner
waters **inside Mackerel Bank** when the wind line is laying at Clemente
(`5to3Q5P7w90`, [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)). Bluefin have been
reported on the bank (`HnqiE05vdXs`); ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
describes fishing it.

## Getting there

**Both documented runs to this bank left Newport, in late July and mid-August
2022.** On 2022-07-29 a charter cleared Newport harbour at about **08:00**,
struggled for bait, and ran outside to Mackerel Bank, where there were **plenty
of boats and no sign of actual fish**; from there it ran down to the
[289](289-284.md), found the water "got less fishy looking the further down we
went", and bent back north to the 152 (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). Two weeks later, on
2022-08-13, a Balboa Angling Club lady-anglers tournament boat launched at
Newport intending either the back side of the 152 ridge or a run out to Mackerel
Bank (`HzE4FHHcvTk`).

**This is the run that gets aborted when it blows.** That tournament morning's
**west wind** made getting that far out tough, so the crew turned back, fished
their way up to the [152 ridge](slide-152-277.md) instead, and ran back out
toward Mackerel Bank only once the wind laid down about **11:00**
(`HzE4FHHcvTk`, [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)). Treat the outside leg
as wind-gated and the inside ridges as the fallback that fishes in the
conditions that shut this run down. **Inside Mackerel Bank** is the standing
name for that fallback: with the wind line laying right at Clemente, the call
for a weekend private boat was to plan the inner waters inside the bank rather
than the outside (`5to3Q5P7w90`, [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

**It is also the leg that gets dropped when fish are already in front of you.**
On the 2022-08-27/28 weekend a boat working the back side of Catalina weighed
**18 nm out to Mackerel Bank** — where bluefin had been seen days earlier —
against a comparable run up to the west end, and bent straight off the island
instead. It raised a spot of bluefin on the **500-fathom curve** about three
miles off, stayed on the curve rather than running on to Clemente or Mackerel
Bank, and raised a second spot five minutes later (`HnqiE05vdXs`).

**Navy access is an island question, and this bank sits off the island.** San
Clemente Island is a U.S. Navy training range whose zones open and close on a
rotating schedule (`XyKF34C5iB4`), and the front side carries a charted naval
security zone at Northwest Harbor — the nearest charted position to this bank at
**9.8 nm** (cameron, [front side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The corpus gives the position and the role, never the ground.** The bank is
charted at **33°02.200'N 118°23.600'W** (cameron) and is named on camera only as
a boundary: the far end of an enumeration — "the 43, the 181, the 182, the 289,
Clemente Ridge, all the way up to Mackerel Bank" (`Blh2BA-7Ono`) — the north end
of the southern tuna zone that runs down to the Mexican border (`-JZpyWLdKlk`),
or the line the inner waters sit inside of (`5to3Q5P7w90`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source: charted depth, fathom curve, bottom composition, relief or
footprint. Nothing here supports a depth-band read, and the bank is not one of
the [BightSST evaluation targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md) (cameron), so a
conditions pull for it has to be requested by position.

**It is the box's outlying corner, not a mark inside a cluster.** The other
named members of the complex are a long way off: the [289](289-284.md) at
**16.2 nm**, the [181](51-181-138.md) at **27.0 nm**, the
[43](43-91-300.md) at **31.2 nm** and the [182](corner-140-182.md) at
**40.5 nm** (cameron). Read the bank as the box's northern boundary marker on a
June–July bluefin plan, not as a piece of structure you check and leave — the
complex is worked as **hours of looking** across one box, glassing and running
contour between features (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).

**Its nearest structure is the island, not the other banks.** The three closest
charted positions are all on the San Clemente front side — White Rock at
**9.0 nm**, Northwest Harbor at **9.8 nm** and Purse Seine Rock at **10.0 nm**
(cameron) — which is why the bank hangs off the
[front-side zone](san-clemente-island-front-side.md) rather than off the
numbered-bank zones it is enumerated with. Nothing in the corpus connects the
bank's water to the island's shoreline structure
([island structure](island-structure.md)).

## How it fishes

**Budget it as a hunt, not a stop.** The bank is the corner of a complex, so the
plan is hours of glassing and running contour across the box rather than a
20–30 minute read of one number (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md),
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).

**Boats are not fish here.** The one documented arrival found **plenty of boats
and no sign of actual fish** on the bank, and the read got worse rather than
better on the leg south to the 289 — "it got less fishy looking the further down
we went" (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). Use the bank as the top of a loop and let the sign
trend decide the next leg, rather than committing to the fleet's position.
Second-hand sign is worth no more: bluefin were called down by the bank in the
last week of August 2022 and the boat that heard it chose 2 nm of island water
over the 18 nm run, and found fish (`HnqiE05vdXs`).

**In October, pick this water off the chart before you run to it.** The stated
method for the fall band the bank sits in is to find the warmest water you can,
then cross it against the chlorophyll chart for the cleanest water
(`OYOda6T3f-8`, [water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water color](../conditions/water-color.md),
[report reading and forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)).
Because the bank is not a BightSST evaluation target, that pull has to be run on
its charted position (cameron).

**Let the morning wind decide whether you go at all.** A morning west wind cost
one trip its outside run entirely, and the run only reopened when the wind laid
down about **11:00** (`HzE4FHHcvTk`) — which on that day left the marlin
program to the inside ridges
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: how the bank itself is worked — troll pattern
