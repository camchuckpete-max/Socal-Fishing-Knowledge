# 51 / 181 / 138

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is a three-mark piece of the numbered
offshore-bank grid west of Point Loma — the **51**, the **181** and the **138**
(cameron) — spanning **5.25 minutes of latitude** (32°48.750'N to 32°54.000'N)
and **3.7 minutes of longitude** (117°49.333'W to 117°53.000'W). The whole zone
is **6.1 nm** across, half the census's 12 nm zone-diameter cap, and all three
marks clustered on their own coordinates: nothing here was attached on distance.

**The 51 and the 138 are 0.8 nm apart — the closest pair of numbered offshore
banks anywhere in the spot library** (cameron). Their names claim crests **87
fathoms** apart, so the two positions either bracket one steep piece of relief or
one of the numbers is not a crest reading. The **181** sits **5.4 nm** from the
51 and **6.1 nm** from the 138, on a bearing of **147–150°** back down to them.

**The 181 carries the zone's corpus and the other two are nearly silent.** It is
named as a bank in the source that defines the naming convention
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), it is a named member of the June–July below-Clemente search box
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`), and it is half of the fall "181/182 Ridge" on the warmest-water
yellowfin path (`OYOda6T3f-8`). The **138** has exactly one corpus appearance —
picked off an SST/chlorophyll plot on camera as a swordfish drift
(`yMiBtZ7k8-w`). Nothing in the corpus names the **51** at all.

## Getting there

**This is outer water, further off the beach than the numbered zone below it.**
The nearest charted coastal positions the spot library offers are **Widow Rock**
in [La Jolla](la-jolla.md) at **24.4 nm** from the zone centre and the **Point
Loma Pipe** at **30.1 nm** (cameron). Read against
[The Corner / 140 / 182](corner-140-182.md) immediately south, whose same two
references are 20.6 nm and 24.1 nm, this zone sits roughly **4–6 nm further
out**. For scale, the [Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) run **~13 nm from
the San Diego bay entrance** (cameron), so the reach here is a little over twice
that.

**Distance offshore is not the same as depth in this Bight.** A mile to a
mile-and-a-half off the beach from Dana Point through Newport out toward Catalina
is already **2,000–3,000 ft** of water (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`,
[Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md)); what earns a run out here is relief
off that plain, not the distance itself.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three positions.** The 181 sits at **32°54.000'N 117°53.000'W**, the 51 at
**32°49.500'N 117°49.500'W** and the 138 at **32°48.750'N 117°49.333'W**
(cameron). The 51-to-138 pair at **0.8 nm** and the 181-to-138 span at
**6.1 nm** are the extremes; both are inside the **12 nm** cap that is the
clustering rule which grouped them.

**A bank is named for the fathom depth of its shallowest point**, at six feet to
a fathom — an isolated high spot rising out of otherwise deep water, with places
all over the ocean at the same reading that are not banks
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way the **51** tops
out at **51 fathoms — about 306 ft**, the **138** at **138 fathoms — about
828 ft**, and the **181** at **181 fathoms — about 1,086 ft**.

**The 181 is confirmed as a bank name by the convention's own source**, listed
with "209, 181, 182, the 43" as the mountains-in-a-desert that hold bait and then
big fish, in a passage that insists structure is what the current, tide and
temperature act on (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms the
51 or the 138 as charted depths, or describes relief, high-spot shape, bottom
composition or preferred drift directions on any of the three.

**The 51 is the second-shallowest bare-numbered mark in the whole Bight grid.**
At **306 ft** it reads shallower than every other numbered bank in every
[Southern California Bight](socal-bight.md) offshore-bank zone except the 43 at
**43 fathoms** (cameron, `Rf1HKJG-SDg`), and it reads as rockfish-depth ground
rather than the tuna and billfish depths the rest of this zone's names claim.
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what is fished on the 51, at any depth.

**The 87-fathom step between the 51 and the 138 is the zone's open question.**
Two names 0.8 nm apart claiming crests **306 ft** and **828 ft** describe about
**522 ft of relief inside three-quarters of a mile**. Standalone structure rising
off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each individual bank still has its
own preferred directions learned over repeat trips
([island structure](island-structure.md)) — and a step that steep is where those
directions would matter most. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether the 51
and the 138 are one feature and its flank or two separate banks.

**The 181 is named as a discrete bank in one source and as half a ridge in
another, and the coordinates favour the first reading.** The naming-convention
list treats it as its own mountain (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), while the fall yellowfin path
names "the 181/182 Ridge" as one feature (`OYOda6T3f-8`) — but the 182 is charted
**15.5 nm southeast** of the 181, past the 12 nm cap, and sits in
[The Corner / 140 / 182](corner-140-182.md) (cameron). That page carries the same
tension from its side. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states whether "181/182"
names two separate banks or one ridge feature running between them.

**A third ridge name is unresolved against this zone.** The swordfish router
lists "the Ridge" among its named working spots beside the 9, the 43, the 178 and
the 302 ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md), `yMiBtZ7k8-w`), and the corpus
elsewhere names both the **Clemente Ridge** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`) and the **181/182
Ridge** (`OYOda6T3f-8`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says which of the two
"the Ridge" means, so no sword doctrine is attributed to the 181 on that basis.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron): the **140** at **8.1 nm** from
the zone centre — and only **5.9 nm** from the 138, tied with The Corner-to-the-91
for the tightest spacing between any two of the Bight's offshore-bank zones — then
the **312** at **10.8 nm**, the **182** at **11.7 nm**, the **284** at
**12.7 nm**, the **166** at **13.1 nm**, the
**43** and **The Corner** at **13.2 nm**, the **289** at **13.7 nm**, the **91**
at **14.8 nm** and the **209** at **15.1 nm**. Of those, only
[The Corner / 140 / 182](corner-140-182.md) is built as a zone page on this
ladder.
⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: the 43 / 91 / 300, 289 / 284 and
209 / 312 groupings that surround this zone are unbuilt, so a fallback plan out
of here has nothing to hand off to.

**The 51 and the 138 sit inside the below-Clemente envelope without being named
in it.** The complex is enumerated as "the 43, the 181, 182, 289, the entire
Clemente Ridge, all the way up to Mackerel Bank" (`Blh2BA-7Ono`); both marks fall
geometrically inside the triangle formed by the 43, the 182 and the 181 —
**5.4–6.1 nm** from the 181 and **9.5–12.6 nm** from the 43 and the 182
(cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source names the 51 or the 138 as part of that search box, so their inclusion is
a position fact, not a doctrine one.

**The corpus's search box is bigger than any one zone on this ladder.** Its named
members fall across four separate census clusters — the 181 here, the 182 at
**11.7 nm** from this zone's centre, the 43 at **13.2 nm**, the 289 at **13.7 nm**
and Mackerel Bank at **30.0 nm** (cameron). Treat this zone as the northeastern
corner of that box on a June–July bluefin plan, not as the whole of it.

**The swordfish pick here was made off a chart, not a logbook.** Working an
SST/chlorophyll plot on camera the presenter chose the 138 first, and the reading
stated for it is a **hard edge** lying across the mark — converging waters form
at any edge, temperature or chlorophyll, and long powerful breaks act as highways
for bait and fish (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`). That makes the 138 a **break-position spot**
rather than a standing destination: you check whether the break is on it before
you go ([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)).

## How it fishes

**Which side of the search split this zone sits on depends on the mark.** The
below-Clemente complex is worked as one search box — you do not "check the 181",
you plan **hours of looking**, glassing and running contour between features,
treating the named banks as reference points inside a single hunting zone. The
opposite type is the compact spot — the 302, the 371, the ground off the West End
of Catalina — a small definable piece of structure you idle up to, grade on the
meter, and either commit to or leave (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). Only the 181 is named
inside the complex. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source places the 51 or the 138 on
either side of that split.

**The June–July read pairs the search box with the moon.** Full moons drive the
night bite in that window, and the plan is hours of looking rather than a
spot-check ([bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md),
[June–July](../seasonal/june-july.md), [moon](../conditions/moon.md)).

**The October read is a temperature read.** To find yellowfin, find the warmest
water available and cross it against a chlorophyll chart for the cleanest — that
SST-max × chlorophyll-min combination is where the fish most likely are, and in
October that combination is what put the 181/182 Ridge on the path
(`OYOda6T3f-8`; [water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)).

**The swordfish numbers make the 138 a contour to set up on, not water to sit
over.** The daytime fishery drifts baits averaging **800–1,100 ft** over deep
contour breaks with the [deep scattering layer](../conditions/deep-scattering-layer.md)
present, setting the drift **up-current of the fathom line** so the bait sweeps
the structure, with a preferred **"down and in"** drift along the contour edge
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)). The 138's
name puts its crest at **828 ft**, the shallow end of that bait band, and the
181's at **1,086 ft**, the deep end. It is a fair-weather fishery — **>15 kt is a
no-go**, comfortable at **10–13 kt**, drift **≤ ~2–2.5 kt**
([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md), [sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: which side of the 138 to set up on, or how the
current runs here.

**Structure and current edge lining up is the stated trigger.** SoCal fish orient
to structure and then to current edges, and when the two line up is when the
banger days happen; on this coast there is enough water to cover that current
edges get fished as much as structure does (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`,
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). Because the 138 was
picked off break position rather than history, the break-aging and advection
rules in
[report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)
decide whether this zone is on the plan on a given day.

**No mark here is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions for this zone are
pulled per charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the three are run as one circuit, or what tide,
light and current do here.

## Spots

Three charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. All three clustered on
their own coordinates — no member was attached to this zone on distance:

- **The 181** — the northern mark and the only position here the corpus carries.
  It is named as a bank in the naming-convention list (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`), a member
  of the below-Clemente search box (`Blh2BA-7Ono`), and half of the fall
  "181/182 Ridge" (`OYOda6T3f-8`). Its 182 partner is 15.5 nm away in another
  zone.
- **The 51** — the middle mark, 0.8 nm from the 138 and the shallowest name in
  the zone at 51 fathoms. Coordinates only; the corpus never names it.
- **The 138** — the southern mark and the closest position in the zone to the
  next grouping south (5.9 nm to the 140). Its single corpus appearance is the
  on-camera swordfish chart pick for a hard edge lying on it (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`).

Charted positions for all three are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position and, for the 181 and the 138, the cites above, so each spot
page is coordinates plus its parent zone until more sources arrive.
