# Middle Grounds

The bank in the middle of the [Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) chain, at
**32°25.810'N 117°15.950'W** (cameron) — the mid-chain structure the islands
circuit crosses between passes at the island edges.

It is fished as a leg of the circuit rather than as a destination: the trip
story it belongs to is
[targeting yellowtail — Coronado Islands](../species/yellowtail-coronado-islands.md).

## Getting there

About **15 nm** from the San Diego bay entrance at Zuniga Jetty, measured
between the charted positions (cameron) — near the middle of a chain the corpus
gives as roughly 13 nm out as a whole.

**Mexican water.** Seasons, limits and licensing here are CONAPESCA/INM, not
CDFW; the paperwork is [Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the approach to this mark specifically —
which end to enter from, and where on the bank to start.

## Structure & bathymetry

The Middle Grounds is the **bank between the islands**, the mid-chain structure
that sits off the island faces rather than on them; the rest of the circuit is
kelp stringers and island edges, with the [Rockpile](rockpile.md) standing off
to the southeast. That difference is the reason the circuit crosses it — the
island patterns and the mid-chain structure get covered in one sweep.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron):
[East of Pukey Point](east-of-pukey-point.md) at **2.0 nm** northeast,
[Ribbon Kelp](ribbon-kelp.md) at **2.0 nm** east-southeast,
[Pukey Point](pukey-point.md) at **2.0 nm** west-northwest, the
[North of North Island rockfish area](north-of-north-island-rockfish-area.md)
at **2.3 nm** northwest, the [Lighthouse](lighthouse.md) at **2.8 nm**
south-southeast, [5-Minute Kelp](5-minute-kelp.md) at **3.5 nm** southeast,
[South Kelp Ridge](south-kelp-ridge.md) at **4.4 nm** south-southeast,
[Coronado Canyon](coronado-canyon.md) at **4.7 nm** north-northwest,
[Lower 9 Mile Bank](lower-9-mile-bank.md) at **7.7 nm** north-northwest and the
[Rockpile](rockpile.md) at **9.6 nm** south-southeast. The kelp marks and the
two points are the island circuit; the canyon and the Lower 9 Mile Bank fish as
rockfish ground.

## How it fishes

**It is trolled, not stopped on.** The Coronados day is a troll-search loop —
figure-8 patterns around the islands plus a pass across the Middle Grounds
between them (nate) — and the troll is prospecting that happens to hook fish
rather than the presentation itself (`HTowqnwAMeA`). Pink and purple
[Halco 190-class divers](../lures/halco-laser-pro.md) are what the zone pulls
as its fish-finder (nate); the method is
[trolling](../techniques/trolling.md), and the conversion — casting a
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md) at sign or a troll strike — is on
the zone guide.

**What you are crossing it for is sign.** The pass earns its place by covering
water between the island patterns, so the read is the general search read:
working birds ([bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md)), boils and
breezing fish, bait on the surface, and marks on the
[sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).

One on-the-water trace has a boat stopped and casting here rather than crossing
— birds worked down over the Middle Grounds late in an August afternoon, then a
fish boiling within range of a blind-cast iron (`4pT0efBD1j0`); one trip, not a
program.
