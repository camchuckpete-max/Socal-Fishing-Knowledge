# Banda Bank / Todos Santos Island

**A zone is a run grouping.** Banda Bank / Todos Santos Island is a two-mark
grouping in the water off Ensenada: **Todos Santos Island** at **31°48.000'N
116°47.400'W** and **Banda Bank** at **31°39.000'N 116°52.000'W** (cameron),
**9.8 nm** apart with the island bearing **023°** from the bank. Both come out
of the spot library's latitude-sorted `## Offshore banks (N→S)` catch-all, so
this is a coordinate cluster rather than a boundary the fishery drew.

**Neither mark is offshore, despite the section they come out of.** Measured
against the nearest charted coastal position, the island lies **3.1 nm** off the
Punta Banda waypoint and the bank **4.3 nm** off the *48 Fa. Spot*; of the whole
offshore-banks section only the **97**, at **1.7 nm** off Punta Salsipuedes, sits
closer to the Baja shore (cameron). This zone is inshore structure that the
library filed offshore, and a planner reading the section heading will
mis-place it.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing either mark.** No transcript, note or
evidence file names Banda Bank at all, and the only appearance of *Todos Santos*
anywhere in the corpus is a lunch anchorage (`0dIwWiOc1NY`), carried under
[Getting there](#getting-there) with its ambiguity flagged. This is therefore a
**position-and-ladder page**: it says where the two marks are, what their names
do and do not claim, and what is missing.

**The grouping itself is the thing to read before using this page.** Neither
member's nearest charted neighbour is the other — six marks in
[Ensenada](ensenada.md) and
[Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md) sit closer to the island
than the bank does, and five sit closer to the bank than the island does
(cameron) — so on coordinates alone this reads as two marks that belong to their
neighbours rather than to each other. The census grouping is left exactly as
approved and is queued for Cameron, not changed here; the arithmetic is under
[Structure & bathymetry](#structure--bathymetry).

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run, heading, timing, fuel figure or sea-
state limit to either mark, and no source states that this zone is fished at all,
out of Ensenada or anywhere else.

**Ensenada is the port this water sits outside of, and the island is inside the
port's own inshore band.** Ensenada is a drive-to port where mackerel are made
just outside the marina before the day starts (`0dIwWiOc1NY`;
[Ensenada](ensenada.md), [making bait](../bait/making-bait.md)). Measured from
the two charted headlands of that zone, **Punta San Miguel** and **Punta
Morro**, the island lies **6.5** and **6.7 nm** off, and the bank **16.3** and
**16.0 nm** (cameron) — the island a short first hop, the bank far enough out to
be a committed stop. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the marina itself has no
charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md), so the port's
own distance figures — the **7–8 mi** high spot (`CKq0Z6ExVs4`) and the **20–30
mi** offshore run (`0bcDBGzQnGw`) — cannot be plotted against these two marks,
and nothing identifies either mark with the high spot.

**The corpus's only mention of the name is a meal, not a fishery.** An Ensenada
trip broke for lunch at anchor in "a beautiful cove here in Todos Santos"
(`0dIwWiOc1NY`) — the crew's own words, with no fishing done there and no
position given. ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): the phrase resolves equally to a
cove on this zone's island and to the wider bay that carries the same name, and
nothing on camera separates them; the mention is recorded, not claimed as this
zone's.

**The zone's coastal neighbours are closer than its own halves are to each
other.** La Bufadora, the northern end of
[Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md), is **5.7 nm** from the
island and **8.6 nm** from the bank; the *48* and *47 Fa. Spots* in that same
zone are **4.3** and **5.1 nm** from the bank; the **97**, in
[La Fonda / Bajamar / Salsipuedes](la-fonda-bajamar-salsipuedes.md), is
**10.3 nm** north-northwest of the island (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: whether either mark is worked as an extension of an Ensenada day, a
Punta Banda day, or as a stop on the way out to the banks.

**From the north it is well past the last documented island stop.** The
**Rockpile**, the outlying southeastern mark of the
[Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md), is **35.3 nm** from the island
(cameron), and the Coronados are themselves the closest island fishing to a
major US port at roughly **13 nm** off the San Diego bay entrance (cameron).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether this water is ever reached from the US
side.

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
closures, protected areas, anchoring restrictions or depth limits anywhere in
this zone — and an inshore island beside a working port is exactly the kind of
place that carries them.

## Structure & bathymetry

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bottom composition, relief, contour, charted
depth, kelp extent, surrounding sounding, preferred directions, drift or current
behaviour at either mark. The `depth_band` is unknown for that reason, not
because the water is shallow.

**The zone pairs two different kinds of structure, which is why it carries two
`waters` terms.** One member is an island — land, with a shoreline, coves and
whatever kelp and ridges go with them — and the other is a bank in open water
**9.8 nm** away (cameron). Those fish by different rules:
[island structure](island-structure.md) makes land-attached perpendicular ridges
**two-directional**, working only on current running parallel to the shore, while
standalone structure rising off the plain **fishes in any direction** but has
preferred directions learned mark by mark. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
describes a ridge, a point, a lee or a preferred current at either member, so
neither can be placed in that typology from the KB.

**Neither member's nearest charted neighbour is its zone partner, and that is a
grouping question rather than a rounding error.** From the island, six charted
marks are nearer than the bank's **9.8 nm** — the Punta Banda waypoint at
**3.1 nm** on **146°**, San Miguel Reef at **3.3 nm** on **349°**, La Bufadora at
**5.7 nm**, Punta San Miguel at **6.5 nm**, Punta Morro at **6.7 nm** and White
Rock at **9.1 nm**, four of them in Ensenada and two in Punta Banda / Santo
Tomás. From the bank, five are nearer — the *48 Fa. Spot* at **4.3 nm** on
**143°**, the *47 Fa. Spot* at **5.1 nm**, La Bufadora and the Punta Banda
waypoint at **8.6 nm**, and the *15 Fa Ridge* at **8.8 nm** — with Isolete level
with the pair separation at **9.8 nm** (cameron).
The pair spans **9.8 nm**, over four-fifths of the census's **12 nm**
zone-diameter cap, so this is a genuine two-stop run and not a cluster a boat
sits on top of. ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the coordinate census
groups these two together while each is closer to marks in
[Ensenada](ensenada.md) and [Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md)
than to the other — Cameron to confirm the grouping before spot pages parent to
it. The zone is written as queued; nothing here merges, splits or renames it.

**The island is the only mark in the library named as an island for a long way
in either direction.** Between the Coronado Islands to the north and San Martin
Island in [San Quintin](san-quintin.md) — **86.4 nm** southeast of it on
**156°** (cameron) — no other entry in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) carries an island name. ⚠ Flagged
gap — no corpus source: anything at all about this island as a fishing feature —
its size, its kelp, which end holds bait, whether it has a lee worth anchoring
in. The [region page](baja-pacific-north.md) flags the same silence one rung up
for Bahía de Todos Santos, the bay the island shares its name with, which is why
the region carries no `bay-harbor` water term (`0dIwWiOc1NY`).

**Banda Bank makes no depth claim, which is unusual for its immediate
neighbourhood.** Under the naming convention a bank takes the **fathom depth of
its shallowest point** ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)), and the marks **4–5
nm** southeast of it wear exactly that — the *47* and *48 Fa. Spots*, part of
the 15, 33, 38, 47 and 48 fathom ladder along the Punta Banda and Santo Tomás
coast (cameron; [Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md)). Banda Bank
carries a place name instead, so the convention yields nothing here. ⚠ Flagged
gap — no corpus source gives a sounding at the bank or says why it is named the
way it is.

**The bank's name and the coastal headland's name are one word apart and the
library does not treat them as a collision.** *Banda Bank* sits **8.6 nm**
southwest of the *Punta Banda* waypoint in [Ensenada](ensenada.md) — the
waypoint bears **041°** from the bank — and the same distance from La Bufadora at the head of
[Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md) (cameron). The library's
naming-collision rule prefixes the closest landmass to separate duplicated names
and lists this pair among neither the collisions nor the unresolved cases
([the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md), [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source states whether the bank is named for the
headland, and a planner given "Banda" alone cannot tell which of the three —
bank, point, or the coastal zone — is meant.

**Seaward, the bank grid starts about 11 nm out.** The nearest numbered marks are
the **475** at **11.5 nm** on **255°**, the **60** at **16.1 nm** on **160°**,
the **450** at **16.3 nm** and the **238** at **17.3 nm**, all measured from
Banda Bank (cameron) — [385 / 238 / 475](385-238-475.md),
[1140 Finger / 450](1140-finger-450.md) and
[Baja - 230 / Peanut Bank / 60](baja-230-peanut-bank-60.md). Banda Bank is
therefore the inshore edge of the Baja bank grid at this latitude, the last
structure before the fathom-spot ladder on the coast. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source frames it that way, or any way.

**The species claims stop at the zone line on every side.** Barracuda, bonito
and yellowtail are named as the standing inshore mix off Ensenada, with a
down-coast point-and-reef stop fished for [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md)
and a ~300 ft rock bottom producing vermilion, bocaccio and lingcod
(`0dIwWiOc1NY`; [Ensenada](ensenada.md),
[rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)). Baja coastal structure
from Ensenada southward holds yellowtail more or less year-round with the grade
improving as you go south (cameron; [Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md)).
Neither frame is extended onto these two marks, because no source extends it —
and presence is not catchability in this region even where fish are documented.

**What the corpus puts in the surrounding water is placed by distance from the
port, never by mark.** Yellowfin were reported showing **30–40 mi** out of
Ensenada, and the crew chose closer structure over the run (`0dIwWiOc1NY`);
skipjack and yellowfin were worked around a floating object about **35 mi** out
on temperature breaks west of the port, with a striped marlin seen jumping on a
dolphin-school foamer the same trip (`0bcDBGzQnGw`). Both
bands lie well outside this zone, and neither carries a bearing narrow enough to
put a fish on a position. [Yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) and
[bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) carry that material at region level.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the island and the bank are worked as one
circuit, or what current, tide, light or swell do here — and with the two
**9.8 nm** apart (cameron), whether they belong in one day is itself unanswered.

**The only program the corpus scopes to this water is the port's, and it runs
straight past both marks.** The stated Ensenada offshore plan is a run of
**20–30 mi due west** to a temperature break, then north or west off the break by
sea conditions, with a fallback search **20–30 mi north**, trolling cedar plugs
to locate fish and working deliberately away from the fleet (`0bcDBGzQnGw`;
[Ensenada](ensenada.md), [water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). Both of this zone's
marks sit inside the first quarter of that run. On the inshore side the same port
produces a slow-trolled mackerel and cast-jig program on points and reefs, worked
in the gray-light window and abandoned when the tide was wrong (`0dIwWiOc1NY`;
[slow-trolling bait](../techniques/slow-trolling-bait.md),
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md),
[tide and slack](../conditions/tide-and-slack.md)). Applied here, that makes the
island **water you would pass on the way out and could fish on the way in** —
which is a plausible read of the geometry, not something any source says.

**No method can be scoped to the bank until something gives it a depth.** The
yo-yo, dropper-loop and slow-pitch methods the neighbouring marks imply
([yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md),
[dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md),
[slow-pitch jigging](../techniques/slow-pitch-jigging.md)) each want a known
bottom, and this zone has none. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any technique,
lure, line class or drift scoped to either mark.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron) — the whole zone, and different enough from each
other that they are two separate propositions rather than one program:

- **Todos Santos Island**, the northern and eastern of the pair: land inside the
  bight formed by the Ensenada headlands, ringed by charted marks belonging to
  two other zones and closer to all of them than to its own zone partner. It is
  the only island-named position in the spot library between the Coronados and
  San Martin Island, and the corpus says nothing about how it fishes.
- **Banda Bank**, **9.8 nm** south-southwest of it on **203°** (cameron):
  open-water
  structure carrying a place name rather than a fathom number, sitting just
  inshore of the numbered bank grid and just outside the Punta Banda fathom-spot
  ladder.

Charted positions for both are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
