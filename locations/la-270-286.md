# LA - 270 / 286

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is two charted marks in the corridor between
the Los Angeles shore and Catalina's West End — **LA - 270** at
**33°41.000'N 118°34.500'W** and the **286** at **33°34.500'N 118°36.000'W**
(cameron). They lie **6.5 minutes of latitude** and **1.5 minutes of longitude**
apart, which works out to **6.6 nm** on a bearing of **191°**: LA - 270 to the
north, the 286 to the south, well inside the census's **12 nm** zone-diameter
cap.

**Neither mark is named anywhere in the corpus.** No transcript, knowledge note
or evidence file mentions LA - 270 or the 286; the zone exists on coordinates
from [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) (cameron). What the corpus does
carry is the **water**, described by port and direction rather than by mark: in
early August 2022 the way to guarantee a shot at bluefin was to launch out of
**Long Beach** and run up to the ground biting "up by Santa Barbara and north
west of Catalina," and the same report named "the tuna zone off Cat" among the
places a wind event was about to blow out (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). That is a description
of the neighbourhood, not of these two positions, and everything below that a
source would have to supply per mark is flagged. Flags aggregate in
[the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

**The geometry's one hard finding is how close the 286 is to Catalina.** Its
nearest charted position in the entire spot library is not its zone-mate: it is
**Johnson Rock** on [Catalina's front side](catalina-island-front-side.md) at
**5.9 nm**, and **seven** charted Catalina positions sit closer to the 286 than
LA - 270 does at **6.6 nm** (cameron). This is bank ground inside a short run of
an island, not isolated open-ocean structure — which is what separates this zone
from its neighbours out on the plain.

**Two isolated high spots in a transit corridor are exactly what stands seas

**locally** — the worked example is the ridge off Catalina's east end, rough for
a few miles and laying back down **3–4 mi** past it, with the instruction to mark
bathymetric features on the transit and expect locally worse seas over them
rather than reading one rough patch as a blown-out zone (`zKmZ4zql2ws`,
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
reports seas over LA - 270 or the 286.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The two positions, and the line they sit on.** LA - 270 is at
**33°41.000'N 118°34.500'W** and the 286 at **33°34.500'N 118°36.000'W**
(cameron) — **6.5 minutes of latitude** and **1.5 minutes of longitude** apart,
**6.6 nm** on **191°**, near enough a north–south pair. The 286 sits **1.17
minutes of longitude**, about **1.0 nm**, *east* of West End Humps 2, the
westernmost charted Catalina position in the library (cameron), so this is water
off the island's **north** side rather than west of it.

**The nearest marks to LA - 270 are in the next zone, and by a hair.** LA - 270
measures **6.6 nm** to its own zone-mate, **6.8 nm** to the **307** and
**7.1 nm** to **The Boot (504)** (cameron) — its three nearest charted positions
inside half a mile of each other in distance, yet the last two are parented to
[The Boot (504) / 307](boot-504-307.md). The arithmetic is what splits them: a
four-mark group would have spanned **13.4 nm** from the 307 to the 286 and
breached the **12 nm** cap, so the census cut two pairs out of four marks that
all sit within a short run of one another. Carried here as queued rather than
re-parented, because the zone list is set by the census and not by this page. The
practical consequence for a day plan: this is one piece of continuous bank ground
published across two pages, and a boat on LA - 270 is **6.8 nm** from a mark on
the neighbouring page.

**The 286's nearest charted position is an island, not a bank.** Johnson Rock on
Catalina's front side lies **5.9 nm** off it, and **seven** Catalina positions —
Johnson Rock, Eagle Rock, Stony Point, Parson's Landing, Catalina - West Cove,
West End Humps 1 and Arrow Point — are all closer to the 286 than its own
zone-mate is (cameron). LA - 270, at **12.4 nm** from the nearest of them, is the
mark that sits out in open water.

**The names are the only depth claims in the zone.** A bank is an isolated high
spot rising out of otherwise deep water and takes the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way:

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LA - 270 | 270 fathoms | about 1,620 ft |
| 286 | 286 fathoms | about 1,716 ft |

**Both readings behave the way the naming convention says they should.** The
source that states the convention describes the water from **Dana Point through
Newport and Marina del Rey out to Catalina** as already **2,000–3,000 ft** deep
and mostly featureless, the plain a bank is an isolated high spot rising *out of*
(`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read as fathoms, both names land **shallower** than that plain —
**1,620 ft** and **1,716 ft** — which is what a high spot in this corridor would
read. Both figures also fall inside the **150–350 fathom** contour band the SoCal
daytime swordfish fishery works ([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md),
[deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms either number as a charted reading, or
gives relief, bottom composition or surrounding depth at either mark.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the naming convention is stated with a
hedge and with distance-named counterexamples that this page drops, and the depth
table plus the front-matter `depth_band` rest entirely on applying it as a rule.
`Rf1HKJG-SDg` says there are banks like that up and down the coast but **most** of
them are named after the highest part of the bank on the sounder, and it names
its own exceptions in the same breath: the Nine Mile Bank is not nine miles deep,
it is nine miles off the coast, and the 14 is fourteen miles off the beach. It
also warns the reading does not run backwards — there are places all over the
ocean at a given fathom number that are not banks. The isolated-high-spot framing
and the six-feet-to-a-fathom figure both verify. The same hedge is already
flagged on the 1010 Trench / 378 / 213 page.

**The "LA" prefix is the library's, and the library cannot check it.** Cameron's
landmass-prefix collision rule prefixes a colliding name with the **closest
landmass** ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)), and the collision here is real:
**Baja - 270** sits **138.5 nm** south of this mark (cameron). LA - 270 is the
only "LA -" name in the library. Measured against the positions the library
actually carries, the closest landmass to it is **Catalina at 12.4 nm**, not the
mainland at **36.5 nm** — but the library holds no Los Angeles shoreline
coordinates at all, so that comparison cannot settle the prefix. Cameron's own
unresolved "The Slide" ×2 case shows the rule reaching its limit the same way
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)); this is logged as a queued question, not an
error.

**Neither mark is attached to land, which sets how each would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: high-spot shape, preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at
either mark.

## How it fishes

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states

**The geometry reads as two compact spot-checks, and the source's compact list
stops just short of naming this water.** The Bight's two zone types are the
complex — a **~50–60 square-mile** box you plan hours of looking across — and the
compact spot you idle onto, grade on the meter, and either commit to or leave in
**20–30 minutes**; the named compact examples are **302, 371 and
"west-of-Catalina"** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). These two
marks are north of the West End rather than west of it — the 286 is **1.0 nm**
east of Catalina's westernmost charted position (cameron) — so that list does not
reach them. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source places either mark on either side of
the split. Two isolated marks **6.6 nm** apart is a short enough hop to read both
inside a morning, which is the compact pattern rather than the search-box one.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the **20–30 minutes** is not in
`Blh2BA-7Ono`. That transcript carries no minute figure and no idle-up /
grade-the-meter / commit-or-leave timer language anywhere; its only search-time
instruction is that you should expect to spend quite a bit of time driving around
and not expect to drive to a spot and find the fish. The 50–60 square-mile half
of the split does verify, attached to the below-Clemente complex. The same figure
sits uncited on [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), where the `Blh2BA-7Ono` cite
attaches only to the complex.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the third compact example in
`Blh2BA-7Ono` is the stuff off the west **end** of Cat, not "west of Catalina" —
a looser phrase meaning the water off Catalina's West End generally. This page
then argues these two marks fall outside the compact list because they sit north
of the West End rather than west of it, a distinction the source's actual wording
does not draw.

**A NW blow is the weather question, and this zone sits on the seam of the
rollover map.** A big NW event rolls the **northern Channel Islands** and the
**LA / Ventura coastal shelf** hardest — **5–8 °F** off the surface temperature —
while the Coronados, Catalina and San Clemente held their water through the same
event (`YZtX1MiT0y8`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). These marks
lie **5.9 nm** off Catalina's West End but **36.5 nm** or more off the charted
coast (cameron) — between the half that rolls and the half that holds. Against
that, the August 2022 forecast named "the tuna zone off Cat" among the water
expected to blow out alongside the Channel Islands, San Clemente, San Nicolas and
Santa Barbara Island (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source classifies
LA - 270 or the 286 on the rollover map; treat it as an open question and pull the
water per position.
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the wind direction and the temperature
range both go past `YZtX1MiT0y8`. No direction is stated in it — "northwest" and
"NW" do not appear at all; it describes a windy week in which the Channel Islands
