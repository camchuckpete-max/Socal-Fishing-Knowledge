# Lighthouse Point (the lighthouse)

**[Baja only]** — the **beach fishery** of the [East Cape](east-cape.md), and
the one ground in that zone the corpus names properly. A local captain calls it
*"one of the main fishing spots that we have here"* and puts *"a drop very close
to Shore"* against the sand, with the summary that *"everything's right here on
this Edge"* (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`). The lighthouse the point is named for is dated,
loosely, to *"probably back in the '80s"* (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`).

**It is fished from the sand, not from a boat.** Sharks, roosterfish,
[jacks](../species/pacific-crevalle-jack.md),
[dorado](../species/dorado.md) and — by the captain's claim —
[yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) are all described as catchable by
casting off this beach (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`). The beach is called *"world famous"* and
*"the worldclass Lighthouse"* for roosterfish (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`, `YijeuGOYoVQ`),
and it is the ground the resort's ATV sight-fishing program works
(`YijeuGOYoVQ`).

**The whole record is one operation's December-2016 week plus one April-2020
plan.** Three of the four sources are consecutive episodes of the same
StokedOnFishing trip to the Hotel Buena Vista Beach Resort (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`,
`YijeuGOYoVQ`, `mG8ZZLFGlT8`), and the fourth is the same event four years later
naming the point as the day's plan (`APsnsunT4gM`). Read the page as one
resort's beach program, not the point's full fishery.

**It carries no charted position in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md).**
That library's sections stop at San Quintín on the Pacific side and hold nothing
on this coast, so a conditions pull runs against
[the zone](east-cape.md) rather than against this point, and no coordinates are
published here.

⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): **whether the offshore run's "the point" is this
headland is never stated.** The boats run out *"to the point down here"* for the
offshore troll (`mG8ZZLFGlT8`), and the KB keeps that ground separate from this
one rather than merging them
([the zone page](east-cape.md#spots)). Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

## Getting there

**You ride to it, along the beach.** The resort's beach program runs off
**rented ATVs** cruising the sand, and the point is the ground that program
works (`YijeuGOYoVQ`). Boats are not part of it: the offshore and panga
fisheries out of the same trip leave from a slip and go elsewhere
([the zone page](east-cape.md#getting-there)). Getting to the zone at all — the
flight into Cabo San Lucas, the resort van, the six-day/three-fishing-day trip
format — is on [East Cape](east-cape.md#getting-there).

**The clock rule here is about light, not the fish.** Start
**after 9:00**, because *"the idea of coming out after 9 is because you need
light to see the fish"* (`YijeuGOYoVQ`). That makes this a mid-day ground in a
zone whose boat programs leave at dawn.

**Some anglers come for this alone.** A share of the resort's guests fish
*"strictly for surf fishing"* and spend **three or four days on the beach**
rather than taking a boat (`YijeuGOYoVQ`).

**Regulatory — Mexico. Jurisdiction: CONAPESCA for fishing, INM for entry.
Checked as of 2026-08. CDFW rules do not apply on this side of the line.
Verify current requirements before you fish**
([Mexican waters](mexican-waters.md), [the zone page](east-cape.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The point's structure is one edge, and the corpus never sounds it.** The whole
description is *"a drop very close to Shore"* with *"everything's right here on
this Edge"* (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`) — no depth, no distance out to the drop, no bottom
composition and no extent. That edge is the reason the assemblage below is
reachable from dry sand: the same water that would be a boat ground elsewhere in
the zone comes to the beach here.

**Fish work inside the surf line.** Roosterfish were seen cruising **five feet
off the shore**, with three or four fish sighted in one session (`YijeuGOYoVQ`).
The casts that fish this ground are correspondingly short — a bait soak here was
a **~30 ft** cast held in place (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`).

**The depths on record in this zone belong to other grounds.** The 30–60 ft the
flylined-squid tuna held in (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`), and the 52 ft under the inshore
tuna ground under 2 mi off (`_c6UI3lGBVg`), are boat positions and are not
stated at this point
([the zone page](east-cape.md#structure--bathymetry)). Nothing here is read off
a meter — the general method is
[electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md), and this is a
sight fishery instead ([search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any sounding, contour, bottom type, rock,
sand-channel or surf-structure detail at this point; how far out the drop sits;
or what the beach itself is made of.

## How it fishes

**Do not cast until you see a fish.** The program is stated flatly —
*"it's sight fishing, so cruising, we're not going to fish until we see
something"* — and what you look for is **the shadow**, not the fish
(`YijeuGOYoVQ`). The ATVs are the search vehicle and the after-9:00 start is
what makes the water readable
([search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)).

**The surf kit is a 12 ft rod and a light throw.** The stated setup is a
**12 ft** rod throwing **1½–2 oz**, with a **3 oz** skipped lure as the heavy
end, and the roosterfish retrieve is deliberately loud —
*"the more erratic and splash they can make, it's the best for rooster"*
(`YijeuGOYoVQ`).

**The bait version is the opposite of that, and it is short.** A **~30 ft** cast
is held stationary and thumbed on the spool, with the beach retrieve described
as *"slow rolling it back in, creeping it back in"* (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`).

**Circle hooks carry this beach, and the coaching is a count.** Let the fish
take it and run — *"give him a 10 count, at least 10 seconds"* — then
**wind, just wind wind wind** rather than swinging, on the reasoning that
*"it's pretty tough to snag on a circle hook"* (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`;
[hooks](../tackle/hooks.md)). On the trip's later beach session the instruction
was again to *"just take your time"* rather than rush the hookset
(`mG8ZZLFGlT8`).

**Once you find them the window is short.** With fish located, the hookup came
*"within seconds"* to at most *"a couple minutes"* (`mG8ZZLFGlT8`) — this is a
ground where the search is the work and the bite is not.

**The line is the fragile part, but the record for that is off a boat.** Fish
*"biting the Spectra"* is noted on the same trip's inshore flylined-squid
session, not on the sand (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`); it is the argument for the
fluorocarbon top shot the zone's inshore rigs run
([the zone page](east-cape.md#how-it-fishes),
[bite leaders](../rigging/bite-leaders.md)), and no source states a line class
or leader for the beach here.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: tide, swell, moon, current or wind at this
