# San Clemente Island — Front Side

The mainland-facing side of San Clemente Island, running N→S from Northwest
Harbor at the northwest end to the Pyramid Cove / Pyramid Head end, with seven
charted spots — six on the island shore plus the attached offshore bank at
Mackerel Bank (cameron). Its documented program is a **morning fishery**: the
front-side yellowtail bite is "a morning thing" that slows into a pick day of
bass, barracuda and bonito, so the arrival time decides which species the trip
actually gets (`Blh2BA-7Ono`). The island is an overnight-range destination
rather than a full-day run (`LTCFjqtSI8g`) and a U.S. Navy training range whose
zones open and close on a rotating training schedule (`XyKF34C5iB4`), and the
northern-most spot of the run is charted as a naval security zone (cameron). At
its offshore edge Mackerel Bank is the northern limit of the
**below-Clemente search box**, which is hunted for hours rather than checked
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the morning-fishery framing hardens a
hedged, secondhand line. `Blh2BA-7Ono` says "yellows on the front side **seems
like** it's a morning thing **according to jeff's on the thunderbird** said
it's good in the morning and kind of slows down becomes a piccolo day bass
barracuda stuff like that in the mix of bonita" (@00:02:35) — one captain's
read relayed inside one dated weekly report, not the zone's standing program.
"Pick day" is a reading of the ASR garble "piccolo day".

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): by the spot library's own coordinates the
northern-most of the seven charted spots is Mackerel Bank at 33°02.200'N, not
Northwest Harbor at 33°02.000'N in [the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md)
(cameron). "The northern-most spot of the run" holds only if the run is scoped
to the six shoreline spots and the attached bank is set aside.

## Getting there

**Plan it as an overnight, not a day trip.** San Clemente Island sits at
overnight range with Santa Barbara Island, unlike the full-day-accessible
Coronado Islands, Channel Islands and Catalina — which is why the jig stick
carries a 50 lb top shot here against the 40 lb used closer in (`LTCFjqtSI8g`,
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)). The crossing is described as
roughly 60 miles straight off the beach (`XyKF34C5iB4`).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): `LTCFjqtSI8g` sets the 50/40 split on
**one** of its two jig sticks and names a third member of the 50 lb group — "if
I'm fishing on overnight Island like San Clemente Island or Santa Barbara
island **or going down anywhere in Baja** I'm going to be running 50 pound…
but anywhere else Coronado Islands Channel Islands Catalina Island any of the
local full day trips areas I'm gonna be running 40 pounds" (@00:08:26). That
split belongs to his 10 ft schoolie surface-iron stick; his heavy 9 ft jig
stick runs 50 lb monofilament everywhere with no location conditional. Stated
of "the jig stick", the rule is broader than its source. The 60-mile crossing
verifies verbatim — "60 mi one way and that's straight off the beach"
(`XyKF34C5iB4` @00:09:43).

Navy training range, not open coastline. Zones open and close for military
training on a rotating schedule rather than a fixed one, so checking which
zones are open is a standard pre-trip step alongside safety prep for the
roughly 60-mile crossing (`XyKF34C5iB4`). **Jurisdiction: U.S. Navy, as of the
2025-04-19 seminar — verify current access before running the crossing.**

## Structure & bathymetry

The shore runs from the harbor at the northwest end through two named rocks
mid-side to the Pyramid group at the southeast end: Northwest Harbor, White
Rock, Purse Seine Rock, then Pyramid Cove, Pyramid Reef and Pyramid Head
(cameron).

**The documented shallow structure is boiler rock, and it is very shallow.**
Inside Northwest Harbor a boat anchors tight to island rock in about **6 ft of
water** and fishes sardines over it, taking calico to **8 lb**
(`KCcEqHSZ84k`). On a second session at another shallow rock inside the same
harbor the water was unusually calm and clear for the island at **30–40 ft
visibility**, with fish taken very shallow and tight to the rock and constant
re-tying needed because break-offs on the rock were the recurring problem
(`KCcEqHSZ84k`). The island group in general is framed as a hazard stack of
thick kelp, razor-sharp boiler rocks and big waves (`iQLyBzhOSi8`).

all verify. The same "anchors/anchored" wording repeats in the front matter,

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the second-session detail is not in the

up to Mackerel Bank — which reads as **one ~50–60 square-mile search box**

## How it fishes

**Get there for the morning.** The front-side program is time-gated before it
is anything else: yellowtail is the early bite, and the day then becomes a pick
of bass, barracuda and bonito in the mix (`Blh2BA-7Ono`). An island-level
evening call from the same boat fleet puts a second window at roughly
**5:00–5:45 pm**, given as one boat's read of one day rather than a rule
(`V_ONnegk95M`, [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md)).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the 5:00–5:45 window is a **morning**
window, not an evening one. `V_ONnegk95M` is a dockside evening briefing about
the next day — "good evening everybody" (@00:03:25), "when you guys are
fishing tomorrow" (@00:05:04) — and the times are given as when to start
soaking baits then: "nothing happening until about five / five thirty / five
forty five something like that / that's when the stuff starts to happen /
that's when you wanna start soaking baits" (@00:04:14). The next morning on the
water confirms it: "all right little gray light bite / that's what captain jeff
was saying" (@00:09:33). The transcript also names no side of the island, and
"the same boat fleet" holds only for the El Dorado charters — `Klfb433I3Uk`
and `yuXr3IJ8ybg` are different boats.

**Bait choice follows what the island has.** Live squid is what the Pyramid
Head bite ran on (`yuXr3IJ8ybg`); when the boat is on sardines, the front-side
build is **30 lb** line to roughly a **2/0–3/0 hook**, with the day's baits
called the right size for that hook (`nsUdT-zXI8s`,
[flyline](../techniques/flyline.md),
[fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md)).

**Weight the flyline when the grade wants it deeper.** Off Pyramid Head a
better grade of fish wanted the sardine below the surface, and the crew added a
**½ oz to ¾ oz** rubber-core or sliding sinker to the flyline to get it there —
a conditions-driven exception to the no-weight rule, not a replacement for it
(`Ow3an9lSVh4`, [flyline](../techniques/flyline.md),
[sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md)).

**Fight shallow-rock fish with the tip high and never pump.** Anchored in about
6 ft tight to rock, hold the rod tip as high as possible and wind as fast as
possible without ever pumping — a dropped tip gives the fish the leverage to
bury into the rock and root the leader off. Line for that scenario is **65 lb
braid straight to a 30 lb fluorocarbon top shot** (`KCcEqHSZ84k`,
[chunking](../techniques/chunking.md)).

**A working bass cadence off this side is bottom-up, then free-spool back.**
Once a producing spot was located at Northwest Harbor the coaching was to drop
straight to the bottom, wind **10 or 15 turns** up, free-spool back down and
repeat (`iQLyBzhOSi8`, [swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md)).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): the cadence is not located at Northwest
Harbor and no lure is named. By "we finally found the spot… you're hitting the
bottom of that, yeah, then 10 or 15 turns up and back in free spool"
(@00:14:41) the boat has executed its stated plan to leave the harbor and work

[swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md) routing is inference. The turn count
verifies verbatim.

**Do not pre-judge the day's spots off one current read.** Because two spots a
short run apart on this island can run opposite directions on the same day, the
region-wide current-coherence shortcut does not hold here — check the current at
the spot (`sIoNELGlxmk`, [island structure](island-structure.md)).

**Budget the offshore end as a hunt.** The Mackerel Bank end of this zone is
part of a complex, not a compact spot: plan hours of glassing and running
contour across the box rather than a 20–30 minute read (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md),
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): "20–30 minute read", "glassing" and
"running contour" are all absent from `Blh2BA-7Ono`. The search method it
actually gives is "you just gotta look around run bank to bank you know look
for color changes temperature changes bird life anything that looks different

depredation handling is in
[fighting a big bluefin](../techniques/fighting-big-bluefin.md).

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source): both cites drift in wording.

  (cameron), and the only front-side spot the corpus fishes by name: the
  6 ft shallow-rock calico program (`KCcEqHSZ84k`) and a January tournament
  landing in dirty, washed-out water (`iQLyBzhOSi8`).
- **Mid front side** — White Rock (San Clemente Is.), Purse Seine Rock.
  ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source names either spot.
- **Pyramid end** — Pyramid Cove, Pyramid Reef, Pyramid Head. This is the
  corpus's producing end of the side: the live-squid mixed bite (`yuXr3IJ8ybg`)
  and the off-the-east-end yellowfin grade (`Ow3an9lSVh4`) both sit here.
  Pyramid Cove is one of the BightSST evaluation targets
  ([BightSST eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md), cameron).
- **Attached offshore bank** — Mackerel Bank, charted in the library's
  offshore-banks list and assigned to this zone as its nearest run grouping
  (cameron). It is named only as part of the below-Clemente search box and the
  fall warmest-water path (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, `OYOda6T3f-8`); ⚠ Flagged gap — no
  corpus source describes fishing the bank itself.

**Name collision.** This White Rock is a distinct spot from the White Rock
charted in the [Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md) zone
([White Rock](white-rock.md), cameron).

Charted positions for all seven spots are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md); the per-spot pages carry them.

⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the per-spot pages do not cover all
seven. Only five child pages exist — Northwest Harbor, Purse Seine Rock,
Pyramid Cove, Pyramid Reef and Pyramid Head. `locations/mackerel-bank.md` was
never built (its gazetteer row is `escalated`), and [White
Rock](white-rock.md) is the Punta Banda spot at 31°41.080'N 116°40.400'W
parented to [Punta Banda / Santo Tomas](punta-banda-santo-tomas.md), so the San
Clemente White Rock at 32°53.512'N 118°26.360'W has no page of its own.
