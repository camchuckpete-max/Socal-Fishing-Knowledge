# North County Artificial Reefs

The built-structure zone of San Diego's north county coast: three numbered
artificial-reef series — Pendleton, Oceanside and Carlsbad — charted as
**38 waypoints** in the spot library (cameron). Artificial reefs are built,
fish-holding structure sitting close to most SoCal harbors, most of them
fished hard for years; the reason big fish are still on them is that most
anglers do not fish them properly (`f4qYtHACGyk`). One program runs the whole
zone — anchor up, build a chum line, and fish light line over the structure
([chunking](../techniques/chunking.md)) — and it is not the kelp-and-pipe
coastal fishing of the neighbouring Oceanside / North County strip.

## Getting there

These reefs sit in **shallow water close to the harbors** — the same closeness
that makes them a short run is why they have been fished hard for years
(`f4qYtHACGyk`).

## Structure & bathymetry

**Built structure, not natural bottom.** The SoCal artificial-reef system was
built specifically for sport fishermen in the **1950s–70s**, funded by
sportfishing money rather than by a commercial-fishing program
(`bM2vUS1B-yQ`). What that buys the zone is fish-holding relief in shallow
water within reach of the harbors (`f4qYtHACGyk`).

**The three series** (cameron):

- **Pendleton AR** — an AR Center waypoint plus numbered drops 1, 3, 4, 6
  and 7. Fished around **35–40 ft** of water (`bM2vUS1B-yQ`) — the only
  charted or stated working depth in the zone.
- **Oceanside AR** — two grids: the **1A–1H** cluster (8 waypoints) and the
  **2A–2L** cluster (12 waypoints, two of them sharing the label 2L), 20
  between them.
- **Carlsbad AR** — **1–12**, twelve waypoints.

The series run roughly N→S down the north county coast, from
**33°19.500'N 117°31.700'W** at the Pendleton AR Center to
**33°04.970'N 117°19.320'W** at Carlsbad AR 12 (cameron).

**One water cell, many drops.** All three series are **sub-pixel clusters**:
for modelling (SST/chlorophyll lookups) each collapses to about one MUR
pixel, while for navigation every waypoint stays a distinct drop
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), cameron). A day plan pulling BightSST
conditions treats Pendleton, Oceanside and Carlsbad as three cells, not 38.

**Library housekeeping** (cameron): the Pendleton series is charted 1–7 in
the [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md) but only 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 carry
coordinates in the library; the Oceanside 2-series skips 2D; and two distinct
positions — 33°12.910'N 117°25.307'W and 33°13.050'N 117°25.402'W — are both
labelled "Oceanside AR 2L".

**The geometry says which of those two facts explains the other, and it is not
the obvious one.** Stepping the 2-series in library order, consecutive letters
sit **0.09–0.16 nm** apart, broken twice by longer jumps between legs of the
grid (0.50 nm at 2F, 0.65 nm at 2J). The second "2L" continues from the first
at **0.161 nm** — squarely in the consecutive-letter band, on the same
northward line. It is a **thirteenth module carrying on past 2L**, not a second
fix on the same one. And it is not the missing 2D either: 2D belongs between 2C
and 2E, down in the 33°12.4–12.5'N band, while this position is the
northernmost of the whole series. ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal,
resolved by geometry): **both waypoints stand and neither is dropped** — two
real modules 0.16 nm apart. What is genuinely unknown is the second one's
label, most likely 2M under the series convention, and separately why 2D
carries no coordinates. Naming it is Cameron's call; publishing both is not.

## How it fishes

**Anchor, chum, fish light.** The zone's program is the anchored-chum program,
and the execution lives in [chunking](../techniques/chunking.md):

- **Build prey density, do not just feed.** Periodically add another handful
  of cut chunks to raise the prey-density level; past a certain density even
  fish that were not feeding come over and eat because they see the others
  doing it, which is what turns a slow pick into a frenzy (`f4qYtHACGyk`).
  Stated as a general SoCal principle, not a reef-only trick (`BdRX4b8Fo5w`).
  A private boat that cannot carry sportboat-scale bait gets the same effect
  from a chum tube (`JK2-cpaXkI8`).
- **Never chum off the stern.** At anchor the current runs down the *sides* of
  the boat, so hand chum goes as far up the side as you can throw it and sinks
  through the water column alongside you rather than drifting away astern
  (`yKaHcxX46l4`); a chum bucket is tied off at the bow by the anchor for the
  same reason (`xI9tPJFXbUM`, `bM2vUS1B-yQ`). Cut squid heads, tentacles and
  guts are the cheap chum source for this style (`yKaHcxX46l4`); the worked
  example at the Pendleton reef, in 35–40 ft, uses a bucket of ground-up
  sardines, mackerel or anchovies (`bM2vUS1B-yQ`).
- **Fish far lighter than instinct says.** The stated #1 mistake at artificial
  reefs is fishing way too heavy — oversized line, oversized hooks, swivels
  and beads. These reefs are shallow, close to harbors and fished for years,
  so the fish still on them are line-shy: a **No. 4–No. 6** hook
  (`bM2vUS1B-yQ`) tied straight to mono, a **1/16 oz** split shot 2–3 in above
  it by default, **1/8 oz** only if current demands it, **never heavier than
  1/4 oz**, and a spaghetti-thin strip of bait rather than a wad
  (`f4qYtHACGyk`). Going heavier puts the
  bait on the bottom, which draws shovelnose guitarfish and bat rays instead
  of the target species (`f4qYtHACGyk`). Gear class is light spinning or a
  small level-wind ([gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md)).
- The suspended-bait terminal detail for the zone's most reliable resident is
  in the [sheephead](../species/sheephead.md) router — a
  [sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md) kept off the bottom.

## Spots

Thirty-eight charted waypoints in three numbered series (cameron). A numbered
waypoint in an artificial-reef series carries no fishing identity of its own,
and the library already treats each series as one water cell, so the series —
not the individual waypoint — is the unit a day plan works with:

- **Pendleton AR** — the AR Center plus 1, 3, 4, 6, 7; the northern series,
  and the only one with a stated working depth (35–40 ft, `bM2vUS1B-yQ`).
- **Oceanside AR** — the 1A–1H cluster and the 2A–2L cluster, two tight grids
  about 2 nm apart (cameron).
- **Carlsbad AR** — 1–12, the southern series.

Charted positions for all thirty-eight waypoints are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md); no waypoint carries corpus
detail beyond its position.
