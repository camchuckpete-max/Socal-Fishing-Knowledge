# Upper 500 / Hidden Bank

**A zone is a run grouping.** Upper 500 / Hidden Bank is a north–south piece of
the numbered offshore-bank grid in open water west of Ensenada — three charted
positions (cameron) strung down **17 minutes of latitude** (32°03.000'N to
31°46.000'N) inside **2 minutes of longitude** (117°28.000'W to 117°30.000'W).
**Hidden Bank** and the **Upper 500** sit on the same charted meridian,
**117°30.000'W**, **8.0 nm** apart, and are the pair the zone is named for;
**Upper Hidden Bank** to the north was attached to them by the census.

**One corpus report reaches this zone, and it reaches it by name.** A productive
stretch of water in 2020 ran "from the 302 bank down to the hidden bank"
(`49joKHD7Umc`) — the southern anchor of that corridor is in this zone whichever
of the two Hidden Banks the report meant. Everything else here is position and
ladder: what the marks are, what their names claim, and what is missing. The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered grid — fifty-one charted positions over seventeen bank zones with no
depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run, heading, timing or fuel figure to
this zone from any port on either side of the border, and no source names a port
that these three marks are fished out of.

**Ensenada is the closer port to all three marks, and it is the closest by a
wide margin at the southern end.** [Punta San Miguel](ensenada.md) at
**31°54.110'N 116°44.980'W** sits **37.6 nm** from Upper Hidden Bank, **38.2 nm**
from Hidden Bank and **39.1 nm** from the Upper 500 — the zone runs almost
parallel to that port, so the whole thing is one distance band from it. The
**Point Loma Pipe** at **32°40.350'N 117°17.000'W** off San Diego is **38.5 nm**
from Upper Hidden Bank but **47.6 nm** from Hidden Bank and **55.5 nm** from the
Upper 500 (cameron): from the US side the zone is a **17 nm** spread of run
lengths, from the Mexican side it is barely **1.5 nm**.

**The one corpus statement about reaching this water calls it close to San
Diego.** The 2020 report frames the whole 302-to-hidden-bank corridor as "still
pretty close to home to San Diego" and describes private boats working it
(`49joKHD7Umc`). Read against the charted positions that holds for the northern
anchor — Upper Hidden Bank is the closest mark in the zone to the Point Loma
Pipe — and stretches at the southern end (cameron).

**The Ensenada distance ladder is the only port-scale reference the corpus
offers for this water.** The offshore grounds out of that port run **20–50 mi**
west, with a tournament bank at **42–43 mi** (`0bcDBGzQnGw`, `Ix0gG0-l3v0`,
[Ensenada](ensenada.md)). This zone's three marks lie **37.6–39.1 nm** from
Punta San Miguel — about **43–45 statute miles** — which falls inside that band
on a statute reading (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source says whether
those port figures are statute or nautical miles, and none of them names a bank,
so nothing attaches the Ensenada offshore program to these positions.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The zone is a line, not a cluster.** All three positions fall within
**2 minutes of longitude** of each other, and two of them share the meridian
exactly: Hidden Bank at **31°54.000'N 117°30.000'W** and the Upper 500 at
**31°46.000'N 117°30.000'W**, **8.0 nm** apart. Upper Hidden Bank at
**32°03.000'N 117°28.000'W** lies **9.2 nm** north of Hidden Bank and **17.1 nm**
north of the Upper 500 (cameron) — the widest span in the zone, and past the
census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, which is why it was attached rather than
clustered.

**One name in the zone claims a depth.** A bank takes the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`):

| mark | name read as depth | at six feet to a fathom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upper 500 | 500 fathoms | about 3,000 ft |
| Hidden Bank | no number in the name | — |
| Upper Hidden Bank | no number in the name | — |

**Read as a depth, the 500 sits below every working band in the KB.** The SoCal
swordfish fishery works the **150–350 fathom** contour
([swordfish](../species/swordfish.md)) and 500 fathoms reads well past the deep
end of it; SoCal daytime deep-drop baits average **800–1,100 ft**
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)) and the deep
rockfish game runs **300–900 ft**
([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)), both a fraction of about
3,000 ft. That figure is also the deep end of the **2,000–3,000 ft** the lexicon
gives for the open water inside the Bight ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)), so
read literally the name claims a high spot that tops out about level with the
surrounding plain. This is a reading of a name against doctrine stated for other
water, not a claim that anything here is fished that deep. ⚠ Flagged gap — no
corpus source confirms the 500 as a charted depth, or gives any depth at either
Hidden Bank.

**The upper/lower convention runs through this zone twice, and one of the two
pairs sits entirely inside it.** Upper Hidden Bank and Hidden Bank are **9.2 nm**
apart on nearly the same meridian — close enough that both fall in one run
grouping, which no other prefixed pair in the library does. The Upper 500's
counterpart is **42.5 nm** away: the **Lower 500** at
**31°10.500'N 117°02.500'W** belongs to the 1140 Finger / 450 zone. Upper Cross
and Lower Cross are **60.0 nm** apart on one meridian
([Upper Cross / 421 / 390](upper-cross-421-390.md)), and the **Upper Finger
Bank** carries the prefix with no charted lower counterpart at all (cameron).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source defines what *Hidden* denotes as a structure
term, or whether upper/lower means anything beyond relative latitude.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: bottom composition, relief, contour, high-spot shape, surrounding depth,
preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at any of these three marks.
The `depth_band` is unknown for that reason, not because the water is shallow.

**The nearest charted mark is not in this zone.** The **480** at
**31°42.000'N 117°18.000'W** lies **11.0 nm** from the Upper 500 — closer to it
than Upper Hidden Bank is — but belongs to [385 / 238 / 475](385-238-475.md),
**22.7 nm** away centre to centre (cameron). Of the zones proper, Baja - 270 /
Double 220 / 295 is **19.4 nm** southeast,
[1010 Trench / 378 / 213](1010-trench-378-213.md) **21.5 nm** west with its
**1010 Trench** the closest mark in it at **15.1 nm**, 101 / 425 **22.4 nm**
northeast and [Coronados - 230 / 302 (Kidney Bank) / 226](coronados-230-302-226.md)
**29.8 nm** north across the maritime boundary, with the **371** its closest
mark at **22.1 nm** (cameron). ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: neither
Baja - 270 / Double 220 / 295 nor 101 / 425 carries corpus material or a page to
hand off to.

## Spots

Three charted spots (cameron). They group as a meridian pair plus one
attachment:

- **The meridian pair — Hidden Bank and the Upper 500**, both on
  **117°30.000'W** and **8.0 nm** apart (cameron), the only part of this zone
  that clustered on its own and the pair the zone is named for. Fished in one
  trip, this is the leg.
- **Upper Hidden Bank** — the northern attachment, **9.2 nm** from Hidden Bank
  and **17.1 nm** from the Upper 500, **13.1 nm** from the pair's centre
  (cameron). It is the mark nearest San Diego in the zone, and one of the two
  candidates for the "hidden bank" in the 2020 corridor report — Hidden Bank
  itself is the other (`49joKHD7Umc`).

Charted positions for all three are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position and the one unresolved corridor mention, so each spot page is
coordinates plus its parent zone until sources arrive.
