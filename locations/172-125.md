# 172 / 125

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is three charted marks in the open water west
of Catalina (cameron). The **172** and the **125** are a tight east–west pair
**6.8 nm** apart just off the island's West End; the **499** sits **12.6 nm**
south of the 125, alone in the gap between Catalina's back side and San Clemente
Island. The census clustered the pair on their own coordinates and attached the
499 on distance, which is why the zone's name carries only two of its three
marks.

**The 499 is the mark that carries the corpus, and it carries one species.** It
is the named next step for July 2022 bluefin that had moved in between San
Clemente and Catalina (`Blh2BA-7Ono`) and a named station on the fall warm-band
arc, spoken of as "499 Zone" (`OYOda6T3f-8`). The **172** and the **125** appear
nowhere in the corpus but the coordinate library. So this is a real zone with one
documented mark and two that are position-only.

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any run distance, heading, timing or fuel
figure to this zone from any port, and no source names a port these three marks
are fished out of.

**Catalina is what this zone is close to.** The 125 sits **4.6 nm** from the
West End Humps and **5.2 nm** from Eagle Rock on the
[back side](catalina-island-backside.md); the 499 sits **12.0 nm** from
Farnsworth Bank and **13.0 nm** from the West End Humps; the 172, furthest from
the island, still sits **11.3 nm** off it and **9.3 nm** from the Cave Canyon
water at [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) (cameron). The 499 is
also **15.0 nm** off the back side of
[San Clemente Island](san-clemente-island-back-side.md) and **16.4 nm** off
Northwest Harbor on its front side. Every mark here is within a short leg of an
island that can be run to and sheltered behind, which is the shape the corpus
shows for this water: fish worked as a slide-off from an island program rather
than as a destination of their own.

**The mainland is a long way further.** The nearest charted mainland position in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) is Pelican Point in
[South Orange County](south-orange-county-crystal-cove.md) at **43.0 nm** from
the 125, with the Dana Point hundred-fathom curve at **46.2 nm** (cameron). The
library carries no charted positions on the Los Angeles or Long Beach coast, so
those are a floor on the run from the south, not a run distance from the ports a
boat would actually leave from. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: distance and
run time from Long Beach, San Pedro, Newport or Dana Point, and the sea-state
envelope the crossing demands.

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries, restricted areas or
depth-based rockfish rules stated for any position in this zone. The MPA
advisories the spot library records in this part of the Bight attach to Catalina
and Santa Barbara Island shoreline spots, not to these three offshore marks
(cameron).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three marks and what separates them.** The 172 sits at **33°30.200'N
118°50.500'W**, the 125 at **33°29.000'N 118°42.500'W** and the 499 at
**33°16.500'N 118°44.500'W** (cameron). The 172 and the 125 are separated by
**1.2 minutes of latitude and 8.0 minutes of longitude** — an almost due
east–west pair, **6.8 nm** apart and well inside the census's **12 nm**
zone-diameter cap, which is the clustering rule that grouped them.

**The 499 was attached, not clustered.** It lies **12.6 nm** from the 125 and
**14.6 nm** from the 172 (cameron) — past the 12 nm cap, so it did not cluster
with the pair; the census attached it as the nearest grouping it could be fished
with, at **8.8 nm** from the zone's midpoint. It is carried here as queued rather
than re-parented, because the zone list is set by the census and not by this
page.

**All three marks sit on the edges of a search block the corpus draws by
naming its corners.** The July 2022 above-Clemente block runs "from Cat to the
Osborn Bank to Nick to that 7-Eleven outside of Clemente" (`Blh2BA-7Ono`,
carried at [San Nicolas Island](san-nicolas-island.md)). The West End Humps to
[Osborn Bank](osborn-bank.md) leg measures **22.1 nm** direct; the 125 sits
**4.6 nm** and **18.3 nm** from those two ends and the 172 **11.3 nm** and
**13.2 nm**, so both lie essentially on that leg. The West End Humps to the 711
leg measures **29.4 nm** direct, and the 499 sits **13.0 nm** and **17.7 nm**
from its ends — on that leg too (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
states the block as a boundary rather than a set of named corners, so whether any
individual mark here is inside it or on its edge is not stated.

**The names are the only depth claims in the zone.** A bank is an isolated high
spot rising out of otherwise deep water and takes the **fathom depth of its
shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way:

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 125 | 125 fathoms | about 750 ft |
| 172 | 172 fathoms | about 1,032 ft |
| 499 | 499 fathoms | about 2,994 ft |

The 172's reading falls inside the **800–1,100 ft** band SoCal daytime swordfish
baits are set in ([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)),
near its deep end; the 125's reading is shallower than that band. The 499's reading is the odd one: **2,994 ft** is
the same depth as the deep, mostly featureless bottom the naming convention
describes banks as rising *out of* — the 2,000–3,000 ft plain that runs from Dana
Point through Newport out to Catalina (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source confirms any of the three numbers as a charted depth, or says whether the
499 is named for a depth at all.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron): the **286** at **7.7 nm** from
the 125, the **175** at **8.6 nm** from the 172, **Kidney Bank (63)** at
**9.6 nm** from the 172, the **474** at **11.2 nm** from the 499, **LA - 270** at
**13.7 nm** from the 125, **The Boot (504)** at **14.8 nm** from the 125 and the
**711** at **17.7 nm** from the 499. ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: the
Kidney Bank (63) / 175, LA - 270 / 286, 474 / 711 and The Boot (504) / 307
groupings all sit within a short run of this zone and none of them has a page on
this ladder yet, so there is nothing to hand a fallback plan off to.

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source: bottom composition, relief, high-spot shape, surrounding depth,
preferred directions, drift or current behaviour at any of these three marks.

## What's there

| species | season | what this zone does for them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) | July and October, both documented in 2022 | **July:** fish that had been outside San Clemente slid in between San Clemente and Catalina, and the 499 was called the predictable next step — "it was a logical step that they were to the 499 or places like that," with boats that went and looked catching them (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [June–July](../seasonal/june-july.md)). **October:** the warm band runs down the back side of Catalina and hooks around to San Clemente — "that's at 499 Zone" — spoken as where the warm water goes, not as a bite; in the same week the other areas that had held bluefin in the past did not sound like they held a lot, and the volume had slid out to Tanner and Cortez (`OYOda6T3f-8`, [October](../seasonal/october.md), [bight geography](bight-geography.md)) |
| [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) / [white seabass](../species/white-seabass.md) | ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source | Both are named at the islands either side of this water rather than on these marks; the July 2022 report has boats fishing them at San Clemente and San Nicolas sliding off the island to add bluefin, not the reverse (`Blh2BA-7Ono`) |

**The 499's two corpus appearances are both position-in-a-migration, not a
standing fishery.** July 2022 names it as where fish moving between the islands
should turn up next; October 2022 names it as the point where the fall warm band
hooks from Catalina's back side across to San Clemente. Neither describes a bite
on the mark, a depth fished, a bait, a technique or a grade.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether
[yellowfin tuna](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md), [dorado](../species/dorado.md),
[striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md),
[swordfish](../species/swordfish.md) or
[rockfish and lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md) are targeted anywhere in
this zone, and nothing describes bait, squid, temperature or water colour at any
of the three positions. Nothing in the corpus names the **172** or the **125** at
all. The silence is a gap in the sources, not a statement that those marks are
empty.

**Paddy water is named elsewhere in the Bight.** The best paddy water is **south
of San Clemente Island / Catalina** and the coastal strip from **Dana Point to
Oceanside and southward**, with paddies thinning north of Dana Point
(`HzE4FHHcvTk`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md)). This zone sits west and
north of Catalina, outside that named band. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
describes paddies over these three marks either way; see
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md) for the mechanics.

## How it fishes

**This is search-box water, and the search box is bigger than the zone.** The
above-Clemente block is worked as one big area rather than a circuit of marks:
the fish "aren't really holding hard to structure," can move **10–15 miles
overnight**, and boats already on them lose them because there is too much water
to cover (`Blh2BA-7Ono`). Budget hours of looking against
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md) and read the block the
way [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md) reads the other named boxes; the overnight
displacement is what
[report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)
applies to yesterday's report before you point the boat at the 499.

**The same report's compact-spot examples may include this zone, and that is
unresolved.** The contrast drawn against the big below-Clemente box is "running
to the 302 or 371 or even running up to the stuff off the West End of Cat"
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`) — the compact type you idle onto, grade on the meter, and either
commit to or leave ([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md)). The 125 sits **4.6 nm**
from the charted West End Humps and the 172 **11.3 nm** (cameron), so these marks
are plausibly part of what that phrase covers. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source
names a charted mark for "the stuff off the West End of Cat," so whether this
zone is spot-check water, part of the surrounding search box, or both on
different days is unstated.

**In October the chart decides whether this zone is on the plan at all.** The
fall regime is to "look 100% at your temp chart and chlorophyll," with bluefin on
the **cool** side of a break when the water is warm and the warm side when it is
cold ([October](../seasonal/october.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)). The 499 is a station on a band in
motion, not a fixed destination — in the week of 2022-10-20 the warm band was
where it hooked to San Clemente while the bluefin volume was out in the slightly
cooler water from the 381 off San Clemente's east-end back side to Tanner and
Cortez (`OYOda6T3f-8`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md)).

**In June–July the moon runs the window.** Full moons drive the bluefin night
bite, and the days leading into the full moon are the strongest documented
windows ([June–July](../seasonal/june-july.md),
[moon](../conditions/moon.md)).

**No mark here is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions for this zone are
pulled per charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the zone's program in full. Nothing states
which mark to start on, whether the three are run as one circuit, which end to
work first, or what tide, light and current do here.

## Spots

Three charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. Two clustered on their own
coordinates and one was attached on distance:

- **The 172** — the northwest mark, and the closest position in the zone to
  [Santa Barbara Island](santa-barbara-island.md) (9.3 nm) and to the Kidney Bank
  (63) / 175 grouping (8.6–9.6 nm). Coordinates only; the corpus never names it.
- **The 125** — the eastern mark and the one closest to land, 4.6 nm off
  Catalina's West End Humps. Coordinates only; the corpus never names it.
- **The 499** — the southern mark, attached to this zone rather than clustered
  into it, and the only position here the corpus carries: the predicted holding
  water for July 2022 bluefin moving between the islands (`Blh2BA-7Ono`) and the
  point where the fall warm band hooks from Catalina across to San Clemente,
  spoken of as "499 Zone" (`OYOda6T3f-8`).

Charted positions for all three are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position and, for the 499, the two cites above, so each spot page is
coordinates plus its parent zone until more sources arrive.
