# Upper Cross / 421 / 390

**A zone is a run grouping.** Upper Cross / 421 / 390 is a piece of the numbered
offshore-bank grid in open water southwest of San Diego — four charted positions
(cameron) spanning **8 minutes of latitude** (32°00.000'N to 32°08.000'N) and
**26 minutes of longitude** (117°48.800'W to 118°14.833'W). Three of them —
the **421**, the **390** and **Upper Cross** — sit inside 11 nm of each other
and are the cluster the zone is named for; the **60 Mile Bank (53 Fa.)** to the
west is a lone bank the census attached here as the nearest grouping it could be
fished with.

**Nothing in the corpus describes fishing any of these four marks.** The
[region page](baja-pacific-north.md) carries the same gap across the whole
numbered-bank grid — fifty-one charted positions over seventeen bank zones with
no depth, no target species and no doctrine attached to one of them. This is
therefore a **position-and-ladder page**: it says where the zone is, what the
spot names claim, and what is missing.

**This zone sits closer to San Diego than to Ensenada, and it is Mexican-side
water.** It is assigned to [Baja Pacific — North](baja-pacific-north.md), so a
boat leaving the United States crosses the border to fish it, but the mainland
positions nearest this zone are on the US side: the **Point Loma Pipe**
at **32°40.350'N 117°17.000'W** sits **44–61 nm** from the four marks here, against
**55–77 nm** from **Punta San Miguel** at **31°54.110'N 116°44.980'W** outside
Ensenada (cameron). That reverses the pattern of the
[1010 Trench / 378 / 213](1010-trench-378-213.md) zone to the south, where the
Ensenada positions are the closer ones. For scale, the
[Coronado Islands](coronado-islands.md) — the closest island fishing to a major
US port — run **~13 nm from the San Diego bay entrance** (cameron), so this zone
sits roughly four times that distance out.

## Structure & bathymetry

**The three-bank core is what makes this one zone.** The 421 at **32°08.000'N
117°52.000'W**, the 390 at **32°05.000'N 117°48.800'W** and Upper Cross at
**32°00.000'N 118°00.000'W** (cameron) sit **4.0 nm** (421 to 390), **10.5 nm**
(421 to Upper Cross) and **10.7 nm** (390 to Upper Cross) apart — every pair
inside the census's **12 nm** zone-diameter cap, which is the clustering rule
that grouped them. The 421 and the 390 are the tightest pair in the zone by a
wide margin.

**The 60 Mile Bank was attached, not clustered.** It lies at **32°04.000'N
118°14.833'W** (cameron), **13.5 nm** from the zone centre and **13.2 nm** from
Upper Cross, but **19.8 nm** from the 421 and **22.1 nm** from the 390 — well
past the 12 nm cap, and the widest span in the zone. It is carried here as
queued rather than re-parented, because the zone list is set by the census and
not by this page.

**The names are the only depth claims in the zone.** A bank takes the **fathom
depth of its shallowest point** as its name, at six feet to a fathom
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way the 421 tops out
at **421 fathoms — about 2,526 ft** and the 390 at **390 fathoms — about
2,340 ft**, deep-water banks rather than anything fished on the surface of the
structure, and both deeper than the deepest working depth in the KB: SoCal
daytime swordfish baits average **800–1,100 ft**
([deep-drop swordfishing](../techniques/deep-drop-swordfishing.md)).

**The 60 Mile Bank's suffix is the one shoal reading in the zone.** Its library
name carries **(53 Fa.)** — 53 fathoms, about **318 ft** on the same convention
(cameron). That reads roughly seven to eight times shallower than either
numbered bank here, and it is the only mark in the zone in the depth range the
Baja yo-yo fishery works: on the [Colonet](colonet.md) stretch the charted crests
read **28 and 41 fathoms** — about 168 and 246 ft — and are named as deep enough
to call for the heaviest yo-yo iron (`hteLeDIy9Qs`), with the yellowtail
themselves found **120–180 ft and sometimes 220 ft** (`YAKOv9bXKO0`). The 53 Fa.
reading sits below all of those. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms any
of the three numbers as a charted depth, or says what the bottom does at the
60 Mile Bank.

**The "60" in the name does not resolve against anything in the library.** The
lexicon records a distance-naming exception — Nine Mile Bank and "The 14" are
named for statute miles off the coast, not for a fathom depth
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`) — and this name reads the same
way, but the bank sits **60.9 nm (70 statute miles)** from the Point Loma Pipe
and **76.9 nm (88 statute miles)** from Punta San Miguel (cameron). ⚠ Flagged
gap — no corpus source states what the 60 measures, or from where.

**Upper Cross and Lower Cross are a charted pair.** Upper Cross at
**32°00.000'N 118°00.000'W** and Lower Cross at **31°00.000'N 118°00.000'W**
(cameron) share the same charted longitude and sit exactly **60 minutes of
latitude — 60.0 nm — apart**, a north/south naming pair on one meridian. The
same upper/lower convention runs elsewhere in the Baja grid on **Upper Hidden
Bank** against **Hidden Bank** and on the **Upper 500** against the **Lower
500**; the **Upper Finger Bank** carries the prefix with no charted lower
counterpart in the library (cameron). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source defines
what *Cross* denotes as a structure term, or whether upper/lower means anything
beyond relative latitude.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bottom composition, relief, contour, high-spot
shape or current behaviour anywhere in this zone. The `depth_band` is unknown
for that reason, not because the water is shallow.

**The zone's charted neighbours are mostly unbuilt.** The nearest zone in the
library is E. Butterfly / San Salvador Knoll, **13.5 nm** due north across the
maritime boundary in [the Bight](socal-bight.md);
[1010 Trench / 378 / 213](1010-trench-378-213.md) is **17.9 nm** south, with its
**378** the closest single mark at **12.7 nm** from this zone's centre; W.
Butterfly / 157 is **25.3 nm** northwest and
[Coronados - 230 / 302 (Kidney Bank) / 226](coronados-230-302-226.md) **26.7 nm**
northeast (cameron). ⚠ Flagged stub — no corpus source yet: neither Butterfly
zone carries corpus material or a page to hand off to.

## Spots

Four charted spots (cameron). They group as a core plus one attachment:

- **The core three — the 421, the 390 and Upper Cross**, inside 11 nm of each
  other (cameron) and the only part of this zone that clustered on its own. The
  421 and the 390 are 4.0 nm apart and would be fished as one stop before
  either is fished with Upper Cross.
- **The 60 Mile Bank (53 Fa.)** — the western outlier, 13.5 nm off the zone
  centre and up to 22.1 nm from the far side of the core; also the only name
  here carrying a shoal-range depth label and the only one named for distance
  rather than depth.

Charted positions for all four are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). No spot here carries corpus detail
beyond its position, so each spot page is coordinates plus its parent zone until
sources arrive.
