# 289 / 284

**A zone is a run grouping.** This is two charted marks on the numbered
offshore-bank grid east-northeast of San Clemente Island's southeast end — the
**289** at **32°56.000'N 118°05.700'W** and the **284** at **32°54.500'N
118°05.000'W** (cameron). They lie **1.5 minutes of latitude** and **0.7 minutes
of longitude** apart, which works out to **1.6 nm**: the 284 bears **159°** from
the 289, and the 289 bears **339°** back.

**At 1.6 nm across, this is the tightest bank cluster on the ladder.** No other
zone the census cut out of the offshore-banks list in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md) has a smaller diameter — only the
sub-pixel international artificial-reef group on the border is packed closer — and 1.6 nm is
roughly an eighth of the **12 nm** cap that drew it (cameron). Both marks
clustered on their own coordinates — nothing here
was attached on distance — so the zone is a genuine pair rather than a grouping
of convenience.

**The 289 carries the corpus and the 284 is silent.** The 289 is one of four
numbered marks named inside the June–July **below-Clemente complex**
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`), the outer corner of a documented August 2022 charter that found
it "less fishy looking" and left (`CMQkHQMxbXM`), the far end of the September
2022 dorado-and-bluefin spread (`5to3Q5P7w90`), and an outer bank named again in
the October 2022 fall report (`XLVUhV8DW64`) — four consecutive months of 2022
Bight reports. Nothing in the corpus names the **284** at all.

**What the corpus never gives is this water on its own terms.** Every mention of
the 289 is as a member of a bigger unit — the search box, the spread, the outer
banks — and none of them describes the bottom, the drift, the depth or a program
for these two marks. Everything below that a source would have to supply is
flagged, and those flags aggregate in [the gap report](../sources/gap-report.md).

## Getting there

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a stated distance, heading, run time or fuel
figure to either mark, from any port.

**The one documented run reached the 289 from Newport by way of Mackerel Bank.**
A charter cleared Newport harbour at about **08:00**, struggled for bait —
sardines, Spanish mackerel and small bonito instead of the mackerel wanted in
case of marlin or swordfish — and ran outside to **Mackerel Bank**, where there
were plenty of boats and no sign of fish; from there it ran down to the **289**,
found the water "got less fishy looking the further down we went", and bent back
north to the **152** (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). Mackerel Bank is **16.2 nm** west-northwest
of the 289 and the 152 is **19.3 nm** north (cameron), so the 289 was the outer,
southern corner of that loop rather than its destination.

**This is offshore-fleet water, not a coastal run.** The nearest charted coastal
positions in the spot library are **Box Canyon** in
[Oceanside / North County](oceanside-north-county.md) at **33.9 nm**, **San Mateo
Point** in [Dana Point](dana-point.md) at **36.2 nm**, **Widow Rock** in
[La Jolla](la-jolla.md) at **37.9 nm** and the **Hill Street** mark off
[Point Loma](point-loma.md) at **43.0 nm**, all measured from the 289 (cameron).
In August 2022 most of the overnight boats out of **Newport and Dana Point** were
fishing offshore rather than San Clemente Island, and most of the private effort
was on "the banks below Clemente" (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus
source names the port either mark is normally fished out of, or gives a run time
from one.

**San Clemente Island is the nearest land, off to the west.** **Pyramid Head** is
**14.9 nm** at **242°** from the 289 and **14.8 nm** at **248°** from the 284
(cameron), and that end of the island is built as
[San Clemente Island — Front Side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md), with
Mackerel Bank attached to it at its offshore edge. Nothing in this zone is in the
island's lee.

**Regulatory — United States. Jurisdiction: California / CDFW. Checked as of
2026-08. Verify current rules before you fish** ([US waters](us-waters.md)).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: closures, MPA boundaries or restricted areas
stated for either position. The two naval security zones the corpus knows in this
part of the Bight — **Northwest Harbor** and **Castle Rock** — sit on San Clemente
Island **25.5 nm** and **26.9 nm** west of the 289 (cameron) and belong to that
island's zone pages, not to these marks. The corpus also names the **Cow Cod
Conservation Area** as an offshore closure with its own depth limit and its own
as-of date ([rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)) but gives no
boundary for it, so whether either mark falls inside it is unverified — **check
the CCCA boundary against your position with CDFW/NOAA before dropping.**
Boundary compliance is positional, not depth-based ([US waters](us-waters.md)).

## Structure & bathymetry

**The two positions.** The 289 is at **32°56.000'N 118°05.700'W** and the 284 at
**32°54.500'N 118°05.000'W** (cameron) — **1.6 nm** apart on a north-northwest to
south-southeast line, with the 284 only **0.7 minutes** of longitude east of the
289. Both sit on latitudes **inside** San Clemente Island's own span: the island's
southernmost charted position, Desperation Reef, is **10.5 nm** south of the 289
and **9.0 nm** south of the 284 in latitude (cameron). These marks are abeam the
island, not below it.

**A bank is named for the fathom depth of its shallowest point**, at six feet to a
fathom — an isolated high spot rising out of otherwise deep water, with places all
over the ocean at the same reading that are not banks
([zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md), `Rf1HKJG-SDg`). Read that way:

| spot | name read as fathoms | in feet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 289 | 289 fathoms | about 1,734 ft |
| 284 | 284 fathoms | about 1,704 ft |

**The two names claim crests 5 fathoms — about 30 ft — apart across 1.6 nm.**
That is either one broad piece of relief carrying two waypoints, or two separate
humps of nearly identical height a mile and a half from each other; the names
cannot tell you which. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source confirms either number as
a charted reading, or gives relief, high-spot shape, bottom composition or
surrounding depth at either mark.

**These are the deepest-reading names in the below-Clemente complex.** Its other
numbered members read **43 fathoms** (about 258 ft), **181 fathoms** (about
1,086 ft) and **182 fathoms** (about 1,092 ft) (`Rf1HKJG-SDg`, `Blh2BA-7Ono`,
cameron) — so on names alone this pair is the complex's deep corner by roughly
600 ft. Whether that reflects the bottom or the naming is unverified.

**The 289 is enumerated beside "the entire Clemente Ridge", not as part of it.**
The complex is listed as "the 43, the 181, 182, 289, the entire Clemente Ridge,
all the way up to Mackerel Bank" (`Blh2BA-7Ono`), and a September 2022 report runs
the fish out to the east end of San Clemente, down Clemente Ridge, to the 289, in
that order (`5to3Q5P7w90`). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source gives Clemente Ridge
a position and the spot library carries no coordinates for it (cameron), so
whether the 289 sits on the ridge's outer end or clear of it cannot be settled
here. Plot a report that says "the ridge" carefully — the corpus also uses that
word for the **181/182 Ridge** ([51 / 181 / 138](51-181-138.md)).

**Nothing here is attached to land, which sets how it would be worked.**
Standalone structure rising off the plain fishes in **any** direction, but each
individual bank still has its own **preferred directions** learned over repeat
trips ([island structure](island-structure.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source:
preferred directions, drift behaviour or current set at either mark.

**Charted neighbours, nearest first** (cameron), measured from the 289 unless
noted: the **181** at **10.9 nm** east — and only **10.1 nm** from the 284, the
closest outside mark to this zone — then **Pyramid Head** on San Clemente Island at
**14.9 nm**, the **209** at **14.9 nm** northeast, the **51** at **15.1 nm**, the
**312** at **15.4 nm**, the **138** at **15.5 nm**, the **277** at **16.0 nm** due
north, **Mackerel Bank** at **16.2 nm**, the **43** at **17.8 nm** south, the
**152** at **19.3 nm**, the **81** at **20.3 nm** southwest, the **182** at
**24.5 nm** and **Catalina's East End Light** at **24.6 nm** northwest.

**The surrounding zones are already built, so a fallback plan out of here has
somewhere to go.** Centre to centre, the built neighbours are
[51 / 181 / 138](51-181-138.md) at **13.2 nm** east-southeast,
[209 / 312](209-312.md) at **15.0 nm** northeast,
[43 / 91 / 300](43-91-300.md) at **18.9 nm** south-southeast,
[The Slide / 152 / 277](slide-152-277.md) at **19.8 nm** north,
[The Corner / 140 / 182](corner-140-182.md) at **21.6 nm** southeast and
[81 / 381](81-381.md) at **24.9 nm** southwest, with
[San Clemente Island — Front Side](san-clemente-island-front-side.md) carrying
Mackerel Bank to the west (cameron).

**The 284 is inside the search box by position and outside it by name.** The
enumeration names the 289 and not the 284 (`Blh2BA-7Ono`), yet the two marks are
**1.6 nm** apart (cameron) — second only to the 51 and the 138 at **0.8 nm** among
the numbered banks in the library ([51 / 181 / 138](51-181-138.md)). ⚠ Flagged gap
— no corpus source names the 284 as part of the complex, so its inclusion is a
position fact, not a doctrine one.

**This zone is the complex's island corner.** Of the complex's four numbered
members the 289 is much the closest to San Clemente Island — **14.9 nm** from
Pyramid Head, against **21.5 nm** for the 43, **24.3 nm** for the 181 and
**33.6 nm** for the 182 — and the unnamed 284 is closer still at **14.8 nm**
(cameron). The box's northern limit, Mackerel
Bank, is a further **16.2 nm** west-northwest and belongs to the island's zone
(`Blh2BA-7Ono`, cameron). Read this pair as the western end of the search box, the
part you cross on the way to or from the island.

**The complex is bigger than any one zone on this ladder.** Its named members fall
across four separate census clusters plus the island zone — the 289 here, the 181
at **10.9 nm**, Mackerel Bank at **16.2 nm**, the 43 at **17.8 nm** and the 182 at
**24.5 nm** (cameron). Treat this page as a corner of the box on a June–July
bluefin plan, not as the whole of it.

**This zone sits outside the September volume square, off its inshore, eastern
side.** The
**SBI – Nick – Clemente – Catalina** box is where the bigger bluefin were in
September 2022 (`5to3Q5P7w90`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[September](../seasonal/september.md)). Both marks lie east of *every* charted
Catalina and San Clemente position in the library — the 289 is **10.5 nm** east of
the easternmost Catalina position — and **21.2 nm** south of Catalina's
southernmost (cameron). The same report's dorado-and-bluefin spread does reach
this water, so the square and the spread are two different claims and only the
second includes the 289.

**Paddy geography puts this zone on the good side of one line and off the other.**
The best paddy water is **south of San Clemente Island / Catalina** and the
coastal strip from **Dana Point to Oceanside and southward**; north of Dana Point
paddies thin out and hold fewer fish (`HzE4FHHcvTk`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md)). These marks are **21.2 nm** south of
Catalina's southernmost charted position and **26.8 nm** south of Dana Point's,
but they sit **abeam** San Clemente Island rather than south of it (cameron).
⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source describes a paddy over either position;
mechanics are in [kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether
[striped marlin](../species/striped-marlin.md),
[white seabass](../species/white-seabass.md) or
[rockfish and lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md) are targeted anywhere in
this zone, and nothing describes bait, squid, temperature or water colour over
either mark. The silence is a gap in the sources, not a statement that these marks
are empty.

## How it fishes

**The search rule applies here, not the spot-check rule.** You do not "check the
289" — the below-Clemente complex is planned as **hours of looking** across one
**~50–60 square-mile** box, glassing and running contour between features, with
the named banks as reference points inside a single hunting zone. The opposite
type is the compact spot — the 302, the 371, the ground off Catalina's West End —
which you idle up to, grade on the meter, and either commit to or leave in
**20–30 minutes** (`Blh2BA-7Ono`, [zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md),
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md)). Budget this zone as
part of a half-day commitment, not as a stop.

**The zone's own geometry is far smaller than the box it belongs to.** **1.6 nm**
between the two marks (cameron) means reading both is a single pass; the hours the
doctrine asks for get spent in the water around them, not in running between them.

**The one documented visit here was a blank, and the reason given was the water.**
Running down from Mackerel Bank the sign got worse rather than better — "less
fishy looking the further down we went" — and the boat turned back north to the
152, then the Avalon Bank, then toward the 14 Mile Bank, having planned the day
around a tide at about **11:00 or noon** and around fish known to be off Catalina's
east end (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). That is the complex's own doctrine being executed: the
289 was read on the way through and abandoned on sign.

**The wind-rollover map favours this water, without naming it.** South of Newport
is "less prone to wind-wave-driven rollovers", and the Coronados, the tuna
grounds, Catalina and San Clemente Island all held their water through a NW event
that pulled **5–8 °F** off the northern Channel Islands and the LA / Ventura shelf
(`HzE4FHHcvTk`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md),
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md)). Two of those
named holders bracket this zone — **Catalina's East End Light 24.6 nm**
northwest and **Pyramid Head 14.9 nm** west (cameron) — and the one documented run
out here left Newport and went outside and south (`CMQkHQMxbXM`). ⚠ Flagged gap —
no corpus source places these two marks on that map directly. Exposure is a
separate
question from temperature: on the August 2022 report the call for the coming
weekend was that it was "not gonna be good weather to go out there on your own
boat" (`CMQkHQMxbXM`) — a private-boat weather veto on this exact water
([sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

**The fall read out here is a chart read.** From October the regime flips to
looking **100 % at the temperature chart and chlorophyll** (`XLVUhV8DW64`,
`OYOda6T3f-8`, [bight geography](bight-geography.md)), and the stated method for
finding yellowfin is the warmest water available crossed against a chlorophyll
chart for the cleanest (`OYOda6T3f-8`,
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[water colour](../conditions/water-color.md)). The 289 is on the outer-bank side
of that read (`XLVUhV8DW64`).

**Structure and current edge lining up is the stated trigger.** SoCal fish orient
to structure and then to current edges, and the banger days come when the two line
up; there is enough water on this coast that current edges get fished as much as
structure does (`yMiBtZ7k8-w`,
[current & structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)). With no program
recorded for these marks, break position and break age decide whether the zone is
on a given day's plan
([report reading & forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)),
and the meter decides once you are there
([electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**Neither mark is a BightSST eval target**, so conditions here are pulled per
charted position rather than read off a scored spot
([eval targets](bightsst-eval-targets.md)). The nearest eval target is **Pyramid
Cove** on San Clemente Island, **16.1 nm** west-southwest of both marks (cameron)
— useful as the island-end reference for the complex, too far off to stand in for
this water.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: which mark to start on, whether the two are run
as one circuit, or what tide, light, current or swell do here.

## Spots

Two charted spots (cameron), listed north to south. Both clustered on their own
coordinates — neither was attached to this zone on distance:

- **The 289** — the northern mark and the only position here the corpus carries.
  It is a named member of the below-Clemente search box (`Blh2BA-7Ono`), the outer
  corner of the August 2022 Newport run that found it dead (`CMQkHQMxbXM`), the
  far end of the September 2022 dorado-and-bluefin spread (`5to3Q5P7w90`), and one
  of the outer banks named in the October 2022 report (`XLVUhV8DW64`). It is the
  closer of the two to Mackerel Bank (**16.2 nm**) and to the 277 (**16.0 nm**
  due north).
- **The 284** — the southern mark, **1.6 nm** at **159°** from the 289, and the
  closer of the two to the marks east and south — **10.1 nm** to the 181,
  **14.0 nm** to the 51 and **16.2 nm** to the 43 — and marginally the closer to
  **Pyramid Head** to the west at **14.8 nm** (cameron). Coordinates only; no
  corpus source names it.

Charted positions for both are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md). Neither spot carries corpus detail
beyond its position and, for the 289, the cites above, so each spot page is
coordinates plus its parent zone until more sources arrive.
