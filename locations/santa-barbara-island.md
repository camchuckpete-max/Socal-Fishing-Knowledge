# Santa Barbara Island

**A zone is a run grouping.** Santa Barbara Island — "SBI" in the fleet's
shorthand (`XLVUhV8DW64`) — is the smallest and most compact island zone in the
Bight: eight charted spots (cameron), seven of them wrapped around a shoreline
that fits inside a box roughly **1.9 nm east–west by 1.4 nm north–south**
computed from the library's own positions, plus Osborn Bank, which the census
attaches here on distance. The island itself is a
[calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md) and squid-driven
[yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) fishery with island
[rockfish](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md) behind it; the water off it is
[bluefin](../species/bluefin-tuna.md) ground — the northern corner of the
September **volume square** SBI–Nick–Clemente–Catalina (`5to3Q5P7w90`,
[zone lexicon](zone-lexicon.md#the-volume-square)) and a station on the fall
warm-band arc that runs backside Santa Cruz/Rosa → SBI → backside Catalina →
the 499 and Clemente (`OYOda6T3f-8`,
[bight geography](bight-geography.md#the-warm-band-tracking-path-fall)).

Two things set the character of the zone. **Swell decides whether the island is
fishable at all** — it blew out on day one of a November 2014 tournament and
fished the next day once the swell came down (`IATPg9110CE`). And **fleet
coverage is thin**: a quiet week from SBI is a read on how many boats went, not
on how many fish are there (`5p6gu14ZC4w`; see
[report reading](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)).

## Getting there

**Plan it as overnight range, not a day trip.** The jig-stick top-shot rule
puts Santa Barbara Island in the same class as San Clemente Island — **50 lb**
mono rather than the 40 lb run at the full-day-accessible Coronado, Channel and
Catalina islands, because these spots carry a true shot at a trophy yellowtail
at any moment (`LTCFjqtSI8g`,
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)).

**From Catalina it is a short hop.** A private tournament boat left Two Harbors
and was on the island in **about an hour and 15–20 minutes**, arriving in the
dark to start on the beach at first light (`PKf7G3uL4io`).

**Sportboats run it overnight and anchor in the dark.** An 85 ft boat out of
Long Beach ran out to fish the island the next morning, with the stated plan to
"get there in the dark, get anchored up and be sitting on the numbers"
(`SGbynqaiHdY`). The captain's reason is a report-reading lesson: the island
had a reported mid-morning bite, but "the boat that tells me it's been a
mid-morning bite is the boat that gets there mid-morning" — arrival time, not
fish behaviour (`SGbynqaiHdY`,
[report reading](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md)).

**The zone goes down with the outside water.** An August 2022 wind event was
forecast to blow out the Channel Islands, San Clemente, San Nicolas, Santa
Barbara Island and the tuna zone off Catalina in one go (`CMQkHQMxbXM`); a
November 2014 tournament found the island "all blown out" one day and fishable
the next as the swell dropped (`IATPg9110CE`,
[sea state](../conditions/sea-state.md)).

**The tuna zone is a separate run from the island.** The bluefin ground worked
in October 2025 sat **just northeast of the island**, with the day's plan to
work south from there — a **45-minute ride at 40 kt** from the bait stop, which
is not a named port (`mDmbGdQAy-4`).

**California / CDFW — an MPA boundary cuts the island's east end.** The spot
library's Cave Canyon white seabass entry carries its own instruction: **stay
north of the MPA line at 33 28.500** (cameron). As of 2026-08; **verify current
CDFW boundaries before fishing.**

## Structure & bathymetry

**The island is small enough to fish end to end in a day.** The library's
charted positions run from Arch Reef and Webster Point on the west end, through
the Caverns Area, to Landing Cove and the Cave Canyon zone on the east end,
with Three Sisters and Sutil Island off the south end (cameron) — the whole set
inside the ~1.9 × 1.4 nm box above.

**Two of the named spots are boilers, and the library says so.** Arch Reef is
logged "caution — boilers in big swell" and Three Sisters "caution — boilers"
(cameron): submerged rock that stands up and breaks when the swell is up, which
is the same feature that makes them fishable structure and makes them dangerous
on a big day ([island structure](island-structure.md)).

**Kelp over a ridge is the anchoring structure.** A July 2017 charter anchored
in **roughly 102 ft** on a spot with kelp on it and a ridge coming off the
island, with **red crab** on the structure; when conditions changed so the
lines would not lay into the kelp, the boat moved out to the outer edge on the
**back — weather — side** and anchored in **20 fathoms**, fishing meter marks
behind the boat (`SGbynqaiHdY`). Kelp extent is not constant: a March 2012 crew
remarked there had been noticeably more kelp at their spot on a prior visit
(`EU_Dod4wfYw`).

**The shoreline fishes as rock, gap and beach.** Tournament crews worked fish
tight "all the way up against that rock" and through a **narrow gap between two
rocks** (`IATPg9110CE`), and separately found "a little stretch of beach that
was biting," with fish holding in the foam in inches of water off the sand
(`IATPg9110CE`, `PKf7G3uL4io`).

**Osborn Bank is not shoreline.** It is catalogued in the spot library's
offshore-banks section and lands in this zone as the nearest centre — **6.1 nm
from the zone centre**, 7.0 nm from the island group's own centre, both figures
computed from the library's charted positions (cameron). It behaves like a
standalone bank and like a temperature edge: in August 2016 the water read
**70 °F** right at the bank edge about **a mile and a half** off the boat's
position and **72 °F** where the boat sat, with warmer water — and the
yellowtail and yellowfin — **southeast of the bank** (`i3qIAHW-SJc`,
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md)).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: charted depths, bottom composition, kelp
extent or working depth bands for any named spot in this zone, including Osborn
Bank's own depth over the high spot.

## How it fishes

**The zone is two fisheries on one run.** The shoreline program — bass, squid
yellowtail, rockfish — works the ~2 nm of island structure (cameron); the tuna
program
works the water northeast of the island and Osborn Bank, and is a separate run
with separate gear.

**Be anchored before daylight.** The stated island program is to arrive in the
dark and sit on the numbers rather than run out into a reported mid-morning
bite (`SGbynqaiHdY`).

**Squid is the currency on the island.** The July 2017 program anchored and
fished live squid on deliberately **light sinkers** so the bait sank naturally
in the current, waiting on some tide — the pre-trip call being a **½ lb
slider**, **30 lb** line (25 lb for a lighter option) and **2/0 to 5/0** hooks
(`SGbynqaiHdY`, [sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md)). When the
kelp lay-in stopped working the answer was to move rather than wait: out to the
outer edge in 20 fathoms on the weather side, fishing marks behind the boat
(`SGbynqaiHdY`, [electronics and sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)).

**The bass program is a slow bottom crawl worked tight to structure.** Cast in,
let the plastic sink close to bottom, then **slow roll it right along the
bottom**, stopping it to let it fall again — the bites come on the fall after
the pause (`IATPg9110CE`); the next day the same retrieve was "creeping it
across the bottom" while staying out of the structure, fished right up into
skinny water with long casts down the beach (`PKf7G3uL4io`,
[swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md),
[soft-plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md)). Rod length here
is a stated trade-off rather than a rule: **8 to 8½ ft** reaches back into
structure but wears you down over a day, a **7 ft** rod has "tons of leverage"
for pulling fish out of rock and kelp (`IATPg9110CE`).

**Iron runs a 50 lb top shot here** rather than the 40 lb used at the
full-day-accessible islands (`LTCFjqtSI8g`,
[surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md)).

**Off the island, the tuna program is match-the-bait, not cover-water.** In
October 2025 foamers all around the boat refused both a trolled spreader bar
and cast plugs; the fish were locked onto saury, anchovy and microbait, and
what converted was going back to a **dead flying fish under the kite**
(`mDmbGdQAy-4`, [kite fishing](../techniques/kite-fishing.md),
[bluefin tuna](../species/bluefin-tuna.md#situations--techniques)). The fight
style on this ground is the beltless, rail-locked "railroading" method
(`usHl-4SfqDA`, `mDmbGdQAy-4`,
[fighting big bluefin](../techniques/fighting-big-bluefin.md)).

**On Osborn Bank the bite is a graylight bite.** It starts around **4:30–5:00
a.m.** and builds (`i3qIAHW-SJc`); that morning produced fish on a **sinker-free
sardine** simply thrown out, held away from the boat and left to wiggle, on the
[dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md), and on sardine fished down
**60–70 ft "on the sink"** (`i3qIAHW-SJc`,
[rubber-band deep rig](../rigging/rubber-band-deep-rig.md)). Fish moved deeper
under the boat as the morning went on (`i3qIAHW-SJc`).

## Spots

Eight spots: seven around the island, and one offshore bank the census attaches
to this zone on distance.

- **West end and the boiler reefs** — Webster Point, Arch Reef and the Caverns
  Area. Arch Reef is logged with a boiler caution for big swell (cameron); this
  is the end of the island the tournament crews worked as rock-and-gap
  structure (`IATPg9110CE`).
- **East end** — Landing Cove and the Cave Canyon WSB zone, the named white
  seabass ground, whose library entry carries the island's one MPA
  instruction — stay north of 33 28.500 (cameron).
- **South end** — Three Sisters, a second boiler caution, and the Sutil Island
  squid / yellowtail zone, the library's name for the squid ground off the
  islet at the island's south end (cameron).
- **Census-assigned offshore bank** — Osborn Bank, catalogued in the library's
  offshore-banks section and attached here at 6.1 nm as the nearest zone
  centre; it is the zone's tuna structure and its temperature edge
  (`i3qIAHW-SJc`).

The anchoring spot in ~102 ft of kelp-and-ridge water (`SGbynqaiHdY`), the
"stretch of beach that was biting" (`IATPg9110CE`) and the bluefin ground
northeast of the island (`mDmbGdQAy-4`, `usHl-4SfqDA`) are all fished ground the
library does not name as spots; they stay in
[the evidence file](evidence/santa-barbara-island.md) rather than becoming
pages. Charted positions for all eight are in
[the spot library](../sources/spot-lists.md); the per-spot pages carry them.
