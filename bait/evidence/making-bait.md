## Ensenada, Baja — local mackerel types

- `0dIwWiOc1NY` (StokedOnFishing, 2026-02-17 upload, Ensenada, Baja
  California — guide Alejandro, a local full-time commercial fisherman;
  unregistered channel): crew made mackerel bait just outside the marina
  during what's described on camera as late-spring/early-summer mackerel
  spawning, when the bait runs close to shore and spread out. Two local
  mackerel types used: "Spanish jack" (the SoCal slang name given on camera)
  — a slower swimmer but tough, staying alive in the tank for hours — and
  "greenback" — smaller, mini-tuna-shaped, flashier and faster. No sabiki
  hook count, line rating, or sinker weight given for this session.
