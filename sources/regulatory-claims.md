# Regulatory Claims Register

**Every regulation-shaped claim in the knowledgebase, in one place, so it can
be re-verified in one pass.**

Regulations are not fishery knowledge. Seasons, bag and gear limits, size
minimums, licences, and closures are set by an agency, they change without
notice, and a stale one is worse than a missing one. Per
[CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md), every such claim in a note carries its
**jurisdiction**, an **as-of date**, and a **verify-current flag**. This
register is the index of those claims — it does not replace them, and the
note always holds the fuller context.

**⚠ Nothing here is current by virtue of being written down.** Check the
issuing agency before you fish. For California that is CDFW; for Mexican
waters it is CONAPESCA (permits) and INM (FMM), and a Baja limit differing
from a CDFW one is **two jurisdictions, not a conflict**.

## Why this file exists

Batch 2's evaluator was told to escalate *any* regulatory claim to Cameron,
regardless of whether it was correctly formatted. It did — 21 evaluator
escalations, of which roughly 18 were flags on already-compliant work, with
the evaluators saying so in the escalation text itself. That buried the two
or three entries that genuinely needed a decision.

Splitting the two concerns fixes it. A **compliant** regulatory claim belongs
here, in a register Cameron reviews periodically or before a trip. Only a
claim that **fails** the format, or that raises a real judgment question,
should interrupt anyone. The Phase 5 evaluator change enforces that split.

## Known gap

**Mexican licensing and entry requirements are absent from the knowledgebase**
— `FMM`, `tourist permit`, and `fishing licence` return no hits outside this
file. Anyone planning a Baja trip needs the FMM, a CONAPESCA fishing permit,
a passport, and Mexican auto insurance, and the KB currently says none of it.
Phase 3 closes this from `P36VGPPf120`, which covers the permits, the border
route, and the insurance carriers by name.
