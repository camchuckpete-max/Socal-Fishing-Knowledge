# Fact-check ledger

(sources/plan-review.md). One row per flag; written by the fact-check and
external-verification passes and by resolve-cites. **A row is a question,
not a verdict** — single-source ≠ wrong; nothing is deleted on the strength
of a flag. Categories: single-source | contradicted-by-source |
contradicted-internal | external-mismatch | cite-unresolved | unverifiable.

` — external-mismatch: …` to the detail cell of rows tagged `ext-verify`.
