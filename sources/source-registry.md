confidence rubric in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)). Repeated doctrine from a source
listed here is `high`; a single mention is `medium`; **channels not listed here
cap at `medium`**; sponsored/promotional claims are `low` regardless of channel;
anything sourced `cameron` is `high` (with the doctrine-vs-open-item nuance in
CLAUDE.md).
