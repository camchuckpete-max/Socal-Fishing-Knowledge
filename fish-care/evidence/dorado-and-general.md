## General principles (all species)

- `5to3Q5P7w90` (as above): the same Bight-report aside gives the rock-salt
  slurry recipe (~2 cups per 7–10 lb bag of ice, plus a splash of seawater)
  and demonstrates rinsing a bled tuna's cavity in salt water rather than the
  boat's fresh-water hose, "you don't want to do this with a fresh water
  hose ever."

## Fillet handling & storage (all species)

- `2gHRrR3D8rY` (StokedOnFishing, "How to Easily Fillet a Halibut
  Step-by-step," 2019-06-06; unregistered channel — medium confidence): a

  soak, salt-only rinse, dry before freezing, water-displacement bagging,
  storage windows) are carried here as general fillet-care practice, not
  halibut-specific.
