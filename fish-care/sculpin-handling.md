# Sculpin (Venomous Spine) Handling

Sculpin — the reddish bottomfish anglers pull up on rockfish/bottom gear,
commonly called "sculpin" on SoCal boats (California scorpionfish) — carry
venomous spines over most of the body. This note is a safety/handling
procedure only: it does not cover where or when to find sculpin or a
dedicated technique to target them, just how to unhook and handle one without
getting stuck.

## Safe unhooking

- **Thumb in the lower lip, hold on tight.** Pin a thumb into the sculpin's
  lower lip for the grip — handling it like "a little rattlesnake"
  (`ty8FtA3Y2bA`). Once gripped, do not let go: if the fish wiggles free of
  the grip, a spine goes into the hand.
- **Never grab the hook with pliers to shake the fish loose.** The fish spins
  in place on the hook and drives a spine — back, face, pec fin, or anal fin —
  into the hand. Keep the thumb-in-lip grip locked the entire time the hook
  backs out (`ty8FtA3Y2bA`).

## Spine locations (where it can get you)

- Spines run the length of the back.
- The head carries roughly 50 spines across the face, including around the
  eyes (`ty8FtA3Y2bA`).
- More spines sit behind the head, on the sides.
- Large spines on the pectoral fins.
- Three large spines on the anal fin.

All of these spines carry bacteria. A puncture is not lethal but is described
as "phenomenal[ly]" painful (`ty8FtA3Y2bA`).

## On-deck practice & the broader rockfish caution

- **Deckhands catch it first.** On SoCal party boats, deckhands intercept
  sculpin coming over the rail about 95% of the time and call it out so
  anglers don't touch it themselves (`J40VxLNuZhk`).
- **Treat every rockfish spine as off-limits — sculpin is the one that's
  genuinely venomous.** No on-deck sorting rule for spines is reliable, so the
  operational answer is the same either way. Tackle Express frames the
  general SoCal rockfish spine coating as a stinging mucus rather than venom
  — unpleasant, not the same hazard class — and singles out sculpin
  (California scorpionfish) as venomous enough to "ruin your day"
  (`J40VxLNuZhk`); [rockfish & lingcod](../species/rockfish-lingcod.md)
  carries a differing **mild venom** framing for general rockfish dorsal
  spines from a separate source. Both positions land on the same deck rule:
  avoid all spines on all rockfish.

## Regulations

**California / CDFW, as of 2022-04-16 (`ty8FtA3Y2bA`) — verify current CDFW
rules before keeping:** minimum size to keep is 10 inches.

## Table quality

Sculpin is good eating — sweet, white meat — despite the spines
(`ty8FtA3Y2bA`).
