# Dehooking & Release Handling

Getting a hook out of a fish you intend to release, and measuring it, without
doing the damage that makes the release pointless. This note is procedure
only — the *ethic* of when to release (the Cedros voluntary
catch-and-release policy, crew keep-some/release-most practice) lives in
[calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md), and venomous-species handling is
separate in [sculpin handling](sculpin-handling.md).

Source is a short dedicated tutorial from Capt. Dave Hansen (Your Saltwater
Guide, `VUb7a3sP8zQ`, 2022-04-23), demonstrated on a bait-fed calico bass.
`dave-hansen` is a [registered voice](../sources/source-registry.md), but
this is the first commit of this specific procedure — `medium` pending
repetition.

## Deep-hooked fish — go in through the gill opening

The problem case is a fish that has taken the bait (squid, in the source)
well down, so the hook is not visible or reachable at the mouth.

- **Clear the bait first.** Pull the bait out of the fish's mouth so you can
  actually see where the hook is sitting before you reach for it.
- **Enter through the gill opening, not the mouth.** Put the pliers in
  through the gill opening, look down inside, grab the hook, and pull it
  back out that way.
- **The point of the method:** it avoids "turning the fish inside out" —
  i.e. dragging the hook back up through the throat and stomach, which is
  what damages a fish you mean to release.

## Measuring a fish you may release

- **Mouth closed.** Close the fish's mouth before laying it on the measuring
  board — an open mouth adds length that isn't there.
- **Hold, don't pull.** Lay the fish on the board and hold it in place;
  don't stretch it against the stop to make length.

## Legal size

- **Regulatory (California / CDFW, as reported in the 2022-04-23 source — a
  regulation, not fishery knowledge; verify current CDFW rules before you
  keep or release):** the calico bass minimum was stated on camera as
  **14 inches**. The same figure is logged with fuller context in
  [calico bass](../species/calico-bass.md); this note does not restate the
  citation, it points at it.

**Not carried from this source:** the video also states that a legal-size
fish *must* be killed ("if he's 15 inches … you got to kill him, that's what
it says right in the book"). No such must-keep rule is stated anywhere else
in this corpus, and it is not how a minimum-size regulation works — a size
limit sets a floor for what *may* be kept, not an obligation to keep. Logged
here as a claim seen and deliberately not carried, per the same treatment
given other implausible on-camera claims.
