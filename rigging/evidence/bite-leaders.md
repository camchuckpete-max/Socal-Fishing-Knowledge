# Evidence — Bluefin Bite Leaders

Per-source provenance backing [Bluefin Bite Leaders](../bite-leaders.md).

## When to use

- `42A8Owhc8fw` (Ray Sharifi, Dirty Hookers, "Surface Irons & Sand Bass! +
  Tackle Update!," 2023-08-02; `ray-sharifi` registered — Baja/Cortez claims
  eligible for high, but this is a SoCal-bight claim so it caps at medium):
  casting-metal bite-leader variant for a bluefin foamer, ~2 ft/150–200 lb,
  framed as an optional confidence add over a bare top-shot-to-lure
  connection, not a requirement.

## Parameters

- `kO_BqzUYayc` (BDOutdoors, "How To Make Bite Leaders: Most Critical Part of
  a Bluefin Setup," 2024-07-22; presenter not named on camera, unregistered —
  medium confidence): primary source for the base build — the 2–2.5 ft length
  cap and why (swivel won't clear the guides), 130 lb fluoro over 80–100 lb
  braid, the 1.6 crimp/1.6 chafe-sleeve sizing, crimp-pressure and tag
  mechanics, the main-line swivel connection, the direct-to-ring vs
  split-ring jig connection, and the failure-modes list.
- `6wbO7qaU3sI` (SearcherSportfishing, tackle-shop demo, "Tackle Tip Thursday
  Vol. 28 (Leaders)," 2018-12-27; unregistered channel, speaker not named on
  camera — medium confidence): flat-fall-class jig leader build, swivel top /
  heavy-duty Owner-brand split ring bottom; no pound-test or crimp-sleeve
  size stated on camera.
- `KGrussv1s3U` (SearcherSportfishing, tackle-shop demo, "Tackle Tip Thursday
  Vol. 14," 2018-09-26; unregistered channel, speaker not named on camera —
  medium confidence): earlier flat-fall leader build on the same channel,
  states the pound-test and crimp size the Vol. 28 build above omits — 24 in
  of 200 lb fluorocarbon (brand name spoken on camera renders unrecognizably
  in captions, asr-uncertain), size 1 crimp sleeve, ~1/8 in flared/uncrimped
  ends. Shows only the jig-end loop, not the main-line-side connection.
- `j37zxs33gws` (West Coast Jiggerz, "Knife Jigging Basics for Bluefin Tuna
  with West Coast Jiggerz," BDOutdoors channel, 2023-05-08; no
  channel-registration or confidence detail given on camera in this note's
  source clip): the direct-tie, no-crimp leader school — 80–100 lb braid, FG
  knot to a 15–20 ft mono/fluoro top shot tied direct to the jig. Corroborated
  in [knife/speed jigging](../../techniques/knife-jigging.md) and its
  evidence file, which also logs the other leader-connection builds fished
  alongside these two.
