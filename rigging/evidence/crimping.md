# Evidence — Crimping — Sleeve Crimps for Heavy Leaders

Per-source provenance backing [Crimping — Sleeve Crimps for Heavy
Leaders](../crimping.md).

## When to use

- `3zXcrGsIL-c` (Billy, Fisherman's Landing tackle shop, via
  SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 116 — Crimping,"
  2020-09-10; unregistered channel, single mention — medium confidence):
  demonstrated on 400 lb line (branded "P-Line" on camera; mono vs. fluoro
  not stated), a big-game/long-range leader class, with a High Seas-brand
  crimp (heard on the audio as "A2"; exact sizing designation unconfirmed —
  asr-uncertain) and a matching chafe/chafing sleeve sized for that test.
- `fgTmUq78ofQ` (Art Taylor/Mike, SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday
  Vol. 147 — Live at Turner's Outdoorsman," 2021-05-14;
  `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel, medium confidence, single
  mention of this specific reasoning): why crimping is the standard
  connection over a tied knot at the top end of heavy leader classes
  (200 lb example); crimp sleeve matched to a swivel, lighter main line
  (80 lb example) tied to that swivel with a San Diego jam knot; leader sold
  by the roll (25 ft roll of 200 lb example) typically labeled with its
  matching crimp size.
- `jQzOdmP0zoQ` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 144 —
  Hollow and Solid Line," 2021-04-22; registered channel/series, single
  mention — medium confidence): a second, shorter framing for the
  top-shot-to-mainline connection specifically — trip length (10-day
  example) plus gear size as the trigger to switch from a knot to a crimp,
  rather than a stated pound-test threshold.

## Parameters

- `3zXcrGsIL-c` — same source as above: build order (crimp sleeve, then
  chafe sleeve, then hook/swivel, tag end passed back through the crimp
  sleeve), ball-the-tag-end mechanic, flared-ends rule (~a couple mm per
  end), firm-handshake crimp pressure, two presses per sleeve (one per end),
  and the don't-torque-test-it-against-the-boat caution.
- `o6Sawz5S7bk` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 73 (Wahoo
  Leaders)," 2019-11-07; `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel/series,
  single mention — medium confidence): wire crimped fully tight at both
  ends with no flared "valley," stated on camera as explicitly different
  from the mono/fluoro build. Demonstrated building a wahoo leader from
  275 lb steel wire with a crimp sleeve and swivel at each end.
- `S80GRyuAbLY` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 160 (Wahoo
  Wire Leaders)," 2021-11-04; registered channel/series, single mention —
  medium confidence): match sleeve number to the wire's stated pound test;
  example given is 60 lb wire → size A2 sleeve; an undersized sleeve (A2 on
  40 lb wire) crimps loose and lets the connection slip under fighting drag.
- `_Z4yMtrYgeA` ("Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 123 (Wahoo Leader)," 2020-10-29):
  duplicate-of `S80GRyuAbLY` — the same demonstration re-cut under an
  earlier volume number/title, not an independent second source.
- `uWGGeDOprsE` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tips for Alijos Rocks and The
  Ridge," 2009-08-25; speaker not identified on camera;
  `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel/series): an earlier (2009),
  independently sourced citation of the same match-crimp-to-wire-test rule,
  for wahoo casting/trolling-lure wire specifically; no specific size
  pairing given (unlike the 60 lb → A2 example above) — the advice given is
  to ask the tackle store for the crimp that matches whatever wire is
  bought. Predates, and now corroborates as repeated doctrine alongside, the
  `S80GRyuAbLY`/`_Z4yMtrYgeA` citation above. Full trip-tackle context in
  [Searcher five-outfit quiver — 7-day trip to Alijos Rocks and "The
  Ridge"](../../tackle/searcher-alijos-rocks-ridge-7-day-quiver.md#terminal-tackle).
