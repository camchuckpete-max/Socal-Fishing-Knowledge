# Evidence — FG Knot & Albright Knot

Per-source provenance backing [FG Knot & Albright Knot](../fg-and-albright.md).

## Parameters

- `Bn9fRKUmQ-U` (Norm Fujimoto, Izorline, on SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip
  Thursday Vol. 48," 2019-05-16; unregistered channel — medium confidence):
  the earliest Albright demo in this corpus, about five months before the
  spaced-then-overlap version below. Demonstrates the double-back wrap
  variant logged in the main note.
- `-BO1lMCTamg` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday," 2019-10-10;
  unregistered channel — medium confidence): introduces the
  spaced-then-overlap wrap pattern, stating on camera that spacing the wraps
  then overlapping them on the return pass "cinches everything down" and
  ends up "a little bit smaller" than a one-way wrap. Ties 9 wraps on camera.
- `5472APCgym8` (Ryan, SearcherSportfishing, 2021-05-20; unregistered
  channel — medium confidence): repeats the same spaced-then-overlap
  pattern about 19 months after `-BO1lMCTamg`, naming it the "Modified
  Albright," giving the same reasoning in different words — leaving space
  "helps it cinch down a little bit better at the end" — and stating 7-to-10
  wraps as the working range while tying 7 on camera.
- `2fwj24S9S-o` (Rick, Fisherman's Landing Tackle, on SearcherSportfishing,
  2023-03-16; unregistered channel/speaker — medium confidence): frames the
  tie generally as joining two lines of dissimilar diameter/strength rather
  than braid-specific, demoed on 30 lb main line to a heavier leader. States
  there is "no dead-set number" of wraps, just wrap "until I got a good
  looking length of my knot" — six or seven shown on camera.
- `shZCjX2-fkI` (Captain Armando, SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Talk Live:
  Bluefin Tuna," 2021-01-14; `searcher-sportfishing` registered channel,
  single mention — medium confidence): calls the 6-7-down/6-7-back-up
  pattern on camera "the easiest knot to tie." Also confirms the crew ties
  the identical Albright straight onto hollow spectra on this boat's kite
  outfits, rather than needle-splicing the hollow line.
