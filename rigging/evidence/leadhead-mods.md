## Squid rigging for white seabass

- `Y2bXn44lfqo` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-08-30, Catalina Island): the leadhead
  was guessed at 3/4 oz on camera, corrected on the water to 5/8 oz.
- `Y2bXn44lfqo` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-08-30, Catalina Island): the white
  seabass on this trip were caught and released alive for a broodstock
  program, which is why the crew cared about mouth- vs. gut-hooking when
  contrasting the active leadhead-and-squid style with a passive
  dropper-loop soak strung with an Ahi twist (J-style) hook.
- `xFS3MW4GpDU` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-08-23, Catalina Island — earlier leg
  of the same trip series; crew Jock and Charlie Albright): described most
  of their white seabass fishing as "broad in hand" — actively casting
  leadhead and squid — contrasted with anglers who fish fake grounds, deep
  water, or a kelp edge/reef with a dropper loop dropped and left to sit;
  compared the style to active largemouth bass fishing.
- `xFS3MW4GpDU`: unregistered channel, medium confidence. The leader
  connection/rig name for the 50 lb braid / 30 lb fluorocarbon build was
  garbled by auto-captions ("uh 30 30 50 braid") and omitted from the main
  note per the ASR hazard rule; asr-uncertain(leader connection name).
