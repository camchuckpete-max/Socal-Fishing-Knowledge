# Perfection Loop Knot — Sliding-Loop Hook Tie

An "old school" terminal knot that ties a **free-sliding loop** rather than a
fixed eye — the hook rides on the loop and can pivot and slide back and forth
on it, "kind of like a ring" (`_rf1TqLh1yE`). Kept as its own note rather than
folded into the loop-knot entry in [Essential SoCal Knots](essential-knots.md)
— that note is already near its length-split threshold, and this is a
mechanically distinct loop-forming knot with its own name.

## When to use it

- Brought back from 1980s trolling-hook and trolling-lure rigging; usable for
  any application, demonstrated here for live bait (`_rf1TqLh1yE`).
- **Live-bait rationale:** pinning a live sardine on the sliding/pivoting
  loop "maybe... swims a little bit better" than on a fixed-eye tie
  (`_rf1TqLh1yE`) — a stated impression, not a confirmed mechanism. ⚠ Flagged
  gap — no corpus source: mechanism (no stated reason the extra freedom of
  motion would improve swim action over a fixed eye).
- Stated as "a very strong knot" (`_rf1TqLh1yE`).
- **Loop size is adjustable:** a smaller first loop shrinks the finished
  ring, a larger one grows it — size it to the hook eye and how freely the
  hook should swing (`_rf1TqLh1yE`).
- **Same knot, a different job elsewhere in this KB:** also used rope-to-rope
  as the loop-to-loop connection joining a hoop net's main rope to its spare
  "rope shot" extension section — see [hoop
  netting](../techniques/hoop-netting.md#rope-length-match-it-dont-over-carry-it-qs9oesh3b_w).
  That use ties rope, not a hook, but is the same named knot.

## Parameters

- **Mechanic:** start with about 8–10 in of tag line. Form a first
  overhand-style loop, pass the tag through the hook eye, then bring the hook
  down close to the tying hand. Form a second loop and grasp both loops
  between the fingers, bring the hook over the top of a third loop and back
  through the first loop, then cinch down. Tied correctly, the two lines
  running through the finished loop sit parallel, and the knot draws down
  into a clean loop/ring in the line (`_rf1TqLh1yE`). ⚠ asr-uncertain(loop
  count and hand mechanics) — thin auto-generated captions on a 168-second
  clip; watch the source video for the visual steps rather than re-tying from
  this paraphrase alone.
- **Sizing judgment:** the first loop sets the finished ring size — smaller
  for a tighter, more fixed-feeling loop, larger for more hook play
  (`_rf1TqLh1yE`).
- No species, region, or specific rig/application beyond live bait and
  trolling is stated in this clip.
