# Bait-Tank Time-Saver — Artificial "Double[-]8 Octopus" on the Rock-Cod Rig (Tackle Express)

Tackle Express (`IcS7QbJlSDA`, "Stop wasting time at the bait tank when the
bite is ON!," uploaded 2026-03-25, 36 s; unregistered channel — medium
confidence). **Same clip, fuller context:** this short is a re-cut of a
passage inside the same channel's longer video, `qh3hL2Dt3HY`, "Saltwater
Fishing Basics for Southern California" (uploaded 2024-01-12, presenter named
on camera as "Harry") — word-for-word identical narration, so this is **not
independent confirmation**, just the same claim carried by two video_ids in
the corpus. The longer video is what supplies the rock-cod/dropper-loop
framing below; the 36 s short alone doesn't establish what rig or species this
applies to.

- **The setup this applies to:** a standard rock-cod
  [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) rig fished with a torpedo
  sinker — including a pre-made rig sold with both swivels and hooks already
  tied on, captions rendering the brand **"Pine rock cod rig[s]"** (read as
  **P-Line**, the same brand already resolved in [rockfish leader
  line](tackle-express-rockfish-leader-line.md) from this channel). The
  default bait on that rig is a piece of squid off the bait tank.
- **The problem:** on a hot bite, walking from the bow back to the bait tank
  for more squid between drops costs fishable time.
- **The fix — carry an artificial substitute in the tackle box, not the
  tank.** Auto-captions render the product name **"double[-]a[/-8]
  octopus[es]"** — **asr-uncertain**, not confirmed against a catalog (read
  here as a soft-plastic octopus/squid-imitation bait, packaged or sized as a
  "double" set; the exact product isn't resolved). Thread a couple onto the
  hook in place of squid.
- **Optional scent add:** "match with a little bit of squid for some scent,
  but you don't always need that" — a small piece of real squid fished
  alongside the artificial trailer, stated as a scent boost, not a
  requirement.
- **When to reach for it (as stated on camera):** fishing from the bow, far
  from the bait tank, on a fast bite for **reds** (rockfish/vermilion) — the
  artificial substitute, with or without the squid scent add, is offered
  specifically to keep a line fishing through a hot bite instead of breaking
  it to walk to the tank.

## A third Tackle Express clip — the same rig named for rockfish, torpedo sinker confirmed (Q6ACpkS93sk)
