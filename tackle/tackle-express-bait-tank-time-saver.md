- **The setup this applies to:** a standard rock-cod
  [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) rig fished with a torpedo
  sinker — including a pre-made rig sold with both swivels and hooks already

- **The problem:** on a hot bite, walking from the bow back to the bait tank
  for more squid between drops costs fishable time.
- **The fix — carry an artificial substitute in the tackle box, not the
  tank.** Auto-captions render the product name **"double[-]a[/-8]
  octopus[es]"** — **asr-uncertain**, not confirmed against a catalog (read

  "double" set; the exact product isn't resolved). Thread a couple onto the
  hook in place of squid.
- **Optional scent add:** "match with a little bit of squid for some scent,
  but you don't always need that" — a small piece of real squid fished
  alongside the artificial trailer, stated as a scent boost, not a
  requirement.
- **When to reach for it (as stated on camera):** fishing from the bow, far
  from the bait tank, on a fast bite for **reds** (rockfish/vermilion) — the
  artificial substitute, with or without the squid scent add, is offered
  specifically to keep a line fishing through a hot bite instead of breaking
