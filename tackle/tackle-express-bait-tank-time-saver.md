# Bait-Tank Time-Savers — Artificial Octopus and Pre-Scented Gulp on the Rock-Cod Dropper Loop (Tackle Express)

Walking from the bow to the bait tank for more squid between drops costs
fishable time on a hot rock-cod bite. Two artificial substitutes fish the
same standard rock-cod double dropper loop without that walk: a soft-plastic
octopus/squid trailer, and pre-scented Berkley Gulp soft plastic. Both thread
onto the hook in place of a fresh piece of bait and let a line keep fishing
through a fast bite instead of breaking it to re-bait from the tank.

## Soft-plastic octopus/squid trailer

- **The rig:** a standard rock-cod [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md)
  fished with a torpedo sinker, including the [premade P-Line rock cod
  rig](tackle-express-premade-rock-cod-rig.md) sold with both swivels and
  hooks already tied on. The rig's default bait is a piece of squid off the
  bait tank (`IcS7QbJlSDA`, `qh3hL2Dt3HY`).
- **The substitute:** thread a couple of soft-plastic octopus/squid-imitation
  baits onto the hook in place of squid — captions render the product
  "double-8 octopus" (asr-uncertain; not confirmed against a catalog)
  (`IcS7QbJlSDA`).
- **Optional scent add:** pair the artificial trailer with a small piece of
  real squid for scent — a boost, not a requirement (`IcS7QbJlSDA`).
- **When to reach for it:** fishing from the bow, far from the bait tank, on
  a fast bite for reds — rockfish/vermilion (`IcS7QbJlSDA`).

## Pre-scented Berkley Gulp soft plastic

- **The rig:** a double dropper loop, sinker sized to depth and conditions —
  a torpedo sinker, per a separate same-channel clip that names rockfish as
  the target (`Q6ACpkS93sk`) — fished with **1/0 Owner Aki Twist hooks**
  (`Q4WWkbc9nag`, `S9sQ8Vg8-5o`, `eqRMpfcuM2s`, `Q6ACpkS93sk`). The `Q6ACpkS93sk`
  clip offers the Aki Twist as one hook choice among others ("a choice of hook
  of whatever you guys would like to fish") rather than a fixed pick.
- **The bait:** a scented soft-plastic **Berkley Gulp** bait — captions
  render "the Berkley Gulp we have swimming mullets. These ones happen to be
  the grub series" (asr-uncertain whether this names one product or two: a
  Gulp Swimming Mullet offered in a "grub" pack, or a separate Swimming
  Mullet and Grub shown together) — threaded on in place of a sardine,
  anchovy, or a piece of squid fished for scent (`eqRMpfcuM2s`).
- **The pitch:** keep fishing without breaking to re-bait from the tank —
  "catch fish after fish" on one pre-scented bait instead of a fresh piece
  between drops (`Q4WWkbc9nag`).
- No species is named on camera in the `Q4WWkbc9nag` clip itself; the
  separate `Q6ACpkS93sk` clip names SoCal rockfish for the same rig and hook
  but does not itself confirm the species `Q4WWkbc9nag` was shot for.
