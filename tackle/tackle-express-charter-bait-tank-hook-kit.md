# Charter Bait-Tank Hook & Leader Kit — Whatever's in the Tank (Tackle Express)

On a SoCal charter, the bait tank's contents — sardine, anchovy, squid, or
mackerel — aren't known until the boat is underway, so the fix is a hook and
leader assortment sized to cover whichever bait shows up, across island
yellowtail/calico, kelp-paddy dorado/yellowtail, offshore bluefin/yellowfin,
and bottom fishing (`3xx_vES0kdo`). This is a parameter checklist, not a
selection framework — the underlying circle-vs-J and bait-sets-the-size
doctrine lives in [hooks](hooks.md).

## Islands yellowtail — Owner Flyliner, ring vs. non-ring

- **Owner Flyliner hook**, ring or non-ring, for yellowtail at the islands —
  ring vs. non-ring is angler preference, with a stated preference here for
  non-ring, no reason given (`IUuP8gGaAAo`). See
  [hooks — circle vs. J](hooks.md#circle-vs-j--the-core-decision-swrsycmt4tw-m2q22sppkem)
  for the broader ring-vs-non-ring split: one source's personal "old school"
  non-ring pick, against a countervailing view that a ring may let the bait
  swim more freely.
- The same clip names a hook captioned **"Aki Twist"** for squid and seabass
  (asr-uncertain — exact model unresolved). It may be the **Owner Aki hook**,
  logged at **1/0** in
  [hooks — Owner Aki hook](hooks.md#owner-aki-hook--season-stock-up-and-channel-islands-sizing),
  or the **"Oy Twist"** cited below under Squid — unresolved between the two
  (`IUuP8gGaAAo`). A separate clip pairs the same "Aki Twist" spelling with
  **1/0 Owner** hooks on a double [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md)
  for a scented packaged bait, no species named on camera (`Q4WWkbc9nag`; see
  [Bait-Tank Time-Saver](tackle-express-bait-tank-time-saver.md#pre-scented-berkley-gulp-soft-plastic))
  — the matching 1/0 size is a lean toward the Owner Aki hook, not a
  confirmed match.
- Rock fishing: gorilla hooks — see
  [Bottom fishing](#bottom-fishing-rockfish-whitefish-sheephead), below
  (`IUuP8gGaAAo`).
- Tuna fishing: Mutu circle hooks — see
  [Tuna — flyline](#tuna-bluefin-yellowfin--flyline), below (`IUuP8gGaAAo`).

## Anchovy — small fly-liner hooks

- **Owner, size 4**, stepping up to **size 2** for larger anchovies
  (`3xx_vES0kdo`).
- A second size-4 anchovy hook, captioned **"OSHAY must add"** (asr-uncertain
  — model unresolved) (`3xx_vES0kdo`).

## Sardine — ringed fly-liner

- **Size 2 ring fly-liner**, hooked in the shoulder or the butt to swim the
  bait away from the boat (see [fishing live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md)
  for the mechanism) — for a kelp paddy (dorado, yellowtail) or the islands
  (yellowtail, calico) (`3xx_vES0kdo`). The same size-2 pick is independently
  named the **Owner Ringed Flyliner** in
  [hooks](hooks.md#owner-ringed-flyliner--coronado-islands-and-channel-islands-sizing).

## Tuna (bluefin, yellowfin) — flyline

- A **ringed Mutu-style circle hook** ("ring mu"), **size 4 or 2**, for
  flylining bluefin/yellowfin on the surface (`3xx_vES0kdo`) — consistent
  with the Owner Mutu circle-hook doctrine in
  [hooks — size](hooks.md#size--bait-first-fish-second).
- Paired with **75 lb Blue Label fluorocarbon** — heavier than the 25 lb
  flyline-leader standard below, preserved as stated (`3xx_vES0kdo`). "Blue
  Label" is Seaguar's line; the same channel names it in full as **Seaguar
  Blue Label** in the
  [Jarry Rig leader](tackle-express-night-bluefin-tg-jig-rig.md#leader--the-jarry-rig).

## Squid — dropper loop and slider

- **Owner**, captioned **"Oy Twist"** (asr-uncertain — exact model
  unresolved), sized **5/0 and 7/0** for small and big squid respectively —
  corroborated across two clips from the same channel (`3xx_vES0kdo`,
  `Eo_bA8IWvyU`); the 5/0–7/0 read comes from `Eo_bA8IWvyU`'s caption "an Oy
  Twist hook between 50 to 70," resolved with the same slash-dropping ASR
  pattern that renders "1/0" as "10" elsewhere in this corpus. Possibly the
  same hook as the "Aki Twist" cited above for squid/seabass — unresolved.
- **Weight for dropper loop:** a **10–12 oz torpedo sinker** (`3xx_vES0kdo`);
  a second source gives **8–12 oz** for the same rig (`Eo_bA8IWvyU`).
- **Sliders: 1/8 oz to 1/2 oz**, sized to current, for flylining squid,
  sardine, and mackerel — gets the bait deeper and off the surface when
  birds are picking baits fished up top (`3xx_vES0kdo`). See
  [sliding sinker](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md).

## Sea bass — squid vs. fin-bait backup

[White seabass](../species/white-seabass.md) eat squid, but also fin bait —
sardines and mackerel. When squid isn't available (weather, or squid simply
aren't around), fin sardine/mackerel is the backup, so a J-hook assortment
sized for fin bait matters, not an afterthought (`9MzNNwHiBXs`).

- **Owner Flyliner hooks, 1/0 and 2**, for sardine/mackerel 5–7 in —
  corroborated across two clips from the same channel (`9MzNNwHiBXs`,
  `Eo_bA8IWvyU`).
- Bait sets the size: bigger or smaller bait calls for a different hook size
  — see [hooks — size](hooks.md#size--bait-first-fish-second).
- Also fishes on a [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) — the same
  hooks serve both a flylined and a dropper-loop presentation
  (`9MzNNwHiBXs`).

## Bottom fishing (rockfish, whitefish, sheephead)

- **Size 1 and size 3/0 "gorilla lights"** — Owner Gorilla Light hooks,
  carried for bottom/ground fishing to fill the sacks (`3xx_vES0kdo`). The
  same product is logged at **size 2 and 4** for a 15 lb finesse bluefin rig
  in [hooks — a finesse-rig hook ladder by grade](hooks.md#a-finesse-rig-hook-ladder-by-grade)
  — a different application and different sizes.

## Leader

- All the fly-liner hooks above fish on **fluorocarbon**.
- **25 lb Blue Label** is the standard flyline leader for tuna and the
  islands (`3xx_vES0kdo`).
- Carry heavier fluoro too — **40 and 50 lb** — to feed line to a fish eating
  heavy, improving hookup and landing odds over forcing everything onto the
  25 lb standard (`3xx_vES0kdo`).
