## Her pick

- **Reel:** **Accurate Valiant Superlight, 500 Narrow** — her own words: "a
  setup that I suggest for the ladies that I like to fish, and many of them
  do." Consistent with the sized-Valiant naming pattern already logged
  elsewhere in this KB (e.g. Valiant 600 Narrow, Valiant 800, Valiant 2600
  Narrow — see [rod & reel selection](rod-and-reel-selection.md) and
  [Searcher three-outfit minimum
  quiver](searcher-three-outfit-minimum-quiver.md)), here at the light end of
  that size range.
- **Stated reason:** lightweight enough to hold all day without arm
  fatigue — "you can hold it all day, your arm doesn't get tired."
- **Line:** spooled with braid and a leader; named as capable of fishing 30,
  40, or 50 lb test (three line-class options named on camera, application
  not specified beyond "you can use").
- Framed as popular specifically with her women customers — "many of the
  women have purchased this since it's lightweight" — but the stated
  mechanism (lighter reel body, less arm fatigue over a long day) isn't
  itself gender-specific.
