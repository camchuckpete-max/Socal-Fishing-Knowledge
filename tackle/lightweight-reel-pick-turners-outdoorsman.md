# Lightweight All-Day Reel Pick — Accurate Valiant Superlight, 500 Narrow

A tackle shop's lightweight-reel pick for anglers who want to hold a
conventional outfit all day without arm fatigue: the **Accurate Valiant
Superlight, 500 Narrow**. It sits at the light end of the sized-Valiant
lineup already documented in this KB — Valiant 600 Narrow, 800, and 2600
Narrow (see [rod & reel selection](rod-and-reel-selection.md), [Searcher
three-outfit minimum quiver](searcher-three-outfit-minimum-quiver.md), and
[bluefin 50–80 lb bait outfit ladder](bluefin-50-80lb-bait-outfit-ladder.md)).

## The pick

- **Reel:** Accurate Valiant Superlight, 500 Narrow — "a setup that I
  suggest for the ladies that I like to fish, and many of them do"
  (`t8GP_-DMlSU`).
- **Reason:** a lighter reel body holds up over a full day without arm
  fatigue — "you can hold it all day, your arm doesn't get tired"
  (`t8GP_-DMlSU`).
- **Line:** braid over a leader, rated for 30, 40, or 50 lb test; the
  application isn't specified beyond "you can use" for each (`t8GP_-DMlSU`).

The shop reports the reel selling specifically to its women customers —
"many of the women have purchased this since it's lightweight"
(`t8GP_-DMlSU`) — but the stated mechanism, a lighter reel body reducing arm
fatigue over a long day, applies to any angler holding a rod all day, not
only to that customer group.
