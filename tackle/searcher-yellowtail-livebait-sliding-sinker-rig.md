# Searcher Yellowtail Live-Bait Sliding-Sinker Rig — 30 lb Class

The standard bread-and-butter live-bait rig for yellowtail on both local and
long-range San Diego trips (`yGXSrUauo2w`). It runs the same 30 lb-class
rod/reel/line as the [Searcher 30 lb large-tuna
outfit](searcher-30lb-large-tuna-outfit.md), but swaps in a J hook and a
sliding egg sinker in place of that rig's circle hook straight to the
leader. Built for yellowtail in the 12–22 lb range; no heavier-grade version
is given.

## Applies to

Yellowtail in the **12–22 lb range** — the stated ceiling for this build, "if
the [yellowtail] are not too big... maybe in the 12 to 22 pound range"
(`yGXSrUauo2w`). No heavier-grade build is given on camera.

## The base outfit — shared with the 30 lb tuna rig

- **Reel:** Penn Fathom 15, two-speed.
- **Rod:** 8 ft, rated 15–40 lb.
- **Line:** 30 lb top shot (mono).

This rod/reel/line combination matches the [Searcher 30 lb large-tuna
outfit](searcher-30lb-large-tuna-outfit.md) (`yGXSrUauo2w`) — the same 8 ft,
15–40 lb-rated rod, the same 30 lb top shot, and the same Fathom 15 reel
family. **The two builds diverge only at the hook and terminal end**: the
tuna outfit runs a circle hook straight to the leader, while this yellowtail
build swaps in a J hook and a sliding sinker (see both below).

## Hook — J over circle, sized to the bait

- For yellowtail up to about 22 lb, a J hook outfishes a circle hook; a
  circle hook still works, it's just not the preference (`yGXSrUauo2w`).
- **Size:** roughly 2/0, stepping up to 4/0 if the bait is large
  (`yGXSrUauo2w`). Captions render these as "20" and "40" — read here as the
  aught sizes 2/0 and 4/0, since a size-20/size-40 hook doesn't exist in
  this fishery, consistent with the 2/0 yellowtail J-hook figure already
  logged in
  [hooks](hooks.md#picking-by-whats-biting-mid-trip).
- Corroborates the J-hooks-for-yellowtail doctrine in [hooks — circle vs.
  J, the core
  decision](hooks.md#circle-vs-j--the-core-decision--) —
  reaction eaters that run at the boat; see that note for the full
  cross-source reasoning.

## Sliding sinker — straight to the hook, no stopper

- An egg sinker slides directly down to the hook — "slides unfettered right
  down to the hook," with no bead, stopper, or other hardware needed
  (`yGXSrUauo2w`).
- This is a leaner build than the Carolina-rig format documented as the
  general sliding-sinker pattern in [sliding sinker — building the
  rig](../techniques/sliding-sinker.md#building-the-rig) (egg sinker → bead
  → swivel → leader → hook) — no swivel or separate leader section is shown
  or described on camera here.
- **Weight:** 2, 3, or 4 oz, chosen for the day's conditions — step up for
  more wind or when the fish are sitting deeper (`yGXSrUauo2w`). Above 4 oz,
  a different rig works better (not detailed on camera — a forward
  reference to unaired content). ⚠ asr-uncertain: a garbled caption
  fragment ("this little one is just a one") appears just before the 2/3/4
  oz figures while multiple sinkers are shown on camera, which could
  indicate a lighter ~1 oz option was also demonstrated; too unclear to
  carry as a fourth weight, so only the clearly stated 2–4 oz range is kept
  here.
- Adjust weight through the day as the fish "change their mind," rather
  than treating the day's starting weight as fixed (`yGXSrUauo2w`).

## Why this rig

- Gets the bait away from the boat (`yGXSrUauo2w`).
- Gets the bait under bonita (or similar) working the surface and in front
  of the yellowtail faster than an unweighted presentation — the stated
  reason to reach for this rig over a flylined bait specifically when
  surface bycatch is intercepting baits before they reach the target fish
  (`yGXSrUauo2w`).

## Weight contrast with other yellowtail sliding-sinker weights

This rig's 2–4 oz working range runs notably heavier than the ~1/4–1/2 oz
egg-sinker weights logged for island yellowtail (Cedros, San Benito, and
other SoCal/Baja islands) in [sliding sinker — evidence, Cedros/San Benito
island
observations](../techniques/evidence/sliding-sinker.md#yellowtail--cedros--san-benito--socal-islands).
No location is given beyond "aboard the Searcher" (`yGXSrUauo2w`). ⚠
Flagged gap — no corpus source: whether the difference tracks San Diego
local/long-range fishing versus island-specific conditions, or a different
convention entirely, is unresolved.
