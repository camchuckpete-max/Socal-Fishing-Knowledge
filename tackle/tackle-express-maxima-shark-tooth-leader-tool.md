# Maxima Shark Tooth — Leader-Spool Band & Cutter (Tackle Express)

A leader-spool storage tool: a plastic band that wraps over spooled line to
keep it from unspooling into a tangle, with a built-in cutter, sold three to
a pack (`sPgP0aBUQm8`). This is a straight product-feature pitch with no
technique or decision content, capped at **low** confidence in full.

## What's claimed

- **Use case: managing leader line on a spool** — bulk spools, small leader
  spools (fluorocarbon leader specifically named), or "just regular spools
  when you just need a little bit of line" (`sPgP0aBUQm8`). Stated caveat:
  **not meant for spooling an entire reel** — a related but separate point
  from [spooling line — leader material isn't spool
  line](spooling-line-tension-and-twist.md#leader-material-isnt-spool-line)
  (don't fill a reel with leader-grade material).
- **Mechanism.** Line feeds through a small hole in the plastic band; the band
  wraps over the spooled line and holds it in place, keeping it from
  unspooling into a tangle when stored — leader spools kept loose in
  Plano-style boxes otherwise "turns into a giant mess" (`sPgP0aBUQm8`).
- **Dispensing.** Pull off however much line is needed, then cut it with the
  **built-in cutter on the tool itself** (wrap the line around the tool and
  cut). After cutting, the band leaves a **tag end** so the next pull is easy
  to find (`sPgP0aBUQm8`).
