# Maxima Shark Tooth — Leader-Spool Band & Cutter (Tackle Express)

Tackle Express, "Tackle Express New Products May 5, 2019" (`sPgP0aBUQm8`,
uploaded 2019-05-01), presenter self-identified on camera as "Chad from Tackle
Express" — a ~90-second segment inside a six-product new-arrivals round-up. A
straight product-feature pitch with no technique or decision content — capped
at **low** confidence in full per the sponsored/promotional rule, on top of
`tackle-express` being an unregistered channel. Unlike most of the other
products in this video (freshwater/bass-technique tackle, skipped — see
[HOW Co split-ring pliers](tackle-express-how-co-split-ring-pliers.md) for
what else was and wasn't pulled from this source), the presenter's stated use
case ties this one directly to leader management, a topic this KB already
covers at length.

## What's claimed

- **Maxima Shark Tooth — a leader-spool management tool.** Presenter has used
  it "for years"; states the product changed hands between manufacturers over
  time and is now made by Maxima. Sold **three to a pack**.
- **Use case: managing leader line on a spool** — bulk spools, small leader
  spools (the presenter names fluorocarbon leader spools specifically), or
  "just regular spools when you just need a little bit of line." Stated
  caveat: **not meant for spooling an entire reel** — a separate point from,
  but consistent in spirit with, the caution already logged in
  [spooling line — leader material isn't spool line](spooling-line-tension-and-twist.md#leader-material-isnt-spool-line-tackle-express-gn2yquuu6em-are-you-spooling-your-line-wrong-watch-this-fishing-fix-2026-06-15-unregistered-channel-single-mention--medium-confidence)
  (a different Tackle Express clip's related but distinct point — don't fill
  a reel with leader-grade material).
- **Mechanism.** Line feeds through a small hole in a plastic band; the band
  wraps over the spooled line and holds it in place, keeping it from
  unspooling into a tangle when stored — the presenter's own comparison is
  leader spools kept loose in Plano-style boxes "turns into a giant mess"
  without it.
- **Dispensing.** Pull off however much line is needed, then cut it with a
  **built-in cutter on the tool itself** (wrap the line around the tool and
  cut). After cutting, the band leaves a **tag end** so the next pull is easy
  to find.
