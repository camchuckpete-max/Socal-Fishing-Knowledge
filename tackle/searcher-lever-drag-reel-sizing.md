# Searcher Tackle Tip Thursday — Lever-Drag Two-Speed Reel Sizing (Penn, Searcher Sportfishing)

SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 78 (Lever Drags)," `ddataaVWoDc`,
2019-12-12. `searcher-sportfishing` is a registered channel/series, but this
episode is explicitly sponsor content — it opens by naming Penn as one of the
**Searcher's sponsors that supplies the boat's loaner-gear reels** — so, per
the registry's sponsored-claim caveat, the specific reel models here stay
**low confidence** (product endorsement); only the general size-to-application
pairing logic would qualify for the channel's usual medium. Split into its own
note rather than added to [rod & reel selection](rod-and-reel-selection.md)
(already over this repo's size guideline) or
[gear classes](gear-classes.md#rail-rod-class--what-makes-a-rod-a-rail-rod-mechanism)
— linked from both.

Two Penn Fathom two-speed lever-drag sizes, presented as the Searcher's own
loaner-gear picks at two points on the size ladder:

- **Penn Fathom 30, two-speed lever drag** — the smaller of the two, described
  on camera as the reasonably priced, smaller two-speed reel of the pair
  (captions render this "the reasonable priced smaller two feet reel" —
  read as "two-speed reel" given the segment is a two-speed-reel comparison
  throughout; flagged `asr-uncertain`). Line: **65 lb
  spectra/braid** backing to a **20–40 lb monofilament top shot** (captions
  render the range ambiguously — "anywhere from 25 to 20 to 40 pound
  monofilament" — read as a 20–40 lb top-shot window; the extra "25" is
  unresolved, flagged `asr-uncertain`). Cited for "great line capacity" and "a
  great drag system." No specific technique or species is named for this
  size beyond general boat loaner-gear duty.
- **Penn Fathom 60, two-speed lever drag** — paired with **Seeker rail rods**
  aboard the Searcher for "bigger fish." The place named on camera is
  captioned "the loop the island" — not resolved to an actual place name and
  not inferred (per the name-a-place-only-if-the-source-names-it rule).
  Line: **80 lb braid** to **up to 100 lb monofilament**; the captions also
  give a "probably 60 to 200 pound" figure whose referent (a line-strength
  range vs. a target-fish-grade range) is unclear from context and is left
  unresolved, flagged `asr-uncertain`. Cited as "great for fish and larger
  tuna," and separately named as usable for a [dropper
  loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) rig fishing yellowtail on the bottom.
  - **Corroborates** the Penn Fathom 60, two-speed reel already logged for
    rail-rod use in
    [gear classes — rail-rod class](gear-classes.md#rail-rod-class--what-makes-a-rod-a-rail-rod-mechanism)
    (Capt. Art Taylor, flat-fall bluefin rig, `an_uw-5pjfw`) — same reel
    model and rail-rod pairing, a different presenter and a different
    application (general "bigger fish" trolling/rail use plus bottom
    dropper-loop yellowtail here, vs. flat-fall bluefin there); kept side by
    side as two data points on the same reel, not merged into one claim.
- **Durability, both sizes:** cited as "durable" and used hard as **loaner
  gear** aboard the Searcher, "put to the test" by heavy use and weather
  exposure over the course of a season. Captions render the line "being
  both loner deer they get put to the test" — read as "loaner gear"; the
  second, "both"-paired use case is unresolved from the garbled captions
  and not asserted, flagged `asr-uncertain`.
