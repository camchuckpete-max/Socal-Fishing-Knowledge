# Daiwa Saltiga 300 Round Jigging Reel — Hyperdrive Digi Gear, Hyper Double Support, Spool Lock (Tackle Express)

Tackle Express, "This Jigging Reel is an Absolute TANK – Saltiga 300 Round"
(`Wk4wKFLbWEQ`), is a 142-second product-feature rundown for one reel model,
closing with a shop plug for tackleexpress.com. It is a straight promotional
feature list for one SKU, with no technique, sizing, or decision content, so
per the confidence rubric it is capped at low regardless of channel.

## What's claimed

- **Line class — 20 and 30 lb braided line.** Built as a light-line jigging
  reel around that line range; the drag and gear train are pitched as "way
  more capable than what the line will hold" (`Wk4wKFLbWEQ`).
- **Hyperdrive Digi Gear.** Larger gears with a finer cut, pitched as adding
  torque and power together with smoothness — turning the handle on camera
  draws the claim "the smoothest reel that we have probably ever put our
  hands on" (`Wk4wKFLbWEQ`). No gear ratio, retrieve rate, or comparative
  baseline is given.
- **Hyper Double Support.** Two bearings on the pinion gear, stated to take
  pressure off the spool and add torque and power (`Wk4wKFLbWEQ`). No
  bearing size, material, or total bearing count elsewhere in the reel is
  given.
- **Spool lock — not a clicker.** A dedicated feature for breaking off a
  bottom snag: click the lock in, pull, and the line parts cleanly, framed as
  a deliberate safety/utility feature distinct from a ratcheting clicker
  (`Wk4wKFLbWEQ`).
- **Hyper Armor Housing.** An aluminum body plus an aluminum side plate,
  pitched as producing a more rigid frame, summarized on camera as "a very
  very sturdy tank of a product in your hand" (`Wk4wKFLbWEQ`).
- **Hyperdrive Clutch.** A reinforced clutch mechanism, stiff and
  heavy-duty, engaging free spool and gear reliably; stated to be built so
  it's "never going to fail you while out on the water" (`Wk4wKFLbWEQ`). No
  engagement-force figure or comparative baseline is given.

No gear ratio, max drag figure, weight, line capacity, or price is given in
this clip (`Wk4wKFLbWEQ`). The clip does not state whether the "300 round"
designation refers to a size in Daiwa's own numbering, and no target species
or technique (speed-jig vs. slow-pitch vs. other) is named on camera.
