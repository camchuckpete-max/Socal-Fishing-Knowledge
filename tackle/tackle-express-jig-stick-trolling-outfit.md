# Jig Stick Doubles as a Trolling Outfit — Reel/Line Pairing and the Clicker Sequence (Tackle Express)

An 8-ft jig-stick outfit doubles as a trolling rod without rigging a second
setup: an 8-ft Calstar blank paired with a 20-size reel, spooled with 65 lb
braid for close to 400 yd of capacity, and a two-step clicker sequence
converts it from trolling to fighting mode on the bite (`BNp-gSOpWBw`). The
same outfit is fished at Cedros Island for yellowtail as a deckhand-style
rod with no reel seat, one piece of a four-rod quiver (`_C7TJoGT0nw`).

## The dual-purpose setup

- **8-ft jig stick** (Calstar blank) paired with a **20-size reel** — named
  on camera both as **Torium** and **Trinidad's**, captioned in this clip
  "Calstar Trinidad 20" (read as the Calstar rod paired with a Trinidad
  reel, not a single product name) (`BNp-gSOpWBw`).
- Spooled with **65 lb braid**, giving close to **400 yd** of capacity —
  sized to avoid getting spooled (`BNp-gSOpWBw`).
- **"That 20-size reel is kind of the size I like to fish"** for this
  dual-purpose role — a personal preference, not elaborated against other
  reel sizes (`BNp-gSOpWBw`).
- Running the same rod as both the jig stick and the trolling outfit is
  given no mechanism beyond "it was easy" (`BNp-gSOpWBw`). ⚠ Flagged gap —
  no corpus source: mechanism.
- Same **8–10 ft / 25–60 lb surface-iron/jig-stick class** as
  [gear classes — rod/reel classes](gear-classes.md#rod--reel-classes);
  this pairing adds trolling as a second application on that class, plus
  the specific reel models and line-capacity figures above.

## Clicker sequence — troll, then convert to fight mode

1. **Clicker on, drag backed off a couple of clicks** while trolling — the
   clicker signals a strike audibly, and the light drag keeps the rod from
   loading up while it's only dragging a lure (`BNp-gSOpWBw`).
2. **On a bite: advance the drag two to three clicks and turn the clicker
   off** — this converts the outfit from trolling to fighting the fish
   (`BNp-gSOpWBw`).

No drag numbers, a line class tested beyond the 65 lb braid above, or the
lure/species this was run for are given in this clip (`BNp-gSOpWBw`).

## Cedros application — reel clamp and quiver context

- Fished at **Cedros Island**, Baja Pacific (`baja-pacific-north`), for
  [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) (`_C7TJoGT0nw`).
- The rod is a **deckhand-style jig stick with no reel seat**, so a **reel
  clamp** is required to position the reel where the angler wants it
  (`_C7TJoGT0nw`).
- The Torium ships with a stock reel clamp, swapped here for a **DFP
  clamp** for more grip/surface area on the blank — a cosmetic/feel
  upgrade, not a functional requirement ("you don't need it, but it is
  just a nicer look") (`_C7TJoGT0nw`).
- The deckhand-rod/no-reel-seat/reel-clamp point and the
  Torium-stock-vs-DFP-clamp detail repeat verbatim in a separate re-cut
  short (`y9YlqMQh3BI`) — the same footage, not independent confirmation,
  so confidence stays at medium.
- This outfit is one rod in a four-rod Cedros quiver:
  [Tackle Express — Cedros four-rod quiver](tackle-express-cedros-four-rod-quiver.md).
