# Penn Handle Knob Replacement Kits — Fathom, Torque, Squall (Tackle Express)

Tackle Express, "Penn Handle Knob Replacement Kits for your Penn Fathoms
Torques and Squalls" (`QQlQcPXARWk`), is a 59-second shop-floor how-to for
swapping a Penn conventional reel's handle knob using a replacement kit. It
is a procedural repair walkthrough with no pricing, purchase link, or sales
pitch on camera, so per the confidence rubric it takes the
unregistered-channel default of **medium** rather than the `low` reserved
for sponsored/promotional content. For the visual steps, see the source:
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QQlQcPXARWk>.

## What's claimed

- **Fitment.** The kit fits all new Penn Fathom reels that ship stock with a
  T-bar handle, plus older Penn Fathoms and Penn Torque reels — one kit spans
  the current and prior Fathom handle style as well as the Torque line
  (`QQlQcPXARWk`). Penn Squall is named in the video title as also
  compatible, though the on-camera demo itself doesn't call out a
  Squall-specific step (`QQlQcPXARWk`).
- **Tools needed.** A Phillips screwdriver, a flathead screwdriver, and a
  Torx screwdriver — described on camera as the complete tool list for the
  swap, no reel-specific tool required (`QQlQcPXARWk`).

## Swap procedure

Paraphrased from the demo, not a transcript (`QQlQcPXARWk`):

1. Remove the two Phillips screws holding the handle cap on; the cap lifts
   right off.
2. Underneath the cap is a flathead screw — remove it.
3. Retrieve the washer and screw that come out with it; the same washer and
   screw are reused to attach the new knob, not replaced.
4. Slide the new knob onto the handle shaft.
5. Drop the washer and screw back down inside the new knob to secure it.
6. Replace the cap and drive it back down with the Torx screws.
