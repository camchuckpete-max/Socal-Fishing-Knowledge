## What's claimed

- **Fitment.** The kit fits **all new Penn Fathom reels that ship stock with a
  T-bar handle**, plus **older Penn Fathoms** and **Penn Torque** reels — one
  kit spans the current and prior Fathom handle style as well as the Torque
  line. Penn Squall is named in the video title as also compatible, though the
  on-camera demo itself doesn't call out a Squall-specific step.
- **Tools needed.** A Phillips screwdriver, a flathead screwdriver, and a Torx
  screwdriver — described on camera as the complete tool list for the swap,
  no reel-specific tool required.

## Swap procedure (paraphrased, not a transcript)

1. Remove the two **Phillips** screws holding the handle cap on; the cap lifts
   right off.
2. Underneath the cap is a **flathead** screw — remove it.
3. Retrieve the **washer and screw** that come out with it; the same washer
   and screw are reused to attach the new knob, not replaced.
4. Slide the **new knob onto the handle shaft**.
5. Drop the washer and screw back down inside the new knob to secure it.
6. Replace the cap and drive it back down with the **Torx** screws.
