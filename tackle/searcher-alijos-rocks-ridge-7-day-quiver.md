# Searcher Five-Outfit Quiver — 7-Day Trip to Alijos Rocks and "the Ridge"

Five minimum rod/reel outfits for a 7-day trip to [Alijos Rocks](../locations/alijos-rocks.md)
(`baja-pacific-south`) and "the Ridge" — resolved elsewhere in this KB as
running to the named high spot Uncle Sam Bank; see [wahoo — where &
when](../species/wahoo.md#where--when) for the run-distance detail
(`uWGGeDOprsE`). The five cover offshore tuna/dorado, a wahoo bomb, 100
lb-class tuna, and an interchangeable troll/bottom outfit. This is a 2009
citation, independently sourced from this KB's other Searcher outfit-quiver
notes (2019–2022); none of those name this same five-outfit combination for
this trip.

## The five (minimum) rod/reel outfits

1. **25 lb outfit** — offshore, smaller tuna and dorado; doubles as a
   live-bait-catching outfit to replenish the boat's bait supply
   (`uWGGeDOprsE`). No rod length or reel model given.
2. **40 lb outfit** — for bigger fish; described on camera as "a little on
   the lighter side" for that use, with no species named beyond "bigger
   fish" (`uWGGeDOprsE`).
3. **50 or 60 lb outfit, 6 ft 6 in rod, high-speed gear-ratio reel — wahoo
   bomb.** Fishes a [wahoo bomb](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md) (single
   hook, wire leader); 50 or 60 lb test is "very, very important" for this
   application (`uWGGeDOprsE`). [Wahoo bomb & Raider jig
   casting](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md) specs a longer, roughly 9 ft
   jig-stick-class rod for the same presentation — a different figure than
   this 6 ft 6 in rod.
4. **50 or 60 lb outfit, two-speed reel — 100 lb-class tuna.** When 100
   lb-class tuna are biting 50 or 60 lb test on a fly-lined sardine, a
   two-speed reel is "key" for dropping into low gear on a heavier fish
   (`uWGGeDOprsE`) — matching the 100 lb yellowfin logged at Alijos Rocks in
   [yellowfin tuna — where & when](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md#where--when).
   Two-speed reels are "an innovation in the tackle industry" that help "when
   you get a heavier fish and you can go into low gear" (`uWGGeDOprsE`).
   **Smaller two-speed alternative:** for 30 or 40 lb test, a smaller
   two-speed reel — a reel captioned "Avitz" (read as Avet, unconfirmed) ⚠
   asr-uncertain, or a new, smaller Shimano two-speed reel "coming out this
   year" (2009) (`uWGGeDOprsE`).
5. **80 lb trolling outfit, single- or two-speed — wahoo trolling, doubles as
   a bottom outfit.** With a two-speed reel, this outfit interchanges between
   trolling for wahoo and a [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md)
   with a 16 oz weight, fished to the bottom with a live sardine or mackerel
   for yellowtail (`uWGGeDOprsE`) — one interchangeable outfit rather than
   two dedicated rods. The 16 oz sinker figure matches the giant-yellowtail
   dropper-loop weight in [Searcher 6-to-8-day trip — the fourth (heavy)
   outfit](searcher-6-to-8-day-heavy-outfit.md#applications) and [surgeon's
   loop — parameters](../rigging/surgeons-loop.md#parameters).

## Terminal tackle

- **J-hook assortment:** sizes 20 to 70 — for tuna and for bottom fishing
  with a dropper loop (`uWGGeDOprsE`). No unit given beyond "standard J
  hooks."
- **Circle hook assortment — decision rule:** "if at Alijos Rocks the fish
  are 60 to 80 lbs and they're only biting 40 or 50 lb [test], then you need
  a circle hook" (`uWGGeDOprsE`). No manufacturer or size is named on camera
  — circle-hook sizing varies by manufacturer, so ask a tackle-store dealer
  for 40 or 50 lb test. This is a grade-vs-line-test trigger (a big fish
  committing only to comparatively light line) — a different trigger than
  the bluefin-teeth and flat 40 lb cutoff triggers in [hooks — circle vs.
  J](hooks.md#circle-vs-j--the-core-decision-swrsycmt4tw-m2q22sppkem).
- **Fluorocarbon leader class:** match the running line's class or step up
  one size — worked example, 40 lb monofilament running line to 50 lb
  fluorocarbon leader (`uWGGeDOprsE`). [Line &
  leader — connections](line-and-leader.md#connections-parameters--steps-are-in-the-rigging-notes)
  gives a same-rated-size mono-to-fluoro rule for knot integrity; this rule
  is about picking the leader's class relative to the main line and
  explicitly allows stepping the leader heavier, not just matching it.
- **Trolling lure:** a medium-size Marauder, part of "the wahoo package";
  "Tony the Tiger" and black-and-purple are named as working well
  consistently (`uWGGeDOprsE`). No size, weight, or rigging detail is given
  beyond "medium." [Searcher four-outfit Guadalupe quiver — Outfit
  4](searcher-four-outfit-guadalupe-quiver.md#outfit-4--talica-25-80100-lb-test--guadalupe-island-three-application-outfit)
  and [wahoo — where & when](../species/wahoo.md#where--when) name the same
  Marauder for Ridge trolling without color detail.
- **Wire assortment — casting/trolling wahoo lures** (bombs and other heavy
  casting lures): seven-strand braided wire with crimps, or single-strand
  wire (`uWGGeDOprsE`).
- **Wire for live-bait fishing:** 40 lb up to 60 lb wire (`uWGGeDOprsE`) —
  captioned "seven strand monofilament" ⚠ asr-uncertain (mono is not
  multi-strand; likely a repeated reference to seven-strand wire). This range
  is within, though not identical to, the 200–300 lb, 44 lb, and 60 lb wahoo
  live-bait wire figures from later Searcher citations in [wahoo — doctrine &
  conflicts](../species/wahoo.md#doctrine--conflicts).
- **Crimps must match the wire's stated pound test exactly** — a crimp
  bigger than the wire needs won't seat correctly; ask the tackle store for
  the right crimp for the wire being bought (`uWGGeDOprsE`). This matches the
  crimp-to-wire-test rule in [crimping —
  parameters](../rigging/crimping.md#parameters). Also bring crimping tools,
  small black rings, and black swivels (`uWGGeDOprsE`).
- **Jig assortment — wahoo:** Burns Bombs and Braid Bombs (`uWGGeDOprsE`) —
  see [wahoo bomb & Raider jig casting — lure
  choice](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md#lure-choice) for the other
  named cast-lure brands (Raider, Catch/Catchy Tackle, Stinger).
- **Jig assortment — yellowtail (heavy yo-yo jigs):** blue and white,
  dorado-colored, and scrambled egg; Salas 6X Jr. is named as the reference
  jig, with Iron Man or Kicker named as equivalent alternative brands in the
  same class (`uWGGeDOprsE`). See [iron jigs](../lures/iron-jigs.md) and
  [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) for the colors and Salas 6X Jr.
  model.
- **Hook sharpening:** bring a file — factory hooks (trolling lures, bait
  hooks, bombs) are "not sharp when it comes out of the package," so every
  hook is sharpened before fishing (`uWGGeDOprsE`). [Wahoo bomb & Raider jig
  casting — common failures](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md#common-failures)
  documents the mechanism (a wahoo's bony jaw) for the wahoo-specific case;
  this citation states the practice without that mechanism.
- **Half a dozen big weights, 16 oz** (`uWGGeDOprsE`) — matches Outfit 5's
  dropper-loop sinker weight above.

## Not carried forward

The boat sells yo-yo jigs, pre-made wahoo bait leaders (packs of five), and
spectra/monofilament line on board, so an angler doesn't have to buy
everything before the trip (`uWGGeDOprsE`). This is a boat-service
convenience note, not fishery decision knowledge — skipped per the curation
bar.
