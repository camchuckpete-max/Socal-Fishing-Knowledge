# Searcher Five-Outfit Quiver — 7-Day Trip to Alijos Rocks and "The Ridge" (SearcherSportfishing, 2009-08-25)

SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tips for Alijos Rocks and The Ridge" (`uWGGeDOprsE`,
uploaded 2009-08-25 — a 6:52 segment). Speaker not identified on camera
(auto-generated captions, no self-introduction). This is the same upload date
as, and plausibly the same interview session as, "Fall, 2009 on Searcher"
(`_r08B4bItAY`), which this KB attributes elsewhere to **Capt. Art** — not
confirmed here, so this note is attributed to the channel only, not a named
person. `searcher-sportfishing` is a registered channel/series
([source registry](../sources/source-registry.md)) — single mention of this
specific five-outfit combination, **medium** confidence on the mechanism/
parameters below; named brands/models/colors stay **low** per the channel's
sponsored-claim caveat.

Framed on camera as tackle recommendations for a **7-day trip to Alijos
Rocks** (`baja-pacific-south`) **and "the Ridge"** — the same "the Ridge"
already resolved elsewhere in this KB as running to a named high spot,
**Uncle Sam Bank** — see [wahoo — where &
when](../species/wahoo.md#where--when) for the run-distance/location detail
this note doesn't repeat. This is an early (2009), independently sourced
data point predating this KB's other Searcher outfit-quiver notes (2019–2022)
by roughly a decade; kept side by side with them rather than merged, since
none of those name this same five-outfit combination for this specific trip.

## The five (minimum) rod/reel outfits — "at least five rods"

1. **25 lb outfit** — for **offshore, smaller tuna and dorado**, and doubling
   as a **live-bait-catching outfit** for replenishing the boat's bait supply
   when needed. No rod length or reel model given.
2. **40 lb outfit** — for **bigger fish**, described on camera as "a little
   on the lighter side as far as fishing for bigger fish" — no species named
   beyond that.
3. **50 or 60 lb outfit, short 6 ft 6 in rod, high-speed gear-ratio reel —
   wahoo.** Stated application: fishing a **bomb** (a wahoo lure with a
   single hook and a wire leader) — the 50 or 60 lb test is called "very,
   very important" specifically for this application. See [wahoo bomb &
   Raider jig casting](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md) for this KB's
   other, later-sourced gear-class detail for the same presentation (a longer,
   ~9 ft jig-stick class rod is documented elsewhere — this 6 ft 6 in figure
   is a shorter, independent, earlier data point, kept side by side rather
   than reconciled).
4. **50 or 60 lb outfit, two-speed reel — 100 lb class tuna.** Stated
   reasoning: when 100 lb-class tuna are biting **50 or 60 lb test** on a
   **fly-lined sardine**, having a two-speed reel is "key" — matches the
   **100 lb yellowfin at Alijos Rocks** already logged in
   [yellowfin tuna — where & when](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md#where--when)
   from the companion 2009-08-25 clip, and reads as the tackle-side citation
   for that same grade/ground. General two-speed doctrine stated alongside:
   two-speed reels are called "an innovation in the tackle industry" that
   help "when you get a heavier fish and you can go into low gear."
   **Smaller two-speed alternative:** a smaller two-speed reel for **30 or
   40 lb test** is offered as an option, naming two sources for smaller
   two-speed reels newly available that year (2009): a reel captioned
   **"Avitz"** (read as **Avet**, not confirmed — flagged `asr-uncertain`)
   and a new, smaller **Shimano** two-speed reel described as "coming out
   this year."
5. **80 lb trolling outfit, single- or two-speed — wahoo trolling, doubling
   as a bottom outfit.** If the reel is two-speed, the same outfit
   "interchange[s]" between **trolling for wahoo** and **bottom fishing**: a
   [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) with a **16 oz weight**,
   fished to the bottom with a **live sardine or live mackerel** for
   **yellowtail**. Framed as a way to bring one interchangeable outfit rather
   than two dedicated rods — the 16 oz sinker figure matches, parameter for
   parameter, the 16 oz Guadalupe/Baja-Peninsula giant-yellowtail
   dropper-loop weight already logged in [Searcher 6-to-8-day trip — the
   fourth (heavy) outfit](searcher-6-to-8-day-heavy-outfit.md#four-stated-applications-for-this-one-outfit)
   and [surgeon's loop (giant yellowtail,
   long-range)](../rigging/surgeons-loop.md#sinker--hook-cdjt_i_pbhq) — a
   further, independent (and earlier) citation of the same figure.

## Terminal tackle

- **J-hook assortment:** sizes **20 to 70** (as captioned; no unit given
  beyond "standard J hooks" — carried as stated) — for **tuna** and for
  **bottom fishing with a dropper loop**.
- **Circle hook assortment — decision rationale for when to reach for one:**
  "if at Alijos Rocks the fish are 60 to 80 lbs and they're only biting 40 or
  50 lb [test], then you need a circle hook." No manufacturer or size is
  recommended on camera — an explicit refusal to name one, on the stated
  reasoning that circle-hook sizing varies by manufacturer; the advice given
  is to ask a tackle-store dealer what's best for 40 or 50 lb test in a
  circle hook. This is a distinct trigger from this KB's other logged
  circle-hook-switch reasoning (bluefin teeth abrasion at any grade, or a
  flat 40 lb weight cutoff) in
  [hooks — circle vs. J](hooks.md#circle-vs-j--the-core-decision-swrsycmt4tw-m2q22sppkem) —
  here the trigger is **grade-vs-line-test mismatch** (a big fish only
  committing to comparatively light line) rather than teeth or a fixed weight
  threshold.
- **Fluorocarbon leader class — match or step up one size:** "you either want
  to match the line class that you have or be one step above" — worked
  example given: **40 lb monofilament** running line, **50 lb fluorocarbon**
  leader. This is a distinct rule from, and not reconciled with, the
  same-rated-size-on-both-sides mono-to-fluoro knot-integrity rule already
  logged in [line & leader — connections](line-and-leader.md#connections-parameters--steps-are-in-the-rigging-notes)
  (Izorline, Fred Hall Show) — that rule is about the knot holding; this one
  is about picking the leader's own class relative to the main line, and
  explicitly allows stepping the leader heavier, not just matching it.
- **Trolling lure — a "medium-size marauder,"** named as part of "the wahoo
  package." Two colors named as working "really well on a consistent basis":
  **"Tony the Tiger"** and **black and purple**. No size, weight, or rigging
  detail given beyond "medium." This corroborates, with color detail, the
  Marauder already named without specs for "the Ridge" trolling in
  [Searcher four-outfit Guadalupe quiver — Outfit
  4](searcher-four-outfit-guadalupe-quiver.md#outfit-4--talica-25-80100-lb-test--guadalupe-island-three-application-outfit)
  and [wahoo — where & when](../species/wahoo.md#where--when).
- **Wire assortment — casting/trolling lures for wahoo** (bombs and other
  heavy casting lures): either **seven-strand braided wire with crimps**, or
  **single-strand wire**.
- **Wire for live-bait fishing:** captioned "seven strand monofilament" —
  flagged `asr-uncertain`, since mono is not multi-strand; likely a repeated
  reference to seven-strand **wire** rather than a distinct material. **40 lb
  up to 60 lb wire** for live bait — within the range of, though not
  identical to, this KB's other logged wahoo live-bait wire figures (200–300
  lb, 44 lb, and 60 lb from later Searcher citations in
  [wahoo — doctrine & conflicts](../species/wahoo.md#doctrine--conflicts)) —
  kept side by side, not reconciled.
- **Crimps must be matched exactly to the wire's stated pound test** — buying
  crimps bigger than the wire needs creates a mismatch that won't seat
  correctly; ask the tackle store for the right crimp for the wire being
  bought. This is a further, earlier (2009), independent citation of the
  same match-crimp-to-wire-test doctrine already logged in
  [crimping — parameters &
  judgment](../rigging/crimping.md#parameters--judgment-billy-fishermans-landing--searchersportfishing).
  Also bring **crimping tools**, plus **small black rings and black
  swivels**.
- **Jig assortment — wahoo:** **Burns Bombs** and **Braid Bombs** named as
  two good picks ("really good," two suggestions) — brand names not
  previously logged elsewhere in this KB's wahoo lure coverage (see
  [wahoo bomb & Raider jig
  casting](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md#lure-choice) for the other
  named cast-lure brands: Raider, Catch/Catchy Tackle, Stinger).
- **Jig assortment — yellowtail (heavy yo-yo jigs):** colors named **blue and
  white**, **dorado-colored**, and **scrambled egg**. **Salas 6X Jr.** named
  as the reference jig, with **Iron Man** or **Kicker** named as equivalent
  alternative brands in the same class. All three colors and the Salas 6X
  Jr. model are already documented in this KB (see [iron
  jigs](../lures/iron-jigs.md) and [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md));
  "Iron Man" and "Kicker" as named Salas-6X-Jr-equivalent brands are a new
  data point not previously logged here.
- **Hook sharpening:** bring a **file** — factory hooks (on trolling lures,
  bait hooks, and bombs alike) are "not sharp when it comes out of the
  package," so every hook gets sharpened before fishing. Framed on camera for
  wahoo specifically, but no jaw/mouth mechanism is stated in this clip — that
  reasoning (a wahoo's "very, very bony jaw") is a different, already-logged
  citation, not this one; see [wahoo bomb & Raider jig casting — common
  failures](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md#common-failures) for that
  citation and its stated mechanism, which this data point corroborates on the
  practice (sharpen before fishing) without repeating its stated reason.
- **Half a dozen big weights, 16 oz** — matching Outfit 5's dropper-loop
  sinker weight above.

## Not carried forward

The video closes with a note that the boat sells yo-yo jigs, pre-made wahoo
bait leaders (packs of five), and spectra/monofilament line on board, so an
angler doesn't have to buy everything before the trip. This is a boat-service
convenience note, not fishery-specific decision knowledge, so it is not
carried into this note beyond this mention (curation bar).
