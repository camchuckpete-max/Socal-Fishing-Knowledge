# Tackle Express — Cedros Four-Rod Quiver (the Gear Vault)

Tackle Express, "What You ACTUALLY Need for Cedros Fishing Trips | The Gear
Vault," `_C7TJoGT0nw`, uploaded 2025-09-26 — a 32-minute counter interview
with **Logan**, a Tackle Express employee who had fished a Cedros Island trip
about a month prior. `tackle-express` is an **unregistered** channel —
**medium** confidence. Region: Baja Pacific, Cedros Island
(`baja-pacific-north`). This is the four-rod quiver Logan brought and used
fully on that trip ("everything I brought, I used"), for a lighter-grade
(19–33 lb) Cedros yellowtail bite already logged in [Cedros
Island](../locations/cedros-island.md#what-you-are-fishing-for). Two shorter
clips cut from this same interview cover setups 1 and 3 below as standalone
notes; this note adds the two setups (2 and 4) not otherwise captured, and
indexes all four together as the trip's quiver.

## Setup 2 — medium Colt Sniper / structure rod

A **Phoenix rod, 9 ft, rated 20–50 lb**, paired with an **Okuma Andros**
reel — the **narrow** version, picked for a smaller hand ("small hands, so
just one thumb kind of covers the whole spool"), a **lever-drag two-speed**,
not a casting reel. Spooled with **65 lb** line and a **60 lb fluorocarbon**
leader run **about 5 ft**. Application: casting **Colt Snipers** (see [iron
jigs — Cedros Colt Sniper
versatility](../lures/iron-jigs.md)) and some **Z-Man** soft plastics (see
[soft-plastic swimbaits — Cedros Z-Man Diesel
MinnowZ](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md)); a couple of **yo-yo jigs**
were also dropped on it with no issue. The stated reason for the two-speed:
drop a Colt Sniper through the bottom, hook a bigger fish, pop the reel into
low gear to pull it clear of the rocks, then pop back to high gear to keep
fighting — a shift-down-to-clear-structure, shift-back-up-to-fight sequence
distinct from, but consistent with, the general two-speed-for-leverage
doctrine in [two-speed low-gear
fight](../techniques/two-speed-low-gear-fight.md).

## Setup 4 — the favorite: a low-profile baitcaster

**United Composite, 9 ft, model "9E"**, paired with a reel captioned
"Alexa 500" — read here as a **Daiwa Lexa 500**, cross-checked against this
same channel's companion clip (`QEmxUIGmKbo`, see [casting reel for sea bass
and yellowtail](tackle-express-casting-reel-for-seabass-yellowtail.md)),
which independently names the Daiwa Lexa as one of three recommended casting
reels for this exact species pairing — asr-uncertain on the exact model
number, not otherwise confirmed on camera. Specified as the **HL** version
(left-handed retrieve; the angler fishes all his baitcasters left-handed) at
a **7:1** gear ratio, described as a deliberate middle ground: slower than an
8:1 (avoids getting "dogged" by a hard-pulling fish) while still fast enough
to speed up the retrieve when wanted — more torque from the lower ratio.
Spooled with **80 lb Maxcuatro** braid, **size 65**, roughly **350 yd**, with
**60 lb fluorocarbon** leader already tied on before the trip.

**A recent purchase, right before this trip, that outperformed expectations
on camera:** no backlashes across the trip ("even professionals will still
backlash... one out of 10 times. This reel here, no backlashes, nothing"),
and the longest casts of anyone on the boat. Stated reason it became the
favorite of the four setups: it has enough power to cast surface irons and
yo-yo jigs, land the trip's biggest fish, and it carries a **level wind**
("you just have to turn the handle, you don't have to worry about your line
management") plus an **external braking system and spool tensioner** —
adjustable in the field, e.g. dialing down the brake when switching from a
light surface iron to a heavier **60 g Colt Sniper** that started backlashing
on the heavier lure. Stated advantage over the trip's conventional reels
(the Torium/Trinidad-based setup 3 above): **"you can't do that with a Torium
or any of those conventional reels"** — the external brake/tensioner
adjustment is specific to the low-profile baitcaster design.

**Same purchase story re-cut as a standalone short (Tackle Express, "Why a
deck hand rod is a game-changer for your next trip," `y9YlqMQh3BI`,
2026-05-16, 38 sec):** repeats the United Composite 9-ft "9E" / Lexa 500
pairing and the "recent purchase right before I went down to Cedros... it did
awesome" line verbatim — same Logan interview footage re-cut, not independent
confirmation, so this stays at `medium` confidence.
