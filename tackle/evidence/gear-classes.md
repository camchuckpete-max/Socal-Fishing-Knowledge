## Rail-rod class

- `Y2XZ34-Tpa0` (searcher-sportfishing, 2019-06-06, "Tackle Tip Thursday
  Vol. 51 (Rail Rod)"): names the Seeker OSP 1x3 (7 ft composite) as the rail
  rod for the Searcher's 1.5-day and 3-day trip range. Registered channel,
  single mention — medium confidence for the mechanism/parameters.
- `aAqKHeyBwEo` (searcher-sportfishing, 2019-04-04, "Tackle Tip Thursday
  Vol. 42 (Seeker Rods)"): independent, earlier citation of the OSP 1x3 —
  nicknamed "the Guadalupe special" on camera, rated 60–100 lb, "for that
  grade of fish we find out there" (no place named beyond the nickname
  itself). Frames the trip as "the Searcher's seven-day trip," a different
  trip length than Vol. 51's stated 1.5-/3-day framing — both plausibly true
  across the boat's trip-length range. Also introduces the lighter Seeker SSR
  7650 (7 ft 6 in, rated 50 lb, "a little bit extra length and a little bit
  of a stiffer tip" than a standard casting rod), captioned a wahoo bomb rod
  that also doubles as a rail rod for "a wahoo and tune up to... 150 or 60
  pounds" (asr-uncertain: read here as "wahoo and tuna," neither the species
  word nor the number range corrected) with 6–8 in of extra foregrip, "to lay
  this rod on the rail" once a fish is hooked; caption reads "great Grail
  rod" (asr-uncertain: read as "great rail rod" given the foregrip/rail
  sentence that immediately follows). Registered channel, single mention —
  medium confidence for the mechanism/parameters.
- `an_uw-5pjfw` (searcher-sportfishing, 2020-11-12, "Tackle Tip Thursday
  Vol. 125 (Flat Fall & set-up)," Capt. Art Taylor): pairs a Penn Fathom 60
  two-speed with a Seeker OSP rail rod (captioned "3 to 1" — asr-uncertain,
  plausibly the same OSP 1x3 rather than a distinct model) for flat-fall
  bluefin; heavy action, graphite, 7 ft, same extra-long hypalon foregrip.
  Registered channel, single mention — medium confidence for the
  mechanism/parameters.
- `scScYJJF95Y` (searcher-sportfishing, 2022-09-08, "Tackle Tip Thursday
  Vol. 196 (Work Smarter with Seeker's 2x4 Rod)," Rick Holt & Sophia Nguyen,
  Seeker Rods): full spec citation for the OSP 2x4 (80–130 lb, 7 ft 3 in
  composite, "heavy duty guides," an "indestructible" foregrip). Holt and
  Nguyen are Seeker Rods staff naming their own company's product — low
  confidence, sponsored-claim caveat on the named model; the mechanism and
  parameters rate normally. Name discrepancy, asr-uncertain: this video's
  auto-caption gives "Sophia Nguyen," while the Seeker Athena flyline-rod
  pick in [rod & reel selection — flyline reel & rod

  "Sophia Quinn," auto-captioned from a different 2019 video in the same
  series — both are Seeker Rods reps on the same Tackle Tip Thursday
  program, plausibly one person heard two ways across two caption passes,
  but neither video's title or description gives a surname, so both
  spellings are carried.
- `sjOJiR6_HJ4` (searcher-sportfishing, 2023-12-07, "Tackle Tip Thursday
  Vol. 229 (Rail Rods)," Steve Carson): a full four-tier rail-rod ladder by

  worked through four named models (7 ft 8 in 40–100 lb; "Carnage 3" 7 ft
  60–100 lb; "8130" 7 ft 80–130 lb; an 80–150 lb model, name unstated) with

- `MPC_OQjvO-o` (searcher-sportfishing, 2020-04-09, "6 to 8-day trip
