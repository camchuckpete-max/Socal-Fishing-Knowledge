- **Observed** (`QHY5kmU7OTU`, filmed 2021-01-28, location not stated on
  camera): the Searcher crew's own on-the-water pattern from the 2020
  season — the sinker rig tended to produce best around sunset, just after
  sunset, and right before sunrise, on a school of fish holding under a
  stopped or drifting boat. Daytime flat-fall jigs sometimes drew nothing
  from the same school while the sinker rig connected, at which point the
  rest of the boat would switch over.
