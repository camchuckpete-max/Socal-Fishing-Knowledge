## On-the-water jig swap

- `DuNkl8F45NE` (Tackle Express, James, 2022-06-04): narrated live during
  one session. Started on a 250 g "SK" jig in a zebra glow pattern. When
  the fish moved higher in the water column and were seen swimming faster
  past the boat, switched to a 200 g "SL" jig — same knife profile, still
  darting down through the column but not so fast it "rockets past"
  faster-moving, shallower fish. When the captain then began calling fish
  300–400 g grade, 400 ft deep, swapped again toward something like a
  300 g West Coast Jiggerz or a Mustad Rip Roller — the next step up once
  the called depth/grade outgrew the 200 g jig already on the line. Single
  session, not corroborated elsewhere in this corpus; kept in the main note
  only as a one-line trace beside the carry-a-spread doctrine it
  illustrates.
