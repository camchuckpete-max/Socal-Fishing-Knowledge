## Verifying the target — hand-feel, hand-wrap, and scale tests

- `o8vLdz7OmaE` (Dave Hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-02-25): demonstrates
  the hand-pull test on his own setup — 40 lb braid to a fluorocarbon top

  method, not as a general line-class parameter.
- `o8vLdz7OmaE` (same source): states a personal preference, single mention —
  because a star drag is easier to fine-tune mid-fight than a two-speed lever
  drag, Hansen generally prefers star-drag reels; an attributed opinion, not
  corroborated doctrine, so it stays here rather than the main note.
