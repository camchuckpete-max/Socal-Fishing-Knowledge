# Evidence — Tungsten (TG) Jig Rig for Bluefin (Tackle Express)

Per-source provenance for [Tungsten (TG) jig rig for bluefin — night, day,
and surface](../tackle-express-night-bluefin-tg-jig-rig.md). Tackle Express
is an unregistered channel throughout; several of these clips are re-cuts of
the same footage rather than independent trips.

## Source cluster and re-cut relationships

- `3-0kaaHqA7A` ("Best Reel & Line for Night Bluefin Jigging — Don't Guess
  Your Depth!", uploaded 2025-06-21): the shortest clip; names the
  reel/line/leader/depth/retrieve ladder. Captions render the jig class both
  "PG jigs" and "TG jigs" within the same clip — read as TG (tungsten)
  throughout, matching the documented Daiwa TG jig.
- `CsKEtrcLgiQ` ("Are Tungsten Jigs Worth It for Bluefin?", uploaded
  2025-06-18, three days before `3-0kaaHqA7A`): a fuller, unedited cut of the
  same footage, plus additional daytime/foamer content not present in the
  shorter clip. Confirmed on extraction of `CsKEtrcLgiQ` (batch 3): the
  reel/line/leader/depth/retrieve segment, the day/surface re-rig cycle, and
  the Captain Brian/*Independence*/189 lb origin story are the same footage
  repeating itself, not independent confirmation of those parameters
  (prefer-latest / no-independent-reconfirmation rule for re-cut footage).
  The handful of details not present in the other clips in this cluster are
  cited to `CsKEtrcLgiQ` specifically in the main note.
- `dBAk9NScxSc` ("How to Rig Tungsten Jigs for BIG Bluefin Tuna", uploaded
  2025-06-20, inside the same 2025-06-16–2025-06-21 cluster): a 65-second cut
  covering the same night/day/foamer re-rig cycle. Contributes one reasoning
  detail not stated elsewhere in the cluster — the unseen-leader-in-the-dark
  rationale for the night rig, and the daytime-visibility rationale for
  swapping off it — folded into the main note's Leader section. Otherwise
  reads as a re-cut of the same talking points; no parameter here goes
  beyond the day/night/foamer rig already logged from the other clips.
- `A7rveRBkL-g` ("Tungsten Jigs: The Secret to More Bites This Summer",
  uploaded 2025-06-16, two days before `CsKEtrcLgiQ`): the same narrator's
  day/night/surface rigging summary and the same Captain Brian/*Independence*/
  189 lb story as `CsKEtrcLgiQ`, plus one detail not in that transcript
  (fishing the jig three more days after the first hookup). Reads as pulled
  from the same footage/talking points as `CsKEtrcLgiQ`, not an independent
  trip — its **Day, surface, size, and durability** and **Origin story**
  material should be read as one voice repeating itself, not two-source
  confirmation.
- `BsWb5BQ1Tdc` ("The Jarry Rig: Built for Big Tuna — Only at Tackle
  Express", uploaded 2025-08-04): a separate, later shoot naming and
  correcting the leader-build name to "Jarry" — the video's own title
  spelling overrides the ASR "Jerry" spelling carried by the four clips
  above.
- `DuNkl8F45NE` ("How to Catch a Trophy Bluefin Tuna on a Jig", James,
  uploaded 2022-06-04): a three-years-earlier citation of the same
  James-and-Harry leader build, with a 200–300 lb (stepped to 400 lb)

## Origin story (testimonial, not decision doctrine)

- `A7rveRBkL-g`, `CsKEtrcLgiQ` (Tackle Express, 2025-06-16/2025-06-18,
  aboard the *Independence*): Captain Brian handed the narrator a tungsten
  jig and told him to fish it; the narrator tied it on — by his own account,
  because he listens to the boat's captains, not for any stated bait/depth
  reason — dropped it, and hooked a 189 lb bluefin, a personal best, on the
  first drop. He fished the jig for the following 3 days and lost count of
  how many fish it caught. The video states this happened "last year,"
  which resolves against the 2025-06-16 upload date to 2024. The only stated
  reason for tying it on is the captain's recommendation, not a technical
  trigger — kept here as shop testimonial, not fishery-wide decision logic.
