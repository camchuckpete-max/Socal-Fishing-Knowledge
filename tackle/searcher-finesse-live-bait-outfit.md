# Searcher Finesse Live-Bait Outfit — 20 lb and 15 lb (Steve Carson, Penn/SearcherSportfishing, 2020-10-01)

Steve Carson, aboard the **Searcher** (`ptoIvB2MspE`, "Tackle Tip Thursday
Vol. 119 (Live Bait Outfit)," uploaded 2020-10-01 — a short, 3.5-minute
dockside segment ahead of a 3-day offshore trip). `searcher-sportfishing` is a
**registered** channel/series and Carson a recurring named voice on it; the
rig's line/leader/hook parameters read **medium** confidence here as a single
mention within this note, but they closely corroborate — and, at 2020-10-01,
predate by roughly 1.5–3 years — the same rig logged from two later Carson
citations in [bluefin rig ladder by grade — Rig 1, 15 lb
finesse](bluefin-rig-ladder-by-grade.md#rig-1--15-lb-finesse-the-one-you-need-most),
where the repeated parameters read **high** per the registry's mechanism/
parameter rule. The named reel is a product pick and stays **low** confidence
regardless, per this channel's sponsored-claim caveat (Carson is Penn's rep).

Framed on camera as a **fall offshore trip** essential that most anglers
*don't* already have in their arsenal — explicitly distinct from the
calico-bass/bay gear an angler probably already owns. No location beyond
"aboard the Searcher" is named on camera, so none is inferred here.

## The 20 lb outfit

- **Reel:** a small conventional live-bait reel, **no level wind** — his named
  pick, the **Penn Squall II 15** (product/model pick — low confidence per the
  sponsored-claim caveat).
- **Line:** reel filled **about 3/4 of the way up with braid**, topped with a
  **~50 yd top shot of 20 lb test monofilament** — his own emphasis on the
  exact number: "not 25, 20." Matches the ~50 yd top-shot figure and the 20 lb
  test already logged for Rig 1 above.
- **Leader:** **4- to 5-ft of 20 lb fluorocarbon**, tied to the top shot
  (knot not shown clearly on camera).
- **Hook:** **Owner Gorilla Light, size 4** — fine wire, his own words: "that
  is a small hook." **A size discrepancy against the later (2023-11-14) Rig 1
  citation, kept side by side rather than reconciled:** that later talk has
  Carson naming his pick as size 2, "he'd use a 4 if it came in that size" —
  read as implying a 4 wasn't available to him at that point. This earlier
  (2020) citation has him naming and actually fishing a size-4 Gorilla Light.
  Not resolved in this corpus — may reflect a product-line change between
  2020 and 2023, or simply which size he had rigged that day.
- **When (his own framing):** when the bait isn't very good and/or the fish
  are finicky — schools of **15, 20, 30, maybe even 40 lb** tuna that "just
  won't bite anything heavier than this." Explicit: not built for 100 lb tuna.
- **Drag and knots — his own emphasis:** pair it with a reel that has a very
  good drag, and have a crewman set the drag if you're not sure how; every
  knot has to be perfect — "everything has to be perfect if you're going to
  catch a 30 or 40 lb tuna on one of these finesse rigs."
- **The tradeoff, stated explicitly:** fishing this light risks not landing a
  bigger fish that happens to eat — but without it, some days you don't get
  bit at all: "if you don't get a bite, you can never land anything... some
  days this is just the only thing they'll bite."

## A lighter step-down — 15 lb

For days when the bait is "really not too good" or the fish are "really
extra finicky" and not too big, Carson re-spools the **same reel and setup**
one step lighter, construction otherwise identical to the 20 lb outfit above:

- **Line:** **15 lb test monofilament** top shot (in place of the 20 lb top
  shot).
- **Leader:** **4-ft of 15 lb test fluorocarbon**.
- **Hook:** the same **Owner Gorilla Light** fine-wire hook. "I bet that
  barely even shows up on camera... but if you can't see it, neither can the
  fish. Plus, your bait can swim a lot more easily with that."
- **Grade expectation:** "lots and lots of schools, not any giant ones" —
  not a 100 lb-tuna rig, same as the 20 lb version.

**Observed** (SearcherSportfishing, `ptoIvB2MspE`, 2019 — resolved from "last
year" against this video's 2020-10-01 upload date; aboard the Searcher,
exact spot/trip not further specified on camera): Carson caught a **44 lb
bluefin tuna** on this exact 15 lb rig, "because I couldn't get a bite any
other way."
