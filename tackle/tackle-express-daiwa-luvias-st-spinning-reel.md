## What's claimed

- **Air Drive Design** — applied to the bail, rotor, and spool; claimed to
  reduce weight for effortless rotation and an easier startup when turning
  the handle (`RKfI8g_aRu8`).
- **Carbon body** — captioned "Zion MQ body" (`RKfI8g_aRu8`). Daiwa's known
  carbon-composite reel-body material is **Zaion**, and "Zion" reads as an
  ASR mishearing of that; the "MQ" qualifier can't be confirmed from this
  transcript (no on-screen text or description repeats it). ⚠ asr-uncertain:
  the full material name ("Zaion MQ"?) is not independently confirmed.
  Claimed lightweight, corrosion-resistant, and "very smooth" (`RKfI8g_aRu8`).
- **One-piece lower body / gear-drive system** — captioned "monocock," read
  here as **monocoque** construction (`RKfI8g_aRu8`) — claimed to let Daiwa
  go to a one-piece body that minimizes flex (`RKfI8g_aRu8`).
- **Oil-type main bearing** (vs. a grease-type bearing) — claimed to give
  "ultra light rotation," paired with **Mag Seal** technology: a magnetized
  oil at the seal, claimed to repel salt, water, and sand from entering the
  reel's internals (`RKfI8g_aRu8`).
