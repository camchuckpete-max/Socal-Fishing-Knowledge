## Claimed features

- **Air Drive Design** — applied to the bail, rotor, and spool; claimed to
  reduce weight for effortless rotation and an easier startup when turning
  the handle.
- **Carbon body** — captioned "Zion MQ body." Daiwa's known carbon-composite
  reel-body material is **Zaion**, and "Zion" reads as an ASR mishearing of
  that; the "MQ" qualifier can't be confirmed from this transcript (no
  on-screen text or description repeats it), so the exact material name is
  flagged `asr-uncertain`. Claimed lightweight, corrosion-resistant, and
  "very smooth."
- **One-piece lower body / gear-drive system** — captioned "monocock,"
  read here as **monocoque** construction — claimed to let Daiwa go to a
  one-piece body that minimizes flex.
- **Oil-type main bearing** (vs. a grease-type bearing) — claimed to give
  "ultra light rotation," paired with **Mag Seal** technology: a magnetized
  oil at the seal, claimed to repel salt, water, and sand from entering the
  reel's internals.
