# Baitcaster Gear Ratio for Landing Big Yellowtail — 7:1 as the Middle Ground (Tackle Express)

Tackle Express, "Why this reel is a total game changer for landing big
Yellowtail.," `pOFsjDTqaxY`, uploaded 2026-05-07 — a 55-second tackle-counter
clip. `tackle-express` is an **unregistered** channel, single mention —
**medium** confidence for the mechanism/parameter content below. The "total
game changer," backlash-free, out-cast-the-boat testimonial framing is a
product endorsement and stays **low** per the sponsored-claim rule. The
reel's brand/model is never stated in the transcript beyond two internal
designations, "HG" and "HL" — not carried as a brand or model name.

## What's claimed

- **One baitcaster for both the cast and the grind.** Framed as having
  "enough power to throw your surface irons, yo-yo jigs, hook that bigger
  fish, and be able to get it to the boat" — one reel covering
  [surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md) casting and
  [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) grinding on the same trip.
- **HG vs. HL as a handle-side model split.** Asked "which one is it, the
  HG or the HL?", the presenter answers **HL**, stating he fishes his
  baitcasters left-handed. Read in context as the reel's right- vs.
  left-hand retrieve variants; not otherwise explained in the transcript.
- **7:1 gear ratio, framed as a deliberate middle ground:** "that middle
  ground so you could fish things slower, or you could speed up your handle
  and fish things a little quicker" — one reel spanning both a slow
  presentation and a faster retrieve, rather than needing separate slow- and
  fast-ratio reels.
- **Torque vs. gear ratio — the stated mechanism:** contrasted directly with
  an 8:1 reel — *"instead of going with 8:1 to where I'd be getting dogged
  every now and then, having that little bit more torque is beneficial."*
  The claim: the lower (7:1) ratio trades a little cranking speed for more
  torque, and that torque matters once a big fish is loading up the reel.
- **Backlash-free casting, personal testimonial:** the presenter states this
  reel gave him no backlashes over a session where he was, in his telling,
  casting farther than anyone else on the boat — a personal testimonial, not
  a reel-class claim (promotional framing, low confidence).
