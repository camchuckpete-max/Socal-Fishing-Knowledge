# Bates EDC 100 — Digitally Assisted Brake (DAB) Baitcaster (Tackle Express)

The Bates EDC 100 is an entry-level baitcaster using a Digitally Assisted
Brake (DAB) system to cut backlash on the cast (`9hXW9JCffsU`). It is a
promotional single-product spot for one SKU, capped at **low** confidence
per the confidence rubric regardless of channel (`9hXW9JCffsU`) — kept here
as a product/spec record, not as fishery doctrine.

## Digitally Assisted Brake (DAB)

Self-powered; senses spool speed thousands of times per second and brakes
the spool during the cast, with the stated benefit of
longer casts and less backlash (`9hXW9JCffsU`). No further mechanical detail
— weight count, adjustment procedure — is given (`9hXW9JCffsU`); contrast the
[Accurate Tern 2's cast-control brake](tackle-express-accurate-tern-2-reel.md),
a documented, adjustable weight-count spool brake on a different reel and
channel clip.

## Frame and capacity

Cast-aluminum frame; holds up to 100 yds of 12 lb mono (`9hXW9JCffsU`).

## Positioning

Marketed as an entry-level, "everyday angler" baitcaster at an affordable
price point (`9hXW9JCffsU`) — the "EDC" in the model name reads as this
everyday-carry positioning, not confirmed by any other source
(`9hXW9JCffsU`).
