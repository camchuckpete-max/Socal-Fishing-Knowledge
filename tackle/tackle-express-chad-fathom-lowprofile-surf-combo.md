## The setup

- **Reel — Penn Fathom Low Profile, 300 size.** A low-profile baitcaster,
  distinct from Penn's round two-speed lever-drag Fathom/Fathom II line
  already documented in [Penn Fathom II and Penn
  Authority](tackle-express-icast2022-penn-fathom2-authority.md) and
  [Speedmaster vs. Fathom reel sizing](tackle-express-speedmaster-fathom-reel-sizing.md).
  Chad's stated reasons for the pick: casts well with minimal backlash, and a
  minor backlash clears easily; likes the handle; runs the same combo across
  lakes, surf, and calico bass ("calos").
- **Drag spec called out as inconsistent across the line.** The **300 size
  carries 30 lb of drag**; the **400 size carries only 25 lb** — Chad notes
  the smaller reel out-dragging the larger one without offering a reason
  ("I'm not sure why... I'm not really a techy kind of guy").
- **Rod — Okuma Seros, 7'11" (stated on camera as "711"), medium-heavy.**
  Chad's stated reasoning for the length: shorter than what's "really
  considered a surf rod" (which normally favors casting distance), chosen
  deliberately for **tighter spaces with structure close to shore** where he
  doesn't need maximum distance — a length-vs-maneuverability trade-off, not
  a compromise he's unhappy with. Secondary use: throwing umbrella rigs at
  the lake, aided by a **softer tip** that also lets him throw smaller
  topwater lures.
- **Lure — Daiwa HMKL jerkbait** (brand confirmed by the human-typed title,
  "Daiwa"; captions render the model as "HMKL," kept as captioned since it
  reads as a distinct model designator rather than a phonetically garbled
  word, but flagged `asr-uncertain` since the exact spelling isn't
  independently verified against a catalog). Fished in the surf. Chad's
  stated reasons for the pick: an "underrated" model that "doesn't sell
  really well" despite performing; a large weight-transfer system for long
  casts; a "circuit bill" on the lip; doesn't dive too deep even worked on a
  slow roll. **Observed** (tackle-express, 2025-09-09, SoCal surf, beach not
  named): corbina and "bigger perch" caught on this lure.
