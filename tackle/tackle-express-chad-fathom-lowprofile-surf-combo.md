# Penn Fathom Low Profile — Staff-Pick Surf/Lake Combo (Tackle Express)

A Penn Fathom Low Profile 300 baitcaster, an Okuma Seros 7'11" medium-heavy
rod, and a Daiwa HMKL jerkbait are run as one combo across lakes, the surf,
and calico bass (`Y5crw_rQpeg`). It is a shop "Staff Picks" segment for three
named products, capped at **low** confidence throughout per the confidence
rubric ("low — sponsored or promotional claim, regardless of channel"),
`tackle-express` being an unregistered channel on top of the promotional
framing (`Y5crw_rQpeg`) — kept here as a product/spec record, not fishery
decision logic.

## Reel — Penn Fathom Low Profile, 300 size

A low-profile baitcaster, distinct from Penn's round two-speed lever-drag
Fathom/Fathom II line documented in [Penn Fathom II and Penn
Authority](tackle-express-icast2022-penn-fathom2-authority.md) and
[Speedmaster vs. Fathom reel sizing](tackle-express-speedmaster-fathom-reel-sizing.md).
Casts well with minimal backlash, and a minor backlash clears easily; the
handle is a plus; runs the same combo across lakes, surf, and calico bass
("calos") (`Y5crw_rQpeg`).

Drag rating is inconsistent across the line size-for-size: the **300 size
carries 30 lb of drag**, the **400 size only 25 lb** — the smaller reel
out-drags the larger one, with no mechanism given for the inversion
("I'm not sure why... I'm not really a techy kind of guy") (`Y5crw_rQpeg`).

## Rod — Okuma Seros, 7'11" medium-heavy

Length stated on camera as "7'11"" ("711") — shorter than what's normally
considered a surf rod, a length class that usually favors casting distance
(`Y5crw_rQpeg`). The shorter length is a deliberate trade-off for tighter
spaces with structure close to shore, where maximum casting distance isn't
needed — not a compromise disliked by the pick (`Y5crw_rQpeg`).

Secondary use: throwing umbrella rigs at the lake; a softer tip also lets it
throw smaller topwater lures (`Y5crw_rQpeg`).

## Lure — Daiwa HMKL jerkbait

Brand confirmed as Daiwa by the on-screen title text; captions render the
model as "HMKL," kept as captioned since it reads as a distinct model
designator rather than a phonetically garbled word, but the exact spelling
isn't independently checked against a catalog (⚠ asr-uncertain)
(`Y5crw_rQpeg`).

Fished in the surf. Called "underrated" and a poor seller despite performing
well; built with a large weight-transfer system for long casts and a
"circuit bill" on the lip; doesn't dive too deep even worked on a slow roll
(`Y5crw_rQpeg`).
