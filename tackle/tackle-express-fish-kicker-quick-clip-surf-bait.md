# Fish Kicker 25 lb Quick Clips — Snapless Surf-Bait Rigging (Tackle Express)

The Fish Kicker 25-lb quick clip rigs a surf bait without a snap: one small
arm hooks through the bait's eye, and pushing the back of the clip
opens/releases it (`k23JzE3Fr-I`). It replaces a cross-lock snap on surf
baits specifically because a cross-lock closure can work open and lose the
bait; the clip has no such closure to fail (`k23JzE3Fr-I`).

## What's claimed

- **Product:** Fish Kicker 25-lb quick clips, sold in a small pack sized to
  drop straight into a tackle box (`k23JzE3Fr-I`). The clip also doubles as a
  general small-parts holder for other loose tackle-box items (`k23JzE3Fr-I`).
- **Mechanism:** one small arm hooks into the eye of the bait; pushing the
  back of the clip releases it, and the bait seats onto the clip when the arm
  is inserted through the eye (`k23JzE3Fr-I`).
- **Why it beats a cross-lock snap on a surf bait:** it is easy and simple to
  use, and it removes the cross-lock closure's failure mode — a cross-lock
  snap can work open and release the bait; this clip has no such closure
  (`k23JzE3Fr-I`).
- No species, leader material, or rod/reel pairing is given; the clip is
  shown as a rigging aid for surf baits generally, not tied to a specific
  surf-fishing setup.
