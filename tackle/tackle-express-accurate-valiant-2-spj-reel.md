# Accurate Valiant 2 SPJ — Centrifugal Brake, Strike Button, Single/Two-Speed (Tackle Express)

The Accurate Valiant 2 SPJ is a slow-pitch-jigging reel line offered in
single-speed and two-speed models, built around an automatic centrifugal
brake, a strike button, and a redesigned palm side (`A6-KPjRwGSQ`).

## Centrifugal brake

The centrifugal brake — called the "number one feature" — sits on the inside
of the palm side and acts automatically through the cast rather than at a
fixed setting: at the start of the cast the brake pushes out to regulate the
spool and help prevent backlash, and as the cast progresses and the lure's
weight pulls line out, the brake contracts, adding distance toward the end
of the cast (`A6-KPjRwGSQ`). No weight count or adjustment procedure is
given — contrast the [Accurate Tern 2's cast-control
brake](tackle-express-accurate-tern-2-reel.md), a different Accurate reel
line with a documented adjustable weight count (10 per side) on an analogous
spool-braking feature.

## Strike button

A strike button ships across "all these new Valiant 2 models," not
SPJ-specific to this clip (`A6-KPjRwGSQ`). It's framed as a guard against
over-striking, for an angler who "gets excited" and goes "a little too far"
(`A6-KPjRwGSQ`); the clip gives no detail on how the button engages or how
it interacts with drag.

## Palm-side redesign

The second-generation Valiant palm side removes the first-generation
Accurate reels' "two-tone scratchy look" — cuts and fine pieces — for a
redesign described as "extremely ergonomical with no sharp edges"
(`A6-KPjRwGSQ`). Cosmetic/ergonomic claim only; no further spec given.

## Model lineup

Valiant 2 SPJ models are offered in single-speed and two-speed
(`A6-KPjRwGSQ`).
