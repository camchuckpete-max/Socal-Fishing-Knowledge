## What's claimed

- **Automatic centrifugal braking** — called the "number one feature."
  Internal brakes on the inside of the palm side act automatically through
  the cast: at the **start** of the cast the brakes push out to regulate the
  spool and help prevent backlash; as the cast progresses and the lure's
  weight pulls line out, the brakes **contract**, described as letting the
  angler gain added distance toward the **end** of the cast. A progressive,
  self-adjusting brake through a single cast, not a fixed setting — no
  further mechanical detail (no weight count, no adjustment procedure) is
  given, contrast the [Accurate Tern 2's cast-control
  brake](tackle-express-accurate-tern-2-reel.md), a different Accurate reel
  line/clip that documents an adjustable weight count on an analogous
  spool-braking feature.
- **Strike button** — new across "all these new Valiant 2 models" (i.e. not
  SPJ-specific per this clip). Stated on-camera reason: it helps an angler
  who "gets excited" and goes "a little too far" — read as an
  anti-overreaction/over-strike safeguard. Not further mechanically
  described (no detail on how the button engages, or its interaction with
  drag).
- **Palm-side ergonomic redesign vs. first-generation Accurate reels** — the
  first-gen palm side had a "two-tone scratchy look" with cuts and fine
  pieces; the second-generation Valiant reels remove those, described as
  "extremely ergonomical with no sharp edges." A cosmetic/ergonomic claim,
  no further spec given.
- **Model availability:** Valiant 2 SPJ models are offered in both
  **single-speed** and **two-speed**.
