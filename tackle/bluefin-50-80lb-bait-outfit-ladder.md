# Bluefin 50–80 lb Bait Outfit Ladder — Three-Rig Quiver

Three bait outfits, one per line class, cover most of the 50–80 lb bluefin
bite. Rig all three at once and fish the heaviest first, stepping down only
when it isn't getting bit, rather than starting light and working up
(`Yd3J4igs-QA`). Together the three outfits "cover 80% of what we're doing"
for this grade (`Yd3J4igs-QA`).

## The three outfits

| Outfit | Reel | Line | Hook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | reel captioned turn-500-size (asr-uncertain model) | 65 lb Spectra + short fluorocarbon leader (length not given) | 2/0 (captioned "a to AH hook"; asr-uncertain reading) |
| 2 | Valiant 2600 narrow (model number asr-uncertain) | 50 lb test (mono vs. fluorocarbon not specified) | 4/0 (captioned "a for odd hook"; asr-uncertain reading) |
| 3 | Valiant 600 narrow | 60 lb fluorocarbon | 6/0 (captioned "a six I took"; asr-uncertain reading) |

(`Yd3J4igs-QA`) for the full row set. The segment separately frames the
ladder in general terms as fishing "forty, fifty, sixty pound test" —
numbers that don't match the three specific builds above (65 lb Spectra /
50 lb test / 60 lb fluorocarbon) (`Yd3J4igs-QA`).

## Bite strategy — start heavy, drop down

Fish the heaviest of the three outfits first; if it isn't getting bit, step
down through outfit 2 and then outfit 1 (lightest) rather than starting
light and working up: *"depending on the bite I like to start with the
heaviest line, no bite then drop down, rather than going from light to
heavy"* (`Yd3J4igs-QA`).
