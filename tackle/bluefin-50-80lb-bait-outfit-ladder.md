## Target grade and framing

**50–80 lb bluefin tuna.** Gary opens by framing the outfits as fishing
**"forty, fifty, sixty pound test"** — a general statement that does not line
up number-for-number with the three specific builds given immediately after
(65 lb Spectra / 50 lb test / 60 lb fluorocarbon). Preserved as stated rather
than reconciled — treat the "40/50/60" line as his rough framing of the
overall class range, not a fourth data point to force-fit against the three
named outfits below.

## The three outfits

- **Outfit 1:** reel captioned **"turn 500 size reel"** (asr-uncertain model

- **Outfit 3:** **Valiant 600 narrow** reel, **60 lb fluorocarbon**, hook
  captioned "a six I took" — read as a **6/0 hook**; asr-uncertain.

## Bite strategy — start heavy, drop down

**Stated doctrine:** *"depending on the bite I like to start with the
heaviest line, no bite then drop down, rather than going from light to
heavy."* Fish the heaviest of the three outfits first; if it isn't getting
bit, step down through outfit 2 and then outfit 1 (lightest) rather than
starting light and working up.

**His stated close:** these three bait outfits together "cover 80% of what
we're doing" for this grade.
