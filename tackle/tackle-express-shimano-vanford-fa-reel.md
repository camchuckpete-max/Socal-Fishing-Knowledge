# Shimano Vanford — MGL Rotor, CI4+ Body, and Infinity Drive (Tackle Express)

A Tackle Express feature rundown for the Shimano Vanford spinning reel, split
across two re-cut clips of one source recording (`cBATKqWkQws`, `q0it3pyUW6Q`).
The reel is the priciest of three compared in that recording; the other two
are never named. Title text calls it the **Vanford FA**, but the on-camera
reference only says "the Vanford lineup," so the FA suffix is unconfirmed on
camera. No size, gear ratio, drag figure, line capacity, or price is stated in
either clip; per the confidence rubric (sponsored/promotional claim from an
unregistered channel), this note is capped at **low** confidence in full.

## What's claimed

- **MGL rotor.** A magnesium/aluminum/carbon-fiber mix ("Magnalite") that
  trims rotor weight and bulk, pitched as less handle-turning fatigue and a
  smoother overall feel (`cBATKqWkQws`). The expansion is recorded as stated
  but is unverified against a second source in this clip: "Magnalite" is
  also an unrelated cast-aluminum cookware brand, and this three-material mix
  does not match how Shimano's MGL rotor is described elsewhere in this KB's
  sources (see Related).
- **CI4+ body.** A lighter, reinforced carbon fiber claimed **2.5× more
  rigid** than "the first generation" (no baseline model or year given),
  pitched as durable enough to take abuse and keep producing (`q0it3pyUW6Q`;
  not reached in the shorter `cBATKqWkQws` cut).
- **Infinity Drive.** Reduces handle-turning friction, pitched as smoother,
  more even line lay with minimal issues (`q0it3pyUW6Q` only, past
  `cBATKqWkQws`'s cutoff).
