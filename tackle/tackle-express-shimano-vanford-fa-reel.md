## What's claimed

- **MGL rotor.** Presenter expands the initialism as "Magnalite" and
  describes the rotor as a magnesium/aluminum/carbon-fiber mix. This
  expansion is recorded as stated, but flagged `asr-uncertain`: "Magnalite"
  is also an unrelated cast-aluminum cookware brand, and the claimed
  three-material mix doesn't match how Shimano's MGL rotor is described
  elsewhere in this KB's sources (see Related below) — unverifiable against a
  second source in this clip. Claimed effect: a lighter, more compact rotor,
  pitched as less handle-turning fatigue and a smoother overall feel.
- **CI4+ body.** Described as a lighter, reinforced carbon fiber, claimed
  **2.5× more rigid** than "the first generation" (no baseline model or year
  given). Pitched as durable enough to take abuse and keep producing
  (`q0it3pyUW6Q` only — past `cBATKqWkQws`'s cutoff).
- **Infinity Drive** (`q0it3pyUW6Q` only, not reached in the shorter
  `cBATKqWkQws` cut). Described as reduced handle-turning friction, pitched
  as smoother, more even line lay with minimal issues.
