## What's claimed

- **MGL rotor.** Presenter states "MGL stands for Magnalite" and describes it
  as "a magnesium aluminum and carbon fiber kind of mix between them all."
  This expansion of the initialism is recorded as stated, but is flagged
  `asr-uncertain`: "Magnalite" is also the name of an unrelated cast-aluminum
  cookware brand, and the claimed material mix (magnesium/aluminum/carbon
  fiber) does not match how Shimano's MGL rotor is described elsewhere in
  this KB's sources (see the Related note below) — this could not be verified
  against a second source in this clip. Claimed effect: a lighter, more
  compact rotor, which is pitched as less handle-turning fatigue and "a much
  nicer, smoother fishing operation" overall.
- **CI4+ body.** Called "a lighter reinforced carbon fiber." Claimed that
  "this new gen CI4+ is 2 and 1/2 times more rigid than the first
  generation" (no baseline model or year given for "first generation").
