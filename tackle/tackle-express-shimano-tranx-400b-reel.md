## What's claimed — B version vs. the previous A version

- **SVS MDTune braking system.** Eight internal pins, adjustable up/down
  inside the reel, **plus an external dial** that lets you fine-tune the cast
  brake on the fly for changing conditions — called out as the most impressive
  of the three upgrades.
- **Solid core body** (new on the B version). Stated to keep the gears,
  bearings, and internal components meshing properly over a long period of
  time, extending reel life; also said to transfer more feel/energy into the
  palm of the hand — called out specifically for bass fishing and reading a
  swimbait bite.
- **X-Protect** (an upgrade from the previous version's Core Protect). Said to
  make it much harder for water to reach the gears and bearings, which is
  credited with making those components feel better and last longer.

## Other stated specs

- Available in **right- and left-handed** versions.
- Pitched as suited to **both saltwater and freshwater** applications.
