## The two classes, by mechanism

- **Star drag — better free spool, better cast.** A star drag reel's spool
  disengages more cleanly from the drag system, so it spins freer both for
  **fly-lining baits** (the source names anchovies and sardines) and for
  **casting surface lures**. This is the same free-spool mechanism already
  documented in more depth (spool weight/stiffness, the out-of-gear
  light-touch test) in [rod & reel selection — flyline reel & rod
  selection](rod-and-reel-selection.md#flyline-reel--rod-selection).
- **Lever drag — stronger, better drag system; not as good a free spool.** The
  trade-off runs the other way: a lever drag reel gives up some free-spool
  performance for a drag system the source calls stronger and better overall.
- **Lever drag's stated advantage: on-the-fly drag adjustment during the
  fight.** The source's own reasoning is that you can adjust drag with a
  lever drag **"during the flight of the fish easier,"** with the option for
  more or less drag, **"very easily done"** — i.e., mid-fight adjustability,
  not just raw drag strength, is called out as the reason to prefer lever
  drag once a fish is on. This is a distinct point from the free-spool/
  casting trade-off above and isn't duplicated elsewhere in this KB as of
  this note.
- **Two-speed option, named specifically for bluefin.** The source closes by
  pairing the "better drag system" side of lever drag with a **two-speed
  option for catching bluefin** — consistent with, and not adding a new
  parameter beyond, the two-speed-for-bluefin doctrine already covered in
  [two-speed reel technique](../techniques/two-speed-low-gear-fight.md) and
  the multi-day-trip framing in [rod & reel selection — two-speed reels for
  multi-day bluefin
  trips](rod-and-reel-selection.md#two-speed-reels-for-multi-day-bluefin-trips--fishermans-landing--searcher-counter-tip-searchersportfishing).

## Reach for this when

- **Star drag** — flylining live bait (anchovies, sardines) or casting a
  surface lure, where getting bit depends on how freely the spool pays out
  or how far the cast carries.
- **Lever drag** — once the priority shifts to fighting the fish: adjusting
  drag pressure through the fight, or stepping up to a two-speed lever-drag
  reel for bluefin-grade fish.
