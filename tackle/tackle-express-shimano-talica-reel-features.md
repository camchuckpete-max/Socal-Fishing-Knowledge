# Shimano Talica — S Compact Body, Infinity Drive, Hagane Body (Tackle Express)

An 88-second Tackle Express feature rundown (`af7a1tR2B84`, presenter
unnamed) for the redesigned Talica series, with no model size (e.g., Talica
12/16/20/25), gear ratio, max drag figure, line capacity, or price given.
Straight product-feature marketing copy with no technique, sizing, or
decision content — per the confidence rubric ("low — sponsored or
promotional claim, regardless of channel") this note is capped at **low** in
full, on top of `tackle-express` being an unregistered channel.

## What's claimed

- **S Compact body.** Reduces overall reel size **6%** versus the prior
  generation (no specific prior model named for the comparison) and makes
  the reel **25% smaller on the palm side**, pitched as easier to palm and
  fight big fish comfortably over a full day on the water (`af7a1tR2B84`,
  `sl2MRqpiS60`).
- **40% reduced line-turning rotation.** A fight-endurance feature: less
  rotational resistance is said to let the handle turn smoother and easier
  under a long battle, with less resistance/fatigue on the angler
  ("line-turning rotation" is the clip's own phrase — it does not clarify
  whether this is spool rotational resistance or handle-turn resistance
  specifically, preserved as stated rather than resolved) (`af7a1tR2B84`).
- **Infinity Drive.** "Extremely powerful ... with no binding effect":
  mechanism as stated is a bearing supported on a shielded shaft, rather than
  the shaft riding directly on the bearing, giving better power delivery,
  less binding, and a reel that feels solid/rigid ("no tweaking") from the
  start to the finish of a fight (`af7a1tR2B84`). Shimano's Infinity Drive is
  named, with an overlapping reduced-friction/consistent-handle-turning
  description, on a different, unnamed-model Shimano reel elsewhere in this
  channel's catalog — see [CI4+ reel
  features](tackle-express-ci4-plus-reel-features.md). Neither clip states
  whether it's the identical mechanism across model lines, so that is kept as
  a separate citation rather than merged into one claim.
- **Hagane body.** Described on camera only as "one of Shimano's potent
  options ... that's in almost all of their reels," with no further
  mechanical detail (`af7a1tR2B84`; captioned "Hagan" — corrected per the ASR
  hazard rule to Shimano's actual Hagane Body material/feature name, since
  there is no such Shimano component as "Hagan" and the clip's own
  description — a core body material used across nearly all of Shimano's
  reels — matches it). A separate clip adds the mechanical detail this one
  omits: a high-end, rigid material chosen to keep the frame from binding or
  flexing under load, holding "the most solid composition it can" with "no
  flexing or binding whatsoever" (`sl2MRqpiS60`).
- **Core Protect.** Applied to the inside of the reel's gears, specifically
  the **main gear and pinion**, described as a water-resistant coating — the
  same coating already documented on the [Shimano Nasci
  FC](tackle-express-shimano-nasci-fc-reel.md) (`pL7ZGuMVwo4`; captioned
  "Corprotect" — corrected per the ASR hazard rule to Shimano's actual Core
  Protect feature name). The clip cuts off mid-sentence right after "it's
  going to repel the [...]" — the word being repelled (water, presumably) and
  any corrosion-protection framing are not actually spoken, so neither is
  asserted here; no further mechanism detail, and no model/size, is given.

No model size, gear ratio, max drag figure, line capacity, or price is given
in any of the three clips.
