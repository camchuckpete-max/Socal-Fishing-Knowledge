## What's claimed

- **New S Compact body.** Reduces overall reel size by **6%** versus the
  prior generation (no specific prior model named for the comparison).
- **40% reduced line-turning rotation.** Framed as a fight-endurance feature:
  under a long battle, the reduced rotational resistance is said to let the
  handle turn smoother and easier, with less resistance/fatigue on the
  angler. ("Line turning rotation" is the clip's own phrase; it does not
  clarify whether this is spool rotational resistance or handle-turn
  resistance specifically — preserved as stated rather than resolved.)
- **25% smaller on the palm side.** Pitched as making the reel easier to palm
  and fight big fish comfortably over a full day on the water.
- **Infinity Drive.** Called "extremely powerful ... with no binding effect."
  Mechanism as stated: a bearing supported on a **shielded shaft**, rather
  than the shaft riding directly on the bearing — said to give better power
  delivery, less binding, and a reel that feels solid/rigid ("no tweaking")
  from the start to the finish of a fight. Shimano's Infinity Drive is also
  named, with an overlapping "reduced friction / consistent handle turning"
  description, in a different, unnamed-model Shimano clip from this same
  channel — see [CI4+ reel features](tackle-express-ci4-plus-reel-features.md).
  Neither clip states whether it's the identical mechanism across model
  lines, so this is kept as a second, separate citation rather than merged
  into one claim.
- **Hagane body.** Captioned "Hagan" — corrected here per the ASR hazard rule
  to Shimano's actual material/feature name (there is no such Shimano
  component as "Hagan"; the clip's own description — a core body material
  used across nearly all of Shimano's reels — matches Shimano's Hagane Body
  line). Described on camera only as "one of Shimano's potent options ...
  that's in almost all of their reels," with no further mechanical detail
  given.

No model size (e.g., Talica 12/16/20/25), gear ratio, max drag figure, line
capacity, or price is given in this clip.
