# Premade P-Line Rock Cod Rig — Two Swivels, Tie and Drop (Tackle Express)

A P-Line premade rock cod rig is a factory-tied gangion-and-swivel package —
the packaged alternative to hand-tying a [dropper-loop
gangion](../techniques/dropper-loop.md#rock-cod-quick-gangion) at the rail.
The pitch is rigging speed: tie two connections and fish, rather than tying
loops and hooks on the boat (`aqZxUmefwcw`, `qh3hL2Dt3HY`).

## Package construction

Two swivels bookend the pre-tied leader: the top swivel is for tying on the
main line, the bottom swivel carries a **clip** for a torpedo sinker. The
hooks — a [dropper-loop gangion](../techniques/dropper-loop.md#rock-cod-quick-gangion)
— come already tied into the leader between the two swivels, so no rigging
happens on the boat beyond those two connections (`aqZxUmefwcw`,
`qh3hL2Dt3HY`).

## Deploying it

Tie the main line to the top swivel, clip a torpedo sinker to the bottom
swivel's clip, bait the hooks — a small piece of squid works — and drop
straight to the bottom (`aqZxUmefwcw`).

## What it doesn't specify

No sinker weight, hook size/style, or leader line class is given on camera.
Those follow the general rock-cod dropper-loop parameters in [dropper
loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md#rock-cod-quick-gangion) and [rockfish
leader line](tackle-express-rockfish-leader-line.md) rather than being
specific to the premade package.
