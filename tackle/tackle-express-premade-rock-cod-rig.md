# Premade P-Line Rock Cod Rig — Two Swivels, Tie and Drop (Tackle Express)

Tackle Express (`aqZxUmefwcw`, "Stop wasting time rigging on the boat!,"
uploaded 2026-03-31; unregistered channel, single mention — medium
confidence). A 30-second shop-counter clip on a **P-Line premade rock cod
rig** — the packaged, factory-tied alternative to hand-tying a
[dropper-loop gangion](../techniques/dropper-loop.md#rock-cod-quick-gangion-dave-hansen-build),
already namedropped as an option ("a premade Berkley or P-Line rock cod
rig") in [rockfish leader line](tackle-express-rockfish-leader-line.md).
This clip is the first source in the corpus to describe the package's actual
construction rather than just naming it as an option.

- **Package construction — two swivels.** The backside of the package carries
  a swivel at each end of the pre-tied leader: one swivel at the top for
  **tying on your main line**, and a second swivel with a **clip** at the
  bottom for **attaching a torpedo sinker**. The hooks (a dropper-loop
  gangion, per the leader-line note above) come already tied into the leader
  between the two swivels — no rigging done on the boat beyond those two
  connections.
- **Deploying it:** tie your main line to the top swivel, clip a torpedo
  sinker to the bottom swivel's clip, bait the hooks (his example: a small
  piece of squid), and drop straight to the bottom.
- **The stated point of the product** (per the video's title) is saving
  on-the-boat rigging time — tie two connections and fish, rather than
  tying loops and hooks at the rail.

No sinker weight, hook size/style, or leader line class is given on camera —
those follow the general rock-cod dropper-loop parameters in
[dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md#rock-cod-quick-gangion-dave-hansen-build)
and [rockfish leader line](tackle-express-rockfish-leader-line.md) rather
than being specific to the premade package.
