# Baja Light Setup — Bass/Halibut Rod, Leader Upsized as Yellowtail Insurance (Tackle Express)

A light bass/halibut rod is fished as-is for
[calico](../species/calico-bass.md) and
[sand bass](../species/sand-bass.md) plus
[halibut](../species/california-halibut.md); only the leader is upsized as
insurance against a hooked [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md), instead of
swapping to a dedicated yellowtail outfit (`SL4zZbzdXNA`). It is the lightest
of four rods carried on a Cedros Island, Baja Pacific (`baja-pacific-north`)
trip (`_C7TJoGT0nw`).

## The setup

- **Application:** bass fishing in shallower water — not a dedicated
  yellowtail outfit (`SL4zZbzdXNA`).
- **Rod:** rated 15–30 lb (`SL4zZbzdXNA`).
- **Reel:** Shimano Tranx 400 (`SL4zZbzdXNA`, `_C7TJoGT0nw`).
- **Braid:** 50 lb, spooled to roughly 300 yd (`SL4zZbzdXNA`).
- **Leader:** 15 ft fluorocarbon, tied at 40 lb (`SL4zZbzdXNA`).

## Leader upsized for abrasion resistance

The leader is tied heavier than the rod's own 15–30 lb rating — 40 lb, for
"more abrasion resistance in case you hook that yellowtail" while bass
fishing (`SL4zZbzdXNA`). Only the leader moves up; the rod, reel, and braid
stay at the bass/halibut spec above rather than stepping up to a dedicated
yellowtail outfit.
