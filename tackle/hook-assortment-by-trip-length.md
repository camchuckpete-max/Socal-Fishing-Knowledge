# Hook Assortment by Trip Length — Overnight Through 5–6 Day

A minimal hook box, sized by species and bait rather than a long shopping
list, covers overnight through 3–4 day trips; most of the same hooks carry
over to 5–6 day trips to Guadalupe or, asr-uncertain, Alijos Rocks (rendered
"Leos rocks" in the captions — the school-size-tuna framing matches this
corpus's other Alijos Rocks trip material, e.g.
[trolling — Alijos Rocks, Baja](../techniques/trolling.md#alijos-rocks-baja--bomb-lure-trolling-for-wahoo);
not confidently resolved beyond that inference) for school-size tuna
(`fg2v1kxoTMA`). This is a checklist note — the underlying style/size
selection logic (circle vs. J, bait-sets-the-size) lives in
[hooks](hooks.md) and isn't repeated here.

The models below are demonstrated on Owner Mutu-family hooks "just to keep
everything simple"; brand is a free choice — "you can change out whatever
your favorite brand of hooks are going to be" (`fg2v1kxoTMA`). Treat the
specific model/size numbers as one demonstrated reference set, not a claim
that a given brand is required.

## Yellowtail — kelp paddy / offshore, J hook

- **J hook**, sized **No. 2 up to 2/0** (`fg2v1kxoTMA`).
- The hook demonstrated for this is rendered **"94150"** in the captions —
  asr-uncertain on the brand: the sentence covers Owner Mutu hooks, but
  94150 is not an Owner catalog number. It matches the
  [Mustad 94150 / 94151](hooks.md#mustad-94150--94151--the-historical-anchovysardine-j-hook-baseline-searchersportfishing-tackle-tip-thursday-vol-100-test-of-time-scqnyveqfhy-2020-05-21-searcher-sportfishing-registered-channel--productmodel-pick-low-confidence-per-the-channels-registry-sponsored-claim-caveat),
  already logged in this KB as the classic No. 2 anchovy/sardine J-hook
  baseline (`fg2v1kxoTMA`) — a second, independently dated citation
  (2020-05-21 and 2022-07-21) for the same model number on the same species,
  kept as a separate data point rather than merged since the brand
  attribution here isn't fully resolved.
- Good for **school-size yellowtail, school-size yellowfin, and school-size
  bluefin up to about 20–30 lb** (`fg2v1kxoTMA`).

## Bigger bluefin — step up to circle

- Bigger fish in the same species set call for a **circle hook**, matching
  this KB's circle-for-big-bluefin doctrine (`fg2v1kxoTMA`; see
  [circle vs. J — the core decision](hooks.md#circle-vs-j--the-core-decision-swrsycmt4tw-m2q22sppkem)).
- **Bait size drives hook size**, matching the bait-sets-the-hook-size rule
  in [hooks — size](hooks.md#size--bait-first-fish-second). Most of the time
  the hook is matched to the bait; a **stealth rig** — a bigger bait fished
  on a smaller hook — is the exception, not the default (`fg2v1kxoTMA`).
- Most common circle hook size as of this 2022-07-21 upload: **No. 1 or
  No. 2 Owner Mutu circle hook, or 1/0** (`fg2v1kxoTMA`).

## Sinker rig — bigger bait

- On a tuna [sinker rig](../rigging/rubber-band-deep-rig.md), step up to a
  **3/0 or 4/0 circle hook** when fishing a bigger bait — a larger sardine
  or mackerel (`fg2v1kxoTMA`). This runs bigger than the 1/0–2/0 sinker-rig
  circle-hook range already logged in that note; kept here as a further
  attributed data point rather than merged into one fixed number.
