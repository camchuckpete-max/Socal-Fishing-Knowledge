# Shimano Tranx 300B — SVS Infinity Braking System, Exterior Dial + 8-Pin Internal Block (Tackle Express)

The Shimano Tranx 300B's braking system is two separate controls: an
exterior dial that fine-tunes cast braking without opening the reel, and an
internal brake block stepped up from six pins on the previous generation to
eight pins on this reel (`qLDrhgE7-y0`). This is a straight product-feature
clip on the braking system alone, with no model size beyond 300B, gear
ratio, max drag, line capacity, price, or application stated, so per the
confidence rubric it is capped at low, on top of `tackle-express` being an
unregistered channel (`qLDrhgE7-y0`).

## Braking system

- **SVS Infinity.** Captioned "SVS Infiniti MD tune braking system" — read
  here as Shimano's SVS Infinity braking-system name (the auto-caption
  "Infiniti" is not a Shimano term); "MD tune" is preserved exactly as
  captioned and not otherwise explained in the clip, so it is not expanded
  further here (`qLDrhgE7-y0`). The system carries over from the previous
  Conquest lineup (`qLDrhgE7-y0`).
- **Exterior braking dial.** Sits on the outside of the reel as the
  fine-tuning control, adjustable without opening the reel to reach the
  internal brakes (`qLDrhgE7-y0`).
- **Internal brake block.** Reached by opening the reel; stepped up from six
  pins in the previous generation to eight pins on this reel (`qLDrhgE7-y0`).
- **Stated effect.** The two systems together are claimed to give a "better,
  more efficient, longer cast with fewer backlashes" (`qLDrhgE7-y0`); the
  exterior dial is framed specifically as letting the angler fine-tune
  "without having to open up the internals of the reel every time"
  (`qLDrhgE7-y0`).
