## Outfit 1 — 25 lb, 7 ft rod ("old faithful") — kelp-paddy live bait, doubles as a bait-catching rig

No brand/model is named on camera for this outfit, unlike the three heavier
outfits below.

- **Reel/rod:** a **25 lb-class outfit** on a **7 ft rod**.
- **Application 1 — kelp-paddy live bait:** for **smaller school-size
  yellowfin or bluefin tuna, dorado, or yellowtail on kelp paddies**. Rig: a
  **2/0 hook** tied to the end of the line, with **maybe a piece of 25 lb
  fluorocarbon** leader, [fly-lining](../techniques/flyline.md) a bait while
  drifting.
- **Application 2 — bait catching:** re-rigged with a **sabiki rig** and a
  **4 oz or 8 oz sinker**, used to replenish the boat's **mackerel** bait
  supply when needed. See [making bait](../bait/making-bait.md) and [fishing
  live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md) for the general bait-catching and
  bait-care doctrine this application feeds.

  — so this is now a third, independent same-channel citation of the same
  corruption, corroborating the Talica read rather than left fully unresolved
  — flagged `asr-uncertain` per this KB's convention for a corroborated but
  not title-confirmed brand read.
- **Line:** **60 lb**, or as much as **80 lb** test.
- **Application 1 — casting:** a [wahoo bomb](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md).
- **Application 2 — yo-yo iron for yellowtail:** named jig picks, **Salas 6X
  blue and white** or **Salas 6X scrambled egg** (captioned "salus 6x" — the

  this is a corroborating data point, not a new one).
- **Application 3 — fly-lining bigger tuna:** **60 lb** test for bigger
  bluefin tuna and possibly yellowfin tuna.
- **Application 4 — offshore sinker rig:** a **torpedo sinker and a rubber

  the trip's applications change.

## Outfit 4 — Talica 25, 80–100 lb test — Guadalupe Island, three-application outfit

- **Reel:** captioned "italica uh 25" — read as **Shimano Talica 25** (same
  channel-level "italica"→Talica corroboration as Outfit 3 above, flagged
  `asr-uncertain` on the same basis), spooled with **80 or 100 lb test**.
- **Context:** named on camera for "the trips that we're on right now" —
  **Guadalupe Island** — the only outfit in this video with an explicit place
  name.
- **Application 1 — fly-lining big yellowfin:** **80 to 100 lb yellowfin
  tuna**, fished with **60 or 80 lb test**, [fly-lining](../techniques/flyline.md)
  a sardine.
- **Application 2 — bottom dropper loop for yellowtail:** a **16-ounce
  sinker**, a **6/0 or 7/0 hook**, and a **nose-hooked sardine** dropped to
  the bottom on a [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md). This matches,
  almost parameter-for-parameter, the **16 oz** sinker minimum and the
  **6/0–9/0**-range hook already logged for the same Guadalupe/Baja-Peninsula
  giant-yellowtail dropper-loop rig in [surgeon's loop (giant yellowtail,
  long-range) — sinker & hook](../rigging/surgeons-loop.md#sinker--hook-cdjt_i_pbhq)
  — see the addendum there.
- **Application 3 — trolling:** if headed to "the ridge," troll with "your
  own **marauder** all rigged up" — a named trolling-lure brand, no rigging
  detail (size, color, leader) given on camera. The Searcher carries trolling
  outfits to borrow, but this outfit doubles as a personal [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md)
  rig for an angler who brings their own. No specs are given for the Marauder
  itself, so it is not logged as a distinct lure entry — see [wahoo](../species/wahoo.md)
  for this KB's other named wahoo-trolling lures at "the Ridge."
- **Aside — bigger bluefin:** if there's a chance of encountering bigger
  bluefin, this same outfit also doubles for [flat-fall jigging](../techniques/flat-fall-jigging.md)
  at night.
