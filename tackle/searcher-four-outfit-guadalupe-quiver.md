# Searcher Four-Outfit Quiver — Fisherman's Landing Rental Packages

Four rod/reel outfits Fisherman's Landing rents to anglers without their own
tackle, framed on camera as what to have before leaving the dock rather than
bringing "the whole kitchen sink" (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`). The set runs a 25 lb
kelp-paddy/bait-catching combo, a 40 lb flyline outfit for larger tuna, a
60–80 lb four-application outfit, and an 80–100 lb outfit built around
Guadalupe Island trips. No trip length is stated on camera generally, but
Outfit 4 is framed around "the trips that we're on right now" — Guadalupe
Island — placing the video within a Guadalupe-season window at its
2021-10-07 upload date (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).

## Outfit 1 — 25 lb, 7 ft rod — kelp-paddy live bait / bait-catching

- **Reel/rod:** a 25 lb-class outfit on a 7 ft rod; no brand or model named
  (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- **Kelp-paddy live bait:** for smaller school-size yellowfin or bluefin
  tuna, dorado, or yellowtail on kelp paddies. Rig: a 2/0 hook tied to the
  end of the line, with maybe a piece of 25 lb fluorocarbon leader,
  [fly-lining](../techniques/flyline.md) a bait while drifting (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- **Bait catching:** re-rigged with a sabiki rig and a 4 oz or 8 oz sinker,
  used to replenish the boat's mackerel bait supply when needed
  (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`). See [making bait](../bait/making-bait.md) and [fishing
  live bait](../bait/fishing-live-bait.md) for the general bait-catching and
  bait-care doctrine this application feeds.

## Outfit 2 — Penn Fathom 40 (or 30), two-speed, 40 lb test — larger-tuna fly-lining

- **Reel:** a Penn Fathom 40, two-speed — "it could be a 30" is named on
  camera as an acceptable substitute — spooled with 40 lb test (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- **Application:** [fly-lining](../techniques/flyline.md) baits with 40 lb
  fluorocarbon leader and maybe a circle hook, for larger tuna up to 80 lb
  (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).

This Penn Fathom 40 is a different named reel size than the Penn Fathom 25
Narrow logged for a 40 lb-class all-around tuna outfit in [Searcher 40 lb
all-around tuna outfit](searcher-40lb-all-around-tuna-outfit.md) (a different
presenter and video) — kept as a separate data point, since a Fathom 40 and a
Fathom 25 Narrow are different-sized reels even on the same 40 lb line class
(`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).

## Outfit 3 — Talica 20, two-speed, 60–80 lb test — four-application outfit

- **Reel:** captioned "italica 20 two speed" — read as Shimano Talica 20,
  two-speed ⚠ asr-uncertain; this channel's captions garble Talica the same
  way in multiple other citations already logged in this KB (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- **Line:** 60 lb, or as much as 80 lb test (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- **Casting:** a [wahoo bomb](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md)
  (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- **Yo-yo iron for yellowtail:** Salas 6X blue and white or Salas 6X
  scrambled egg (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`); see [iron jigs](../lures/iron-jigs.md) and
  [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md).
- **Fly-lining bigger tuna:** 60 lb test for bigger bluefin tuna and possibly
  yellowfin tuna (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- **Offshore sinker rig:** a torpedo sinker and a rubber band to get down to
  depth — the breakaway rubber-band mechanic is documented in full in
  [rubber-band deep rig](../rigging/rubber-band-deep-rig.md) (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- Framed on camera as swappable top shots on the same braid/backing as the
  trip's applications change (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).

## Outfit 4 — Talica 25, 80–100 lb test — Guadalupe Island, three-application outfit

- **Reel:** captioned "italica uh 25" — read as Shimano Talica 25 ⚠
  asr-uncertain (same channel-level "italica"-for-Talica corroboration as
  Outfit 3), spooled with 80 or 100 lb test (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- **Context:** named on camera for "the trips that we're on right now" —
  Guadalupe Island — the only outfit in this video with an explicit place
  name (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- **Fly-lining big yellowfin:** 80 to 100 lb yellowfin tuna, fished with 60
  or 80 lb test, [fly-lining](../techniques/flyline.md) a sardine
  (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
- **Bottom dropper loop for yellowtail:** a 16-ounce sinker, a 6/0 or 7/0
  hook, and a nose-hooked sardine dropped to the bottom on a [dropper
  loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`) — matches, almost
  parameter-for-parameter, the 16 oz sinker minimum and 6/0–9/0-range hook
  logged for the same Guadalupe/Baja-Peninsula giant-yellowtail dropper-loop
  rig in [surgeon's loop (giant yellowtail,
  long-range) — parameters](../rigging/surgeons-loop.md#parameters).
- **Trolling:** if headed to "the ridge," troll with "your own marauder all
  rigged up" — no size, color, or leader detail given on camera
  (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`). The Searcher carries trolling outfits to borrow, but this
  outfit doubles as a personal [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md) rig for
  an angler who brings their own. See [wahoo](../species/wahoo.md) for this
  KB's other named wahoo-trolling lures at "the Ridge."
- **Aside — bigger bluefin:** if there's a chance of encountering bigger
  bluefin, this same outfit also doubles for [flat-fall
  jigging](../techniques/flat-fall-jigging.md) at night (`zuAuk-Kfa1Y`).
