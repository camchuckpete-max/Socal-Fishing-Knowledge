# Searcher 6-to-8-Day Trip — the Fourth (Heavy) Outfit

The Searcher's standing minimum for a 1.5-to-4 day trip is three two-speed
outfits at 25, 40, and 60 lb class ([Searcher three-outfit minimum
quiver](searcher-three-outfit-minimum-quiver.md)). Stepping up to a 6-to-8
day trip adds this fourth, heavier rail-rod outfit: "your minimum rod and
reel combos for a 6-8 day trip" becomes four (`MPC_OQjvO-o`). One 80–100 lb
combo covers four jobs — flylining large tuna, dropper-loop yellowtail,
trolling for wahoo, and personal kite fishing (`MPC_OQjvO-o`). The Searcher
runs these 6-to-8 day trips in summer and fall, year 2020 by upload date; ⚠
asr-uncertain: the on-camera season wording is garbled (`MPC_OQjvO-o`).

## The outfit

- **Rod — Seeker OSP 1x3 rail rod** ("the Guadalupe special" — see [gear
  classes — rail-rod
  class](gear-classes.md#rail-rod-class--what-makes-a-rod-a-rail-rod-mechanism)),
  rated **80–100 lb** on camera (`MPC_OQjvO-o`); the same rod is logged at
  **60–100 lb** elsewhere in this KB, ranges overlapping rather than matching
  exactly (gear-classes.md).
- **Reel — three interchangeable options:**
  - **Penn International 30, two-speed lever drag** (`MPC_OQjvO-o`). ⚠
    asr-uncertain: the model suffix (e.g., VS/VSX) is not confidently
    resolved from the caption.
  - **Accurate 50, two-speed twin drag** — named as usable in the same
    application (`MPC_OQjvO-o`).
  - **Shimano TLD 30, 80 lb test** — plausibly the boat's own loaner/rental
    gear (see [Searcher lever-drag reel
    sizing](searcher-lever-drag-reel-sizing.md)); ⚠ asr-uncertain, not
    confidently resolved from the caption (`MPC_OQjvO-o`).
- **Line — 100 or 130 lb Spectra** braid main line, with an **80 lb top
  shot** (`MPC_OQjvO-o`).

## Applications

Four stated uses for this one outfit (`MPC_OQjvO-o`):

1. **Guadalupe Island, large tuna — flylining.** Fished as an 80 lb outfit
   to [flyline](../techniques/flyline.md) a live sardine to large tuna
   (`MPC_OQjvO-o`). The same Guadalupe/large-tuna flyline application, on a
   different outfit, is logged in [Searcher four-outfit Guadalupe quiver —
   Outfit
   4](searcher-four-outfit-guadalupe-quiver.md#outfit-4--talica-25-80100-lb-test--guadalupe-island-three-application-outfit).
2. **"The ridge" or "the coast" — [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md)
   for yellowtail.** 80 or 100 lb test to a 16 oz sinker on the bottom; no
   hook size given on camera (`MPC_OQjvO-o`). "The ridge" and "the coast"
   are named with no further location detail. The 16 oz sinker figure
   matches the giant-yellowtail dropper-loop rig in [Searcher four-outfit
   Guadalupe quiver — Outfit
   4](searcher-four-outfit-guadalupe-quiver.md#outfit-4--talica-25-80100-lb-test--guadalupe-island-three-application-outfit)
   and [surgeon's loop — parameters](../rigging/surgeons-loop.md#parameters).
3. **Trolling, "the ridge or the rocks" — wahoo.** On a 6-to-8 day trip, this
   outfit doubles as a personal [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md) rig for
   wahoo, for anglers who prefer their own setup over the boat's own trolling
   outfits; no lure is named (`MPC_OQjvO-o`).
4. **Guadalupe Island — kite fishing.** Spooled with 100 lb Spectra and a
   short leader as a personal [kite](../techniques/kite-fishing.md) outfit;
   the boat rigs a kite leader for an angler bringing this setup, and also
   carries its own kite outfits for anglers who don't (`MPC_OQjvO-o`). The
   100 lb figure sits at the low end of the 100 lb top-shot-minimum floor
   for a personal kite outfit logged from a different Searcher session —
   that citation specs 130 lb main braid behind the top shot, a figure this
   outfit's single "100 lb Spectra" spec doesn't separately give ([Searcher
   spring bluefin/yellowtail quiver — kite
   outfit](searcher-spring-bluefin-yellowtail-quiver.md#kite-outfit-boat-supplied-or-bring-your-own)).
