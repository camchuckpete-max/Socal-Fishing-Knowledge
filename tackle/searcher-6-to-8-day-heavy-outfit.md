## The heavy outfit

- **Rod:** a **Seeker rail rod**, captioned "a 1 by 3" — read as the same
  **Seeker OSP 1x3** rail rod already logged repeatedly elsewhere in this KB,
  nicknamed "the Guadalupe special" in [gear classes — rail-rod
  class](gear-classes.md#rail-rod-class--what-makes-a-rod-a-rail-rod-mechanism)
  — a further, independent citation of the same rod. Rated **80 to 100 lb**
  on camera (in range with, though not identical to, the 60–100 lb rating
  already logged for the same rod elsewhere in this KB — kept side by side
  rather than reconciled to one figure).
- **Reel — three named options, stated as interchangeable for this
  application:**
  1. A **Penn International 30**, captioned "...uh V" — not confidently
     resolved to a specific model suffix (e.g. VS/VSX), flagged
     `asr-uncertain` — **two-speed, lever drag.**
  2. An **Accurate 50**, **two-speed twin drag** — named as able to "also be
     used in the same application."
  3. A **Shimano TLD 30** with **80 lb test**, drawn from what the captions
     render as "some of our longer" — plausibly "loaner" gear (the boat's own
     rental/loaner fleet, as documented elsewhere in this KB, e.g. [Searcher
     lever-drag reel sizing](searcher-lever-drag-reel-sizing.md)) — not
     confidently resolved, flagged `asr-uncertain`.
- **Line:** **100 lb or 130 lb Spectra** (braid) as the main line, with an
  **80 lb top shot.**

## Four stated applications for this one outfit

1. **Guadalupe Island, large tuna — flylining:** fished as an **80 lb
   outfit** for [flylining](../techniques/flyline.md) a live sardine to
   large tuna. Matches the general Guadalupe/large-tuna flyline application
   already logged (different outfit, same trip context) in [Searcher
   four-outfit Guadalupe quiver — Outfit
   4](searcher-four-outfit-guadalupe-quiver.md#outfit-4--talica-25-80100-lb-test--guadalupe-island-three-application-outfit).
2. **"The ridge" or "the coast" — dropper loop for yellowtail:** **80 lb or
   100 lb test**, a **16 oz sinker** to the bottom, for yellowtail. No hook
   size given on camera. The **16 oz sinker** figure matches, parameter for
   parameter, the Guadalupe/Baja-Peninsula giant-yellowtail dropper-loop rig
   already logged in [Searcher four-outfit Guadalupe quiver — Outfit
   4](searcher-four-outfit-guadalupe-quiver.md#outfit-4--talica-25-80100-lb-test--guadalupe-island-three-application-outfit)
   and [surgeon's loop (giant yellowtail,
   long-range)](../rigging/surgeons-loop.md#sinker--hook-cdjt_i_pbhq) — a
   further, independent citation of the same figure. "The ridge" and "the
   coast" are named on camera with no further location detail; not resolved
   to a specific place per the name-a-place-only-if-the-source-names-it rule.
3. **Trolling, "the ridge or the rocks" — wahoo:** on a 6-8 day trip, this
   same outfit doubles as a personal [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md)
   rig for wahoo. The boat carries its own trolling outfits for anglers to
   borrow; this application is offered for anglers who prefer their own
   setup. Matches the wahoo-trolling-at-"the ridge" application already
   logged (different outfit, same channel) in [Searcher four-outfit
   Guadalupe quiver — Outfit
   4](searcher-four-outfit-guadalupe-quiver.md#outfit-4--talica-25-80100-lb-test--guadalupe-island-three-application-outfit).
   No lure is named in this video.
4. **Guadalupe Island — kite fishing:** as a personal kite outfit, spooled
   with **100 lb Spectra** and a **short leader** — the boat can rig a kite
   leader for an angler bringing this outfit. The boat also carries its own
   kite outfits for anglers who don't want to rig their own. **100 lb** is
   at the low end of, and roughly consistent with, the "100 lb top shot
   minimum on a big, heavy rail rod" personal-kite-outfit floor already
   logged from a different Searcher session in [Searcher spring
   bluefin/yellowtail quiver — kite
   outfit](searcher-spring-bluefin-yellowtail-quiver.md#kite-outfit-boat-supplied-or-bring-your-own)
   — kept side by side as a further data point rather than merged, since
   that citation specs 130 lb main braid behind the top shot and this one
   does not give a separate braid figure beyond the "100 lb Spectra" stated
   for the whole outfit.
