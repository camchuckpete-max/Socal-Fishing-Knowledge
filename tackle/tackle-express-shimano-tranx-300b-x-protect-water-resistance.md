# Shimano Tranx 300B — X-Protect Water Resistance (Tackle Express)

The Shimano Tranx 300B ships with **X-Protect**, a hydrophobic coating on the
inside of the reel body that repels water and limits how much gets into the
reel — the stated successor to **Core Protect** on the previous Tranx lineup
(`vk3jbsINcPw`). This is a 46-second, straight product-feature clip on the
coating alone. Per the confidence rubric, straight promotional copy is
capped at low, on top of `tackle-express` being an unregistered channel
(`vk3jbsINcPw`).

## X-Protect coating

- **Generational upgrade on the Tranx line: Core Protect → X-Protect.** The
  previous Tranx lineup shipped with Core Protect; the 300B covered here
  ships with X-Protect instead (`vk3jbsINcPw`). (Auto-caption "trength
  lineup" is read as **Tranx**, per the human-typed title, and not carried as
  spoken — an ASR miss, not a second product line.)
- **Mechanism.** A hydrophobic coating on the inside of the reel repels
  water and minimizes water penetration into the reel body (`vk3jbsINcPw`).
- **Stated payoff for saltwater use.** Saltwater is corrosive, so the less
  water that gets inside the reel, the smoother it keeps fishing over time
  and the longer it lasts before feeling rough (`vk3jbsINcPw`).
- **Stated ranking.** Called "the highest level of protection that Shimano
  has to offer" — a superlative product claim, not an independently verified
  spec, carried as spoken at the capped-low confidence above
  (`vk3jbsINcPw`).

## Scope and gaps

No model size beyond 300B, gear ratio, price, or a comparison of X-Protect
against Core Protect's own stated performance is given in this clip
(`vk3jbsINcPw`). Core Protect also appears as a feature on a different
(spinning) Shimano reel, the Nasci FC — this clip's claim that Core Protect
is the Tranx line's previous-generation coating, now superseded by
X-Protect on this model, sits beside that note without altering it: neither
clip states whether Core Protect was removed from Shimano's lineup generally
(`vk3jbsINcPw`).
