## Titan Diver Plus — larger swimbait hooks, fully customizable

- **Center pin locking spring** — seats the bait's nose against the hook (the
  same rigging goal as the springs already covered for weedless swimbait
  rigging in [soft-plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md)).
- **Removable underspin blade** — a spin blade mounted under the hook that can
  be taken off entirely.
- **Rubber grommet** — slides up or down the hook shank to pin the bait body
  in place and keep it from sliding up or down the hook.
- **Removable weight** — a weight built onto the hook itself (distinct from a
  separate jig head) that can be slid up or down the shank, or removed
  altogether.
- Stated positioning: the "Plus" series targets anglers who want to
  customize how the bait rides — blade in or out, weight position, weight in
  or out — rather than fish one fixed setup.

## Rigging a Kicker Pickle Kick on the center-pin spring (Tackle Express, `WPYrq_jdK2o`, 2025-01-13)

"How to Rig the Kicker Pickle Kick on a BKK Titandiver Hook for Maximum
Action" — a 90-second how-to, no presenter named on camera, demonstrating the
**center pin locking spring** feature above in use on a **Kicker Pickle Kick**
swimbait. (This confirms "Pickle Kick" as the full product name behind the
"PK" abbreviation — PK3/PK5/PK7 — used for this bait elsewhere in [soft-plastic
swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md).) Unregistered channel, single
mention, no retail pitch on camera — **medium** confidence. No species,
technique, or location named; hardware/rigging procedure only. See the source
video for the visual steps; parameters and judgment calls:

- **Spring seat and centering:** the spring sits directly against the bait's
  nose and is twisted on until it bites into the plastic; the hook shank is
  then fed down the dead-center of the spring so the bait ends up butted
  against the hook, with the spring's attachment loop sitting flat and level.
- **Marking the exit point:** with the bait laid out flat, the hook is lined
  up against it to find where the point will pop out the top before pushing
  through — a thumb held in place or a small gash cut in the bait are the two
  ways named to mark the spot. Stated preference: flip the bait over, match
  the hook to the belly, and feed the point straight through dead-center so
  it exits the top.
- **Weedless seat:** the exit point lands in a **molded hook groove** built
  into the bait — this is what buries the point and keeps the rig weedless.
- **Bait-keeper step (uncertain):** a "little plastic" piece — almost
  certainly the **rubber grommet** bait-keeper named above — is moved out of
  the way before the point is set, then referenced again at the very end of
  the rig; the clip cuts off mid-sentence at that point, so the exact final
  action (sliding it back to lock the bait) is inferred from the earlier
  grommet description, not stated on-camera in full.
