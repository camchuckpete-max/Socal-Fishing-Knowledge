# BKK Titan Diver and Titan Diver Plus — Swimbait Hooks (Tackle Express)

The BKK Titan Diver and Titan Diver Plus are keel-weighted, weedless swimbait
hooks built around a center-pin locking spring, a removable underspin blade,
and a rubber-grommet bait keeper; the Plus line adds a slidable/removable
weight for full rig customization, the standard line runs lighter with a
fixed weight for finesse work (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`). Both mount on the Kicker
Pickle Kick and booty-spin swimbait bodies (`WPYrq_jdK2o`, `akX66b5WGZA`). No
species, technique, or location is named for either hook line — this is
hardware spec and rigging only.

## Titan Diver Plus — larger, fully customizable

- **Center pin locking spring** seats the bait's nose against the hook — the
  same rigging goal as the springs already covered for weedless swimbait
  rigging in [soft-plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md)
  (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`).
- **Removable underspin blade** — a spin blade mounted under the hook that
  can be taken off entirely (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`).
- **Rubber grommet** slides up or down the hook shank to pin the bait body in
  place and stop it sliding on the hook (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`).
- **Removable weight** is built onto the hook itself (distinct from a
  separate jig head) and can be slid up or down the shank, or removed
  altogether (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`).
- The Plus series targets anglers who want to customize how the bait rides —
  blade in or out, weight position, weight in or out — rather than fish one
  fixed setup (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`).

## Titan Diver (standard) — lighter finesse build

- Lighter weight and finesse-oriented versus the Plus line (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`).
- Sizes run from a **size 1** up to a top end given on camera as "40 or 5" —
  almost certainly **4/0 or 5/0**, exact top end asr-uncertain
  (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`).
- Fits the Keitech 2.8 "beautifully" (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`) — Keitech is one of the
  swimbait model benchmarks named in [soft-plastic
  swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md).
- Has the removable underspin blade, same as the Plus line (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`).
- Weight is fixed, not removable, unlike the Plus line's slidable/removable
  weight (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`).
- Runs the same center-pin spring and rubber grommet bait-keeper hardware as
  the Plus line (`L_YH_xT7Pfs`).

## Rigging a Kicker Pickle Kick on the center-pin spring

Confirms "Pickle Kick" as the full product name behind the "PK" abbreviation
— PK3/PK5/PK7 — used for this bait in [soft-plastic
swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md) (`WPYrq_jdK2o`). See the
source video for the visual steps; parameters and judgment calls:

- The spring seats directly against the bait's nose and is twisted on until
  it bites into the plastic; the hook shank is then fed down the dead-center
  of the spring so the bait ends up butted against the hook, with the
  spring's attachment loop sitting flat and level (`WPYrq_jdK2o`).
- With the bait laid out flat, the hook is lined up against it to find where
  the point will pop out the top before pushing through — a thumb held in
  place or a small gash cut in the bait mark the spot. Stated preference:
  flip the bait over, match the hook to the belly, and feed the point
  straight through dead-center so it exits the top (`WPYrq_jdK2o`).
- The exit point lands in a molded hook groove built into the bait, which
  buries the point and keeps the rig weedless (`WPYrq_jdK2o`).
- A "little plastic" piece — almost certainly the rubber grommet bait-keeper
  above — is moved out of the way before the point is set, then referenced
  again at the very end of the rig; the clip cuts off mid-sentence there, so
  the exact final action (sliding it back to lock the bait) is inferred from
  the earlier grommet description, not stated on camera in full
  (asr-uncertain).

## Weedlessness tradeoff vs. the Neck Breaker, and body compatibility

See [soft-plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md) for the full
three-way jig-head comparison (standard open hook, War Baits Neck Breaker,
BKK Titan Diver); the Titan Diver-specific points:

- Despite being a "weedless weighted hook," the Titan Diver's underspin blade
  riding underneath is more likely to grab vegetation and affect the bait's
  action than the War Baits Neck Breaker, which is "extremely weedless" by
  comparison (`akX66b5WGZA`).
- The underspin fishes on either the Kicker booty-spin body or the standard
  Pickle Kick (PK) body — confirming these as two distinct compatible
  bodies, not the same product under two names (`akX66b5WGZA`). On the
  booty-spin body, running two blades instead of the underspin's one doubles
  the vibration and doubles the flash, meant to draw fish up from depth
  (`akX66b5WGZA`).
