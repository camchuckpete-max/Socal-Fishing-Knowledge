# Bluefin Retail Outfit Ladder — High-End vs Budget (Lori Sack, Turner's Outdoorsman, via SearcherSportfishing)

Lori Sack, a tackle sales associate at **Turner's Outdoorsman**'s San Marcos
store, in a short segment on the SearcherSportfishing channel ("Tackle Tip
Thursday Vol. 47 (Bluefin Set-ups)," `hlmDnAct1cA`, uploaded 2019-05-09, 58
seconds). `searcher-sportfishing` is a registered channel/series, but Sack is
a **retail tackle-shop associate, not one of the channel's own recurring
voices** (Capt. Art, Steve Carson, Wendy, Doug Kearn, Logan Watson) — an
unregistered guest presenting her own shop's product picks, explicitly framed
around price for each build. Per the registry's sponsored-claim caveat, the
model picks below stay **low confidence**; this 58-second clip gives no
separable mechanism/parameter content beyond the product picks and their
price tier (contrast with the Accurate/Searcher entries elsewhere in this KB,
which pair sponsored gear with independent line-class or technique doctrine).

Framed as an answer to a recurring shop question: bluefin fishing "can be
kind of expensive," often for a low bite rate ("I call it bluefin watching —
about half the time you see them, half the time they bite"), so customers
want to know what setup to buy at different budget levels. Split into its own
note (not added to [rod & reel selection](rod-and-reel-selection.md), which
the "Two-speed reels for multi-day bluefin trips" entry there already flags
as "already-oversized," or to [Searcher lever-drag reel
sizing](searcher-lever-drag-reel-sizing.md), a different topic — Searcher's
own boat loaner gear rather than a customer's retail purchase).

## High-end build — about $1,200 for the pair

- **Reel:** captioned "Shimano Teleca 20" — read as **Shimano Talica 20**,
  the brand's two-speed lever-drag lineup (asr-uncertain on the exact
  spelling, but corroborated: this exact pairing — **Talica 20 with a
  Phoenix rod** — is already logged in this KB, from a different channel and
  presenter, in [rod & reel selection — a second data point, 1.5–2 day trip
  bag](rod-and-reel-selection.md#a-second-data-point--a-15-2-day-trip-bag-gevnj2y1ep8)
  (Ray Sharifi, Dirty Hookers, `ray-sharifi` registered but Baja-scoped so
  this SoCal claim caps at medium): "40 lb fly line... 8 ft, 20–50 lb
  Phoenix Black Diamond rod + Talica 20." That independent citation resolves
  this note's caption ambiguity rather than leaving it a guess, though it's a
  different line class (his build runs 40 lb, this one 100 lb braid) — a
  corroborating data point on the reel/rod pairing, not a confirmation of the
  line class. Holds **100 lb braid**. Sack's framing: "nice, high-end
  quality, last you forever."
- **Rod:** a **Phoenix rod**, rated **40–100 lb**. "Phoenix" is an
  already-established rod brand in this KB's tackle material (see the
  Phoenix Black Diamond / Phoenix Axis builds in [rod & reel
  selection](rod-and-reel-selection.md#a-second-data-point--a-15-2-day-trip-bag-gevnj2y1ep8)),
  not treated as an ASR corruption here.
- **Total cost:** about **$1,200** for the reel + rod, per Sack.

## Budget build — under $500 for the pair

- **Reel:** **Penn Fathom two-speed 40**, also holding **100 lb braid** —
  called a "workhorse reel." A further data point alongside the other
  Penn Fathom two-speed sizes already logged in this KB (Fathom 25 Narrow,
  30, 40 Narrow, 60 — see [Searcher lever-drag reel
  sizing](searcher-lever-drag-reel-sizing.md) and [Searcher big/giant tuna
  rig ladder](searcher-big-tuna-rig-ladder.md)); this "40" isn't specified
  as "Narrow" on camera, so kept as its own, less-precise data point rather
  than merged into either.
- **Rod:** captioned "a graphtec rod... heavy 80 to 100... graphtec rod,"
  the phrase repeated twice in the same breath — read as **"graphite
  rod"** (a material description, not a brand name) rather than a product
  called "Graphtec," which does not otherwise appear in this corpus;
  flagged `asr-uncertain`. Rated **heavy, 80–100 lb**.
- **Total cost:** under **$500** for the reel + rod, per Sack.

## Her close

Framed as scalable — "whatever your budget is, we can fit it in" — so these
two builds are presented as the ends of the shop's bluefin ladder, not the
only two options available.
