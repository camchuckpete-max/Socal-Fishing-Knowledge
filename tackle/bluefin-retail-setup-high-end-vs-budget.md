# Bluefin Retail Outfit Ladder — High-End vs. Budget

A tackle shop's two-tier bluefin outfit recommendation, bracketing price
rather than line class, fielding a recurring shop question about what to buy
given that bluefin fishing "can be kind of expensive" (`hlmDnAct1cA`) — both
builds hold 100 lb braid, running from about $1,200 at the top to under $500
at the bottom. The framing is the fishery's low hookup rate — "about half
the time you see them, half the time they bite" (`hlmDnAct1cA`) — and
durability: the high-end pair is pitched as one that will "last you forever"
(`hlmDnAct1cA`).

## The two builds

| Tier | Reel | Rod | Line | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High-end | Shimano Talica 20, two-speed lever-drag (captioned "Teleca 20"; asr-uncertain spelling) | Phoenix, rated 40–100 lb | 100 lb braid | about $1,200 for the pair |
| Budget | Penn Fathom two-speed 40 | rated heavy, 80–100 lb (captioned "graphtec rod"; read as a graphite blank, not a brand name — asr-uncertain) | 100 lb braid | under $500 for the pair |

(`hlmDnAct1cA`) for the full row set.

The high-end reel is pitched as "nice, high-end quality, last you forever"
(`hlmDnAct1cA`); the budget reel as a "workhorse reel" (`hlmDnAct1cA`).

The high-end reel/rod pairing — Talica 20 with a Phoenix rod — is
corroborated at a lighter line class (40 lb flyline vs. 100 lb braid here) in
[rod & reel
selection](rod-and-reel-selection.md#a-second-data-point--a-15-2-day-trip-bag-gevnj2y1ep8)
(`gevNj2Y1Ep8`).

The Penn Fathom two-speed 40 here isn't specified on camera as "Narrow," so
it stays a separate data point from the Fathom 25 Narrow / 30 / 40 Narrow /
60 sizes logged in [Searcher lever-drag reel
sizing](searcher-lever-drag-reel-sizing.md) and [Searcher big/giant tuna rig
ladder](searcher-big-tuna-rig-ladder.md) rather than merging into either.

## Scaling to budget

The two builds mark the ends of the shop's bluefin ladder, not the only two
options: "whatever your budget is, we can fit it in" (`hlmDnAct1cA`).
