## What's claimed

- **CI4+ body ("new gen").** Claimed **2.5× more rigid** than "the first
  generation" (no baseline model or year given for that comparison). Pitched
  as robust/sturdy/durable enough to "beat this guy up" and keep producing.
- **Infinity Drive.** Described as reduced friction turning the handle
  overall — said to let the handle spin freely, lay line evenly, and run with
  minimal issues.
- **Infinity Cross.** Described as "advanced gear durability," stated to be
  **dropped down from the previous Stella generation**. Its stated effect —
  less friction, more consistent handle turning — reads as the same claim
  already made for Infinity Drive above; the clip never distinguishes what
  each feature does differently (Infinity Drive vs. Infinity Cross), so that
  overlap is recorded as-is rather than invented apart.
- **Anti-Twist Fin.** Called one of the presenter's bigger selling points: an
  "improved line management helper" that maintains line tension and prevents
  uneven line wrap. A third stated benefit — reduces line **[twist]** — is cut
  off mid-word by the caption track at the clip's end (`asr-uncertain`; "twist"
  is the plain reading given the feature's own name, but the source does not
  finish the sentence, so it is bracketed rather than asserted flatly).

No model name, size, gear ratio, drag figure, line capacity, price, or
