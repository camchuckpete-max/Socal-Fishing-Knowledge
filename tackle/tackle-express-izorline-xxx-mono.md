# Izorline XXX — Spool Sizes, Colors, and a Retailer's Case for Smoke (Tackle Express)

Izorline XXX is a monofilament line one tackle shop calls its best-selling
mono, online and in store (`Ecqt-ZLnvaU`). Two short Tackle Express clips
cover it: one on bulk spool sizes and color, the other on weight-by-application
picks. Both are shop/product promotion on an unregistered channel, so every
claim below is capped at **low** confidence per the sponsored-claim rule.

## Why the shop stocks it

Pitched as extremely strong, low-memory, and inexpensive relative to
fluorocarbon lines — a cost/behavior sales pitch, not an independent test
(`Ecqt-ZLnvaU`).

## Spool sizes and colors

- Sold in three bulk spool weights — **1 lb, 1/2 lb, and 1/4 lb** — sized to
  how much line an angler needs. Yardage varies by spool weight rather than a
  fixed per-spool length, and per-dollar cost is described as running lower
  than "a lot of other comparable options" (`Ecqt-ZLnvaU`).
- Offered in **smoke or clear**. Per a conversation with Izorline relayed
  secondhand on camera — not independently verified — the smoke tint "blends
  in with the clarity of the water" and disappears better underwater than
  clear; the shop's own stated preference is smoke, for that reason
  (`Ecqt-ZLnvaU`).
- This diverges from the general material framing in
  [line & leader](line-and-leader.md), which states braid and mono are both
  fairly visible, with fluoro as the invisible material: the smoke-mono claim
  here is one retailer relaying one manufacturer's pitch for one branded
  line, not independent testing.

## Line weight by application

A second, separate clip gives weight-by-application picks rather than spool
sizes/color (`zWpYFTIZP7w`):

- **25 lb** is described as the most popular weight overall among swimbait
  anglers throwing big swimbait-style lures (`zWpYFTIZP7w`).
- **20 lb** is the presenter's own stated personal pick for fishing "rats and
  crawlers" specifically — a personal-preference data point, not a
  correction of the 25 lb popularity claim (`zWpYFTIZP7w`).
- **2 lb** is called out separately for trout anglers on ultralight rods, for
  the same low-stretch/low-memory reasons given across weights, not a
  distinct trout-specific case (`zWpYFTIZP7w`). Worked example on camera: a
  500-size reel spooled with 2 lb XXX, no leader or splice tied on
  (`zWpYFTIZP7w`).
- Stated line characteristics carry across both weight contexts: strong, low
  stretch, low memory (`zWpYFTIZP7w`). Casting well is called out
  specifically for the 2 lb trout/ultralight setup, not stated as a general
  claim across all weights (`zWpYFTIZP7w`).
