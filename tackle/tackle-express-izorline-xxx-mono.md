# Izorline XXX — Spool Sizes, Colors, and a Retailer's Case for Smoke (Tackle Express)

Tackle Express (Harry, `Ecqt-ZLnvaU`, "Why Izorline XXX Is THE Go-To Mono for
Anglers," uploaded 2025-06-17; unregistered channel, explicit shop/product
promotion — **low confidence**, all claims below per the sponsored-claim rule).
A short retail-counter pitch for Izorline XXX monofilament — the shop's
stated best-selling mono line, online and in store.

- **Why the shop stocks it:** pitched as extremely strong, low-memory, and
  inexpensive relative to fluorocarbon lines — a cost/behavior sales pitch,
  not an independent test.
- **Bulk spool sizes:** sold in three weights — **1 lb, 1/2 lb, and 1/4 lb** —
  sized to how much line an angler needs; yardage varies by spool weight
  (not a fixed per-spool length), and per Harry, per-dollar cost runs lower
  than "a lot of other comparable options."
- **Colors: smoke or clear.** Per a conversation with Izorline relayed
  secondhand on camera (not independently verified), the smoke tint "blends
  in with the clarity of the water" and disappears better underwater than
  clear. Harry's own stated preference is smoke, for that reason.

**Kept side by side with, not merged into, the general material framing in**
[line & leader](line-and-leader.md), which states braid and mono are both
"fairly visible," with fluoro as the invisible material. This clip's
smoke-mono-disappears claim is a single retailer relaying a single
manufacturer's pitch for one branded line — not independent testing — so it
sits beside that general framing as a sponsored data point, not a correction
of it.
