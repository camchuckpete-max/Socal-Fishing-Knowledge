## The glassing protocol

- `cameron` (memory export §2–§3): terns are Cameron's most productive bird to
  follow offshore, worked even with no other birds active; his foamer search
  runs the same run-stop-cast loop at speed.

## Anchoring geometry

- `xI9tPJFXbUM` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2019-08-30, solo presenter, voice
  inferred as Dave Hansen — no diarization on this clip): repeats the
  sand-patch-not-on-the-rock anchoring rule almost three years before
  `6DzbsElGE7E`, in the presenter's own words — framed as anchoring so the
  presented lines drift back onto the structure rather than sitting directly
  over it.
- `BskCsKaTiZw` / `JK2-cpaXkI8` (Your Saltwater Guide, PCS Show seminar,
  2025-03-08 and 2025-03-06): attribution note, not a fishing claim — the
  `BskCsKaTiZw` host was first treated as an unregistered voice because the
  stated biography ("I ran sport boats") didn't visibly match the registered
  `dave-hansen` voice. The companion clip `JK2-cpaXkI8` (2025-03-06, the same
  PCS Show's first seminar, podcast episode #672 to `BskCsKaTiZw`'s #673 two
  days later) has the host self-identify by name as Dave, with wife Kelly,
  sister Donna, a home in Cabo/Mexico, and a family sportfishing business his
  father started in 1947 — all matching the registered `dave-hansen`
  biography. `BskCsKaTiZw` is now treated as `dave-hansen`, registered voice;
  single mention on this specific claim, so confidence stays medium rather
  than promoting to high on attribution alone.

## Confirming the lay reaches the spot

- `JK2-cpaXkI8` (Dave Hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, PCS Show seminar,
  2025-03-06, White Rock, San Clemente Island): worked example — anchored in
  90 ft of water over a hard, cobblestone bottom ("a little bit bigger" than
  loose gravel, not sand, not reef rock), fishing a downhill (LA-to-San Diego,
  north-to-south) current, matching the "coastal deep spots default-prefer
  downhill (N→S)" doctrine in
  [current structure](../../conditions/current-structure.md#the-parallel-current-rule-coastal--island-structure).
  Fish sat at the very front edge of the hard bottom — given the anchoring
  geometry, that edge nearest the boat reads as the up-current face of the
  structure (an inference from this example, not stated in those words on
  camera). A boat fishing White Rock on the downhill current caught "a bunch
  of" yellowtail; a different boat at the identical GPS numbers the very next
  day, with no current running, caught nothing.

## Reading bait behavior

- `XyKF34C5iB4` (Time On The Water seminar panel, 2025-04-19, coastal bass
  water): roughly two weeks before the talk, bass were boiling and visibly
  active on the surface but refused every lure offered; the panel keyed the
  refusal to "sailor jellies" — small floating triangular jelly-like things,
  species not identified on camera — that their baits couldn't imitate. The
  crew left rather than keep working refusing fish.
