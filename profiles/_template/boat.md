# <Your Boat>

Operating envelope + trolling platform. The [day-plan protocol](../../planning/day-plan-protocol.md)
reads this to keep recommendations inside your boat's real limits. Fill in what
you know; leave the rest blank.

## Hull & power
- Make/model, length, engine(s), top speed, cruise, idle speed.
- Fuel range (nm). Sea-state comfort ceiling (the wind/swell you'll turn around at).

## Bait & ice
- Bait tank size + pump (GPH) — see [bait tanks](../../bait/bait-tanks.md).
- Ice capacity per trip — see [fish care](../../fish-care/tuna-care.md).

## Rod holders & trolling geometry
- Total holders / how many usable for trolling; **angles** and **spacing**;
  which is the elevated shotgun (furthest-back lure). Outriggers? Extensions?
- Rod-tip elevation trades running depth — see [trolling](../../techniques/trolling.md).

## Trim / ballast, air draft, ops norms
- Weight distribution running out vs coming in; typical departure; launch ramp(s).

## Home waters
- Where you fish most; how far you'll run.
