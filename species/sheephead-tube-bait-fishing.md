# California Sheephead — tube-bait fishing

**One species, one technique.** The red-crab
[tube bait](../lures/tube-baits.md) is the corpus's big-sheephead program, and
it is a **by-catch** program: the fish comes while island rock is being fished
for calico bass or rockfish, not by being targeted (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`). The size
call is a **1 oz "bullet"** — a slimmer profile than the "Big Game" body of the
same weight, because the fish has a small mouth for its body — thrown at
**5/8–1 oz** right up into the island and worked right above the rocks
(`5Oh3IPIC5g4`). Reach for it wherever the structure carries live red crab, and
reverse to sardine or mint where the spot is saturated with it. How the bait
class is fished in general is [tube-bait fishing](../techniques/tube-bait-fishing.md);
this page is what changes because the fish is a sheephead.

## When this is the call

**Island or reef rock that carries red crab.** The named structure is Catalina,
San Clemente, fish sitting tight to the Coronado Islands, Cedros and
Ensenada/Baja (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`) — see
[California sheephead — situations → techniques](sheephead.md#situations--techniques)
for the router row this page fills in, and
[tube baits](../lures/tube-baits.md#color-selection) for the colour list.

**You are not targeting them.** "We catch the big sheephead while we're bass
fishing for calicos … it's almost a by-catch" — go rockfishing on a red-crab
bait and sheephead come over the rail, go calico fishing on it and the same
thing happens (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`). Nothing in the corpus builds a dedicated
sheephead program around this bait; the dedicated program is bait, chum and a
slider — [sheephead — sliding sinker](sheephead-sliding-sinker.md).

**Reverse the bait where live red crab saturates the spot.** On structure packed
with live red crab, the red-crab bait becomes the hardest one to get bit, and a
sardine or mint bait outfishes it there — the presenter's "Oreo cookie effect,"
stated as satiation (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`). Where the spot holds red crab **without**
being packed with it, the red-crab bait is what fires the fish. The decision
turns on **how much** live crab is on the spot, not on whether any is there.
This reversal is not a sheephead rule — it is stated for the bass on the same
structure too, and it lives in full on
[tube baits](../lures/tube-baits.md#color-selection).

## How California sheephead changes the execution

**The profile is sized to the mouth, not to the fish.** A sheephead's mouth is
small for its body, so "the one ounce bullet is a really good one … a little
smaller profile bait is easier for them to eat" — the bullet out-produces the
larger "Big Game" body of the same 1 oz weight (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`). This is the
opposite of the size logic the general note runs for bass, where a big bait is
thrown deliberately as a size filter.

**The cast goes into the rock, not past it.** Fish **5/8 oz or 1 oz** and "throw
it right up into the island … work it right above the rocks," the same
presentation called "absolutely deadly on calico bass" on that structure — the
sheephead eats off the same retrieve (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`).

**Red crab is the one scent that changes anything for this species.** Across
bass, rockfish "and every other kind of fish" the presenter reports no scent
out-fishing another — "except for sheephead," which he says take the red-crab
scent above the rest (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`). ⚠ Fact-check (single-source): the claim
comes from the co-owner of the bait company presenting his own product.

**Grade: this is what produces the big ones.** A **24 lb** sheephead in 2019
came on a red crab thrown up into the island (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`), and two anglers
took about **five fish of 15–20 lb** on one 2018 trip, "every single one of
them" on the red-crab scent (`0HwYl0HO2tc`). ⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-by-source):
the cited transcript never states a year for this trip — "2018" is not in the
source and appears inferred from the seminar's own year, which is not necessarily
the trip's year (contrast the 24 lb fish above, where the source explicitly says
"last year" against a dated 2020 seminar). ⚠ Fact-check (single-source): both
grade records come from the same HookUp Baits presenter at two Fred Hall
seminars promoting his own bait, so the second corroborates the presenter rather
than the bait; queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any sheephead-specific retrieve cadence,
working depth, bite read or hook-set on this bait. The cadences on
[tube-bait fishing](../techniques/tube-bait-fishing.md) are stated for
yellowtail, bass, drifting rockfish and tuna; no source says which of them the
sheephead by-catch comes on, or how the bite feels different.

## Rigs & gear

- **Bass casting / swimbait class** — the same outfit the island bass program is
  already in hand, since this is a by-catch of that program. Class terms in
  [gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md#lure-classes-cross-reference).
- **Bait weight: 5/8 oz or 1 oz, in the bullet profile** (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`). The
  bait's internal lead head is the whole terminal end — construction, colours
  and scents are in [tube baits](../lures/tube-baits.md).
- **At Cedros the kit named is 1 oz "Big Game" and 1 oz bullet red crabs**, the
  pair the presenter says covers everything on that island (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`) — the
  bullet is the one carrying the sheephead call above.
- ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: rod length or rating, line class, leader
  class or hook change specific to sheephead on this bait. The parabolic-rod and
  line-class ladder on
  [tube-bait fishing](../techniques/tube-bait-fishing.md#rod-parabolic-bend-for-big-baits)
  is keyed to bait weight, not to the species.

## Differs from the general method

Against [tube-bait fishing](../techniques/tube-bait-fishing.md), which owns the
rod and line ladder, the slack-line sink read, the double-bait dropper rig and
the per-species cadences:

- **Profile is picked for the fish's mouth, not for casting distance.** The
  general note's size call trades profile against distance — 1–1.5 oz to reach
  boat-shy fish, dropping to 5/8 oz when fish follow without committing — and
  runs 3–4 oz as a deliberate big-bait size filter. Here the 1 oz bullet is
  chosen because a small-mouthed fish can take it in, and the big-bait filter
  does not apply (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`).
- **Scent is a species variable here and nowhere else in the class.** No scent
  in the [tube-bait line-up](../lures/tube-baits.md#scent) out-fishes another on
  bass, rockfish or anything else; sheephead are the one stated exception, and
  the exception is red crab (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`).
- **The target is somebody else's.** Every other application on the general note
  — the yellowtail pop-retrieve, the party-boat drift bounce, the paddy and
  skipjack-layer casts — is fished at the species named. This one is fished at
  calicos or rockfish, and the sheephead is what the bait brings with it
  (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`).
- **NOT a delta: the red-crab saturation reversal.** The switch to sardine or
  mint on a crab-packed spot is stated for the bass on that structure as much as
  for sheephead, and belongs to the bait class — it is repeated here because it
  changes which bait you tie on for this fish, not because it is a sheephead
  rule.
