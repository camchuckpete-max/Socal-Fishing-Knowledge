# Pacific Crevalle Jack (Toro)

The Pacific crevalle jack (*Caranx caninus*, "toro") is an inshore jack of the
warm Baja coast that turns up inside somebody else's mixed-bag day rather than
as a species you run to: the one corpus record of a landed fish came in a
morning that also produced four tuna (`84XPJAeH0Rw`). It reaches Southern
California only as a warm-water stray. No source gives a crevalle-specific
season, water temperature, sonar read, retrieve, size or handling rule, so
every technique and sign statement below is transferred from the
yellowtail-class structure-jack program and carries a flag; the only sourced
program is a live sardine on a beach drop at East Cape (`APsnsunT4gM`).

## Where & when

- **East Cape, Baja California Sur — the only sourced location.** Crevalle are
  named at **Lighthouse Point**, a beach point whose drop sits close enough to
  the sand that sharks, roosterfish, jacks, dorado and yellowfin tuna are all
  catchable by casting from the beach (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`), and one was landed out
  of the same resort fishery in a mixed morning catch with four tuna
  (`84XPJAeH0Rw`). See [East Cape](../locations/east-cape.md).
- **Presence is not a season here.** No month, water temperature or run timing
  is stated for crevalle at East Cape, and the three recordings are upload
  dates, not catch dates. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: when crevalle are
  catchable at East Cape rather than merely present.
- **Rest of the Sea of Cortez, north to
  [Bahía de los Ángeles](../locations/bahia-de-los-angeles.md):** warm months,
  inshore, around rocky points, structure, boils and river/estuary mouths, most
  reliable when the water is warm — see
  [water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md).
  ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): no corpus source places a crevalle anywhere in
  the Cortez outside East Cape, or ties them to a temperature.
- **Southern California — stray only.** Expect them only when a strong
  warm-water push (warm-year / El Niño-type conditions) carries tropical fish
  north into range: the peak-to-late
  [warm-stable regime into fall fragmentation](../conditions/water-regimes.md),
  [August](../seasonal/august.md) into
  [September](../seasonal/september.md).
  ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): no corpus source records a Pacific crevalle jack
  in the Southern California Bight.

## Presence & forage

- **They come with the inshore assemblage on a close-to-shore drop, not
  alone.** The Lighthouse Point drop holds sharks, roosterfish, jacks, dorado
  and yellowfin tuna on the same edge (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`), and the one landed
  crevalle arrived in a morning that also boxed four tuna (`84XPJAeH0Rw`). The
  planning consequence: a crevalle is by-catch of an inshore mixed-bag program,
  not a fish you build a run around.
- **Live sardines are the bait the sourced inshore program is built on** — the
  East Cape crew makes sardines first, then fishes "roosters, some jacks, some
  snappers" (`APsnsunT4gM`). That is hook bait, not a demonstrated natural
  forage.
- ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what Pacific crevalle jack eat naturally,
  and what bait, structure, current or temperature puts them on a given point.

## Feeding triggers

- **The Lighthouse Point beach fishery is worked by sight, and also fished in
  the afternoon and at night** (`mL4Ph7t0WcQ`). Scope: that is stated for the
  point's beach program as a whole, so it says when the spot is fished, not
  when crevalle eat.
- ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any light window, tide or slack gate,

## Finding them (sign & sonar)

- **Visual sign:** surface boils and bait busting near inshore structure —
  points and rock piles. Birds working the boil help locate them; see
  [bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md),
  [search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md) and
  [foamer casting](../techniques/foamer-casting.md).
  ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): transferred from the general structure-jack
  pattern; no corpus source describes crevalle sign.
- **Sonar:** treat as a yellowtail-class structure fish — marks near
  points and structure in the mid-to-upper column over hard bottom. General
  method: [electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).
  ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a crevalle-specific sonar signature or
  depth; the depth read above is inference from the structure-jack pattern, not
  a source read.

## Gear summary (class terms)

- **Rod/reel:** [surface-iron / jig-stick class](../tackle/gear-classes.md) for
  casting and **40–60 lb class** for yo-yo and bait — a hard-fighting jack that
  rewards backbone; the flyline / soft-rod class covers the sourced live-sardine
  beach-drop program (`APsnsunT4gM`).
- **Lures:** [iron jigs](../lures/iron-jigs.md) and
  [poppers and stickbaits](../lures/tuna-poppers-and-stickbaits.md).
- **Terminal:** strong, abrasion-tolerant leader — they fight brutally around
  structure and are not leader-shy, so heavier terminal costs few bites. In
  Baja California Sur a [circle hook](../tackle/hooks.md) is mandatory — see
  [Regulations](#regulations). The one sourced program's terminal is **50 lb
  spectra to a 30 lb fluorocarbon top shot and a #2 circle hook** sized for the
  sardine and chosen "in case we get any roosters" (`APsnsunT4gM`) — see
  [line and leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md).
  ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): those figures come from a session that landed a
  dorado, not a crevalle; no corpus source gives a crevalle-specific leader
  class, hook size, jig weight or line class, and none exists at all for the
  iron rows.

## Doctrine & conflicts

Section order below is not a ranking.

- **Route this page as a yellowtail-class structure jack, and treat that as a
  working placeholder rather than doctrine.** What the choice turns on: the
  only sourced crevalle program is a **bait** program — a live sardine on a
  beach drop (`APsnsunT4gM`) — while every iron, popper and sonar statement on
  the page is transferred from a different species. So on an East Cape inshore
  day, fish the sourced bait program; anywhere else, the iron approach is an
  untested transfer, and a crevalle-specific source should replace it rather
  than be reconciled with it.
- **Open item, not doctrine:** target them opportunistically if a warm-water
  push brings them into range (cameron). Revisit and upgrade this note if a
  credible SoCal/Baja toro source enters the corpus.
