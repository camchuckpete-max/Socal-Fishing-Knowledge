# Evidence — Spotted Bay Bass

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[spotted bay bass](../spotted-bay-bass.md). One line per observation, grouped
under the parent note's section headings, in its order. Observations never
change the parent's stated doctrine; one that materially supports or
contradicts it also leaves a one-line cited trace beside that doctrine in the
parent.

## Where & when

- `YgqXf9iICyg` (BDOutdoors, SoCal Bight Fishing Academy Ep. 2, 2023-01-06,
  Newport Bay / Mission Bay / San Diego Bay): the parent's backbone for bay
  hydraulics, seasonal movement and spawning — bay shape and pinch points
  setting current, the turbulence → zooplankton → bait fish → spotty chain, the
  summer move to the harbor mouth, the winter retreat to stable-temperature
  zones, and the slack-tide crankbait window. Registered voice; a Google-Earth
  and whiteboard breakdown with a three-minute Newport clip attached.
- `GVP3IChsmRQ` (BDOutdoors, SoCal Bight Fishing Academy Ep. 3, 2023-01-13,
  Newport Harbor, filmed 2022-12-30): Jimmy Decker's crankbait, Zoom-fluke and
  Ned-rig walk-through after overnight rain on a very low tide, with the harbor
  water still about 58 °F and the fish still in summer positions — 15–18 fish in
  roughly three hours, including "a bunch of dinks." Registered voice.
- `lP6cg4eEU6s` (Fisher Newb, 2020-03-06, Sav-On Tackle / IROD seminar): the
  speaker's personal-best bay bass have come at **slack low tide, around noon
  with the sun high**, running a crankbait shallow through water most anglers
  would read as inactive; he frames it explicitly as a **reaction bite**, not a
  feeding-window bite — a contrasting personal pattern beside the parent's
  moving-water doctrine, not a competing rule. Unregistered channel;
  single-camera seminar with speakers not diarized, the speaker addressed only
  as "Jeremy" in the Q&A.
- `6-mi3Qxn37c` (StokedOnFishing, 2019-05-04, Mission Bay): a **4–5 ft** tidal
  swing called the target range — a **0.21 ft low at 06:41** building to a
  **3.7 ft high at noon** was called "an excellent tide" — with a much bigger
  swing (his example **~7 ft**) described as pushing the fish to hunker down and
  forcing a move to the back bay to keep catching; air 80 °F over 64 °F water.
  Supports the parent's tide-driven doctrine. Unregistered channel,
  presenter-inferred (multi-speaker, no diarization).
- `SdwwpQMJEOI` (StokedOnFishing, 2025-05-16, Long Beach / Alamitos Bay — Olive
  Crest ProAm charity bass tournament): a team fishing spotted bay bass for the
  first time reported no bites through a slack/no-current stretch, then
  connected once current came back on — "current... no current... finally got
  current, we got bites." Supports the parent's tide-driven doctrine.
  Multi-speaker, unregistered channel, presenter not identified.
- `Cobp85UvHmM` (StokedOnFishing, 2018-02-07 upload, La Bocana estuary
  (Estero), Baja California Sur, Pacific side — "Stoked On La Bocana," part 2):
  inshore estuary fishing produced grouper as the clearly-shown landed catch;
  the closing narration also names "radical spotties and little groupers in the
  Estero" and says they "get a lot bigger than this," with no spotted bay bass
  distinctly shown being landed and no tide, structure or technique detail
  given. Region label: Baja Pacific south. Single-source range claim on an
  unregistered channel, held here rather than asserted as range doctrine in the
  parent (the parent's region gate is `socal-bight`).

## Feeding triggers

- `AH4Eiya1Hn0` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2024 PCS Show live seminar, 2024-03-09):
  Alan Clowers (Captain Clowers Charter & Guide Service) on San Diego Bay —
  check the tide swing before you go, "find something else to do" on a 1–2 ft
  swing, ~3.5–6.5 ft is the area's best; wind builds around 11:00 "like
  clockwork," and an outgoing tide against a westerly 10–12 kt afternoon wind
  cancels the drift. Unregistered speaker, single mention; his figures are a
  different bay from, and the same order of magnitude as, the Mission Bay
  numbers in `6-mi3Qxn37c`, and the parent states both rather than reconciling
  them.
- `46ha7J1Fc6A` (Roman Castro, 2020 Fred Hall Show, Long Beach, 2020-03-12):
  Garrett of Owner Hooks on the seasonal speed switch for San Diego Bay /
  Mission Bay shore and kayak fishing — a Megabass Dark Sleeper worked slow and
  deep with a read-the-fish pause in winter, a quick-swept crankbait as a
  reaction bite in summer. Unregistered speaker, single mention. The same
  speaker's treble-to-single-hook swap for kelp fishing is a
  [calico bass](../calico-bass.md) point, logged in
  [inshore crankbaits](../../techniques/inshore-crankbaits.md#rigging).

## Finding them (sign & sonar)

- `k4LCL9ALryA` (StokedOnFishing, 2012-04-17, San Diego Bay, SWBA tournament
  pre-fishing): at an early stop, bites came in water the crew called "pretty
  deep here" — roughly **18–20 ft**, deeper than the 12–14 ft Newport band —
  taken bottom-bouncing right as the bait dropped near bottom; the structure is
  not specified in that segment, and the crew only started dock and piling
  flipping later in the same session. Multi-speaker, unregistered channel.
- `SImABCBBxAo` (StokedOnFishing, 2020-05-13, location not stated): the
  presenter called fish suspended around **18–20 ft** in **30 ft** of water —
  another data point deeper than the 12–14 ft band. Multi-speaker, unregistered
  channel; asr-uncertain(the exact split between the suspension depth and the
  bottom depth — audio partly garbled, figures as best transcribed).
- `EGKesj7V64M` (Roman Castro, 2019-06-18): the spearfishing/diver-informed
  reads behind the parent's grass section — blades leaning with the flow, brown
  versus green grass as an oxygen proxy, loose clustering on a favoured patch,
  the half hour either side of the turn as the slow window, and the unload-the-
  rod landing sequence. Registered voice.
- `6L8nIFeqvkw` (Roman Castro, 2019-04-24): the kayak zone → spot search
  pattern — cast counts, overlap and when to restart the pattern — logged in
  full in [search & glassing](../../planning/search-and-glassing.md).
  Registered voice.

## Situations → techniques

- `46ha7J1Fc6A` (Roman Castro, 2020 Fred Hall Show, Long Beach, 2020-03-12):
  duplicate-of the Feeding triggers entry above, kept here for the second
  interview — Rick Fuentes (Fishing Syndicate) on the San Diego Bay / Mission
  Bay structure program, with a Newport Harbor extension (10 ft- and
  16 ft-rated crankbaits in the deeper eelgrass, called "dynamite on the really
  really big spotted bay bass"). The structure-program parameters from this
  interview — gear ratio, line and leader classes, bait and jighead sizing and
  the fight rule — are logged in
  [evidence — spotted bay bass — structure pitching](spotted-bay-bass-structure-pitching.md).
  "Mouths are funny critters" — a spotty will hide behind a single blade of
  eelgrass and consider itself hidden. Unregistered speaker, single mention.
- `lP6cg4eEU6s` (Fisher Newb, 2020-03-06): duplicate-of the Where & when entry
  above, kept here for the hook-them-outside-the-cover reasoning and the
  tide-height and shade reads on the router. The bottom-grind craw/creature
  program from this seminar — the bottom-composition read, the
  re-establish-contact pause, the fall-rate variation and the `asr-uncertain`
  "war bait" brand rendering — is logged in
  [evidence — spotted bay bass — bottom jig crawl](spotted-bay-bass-bottom-jig-crawl.md).
  Unregistered channel.
- `XyKF34C5iB4` (Time on the Water, saltwater bass seminar, Pacific Coast
  Sportfishing show, 2025-04-19): panelist "Lane" gave the Newport Harbor
  three-bait plan (downsized PK3 on a neck-breaker head in "spotty" colour, 1 oz
  War Baits HD spinnerbait) and the clear-water switch to docks; panelist
  personal-best spotties were given as **2.8 lb** and **3.5 lb** ("on the sunset
  wall"), against a stated search for a 3 lb-plus fish; the panel also described
  the not-biting decision cycle — grass to docks to rock, fast bait then slow at
  each, and slow-crawl a jig instead of relocating when a long drive is already
  sunk into the spot. Unregistered channel, multi-speaker.
- `AH4Eiya1Hn0` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2024-03-09): duplicate-of the Feeding
  triggers entry above, kept here for the two other speakers — Pablo Fernandez
  (Fernandez Charters, Mission Bay / La Jolla) named the Ingram/Ingraham Street
  Bridge as a standout Mission Bay spotty spot alongside jetties and docks
  generally ("they're actually everywhere... it's hard not to catch them"),
  called activity year-round but "from now [March] till fall they really start
  pumping," described the tide window loosely as "slack tide to either side of
  slack," and gave the sabiki-the-local-bait-then-fish-it-live method — "every
  single one gets eaten." Alan Clowers runs a Berkeley Gulp 3 in shrimp
  (natural and "New Penny") almost exclusively on his San Diego Bay charters.
  Unregistered speakers, single mentions.
- `G6YRT4HNxr8` (Roman Castro, 2018-04-17, Mission Bay): the saltwater umbrella
  rig build and how it is fished for bay bass. Registered voice.
- `um5MAeCjNDg` (BDOutdoors w/ Nate Winnicki, 2023-12-26, San Diego Bay): the
  bay-bassin' Ned rig walk-through behind the parent's Ned row — mushroom head
  plus a stretchy ZMan finesse TRD, one bait catching 50 fish, fished for
  spotties and halibut on the same rig; detail lives in
  [ned rig](../../techniques/ned-rig.md). Registered voice.

## Landing & handling

- `usHl-4SfqDA` (StokedOnFishing, 2024-11-13, Long Beach Harbor): on a mixed
  calico / sand bass / spotted bay bass harbor session, spotted bay bass were
  called out as carrying no minimum size limit against the 14 in for calico and
  sand bass, and described as typically released rather than kept — a fighting
  fish, not a table fish, in this crew's practice. Unregistered channel.

## Zone material

Corpus material by bay, parked here until species×zone guides exist for spotted
bay bass. Each bay below is a flagged stub in the parent's Zone guides section.

- **San Diego Bay** — `YgqXf9iICyg` (big mouth so slower main-channel current,
  a ripping pinch point at the Coronado Bridge that is a popular tournament
  stretch, a very shallow back bay, and the isolated launch ramps by the Coast
  Guard helicopter port as the type example of isolated structure — three or
  four casts and move), `AH4Eiya1Hn0` (the 1–2 ft / 3.5–6.5 ft tide-swing
  thresholds, the 11:00 wind, the Gulp shrimp program), `k4LCL9ALryA` (SWBA
  pre-fishing bites at 18–20 ft), `46ha7J1Fc6A` (the structure-pitching and
  seasonal-speed interviews).
- **Mission Bay** — `6-mi3Qxn37c` (the 4–5 ft tide-swing target and the
  back-bay fallback on a ~7 ft swing), `AH4Eiya1Hn0` (the Ingram/Ingraham
  Street Bridge, jetties and docks), `G6YRT4HNxr8` (the umbrella rig),
  `YgqXf9iICyg` (a lot of water through a small opening, shallow overall;
  eelgrass shorelines with ducks diving as the author's best read).
- **Newport Bay / Newport Harbor** — `GVP3IChsmRQ` (the 2022-12-30 session:
  17→12 ft outside the mooring cans, 13→11–12 ft on the back side, a 12–14 ft
  working band and a bite 4 ft under the boat), `YgqXf9iICyg` (wide mouth,
  moderate main channel, faster back-bay channel), `XyKF34C5iB4` (Lane's
  three-bait shore plan and the clear-water switch to docks), `46ha7J1Fc6A`
  (the deeper eelgrass beds on 10 ft and 16 ft cranks).
- **Long Beach / Alamitos Bay** — `SdwwpQMJEOI` (the Olive Crest ProAm slack-
  then-current bite), `usHl-4SfqDA` (harbor session and the no-minimum-size
  call).
- **La Bocana estuary (Estero), Baja California Sur** — `Cobp85UvHmM` (spotties
  named in the estuary's population, no fish distinctly landed on camera).
