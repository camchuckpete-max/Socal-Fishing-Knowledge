# Evidence — Barred Sand Bass

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[sand bass](../sand-bass.md). One line per observation, grouped under the
parent note's section headings, in its order. Observations never change the
parent's stated doctrine; one that materially supports or contradicts it also
leaves a one-line cited trace beside that doctrine in the parent.

## Where & when

- `HMdrP4-i9MM` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-10-11, off North County San Diego near
  Camp Pendleton): on the annual Okuma kids/family charter also logged in
  [calico bass](calico-bass.md), sand bass were mixed into the calico catch
  over hard bottom with kelp not reaching the surface in roughly 40–42 ft,
  including one angler's first fish ever; legal size was called out on camera
  as 14 in, matching the calico figure. Unregistered channel.
- `SdwwpQMJEOI` (StokedOnFishing, 2025-05-16, Long Beach / Alamitos Bay —
  Olive Crest ProAm charity bass tournament): one boat's pre-fishing plan named
  sand bass and calico bass as the day's target species on a reef spot near
  Long Beach, working live bait plus soft-plastic paddle-tail swimbaits and
  A-rigs; no conditions, depth or outcome given beyond the gear plan.
  Unregistered channel; asr-uncertain(the reef spot's name, given on camera but
  too garbled to carry — omitted).
- `0dIwWiOc1NY` (StokedOnFishing, 2026-02-17 upload, just outside the marina,
  Ensenada, Baja California): a "regular size" sand bass, called good eating on
  camera, came incidentally while the crew jigged mackerel for bait — a
  bait-stop bycatch data point, not a targeted session. Region label: Baja
  Pacific north. Unregistered channel; asr-uncertain(one angler's catch was
  jokingly nicknamed "Diamond Valley bass" before being confirmed as a sand
  bass — crew banter, not a species name, and not carried as terminology).
- `XyKF34C5iB4` (Time on the Water, saltwater bass seminar, Pacific Coast
  Sportfishing show): three panelists gave their personal-best sand bass as
  9.5 lb off the Long Beach break wall, 9 lb 10 oz on a crankbait inside Long
  Beach Harbor, and one unweighed fish estimated in the 9–10 lb range — the
  corpus's only sand-bass grade figures above the 6 lb "big one" benchmark in
  `P2OzCf2CwXI`.
- `5D1vx29LVpI` (BDOutdoors SoCal Bight fishing report, 2022-12-15): a coastal
  sport boat ran "hit and switch" fishing for sand bass, called a standing good
  option for mid-December, on a swimbait fished on the bottom or a slug.
- `Blh2BA-7Ono` (BDOutdoors SoCal Bight fishing report, 2022-07-21): the sand
  bass bite along the beach slowed considerably after a big swell upwelled and
  cooled the water over the full moon; the same report's Palos Verdes charter
  still had good bass fishing on swimbaits, and the same report states the
  full-moon pattern ("wherever you're fishing bass ... expect good early and
  it's gonna slow down for a big part of the day") off a Catalina calico day,
  not off a sand bass day.

## Presence & forage

- `kwMIgkCtFUE` (BDOutdoors, SoCal Bight Fishing Academy Ep. 5, "Breaking down
  bass structure"): the structure typology behind the parent's Where & when and
  Presence & forage — shale bottom under shifting sand reading harder than the
  chart, isolated small-car-sized rocks on open sand off Huntington Beach,
  wrecks/artificial reefs/sewer pipes fishing alike, and the
  current-upwelling → zooplankton → bait fish → bass chain. Registered voice.
- `3tur7-VCM2g` (BDOutdoors, SoCal Bight Fishing Academy Ep. 8, 2023-02-17,
  coast from below Huntington Beach to off Long Beach): called the worst
  wintertime coastal bass fishing seen in at least ten years and attributed it
  not to a lack of fish but to acres of bait everywhere keeping the fish from
  aggregating on any spot. Registered voice.
- `5Oh3IPIC5g4` (Chad, HookUp Baits co-owner, Fred Hall seminar, 2020): the
  red-crab "Oreo cookie effect" on San Diego bass grounds — when the fish are
  packed with live red crab a red-crab bait is the hardest thing to get bit on
  and a sardine-pattern bait is eaten immediately, and a couple of San Diego
  tournaments were won on that read. Scoped on camera to "bass," not to barred
  sand bass specifically. Promotional seminar for the presenter's own product
  line — low confidence under the registry rubric.
- `usHl-4SfqDA` (StokedOnFishing, 2024-11-13, Long Beach Harbor): with wind off
  the breakwater the crew fished wind-protected harbor reefs and pilings on
  artificials for a calico / sand bass / spotted bay bass slam, and called out
  old pilings over new ones because the old pilings carry the crustacean
  growth. Unregistered channel.

## Situations → techniques

- `P2OzCf2CwXI` (BDOutdoors, "Sand Bass 101," 2026-07-06, studio): the parent's
  backbone — the anchovy slider outfit, the plastics and colour list, the
  weedless option, the A-rig chain reaction, and the two artificial-lure
  outfits. Registered voice; a studio tackle walk-through, not a trip report.
- `9br4Z4sfcNI` (BDOutdoors w/ Nick, California Current, 2024-08-12, studio):
  the double-swimbait rig tied on camera — three-way swivel, 5 in and 7 in
  paddle-tails, 12 in and 24 in leaders, 65 lb braid to 40 lb fluorocarbon on a
  300-size low-profile reel and an 8 ft heavy rod — pitched for summer fish
  chasing bait high in the kelp.
- `5p6gu14ZC4w` (BDOutdoors SoCal Bight fishing report, 2022-10-26): the
  wintertime sand bass and lingcod rig demonstrated at the end of the report —
  an 8 in slug on a 3 oz sled head with a screw-lock head and assist hook so a
  missed bite doesn't pull the plastic down the shank, with a lot of the bites
  coming on the sink.
- `-eaaWPN5Fxk` (BDOutdoors, SoCal Bight Fishing Academy Ep. 7, 2023-02-10,
  "Calico and sand bass tackle"): the deep-structure rod spec (8 ft graphite
  heavy, 300-size reel, 50 lb braid, 40 lb fluorocarbon) with the explicit
  warning against extra-heavy or double-extra-heavy blanks because the stiffer
  tip pulls hooks, especially on sand bass; also the stand-off-and-bomb-a-slug
  presentation at the La Jolla artificial reefs on a 1 oz head, where the bait
  rarely travels 10–20 ft before a big fish eats it; also the deep-spot knife /
  slow-pitch jig for 80–120 ft when drift or current stops you fishing a small
  spot vertically — assist hooks at the top, long rod sweeps with a slack-line
  sink, 10–15 turns up, and minimal thumb pressure in free spool so the jig
  drops straight instead of scoping line out. Registered voice.
- `0HwYl0HO2tc` (Fred Hall 2018 Long Beach HookUp Baits seminar): the
  double-bait tube dropper rig and the 3–4 oz big-bait program, logged in full
  in [tube-bait fishing](../../techniques/tube-bait-fishing.md). Promotional
  seminar for the presenter's own product line — low confidence, and the same
  product line as `5Oh3IPIC5g4`, so the two are one sponsored voice rather than
  two independent sources.
- `LqW32NSkObY` (Tommy Ponce, kayak guide, Dana Point, Fisher Newb seminar,
  2019-03-13): a bait or bottom presentation off rock structure — a sardine
  near a rock pile — fished from a kayak the same as from a boat. Single
  mention on the sand bass point; unregistered channel.
- `7T6dIYqr3KI` (SearcherSportfishing, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 141,"
  2021-04-01): names sand bass, alongside sculpin, rockfish and lingcod, as a
  species slow-pitch jigging is "very effective" for, with no conditions,
  depth or scenario attached — a species list, not a situation. Unregistered
  channel.
- `5fxAN1Ofn8M` (Tackle Express, 2026-03-18): for schooling or suspended bass
  where the fish's position in the column is unknown, keep a heavy (~20 g)
  sinking jig tied on through fall and winter and jig it through any part of
  the column, thumbing the spool on the drop on conventional tackle; the bait
  itself is never named. Unregistered channel, single mention.

## Doctrine & conflicts

- `JK2-cpaXkI8` (Dave Hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, 2025-03-06, PCS Show
  seminar): the migration-vs-squid-vs-bait-size passage behind the parent's
  Doctrine & conflicts section, stated as a rebuttal to a claim that Humboldt
  squid ate the sand bass. Registered voice, single mention on this point. He
  does not say "legal" on camera beside the 14 in figure, which nonetheless
  matches the registered California minimum size.
  asr-uncertain(the bait species in the 8 in bait-size passage — rendered once
  as "sard[ine]" and once elsewhere as "anchovies," too garbled to confirm, so
  the parent leaves the species unstated).

## Landing & handling

- `SdwwpQMJEOI` (StokedOnFishing, 2025-05-16, Long Beach Yacht Club weigh dock,
  Alamitos Bay): duplicate-of the Where & when entry above, kept here for the
  handling instruction — at the live weigh-in for a mixed calico / sand bass /
  spotted bay bass catch, dock staff told anglers releasing fish to grab them
  by the middle of the body, not the tail. Unregistered channel.
- `usHl-4SfqDA` (StokedOnFishing, 2024-11-13, Long Beach breakwater):
  duplicate-of the Presence & forage entry above, kept here for the release
  ethic — the local slogan "our bass are slow to grow, so let them go to fight
  another day," with photo-and-release described as the encouraged local
  practice for SoCal calico and sand bass. Unregistered channel.
- `ohR9DeBOU9E` (Dave Hansen, Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-04-25): a
  90-second fillet demonstration on a sand bass, described as the same cut as a
  calico with a bigger fillet off the bigger fish. Registered voice.
