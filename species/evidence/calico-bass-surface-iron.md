# Evidence — Calico Bass — surface iron

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[calico bass — surface iron](../calico-bass-surface-iron.md). One line per
observation, grouped under the parent note's section headings, in its order.
Observations never change the parent's stated doctrine; one that materially
supports or contradicts it also leaves a one-line cited trace beside that
doctrine in the parent. Full calico source provenance, including the sources
this page does not draw on, is in [calico bass evidence](calico-bass.md).

The page is all on-the-water footage: two Strictly Irons Cedros episodes
(`jared-saaib`, a registered voice) and two StokedOnFishing episodes (an
unregistered channel, capped at medium), which is why the page sits at
`medium`.

## When this is the call

- `IdMJnlg_rZk` (Strictly Irons, 2024 four-day fly-in trip, upload 2024-10-08,
  Cedros Island, "Day 1"): after "insane" yellowtail fishing inside the first
  two hours of day one the bite "finally slowed down," so the crew "headed in
  to catch some Calico bass… and just wait for the yellowtail to pop up
  again" — the iron program run as a deliberate mid-day filler rather than a
  fallback. Registered voice; on-the-water observation, single trip.
- `2cxeg_AE418` (Strictly Irons, 2025-01-23, Cedros Island, "Day 2"): the same
  in-tight calico ground on a later trip — "that's a lot of calico… it's
  raining calicos out here," with fish visible in numbers around the boat.
  Registered voice; single session.
- `3SATCeA3KaU` (StokedOnFishing, 2014-11-13, Geronimo Island to Chester's
  Rock, south end of Cedros Island, "MC Swimbaits Skiff Trip part 3"): the
  reverse of the same relationship — an afternoon session had "big calico
  choking the surface iron," and the following morning's deliberate yellowtail
  push was repeatedly interrupted by them ("pesky 5-pound calicoes… putting off
  my 50-pound yellow"). Birds are the stated search cue: "birds flared up, came
  out here, threw the jig." Duplicate-of the Cedros entry in
  [calico bass evidence](calico-bass.md), kept here for the iron detail.
  Unregistered channel.

## How calico bass changes the execution

- `I-QBxuV2p7M` (StokedOnFishing, 2021-10 trip per on-camera reference,
  2022-06-15 upload, Cedros Island, "Stoked to Be Back in Cedros Island Calico
  Bass Fishing"): after a full day's yellowtail trolling produced zero bites
  the crew diverted to a calico ground and worked it as drifts over reef
  bottom — "reef reef reef reef… 18, 18, 20 ft" — with birds working overhead
  marking the fish. The bite was wide open on surface iron, "catching them
  pretty much every cast," with repeated doubles and a fish called at five
  pounds. Asked about surface sign, the answer on camera is that there was
  none — the fish were found on the meter and the birds. Duplicate-of the
  Cedros entry in [calico bass evidence](calico-bass.md), kept here for the
  depth, drift and colour detail. Unregistered channel.
- `I-QBxuV2p7M` (same session): two anglers ran different colours side by side
  — "I'm throwing white, he's throwing blue-greenish" — and both produced. Also
  logged in [surface iron — colour
  evidence](../../techniques/evidence/surface-iron-color.md). A one-session
  data point, not a colour doctrine. Unregistered channel.
- `IdMJnlg_rZk` (Strictly Irons, 2024, Cedros Island, "Day 1"): the mid-day
  beach program is called on camera as a "small… 5½ in" iron "targeting calico
  bass here in tight on the beach." Registered voice;
  asr-uncertain(the jig's brand name, rendered "righteous" in the captions —
  the size and the in-tight application are clear, the model is not, so the
  parent note carries the size only).
