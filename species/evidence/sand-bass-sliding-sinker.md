## How barred sand bass changes the execution

- `P2OzCf2CwXI` (BDOutdoors, "Sand Bass 101," 2026-07-06, studio): the anchovy
  slider walk-through this page is built from — an 8.5 ft blank, framed as the
  8–8.5 ft / 12–30 lb finesse fly-line class, chosen for whippiness in the tip
  because anchovies are small baits and the gear has to be downsized to fish
  them; a small narrow star-drag reel praised for an oversized handle, on the
  reasoning that the fishing is vertical and sand bass pull harder than rockfish
  and harder than calico, so leverage is what gets them out of the rocks; 30 lb
  braid to about 10 ft of 20 lb fluorocarbon, sized off a ~6 lb big fish and
  called enough to keep getting bit when the bite goes scratchy; a size 2 J hook
  and a 1/2 oz egg sinker, sized up for current or wind and down on a glassy
  day; and the presentation itself — cast out and sink the anchovy slowly
  through the column, because sand bass are structure-oriented but not locked to
  the structure the way lingcod or cabezon are and hover slightly above or
  within the reef, wreck or kelp. Registered voice; a studio tackle
  walk-through, not a trip report. The same source's parent-note entry, covering
  its plastics, weedless, A-rig and dropper-loop material, is in
  [sand bass evidence](sand-bass.md#situations--techniques).
