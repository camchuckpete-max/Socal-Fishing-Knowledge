# Evidence — California Sheephead — sliding sinker

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[California sheephead — sliding sinker](../sheephead-sliding-sinker.md). One
line per observation, grouped under the parent note's section headings, in its
order. Observations never change the parent's stated doctrine; one that
materially supports or contradicts it also leaves a one-line cited trace beside
that doctrine in the parent. Full California sheephead source provenance,
including the sources this page does not draw on, is in
[sheephead evidence](sheephead.md).

**Confidence note:** `confidence: medium` because the whole program — hook size
and finish, the weight ceiling, the suspension rule and the bait ranking —
rests on one short tips video from a registered voice (`OSbAHdB4uPs`), with the
hook-size call corroborated only by that same voice's cut-squid video
(`yKaHcxX46l4`).

## How California sheephead changes the execution

- `OSbAHdB4uPs` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2021-01-12, SoCal artificial reefs;
  registered voice — dave-hansen): the dedicated sheephead-tips video and the
  source of every parameter on this page — "the number two or the number four
  bronze thin wire black bronze hook, no chrome, give the chrome hooks to your
  buddies"; "small weight, little slider quarter ounce half ounce, not bigger
  than a half because you don't want to go flying by"; "you don't want your
  bait laying on the bottom … their mouth is in front, it's not on the bottom,
  if you lay your bait on the bottom what you're going to catch is shovel nose
  sharks, bat rays, maybe a smooth hound … maybe a leopard shark"; and the bait
  ranking — "that strip of squid or that piece of muscle or pismo clams, if you
  got some extra cash pismo clams are always the go-to bait for big sheephead …
  cut it into little strips," with mussel put on a treble hook "because of the
  fact that it's just a big wad of slime." Framing in the same video — chummed
  reefs producing sheephead, sargo and "sugar bass" together, the cold-water
  pitch and the take-your-kids pitch — is logged in the parent species evidence
  file under [Where & when](sheephead.md#where--when). Closes on a Baja Jerky
  discount code and a membership pitch, skipped per the curation bar
  (promotional, not fishing knowledge).
- `yKaHcxX46l4` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-07-29, cut-squid bait method;
  registered voice): squid strips framed for "sheephead or sargo or the smaller
  bass … when you don't have live bait." Prep and hooking detail — head and
  tentacles into the chum bag, body split and cut into thin strips, hooked at
  the very tip so it threads on "just like a piece of spaghetti," never "a big
  ball … a big wad … a square meal" — with the smaller hook called out for
  sheephead specifically: "sheephead are going to eat this smaller hook all day
  every day in this strip." The generalized strip prep lives on
  [hooks](../../tackle/hooks.md); the chum-line half is on
  [chunking](../../techniques/chunking.md).

## Rigs & gear

- `f4qYtHACGyk` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2020-11-07, SoCal artificial reefs;
  registered voice): the same chummed-reef program, but the terminal build shown is a
  hook tied straight to the mono with a 1/16–1/4 oz split shot 2–3 in above it
  — "way too heavy a line, way too big of a hook, swivels and beads and crap"
  named as the common mistake — not the slider. Carried here only for the
  straight-tie rule; the full split-shot build lives on
  [chunking](../../techniques/chunking.md).
- `QEpa3WYbEa8` (Tackle Express, 2025-08-13, 23-second clip, "JUMBO California
  Sheephead on Sliding Egg Sinker Rig"): the sliding-egg-sinker rig is stated in
  the **title only** — no rigging is shown or described in the audible content,
  which is the crew reacting to the fish on deck. Duplicate-of `qKOmJH_WtJo`
  (two cuts of one landing, uploaded five weeks apart under different titles);
  the other cut is logged in the parent species evidence file under
  [Feeding triggers](sheephead.md#feeding-triggers). A title is the uploader's
  claim, not footage.
