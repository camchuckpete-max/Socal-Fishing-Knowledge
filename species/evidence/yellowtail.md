# Evidence — Yellowtail

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing [yellowtail](../yellowtail.md). One line per observation, grouped under the parent note's section headings, in its order. Observations never change the parent's stated doctrine; a contradicting one is also traced beside that doctrine in the parent.

## Where & when

### Santa Barbara Island (Channel Islands)

- `SGbynqaiHdY` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-07-18 upload, Santa Barbara Island): "STOKED On Save The Brave," first annual charity charter for 15 military veterans aboard the 85 ft El Dorado, Long Beach Sport Fishing — a distinct, earlier trip from the similarly formatted 15-veteran SCI charter `nsUdT-zXI8s`; anchored in the dark near kelp and a ridge off the island (~102 ft, red crab present on the structure), fishing live squid on light sinkers so the bait sank naturally in the current — yellowtail said to swim that corner "every once in a while," rockfish the fallback; mid-session, with the kelp bite not materializing as it reportedly had the day before, the captain repositioned to the outer edge in 20 fathoms on the island's back (weather) side, reasoning conditions had shifted enough that lines laid into the kelp weren't reaching the fish — a same-zone day-to-day-conditions data point; the session was dominated by mixed rockfish ("red"/vermilion) on the squid (detail in [rockfish & lingcod](../rockfish-lingcod.md)); a late fish the crew rated above the rockfish ("that's where it's at") came on a captioned "squish jig" — read as a possible squid-pattern jig, consistent with the zone's squid-colored-jig change-up, but the species was never named and is not carried as a confirmed yellowtail; asr-uncertain(jig name); the pre-trip segment showed a star-drag "Avenger" combo pre-spooled with 20 lb (veterans' giveaway gear, not necessarily crew gear; brand not otherwise cross-referenced) and named the destination "St Clemente Island," at odds with the SBI location shown for the rest of the trip — flagged, not reconciled; unregistered channel.

## Zone material (pending zone guides)

Per-zone doctrine extracted from the parent's retired spot tour (2026-08-24
rework). Parked here, one line per item, for the zone-guide pass to mine —
nothing was deleted, only moved.

- **Santa Barbara Island / Sutil** — squid-driven zone: "you take a squid to any of these islands you'll probably have a good chance of catching the yellowtail" (`5p6gu14ZC4w`); when the squid drops deep or moves, the bite dies but the fish did not leave — relocate the forage on the meter before writing the zone off (`YZtX1MiT0y8`); shows in both the warming-transition and fall-fragmentation regimes.
- **Catalina** — structure yellows on hard bottom; fall fish while the water holds: ~40 yellows in a late-October day, "no reason you couldn't catch a yellowtail" while temps stay good (`5p6gu14ZC4w`); anchor-and-chum live-squid stops produce fast when squid is around (`MUpvP-Yl2R0`, `8THSuqoPI_Q`, `oadK6zIYyCo`); Farnsworth Bank (caption-garbled "foreign First Bank," identified by its closure rule) gives good fall yo-yo fishing — keep-list restriction in the parent's Regulations (`5p6gu14ZC4w`); see [island structure](../../locations/island-structure.md).
- **Coastal kelps (LA–San Diego)** — kelp-line and coastal-structure fish in the warm-stable regime; squid on the coast pulls them in (`5p6gu14ZC4w`); surface iron along the kelp edge is the classic approach (cameron; corpus coverage thin).
- **San Clemente Island** — a squid fishery on the back side / east end: 20–25 lb average, high teens to high 30s (`YZtX1MiT0y8`); squid outfishes sardine on the dropper loop when both are down (`sHnqSIOjTdM`); a banner squid day ran to 167 fish on 30 lb mono with a 30 lb fluoro top shot (`zQtExV8Z2eY`); resident sea lions work hooked fish here (parent's Landing & handling).
- **La Jolla and the SD offshore banks** — structure yellows on hard bottom and pinnacles, strongest warming transition into warm-stable (uncited legacy claim, flagged in the spot harvest); presence runs year-round while half-day boats can go months between fish (cameron, pilot feedback 2026-08-24).
- **Cortez Bank** — grades 20–40 lb with a "larger grade" reputation (`UfuiWFVvz2E`); the anchored program is flyline with the current (25–40 lb to a No. 2 J hook), yo-yo iron, and surface iron, with current gating the bite (`fDSd9kqwYW0`, `UfuiWFVvz2E`); hooked fish "swim straight down" into the rocks on lighter line (`UfuiWFVvz2E`); the fleet calls resident fish "Home Guard" across boats (`c3NFkQbdDy0`, `tU4jhAkdzNw`). The run distance, the bank's depth bands, and the Cortez/Tanner naming tension are location knowledge and live on the zone page — [Tanner Bank](../../locations/tanner-bank.md), which carries Cortez Bank as its southern mark.
- **Coronado Islands** — "The Coronados are synonymous with yellowtail" (`D5DR7Kx42_A`); the western coast, exposed to colder Pacific currents and tidal swings, stays bait-rich (`D5DR7Kx42_A`) — the zone now has a committed guide, [targeting yellowtail — Coronado Islands](../yellowtail-coronado-islands.md), which carries the program.
- **Offshore paddies** — in a strong warm-water (El Niño) year, paddies 4–30 ft wide hold 5–35 lb fish (`UfuiWFVvz2E`); go light when they're shy — "got to go light on these sometimes," a 20 lb-line paddy fish (`VWClGAn2WEw`).
- **Ensenada → La Bocana (Baja Pacific coast)** — Ensenada reef/point fish are constantly on the move rather than holding (`0dIwWiOc1NY`); high spots (~25 ft cresting from ~200 ft) hold them (`CKq0Z6ExVs4`); offshore floating objects ~35 mi out draw them alongside skipjack/yellowfin sign (`0bcDBGzQnGw`); La Bocana inshore rock structure in 25–30 ft — "yellows on the surface iron always" (`Cobp85UvHmM`, `qri15R3caYE`); the parent's frame — coastal structure, islands, and ridges between the border and BOLA carrying yellowtail more or less year-round, grade improving south — is cameron doctrine, corpus coverage thin.
- **Alijos Rocks** — a remote offshore bank on multi-day long-range trips (edge-of-scope Baja, flagged); yellowtail can be the standout of a mixed bag with yellowfin, wahoo, and dorado (`6kpWn2sXokI`).
- **La Paz (Sea of Cortez side)** — *jurel* on shallow rock structure: live-bait slow-troll in as little as 12 ft, doubles close to 30 lb (`ORC1A68cEeM`); flylined mackerel at shared high spots (`qM7iOO7fOBw`); dropped-bait slow-troll on bank high spots like "El Bajo" (~50 ft at low tide, ≤80 ft at high) (`92y14x33etQ`).
- **Guadalupe Island** — a big-fish structure fishery on steep rock drop-offs: dropper-loop fish nicknamed "water buffalo," about eight of ten hookups lost to rock cutoffs even on heavy leader (`RgtkbmBFUXI`); the only recorded weight figure is up to 25 lb, from a 2009 season recap (`Sx40JvCXFuA`), with the sign at the island's north end (`_r08B4bItAY`).
- **The Ridge / Uncle Sam Bank** — the fall (late September / early October) seven-day-trip destination alongside Alijos Rocks, "famous for yellowtail and black sea bass and grouper and yellowfin tuna" in that window (`_r08B4bItAY`); bottom-fished with an 80 lb interchangeable trolling/dropper-loop outfit, 16 oz weight, live sardine or mackerel (`uWGGeDOprsE`; [Searcher five-outfit quiver](../../tackle/searcher-alijos-rocks-ridge-7-day-quiver.md)); distances from the same clip in [wahoo](../wahoo.md#where--when).
- **Cedros Island** — a resident, catch-and-release-heavy fishery: a true resident "home guard" yellowtail (40+ lb, sometimes 30 lb) can turn up anywhere around the island — the reason for the 40 lb line-class floor (`WE643Fue1_A`; [line & leader](../../tackle/line-and-leader.md)); North Point is the call for the bigger 30–40 lb fish (`wdbqTio1SQU`); a shallow-structure program works rocks cresting at 28–30 ft on slow-trolled giant mackerel (`QSmE3mdEL28`, `Zo92MG459gQ`); the bite varies day to day at the same spot — "every single day is different here" (`I-QBxuV2p7M`); October frames the island as a multi-species draw with yellowtail the anchor, grade tied to water state, not the calendar (`A8SuzB5qiKE`, `6EDQtQHEwFE`).
- **Chester's Rock** — the rock at the south end of the Cedros complex is "historically a good big yellowtail spot" for 40–50 lb fish, and anglers on 40–50 lb line landed theirs while several on lighter line lost fish in the shallow rock (`_r_qKX_7080`). The "Yellow Tail triangle" geography (Cedros / the Benitos / Punta Eugenia), the run legs from San Diego, and the rock's depth profile are location knowledge and live on the zone page — [Cedros / San Benitos](../../locations/cedros-island.md), which carries all three cited to the same id.
- **San Benito Islands** — squid-keyed (the 30–40 fathom bait-migration read is in the parent's Presence & forage, `JaKSGkZ6CAc`); the mix runs bait, surface iron, and yo-yo through the day (`SH7zOA9ZF3o`); a dead anchor bite can revive by drifting over the marks (`97clKtVsEOs`); deep-diving fish rock you off on "anything that's structure, a piece of weed" (`SH7zOA9ZF3o`).
- **Bahía de los Ángeles (BOLA)** — "legendary yellowtail grounds … yellowtail everywhere on this high spot" (`vqsD0qpwcJA`); late warm-stable ([September](../../seasonal/september.md)) fish are on the bottom — worked with dropper loops and yo-yo iron (cameron).
