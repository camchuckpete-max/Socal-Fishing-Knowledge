# Evidence — Yellowtail — surface iron

The observation layer behind
[yellowtail — surface iron](../yellowtail-surface-iron.md). One line per
observation, grouped under the section it supports. Observations never change
the note's stated doctrine.

## How yellowtail changes the execution

- `oadK6zIYyCo` (Catalina): an iron sunk about 30 ft before winding drew a
  first-cast strike.
- `9tIp4n7q850` (Cedros): the same sink-then-wind on a Tady 45 framed on camera
  as a near-"yellowtail guarantee."
- `VpW91AKOFVQ`: yellowtail eating the jig at the boat/kayak after trailing it
  in, with the tip-down retrieve worked all the way back.
- `JgXMpjxRxmo`: a small iron over small anchovy out-producing larger jigs for
  bigger yellowtail, described as strongest earlier in the year.
- `Ec2GaNpiOHI`: the flat denial of species-specific retrieves, kept beside the
  speed doctrine rather than merged into it.

## When this is the call

- `0DKmYId1NN4`: staged-up fish reading as surface-iron and light-bait fish, and
  the call to go all-in on the iron once they boil. Scoped to structure fish.
- `ejUTAnONf7o`: 20–25 kt+ after a run of surface days, fleet bite moving mostly
  to yo-yo with the iron still picking fish.

## Rigs & gear

- `lYyi_Yh3S5g` (Searcher captain, 2009): Tady 45 / Salas 7X named as the
  favourite surface iron, chosen for casting distance, singled out for
  yellowtail at Cedros and the San Benitos.
