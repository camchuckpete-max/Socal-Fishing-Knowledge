## Hooking the bait — two placements, kept side by side

- `HTowqnwAMeA` (see above): the through-the-roof-of-the-mouth hooking method
  for yellowtail, best with mackerel but applicable to sardine, and the
  mouth-open reasoning (a bait with its mouth clamped shut can't breathe).
  Stated explicitly against the sideways nose hook, which can rotate onto the
  far side of the bait's head or start the bait spinning. duplicate-of the
  trigger entry above (same video, different claim).

## Rigs & gear

- `SAltQjih0ms` (see above): 12–18 in loop with a 12 in minimum, double San
  Diego jam knot on a 24 in loop, loop 3–4 ft above a 16 oz torpedo sinker, 5/0
  hook, and the breakaway overhand knot above the sinker.
- `YntRJAN88fs` (see above): the corroborating build — 14–18 in loop, hook 30–36
  in above a 16 oz torpedo sinker, doubled San Diego jam knot, breakaway
  overhand knot tied into 80 lb-test mainline, for yellowtail or grouper holding
  on bottom structure. duplicate-of the execution entry above (same video).
- `HTowqnwAMeA` (see above): hook upsizing from flyline size — a 2/0 flyline
  hook becomes a 3/0 or 4/0 on the dropper loop. duplicate-of the entries above
  (same video, different claim).
