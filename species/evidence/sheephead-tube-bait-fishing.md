# Evidence — California Sheephead — tube-bait fishing

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[California sheephead — tube-bait fishing](../sheephead-tube-bait-fishing.md).
One line per observation, grouped under the parent note's section headings, in
its order. Observations never change the parent's stated doctrine; one that
materially supports or contradicts it also leaves a one-line cited trace beside

**Confidence note:** `confidence: low` because the entire program — the profile
call, the weight range, the presentation, the scent exception and both grade
records — comes from one presenter, the HookUp Baits co-owner, promoting his own
product at two Fred Hall Show seminars (2018 and 2020) on two unregistered
channels. The sponsored-claim rule caps that at low regardless of channel, and
the 2018 seminar corroborates the presenter rather than the bait. Queued in
[the fact-check ledger](../../sources/fact-check-ledger.md).

## When this is the call

- `5Oh3IPIC5g4` (Fisher Newb, 2020-03-12, Fred Hall Show 2020 HookUp Baits
  seminar, presenter Chad, HookUp Baits co-owner; unregistered channel,
  promotional seminar for the presenter's own product): the by-catch framing —
  "we catch the big sheep head while we're bass fishing for calicos … it's
  almost a bycatch … go rock fishing and use a red crab bait, you'll catch
  sheephead; go calico bass fishing, use the red crab bait, you're gonna catch
  sheephead" — and the red-crab structure list: Catalina, San Clemente, tight to
  the Coronado Islands, Cedros Island (ASR renders it "seed Rose Island") and
  Ensenada/Baja (asr-uncertain: the island name). Same video calls sheephead the
  brand's "almost our mascot fish" and the presenter says he has "never caught
  so many big sheep head in my life" since fishing the red-crab bait mainly —
  both logged in the parent species evidence file under
  [Presence & forage](sheephead.md#presence--forage).
- `5Oh3IPIC5g4`, the saturation reversal ("Oreo cookie effect"): on a spot
  "absolutely packed with red crab … the hardest bait to get bit on is red
  crab," where a sardine or mint bait "gets hit immediately" — reported as
  winning a couple of San Diego tournaments in 2019 on that read, with the
  counter-case stated too ("when there's red crab kind of in that area … but
  they're not packed with them, that's when the red crab baits absolutely kill
  them"). Stated for the bass being fished as much as for sheephead; the full
  entry is in [tube baits evidence](../../lures/evidence/tube-baits.md).

## How California sheephead changes the execution

- `5Oh3IPIC5g4`, sizing: "usually the one ounce bullet is a really good one
  because they have the smaller [mouth], so a little smaller profile bait is
  easier for them to eat," with the scent added — "fill it full of this, you'll
  catch the biggest sheep heads you ever caught with that combination."
- `5Oh3IPIC5g4`, the island cast: "so you do that five eighths, one ounce, and
  throw it right up into the island … work it right above the rocks — that's
  absolutely deadly on calico bass and sheephead," stated for fish sitting tight
  to Catalina, San Clemente and the Coronado Islands.
- `5Oh3IPIC5g4`, the scent exception: asked for a favourite scent, the presenter
  says that "on bass and rockfish and every other kind of fish" he has not found
  one working better than another, "except for sheephead — sheephead absolutely
  love this red crab scent." A product claim from the product's co-owner;
  flagged on the parent.
- `5Oh3IPIC5g4`, grade: "that's how [in 2019] I caught [a] 24 pound sheephead,
  and it was throwing a red crab up into the island" (the seminar is 2020-03-12
  and says "last year," so 2019).
- `0HwYl0HO2tc` (Orange County SUP Fishing, 2018-03-08, Fred Hall 2018 Long
  Beach HookUp Baits seminar, same presenter and product; unregistered channel,
  promotional): between two anglers, "about five sheep[head] between 15 to 20
  pounds, every single one of them came on the [red-crab scent]" — the second
  grade record for the red-crab program, from the same presenter, so
  corroboration of the presenter rather than independent corroboration of the
  bait. The passage follows the Cedros yellowtail account in the same talk, but
  the ASR does not place the sheephead session, so no location is claimed.

## Rigs & gear

- `5Oh3IPIC5g4`, Cedros: "one ounce Big Games red crabs and one ounce bullet red
  crabs, that's really all you need to go there — you're gonna catch everything
  at [Cedros] Island on that red crab bait." The rest of the seminar's rod, line
  and leader ladder is keyed to bait weight rather than to species and is logged
  in [tube-bait fishing evidence](../../techniques/evidence/tube-bait-fishing.md).
