# Evidence — Ocean Whitefish

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[ocean whitefish](../ocean-whitefish.md). One line per observation, grouped
under the parent note's section headings, in its order. Observations never
change the parent's stated doctrine; a contradicting one is also traced beside
that doctrine in the parent.

**Confidence note (demoted from the parent's prose, 2026-08-27):** the note is
`confidence: medium` because the standout SoCal-specific account — "underrated,"
"you'll catch a hundred of them," a legit 30-lb-class dropper-loop fish — comes
from a single Channel Islands variety day (`6-8KfjEg0x8`). Everything else in
the corpus records whitefish as a by-catch on somebody else's program.

## Where & when

- `6-8KfjEg0x8` (BDOutdoors, 2026-07-23, Channel Islands out of Ventura,
  Channel Islands Yacht Club / Freedom Boat Club charter): a squid tank made
  before running out produced "monster-sized whitefish to start it off" on the
  shallow stop, then the boat moved to one ~175 ft rock for reds, blue bass,
  bocaccio and short lingcod; the whitefish stop itself is described only as
  "the shallows," with no depth given on camera. The narrator called the
  species "one of the prime species here in the Channel Islands," said "they
  school up like crazy up here — you'll catch a hundred of them," rated them
  "pound for pound, highly underrated," and called the fish "a legit 30-lb
  dropper loop rig." A couple of sheephead were lost on the same stop.
- `Y1xeieQI3B4` (StokedOnFishing, 2025-05-10, Cortez Bank aboard the 85 ft
  *El Dorado*; unregistered channel): a large grade of ocean whitefish —
  "look at the size of that white fish," "the white fish are absolutely
  Jurassic" per the crew — came from a mid-trip rockfishing stop after the boat
  had already boxed limits of bluefin tuna ("limits of blue fin tuna for 23
  passengers" on camera) and a good stack of yellowtail; see the
  [rockfish & lingcod](../rockfish-lingcod.md) Cortez Bank entry for the full
  stop. This is an **offshore bank, not an island** — the first region in this
  note beyond the Channel Islands — and is consistent with whitefish sharing
  hard-bottom high spots with the rockfish/lingcod complex. No depth or exact
  grade given on camera.
- `fDSd9kqwYW0` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-02-22, Cortez Bank out of Long Beach
  aboard the 85 ft *El Dorado*; unregistered channel): "reds and white fish" on
  squid off a drift the crew put at roughly **150 ft**, with sheephead in the
  same bag; squid named on camera as "the name of the game."
- `CKq0Z6ExVs4` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-11-25 upload, a high spot roughly
  **7–8 mi** from the Hotel Coral marina, Ensenada, Baja California, "Navico
  Weekend Getaway"; unregistered channel): a whitefish was **the first fish of
  the day** at a high spot the crew described as coming up to about **25 ft
  from roughly 200 ft**, a mark they expected to hold bottom fish, yellowtail
  and barracuda; see the [rockfish & lingcod](../rockfish-lingcod.md) Ensenada
  entry for the rest of the mixed bottomfish bag from the same stop. A further
  Baja region for this note beyond the Channel Islands and Cortez Bank; no
  additional detail given on camera.
- `HGyL7pXy3Ts` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2019-05-20, Catalina Island front side):
  an anchored calico-bass stop produced "a big white fish" with a sea lion
  chasing it up; the banter that follows names a "**10 pound white fish**" the
  sea lion took. The two lines are hard to reconcile in the auto-captions —
  one says the fish was got away "just in time," the next says the sea lion
  "stoled" it (asr-uncertain: whether the 10 lb fish was landed or lost).
- `ouBrIdO7d4k` (SD Fish and Sips, 2025-03-08, Pacific Coast Sportfishing Show
  floor interview, describing La Jolla): a San Diego float-tube angler,
  weather-limited at the time, described the La Jolla float-tube catch as
  "mostly white fish." Show-floor conversation, not on-the-water footage.
- `OYOda6T3f-8` (BDOutdoors, 2022-10-20, weekly Southern California Bight
  report, Catalina): a fly guide was reported catching calicos, bonito "even
  Whitefish on flies," in what the reporter called good steady wintertime
  fishing; the reporter added he did not know how the guide was getting the
  whitefish. Second-hand report, and the island name is rendered "Kathleen" in
  the auto-captions (asr-uncertain: Catalina read from context).

## Presence & forage

- `kwMIgkCtFUE` (BDOutdoors / SoCal Bight Fishing Academy ep. 5, 2023-01-27,
  nearshore bass structure): most of what a meter shows over inshore bass
  structure is perch — blacksmith and blue perch — with "the occasional small
  white fish or sheephead as well," a read the presenter says he confirmed by
  dropping GoPros on the structure.

## Feeding triggers

- `8XIy7hPsFkQ` (BDOutdoors, 2025-08-07, long-range jigging setups): a jig left
  sitting on the bottom gets eaten by whitefish, small cod and "little critters"
  instead of yellowtail — whitefish framed here as the fast bottom eat that
  costs you the drop. The retrieve rule the crewman draws from it is logged with
  the technique in [evidence — yo-yo iron](../../techniques/evidence/yo-yo-iron.md).

## Finding them (sign & sonar)

- `_ZThckj2TIM` (Roman Castro, 2020-04-04, San Diego rockfishing): marks
  hanging on top of structure do not tell you the species — "it could be red
  vermilion rockfish, it could be bocaccio, could be white fish" — and the
  presenter's answer is to drop baits and learn the marks over time.

## Situations → techniques

- `UfuiWFVvz2E` (StokedOnFishing, 2026-08-11, Cortez Bank high spot aboard a
  Constitution Sportfishing trip; unregistered channel): a whitefish — "an
  angry whitefish" — ate a yo-yo iron worked on the Cortez Bank yellowtail high
  spot, in the middle of a yellowtail bite that was also producing on flylined
  bait.
- `PRNMGpLj7Pw` (BDOutdoors, 2026-01-13, slow-pitch long-range report ep. 12):
  slow-pitch jigging is named as the way to turn "rock fishing, ling cod
  fishing, even white fish fishing into sport fishing." No jig weight, cadence
  or depth is given for whitefish specifically.

## Gear summary

- `3xx_vES0kdo` (Tackle Express, 2026-07-22, SoCal charter bait-tank hook
  guide): the presenter keeps **size 1 and 3/0 "gorilla light"** J hooks in the
  box because a charter day sometimes turns into rockfishing for "shallow cod,
  white fish, and sheep head." His **25 lb Blue Label fluorocarbon**
  recommendation in the same clip is scoped to the flyline setup ("that 25 lb
  fly line setup is going to be key"), not to those bottom hooks.
- `7L7OVoXE7KU` (Tackle Express, 2026-06-11) and `DfLcSS-J3g4` (Tackle Express,
  2026-06-02) carry the same on-camera exchange: a good all-purpose **island
  rod** "would work for calicos, whitefish, sheephead, stuff like that,"
  fished in the middle of its line rating. Two uploads of one conversation.

## Regulations

- `iAr6sbRC384` (Fisher Newb, 2020-03-16, Fred Hall Show 2020 modern
  rockfishing seminar, presenter Bobby Martinez; unregistered channel): stated
  from recollection at a live seminar that a **10-fish whitefish allowance
  stacks on the 10-rockfish daily bag** — "you can keep 10 whitefish with your
  10 rockfish." The surrounding canary-rockfish and species-count detail is too
  garbled in the auto-captions to transcribe (asr-uncertain), and the same
  claim is carried on [rockfish & lingcod](../rockfish-lingcod.md) and in
  `sources/regulatory-claims.md`.
- `46ha7J1Fc6A` (Roman Castro, 2020-03-12, Fred Hall Show 2020 interview): a
  San Diego charter operator, speaking just after the rockfish season opener,
  named the boat's current program as "rockfish, lingcod, whitefish and
  sheephead" — the corpus's only statement tying whitefish access to the
  California groundfish opener.
