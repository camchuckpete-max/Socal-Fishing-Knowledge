# Evidence — Bluefin Trolling

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[bluefin trolling](../bluefin-tuna-trolling.md). One line per observation, grouped
under the parent note's section headings, in its order. Observations never
change the parent's stated doctrine; a contradicting one is also traced beside
that doctrine in the parent.

## Situations → techniques

- `xzIaUEDklrE` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-12-14, ~55 mi offshore SoCal, exact bank
  not named on camera): live captain narration (voice heard as "Andrew,"
  presenter-inferred) of an in-season switch from Mad Mac to spreader bar made
  to select for grade, not because the Mad Mac had stopped working — "big [fish]
  one week before, it was Mad Max every week," then over the prior week the boat
  had been "getting only big ones on the spreader bars" while that day's schools
  ran mixed "40 to 100 plus" lb, so the crew swapped "to target the bigger
  ones"; unregistered channel, asr-uncertain("Mad Max" heard for Mad Mac).
  Traced beside the presentation-size axis in the parent as a
  contradicted-internal fact-check flag.
- `xzIaUEDklrE` (same trip, kite ruled out): "we want to get the kite up but
  there's like no wind right now, so it's gonna be really hard to position that
  up — so spreader bars," consistent with the parent's wind-driven
  kite-versus-troll call; the same crew also ran their center/farthest-back Mad
  Mac much farther back than the documented setback to give a boat-shy school
  room — the setback variance is logged in
  [Mad Mac](../../lures/mad-mac.md); unregistered channel.
- `e73wPONTOJU` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-10-06, SoCal island zone): on a morning
  the crew called "not happening" for bluefin, the boat threw a full spread — a
  trolled lure heard on camera as "Mad Max" and a spreader bar (brand heard as
  "Cal lures," not resolved), plus jigs and bait — and came up without a bluefin
  bite, then re-targeted dorado and yellowtail; the crew's framing was that they
  stuck with the bar and Mad-Mac-class lure "because that's been the thing" that
  season, i.e. the season's known producers. A blanked morning on the lures the
  crew judged the best bet, not a contradiction of the doctrine; unregistered
  channel, asr-uncertain(lure and bar brand names).
- `sHnqSIOjTdM` (StokedOnFishing, 2026-04-24 upload, trip in August with the
  year not stated on camera, offshore San Clemente Island, 2.5-day charter
  aboard the Eldorado): after a marked school passed under the boat without
  eating a thrown scoop of bait, the crew ran **four trolled lines** with more
  rods ready and picked up multiple smaller-grade bluefin including a **double
  hookup**, one lure nicknamed "old pinky" on camera (color/model not resolved)
  — trolling converting direct bites on a quiet day rather than only serving as
  a locate tool ("who would have thought trolling's paying off"); see
  [bluefin tuna](../bluefin-tuna.md) for the same trip's contrasting night-jig
  grade; unregistered channel.

## Trolling as the search tool

- `vCskOx6N-XM` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-03-23 upload, trip resolved to late
  October 2021, Tanner Bank, aboard Captain Andrew's own boat, Stoked On Fishing
  Charters): the captain's own program — run the spreader bars to cover ground
  while searching, then once the sounder marks good numbers of fish stop
  trolling and set a dead flying fish under the kite to fish the drift over the
  marks. He added that the bars can still draw strikes from big fish while
  searching, but stated a personal preference for the kite/balloon method once
  fish are found — a preference, not a claim that trolling under-performs the
  kite on hookup rate; unregistered channel. Source of the parent's
  locate-then-convert line.

## Doctrine & conflicts

- `elBPRrdkugU` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-12-22, offshore San Clemente / Catalina):
  running a spreader-bar-plus-"Mad Max" spread, the crew called out "five," "six
  or seven," and "15" **miles an hour** (stated in mph, not the kt used in the
  parent note — kept as spoken, not converted) as the spread's working speeds,
  framing the slower end as getting bit without burning fuel and the "15" as
  skipping over a "dead zone" speed range where "most boats" burn fuel without
  producing; no bounds given for that dead zone. Kept beside, not merged into,
  the kt-denominated bands in [Mad Mac](../../lures/mad-mac.md) and the parent's
  table; unregistered channel, asr-uncertain("Mad Max" heard for Mad Mac).
- `bM2vUS1B-yQ` (Your Saltwater Guide, dave-hansen, live seminar, PCS Show,
  2023-03-03): registered voice, single mention on the 9 kt sportboat ceiling
  and the quarter-gallon-per-mile figure at 14 kt — hence the parent's
  single-source flag and the ledger row; the same seminar credits speed trolling
  with producing bites driving straight over a sounded-out foamer.
- `JiJYamo0QHk` (Utah Spahghettzi Björker, Capt. Dave Hansen live seminar, Fred
  Hall Show 2019 Part 2, 2019-03-12): dave-hansen registered voice, single
  mention on the flat-fall program trade-off (three-to-five-hour fights versus
  six fish in the time a flat-fall boat lands one) — hence the parent's
  single-source flag; not reconciled with, and not a correction to, the
  flat-fall depth/mechanism doctrine in
  [knife jigging](../../techniques/knife-jigging.md#reach-for-this-when).
- `2y0VznL2qk8` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2020-12-29): presenter not named on
  camera, inferred as Capt. Dave Hansen from channel and crew continuity;
  dave-hansen is a registered voice. Source of the trolled-rubber-flyer versus
  dead-boat-dead-flyer wind rule in the parent; the parent keeps it beside the
  Mad Mac speed-troll row as a second framework rather than a correction, since
  the lure, the speed, and the direction the ~8 kt mark points all differ.
- `z1CmWHqe9uk` (BDOutdoors, Nate Winnicki, 2024-10-15, "NEED FOR SPEED — Tips
  on Speed Trolling for Bluefin Tuna"): registered contributor; source of the
  13–15 kt speed-troll band, the Rapala X-Rap Magnum Extreme alternative, and
  the low-profile bite-leader connection. Cameron's reading of "the conditions"
  as wind *and* coverage is his gloss on this source, carried in the parent as a
  (cameron) cite.
