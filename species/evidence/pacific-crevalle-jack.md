# Evidence — Pacific Crevalle Jack

Per-source provenance backing
[Pacific crevalle jack](../pacific-crevalle-jack.md). One line per
observation, grouped under the parent note's section headings, in its order.
Observations never change the parent's stated doctrine; a contradicting one is
also traced beside that doctrine in the parent.

**Confidence note (demoted from the parent's prose, 2026-08-27):** the note is
`confidence: low` because the entire Pacific crevalle jack corpus is three
StokedOnFishing episodes from one unregistered channel, all filmed at the same
East Cape resort event, and only one of them records a landed fish. Two are
species-name mentions inside somebody else's catch recap and one is a stated
plan that the video never confirms. No source anywhere in the corpus describes
crevalle behaviour, season, sonar signature, retrieve, size or handling, so
every technique, sign and sonar statement in the parent is transferred from the
yellowtail-class structure-jack program and carries a fact-check flag.

## Where & when

- `mL4Ph7t0WcQ` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-12-16 upload, Lighthouse Point beach,
  East Cape, Baja California Sur — Hotel Buena Vista Beach Resort trip): a
  local captain described Lighthouse Point as a beach spot with a drop very
  close to shore, "one of the main fishing spots that we have here," world
  famous for roosterfish, fished by sight and also in the afternoon and at
  night, holding a mixed inshore assemblage catchable by casting from the sand
  — sharks, roosterfish, jacks, dorado and yellowfin tuna were all named. The
  lighthouse itself was said to date from the 1980s. First data point in this
  corpus placing jack crevalle at a named East Cape structure spot; a presence
  note only, not a technique or behaviour claim. asr-uncertain: a further
  species name heard as "kuas" was not confidently resolved and is not carried;
  the phrase rendered "big guy Jack Jackel Dorado" is garbled and only the
  jack/dorado pairing is carried.
- `APsnsunT4gM` (StokedOnFishing, 2020-04-08 upload, East Cape, Baja
  California Sur — Hotel Buena Vista Beach Resort trip, "Hot Bite in Baja," 5th
  annual "Fiesta" trip; same resort/event as the `mL4Ph7t0WcQ` and
  `84XPJAeH0Rw` entries): named-target, **not a confirmed catch** — the crew
  made sardines inshore because "the bait situation has been kind of poor
  lately," then stated a plan to "get some roosters, some jacks, some snappers,
  everything at the lighthouse," which is the only sourced statement of crevalle
  as a deliberate target anywhere in the corpus. The trip's actual on-camera
  catch (a ~20 lb beach-caught dorado on a sardine, a separate rip-bait rock
  session, and the closing recap naming marlin, tuna, snappers/pargo and
  roosterfish) never confirms a jack crevalle was landed, so this is weaker
  than the two presence data points. Unregistered channel, sponsor-heavy video
  (Okuma). asr-uncertain: the hook/rig call at the start of the sardine session
  is rendered "Circleville two on three on three out"; the same rig is stated
  cleanly later in the episode (below), which resolves it to a circle hook on a
  30 lb top shot.

## Presence & forage

- `84XPJAeH0Rw` (StokedOnFishing, 2019-02-10 upload, East Cape, Sea of Cortez,
  Baja California Sur — Hotel Buena Vista Beach Resort trip, "Non-Stop Tuna
  Action in Baja Mexico, part 1"; same destination as the `mL4Ph7t0WcQ` entry;
  unregistered channel): a **jack crevalle** was named among the previous day's
  mixed morning catch alongside four tuna, in an on-camera recap — the second
  East Cape data point placing jack crevalle in this same catch mix, and the
  only corpus record of one actually caught. A weight of **65** was called out
  on camera for "the big one," but the audio does not resolve whether that
  figure refers to the jack or to one of the tuna in the same recap, so it is
  not carried as a jack crevalle weight. asr-uncertain: the count phrase
  rendered "about six give Jack" is garbled and no second count is carried.

## Situations → techniques

- `APsnsunT4gM` (StokedOnFishing, 2020-04-08 upload, East Cape, Baja California
  Sur): the one sourced program shape — make live sardines inshore, then fish
  them roughly **100 yd off the beach in 30 ft** of water on the beach drop,
  with jacks among the stated targets. Gear broken down on camera: **50 lb
  spectra main line, 30 lb fluorocarbon top shot, a #2 circle hook** sized for
  the sardine and chosen "in case we get any roosters," on an Okuma Tesoro
  10-size reel and a 7'6" PCH heavy rod (rod brand not stated) — the same
  breakdown carried in [dorado](dorado.md), since the fish it landed on camera
  was the ~20 lb dorado, not a crevalle. duplicate-of the `APsnsunT4gM` entry
  under "Where & when"; kept separate because it is the execution half of the
  same segment.
