# Evidence — Dorado (Mahi-Mahi)

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing [dorado](../dorado.md). One line
per observation, grouped under the parent note's section headings, in its order.
Observations never change the parent's stated doctrine; a contradicting one is
also traced beside that doctrine in the parent.

## Where & when

- `E4vKwRaRueA` (BDOutdoors, Ali Hussainy, 2021-09-21, "Dorado Fishing Tactics,"
  ~15 mi off San Diego): the parent's primary doctrine source — season, the
  green/blue boundary at 71.6 °F measured on a productive paddy, paddy forage
  list, glassing ranges and the care rules. Its execution half — the J-hook
  rationale, the leader step-down ladder and the hook-to-bait matching — is
  logged in
  [dorado — kelp-paddy flyline evidence](dorado-kelp-paddy.md#how-dorado-changes-the-execution).
  Registered source — high confidence.
- `P9H-bpzT7eU` (SearcherSportfishing, Carl Schmidt, Fisherman's Landing,
  2022-09-29, "Tackle Tip Thursday Vol. 198 — Dorado Fishing 101," SoCal): source
  of the 8–15 lb average grade with fish to 30 lb, the voluntary release of 4–6 lb
  fish for table fare, and the 25–40 lb / 8 ft / 30 lb-top-shot / 2/0 J-hook
  outfit. The 3–5 second count, the live-bait-over-jig call and the small-mouth
  J-hook rationale are logged in
  [dorado — kelp-paddy flyline evidence](dorado-kelp-paddy.md).
  Registered channel, single mention on each claim — medium confidence;
  asr-uncertain(the small-mouth rationale — the auto-captions render it as "they
  have a pretty small amount," read in context as "small mouth"); the named
  Calstar 800M rod and Penn Fathom 32 Speed reel are product mentions and stay
  low per the `searcher-sportfishing` sponsored-claim caveat in the source
  registry.
- `HcEh5KOYTH4` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, 2022-10-12, "The proper
  way to fish a Kelp Patty!", location not stated): an oversized kelp paddy
  described on camera as "as big as the boat," spotted through gyro-stabilised

  incidental yellowtail well into October — the fall paddy program still producing

- `e73wPONTOJU` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-10-06 upload, SoCal coastline, exact

  heavy." A documented plan-B pivot: when an overnight bluefin push goes cold,

  medium confidence; asr-uncertain(the 66 count).

  roughly 7–13 nm offshore — a second data point consistent with the parent's

## Presence & forage

- `skRo1z41Dnc` (StokedOnFishing, aired 2015-01-17, "Top Gun 80 Offshore
  Grandslam, part 1," offshore San Diego, SoCal, H&M Landing charter aboard the
  Top Gun 80): dorado sighted jumping around a breezer — open-water surface sign,
  not a discrete kelp paddy. The crew's own day-one summary framed a pattern of
  "Dorado been coming in and then... yellowfin or blue fin will show up and...
  push out the Dorado," and speculated the same breezer had held bluefin two days
  earlier; see [bluefin tuna](../bluefin-tuna.md#finding-them-sign--sonar) for the
  full entry including a same-trip whale-associated tuna sighting. Confirmed catch
  mix was dorado, bluefin, yellowfin and yellowtail — a four-species day. The
  push-out pattern is presenter speculation on one trip and is not carried as
  doctrine. Unregistered channel, sponsor-heavy Simrad episode — medium confidence.
- `9tIp4n7q850` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-05-17 upload, Cedros Island, Baja, "Could
  Not Believe the Halibut Fishing in Cedros This Year"): during a bottom-fishing
  session otherwise producing halibut (see
  [California halibut](../california-halibut.md)), the crew hooked and landed a
  dorado that surfaced from the bottom — called out on camera as unusual ("way out
  here on the bottom... that's amazing"). **Contradicts** the parent's stated
  upper-water-column doctrine and is traced beside it there; logged as a single
  atypical incidental catch during bottom fishing, not a change to the paddy
  program. Unregistered channel — medium confidence.

## Situations → techniques

- `HcEh5KOYTH4` (Your Saltwater Guide / Capt. Dave Hansen, 2022-10-12, location not
  stated): on a pre-qualified, obviously loaded paddy — fish already jumping before
  the boat arrived — the crew drove straight to the edge and pitched baits
  immediately, no wake-up step. Corroborates the flylined-bait #1 ranking in the
  parent's router when fish are already active. duplicate-of the `HcEh5KOYTH4`
  Where & when entry above (same video, different claim).
- `r4J5nP5Bkl4` (StokedOnFishing, trip framed on camera as "this past June,"
  resolved from the 2024-04-26 upload date to June 2023, backside San Clemente
  Island, SoCal, "Summer Bluefin Bite on the ElDorado," 85 ft El Dorado, Captain
  TJ): a kelp-paddy dorado session mixed into the same trip's
  [bluefin tuna](../bluefin-tuna.md) sundowner bite produced roughly a half dozen
  dorado. Live-bait chumming from the tank drew fish from the bow up to the corner
  where anglers were fishing, working under birds sitting over the paddy — a
  bow-to-corner draw consistent with the parent's chumming/wake-up tricks (see
  [chunking](../../techniques/chunking.md)). The bite was described on camera as
  picky, fish behaving as free swimmers rather than committing. One fish — landed
  on 30 lb test, fought boatside including going briefly under and rubbing on the
  hull — connected on a circle hook square on the jaw, called out on camera as
  unusual. **Contradicts** the parent's J-hook-only paddy doctrine
  (`E4vKwRaRueA`) and is traced beside it there; this is the offshore kelp-paddy
  program itself, not the inshore beach context of the `APsnsunT4gM` entry below.
  Species ID on this specific fish is from context (part of the session's dorado
  tally, not independently confirmed on camera) — presenter-inferred. Unregistered
  channel — medium confidence.
