# Evidence — Cabrilla — jerkbait

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[cabrilla — jerkbait](../cabrilla-jerkbait.md). One line per observation,
grouped under the parent note's section headings, in its order. Observations
never change the parent's stated doctrine; a contradicting one is also traced
beside that doctrine in the parent. Full cabrilla source provenance, including
the sources this page does not draw on, is in
[cabrilla evidence](cabrilla.md).

## Provenance

- `EmZO8QiOfik` (Dirty Hookers, 2025-03-04, studio tackle-talk): this page's
  spine — a dedicated cabrilla breakdown by **Cesar**, a rod-company rep with
  **25+ years** fishing the Sea of Cortez for cabrilla, with **Ray Sharifi**
  co-hosting. `cesar` carries a product-endorsement caveat, so his mechanism
  and parameter doctrine is eligible for `high` while specific lure and reel
  model picks stay `low`.
- `M8hOYQ_6rSg` (Dirty Hookers, 2025-05-20, studio tackle-talk): a re-cut of
  the same session that repeats the colours-by-light and 170 → 150 sizing
  material nearly verbatim — not an independent corroboration.
- `dEPuDrhoClM` (Dirty Hookers, 2023-03-03, Tony Reyes Sea of Cortez trip,
  tackle-bag breakdown by Ray Sharifi): a trip-return debrief; `ray-sharifi`
  is registered and **Baja-scoped**, with product/model endorsements at `low`.
  The source of both sides of this page's sizing and bait-class conflicts.
- `T3cEQUqiH8E` (BDOutdoors, 2026-05-22 upload, Puro Fierro cabrilla
  tournament at Bahía de los Ángeles, on-camera start date **May 13**): the
  trip was sent and paid for by Yo-Zuri and the presenter fished team Yo-Zuri
  — **sponsor-heavy**, so lure- and line-model praise is a promotional claim;
  the retrieve description carried here is not a model claim.

## How cabrilla changes the execution

- `T3cEQUqiH8E` (BDOutdoors, Bahía de los Ángeles, tournament practice day):
  the retrieve description — "same exact action and retrieve style, it's just
  burning… you don't do the pauses and jerks and whatever, it is just casting
  wind, casting wind, casting wind. You just get into a cadence."

## Rigs & gear

- `EmZO8QiOfik`: lure attrition figures — 15 baits per six-hour morning
  session, at least three of every consistently-fished colour and four or five
  of the favourites; 60–80 baits packed for a multi-day trip; six baits a day
  as the floor for a non-dedicated angler. On one Bahía de los Ángeles morning
  the crew lost **three baits in under a minute** and three anglers broke off
  inside **45 seconds**, all of it spectra blow-ups on the sheer power of the
  fish rather than lure or hook failure.
- `dEPuDrhoClM`: cabrilla mangle hardware — a hook hanger "pulled, twisted and
  straightened on both sides" on a double hookup, and a hook point fully
  straightened within three fish after being bent back.

## Doctrine & conflicts

- `dEPuDrhoClM` (Dirty Hookers, 2023-03-03, Tony Reyes trip): on sizing, "you
  do want to bring a handful of 150s just in case for whatever reason they're
  only eating that smaller bait," but "even when they're schooled up eating
  these micro baits they still only want the 170" — a **three-quarter-inch**
  difference that "makes a huge difference." Recorded as a tension with the
  light-keyed 170 → 150 downsize; both are the same channel and neither states
  the other's conditions, so nothing is reconciled.
