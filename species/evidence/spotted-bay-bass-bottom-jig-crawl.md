## How spotted bay bass changes the execution

- `lP6cg4eEU6s` (Fisher Newb, 2020-03-06, Sav-On Tackle / IROD seminar): the
  craw/creature-bait program this page is built from — tap the bait out and
  "let it sink to the bottom," then, "depending on the trailer," either a
  straight grind or straight swimming; "learn to feel with my finger" whether
  you are "in some mucky soft bottom, grass, or coming up through structure," a
  read that comes from fishing it over time rather than from one cast; "every
  now and then you're going to stop just to check to see how far I've come off
  the bottom," framed as something a shore angler in particular has to remind
  himself to do because the bait is not staying down consistently, "because
  spotties do like the bottom, especially in the colder months"; and "rate of
  fall can have a big deal of why you might get bit, especially fishing with a
  craw or a crustacean" — a finesse version goes weightless, and the imitation
  is a crab off a piling that jets down to the bottom and pops back up.
  Unregistered channel; single-camera seminar with speakers not diarized, the
  speaker addressed only as "Jeremy" in the Q&A. Bait brand rendered "war bait"
  in the auto-captions — asr-uncertain, not confidently resolved to a model and
  not matched to the
  [War Baits HD spinnerbait](../../techniques/spinnerbaits.md) already
  documented for this fishery. The same seminar's slack-low-tide reaction-bite
  pattern, tide-height and shade reads and hook-them-outside-the-cover
  reasoning stay on the router — see
  [spotted bay bass evidence](spotted-bay-bass.md).
