## When this is the call

## How snook changes the execution

- `EcQK5elTPJE`: the drag instruction is given as fishing it "super tight … to
  where that fish can't pull," with the window stated plainly — "that fight is
  won in the first 5 seconds … you literally got 5 seconds to turn the fish." A
  grouper "he's going back to the rocks, he snaps you off"; a snook "doesn't
  necessarily rock it," and "the way you tell the difference is, a snook, when
  he hits, instead of diving towards the rock … you start coming up."
  asr-uncertain(connective phrasing): the surrounding captions are heavily
  garbled; the drag rule, the five-second figure and the dive-versus-rise tell
  are all legible, the sentences joining them are not.
- `EcQK5elTPJE`: the play-it-clean instruction and the hook-wear reason —
  "sandpaper mouth wears out the hook and you lose it" — sit in the same garbled
  stretch; the instruction is legible, the mechanism is asr-uncertain and is
  flagged as such on the species router under
  [landing & handling](../snook.md#landing--handling).
- `EcQK5elTPJE`: "we have 4X hooks and those … have straightened the hook out of
  a 4X hook," delivered alongside "these are big fish, the groupers go up to
  100 lb down there — don't show up with a peashooter."

## Rigs & gear

- `EcQK5elTPJE`: the tackle block is given as one run of instructions — "about a
  4/0 size reel set up with the Carolina rig, run 80 lb braid … you got to have
  a two-speed reel, do not show up there with a single-speed reel, you'll be
  breaking off all day … 80 lb braid to 80 lb fluoro to the sinker to your
  swivel to a 6 ft piece of 100 to 130 lb test." No sinker weight, style or bead
  is named anywhere in the segment. The 130 lb figure is stated twice, the
  second time against audience scepticism from the floor.
- `EcQK5elTPJE`: "the first six snook I hooked in that fishery all snapped me
  off — I went from 60 lb to 80 to 100 to 130 lb," given as the personal history
  behind the leader spec rather than as a rule derived from anything.
- `EcQK5elTPJE`: "you got your Carolina rig, got your 4X hook, match the size to
  the bait, whether it's the … or sardine"; live shrimp named as the bait "for
  that snook," with the logistics stated flatly — "you don't have a bait
  receiver for live shrimp … you got to get your bait the night before, the guy
  stays out all night catching it for you."
