# Evidence — California Sheephead

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[California sheephead](../sheephead.md). One line per observation, grouped
under the parent note's section headings, in its order. Observations never
change the parent's stated doctrine; a contradicting one is also traced beside
that doctrine in the parent.

**Confidence note (demoted from the parent's prose, 2026-08-27):** the note is
`confidence: medium` because the dedicated program — hook size, weight ceiling,
bait ranking, suspension rule — rests on one short tips video from a registered
voice (`OSbAHdB4uPs`), with the size call corroborated only by that same voice's
squid-bait video (`yKaHcxX46l4`); the per-source detail for that program now
sits in
[sheephead — sliding sinker evidence](sheephead-sliding-sinker.md). The
red-crab grade claims come from one
presenter selling his own bait at two Fred Hall seminars, which caps at low
under the sponsored-claim rule. Everything else in the corpus records sheephead
as by-catch on somebody else's program.

## Where & when

- `OSbAHdB4uPs` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2021-01-12, SoCal artificial reefs;
  registered voice — dave-hansen): the dedicated sheephead-tips video. Opens on
  chummed artificial reefs producing sheephead, sargo and "sugar bass"
  together, frames sheephead as what "lives in southern california every single
  day" against the summer big-game run, and pitches the fishery as the
  cold-water answer and as a way to teach kids. Closes on a Baja Jerky discount
  code and a membership pitch — skipped per the curation bar (promotional, not
  fishing knowledge). The same video's hook, weight, suspension and bait
  parameters are logged in
  [sheephead — sliding sinker evidence](sheephead-sliding-sinker.md#how-california-sheephead-changes-the-execution).
- `Q196GVwVqro` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2024-03-07, PCS Show live Thursday
  seminar; registered voice): artificial reefs named as holding "yellowtail,
  white seabass, sheephead, calico bass, sand bass, sargo — all the fish live
  there," with a viewer's reef day cited on camera as 11 different species in
  one day.
- `oadK6zIYyCo` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-12-23 upload, Catalina Island, "Family
  Fishing Time at Catalina Island"; unregistered channel, sponsor-heavy
  dealership-ad episode): a sheephead landed incidentally during a mixed-species
  session at anchor — bait and technique not stated in the footage — with the
  crew calling it "Poor Man's Lobster" on camera for its table quality.
- `6-8KfjEg0x8` (BDOutdoors, 2026-07-23, Channel Islands out of Ventura): "we
  got some whitefish, we lost a couple sheephead" off the shallow island stop
  before the boat moved to a ~175 ft rock for reds; the day is described as
  having little current, which the narrator called as important as the bait at
  the islands. See the [ocean whitefish](ocean-whitefish.md) entry for the same
  stop.
- `fDSd9kqwYW0` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-02-22, Cortez Bank out of Long Beach
  aboard the 85 ft *El Dorado*; unregistered channel): after limiting on
  rockfish at the edge of the bank the boat "moved into the shallows [and]
  picked up some sheep head and some white fish" on squid, off a drift the crew
  put at roughly 150 ft, before anchoring at the other end of the bank for
  yellowtail.
- `eL1Qm33-Mj0` (StokedOnFishing, 2025-03-31 upload, Cortez Bank aboard the *El
  Dorado*, Captain TJ, out of Long Beach; unregistered channel): a **flylined**
  sheephead came over the rail in the early-morning bite on a trip otherwise
  targeting yellowtail and bluefin at anchor on the bank — "well done, buddy, a
  fly line sheephead." Single observation, demoted from the parent's router
  table 2026-08-27: it shows sheephead can take a flylined bait on an offshore
  high spot, but no source builds a program on it. See
  [flyline](../../techniques/flyline.md) and
  [bluefin tuna](../bluefin-tuna.md) for the rest of that trip.
- `CKq0Z6ExVs4` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-11-25 upload, a high spot roughly 7–8 mi
  from the Hotel Coral marina, Ensenada, Baja California, "Navico Weekend
  Getaway"; unregistered channel): sheephead came off the mixed bottomfish high
  spot described on camera as rising to about 25 ft from roughly 200 ft — the
  same stop logged in the [rockfish & lingcod](rockfish-lingcod.md) and
  [ocean whitefish](ocean-whitefish.md) Ensenada entries. A crew member called
  one fish a **female** on camera, confirming rather than disputing the species
  call, and another warned "watch his teeth" as a fish was handled.
- `A8SuzB5qiKE` (StokedOnFishing, 2024-04-11 upload, Cedros Island with Cedros
  Outdoor Adventures; unregistered channel): October at Cedros named as a
  multi-species window for "resident big yellowtail, yellowfin tuna, dorado,
  calico bass, sheephead and big halibut" — a species-list mention, no sheephead
  catch detail.
- `u0scEBby7nA` (StokedOnFishing, 2025-03-22 upload, Cedros Island with Cedros
  Outdoor Adventures; unregistered channel, sponsor read for Hotel Coral and
  Marina): a "big old sheep head" came in on a day whose end-of-day tally also
  named a 35-lb-class yellowtail off surface iron. No sheephead technique or
  depth given on camera.
- `Ow87j6AALAY` (Tackle Express, 2025-09-22, Amigo Sportfishing trip; title
  names "Bluefin, Sheephead & Rockfish"): a 45-second passenger-limits clip
  whose audible content is all bluefin banter — the sheephead is in the title
  only, with no on-camera detail to carry.

## Presence & forage

- `5Oh3IPIC5g4` (Fisher Newb, 2020-03-12, Fred Hall Show 2020 HookUp Baits
  seminar, presenter Chad, HookUp Baits co-owner; unregistered channel,
  promotional seminar for the presenter's own product — **low confidence** under
  the sponsored-claim rule): calls sheephead the brand's "almost our mascot
  fish," says he has "never caught so many big sheep head in my life" since
  fishing the red-crab bait mainly, and states that in 2019 he caught a **24 lb**
  sheephead throwing a red crab up into the island. Sizing: "usually the one
  ounce bullet is a really good one because they have the smaller [mouth], so a
  little smaller profile bait is easier for them to eat." Weight range for the
  island cast: "five eighths, one ounce, and throw it right up into the island …
  work it right above the rocks." Framing: "we catch the big sheep head while
  we're bass fishing for calicos … it's almost a bycatch, you know." Red-crab
  structure named as Catalina, San Clemente, tight to the Coronado Islands,
  Cedros Island (ASR renders it "seed Rose Island") and Ensenada/Baja
  (asr-uncertain: the island name).
- `0HwYl0HO2tc` (Orange County SUP Fishing, 2018-03-08, Fred Hall 2018 Long
  Beach HookUp Baits seminar, same presenter and product; unregistered channel,
  promotional): between two anglers, "about five sheep[head] between 15 to 20
  pounds, every single one of them came on the [red-crab scent]" — the second
  grade record for the red-crab program, from the same presenter, so
  corroboration of the presenter rather than independent corroboration of the
  bait.
- `kwMIgkCtFUE` (BDOutdoors / SoCal Bight Fishing Academy ep. 5, 2023-01-27,
  nearshore bass structure): most of what a meter shows over inshore bass
  structure is blacksmith and blue perch, with "the occasional small white fish
  or sheephead as well," a read the presenter says he confirmed by dropping
  GoPros on the structure.

## Feeding triggers

- `ShSxNKAcUB4` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2020-12-08, white seabass method video;
  registered voice): the retrieve rule stated for a lightly-weighted squid on
  structure — "let it slowly sink down the water column, when it gets all the
  way down to the bottom reel it back up and throw it back out, constantly be
  moving your bait, I never let my bait set on the bottom"; nine out of ten
  throws get bit by "white sea bass, yellowtail, calico bass, blue perch,
  sheephead, something," and nothing biting is the cue to move. Weight
  philosophy in the same passage: "the least amount of weight as possible," with
  a flylined squid ruled out because it lies on the surface and a seagull eats
  it. Sheephead appear here as one of the species that eat the moving bait, not
  as the video's subject.
- `L3tkGVu516A` (StokedOnFishing, 2014-11-13, Dono, Baja, "MC Swimbaits Skiff
  Trip part 1," opening leg of the Chester's Rock trip; unregistered channel,
  sponsor-heavy episode): a sheephead ate a **homemade "butter knife" jig** —
  described on camera as about two bucks' worth of secondhand-store butter knife
  — and the crew noted the fish came "on the wind up," with the angler's cadence
  given as "slow wind, real real slow, it's like slow as I can turn the handle."
  Same session logged for [calico bass](calico-bass.md) and
  [yellowtail](yellowtail.md).
- `qKOmJH_WtJo` (Tackle Express, 2025-09-18, 33-second clip, "The Secret to
  Hooking Big California Sheephead | Stay in the Strike Zone Longer!"): a giant
  sheephead held up on deck; the strike-zone claim is in the **title only** —
  the audible content is the crew reacting to the fish and pointing out its
  teeth ("look at his little teeth, dude"). A title is the uploader's claim, not
  footage. The second cut of the same landing (`QEpa3WYbEa8`, duplicate-of
  `qKOmJH_WtJo` — two uploads five weeks apart under different titles) carries
  the sliding-egg-sinker claim and is logged in
  [sheephead — sliding sinker evidence](sheephead-sliding-sinker.md#rigs--gear).

## Gear summary

- `yKaHcxX46l4` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2022-07-29, cut-squid bait method;
  registered voice): squid strips framed for "sheephead or sargo or the smaller
  bass … when you don't have live bait," with the smaller hook called out for
  sheephead specifically. The prep and hooking detail, and the quoted
  small-hook line, are logged in
  [sheephead — sliding sinker evidence](sheephead-sliding-sinker.md#how-california-sheephead-changes-the-execution);
  the generalized strip prep lives on [hooks](../../tackle/hooks.md).
- `3xx_vES0kdo` (Tackle Express, 2026-07-22, SoCal charter bait-tank hook
  guide): the presenter keeps **size 1 and 3/0 "gorilla light"** J hooks in the
  box because a charter day sometimes turns into rockfishing for "shallow cod,
  white fish, and sheep head." His 25 lb Blue Label fluorocarbon recommendation
  in the same clip is scoped to the flyline setup, not to those bottom hooks.
- `7L7OVoXE7KU` (Tackle Express, 2026-06-11) and `DfLcSS-J3g4` (Tackle Express,
  2026-06-02) carry the same on-camera exchange: a good all-purpose **island
  rod** "would work for calicos, whitefish, sheephead, stuff like that," fished
  in the middle of its line rating. Two uploads of one conversation.
  `DfLcSS-J3g4` additionally states the **30 lb bait stick** case — "your
  dorado, kelp paddy, yellowtail, yellowfin, small bluefin, um sheephead, all
  your shallow water rockfishing, a 30-lb bait stick covers a lot" — at **7 ft
  6 in and up**.
- `PRNMGpLj7Pw` (BDOutdoors, 2026-01-13, slow-pitch long-range report ep. 12):
  "people are bringing live crabs and stuff out on the boats now … but if you
  don't want to do that, sheep head just absolutely love the slow pitch jigs. I
  don't know why — they definitely favor the stubby ones, the long skinny ones
  they don't like nearly as much." The live-crab remark is the corpus's only
  mention of live crab as sheephead bait and carries no rigging.
- `8XIy7hPsFkQ` (BDOutdoors, 2025-08-07, long-range jigging setups, aboard the
  *Vagabond*): "for some reason I have not figured out, big sheep head really
  love the slow pitch jigs." Second BDOutdoors mention of the same behaviour; no
  jig weight, cadence or depth given.

## Regulations

- `46ha7J1Fc6A` (Roman Castro, 2020-03-12, Fred Hall Show 2020 interview): a San
  Diego charter operator, speaking just after the rockfish season opener, named

  the corpus's only statement tying sheephead access to the California
