## Provenance

- `EmZO8QiOfik` (Dirty Hookers, 2025-03-04, studio tackle-talk): this page's
  only source — a dedicated cabrilla breakdown by **Cesar**, a rod-company rep
  with **25+ years** fishing the Sea of Cortez for cabrilla, with **Ray
  Sharifi** co-hosting. `cesar` carries a product-endorsement caveat, so his
  mechanism and parameter doctrine is eligible for `high` while the specific
  iron model picks (Salas 6X, 6X Jr., Tady 40, Krocodile, jig para) stay

  at `medium`.

## How cabrilla changes the execution

- `EmZO8QiOfik`: the yo-yo segment is a spoken contrast against rockfish
  jigging — the iron is cast, allowed to hit bottom and burned straight back,
  with the deep-band variant burning 30–40 cranks off the bottom before
  letting it settle and starting again, and bites arriving in the first 5–10
  cranks of the pickup. No trip footage backs the segment; it is studio
  doctrine.
