## Where & when

  they've just found out."

## Finding them (sign & sonar)

- `tpmOYXYQwhU` (SearcherSportfishing, 2020-04-22, no place named): in the
  Q&A segment of Tackle Talk Live, a viewer named Howard asks whether opah are
  always an incidental catch or are sometimes targeted; the answer is "in our
  experience it's an incidental catch — you're drifting along and somebody goes
  deep and uses either a sinker or a lure or something like that and they get
  down you know 150 to 200 feet and get a bite and it's a surprise when you get
  it up to color." Presenter not identified on camera. This citation does not
  name tuna as the program in progress, unlike `tio1oeibVlM`'s explicit tuna
  framing — the two are read together, not as independent confirmation of the
  same target species.

## Situations → techniques

- `tpmOYXYQwhU` (SearcherSportfishing, 2020-04-22): the targeting verdict in
  the same answer — "the success rate if you went out and targeted opah would
  be not too productive… it's an incidental catch, they're great great fish to
  catch and they really taste good too." The manifest `upload_date` is
  2020-04-22; the video's own title dates the broadcast 2020-04-21.
- `pCd6QykcZ0w` (SearcherSportfishing, 2023-03-30, Steve Carson aboard the

  bluefin tuna, they might be yellowfin tuna, there might be yellowtail, other
  species, knock on wood maybe even an opah… Google opah, look that one up and
  I guarantee you'll want to catch one, not to mention have one on the table."
  A one-line aside in a tuna-lure tutorial, not an opah account; no depth,
  presentation detail, or catch report attaches to it.
