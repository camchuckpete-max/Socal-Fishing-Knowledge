# Evidence — Pacific Bonito

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing [Pacific bonito](../bonito.md).
One line per observation, grouped under the parent note's section headings, in
its order. Observations never change the parent's stated doctrine; a
contradicting one is also traced beside that doctrine in the parent.

## Where & when

### SoCal coast and islands

- `S2L3KLSQ6Is` (BDOutdoors, 2022-05-26, Catalina Island): weekly Bight report
  — a few yellowtail plus bonito, bass and a few barracuda for anglers fishing
  surface presentations; the earliest-dated SoCal bonito mention in this note's
  corpus.
- `yuXr3IJ8ybg` (StokedOnFishing, 2017-06-09, Pyramid Head, San Clemente
  Island; sponsor-heavy Okuma gear-demo episode, unregistered channel): on a
  live-squid trip a bonita school boiled up around the boat and was called on
  camera as "a good sign," and yellowtail joined the mixed bite shortly after
  — "yellows will roll in right after that."
- `ecJPMTCi-gw` (StokedOnFishing, 2019-06-19, San Clemente Island, 6-pack
  charter; unregistered channel): a flylined live bait was eaten by a bonito
  that left only the head, and a calico bass then came back for the scraps.
- `poqjnb1r1zk` (Your Saltwater Guide, 2019-07-02, Dana Point): a 5:45 am skiff
  start described as "a pretty slow morning" fishing south of Dana Point
  Harbor, then working north of the harbor, with small feathers rigged as a
  short daisy chain trolled at roughly **5.5 kt** and a small sardine-profile
  hard bait (Rapala-class diver) cast and twitched behind the boat. The
  presenter is not named on camera; the channel's registered voice is
  `dave-hansen`, a SoCal sportboat/charter captain, per
  `sources/source-registry.md`, so attribution is likely but unconfirmed for
  this clip.
- `Blh2BA-7Ono` (BDOutdoors, 2022-07-21, San Clemente Island front side, per a
  Thunderbird captain's report): the front-side yellowtail bite is "a morning
  thing" that "kind of slows down, becomes a pick-o day, bass, barracuda stuff
  like that in the mix of bonita"; the same report notes sport boats getting
  yellowtail, bass, bonito and barracuda together.
- `CMQkHQMxbXM` (BDOutdoors, 2022-08-04 report, trip launched out of Newport
  and clearing the harbour about 08:00): a bait-making stop trying for mackerel
  ahead of a marlin/swordfish look produced "sardines, spanish mackerel and
  small bonita" instead — "bait was hard to catch." The same report names
  yellowtail, calico bass and bonita together at the north Channel Islands.
- `-bw1KDfDjv4` (StokedOnFishing, "Simrad Summer, part 1" — a summer trip,
  month not stated on camera; 2015-09-27 upload, the 14 Mile Bank and the
  surrounding 209/267 high spots off San Diego; unregistered channel): surface
  temperature at the 14 Mile Bank read **66–67 °F**, and the day produced "a
  nice jig stop with some Bonita up to about 8 lb" while bluefin were seen but
  would not cooperate; bonito and yellowtail were called mixed in together.
- `OYOda6T3f-8` (BDOutdoors, 2022-10-20): the Island Spirit fishing the coast
  out of Ventura had limits of bass plus "some Bonita and Rockfish"; at an
  island the captions garble — asr-uncertain(place name: "Kathleen") — the
  report has "a lot of Bonita biting, some bass, some yellowtail" with sport
  boats "doing good on the Bonita," and Von Podmore of Salty Fly Guide Service
  "catching Calico, Bonita, even Whitefish on flies."
- `5p6gu14ZC4w` (BDOutdoors, 2022-10-26, inside Catalina and Farnsworth Bank):
  yellowtail are catchable at Catalina on good water temperatures but "you're
  mostly against Bonita, bass and Rockfish this time of year," and it is "not
  really a prime time of year" for a yellowtail there.
- `H-vIGWPIPVc` (StokedOnFishing, presenter Shay, 2019-10-30, San Clemente
  Island; unregistered channel): running inside, the crew spotted "a big big
  big porpoise school," ran up and trolled around it with a deep diver (a Nomad
  sardine-colour plug named on camera) and a cedar plug; the deep diver drew
  the bonito. Single observation — no source generalises porpoise schools as a
  bonito sign.
- `oXunQKSbc2g` (StokedOnFishing, 2019-12-11 upload, October 2019 trip, Cortez
  Bank aboard the El Dorado, a limited-load 2-day charter out of Long Beach;
  unregistered channel): large ("giant") bonito were worked heavily as bycatch
  alongside a [yellowtail](../yellowtail.md#where--when) yo-yo iron and
  surface-iron bite, with on-camera commentary that the bonito were "big and
  mean" and fought hard "pound for pound" against the yellowtail — an
  offshore-bank data point rather than this note's usual nearshore-kelp-edge
  framing.
- `MUpvP-Yl2R0` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-11-23, Catalina Island, roughly a 30 mi
  run with live squid — "the candy" — aboard; unregistered channel): bonito and
  yellowtail were both called around the zone, bonito were seen caught on a
  neighbouring boat, and a bonito came to the rail at anchor.
- `Z3rZqy2Pi8E` (BDOutdoors, 2022-11-24 report of a Sunday 2022-11-20 trip, San
  Clemente Island east end back side): after patterning bass on off-colour
  water with current running in toward the island, "a bunch of Bonita come up
  outside of the kelp so we pulled up out there, they're boiling around,
  jumping"; the fish came on **DD100s and small chrome jigs** on bass gear, at
  **two to three pounds** with the best "maybe six or seven pounds." The same
  report has San Diego boats getting bonito while fishing rockfish, and calls
  bonito part of the same mix at the other islands.
- `nsUdT-zXI8s` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-12-07, San Clemente Island front side,
  free overnight charter for 15 veterans; manual captions, unregistered
  channel): "a lot of Bonita" through the day alongside yellowtail and calico
  bass, one fish called out as "acting like a Bonita at first" before it turned
  out otherwise — a December presence data point.
- `UfuiWFVvz2E` (StokedOnFishing, June trip, year not stated on camera;
  2026-08-11 upload, Cortez Bank aboard Constitution
  Sportfishing; unregistered channel): bonito were called at the bow, one came
  on a pinned live bait, and when a school came up the deck call was "boiling
  up out there, so throw a surface iron in there"; the crew snipped gills at
  the rail "so that all the meat inside of them comes out properly." The
  retrieve instruction that followed the surface-iron call is garbled in the
  captions and is not carried — asr-uncertain(retrieve cadence).

### Baja Pacific

- `qri15R3caYE` (StokedOnFishing, 2018-02-01 upload, La Bocana, Baja California
  Sur, Pacific side; "Stoked On La Bocana," part 1; unregistered channel):
  bonito ("Bonita," some described as big) was named on camera as part of the
  trip's mixed bag alongside yellowtail, sand bass, barracuda and calico bass
  (see the [calico bass](../calico-bass.md) La Bocana entry) — no technique,
  weight or location detail beyond the catch being reported.
- `9tIp4n7q850` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-05-17, Cedros Island; unregistered
  channel): yellowtail "have been mixed in with the Bonita apparently, so we're
  trolling Rapala right now"; on a hooked fish the crew read the drag —
  "typically the Bonita they don't really take a lot of drag" — and twice
  called fish as "no Bonita" from the fight before landing yellowtail, one on
  the yo-yo.
- `YUdbrIm9vrE` (StokedOnFishing, October trip, year not stated on camera,
  2024-03-20 upload, offshore of Cedros Island, Baja; "Cedros Island Goes Off";
  Cedros Outdoor Adventures charter; unregistered channel): a bonita caught
  incidentally while trolling for yellowfin tuna roughly **20–30 mi** offshore
  prompted crew comment that bonito "can bite through the light line" with
  their teeth — traced beside the parent's no-wire gear doctrine, not treated
  as overturning it.
- `0dIwWiOc1NY` (StokedOnFishing, 2026-02-17 upload, Ensenada, Baja California,
  Pacific side; unregistered channel): at least three bonito were caught
  incidentally — a small one on the mackerel-slow-troll/cast-jig yellowtail
  stop (see the
  [trolling](../../techniques/trolling.md#ensenada-baja--slow-trolled-mackerel-plus-cast-jigs-for-yellowtail-on-a-reefpoint)
  Ensenada entry; the crew called it good ceviche material, though the ceviche
  eaten later that day was made from rockfish, not this fish), and two more
  ("that is a big bonito," then "another really nice bonita") on slow-pitch
  jigs at the deeper rockfish/lingcod stop (see the
  [rockfish & lingcod](../rockfish-lingcod.md) Ensenada entry). The crew again
  pushed back on treating bonito as a "trash fish," repeating the
  bleed-and-ice-immediately handling as the difference between poor and good
  table quality — a second, independent data point for that doctrine, this time
  from Baja rather than SoCal.

## Presence & forage

- `yGXSrUauo2w` (SearcherSportfishing, Steve Carson, 2024-04-25, San Diego
  docks, sliding-sinker tackle tip): the rig "helps you get the bait out away
  from the boat if there's Bonita or something like that near the surface — it
  gets your bait a little bit below the Bonita and in front of the Yellow Tail
  quicker."
- `YAKOv9bXKO0` (BDOutdoors, 2026-02-05, long-range report, yo-yo segment): the
  problem with chrome lures anywhere bonito or barracuda are present is that
  "they're a little faster, they're a little quicker than the yellowtail, so
  it's not that the yellowtail won't bite these, it's that the barracuda and
  the bonito may get them first"; the same segment separately covers a Colonet
  red-crab bloom.
- `RfiC8sfIWTk` (Dirty Hookers, 2023-05-04, San Diego offshore, 211 lb bluefin
  tackle breakdown): the day-time picture is "a lot of kelp Patty fish and Open
  Water schools of Bonito and yellowtail," fished on two live-bait setups —
  circle hooks for bluefin, and for a school of yellowtail or bonito a **2/0
  live-bait J hook** ("2 Watt"/"two out" in the captions —
  asr-uncertain(hook size notation)), with bonito taking "J hook or circle
  hook"; fly-line setups on **25 to 30 lb** test.

## Situations → techniques

- `PrdPJy26H8c` (Dirty Hookers, 2019-03-05, SoCal): a custom stick bait modelled
  on the Shimano Colt Sniper caught "about seven or eight Bonito on it in one
  day"; the same tackle-bag walkthrough calls one box "my tuna, skipjack,
  Bonito box" and states the downsize rule — "when I go tuna fishing I pretty
  much downsize everything unless we're going bluefin fishing… skipjack and
  schoolie yellowfin I just treat them as Bonito and I downsize everything" —
  with **80 g** flat-falls named for bonito, yellowfin and skipjack. The same
  segment notes a beat-up blue-and-white Tady 45 whose wear is all from
  yellowtail, never a bonito, and separately that "the only time I'll throw
  chrome is either for barracuda or tuna."

## Landing & handling

- `H-vIGWPIPVc` (StokedOnFishing, presenter Shay, 2019-10-30, San Clemente
  Island; unregistered channel): the on-camera method is to cut or pull the
  gill, which "opens the bloodline and it's gonna bleed out, also kills the
  fish faster," then straight on ice; when filleting, the bloodline "has to be
  taken out" because with it left in "it's gonna taste really really fishy." A
  side-by-side sashimi test against yellowfin from the same trip found the two
  "really hard to differentiate" — one sample firmer and leaner, the other
  softer and fattier — with "nobody would know this is Bonita, they would think
  it was tuna." The presenter had previously treated bonito as a fun-to-catch
  "trash fish" and credited the bleed-and-ice handling for the difference.
