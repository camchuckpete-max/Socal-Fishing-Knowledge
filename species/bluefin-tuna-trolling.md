# Bluefin Tuna — trolling

Which towed presentation to pull for SoCal bluefin, and when. The choice runs on
wind, fish grade, forage size, and whether the day calls for covering water or
sitting over a school — not on which bank you are over. It scales from schoolies
to cows by lure size, and it is the answer whenever the wind is too light to fly
a kite. The router is [bluefin tuna](bluefin-tuna.md); universal towing
mechanics — spread geometry, setback, holder elevation — live in
[trolling](../techniques/trolling.md).

## When this is the call

Reach for a towed presentation when there are fish in a zone but no castable
surface sign, when a 50–60 sq mi search box has to be covered efficiently, or
when the wind is too light to fly a kite and a big-fish presentation still has
to be in the water.

**The kite is the competing call, and the split is conditions, not preference.**
The most effective way to catch **100–300 lb** SoCal bluefin is a flying fish
under a kite (`YsiHziY_PWw`), so trolling is what you do when the kite is not
available to you: too little wind to fly it, or fish scattered far enough that
sitting and kiting over them will not hold you on fish (cameron). Speed-trolling
a Mad Mac — or a Rapala X-Rap Magnum Extreme — in the **13–15 kt** band is the
tool for "locating fish and finding bites when you don't have the conditions to
put that kite up" (`z1CmWHqe9uk`). Kite execution lives in
[kite fishing](../techniques/kite-fishing.md); this note owns what you tow.

Once the choice to tow is made, the presentation follows the day:

| Wind / sea | Fish grade & water | Pull this | Gear class | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Too light to kite (under ~8 kt), or fish too spread out to sit and kite over [a] | Any grade, locating | [Speed-troll](../techniques/speed-trolling.md) a full-size [Mad Mac](../lures/mad-mac.md) 200 — large presentation | Heavy troll class (high tow load) | Locates fish and pulls bites on days the kite won't fly (`z1CmWHqe9uk`) [b] |
| Light-moderate, calm-ish | School grade, mixed | [Spreader bar](../lures/spreader-bar.md) — small presentation, out of the elevated shotgun | 80–100 lb topshot, drag well under the internal line | The bar rides out of the water, so it wants the highest holder; running specs in the [spreader bar](../lures/spreader-bar.md) note (cameron) |
| Calm, school grade, want a swimmer | Schoolies–mid, clean-ish | [DTX Minnow](../lures/dtx-minnow.md) 165 / [Husky Magnum](../lures/rapala-husky-magnum.md) diver | 60–100 lb — swimmer action dies on heavy line | Divers want the low gunnel holders; speeds, depths and setbacks live in the [DTX](../lures/dtx-minnow.md) and [Husky](../lures/rapala-husky-magnum.md) notes (cameron) |
| Calm, mixed or picky | Mixed grade over bait | [Cedar plug](../lures/cedar-plug.md) / [Halco](../lures/halco-laser-pro.md) LP190 XDD deep diver | 40–60 lb | The Halco is the lightest lure in the box; cedar speed and the wash-or-way-back positioning rule live in the [cedar plug](../lures/cedar-plug.md) and [Halco](../lures/halco-laser-pro.md) notes (cameron) |
| 8–15 kt, fishable swell, big fish present | 100–300 lb, any water | Not this note — [kite](../techniques/kite-fishing.md) a flying fish | 150–200 lb class + [flying-fish harness](../rigging/flying-fish-harness.md), 300–400 lb fluoro | The highest-percentage method for that grade (`YsiHziY_PWw`); lay the leader flat on the surface to gaff |

[a] The trigger is a coverage need, not sea state: too little wind to fly the
kite, or fish scattered far enough that sitting and kiting over them will not
hold you on fish (cameron).

[b] Two speed bands for two jobs — **13–15 kt** speed-trolling to locate
(`z1CmWHqe9uk`), **9–12 kt, to 14 kt** for a normal spread (cameron) —
reconciled in [Mad Mac](../lures/mad-mac.md). The low-profile crimped
connection and the J-to-treble swap live in
[speed trolling](../techniques/speed-trolling.md) and the lure note.

**Presentation size against forage.** The spreader bar is the small
presentation and the Mad Mac the large; match what the fish are eating, and let
forage size override the sea-state row when the two disagree (cameron). One
SoCal crew reads the same pair as a grade selector instead — over schools
running **40 to 100 plus** lb they swapped Mad Macs for spreader bars
specifically "to target the bigger ones," with no forage size stated to explain
the reversal (`xzIaUEDklrE`).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): spreader-bar-as-big-fish-selector runs
opposite to the small-presentation axis.

## How bluefin changes the execution

**The troll is a search pattern, not just a presentation.** Because bluefin are
located by sign before they are fished, the read while towing is the same visual
and radar sign the router uses — working terns, foamers, breezers, bird-radar
red blotches at 4–5 mi. Troll **sweeping arcs around the bird and bait area
rather than straight-lining away**, because tuna move at low single-digit mph
and the school is not fleeing, and stay out of the fleet's prop wash
(`HzE4FHHcvTk`). Once the sounder marks good numbers of fish, stop trolling and
set a dead flying fish under the kite to fish the drift over the marks
(`vCskOx6N-XM`): trolling is the locate step, the kite the convert step. Full
method: [search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md),
[electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).

**Two ~8 kt wind rules point opposite ways, and which applies depends on
whether a kite spread is already in the water.** Inside a kite program, ~8 kt
splits trolled from dead-boat presentation: at **8 kt** of wind or more, troll a
rubber flying-fish lure ("Yummy Flyer") under the kite at about **8.5 kt** to
cover water and locate schools, because a trolled bait converts a bite to a
hookup at a better ratio than sitting dead-boat; under ~8 kt, with fish already
spotted or marked heavy on the sounder, go dead-boat with a dead flyer under the
kite or a balloon, dapped on the surface, and hook up by driving the boat gently
forward at **4–5 kt** into the strike (`2y0VznL2qk8`). The Mad Mac row runs the
same ~8 kt mark the other way — it is the floor below which the kite will not
fly at all, and the play becomes a full-size Mad Mac at 13–15 kt
(`z1CmWHqe9uk`), a different lure at a different speed. Mechanics:
[kite fishing](../techniques/kite-fishing.md#trolled-rubber-flyer-vs-dead-boat-dead-flyer-a-wind-driven-choice).

**Boat type gates the speed band.** A SoCal sportboat at full-speed troll tops
out around **9 kt** — "if everybody on the boat is running back and forth... as
fast as they can, we'll get all the way up to nine knots" — so the 13–15 kt band
is a private-boat technique a sportboat structurally cannot match; at **14 kt**
the fuel cost runs roughly a quarter gallon per mile, the worst burn rate on a
private boat (`bM2vUS1B-yQ`). That is a coverage-against-cost tradeoff, not a
claim against the technique: the same source credits speed trolling with
producing bites driving straight over a sounded-out foamer.
⚠ Fact-check (single-source): the 9 kt sportboat ceiling and the
quarter-gallon-per-mile figure at 14 kt.

**A volume program skips the flat-fall entirely.** One SoCal kite-and-flyline
program does not fish the flat-fall/dropped-bait style of bluefin fishing at
all: a hooked fish on that method can tie the boat up for **three, four, or five
hours**, while the kite/flyline program lands **six fish in the time a flat-fall
boat lands one** (`JiJYamo0QHk`). That is a deliberate trade of individual
big-fish fights for volume, not a claim the method fails; flat-fall depth and
mechanism live in
[knife jigging](../techniques/knife-jigging.md#reach-for-this-when).
⚠ Fact-check (single-source): the six-to-one landing-rate comparison.

## Rigs & gear

- **Speed-troll rod:** heavy, high-drag-load **troll class** for full-size Mad
  Macs at speed. Every ~3 ft of rod-tip elevation costs ~1 ft of running depth,
  so divers ride the low gunnel holders and bars and surface skirts ride the
  elevated shotgun (cameron).
- **Kite rod:** dedicated **150–200 lb class**, ~100 lb braid for capacity and
  less wind-blow, never cast (cameron).
- **Terminal:** low-profile **200 lb bite leader class**, crimped, no bulky
  swivel on the speed-troll connection; keep it low-profile so it does not kill
  the swim (`z1CmWHqe9uk`). See [bite leaders](../rigging/bite-leaders.md).
- **Lures by role:** [Mad Mac](../lures/mad-mac.md) (speed-troll),
  [spreader bar](../lures/spreader-bar.md) (school grade),
  [DTX Minnow](../lures/dtx-minnow.md) / [Husky Magnum](../lures/rapala-husky-magnum.md)
  / [Halco](../lures/halco-laser-pro.md) (divers and swimmers),
  [cedar plug](../lures/cedar-plug.md) (mixed). Class lexicon:
  [gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md).

**Spread rules (cameron).** No outriggers, normally **2–3 lures**, with the
furthest-back lure — the shotgun — run out of the elevated holder behind the
seat. Any top-shot strength can be run, so the strength is picked per lure at
plan time, and the rod↔lure pairing weighs four factors: **(1)** tow/drag load
needing a heavy rod (full-size Mad Macs at speed), **(2)** swimmers whose action
dies on heavy line, **(3)** lures needing a softer rod to absorb the strike,
**(4)** line-agnostic lures where the rod just matches the target. Spreader bars
are run in place of conventional bars because there isn't room on the panga for
conventional bars; 2026 is the first season on them, with Half Fish supplying
the bar guidance, so bar-specific tuning is experimental rather than settled
doctrine. The Half Fish internal-line spec and the drag rule live in
[spreader bar](../lures/spreader-bar.md).
⚠ misplaced-content: the panga-space reason for choosing spreader bars over
conventional bars, and the first-season status, are angler/boat constraints
belonging in `profiles/cameron/trolling-lures.md`.

## Differs from the general method

Against [trolling](../techniques/trolling.md), which owns spread geometry,
setback and holder elevation for any species:

- **Speed runs far higher.** The bluefin locate band is **13–15 kt**
  (`z1CmWHqe9uk`) against a normal spread's 9–12 kt — fast enough that it is a
  private-boat technique a sportboat cannot reach (`bM2vUS1B-yQ`).
- **The troll is a search leg, not the fishing.** The pattern is sweeping arcs
  around bird and bait sign rather than straight lines, and the intended
  outcome is to *stop* trolling and convert on the drift (`HzE4FHHcvTk`,
  `vCskOx6N-XM`). General trolling has no such hand-off built into it.
- **Wind picks the presentation, because a kite is the alternative.** No other
  SoCal species has a towed-versus-flown decision hanging on a single ~8 kt
  threshold read two different ways (`2y0VznL2qk8`, `z1CmWHqe9uk`).
- **Presentation size is matched to forage, not to fish grade** (cameron) — the
  opposite of the bigger-lure-bigger-fish default, and itself contested
  (`xzIaUEDklrE`).
