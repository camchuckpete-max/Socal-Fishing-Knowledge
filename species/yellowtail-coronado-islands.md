# Targeting yellowtail — Coronado Islands

The Coronados are the springtime yellowtail trip on San Diego's doorstep: an
island chain roughly 13 mi out, off the coast of Mexico, whose west side is
exposed to colder Pacific currents and tidal swings and stays loaded with
bait (`D5DR7Kx42_A`). The day is a troll-search — divers as the fish-finder,
figure-8s around the islands — that converts to iron and bait the moment you
find life. The zone runs seeing-not-biting cycles with one short bite window
a day, so the day is planned around tide and current, not the clock
(`YZtX1MiT0y8`). Species doctrine, sonar reads, and the full situations table
live in the [yellowtail router](yellowtail.md).

## Mexican waters — hard gate

The islands sit in Mexican waters (`D5DR7Kx42_A`): Mexican jurisdiction
(CONAPESCA/INM), not CDFW. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what a boat run

CONAPESCA/INM rules before crossing the border.

## The program

1. **Set the troll before you reach the islands.** Pink and purple
   [Halco 190-class divers](../lures/halco-laser-pro.md) are the zone's
   fish-finder:
   [troll](../techniques/trolling.md#socal-local-coast--trolling-hard-baits-to-prospect-a-zone-for-yellowtail)
   the yellowtail zones while you search for signs of life — working birds
   ([bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md)),
   [boils and breezing fish](yellowtail.md#finding-them-sign--sonar), bait
   showing on the surface. Trolling here is prospecting that happens to hook
   fish, not the presentation (`HTowqnwAMeA`).
2. **Run figure-8 patterns around the islands, and troll the Middle
   Grounds** between them — the circuit covers the island edges and the
   mid-chain structure in one pass.
3. **Convert on sign or a troll strike.** Cast a
   [surface iron](../techniques/surface-iron.md) at any surface activity
   (`D5DR7Kx42_A`). Fish [metered](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md)
   but not showing get the chum-then-cast sequence: meter the school, throw
   live chum, let them boil, cast into the boil (`4pT0efBD1j0`,
   `dS9gd9k7A7Q`). The long cast is load-bearing — the fish will not let the
   boat drive over the top of them (`dS9gd9k7A7Q`).
4. **Hold a stop while anyone is hooked up.** A hooked fish keeps the school
   near the boat; get a jig back in the water while someone is still bent
   (`gdqI2dNd5_U`).
5. **Tight balls hugging structure get the
   [yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md)**: hit bottom, and the bite
   comes a couple cranks off it — a reaction bite, so burn it
   (`D5DR7Kx42_A`).
6. **With live bait aboard, switch to
   [slow-trolled bait](../techniques/slow-trolling-bait.md)** over the same
   zones; put a [flylined bait](../techniques/flyline.md) out when current
   and tide turn the fish onto bait, and a
   [dropper loop](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) down to bottom marks
   (`D5DR7Kx42_A`).

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: the anchor-vs-drift commit call at the
Coronados — when to drop the hook on a mark versus keep working the circuit.

## Reading the day

- **Seeing-not-biting cycles are the zone's normal state, not the fish
  leaving.** Boats fishing the islands daily see more fish than they catch,
  and each day carries "a little weather window or a little time with bite
  window" when the fish actually eat (`YZtX1MiT0y8`). Inside a cycle there
  is "no shortage of fish" (`YZtX1MiT0y8`) — presence stays high while
  catchability compresses into the window.
- **The window follows tide and current, not the clock.** Interest in live
  bait switches on with current and tide (`D5DR7Kx42_A`) — plan to be on
  fish through the [tide turns](../conditions/tide-and-slack.md) and
  [current changes](../conditions/current-structure.md) rather than grinding
  one spot between windows. ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: mechanism —
  what flips Coronados fish from seeing to eating, and any zone-specific
  gray-light read.
- **Good water here is bait volume.** The west side's exposure to colder
  Pacific currents and tidal swings is what keeps it generally filled with
  bait — the driver behind the chain's biodiversity and its yellowtail
  (`D5DR7Kx42_A`).
- **Wind re-ranks the presentation; it doesn't end the day.** Gusts to 25 kt
  push the fish off the top and the fleet's bite goes mostly yo-yo while the
  surface iron still picks a fish (`ejUTAnONf7o`). Fish swiping the jig
  without committing on a wind day is the
  [wind](../conditions/sea-state.md) turning the bite, not the wrong jig
  (`ejUTAnONf7o`).
- **Big schools forgive the retrieve.** A big Coronado school often eats a
  faster, less exacting retrieve than the finicky small schools on the
  mainland coast (`VpW91AKOFVQ`) — get the jig swimming and keep it moving
  before micro-tuning the cadence.

## Differs from nearby zones

La Jolla and the Coronados both put San Diego boats on yellowtail; the trips
are different animals:

- **Border and paperwork.** La Jolla is US waters under CDFW rules; the
  Coronados are Mexican waters with the permit gate above (`D5DR7Kx42_A`).
- **Drought shape — presence ≠ catchability.** Some yellowtail are in La
  Jolla year-round, but half-day boats can go months without one.
  Coronado droughts run in hours, not months: inside a seeing-not-biting
  cycle the fish still show daily and eat in one short window
  (`YZtX1MiT0y8`).
- **School size sets retrieve tolerance.** Big Coronado schools eat a
  faster, less exacting retrieve; the smaller mainland-coast schools from
  Del Mar to Imperial Beach — the stretch that includes La Jolla — run
  markedly more finicky, especially on bigger-grade fish (`VpW91AKOFVQ`).
- **Program shape.** The Coronados day is the troll-search circuit above.
