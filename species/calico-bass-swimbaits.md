# Calico Bass — boiler-bite swimbait

**One species, one technique.** The boiler-bite program is a calico swimbait
day fished on the **open side** of the water — less kelp, fish showing on top —
where the bait is a **Lucky Craft 119 minnow-profile hard swimbait behind a
5–9 in soft-plastic trailer** and the whole method is **selective**: you cast
at a fish that has already committed rather than working a piece of water to
convince a neutral one (`lP6cg4eEU6s`). Reach for it when calico are boiling on
bait in open water and you can pick individual fish out. The general weedless
kelp rigging, the fast-wind retrieve and the set-on-the-pause hookset are in
[swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md); this page is what changes because the
fish is a calico busting bait in the open.

## When this is the call

**Open water, less kelp, fish showing on top.** The situation is calico
surfacing and busting on bait rather than holding tight to a stringer — the row
the router ranks for it is in
[calico bass — situations → techniques](calico-bass.md#situations--techniques).
The kelp-edge program in [swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md) is the answer
when the fish are on structure instead.

**The water-clarity edge is the target, not the structure.** Look for **less
kelp, more open water** and **crystal-clear water**, over the more typical
stained/seafoam-green water nearby — the clarity line is where to commit the
cast, since the bait "gets lost" and fish spook in the cloudier water beside it
(`lP6cg4eEU6s`). Read it with
[water color](../conditions/water-color.md); the general surface-sign
vocabulary for spotting boiling fish is in
[search and glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).

**July and August are when the size-up applies.** Warmer water raises the
fish's metabolism, so they eat more, eat bigger, and eat more aggressively —
size baits up and fish faster during that stretch (`lP6cg4eEU6s`). See
[June–July](../seasonal/june-july.md) and [August](../seasonal/august.md); the
rest of the seasonal and light-angle read stays on
[calico bass — feeding triggers](calico-bass.md#feeding-triggers).

## How calico bass changes the execution

**Target selection over persuasion — the fish, not the water, is the unit.**
Cast to a visibly committed, aggressive fish and swim the lure past it, rather
than working a piece of water trying to convince a neutral one to bite
(`lP6cg4eEU6s`). That inverts the general note's grade-a-bed-by-cast-count
method: here the decision is made per fish, on sight, before the cast.

**Profile order, worked as a sequence.** Start on a **baitfish/anchovy**
profile, step to a **crab/crustacean-style** profile, then to a **shiny/flash**
change-up (`lP6cg4eEU6s`).

**Fish faster as the water warms.** The July–August instruction is explicitly
not to be scared to fish fast, alongside the bigger bait (`lP6cg4eEU6s`) — a
speed decision tied to water temperature rather than to the current or the
kelp's posture.

**Scent is part of the program.** A garlic-scented spray on the soft plastic is
run as standard here; the mechanism is unstated by the source and the doctrine,
the named brands and the application method stay in
[swimbaits — scent](../techniques/swimbaits.md#scent) (`lP6cg4eEU6s`).

## Rigs & gear

- **Bait: a Lucky Craft 119** — a minnow-profile hard swimbait; the model
  number is not independently confirmed beyond the on-camera name — paired with
  a **5–9 in soft-plastic trailer** (`lP6cg4eEU6s`). The soft-plastic canon,
  including this source's profile and novelty-colour reasoning, is in
  [soft-plastic swimbaits](../lures/soft-plastic-swimbaits.md).
- **Class:** the [bass casting / swimbait class](../tackle/gear-classes.md) —
  the same inshore build the rest of the calico artificials program runs on.
- **Leader is light, and action is the stated reason.** The same source's
  inshore-bass swimbait/crank spec is **20–25 lb fluorocarbon** on **40 lb
  braid**, chosen because heavier leader (30 lb) stiffens enough to deaden a
  swimbait's action; it is recorded in
  [line & leader](../tackle/line-and-leader.md) (`lP6cg4eEU6s`). It is stated
  for inshore bass swimbait and crank fishing generally rather than scoped to
  the boiler program, and it is far lighter than the 40–65 lb kelp/structure
  classes in
  [swimbaits — weedless rigging](../techniques/swimbaits.md#weedless-rigging).

specific boiler program; the retrieve cadence for the hard bait; how the

20–25 lb leader or steps up away from the kelp.

## Differs from the general method

Against [swimbaits](../techniques/swimbaits.md), which owns the weedless kelp
rigging, the fast-wind retrieve, the set-on-the-pause hookset and the
cover-water boat program:

- **The bait is a hard swimbait with a soft trailer, not a plastic on a hook.**
  The general note's whole rigging section is a soft slug or paddle-tail on a
  keel-weighted or lead head; this is a Lucky Craft 119 hard bait running ahead
  of a 5–9 in soft-plastic trailer (`lP6cg4eEU6s`).
- **You pick a fish, not a bed.** The general note's method is a numbers game
  across structure — 20–25 casts to a bed that is biting, 10–12 if it is not,
  then move 100 ft or 100 yards
  ([boat work](../techniques/swimbaits.md#boat-work--covering-water)). Here the
  cast is aimed at one visibly committed fish and a neutral fish is left alone
  (`lP6cg4eEU6s`).
- **Clarity is read the opposite way round from the kelp-edge doctrine, and the
  situation is why.** On a kelp edge a green-stained, near-opaque colour with
  roughly 5–10 ft of visibility is the preferred read, because the stain gives
  fish cover to feed up in the open (`9xNhdu2aBqE`, see
  [calico bass — presence & forage](calico-bass.md#presence--forage)); on the
  boiler bite the crystal-clear side of the line is where to cast, because in
  the cloudier water the bait gets lost and the fish spook up close
  (`lP6cg4eEU6s`). These are not the same fish in the same posture — one is an
  ambusher sitting on structure, the other is already up and chasing bait in
  the open — so the read that applies is set by which of those you are looking
  at, not by which source is preferred.
- **A change-up ladder replaces the single confidence pattern.** The general
  note treats colour as mattering little — fish a green-top/gold-bottom you
  believe in
  ([boat work](../techniques/swimbaits.md#boat-work--covering-water)). Here the
  change-up is an explicit
  sequence run between casts: baitfish/anchovy, then crab/crustacean, then
  flash (`lP6cg4eEU6s`).
- **Speed is set by water temperature, not by the current angle.** The general
  note's retrieve speed varies with fishing with, against or across the current
  and is hunted by cadence
  ([the retrieve](../techniques/swimbaits.md#the-retrieve-and-the-hookset));
  the July–August rule here is a seasonal instruction — warmer water, bigger
  bait, faster retrieve (`lP6cg4eEU6s`).
- **Scent moves from optional to standard.** The general note files scent as a
  confidence tool that earns its keep on bottom-oriented creature baits and is
  *less* necessary on reaction baits; on this program it is run as part of the
  game plan on the soft plastic (`lP6cg4eEU6s`).
