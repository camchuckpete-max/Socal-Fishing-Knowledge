# Skipjack Tuna

Skipjack — "skippies" — are the fish that reaches the offering first on the
SoCal/Baja tuna grounds: they pile into a chum line laid for
[yellowfin](yellowfin-tuna.md) (`lxFNVdDhMy4`) and take the fastest thing in
the water, whether that is a burned cast, a trolled feather, or a live bait
sitting on the surface (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`). Every skipjack decision therefore turns
on what else is in the water: stop and catch a few for chunk and kite bait
(`Ow3an9lSVh4`, `HpPFogLwKOw`), or get a presentation past them to the better
tuna underneath (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`, `KajL5TsgBzU`). No corpus source targets them:
every skipjack record is an incident inside a trip built around bluefin,
yellowfin, or yellowtail.

## Where & when

**Where.** Skipjack are recorded across the whole SoCal/Baja range the corpus
covers, always in company with other tuna: on the
[Cortez Bank](../locations/cortes-bank.md) high spot mixed with yellowtail and
yellowfin (`i3qIAHW-SJc`), on the troll about 35 mi off
[Ensenada](../locations/ensenada.md) ahead of yellowfin and yellowtail
(`0bcDBGzQnGw`), on an offshore troll out of the
[East Cape](../locations/east-cape.md) (`tcso7Lpm_Xs`), across a current break
off [Cabo San Lucas](../locations/cabo-san-lucas.md) (`dgauGbNxP84`), and in
[Bahía de los Ángeles](../locations/bahia-de-los-angeles.md) (`odiIWmut6gM`) and
[Loreto](../locations/loreto.md) (`n4nMCLLW3tY`) in the Sea of Cortez. The
common factor is warm water holding tuna, not a structure type — they show over
the offshore banks, over [kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md), and on
the open troll ([zone lexicon](../locations/zone-lexicon.md)).

**When.** The SoCal records sit in the warm-stable-into-fall part of the year —
August through November — as the water goes from the peak
[warm-stable regime into fall fragmentation](../conditions/water-regimes.md)
that also brings the yellowfin they run with
([August](../seasonal/august.md), [September](../seasonal/september.md),
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md)). Baja and Cortez
records are not confined to that window: a Cabo current-break troll
(`dgauGbNxP84`) and an East Cape offshore troll (`tcso7Lpm_Xs`) both sit outside
it.

⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a stated season window, arrival or departure
date, or SST band for skipjack anywhere in the range. The months above are the
dates on the trips that caught them, not a season any source states.

**Presence vs catchability.** Where skipjack are present they are catchable —
the recurring complaint is the opposite one, that you cannot keep a live bait
away from them (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`, `KajL5TsgBzU`). Plan the day around
[bluefin](bluefin-tuna.md), [yellowfin](yellowfin-tuna.md), or
[yellowtail](yellowtail.md) and treat the skipjack as a bait supply or an
obstacle, per the router table below.

## Presence & forage

- **They hold with the tuna, and give way to them.** On a kelp paddy a school
  that looks like nothing but skipjack can turn over when tuna arrive and "push
  the skippies out" (`Ow3an9lSVh4`) — so a paddy reading as all-skipjack is not
  a reason to leave it.
  ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: mechanism — no source says whether the
  displacement is the tuna crowding them off the bait or the skipjack simply
  moving on.
- **Current breaks hold them.** Trolling back and forth across a
  [current break](../conditions/current-diagnostics.md#current-break-visual-id)
  rather than leaving it to look elsewhere produced skipjack off Cabo San Lucas
  (`dgauGbNxP84`); scoped to that troll, not established for the SoCal grounds.
- **They mix through the yellowfin school rather than schooling apart** — the
  same marks, the same chum line, the same 40–50 ft band (`lxFNVdDhMy4`).
- ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: what skipjack eat naturally. Every corpus
  record is of them eating hook bait or chum — sardines, chunked bait, jigs and
  trolled feathers — and none names a natural forage species, a bait size, or a
  temperature or bait concentration that gathers them.

## Feeding triggers

- **Chum is the switch.** About two minutes of chumming a school marked 40–50 ft
  down brings it up and starts the bite (`lxFNVdDhMy4`). Mechanism as stated: the
  chum draws the school toward the surface and into casting range; method in
  [chunking](../techniques/chunking.md).
- **Speed beats presentation.** With a chum line going, skipjack take the burned
  cast before a settled bait (`lxFNVdDhMy4`), and a live bait fished on the
  surface among them is eaten "every time" (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`). That intercept is
  what makes them the obstacle in the router row below.
- ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any light window (gray light, sunrise),
  tide, slack, moon phase, or temperature swing tied to the skipjack bite. The
  corpus records only chum and boat speed as triggers.

## Finding them (sign & sonar)

- **On the meter:** skipjack mark in the mid column mixed with yellowfin at
  about **40–50 ft** down and boil up hard to chum (`lxFNVdDhMy4`). They are not
  separable from small yellowfin on the screen — the sort happens when they eat,
  and a burning surface retrieve sorts them out fastest (`lxFNVdDhMy4`). General
  method: [electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).
- **On the surface:** the same working birds, foamers, and chum-line boils that
  mark yellowfin, and often a skipjack layer sitting on top of the yellowfin
  rather than beside them (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`) —
  [bird reading](../conditions/bird-reading.md),
  [search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md).

## Gear summary (class terms)

- **Bait and cast:** **20–30 lb flyline class**, with a casting jig or small
  lead bait for the burning retrieve. Owner Mutu circle hooks in **size 4** on
  the lighter line and smaller grade, **size 2 up to 1/0–2/0** on heavier line
  and bigger fish, are used across yellowfin, bluefin and skipjack on the same
  trips (`jQW2HLkMsmY`).
- **Jigs:** an **80 g flat-fall** is the skipjack/bonito/yellowfin size in a box
  that runs 80–500 g, alongside downsized stickbaits (`PrdPJy26H8c`); a
  stickbait burned across a chummed school takes them (`lxFNVdDhMy4`). See
  [flat-fall jigging](../techniques/flat-fall-jigging.md).
- **Troll:** light **troll class** with small
  [tuna feathers & skirts](../lures/tuna-feathers-and-skirts.md) and
  [cedar plugs](../lures/cedar-plug.md) at ~6–8 kt — inside the working bands
  both lure notes state.
- **Downsizing logic:** on a mixed tuna trip, treat skipjack and schoolie-grade
  [yellowfin](yellowfin-tuna.md) as **bonito-sized targets and downsize tackle
  and lures accordingly**; bluefin are the exception, because "you never know
  what size bluefin you're going to run into" (`PrdPJy26H8c`). See
  [bluefin tuna](bluefin-tuna.md) and [Pacific bonito](bonito.md).
- Class lexicon: [gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md).

## Doctrine & conflicts

Section order below is not a ranking.

**The decision turns on whether a better fish is under them or behind them, and
on whether your bait supply is short.** One sign — skipjack all over the
surface — carries three different correct responses:

- **Stop and stock bait.** Do not run past a skipjack school; catch a few and
  keep them for chunk bait rather than burning premium live bait, because chunk
  holds the school at the boat (`Ow3an9lSVh4`, `n4nMCLLW3tY`). Applies when the
  bait tank is the constraint or nothing better is marked.
- **Stay and wait.** A paddy or piece of structure loaded with skipjack can turn
  into a tuna stop when the tuna arrive and displace them (`Ow3an9lSVh4`).
  Applies when the spot itself is worth the time.
- **Get past them.** When yellowfin are confirmed below, a live bait on the
  surface is a wasted bait; a fast-sinking 1 oz bullet or a sliding sinker gets
  through to the fish that matters (`5Oh3IPIC5g4`, `KajL5TsgBzU`). Applies only
  once something better is confirmed underneath.

Skipjack are worth catching in their own right and are a ready live-bait source
when the target is a bigger bluefin, given an interest in **rare/unusual species
and oddball bycatch, not just target fish** (cameron).
⚠ misplaced-content: the stated interest in rare species and oddball bycatch is
profile data, not fishery doctrine — queued in
[`sources/relocation-queue.md`](../sources/relocation-queue.md).

## Landing & handling

- **Keep them when bait is the point.** A caught skipjack goes into the chunk
  supply rather than back over the side when the plan is to hold a school at the
  boat (`Ow3an9lSVh4`, `n4nMCLLW3tY`).
- **Grade:** a few pounds to low teens.
  ⚠ Fact-check (unverifiable): no corpus source states a size or grade range for
  SoCal/Baja skipjack; the only skipjack weight anywhere in the corpus is a
  joking "7 lb skipjack" (`v8tPYYKM2JE`).
- ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bleeding, chilling, gaffing, or table
  handling for skipjack, including whether a fish destined for the chunk supply
  is treated differently from one kept to eat. General care:
  [tuna care](../fish-care/tuna-care.md), [fish care](../fish-care/README.md).
