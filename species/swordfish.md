# Swordfish (Broadbill)

SoCal **daytime deep-drop** swordfish: drop a dead bait into or just below the
[deep scattering layer](../conditions/deep-scattering-layer.md) on deep contour
structure and let the fish come to you. This note is the full module — the
execution lives here because deep-drop is the one technique. Primary source is
the Costa deep-drop seminar (Duane Diego Mellor, Rush Maltz, Ali Hussainy).

## Where & when

- **Season:** late **September – December**; peak **October – mid-November**
  (year-dependent — one year hot late Sept/Oct, the next mid-Nov; last fish
  ~a week before Christmas). Holdover fish are around year-round but generally
  **not worth the fuel Jan–Aug**. See [Nov–Dec priors](../seasonal/november-december.md).
- **Where:** **150–350 fathoms** on contour lines, thumbs, notches, humps.
  Named working spots: the Ridge, the 9, the 43, the 178, the 302, the 138/182
  area on break days. West-coast model — **set up and let the fish come along
  the fathom-curve highways** (see [island structure](../locations/island-structure.md),
  [zone lexicon](../locations/zone-lexicon.md)).
- **Weather ceiling — a fair-weather technique:** **>15 kt = no-go**; comfortable
  ceiling **10–13 kt**; drift **≤ ~2–2.5 kt** for presentation; a **drift sock**
  extends marginal days. Preferred drift is **"down and in"** along the contour
  edges. See [sea-state](../conditions/sea-state.md).
- **Moon:** bites rise on **new and full and the flanking weeks**
  ([moon](../conditions/moon.md)).
- **Water:** SST + chlorophyll breaks = converging masses; prefer **one big
  powerful "highway" break** over finger rips — find the biggest rip in the zone.
  **Structure × current-edge intersection = the banger days** ("you could have
  been there a week earlier and couldn't buy a bite"). See
  [water color](../conditions/water-color.md), [water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md).

## Finding them (sign & sonar)

The fish **ride the DSL by day** (sat-tag work, PIER / Sepúlveda), so you hunt
the *layer and its bait*, not the fish. Requirements before you stop:

- **DSL present at ≥ 600 ft** — prefer the **900 ft – 150 fathom** band.
- **DSL *plus* chunky bait balls** (anchovy / hake / squid) at **100–150+
  fathoms ≈ bites near-assured.**
- Fish the bait **in the DSL and 100–250 ft below it**; baits **average
  800–1,100 ft**, shallower when the layer is shallow (**DSL at 850 → bait at
  900**).
- **Sounder for the DSL:** TVG / gain pushed up (**Furuno ~55–57**; mid-high on
  other brands), ping speed slowed (**~15 of 20**) to paint the layer. On
  Cameron's **Garmin GPSMAP 840xs**: gain up, slower ping, watch for the band.
  Full method: [electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).
- **Diet corroboration:** stomachs full of hake, small rockfish, squid
  (bottom-adjacent feeders); one Catalina fish full of mullet (they range
  inshore at night).

## Situations → techniques

| Situation (conditions) | Do this | Gear class | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deep contour break, DSL ≥600 ft **+** bait balls at 100–150 fm, wind <13 kt, drift ≤2–2.5 kt | **Daytime deep-drop** (this note) — set drift up-current of the fathom line so the bait sweeps the structure | Heavy 2-speed lever-drag class; manual (non-electric) for Cameron | Banger-day setup; ~7–9 min to deploy two baits |
| DSL present but **no** bait balls / no rip | Keep moving — grade the meter, don't drop on a bare layer | — | "Bait *in* the DSL" is the trigger, not the DSL alone |
| Marginal wind (13–15 kt) or drift >2.5 kt | Deploy a **drift sock**; if still >15 kt, **no-go** | + drift sock | Presentation dies with too much drift |
| Bite window | Weight toward **new/full moon ± flanking weeks** and a big highway break | — | Moon + structure×edge stack the odds |

**Bite handling:** the best hookups come from **rods nobody touches** — let them
wolf it (buoy-gear logic). Leads **7–10.5 lb matched to the drift**.

## Gear summary (class terms)

- **Rod/reel:** heavy stand-up, **2-speed lever-drag class**. Cameron's program
  is **manual (non-electric) reels only** — see [gear classes](../tackle/gear-classes.md).
- **Terminal:** heavy **bite leader class** (200 lb+), sinker leads **7–10.5 lb**
  matched to drift, a light stick / breakaway to drop the lead. Rig parameters:
  [bite leaders](../rigging/bite-leaders.md).
- **Bait:** dead baits (squid, mackerel, mullet-class) fished deep.

## Doctrine & conflicts

- **Doctrine (high — Duane Diego Mellor / Rush Maltz / Ali Hussainy):** the DSL +
  bait-ball rule and the structure×current-edge "banger day" model are the core.
- **Cameron (open item, attributed — not doctrine):** researching SoCal daytime
  swordfish as a future program, **manual reels only**; would need different line
  configurations and wants a **library of banked bulk braid spools** to swap reel
  setups per program. See [rods](../profiles/cameron/rods.md).
