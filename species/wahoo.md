# Wahoo

Water temperature is the gate on wahoo, not the calendar: **72 °F or warmer**
is the standing condition for a chance at one, and fall — late September,
October, early November — is when the Baja banks reach it (`4uNPLknRAQg`,
`9IhmYstB8sA`). Wahoo are a San Diego long-range target on two Baja Pacific
grounds, Alijos Rocks and "the Ridge," a mixed-troll catch out of Magdalena Bay
and the East Cape, and a once-in-an-El-Niño stray inside the Bight. Three
programs cover the fishery: a trolled bomb or diving plug, a cast
bomb or Raider jig burned back, and a flylined sardine — all three on **wire
leader**, because the teeth cut mono regardless of test (`G81HN0dIDg0`). See
[haywire twist](../rigging/haywire-twist.md).

## Where & when

**Season — fall, on a temperature gate.** Fall is the stated best window:
late September, October, early November, with the second week of November 2019
— the boat's last trip of that season — producing "tremendous catches"
(`4uNPLknRAQg`). October and
November are separately named as "the best time of year to catch wahoo"
(`9IhmYstB8sA`). The mechanism is incremental summer warming plus the warm
water a hurricane can push up during hurricane season (`4uNPLknRAQg`). Some
seven- and eight-day boats leave as early as **July** to be first on the
grounds when nothing is biting locally, which works only if the water is
already warm enough (`4uNPLknRAQg`). Long-range 6-, 7- and 8-day trips are
timed to fish Alijos Rocks and the Ridge for wahoo from October onward
(`tjBeR9tWd4s`, `ETw_3AFxEcM`).

**Grounds — two Baja Pacific banks, at long-range distance.**
[Alijos Rocks](../locations/alijos-rocks.md) is rock structure running "from
the surface all the way to the bottom" (`97clKtVsEOs`), and "the Ridge" —
whose upper high spot is named **Uncle Sam Bank** — is the alternative; a
seven-day trip fishes one or the other, not both (`_r08B4bItAY`). Wahoo are
called at "the rocks or on the ridge or on kelp paddies down in that area"
alongside 100 lb yellowfin (see
[yellowfin tuna](yellowfin-tuna.md#where--when)) and
[dorado](dorado.md#where--when) (`_r08B4bItAY`). From San Diego, Alijos Rocks
is **488 mi**, a **45-hour run**; the Ridge/Uncle Sam Bank is **450–460 mi**, a
**two-day run** (`_r08B4bItAY`).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): the same grounds are put at "roughly 400
miles" south of San Diego in a second account (`97clKtVsEOs`); both figures
stand.

**Baja Pacific south, inshore of the banks.**
[Magdalena Bay](../locations/bahia-magdalena-lopez-mateos.md) holds a healthy
wahoo and dorado population "come fall time" (`4t_Z75shK_E`).

**Cortez south.** At the [East Cape](../locations/east-cape.md), wahoo come
mixed into the offshore troll for tuna, dorado and marlin — three boated with
a fourth hooked in one session (`tcso7Lpm_Xs`), and a personal-best fish called
the fleet's "first wahoo of the season" in April 2020 (`APsnsunT4gM`).

**Presence is not catchability.** On the Baja lower banks in November, wahoo
swam through the giant-tuna school a boat sat anchored on for four days and
nothing was landed on them (`ASitOLYzFEA`); at Alijos Rocks wahoo appear in a
mixed bag where yellowtail, yellowfin and dorado are what fill the deck
(`6kpWn2sXokI`).

**Southern California Bight — warm-water event only.** During the
**2014–2015 El Niño**, with water at **85 °F**, wahoo were caught at the oil
platforms off **Huntington Beach**; they left when the water cooled
(`JK2-cpaXkI8`). This is a temperature-driven range extension, not a standing
Bight pattern — see [sand bass](sand-bass.md#where--when) for the full ENSO
framing and
[upwelling and turnover](../conditions/upwelling-and-turnover.md) for the
mechanism.

## Presence & forage

**Warm water pushes them onto structure or onto a paddy.** As the water warms,
wahoo "move into the structure or find a kelp paddy to be on" (`4uNPLknRAQg`) —
the same source ties the bite to structure and current generally. See
[kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md),
[current structure](../conditions/current-structure.md).

**Forage is mixed and changes what colour gets bit.** Wahoo target a variety
of different fish as they swim through the rocks, and the lure colour that
draws bites tracks whatever they are feeding on that day — pink some days, blue
or green others, which is why the kit carries all three (`J3FGJj5zYPE`).

## Feeding triggers

- **Temperature is the switch.** 72 °F or warmer
  is the condition for a chance at a bite; below it, the grounds can be right
  and the season right and there is still no fishery (`4uNPLknRAQg`). See
  [water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md).
- **Structure and current.** Bites come around structure and current, or around
  a kelp paddy the fish have gathered on (`4uNPLknRAQg`).
- **The strike itself is a half-bite.** Wahoo often take only the back half of
  a bait or lure and leave rather than eat it whole (`jqyu3wZdNF4`,
  `vNIazq1aVwc`, `4uNPLknRAQg`) — the behaviour that drives the bite-handling
  doctrine below.
- ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: any light window, tide/slack, moon or
  wind effect on the wahoo bite, and no mechanism for why warm water switches
  them on beyond the fact that it does.

## Finding them (sign & sonar)

- **Kelp paddies are the one searchable sign.** Wahoo get gathered up on
  [kelp paddies](../conditions/kelp-paddies.md); troll around the paddy or in
  its vicinity to get stops (`4uNPLknRAQg`). Fall seven-day trips name paddies
  "down in that area" alongside the rocks and the ridge as wahoo water
  (`_r08B4bItAY`).
- **Fish the top of the water column, not the bottom.** Over **90–100 ft** on
  the Ridge, work the band from **60 ft to the surface**; letting a bomb sink
  to the bottom gets triggerfish and whitefish instead (`4uNPLknRAQg`). Ask the
  crew for the depth before counting a jig down.
- ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: a wahoo sonar signature or meter depth,
  bird behaviour, or any surface sign (jumpers, foamers, breezing fish) that
  locates them — every documented catch is a troll, a cast on the slide, or a
  bait fished on structure. General method:
  [search & glassing](../planning/search-and-glassing.md),
  [electronics & sounder](../planning/electronics-and-sounder.md).

## Gear summary (class terms)

- **Trolling:** the heaviest outfit aboard — **80 lb** class with a two-speed
  conventional reel, **100–135 lb** braid under it (`4uNPLknRAQg`); see
  [wahoo trolling — gear-class detail](../techniques/wahoo-trolling.md#gear-class-detail).
- **Casting:** [jig-stick class](../tackle/gear-classes.md) (~9 ft,
  moderate/fast) with a star-drag or two-speed reel in a mid-to-higher retrieve
  ratio, **80 lb** braid and a **40–60 lb** top shot at least as long as your
  longest cast so no connection sits in the guides (`4uNPLknRAQg`). A separate
  build puts casting line at **50 lb**, up to 62 lb, with lighter risking a
  bite-off — see
  [wahoo bomb & Raider jig casting](../techniques/wahoo-bomb-casting.md).
- **Live bait:** [flyline class](../tackle/gear-classes.md), **60–65 lb** braid
  with a **40 lb** top shot (`4uNPLknRAQg`), sardine on a **200–300 lb**
  single- or multi-strand wire leader — see
  [haywire twist — wahoo live-bait leader](../rigging/haywire-twist.md#wahoo-live-bait-leader).
- **Terminal:** wire leader on every presentation; the teeth cut mono
  regardless of test (`G81HN0dIDg0`). See
  [haywire twist](../rigging/haywire-twist.md).
- **Whole-trip quiver:** the five-outfit long-range kit for an Alijos
  Rocks/Ridge seven-day trip is in
  [Searcher five-outfit quiver](../tackle/searcher-alijos-rocks-ridge-7-day-quiver.md)
  (`uWGGeDOprsE`).
- **Deck safety:** razor teeth are a hazard from the moment a fish is
  boat-side — see
  [wahoo handling & deck safety](../fish-care/wahoo-handling.md).

## Doctrine & conflicts

Section order below is not a ranking.

**Free spool and wait is the settled response to a half-eaten bait; what is
contested is which moment triggers it.** All three accounts agree the wahoo
bites a live bait in half, leaves, and comes back for the remaining chunk if
it is given time, and all three give the same fix — reel into free spool, wait
roughly **10–15 seconds**, do not keep winding (`jqyu3wZdNF4`, `4uNPLknRAQg`,
`vNIazq1aVwc`). The trigger differs: free-spool immediately on the initial hit,
before engaging the reel at all, because putting it in gear right after the hit
ends the bite outright (`jqyu3wZdNF4`); or free-spool reactively, after you set
on the bite and come up with nothing but a chunk still on the hook
(`vNIazq1aVwc`); the intermediate account frames the trigger as the bait having
gone dead after the bite rather than a failed hookset (`4uNPLknRAQg`).
**Decision frame:** the choice turns on whether you set the hook at all — an
angler who sets gets the reactive version, an angler who does not gets the
proactive one. It also sits against the "don't swing — wind through the bite"
instruction in the router table (`9IhmYstB8sA`); all four agree on not reacting
aggressively to the strike.

**Wire versus heavy fluorocarbon on lures turns on lure attrition, not on hookup
rate.** The house rule is wire everything, because a lost lure costs **$25** and
the teeth cut mono regardless of test (`G81HN0dIDg0`, `4uNPLknRAQg`).
Howard Fulmer runs heavy fluorocarbon instead, reasoning that with a full box
of lures he can afford to lose one or two to a bite-off (`4uNPLknRAQg`). A
multi-species popper leader of **2–2.5 ft of 100 lb fluorocarbon or mono** —
no wire, no wahoo caveat — is given while naming wahoo among the targets
(`JHMCguO7sXE`) — see
[tuna poppers and stickbaits — casting gear](../lures/tuna-poppers-and-stickbaits.md#casting-gear-for-poppers-and-burn-baits)
and [wahoo trolling — rigging](../techniques/wahoo-trolling.md#rigging).

**Live-bait wire test is unreconciled by a factor of five.** One build runs
**200–300 lb** wire (`9IhmYstB8sA`), another **60 lb**
sleeve-crimped to a 1/0 or 2/0 hook (`S80GRyuAbLY`; the same demonstration
re-aired as `_Z4yMtrYgeA`, re-cut footage rather than independent
confirmation), and **44 lb** to a 3/0 hook for flylining (`G81HN0dIDg0`), with
no stated reason for the gap. **Decision frame:** no source ties the test to
bait size, water clarity or fish grade, so the choice is between a leader that
will not be bitten off and one that will not deaden a bait — pick per your
tolerance for losing fish versus losing bites. All three builds are in
[haywire twist — wahoo live-bait leader](../rigging/haywire-twist.md#wahoo-live-bait-leader).
⚠ Fact-check (contradicted-internal): three wire tests for one application from
one channel.

**Cast versus troll for the same bomb-style lure turns on hookup ratio versus
water covered.** Trolling is preferred at Alijos Rocks specifically for hookup
ratio on the back-half strike (`97clKtVsEOs`); casting the same lure class is
the standard kit, and casting on the slide is called the better of the two for
bite frequency (`-62xDo4UQzo`, `J3FGJj5zYPE`). **Decision frame:** the split is
about which presentation you commit to when only one is possible — trolling for
the hookup ratio on a short strike, casting for the number of bites. On a boat
that can do both, the troll is what stops the boat and the cast is what works
the stop (`4uNPLknRAQg`).

## Landing & handling

- **Let a wound-in lure settle four or five seconds before lifting it clear.**
  A following wahoo can hit it at the boat and come out of the water with it
  (`4uNPLknRAQg`).
- **Clear the deck and announce the fish, gaff it, then neutralize it with a
  bat before anyone touches it** — a flopping wahoo on deck is what causes
  lacerations, and it can take two or three hits to stop one
  (`4uNPLknRAQg`). Crew pull the hooks, not the angler. Full
  procedure: [wahoo handling & deck safety](../fish-care/wahoo-handling.md).
- ⚠ Flagged gap — no corpus source: bleeding, chilling or ikejime protocol
  specific to wahoo beyond getting the fish into the boat's RSW system. General
  care: [fish care](../fish-care/README.md).
