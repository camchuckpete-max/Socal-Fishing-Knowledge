# Evidence — Water Temperature

Per-source provenance backing [Water Temperature](../water-temperature.md).

## The break-side rules

- `i3qIAHW-SJc` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-08-08, "Top Gun 80 Epic 5 Day, part 2,"
  unregistered channel — medium confidence; Cortez Bank → Osborn Bank): Day 1
  fished 74–75 °F water at Cortez Bank for a mixed yellowtail/yellowfin/
  skipjack bite. Day 2 at Osborn Bank (near Santa Barbara Island) the boat
  ran to markedly cooler water — 70 °F right at the bank edge, about 1.5 mi
  off the boat's position, 72 °F where the boat itself sat, explicitly
  "chasing some cooler water" — and found an early-graylight (roughly
  4:30–5:00 a.m.) bluefin bite in it; yellowtail and yellowfin turned up
  later that morning southeast of the bank, on the warmer side of the same
  edge.
