# Evidence — Kelp Paddies

Per-source provenance backing [Kelp Paddies](../kelp-paddies.md).

## Paddy freshness — check fast, then move

- `ZFqe49jRgA0` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, 2019-05-26, "Top 5
  Offshore Fishing Tips"): source of the direct-approach ("drive right up to
  the edge of it like it's the dock") and check-fast-then-move doctrine, plus
  the restock-at-night correction to the "afternoon bite" folklore.
- `8XrMkWPRxgs` (Capt. Dave Hansen, live seminar, Fred Hall Show 2019, filmed
  by Utah Spahghettzi Björker, 2019-03-12): the same direct-approach and
  restock-at-night doctrine, given roughly two and a half months before
  `ZFqe49jRgA0` in near-identical language — the earliest dated statement of
  this doctrine found in the corpus so far, corroborating rather than
  contradicting the later clip.
- `8THSuqoPI_Q` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-09-26 upload, en route to Catalina
  Island, SoCal, "Simrad Summer part 2"; sponsor-heavy Simrad/Okuma episode,
  unregistered channel — medium confidence): crew stopped on a small local
  piece of kelp, reasoning on camera that they'd caught fish before off
  pieces smaller than this one, then butt-hooked a sardine and let it swim
  down. No hookup was confirmed on camera for this stop.
- `Ow3an9lSVh4` (StokedOnFishing, 2014-11-03, offshore SoCal, "Stoked on
  Simrad, part 3"; sponsor-heavy episode, unregistered channel — medium
  confidence): presenter's stated trip tip backing the skipjack-patience
  doctrine in the main note — a patty showing skipjack was fished rather
  than written off, on the reasoning that better-grade tuna can roll in
  later.
- `HcEh5KOYTH4` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, 2022-10-12, "The Proper
  Way to Fish a Kelp Patty"): a paddy spotted roughly 4 mi out through
  gyro-stabilized binoculars already had fish visibly jumping around it
  before the boat closed the distance. The crew skipped any cautious check
  and ran straight to the edge, pitching baits immediately; the paddy was
  loaded with hundreds of dorado plus incidental yellowtail.
- `CrLDC4O8qS8` (Your Saltwater Guide / Dave Hansen, 2018-10-15, "Kelp Paddy
  Fishing for Dorado and Yellowtail"): an earlier trip found a heavily
  loaded paddy — fish already boiling and jumping around it before the boat
  worked it — producing numerous dorado plus one incidental big yellowtail.
  Source's stated rule of thumb: a paddy showing no visible surface activity
  likely holds "no fish in the ocean," i.e. a dead-looking paddy gets
  written off rather than worked cautiously.
- `-bw1KDfDjv4` (StokedOnFishing, 2015-09-27, offshore San Diego, 14 Mile
  Bank/209/267 complex; sponsor-heavy Simrad/Okuma episode, unregistered
  channel — medium confidence): backing anecdote for the re-work-a-paddy
  doctrine in the main note — caught a yellowtail off a paddy, the boat
  drifted off it, circled back, and caught a few more off the same piece.

## Working the paddy — stand off for tuna

- `A6DJoXbID4c` (StokedOnFishing, 2016-10-06 upload, offshore San Diego —
  launched Shelter Island, worked toward the 43/San Clemente Island;
  unregistered channel — medium confidence): on-camera guidance while
  working a kelp paddy for bluefin, backing the ~100-yard stand-off and
  360°-watch doctrine in the main note. Same trip also backs the
  tern-glassing entry in [bird reading](bird-reading.md).

## Finding paddies

- `VWClGAn2WEw` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-10-13, offshore SoCal, "Offshore Kelp
  Paddy Fishing On the ElDorado," a 2.5-day El Dorado charter out of Long
  Beach Sportfishing; presenter-inferred narration, unregistered channel —
  medium confidence): ran south out of an unproductive overnight bluefin
  push into warmer water before finding the paddy bite (exact location not
  stated); backs the electronics-first, drift-to-the-school doctrine and the
  discoloration-spot/work-birds glassing detail in the main note.
- `9jDy4gUUyJk` (StokedOnFishing, 2019-11-04, location/region not stated;
  unregistered channel — medium confidence): a drone-scouted paddy (per the
  drone method in the main note) produced a couple of dorado that day; the
  crew mentioned they might come back and fish the same spot again later.
  Auto-captions around this point are too garbled to confirm more — e.g.
  whether that meant skipping the rest of the paddy line — no count, depth,
  or paddy-line detail beyond that can be read out of the clip
  (asr-uncertain).
