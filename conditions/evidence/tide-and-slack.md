# Evidence — Tide and Slack

Per-source provenance backing [Tide and Slack](../tide-and-slack.md).

## Arrival discipline

- `5to3Q5P7w90` (BDOutdoors, 2022-09-15, backside of Catalina Island): a
  charter (captain Decker) marked bluefin/dorado sign off the fathom curve on
  the backside of Catalina early in the day but chose not to wait around for
  the slack tide; the boat moved on, found only open-water dorado schools
  below, and boated just one fish for the trip.

## Species tide-dependence

- `Y2bXn44lfqo` (StokedOnFishing, 2023-08-30, Catalina Island; unregistered
  channel — medium confidence): an overnight anchored white seabass soak was
  planned around a 4 a.m. slack tide (turn of the tide) — the crew let most
  of the team sleep beforehand but kept one person on deck with lines soaking
  through the night, then got everyone up for the 4 a.m. turn on the belief
  that the bite is more likely to go off at the change.
