# Evidence — September

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[September](../september.md). One line per observation, grouped under the
parent note's section headings.

## Warm water is soft evidence against bluefin, not a hard ceiling

- `5to3Q5P7w90` (BDOutdoors, Bight Report, 2022-09-15 upload, SoCal Bight —
  exact location not named on camera): bluefin logged foaming on the surface
  in 76 °F water, the warmest the reporter had seen them holding while still
  feeding; self-flagged on camera as unusual, since bluefin normally avoid
  water that warm.

## Baja / BOLA — yellowtail hold on the bottom

- cameron (September program, Bahía de los Ángeles): yellowtail sit on the
  bottom and are worked with dropper loops and yo-yo iron — drop to the marks
  on high structure and crank; a bottom program distinct from the Bight's
  surface/paddy yellowtail game.
