# Evidence — November–December

Trip reports and per-source provenance backing
[November–December](../november-december.md). One line per observation,
grouped under the parent note's section headings.

## Tanner / Cortez bluefin — night-dominant, and the grade drops

- `xzIaUEDklrE` (StokedOnFishing, 2022-12-14 upload, ~55 mi offshore SoCal,
  exact bank not named on camera; unregistered channel — medium confidence):
  a trip landed on this exact date reported schools running 40 to 100+ lb,
  called "the best Southern California bluefin tuna fishing we've seen all
  year," with "very big schools" and fish over 100 lb picked out of the mix;
  the crew said they'd been "killing it every trip" for "the last few
  weeks." This sits against, not folded into, the sub-30 lb mid-December
  reversion in the main note — a same-week data point where the grade was
  still running jumbo, not winter-small. The exact bank/zone is not given,
  so it is not confirmed as the same Tanner/Cortez ground as the doctrine.

## Late-window swordfish — flags into mid-December

- `5D1vx29LVpI` (Bight report, 12/15/22, Newport): the Balboa Angling Club
  took its first swordfish flag of the season the previous day (12/14/22) —
  a one-club, one-year data point that flags can come very late in a given
  year, not a prior that the season starts in December.

## Sharp offshore edges — deep, sonar-dependent fish

- `Z3rZqy2Pi8E` (Bight report, 11/23/22, 8 mi off San Clemente Island): a
  63 → 61.5 °F edge in ~1 mi, sign on the edge but the fish deep and
  sonar-dependent.
