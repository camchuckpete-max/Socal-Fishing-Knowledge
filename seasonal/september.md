# September

**Regime: [warm stable](../conditions/water-regimes.md) (late)** — typical-year
mapping; in an anomalous year trust the live water, not the calendar.

**These are priors — the pattern layer, not current intel.** They weight zones
before you pull live [BightSST](../planning/day-plan-protocol.md) conditions.
A stale report is not today's water.

## The volume square holds the bigger bluefin

**The volume square — SBI (Santa Barbara Island) – Nick (San Nicolas) –
Clemente – Catalina — holds the bigger [bluefin](../species/bluefin-tuna.md)**
(`5to3Q5P7w90`). In September, weight this block of water when you're after
grade. See [bight geography](../locations/bight-geography.md).

## Dorado run open water below Catalina

**[Dorado](../species/dorado.md) run in open-water schools below Catalina** —
not on structure, out in the open water (`5to3Q5P7w90`).

## Yellowfin volume slides north with the warm water — bluefin follow

**[Yellowfin](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) volume near the border slides north
with the warm water, and the bluefin slide with them** (`5to3Q5P7w90`). The
whole complex tracks the warm water northward — follow the temperature, not
last week's spot.

## Warm water is soft evidence against bluefin, not a hard ceiling

A single report logged bluefin foaming on the surface in 76 °F water — above
the usually assumed ceiling, and self-flagged by the reporter as unusual
(`5to3Q5P7w90`); see the evidence file for the raw observation. Treat a warm
SST reading as **soft evidence against bluefin, not a hard cutoff** — it
softens the temperature ceiling but does not move the band. See
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md).

## Hurricane remnants — usually a non-event

**Hurricane remnants are usually a non-event** that breaks the heat with the
fishing unaffected (`5to3Q5P7w90`). Don't over-plan around a tropical system
tracking up the Baja coast; expect it to knock down the heat and little else.
Post-storm water can still roll over — see
[report reading and forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md).

## Baja / BOLA — yellowtail hold on the bottom

Bahía de los Ángeles (BOLA) [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) sit on the
bottom in September, worked with
[dropper loops](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) and
[yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md) — drop to the marks on high
structure and crank (cameron). This is a bottom program, distinct from the
Bight's surface/paddy yellowtail game. See
[knife jigging](../techniques/knife-jigging.md) and
[slow-pitch jigging](../techniques/slow-pitch-jigging.md) for the
vertical-jig alternatives over the same structure.
