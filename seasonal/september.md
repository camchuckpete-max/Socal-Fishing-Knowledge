# September

**Regime: [warm stable](../conditions/water-regimes.md) (late)** — typical-year
mapping; in an anomalous year trust the live water, not the calendar.

**These are priors — the pattern layer, not current intel.** They weight zones
before you pull live [BightSST](../planning/day-plan-protocol.md) conditions.
A stale report is not today's water.

## The "volume square" holds the bigger bluefin

**The volume square — SBI (Santa Barbara Island) – Nick (San Nicolas) –
Clemente – Catalina — holds the bigger [bluefin](../species/bluefin-tuna.md).** In
September, weight this block of water when you're after grade. See
[bight geography](../locations/bight-geography.md).

## The rest of the September lineup

- **[Dorado](../species/dorado.md) run in open-water schools below Catalina** — not on
  structure, out in the open water.
- **[Yellowfin](../species/yellowfin-tuna.md) volume near the border slides north with
  the warm water, and the bluefin slide with them.** The whole complex tracks
  the warm water northward — follow the temperature, not last week's spot.

## Bluefin at 76 °F — soft evidence, not a gate

**Bluefin were observed foaming in 76 °F water** — above the usually assumed
ceiling (`5to3Q5P7w90`). Treat warm SST as **soft evidence against bluefin, not
hard**. This is a single observation the reporter himself flagged as unusual:
it softens the temperature ceiling but does not move the band. See
[water temperature](../conditions/water-temperature.md).

## Hurricane remnants — usually a non-event

**Hurricane remnants are usually a non-event** that breaks the heat with the
fishing unaffected (`5to3Q5P7w90`). Don't over-plan around a tropical system tracking
up the Baja coast; expect it to knock down the heat and little else. (Post-storm
water can still roll over — see
[report reading and forecasting](../planning/report-reading-and-forecasting.md).)

## Baja / BOLA — yellowtail on the bottom (Cameron)

**Observed** (Cameron, September program, Bahía de los Ángeles): in September
the [yellowtail](../species/yellowtail.md) at BOLA are **on the bottom**, and Cameron
fishes them with **[dropper loops](../techniques/dropper-loop.md) and
[yo-yo iron](../techniques/yo-yo-iron.md)** — drop to the marks on high structure and crank
(source: cameron). This is a bottom program, distinct from the surface/paddy
yellowtail game up in the Bight. See [knife jigging](../techniques/knife-jigging.md) and
[slow-pitch jigging](../techniques/slow-pitch-jigging.md) for the vertical-jig options over
the same structure.
